# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 22

**11 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | indra: |triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 1

ya eka̱ iddhavya̍ścarṣaṇī̱nāmindra̱ṃ taṃ gī̱rbhira̱bhya̍rca ā̱bhiḥ |
yaḥ patya̍te vṛṣa̱bho vṛṣṇyā̍vān tsa̱tyaḥ satvā̍ purumā̱yaḥ saha̍svān

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 2

tamu̍ na̱: pūrve̍ pi̱taro̱ nava̍gvāḥ sa̱pta viprā̍so a̱bhi vā̱jaya̍ntaḥ |

na̱kṣa̱ddā̱bhaṃ tatu̍riṃ parvate̱ṣṭhāmadro̍ghavācaṃ ma̱tibhi̱: śavi̍ṣṭham

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 3

tamī̍maha̱ indra̍masya rā̱yaḥ pu̍ru̱vīra̍sya nṛ̱vata̍: puru̱kṣoḥ |

yo askṛ̍dhoyura̱jara̱: sva̍rvā̱n tamā bha̍ra harivo māda̱yadhyai̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 4

tanno̱ vi vo̍co̱ yadi̍ te pu̱rā ci̍jjari̱tāra̍ āna̱śuḥ su̱mnami̍ndra |

kaste̍ bhā̱gaḥ kiṃ vayo̍ dudhra khidva̱: puru̍hūta purūvaso'sura̱ghnaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 5

taṃ pṛ̱cchantī̱ vajra̍hastaṃ rathe̱ṣṭhāmindra̱ṃ vepī̱ vakva̍rī̱ yasya̱ nū gīḥ |

tu̱vi̱grā̱bhaṃ tu̍vikū̱rmiṃ ra̍bho̱dāṃ gā̱tumi̍ṣe̱ nakṣa̍te̱ tumra̱maccha̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 6

a̱yā ha̱ tyaṃ mā̱yayā̍ vāvṛdhā̱naṃ ma̍no̱juvā̍ svatava̱: parva̍tena |

acyu̍tā cid vīl̤i̱tā svo̍jo ru̱jo vi dṛ̱l̤hā dhṛ̍ṣa̱tā vi̍rapśin

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 7

taṃ vo̍ dhi̱yā navya̍syā̱ śavi̍ṣṭhaṃ pra̱tnaṃ pra̍tna̱vat pa̍ritaṃsa̱yadhyai̍ |

sa no̍ vakṣadanimā̱naḥ su̱vahmendro̱ viśvā̱nyati̍ du̱rgahā̍ṇi

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 8

ā janā̍ya̱ druhva̍ṇe̱ pārthi̍vāni di̱vyāni̍ dīpayo̱'ntari̍kṣā |

tapā̍ vṛṣan vi̱śvata̍: śo̱ciṣā̱ tān bra̍hma̱dviṣe̍ śocaya̱ kṣāma̱paśca̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 9

bhuvo̱ jana̍sya di̱vyasya̱ rājā̱ pārthi̍vasya̱ jaga̍tastveṣasaṃdṛk |

dhi̱ṣva vajra̱ṃ dakṣi̍ṇa indra̱ haste̱ viśvā̍ ajurya dayase̱ vi mā̱yāḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 10

ā sa̱ṃyata̍mindra ṇaḥ sva̱stiṃ śa̍tru̱tūryā̍ya bṛha̱tīmamṛ̍dhrām |

yayā̱ dāsā̱nyāryā̍ṇi vṛ̱trā karo̍ vajrin su̱tukā̱ nāhu̍ṣāṇi

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 11
