# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 56

**6 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: |pūṣā | gāyatrī, 6 anuṣṭap |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 1

ya e̍namā̱dide̍śati kara̱mbhāditi̍ pū̱ṣaṇa̍m | na tena̍ de̱va ā̱diśe̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 2

u̱ta ghā̱ sa ra̱thīta̍ma̱: sakhyā̱ satpa̍tiryu̱jā | indro̍ vṛ̱trāṇi̍ jighnate

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 3

u̱tādaḥ pa̍ru̱ṣe gavi̱ sūra̍śca̱kraṃ hi̍ra̱ṇyaya̍m | nyai̍rayadra̱thīta̍maḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 4

yada̱dya tvā̍ puruṣṭuta̱ bravā̍ma dasra mantumaḥ | tatsu no̱ manma̍ sādhaya

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 5

i̱maṃ ca̍ no ga̱veṣa̍ṇaṃ sā̱taye̍ sīṣadho ga̱ṇam | ā̱rātpū̍ṣannasi śru̱taḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 6

ā te̍ sva̱stimī̍maha ā̱rea̍ghā̱mupā̍vasum | a̱dyā ca̍ sa̱rvatā̍taye̱ śvaśca̍ sa̱rvatā̍taye
