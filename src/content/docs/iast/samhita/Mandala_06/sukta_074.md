# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 74

**4 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | somārudrau| triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 1

somā̍rudrā dhā̱raye̍thāmasu̱ryaṃ1 pra vā̍mi̱ṣṭayo'ra̍maśnuvantu |
dame̍dame sa̱pta ratnā̱ dadhā̍nā̱ śaṃ no̍ bhūtaṃ dvi̱pade̱ śaṃ catu̍ṣpade

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 2

somā̍rudrā̱ vi vṛ̍hata̱ṃ viṣū̍cī̱mamī̍vā̱ yā no̱ gaya̍māvi̱veśa̍ |

ā̱re bā̍dhethā̱ṃ niṝ̍tiṃ parā̱caira̱sme bha̱drā sau̍śrava̱sāni̍ santu

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 3

somā̍rudrā yu̱vame̱tānya̱sme viśvā̍ ta̱nūṣu̍ bheṣa̱jāni̍ dhattam |

ava̍ syataṃ mu̱ñcata̱ṃ yanno̱ asti̍ ta̱nūṣu̍ ba̱ddhaṃ kṛ̱tameno̍ a̱smat

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 4

ti̱gmāyu̍dhau ti̱gmahe̍tī su̱śevau̱ somā̍rudrāvi̱ha su mṛ̍l̤ataṃ naḥ |

pra no̍ muñcata̱ṃ varu̍ṇasya̱ pāśā̍dgopā̱yata̍ṃ naḥ sumana̱syamā̍nā
