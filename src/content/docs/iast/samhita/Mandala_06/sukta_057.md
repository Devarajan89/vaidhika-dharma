# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 57

**6 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: |iṃdrāpūṣaṇau | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 1

indrā̱ nu pū̱ṣaṇā̍ va̱yaṃ sa̱khyāya̍ sva̱staye̍ | hu̱vema̱ vāja̍sātaye

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 2

soma̍ma̱nya upā̍sada̱tpāta̍ve ca̱mvo̍: su̱tam | ka̱ra̱mbhama̱nya i̍cchati

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 3

a̱jā a̱nyasya̱ vahna̍yo̱ harī̍ a̱nyasya̱ sambhṛ̍tā | tābhyā̍ṃ vṛ̱trāṇi̍ jighnate

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 4

yadindro̱ ana̍ya̱drito̍ ma̱hīra̱po vṛṣa̍ntamaḥ | tatra̍ pū̱ṣābha̍va̱tsacā̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 5

tāṃ pū̱ṣṇaḥ su̍ma̱tiṃ va̱yaṃ vṛ̱kṣasya̱ pra va̱yāmi̍va | indra̍sya̱ cā ra̍bhāmahe

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 6

utpū̱ṣaṇa̍ṃ yuvāmahe̱'bhīśū̍m̐riva̱ sāra̍thiḥ | ma̱hyā indra̍ṃ sva̱staye̍
