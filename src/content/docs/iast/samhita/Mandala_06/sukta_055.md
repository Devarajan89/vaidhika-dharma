# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 55

**6 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: |pūṣā | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 1

ehi̱ vāṃ vi̍muco napā̱dāghṛ̍ṇe̱ saṃ sa̍cāvahai | ra̱thīṝ̱tasya̍ no bhava

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 2

ra̱thīta̍maṃ kapa̱rdina̱mīśā̍na̱ṃ rādha̍so ma̱haḥ | rā̱yaḥ sakhā̍yamīmahe

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 3

rā̱yo dhārā̍syāghṛṇe̱ vaso̍ rā̱śira̍jāśva | dhīva̍todhīvata̱: sakhā̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 4

pū̱ṣaṇa̱ṃ nva1jāśva̱mupa̍ stoṣāma vā̱jina̍m | svasu̱ryo jā̱ra u̱cyate̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 5

mā̱turdi̍dhi̱ṣuma̍brava̱ṃ svasu̍rjā̱raḥ śṛ̍ṇotu naḥ | bhrātendra̍sya̱ sakhā̱ mama̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 6

ājāsa̍: pū̱ṣaṇa̱ṃ rathe̍ niśṛ̱mbhāste ja̍na̱śriya̍m | de̱vaṃ va̍hantu̱ bibhra̍taḥ
