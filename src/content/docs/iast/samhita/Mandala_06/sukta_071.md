# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 71

**6 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | savitā |jagatī, 4-6 triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 1

udu̱ ṣya de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā hi̍ra̱ṇyayā̍ bā̱hū a̍yaṃsta̱ sava̍nāya su̱kratu̍: |
ghṛ̱tena̍ pā̱ṇī a̱bhi pru̍ṣṇute ma̱kho yuvā̍ su̱dakṣo̱ raja̍so̱ vidha̍rmaṇi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 2

de̱vasya̍ va̱yaṃ sa̍vi̱tuḥ savī̍mani̱ śreṣṭhe̍ syāma̱ vasu̍naśca dā̱vane̍ |

yo viśva̍sya dvi̱pado̱ yaścatu̍ṣpado ni̱veśa̍ne prasa̱ve cāsi̱ bhūma̍naḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 3

ada̍bdhebhiḥ savitaḥ pā̱yubhi̱ṣṭvaṃ śi̱vebhi̍ra̱dya pari̍ pāhi no̱ gaya̍m |

hira̍ṇyajihvaḥ suvi̱tāya̱ navya̍se̱ rakṣā̱ māki̍rno a̱ghaśa̍ṃsa īśata

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 4

udu̱ ṣya de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā damū̍nā̱ hira̍ṇyapāṇiḥ pratido̱ṣama̍sthāt |

ayo̍hanuryaja̱to ma̱ndraji̍hva̱ ā dā̱śuṣe̍ suvati̱ bhūri̍ vā̱mam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 5

udū̍ ayām̐ upava̱kteva̍ bā̱hū hi̍ra̱ṇyayā̍ savi̱tā su̱pratī̍kā |

di̱vo rohā̍ṃsyaruhatpṛthi̱vyā arī̍ramatpa̱taya̱tkacci̱dabhva̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 6

vā̱mama̱dya sa̍vitarvā̱mamu̱ śvo di̱vedi̍ve vā̱mama̱smabhya̍ṃ sāvīḥ |

vā̱masya̱ hi kṣaya̍sya deva̱ bhūre̍ra̱yā dhi̱yā vā̍ma̱bhāja̍: syāma
