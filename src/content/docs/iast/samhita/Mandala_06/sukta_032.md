# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 32

**5 suhotro bhāradvāja: | indra: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 1

apū̍rvyā puru̱tamā̍nyasmai ma̱he vī̱rāya̍ ta̱vase̍ tu̱rāya̍ |
vi̱ra̱pśine̍ va̱jriṇe̱ śaṃta̍māni̱ vacā̍ṃsyā̱sā sthavi̍rāya takṣam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 2

sa mā̱tarā̱ sūrye̍ṇā kavī̱nāmavā̍sayadru̱jadadri̍ṃ gṛṇā̱naḥ |

svā̱dhībhi̱ṝkva̍bhirvāvaśā̱na udu̱sriyā̍ṇāmasṛjanni̱dāna̍m

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 3

sa vahni̍bhi̱ṝkva̍bhi̱rgoṣu̱ śaśva̍nmi̱tajñu̍bhiḥ puru̱kṛtvā̍ jigāya |

pura̍: puro̱hā sakhi̍bhiḥ sakhī̱yandṛ̱l̤hā ru̍roja ka̱vibhi̍: ka̱viḥ san

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 4

pu̱ru̱vīrā̍bhirvṛṣabha kṣitī̱nāmā gi̍rvaṇaḥ suvi̱tāya̱ pra yā̍hi
