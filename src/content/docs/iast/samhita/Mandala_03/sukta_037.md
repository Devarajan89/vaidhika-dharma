# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 37

**11 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ|gāyatrī, 11 triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 1

vārtra̍hatyāya̱ śava̍se pṛtanā̱ṣāhyā̍ya ca | indra̱ tvā va̍rtayāmasi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 2

a̱rvā̱cīna̱ṃ su te̱ mana̍ u̱ta cakṣu̍: śatakrato | indra̍ kṛ̱ṇvantu̍ vā̱ghata̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 3

nāmā̍ni te śatakrato̱ viśvā̍bhirgī̱rbhirī̍mahe | indrā̍bhimāti̱ṣāhye̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 4

pu̱ru̱ṣṭu̱tasya̱ dhāma̍bhiḥ śa̱tena̍ mahayāmasi | indra̍sya carṣaṇī̱dhṛta̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 5

indra̍ṃ vṛ̱trāya̱ hanta̍ve puruhū̱tamupa̍ bruve | bhare̍ṣu̱ vāja̍sātaye

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 6

## Verse 8
