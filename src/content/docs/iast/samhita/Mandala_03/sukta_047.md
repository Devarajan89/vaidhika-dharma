# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 47

**5 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 1

ma̱rutvā̍m̐ indra vṛṣa̱bho raṇā̍ya̱ pibā̱ soma̍manuṣva̱dhaṃ madā̍ya |
ā si̍ñcasva ja̱ṭhare̱ madhva̍ ū̱rmiṃ tvaṃ rājā̍si pra̱diva̍: su̱tānā̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 2

sa̱joṣā̍ indra̱ saga̍ṇo ma̱rudbhi̱: soma̍ṃ piba vṛtra̱hā śū̍ra vi̱dvān |

ja̱hi śatrū̱m̐rapa̱ mṛdho̍ nuda̱svāthābha̍yaṃ kṛṇuhi vi̱śvato̍ naḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 3

u̱ta ṛ̱tubhi̍rṛtupāḥ pāhi̱ soma̱mindra̍ de̱vebhi̱: sakhi̍bhiḥ su̱taṃ na̍: |

yām̐ ābha̍jo ma̱ruto̱ ye tvānvaha̍n vṛ̱tramada̍dhu̱stubhya̱moja̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 4

ye tvā̍hi̱hatye̍ maghava̱nnava̍rdha̱n ye śā̍mba̱re ha̍rivo̱ ye gavi̍ṣṭau |

ye tvā̍ nū̱nama̍nu̱mada̍nti̱ viprā̱: pibe̍ndra̱ soma̱ṃ saga̍ṇo ma̱rudbhi̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 5

ma̱rutva̍ntaṃ vṛṣa̱bhaṃ vā̍vṛdhā̱namaka̍vāriṃ di̱vyaṃ śā̱samindra̍m |

vi̱śvā̱sāha̱mava̍se̱ nūta̍nāyo̱graṃ sa̍ho̱dāmi̱ha taṃ hu̍vema
