# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 34

**11 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 1

indra̍: pū̱rbhidāti̍ra̱ddāsa̍ma̱rkairvi̱dadva̍su̱rdaya̍māno̱ vi śatrū̍n |
brahma̍jūtasta̱nvā̍ vāvṛdhā̱no bhūri̍dātra̱ āpṛ̍ṇa̱droda̍sī u̱bhe

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 2

ma̱khasya̍ te tavi̱ṣasya̱ pra jū̱timiya̍rmi̱ vāca̍ma̱mṛtā̍ya̱ bhūṣa̍n |

indra̍ kṣitī̱nāma̍si̱ mānu̍ṣīṇāṃ vi̱śāṃ daivī̍nāmu̱ta pū̍rva̱yāvā̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 3

indro̍ vṛ̱trama̍vṛṇo̱cchardha̍nīti̱: pra mā̱yinā̍maminā̱d varpa̍ṇītiḥ |

aha̱n vya̍ṃsamu̱śadha̱gvane̍ṣvā̱virdhenā̍ akṛṇod rā̱myāṇā̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 4

indra̍: sva̱rṣā ja̱naya̱nnahā̍ni ji̱gāyo̱śigbhi̱: pṛta̍nā abhi̱ṣṭiḥ |

prāro̍caya̱nmana̍ve ke̱tumahnā̱mavi̍nda̱jjyoti̍rbṛha̱te raṇā̍ya

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 5

indra̱stujo̍ ba̱rhaṇā̱ ā vi̍veśa nṛ̱vad dadhā̍no̱ naryā̍ pu̱rūṇi̍ |

ace̍taya̱d dhiya̍ i̱mā ja̍ri̱tre premaṃ varṇa̍matiracchu̱kramā̍sām

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 6

ma̱ho ma̱hāni̍ panayantya̱syendra̍sya̱ karma̱ sukṛ̍tā pu̱rūṇi̍ |

vṛ̱jane̍na vṛji̱nān tsaṃ pi̍peṣa mā̱yābhi̱rdasyū̍m̐ra̱bhibhū̍tyojāḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 7

yu̱dhendro̍ ma̱hnā vari̍vaścakāra de̱vebhya̱: satpa̍tiścarṣaṇi̱prāḥ |

vi̱vasva̍ta̱: sada̍ne asya̱ tāni̱ viprā̍ u̱kthebhi̍: ka̱vayo̍ gṛṇanti

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 8

sa̱trā̱sāha̱ṃ vare̍ṇyaṃ saho̱dāṃ sa̍sa̱vāṃsa̱ṃ sva̍ra̱paśca̍ de̱vīḥ |

sa̱sāna̱ yaḥ pṛ̍thi̱vīṃ dyāmu̱temāmindra̍ṃ mada̱ntyanu̱ dhīra̍ṇāsaḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 9

sa̱sānātyā̍m̐ u̱ta sūrya̍ṃ sasā̱nendra̍: sasāna puru̱bhoja̍sa̱ṃ gām |

hi̱ra̱ṇyaya̍mu̱ta bhoga̍ṃ sasāna ha̱tvī dasyū̱nprārya̱ṃ varṇa̍māvat

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 10

indra̱ oṣa̍dhīrasano̱dahā̍ni̱ vana̱spatī̍m̐rasanoda̱ntari̍kṣam |

bi̱bheda̍ va̱laṃ nu̍nu̱de vivā̱co'thā̍bhavad dami̱tābhikra̍tūnām

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 11

||

śu̱naṃ hu̍vema ma̱ghavā̍na̱mindra̍ma̱smin bhare̱ nṛta̍ma̱ṃ vāja̍sātau |

śṛ̱ṇvanta̍mu̱gramū̱taye̍ sa̱matsu̱ ghnanta̍ṃ vṛ̱trāṇi̍ sa̱ṃjita̱ṃ dhanā̍nām
