# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 41

**9 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 1

ā tū na̍ indra ma̱drya̍gghuvā̱naḥ soma̍pītaye | hari̍bhyāṃ yāhyadrivaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 2

sa̱tto hotā̍ na ṛ̱tviya̍stisti̱re ba̱rhirā̍nu̱ṣak | ayu̍jranprā̱taradra̍yaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 3

i̱mā brahma̍ brahmavāhaḥ kri̱yanta̱ ā ba̱rhiḥ sī̍da | vī̱hi śū̍ra puro̱l̤āśa̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 4

rā̱ra̱ndhi sava̍neṣu ṇa e̱ṣu stome̍ṣu vṛtrahan | u̱ktheṣvi̍ndra girvaṇaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 5

ma̱taya̍: soma̱pāmu̱ruṃ ri̱hanti̱ śava̍sa̱spati̍m | indra̍ṃ va̱tsaṃ na mā̱tara̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 6

sa ma̍ndasvā̱ hyandha̍so̱ rādha̍se ta̱nvā̍ ma̱he | na sto̱tāra̍ṃ ni̱de ka̍raḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 7

va̱yami̍ndra tvā̱yavo̍ ha̱viṣma̍nto jarāmahe | u̱ta tvama̍sma̱yurva̍so

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 8

māre a̱smad vi mu̍muco̱ hari̍priyā̱rvāṅ yā̍hi | indra̍ svadhāvo̱ matsve̱ha

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 9

a̱rvāñca̍ṃ tvā su̱khe rathe̱ vaha̍tāmindra ke̱śinā̍ | ghṛ̱tasnū̍ ba̱rhirā̱sade̍
