# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 39

**10 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 1

indra̍ṃ ma̱tirhṛ̱da ā va̱cyamā̱nā 'cchā̱ pati̱ṃ stoma̍taṣṭā jigāti |
yā jāgṛ̍virvi̱dathe̍ śa̱syamā̱nendra̱ yatte̱ jāya̍te vi̱ddhi tasya̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 2

di̱vaści̱dā pū̱rvyā jāya̍mānā̱ vi jāgṛ̍virvi̱dathe̍ śa̱syamā̍nā |

bha̱drā vastrā̱ṇyarju̍nā̱ vasā̍nā̱ seyama̱sme sa̍na̱jā pitryā̱ dhīḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 3

ya̱mā ci̱datra̍ yama̱sūra̍sūta ji̱hvāyā̱ agra̱ṃ pata̱dā hyasthā̍t |

vapū̍ṃṣi jā̱tā mi̍thu̱nā sa̍cete tamo̱hanā̱ tapu̍ṣo bu̱dhna etā̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 4

naki̍reṣāṃ nindi̱tā martye̍ṣu̱ ye a̱smāka̍ṃ pi̱taro̱ goṣu̍ yo̱dhāḥ |

indra̍ eṣāṃ dṛṃhi̱tā māhi̍nāvā̱nud go̱trāṇi̍ sasṛje da̱ṃsanā̍vān

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 5

sakhā̍ ha̱ yatra̱ sakhi̍bhi̱rnava̍gvairabhi̱jñvā satva̍bhi̱rgā a̍nu̱gman |

sa̱tyaṃ tadindro̍ da̱śabhi̱rdaśa̍gvai̱: sūrya̍ṃ viveda̱ tama̍si kṣi̱yanta̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 6

indro̱ madhu̱ sambhṛ̍tamu̱sriyā̍yāṃ pa̱dvad vi̍veda śa̱phava̱nname̱ goḥ |

guhā̍ hi̱taṃ guhya̍ṃ gū̱l̤ahama̱psu haste̍ dadhe̱ dakṣi̍ṇe̱ dakṣi̍ṇāvān

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 7

jyoti̍rvṛṇīta̱ tama̍so vijā̱nannā̱re syā̍ma duri̱tāda̱bhīke̍ |

i̱mā gira̍: somapāḥ somavṛddha ju̱ṣasve̍ndra puru̱tama̍sya kā̱roḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 8

jyoti̍rya̱jñāya̱ roda̍sī̱ anu̍ ṣyādā̱re syā̍ma duri̱tasya̱ bhūre̍: |

bhūri̍ ci̱ddhi tu̍ja̱to martya̍sya supā̱rāso̍ vasavo ba̱rhaṇā̍vat

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 9

śu̱naṃ hu̍vema ma̱ghavā̍na̱mindra̍ma̱smin bhare̱ nṛta̍ma̱ṃ vāja̍sātau |

śṛ̱ṇvanta̍mu̱gramū̱taye̍ sa̱matsu̱ ghnanta̍ṃ vṛ̱trāṇi̍ sa̱ṃjita̱ṃ dhanā̍nām
