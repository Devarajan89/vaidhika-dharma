# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 39

**6 bṛhanmatirāṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 1

ā̱śura̍rṣa bṛhanmate̱ pari̍ pri̱yeṇa̱ dhāmnā̍ | yatra̍ de̱vā iti̱ brava̍n

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 2

pa̱ri̱ṣkṛ̱ṇvannani̍ṣkṛta̱ṃ janā̍ya yā̱taya̱nniṣa̍: | vṛ̱ṣṭiṃ di̱vaḥ pari̍ srava

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 3

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 4

a̱yaṃ sa yo di̱vaspari̍ raghu̱yāmā̍ pa̱vitra̱ ā | sindho̍rū̱rmā vyakṣa̍rat

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 5
