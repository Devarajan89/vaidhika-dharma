# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 54

**4 avatsāra: kāśyapaḥ | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 1

a̱sya pra̱tnāmanu̱ dyuta̍ṃ śu̱kraṃ du̍duhre̱ ahra̍yaḥ | paya̍: sahasra̱sāmṛṣi̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 2

a̱yaṃ sūrya̍ ivopa̱dṛga̱yaṃ sarā̍ṃsi dhāvati | sa̱pta pra̱vata̱ ā diva̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 3

a̱yaṃ viśvā̍ni tiṣṭhati punā̱no bhuva̍no̱pari̍ | somo̍ de̱vo na sūrya̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 4

pari̍ ṇo de̱vavī̍taye̱ vājā̍m̐ arṣasi̱ goma̍taḥ | pu̱nā̱na i̍ndavindra̱yuḥ
