# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 29

**6 nṛmedha āṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 1

prāsya̱ dhārā̍ akṣara̱nvṛṣṇa̍: su̱tasyauja̍sā | de̱vām̐ anu̍ pra̱bhūṣa̍taḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 2

sapti̍ṃ mṛjanti ve̱dhaso̍ gṛ̱ṇanta̍: kā̱ravo̍ gi̱rā | jyoti̍rjajñā̱namu̱kthya̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 3

su̱ṣahā̍ soma̱ tāni̍ te punā̱nāya̍ prabhūvaso | vardhā̍ samu̱dramu̱kthya̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 4

viśvā̱ vasū̍ni sa̱ṃjaya̱npava̍sva soma̱ dhāra̍yā | i̱nu dveṣā̍ṃsi sa̱dhrya̍k

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 5

rakṣā̱ su no̱ ara̍ruṣaḥ sva̱nātsa̍masya̱ kasya̍ cit | ni̱do yatra̍ mumu̱cmahe̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 6

endo̱ pārthi̍vaṃ ra̱yiṃ di̱vyaṃ pa̍vasva̱ dhāra̍yā | dyu̱manta̱ṃ śuṣma̱mā bha̍ra
