# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 7

**9 kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 1

asṛ̍gra̱minda̍vaḥ pa̱thā dharma̍nnṛ̱tasya̍ su̱śriya̍: | vi̱dā̱nā a̍sya̱ yoja̍nam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 2

pra dhārā̱ madhvo̍ agri̱yo ma̱hīra̱po vi gā̍hate | ha̱virha̱viṣṣu̱ vandya̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 3

pra yu̱jo vā̱co a̍gri̱yo vṛṣāva̍ cakrada̱dvane̍ | sadmā̱bhi sa̱tyo a̍dhva̱raḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 4

pari̱ yatkāvyā̍ ka̱virnṛ̱mṇā vasā̍no̱ arṣa̍ti | sva̍rvā̱jī si̍ṣāsati

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 5

pava̍māno a̱bhi spṛdho̱ viśo̱ rāje̍va sīdati | yadī̍mṛ̱ṇvanti̍ ve̱dhasa̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 6

avyo̱ vāre̱ pari̍ pri̱yo hari̱rvane̍ṣu sīdati | re̱bho va̍nuṣyate ma̱tī

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 7

sa vā̱yumindra̍ma̱śvinā̍ sā̱kaṃ made̍na gacchati | raṇā̱ yo a̍sya̱ dharma̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 8

ā mi̱trāvaru̍ṇā̱ bhaga̱ṃ madhva̍: pavanta ū̱rmaya̍: | vi̱dā̱nā a̍sya̱ śakma̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 9

a̱smabhya̍ṃ rodasī ra̱yiṃ madhvo̱ vāja̍sya sā̱taye̍ | śravo̱ vasū̍ni̱ saṃ ji̍tam
