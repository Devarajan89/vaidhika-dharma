# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 69

**10 hiraṇyastūpa   āṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: |jagatī ,9-10 triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 1

iṣu̱rna dhanva̱nprati̍ dhīyate ma̱tirva̱tso na mā̱turupa̍ sa̱rjyūdha̍ni |
u̱rudhā̍reva duhe̱ agra̍ āya̱tyasya̍ vra̱teṣvapi̱ soma̍ iṣyate

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 2

upo̍ ma̱tiḥ pṛ̱cyate̍ si̱cyate̱ madhu̍ ma̱ndrāja̍nī codate a̱ntarā̱sani̍ |

pava̍mānaḥ saṃta̱niḥ pra̍ghna̱tāmi̍va̱ madhu̍māndra̱psaḥ pari̱ vāra̍marṣati

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 3

avye̍ vadhū̱yuḥ pa̍vate̱ pari̍ tva̱ci śra̍thnī̱te na̱ptīradi̍teṝ̱taṃ ya̱te |

hari̍rakrānyaja̱taḥ sa̍ṃya̱to mado̍ nṛ̱mṇā śiśā̍no mahi̱ṣo na śo̍bhate

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 4

u̱kṣā mi̍māti̱ prati̍ yanti dhe̱navo̍ de̱vasya̍ de̱vīrupa̍ yanti niṣkṛ̱tam |

atya̍kramī̱darju̍na̱ṃ vāra̍ma̱vyaya̱matka̱ṃ na ni̱ktaṃ pari̱ somo̍ avyata

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 5

amṛ̍ktena̱ ruśa̍tā̱ vāsa̍sā̱ hari̱rama̍rtyo nirṇijā̱naḥ pari̍ vyata |

di̱vaspṛ̱ṣṭhaṃ ba̱rhaṇā̍ ni̱rṇije̍ kṛtopa̱stara̍ṇaṃ ca̱mvo̍rnabha̱smaya̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 6

sūrya̍syeva ra̱śmayo̍ drāvayi̱tnavo̍ matsa̱rāsa̍: pra̱supa̍: sā̱kamī̍rate |

tantu̍ṃ ta̱taṃ pari̱ sargā̍sa ā̱śavo̱ nendrā̍dṛ̱te pa̍vate̱ dhāma̱ kiṃ ca̱na

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 7

sindho̍riva prava̱ṇe ni̱mna ā̱śavo̱ vṛṣa̍cyutā̱ madā̍so gā̱tumā̍śata |

śaṃ no̍ nive̱śe dvi̱pade̱ catu̍ṣpade̱'sme vājā̍: soma tiṣṭhantu kṛ̱ṣṭaya̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 8

ā na̍: pavasva̱ vasu̍ma̱ddhira̍ṇyava̱daśvā̍va̱dgoma̱dyava̍matsu̱vīrya̍m |

yū̱yaṃ hi so̍ma pi̱taro̱ mama̱ sthana̍ di̱vo mū̱rdhāna̱: prasthi̍tā vaya̱skṛta̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 9

e̱te somā̱: pava̍mānāsa̱ indra̱ṃ rathā̍ iva̱ pra ya̍yuḥ sā̱timaccha̍ |

su̱tāḥ pa̱vitra̱mati̍ ya̱ntyavya̍ṃ hi̱tvī va̱vriṃ ha̱rito̍ vṛ̱ṣṭimaccha̍

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 10

inda̱vindrā̍ya bṛha̱te pa̍vasva sumṛl̤ī̱ko a̍nava̱dyo ri̱śādā̍: |

bharā̍ ca̱ndrāṇi̍ gṛṇa̱te vasū̍ni de̱vairdyā̍vāpṛthivī̱ prāva̍taṃ naḥ
