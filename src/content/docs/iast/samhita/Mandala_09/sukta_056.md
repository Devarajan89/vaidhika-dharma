# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 56

**4 avatsāra: kāśyapaḥ | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 1

pari̱ soma̍ ṛ̱taṃ bṛ̱hadā̱śuḥ pa̱vitre̍ arṣati | vi̱ghnanrakṣā̍ṃsi deva̱yuḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 2

yatsomo̱ vāja̱marṣa̍ti śa̱taṃ dhārā̍ apa̱syuva̍: | indra̍sya sa̱khyamā̍vi̱śan

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 3

a̱bhi tvā̱ yoṣa̍ṇo̱ daśa̍ jā̱raṃ na ka̱nyā̍nūṣata | mṛ̱jyase̍ soma sā̱taye̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 4

tvamindrā̍ya̱ viṣṇa̍ve svā̱duri̍ndo̱ pari̍ srava | nṝntsto̱tṝnpā̱hyaṃha̍saḥ
