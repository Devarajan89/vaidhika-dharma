# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 22

**7   kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 1

e̱te somā̍sa ā̱śavo̱ rathā̍ iva̱ pra vā̱jina̍: | sargā̍: sṛ̱ṣṭā a̍heṣata

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 2

e̱te vātā̍ ivo̱rava̍: pa̱rjanya̍syeva vṛ̱ṣṭaya̍: | a̱gneri̍va bhra̱mā vṛthā̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 3

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 4

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 5

e̱te pṛ̱ṣṭhāni̱ roda̍sorvipra̱yanto̱ vyā̍naśuḥ | u̱tedamu̍tta̱maṃ raja̍:

## Verse 6
