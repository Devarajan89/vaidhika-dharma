# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 73

**9 pavitra āṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: |jagatī  |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 1

srakve̍ dra̱psasya̱ dhama̍ta̱: sama̍svarannṛ̱tasya̱ yonā̱ sama̍ranta̱ nābha̍yaḥ |
trīntsa mū̱rdhno asu̍raścakra ā̱rabhe̍ sa̱tyasya̱ nāva̍: su̱kṛta̍mapīparan

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 2

sa̱myaksa̱myañco̍ mahi̱ṣā a̍heṣata̱ sindho̍rū̱rmāvadhi̍ ve̱nā a̍vīvipan |

madho̱rdhārā̍bhirja̱naya̍nto a̱rkamitpri̱yāmindra̍sya ta̱nva̍mavīvṛdhan

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 3

pa̱vitra̍vanta̱: pari̱ vāca̍māsate pi̱taiṣā̍ṃ pra̱tno a̱bhi ra̍kṣati vra̱tam |

ma̱haḥ sa̍mu̱draṃ varu̍ṇasti̱ro da̍dhe̱ dhīrā̱ icche̍kurdha̱ruṇe̍ṣvā̱rabha̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 4

sa̱hasra̍dhā̱re'va̱ te sama̍svarandi̱vo nāke̱ madhu̍jihvā asa̱ścata̍: |

asya̱ spaśo̱ na ni mi̍ṣanti̱ bhūrṇa̍yaḥ pa̱depa̍de pā̱śina̍: santi̱ seta̍vaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 5

pi̱turmā̱turadhyā ye sa̱masva̍rannṛ̱cā śoca̍ntaḥ sa̱ṃdaha̍nto avra̱tān |

indra̍dviṣṭā̱mapa̍ dhamanti mā̱yayā̱ tvaca̱masi̍knī̱ṃ bhūma̍no di̱vaspari̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 6

pra̱tnānmānā̱dadhyā ye sa̱masva̍ra̱ñchloka̍yantrāso rabha̱sasya̱ manta̍vaḥ |

apā̍na̱kṣāso̍ badhi̱rā a̍hāsata ṛ̱tasya̱ panthā̱ṃ na ta̍ranti du̱ṣkṛta̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 7

sa̱hasra̍dhāre̱ vita̍te pa̱vitra̱ ā vāca̍ṃ punanti ka̱vayo̍ manī̱ṣiṇa̍: |

ru̱drāsa̍ eṣāmiṣi̱rāso̍ a̱druha̱: spaśa̱: svañca̍: su̱dṛśo̍ nṛ̱cakṣa̍saḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 8

ṛ̱tasya̍ go̱pā na dabhā̍ya su̱kratu̱strī ṣa pa̱vitrā̍ hṛ̱dya1ntarā da̍dhe |

vi̱dvāntsa viśvā̱ bhuva̍nā̱bhi pa̍śya̱tyavāju̍ṣṭānvidhyati ka̱rte a̍vra̱tān

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 9

ṛ̱tasya̱ tantu̱rvita̍taḥ pa̱vitra̱ ā ji̱hvāyā̱ agre̱ varu̍ṇasya mā̱yayā̍ |

dhīrā̍ści̱ttatsa̱mina̍kṣanta āśa̱tā'trā̍ ka̱rtamava̍ padā̱tyapra̍bhuḥ
