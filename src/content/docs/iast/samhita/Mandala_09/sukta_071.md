# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 71

**9 ṛṣabho vaiśvāmitra: | pavamāna: soma: |jagatī ,9 triṣṭup  |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 1

ā dakṣi̍ṇā sṛjyate śu̱ṣmyā̱3sada̱ṃ veti̍ dru̱ho ra̱kṣasa̍: pāti̱ jāgṛ̍viḥ |
hari̍ropa̱śaṃ kṛ̍ṇute̱ nabha̱spaya̍ upa̱stire̍ ca̱mvo̱3rbrahma̍ ni̱rṇije̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 2

pra kṛ̍ṣṭi̱heva̍ śū̱ṣa e̍ti̱ roru̍vadasu̱ryaṃ1 varṇa̱ṃ ni ri̍ṇīte asya̱ tam |

jahā̍ti va̱vriṃ pi̱ture̍ti niṣkṛ̱tamu̍pa̱pruta̍ṃ kṛṇute ni̱rṇija̱ṃ tanā̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 3

adri̍bhiḥ su̱taḥ pa̍vate̱ gabha̍styorvṛṣā̱yate̱ nabha̍sā̱ vepa̍te ma̱tī |

sa mo̍date̱ nasa̍te̱ sādha̍te gi̱rā ne̍ni̱kte a̱psu yaja̍te̱ parī̍maṇi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 4

pari̍ dyu̱kṣaṃ saha̍saḥ parvatā̱vṛdha̱ṃ madhva̍: siñcanti ha̱rmyasya̍ sa̱kṣaṇi̍m |

ā yasmi̱ngāva̍: suhu̱tāda̱ ūdha̍ni mū̱rdhañchrī̱ṇantya̍gri̱yaṃ varī̍mabhiḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 5

samī̱ ratha̱ṃ na bhu̱rijo̍raheṣata̱ daśa̱ svasā̍ro̱ adi̍teru̱pastha̱ ā |

jigā̱dupa̍ jrayati̱ gora̍pī̱cya̍ṃ pa̱daṃ yada̍sya ma̱tuthā̱ ajī̍janan

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 6

śye̱no na yoni̱ṃ sada̍naṃ dhi̱yā kṛ̱taṃ hi̍ra̱ṇyaya̍mā̱sada̍ṃ de̱va eṣa̍ti |

e ri̍ṇanti ba̱rhiṣi̍ pri̱yaṃ gi̱rā'śvo̱ na de̱vām̐ apye̍ti ya̱jñiya̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 7

parā̱ vya̍kto aru̱ṣo di̱vaḥ ka̱virvṛṣā̍ tripṛ̱ṣṭho a̍naviṣṭa̱ gā a̱bhi |

sa̱hasra̍ṇīti̱ryati̍: parā̱yatī̍ re̱bho na pū̱rvīru̱ṣaso̱ vi rā̍jati

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 8

tve̱ṣaṃ rū̱paṃ kṛ̍ṇute̱ varṇo̍ asya̱ sa yatrāśa̍ya̱tsamṛ̍tā̱ sedha̍ti sri̱dhaḥ |

a̱psā yā̍ti sva̱dhayā̱ daivya̱ṃ jana̱ṃ saṃ su̍ṣṭu̱tī nasa̍te̱ saṃ goa̍grayā

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 9

di̱vyaḥ su̍pa̱rṇo'va̍ cakṣata̱ kṣāṃ soma̱: pari̱ kratu̍nā paśyate̱ jāḥ
