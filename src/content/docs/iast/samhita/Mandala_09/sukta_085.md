# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 85

**12 veno bhārgava: | pavamāna: soma: |jagatī , 11-12 triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 1

indrā̍ya soma̱ suṣu̍ta̱: pari̍ sra̱vā'pāmī̍vā bhavatu̱ rakṣa̍sā sa̱ha |
mā te̱ rasa̍sya matsata dvayā̱vino̱ dravi̍ṇasvanta i̱ha sa̱ntvinda̍vaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 2

a̱smāntsa̍ma̱rye pa̍vamāna codaya̱ dakṣo̍ de̱vānā̱masi̱ hi pri̱yo mada̍: |

ja̱hi śatrū̍m̐ra̱bhyā bha̍ndanāya̱taḥ pibe̍ndra̱ soma̱mava̍ no̱ mṛdho̍ jahi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 3

ada̍bdha indo pavase ma̱dinta̍ma ā̱tmendra̍sya bhavasi dhā̱siru̍tta̱maḥ |

a̱bhi sva̍ranti ba̱havo̍ manī̱ṣiṇo̱ rājā̍nama̱sya bhuva̍nasya niṃsate

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 4

sa̱hasra̍ṇīthaḥ śa̱tadhā̍ro̱ adbhu̍ta̱ indrā̱yendu̍: pavate̱ kāmya̱ṃ madhu̍ |

jaya̱nkṣetra̍ma̱bhya̍rṣā̱ jaya̍nna̱pa u̱ruṃ no̍ gā̱tuṃ kṛ̍ṇu soma mīḍhvaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 5

kani̍kradatka̱laśe̱ gobhi̍rajyase̱ vya1vyaya̍ṃ sa̱mayā̱ vāra̍marṣasi |

ma̱rmṛ̱jyamā̍no̱ atyo̱ na sā̍na̱sirindra̍sya soma ja̱ṭhare̱ sama̍kṣaraḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 6

svā̱duḥ pa̍vasva di̱vyāya̱ janma̍ne svā̱durindrā̍ya su̱havī̍tunāmne |

svā̱durmi̱trāya̱ varu̍ṇāya vā̱yave̱ bṛha̱spata̍ye̱ madhu̍mā̱m̐ adā̍bhyaḥ

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 7

atya̍ṃ mṛjanti ka̱laśe̱ daśa̱ kṣipa̱: pra viprā̍ṇāṃ ma̱tayo̱ vāca̍ īrate |

pava̍mānā a̱bhya̍rṣanti suṣṭu̱timendra̍ṃ viśanti madi̱rāsa̱ inda̍vaḥ

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 8

māki̍rno a̱sya pari̍ṣūtirīśa̱tendo̱ jaye̍ma̱ tvayā̱ dhana̍ṃdhanam

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 9

rājā̍ pa̱vitra̱matye̍ti̱ roru̍vaddi̱vaḥ pī̱yūṣa̍ṃ duhate nṛ̱cakṣa̍saḥ
