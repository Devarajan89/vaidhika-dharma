# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 58

**4 avatsāra: kāśyapaḥ | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 1

tara̱tsa ma̱ndī dhā̍vati̱ dhārā̍ su̱tasyāndha̍saḥ | tara̱tsa ma̱ndī dhā̍vati

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 2

u̱srā ve̍da̱ vasū̍nā̱ṃ marta̍sya de̱vyava̍saḥ | tara̱tsa ma̱ndī dhā̍vati

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 3

dhva̱srayo̍: puru̱ṣantyo̱rā sa̱hasrā̍ṇi dadmahe | tara̱tsa ma̱ndī dhā̍vati

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 4

ā yayo̍stri̱ṃśata̱ṃ tanā̍ sa̱hasrā̍ṇi ca̱ dadma̍he | tara̱tsa ma̱ndī dhā̍vati
