# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 32

**6 śyāvāśva ātreya: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 1

pra somā̍so mada̱cyuta̱: śrava̍se no ma̱ghona̍: | su̱tā vi̱dathe̍ akramuḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 2

ādī̍ṃ tri̱tasya̱ yoṣa̍ṇo̱ hari̍ṃ hinva̱ntyadri̍bhiḥ | indu̱mindrā̍ya pī̱taye̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 3

ādī̍ṃ ha̱ṃso yathā̍ ga̱ṇaṃ viśva̍syāvīvaśanma̱tim | atyo̱ na gobhi̍rajyate

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 4

u̱bhe so̍māva̱cāka̍śanmṛ̱go na ta̱kto a̍rṣasi | sīda̍nnṛ̱tasya̱ yoni̱mā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 5

a̱bhi gāvo̍ anūṣata̱ yoṣā̍ jā̱rami̍va pri̱yam | aga̍nnā̱jiṃ yathā̍ hi̱tam

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 6

a̱sme dhe̍hi dyu̱madyaśo̍ ma̱ghava̍dbhyaśca̱ mahya̍ṃ ca | sa̱niṃ me̱dhāmu̱ta śrava̍:
