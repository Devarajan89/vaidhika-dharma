# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 94

**5 kaṇvo ghaura: | pavamāna: soma: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 1

adhi̱ yada̍sminvā̱jinī̍va̱ śubha̱: spardha̍nte̱ dhiya̱: sūrye̱ na viśa̍: |
a̱po vṛ̍ṇā̱naḥ pa̍vate kavī̱yanvra̱jaṃ na pa̍śu̱vardha̍nāya̱ manma̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 2

dvi̱tā vyū̱rṇvanna̱mṛta̍sya̱ dhāma̍ sva̱rvide̱ bhuva̍nāni prathanta |

dhiya̍: pinvā̱nāḥ svasa̍re̱ na gāva̍ ṛtā̱yantī̍ra̱bhi vā̍vaśra̱ indu̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 3

pari̱ yatka̱viḥ kāvyā̱ bhara̍te̱ śūro̱ na ratho̱ bhuva̍nāni̱ viśvā̍ |

de̱veṣu̱ yaśo̱ martā̍ya̱ bhūṣa̱ndakṣā̍ya rā̱yaḥ pu̍ru̱bhūṣu̱ navya̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 4

śri̱ye jā̱taḥ śri̱ya ā niri̍yāya̱ śriya̱ṃ vayo̍ jari̱tṛbhyo̍ dadhāti |

śriya̱ṃ vasā̍nā amṛta̱tvamā̍ya̱nbhava̍nti sa̱tyā sa̍mi̱thā mi̱tadrau̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 5

iṣa̱mūrja̍ma̱bhya1rṣāśva̱ṃ gāmu̱ru jyoti̍: kṛṇuhi̱ matsi̍ de̱vān |

viśvā̍ni̱ hi su̱ṣahā̱ tāni̱ tubhya̱ṃ pava̍māna̱ bādha̍se soma̱ śatrū̍n
