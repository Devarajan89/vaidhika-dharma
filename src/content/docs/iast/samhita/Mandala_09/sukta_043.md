# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 43

**6 medhyātithi: kāṇva: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 1

yo atya̍ iva mṛ̱jyate̱ gobhi̱rmadā̍ya harya̱taḥ | taṃ gī̱rbhirvā̍sayāmasi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 2

taṃ no̱ viśvā̍ ava̱syuvo̱ gira̍: śumbhanti pū̱rvathā̍ | indu̱mindrā̍ya pī̱taye̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 3

pu̱nā̱no yā̍ti harya̱taḥ somo̍ gī̱rbhiḥ pari̍ṣkṛtaḥ | vipra̍sya̱ medhyā̍titheḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 4

pava̍māna vi̱dā ra̱yima̱smabhya̍ṃ soma su̱śriya̍m | indo̍ sa̱hasra̍varcasam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 5

indu̱ratyo̱ na vā̍ja̱sṛtkani̍kranti pa̱vitra̱ ā | yadakṣā̱rati̍ deva̱yuḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 6

pava̍sva̱ vāja̍sātaye̱ vipra̍sya gṛṇa̱to vṛ̱dhe | soma̱ rāsva̍ su̱vīrya̍m
