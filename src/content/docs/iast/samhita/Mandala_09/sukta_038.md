# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 38

**6 rahūgaṇa āṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 1

e̱ṣa u̱ sya vṛṣā̱ ratho'vyo̱ vāre̍bhirarṣati | gaccha̱nvāja̍ṃ saha̱sriṇa̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 2

e̱taṃ tri̱tasya̱ yoṣa̍ṇo̱ hari̍ṃ hinva̱ntyadri̍bhiḥ | indu̱mindrā̍ya pī̱taye̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 3

e̱taṃ tyaṃ ha̱rito̱ daśa̍ marmṛ̱jyante̍ apa̱syuva̍: | yābhi̱rmadā̍ya̱ śumbha̍te

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 4

e̱ṣa sya mānu̍ṣī̱ṣvā śye̱no na vi̱kṣu sī̍dati | gaccha̍ñjā̱ro na yo̱ṣita̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 5

e̱ṣa sya madyo̱ raso'va̍ caṣṭe di̱vaḥ śiśu̍: | ya indu̱rvāra̱māvi̍śat

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 6

e̱ṣa sya pī̱taye̍ su̱to hari̍rarṣati dharṇa̱siḥ | kranda̱nyoni̍ma̱bhi pri̱yam
