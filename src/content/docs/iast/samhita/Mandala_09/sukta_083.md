# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 83

**5 pavitra āṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: |jagatī  |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 1

pa̱vitra̍ṃ te̱ vita̍taṃ brahmaṇaspate pra̱bhurgātrā̍ṇi̱ parye̍ṣi vi̱śvata̍: |
ata̍ptatanū̱rna tadā̱mo a̍śnute śṛ̱tāsa̱ idvaha̍nta̱statsamā̍śata

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 2

tapo̍ṣpa̱vitra̱ṃ vita̍taṃ di̱vaspa̱de śoca̍nto asya̱ tanta̍vo̱ vya̍sthiran |

ava̍ntyasya pavī̱tāra̍mā̱śavo̍ di̱vaspṛ̱ṣṭhamadhi̍ tiṣṭhanti̱ ceta̍sā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 3

arū̍rucadu̱ṣasa̱: pṛśni̍ragri̱ya u̱kṣā bi̍bharti̱ bhuva̍nāni vāja̱yuḥ |

mā̱yā̱vino̍ mamire asya mā̱yayā̍ nṛ̱cakṣa̍saḥ pi̱taro̱ garbha̱mā da̍dhuḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 4

ga̱ndha̱rva i̱tthā pa̱dama̍sya rakṣati̱ pāti̍ de̱vānā̱ṃ jani̍mā̱nyadbhu̍taḥ |

gṛ̱bhṇāti̍ ri̱puṃ ni̱dhayā̍ ni̱dhāpa̍tiḥ su̱kṛtta̍mā̱ madhu̍no bha̱kṣamā̍śata

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 5

ha̱virha̍viṣmo̱ mahi̱ sadma̱ daivya̱ṃ nabho̱ vasā̍na̱: pari̍ yāsyadhva̱ram |

rājā̍ pa̱vitra̍ratho̱ vāja̱māru̍haḥ sa̱hasra̍bhṛṣṭirjayasi̱ śravo̍ bṛ̱hat
