# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 105

**6  parvatanāradau kāṇvau |  pavamāna: soma: | uṣṇik |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 105 | **Verse:** 1

taṃ va̍: sakhāyo̱ madā̍ya punā̱nama̱bhi gā̍yata | śiśu̱ṃ na ya̱jñaiḥ sva̍dayanta gū̱rtibhi̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 105 | **Verse:** 2

saṃ va̱tsa i̍va mā̱tṛbhi̱rindu̍rhinvā̱no a̍jyate | de̱vā̱vīrmado̍ ma̱tibhi̱: pari̍ṣkṛtaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 105 | **Verse:** 3

a̱yaṃ dakṣā̍ya̱ sādha̍no̱'yaṃ śardhā̍ya vī̱taye̍ | a̱yaṃ de̱vebhyo̱ madhu̍mattamaḥ su̱taḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 105 | **Verse:** 4

goma̍nna indo̱ aśva̍vatsu̱taḥ su̍dakṣa dhanva | śuci̍ṃ te̱ varṇa̱madhi̱ goṣu̍ dīdharam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 105 | **Verse:** 5

sa no̍ harīṇāṃ pata̱ indo̍ de̱vapsa̍rastamaḥ | sakhe̍va̱ sakhye̱ naryo̍ ru̱ce bha̍va

---

## Verse 6
