# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 104

**6 parvatanāradau kāṇvau, kāśyapyau śikhaṇḍinyāvapsarasau vā | pavamāna: soma: | uṣṇik |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 1

sakhā̍ya̱ ā ni ṣī̍data punā̱nāya̱ pra gā̍yata | śiśu̱ṃ na ya̱jñaiḥ pari̍ bhūṣata śri̱ye

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 2

samī̍ va̱tsaṃ na mā̱tṛbhi̍: sṛ̱jatā̍ gaya̱sādha̍nam | de̱vā̱vyaṃ1 mada̍ma̱bhi dviśa̍vasam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 3

pu̱nātā̍ dakṣa̱sādha̍na̱ṃ yathā̱ śardhā̍ya vī̱taye̍ | yathā̍ mi̱trāya̱ varu̍ṇāya̱ śaṃta̍maḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 4

a̱smabhya̍ṃ tvā vasu̱vida̍ma̱bhi vāṇī̍ranūṣata | gobhi̍ṣṭe̱ varṇa̍ma̱bhi vā̍sayāmasi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 5

sa no̍ madānāṃ pata̱ indo̍ de̱vapsa̍rā asi | sakhe̍va̱ sakhye̍ gātu̱vitta̍mo bhava

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 6

sane̍mi kṛ̱dhya1smadā ra̱kṣasa̱ṃ kaṃ ci̍da̱triṇa̍m | apāde̍vaṃ dva̱yumaṃho̍ yuyodhi naḥ
