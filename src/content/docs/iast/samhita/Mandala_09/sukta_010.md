# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 10

**9 kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 1

pra svā̱nāso̱ rathā̍ i̱vā'rva̍nto̱ na śra̍va̱syava̍: | somā̍so rā̱ye a̍kramuḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 2

hi̱nvā̱nāso̱ rathā̍ iva dadhanvi̱re gabha̍styoḥ | bharā̍saḥ kā̱riṇā̍miva

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 3

rājā̍no̱ na praśa̍stibhi̱: somā̍so̱ gobhi̍rañjate | ya̱jño na sa̱pta dhā̱tṛbhi̍:

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 4

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 5
