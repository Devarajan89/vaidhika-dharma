# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 106

**((14) 1-3, 10-14   agniścākṣuṣa:, 4-6 cakṣurmānava:, 7-9 manurāpsava: | pavamāna: soma: | uṣṇik |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 1

indra̱maccha̍ su̱tā i̱me vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ yantu̱ hara̍yaḥ | śru̱ṣṭī jā̱tāsa̱ inda̍vaḥ sva̱rvida̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 2

a̱yaṃ bharā̍ya sāna̱sirindrā̍ya pavate su̱taḥ | somo̱ jaitra̍sya cetati̱ yathā̍ vi̱de

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 3

a̱syedindro̱ made̱ṣvā grā̱bhaṃ gṛ̍bhṇīta sāna̱sim | vajra̍ṃ ca̱ vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ bhara̱tsama̍psu̱jit

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 4

pra dha̍nvā soma̱ jāgṛ̍vi̱rindrā̍yendo̱ pari̍ srava | dyu̱manta̱ṃ śuṣma̱mā bha̍rā sva̱rvida̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 5

indrā̍ya̱ vṛṣa̍ṇa̱ṃ mada̱ṃ pava̍sva vi̱śvada̍rśataḥ | sa̱hasra̍yāmā pathi̱kṛdvi̍cakṣa̱ṇaḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 6

a̱smabhya̍ṃ gātu̱vitta̍mo de̱vebhyo̱ madhu̍mattamaḥ | sa̱hasra̍ṃ yāhi pa̱thibhi̱: kani̍kradat

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 7

pava̍sva de̱vavī̍taya̱ indo̱ dhārā̍bhi̱roja̍sā | ā ka̱laśa̱ṃ madhu̍māntsoma naḥ sadaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 8

## Verse 9

## Verse 10
