# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 111

**3 anānata: pārucchepi: | pavamāna: soma: |atyaṣṭi: |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 111 | **Verse:** 1

a̱yā ru̱cā hari̍ṇyā punā̱no viśvā̱ dveṣā̍ṃsi tarati sva̱yugva̍bhi̱: sūro̱ na sva̱yugva̍bhiḥ |
dhārā̍ su̱tasya̍ rocate punā̱no a̍ru̱ṣo hari̍: | viśvā̱ yadrū̱pā pa̍ri̱yātyṛkva̍bhiḥ sa̱ptāsye̍bhi̱ṝkva̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 111 | **Verse:** 2

tvaṃ tyatpa̍ṇī̱nāṃ vi̍do̱ vasu̱ saṃ mā̱tṛbhi̍rmarjayasi̱ sva ā dama̍ ṛ̱tasya̍ dhī̱tibhi̱rdame̍ |

pa̱rā̱vato̱ na sāma̱ tadyatrā̱ raṇa̍nti dhī̱taya̍: | tri̱dhātu̍bhi̱raru̍ṣībhi̱rvayo̍ dadhe̱ roca̍māno̱ vayo̍ dadhe

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 111 | **Verse:** 3

pūrvā̱manu̍ pra̱diśa̍ṃ yāti̱ ceki̍ta̱tsaṃ ra̱śmibhi̍ryatate darśa̱to ratho̱ daivyo̍ darśa̱to ratha̍: |

agma̍nnu̱kthāni̱ pauṃsyendra̱ṃ jaitrā̍ya harṣayan | vajra̍śca̱ yadbhava̍tho̱ ana̍pacyutā sa̱matsvana̍pacyutā
