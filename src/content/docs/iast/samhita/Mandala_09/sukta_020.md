# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 20

**7   kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 1

pra ka̱virde̱vavī̍ta̱ye'vyo̱ vāre̍bhirarṣati | sā̱hvānviśvā̍ a̱bhi spṛdha̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 2

sa hi ṣmā̍ jari̱tṛbhya̱ ā vāja̱ṃ goma̍nta̱minva̍ti | pava̍mānaḥ saha̱sriṇa̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 3

pari̱ viśvā̍ni̱ ceta̍sā mṛ̱śase̱ pava̍se ma̱tī | sa na̍: soma̱ śravo̍ vidaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 4

a̱bhya̍rṣa bṛ̱hadyaśo̍ ma̱ghava̍dbhyo dhru̱vaṃ ra̱yim | iṣa̍ṃ sto̱tṛbhya̱ ā bha̍ra

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 5

tvaṃ rāje̍va suvra̱to gira̍: so̱mā vi̍veśitha | pu̱nā̱no va̍hne adbhuta

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 6

sa vahni̍ra̱psu du̱ṣṭaro̍ mṛ̱jyamā̍no̱ gabha̍styoḥ | soma̍śca̱mūṣu̍ sīdati

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 7

krī̱l̤urma̱kho na ma̍ṃha̱yuḥ pa̱vitra̍ṃ soma gacchasi | dadha̍tsto̱tre su̱vīrya̍m
