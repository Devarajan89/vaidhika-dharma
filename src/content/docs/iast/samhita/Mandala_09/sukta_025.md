# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 25

**6 dṛl̤ahacyuta āgastya: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 1

pava̍sva dakṣa̱sādha̍no de̱vebhya̍: pī̱taye̍ hare | ma̱rudbhyo̍ vā̱yave̱ mada̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 2

pava̍māna dhi̱yā hi̱to̱3'bhi yoni̱ṃ kani̍kradat | dharma̍ṇā vā̱yumā vi̍śa

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 3

saṃ de̱vaiḥ śo̍bhate̱ vṛṣā̍ ka̱viryonā̱vadhi̍ pri̱yaḥ | vṛ̱tra̱hā de̍va̱vīta̍maḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 4

viśvā̍ rū̱pāṇyā̍vi̱śanpu̍nā̱no yā̍ti harya̱taḥ | yatrā̱mṛtā̍sa̱ āsa̍te

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 5

a̱ru̱ṣo ja̱naya̱ngira̱: soma̍: pavata āyu̱ṣak | indra̱ṃ gaccha̍nka̱vikra̍tuḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 6
