# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 40

**6 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| somāpūṣaṇau, 6(antyārdharcasya) aditiḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 1

somā̍pūṣaṇā̱ jana̍nā rayī̱ṇāṃ jana̍nā di̱vo jana̍nā pṛthi̱vyāḥ |
jā̱tau viśva̍sya̱ bhuva̍nasya go̱pau de̱vā a̍kṛṇvanna̱mṛta̍sya̱ nābhi̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 2

i̱mau de̱vau jāya̍mānau juṣante̱mau tamā̍ṃsi gūhatā̱maju̍ṣṭā |

ā̱bhyāmindra̍: pa̱kvamā̱māsva̱ntaḥ so̍māpū̱ṣabhyā̍ṃ janadu̱sriyā̍su

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 3

somā̍pūṣaṇā̱ raja̍so vi̱māna̍ṃ sa̱ptaca̍kra̱ṃ ratha̱mavi̍śvaminvam |

vi̱ṣū̱vṛta̱ṃ mana̍sā yu̱jyamā̍na̱ṃ taṃ ji̍nvatho vṛṣaṇā̱ pañca̍raśmim

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 4

di̱vya1nyaḥ sada̍naṃ ca̱kra u̱ccā pṛ̍thi̱vyāma̱nyo adhya̱ntari̍kṣe |

tāva̱smabhya̍ṃ puru̱vāra̍ṃ puru̱kṣuṃ rā̱yaspoṣa̱ṃ vi ṣya̍tā̱ṃ nābhi̍ma̱sme

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 5

viśvā̍nya̱nyo bhuva̍nā ja̱jāna̱ viśva̍ma̱nyo a̍bhi̱cakṣā̍ṇa eti |

somā̍pūṣaṇā̱vava̍ta̱ṃ dhiya̍ṃ me yu̱vābhyā̱ṃ viśvā̱: pṛta̍nā jayema

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 6

dhiya̍ṃ pū̱ṣā ji̍nvatu viśvami̱nvo ra̱yiṃ somo̍ rayi̱pati̍rdadhātu |

ava̍tu de̱vyadi̍tirana̱rvā bṛ̱had va̍dema vi̱dathe̍ su̱vīrā̍:
