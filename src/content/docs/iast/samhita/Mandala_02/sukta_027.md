# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 27

**17 kūrmo gārtsamado, gṛtsamado vā| ādityāḥ | triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 1

i̱mā gira̍ ādi̱tyebhyo̍ ghṛ̱tasnū̍: sa̱nād rāja̍bhyo ju̱hvā̍ juhomi |
śṛ̱ṇotu̍ mi̱tro a̍rya̱mā bhago̍ nastuvijā̱to varu̍ṇo̱ dakṣo̱ aṃśa̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 2

i̱maṃ stoma̱ṃ sakra̍tavo me a̱dya mi̱tro a̍rya̱mā varu̍ṇo juṣanta |

ā̱di̱tyāsa̱: śuca̍yo̱ dhāra̍pūtā̱ avṛ̍jinā anava̱dyā ari̍ṣṭāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 3

ta ā̍di̱tyāsa̍ u̱ravo̍ gabhī̱rā ada̍bdhāso̱ dipsa̍nto bhūrya̱kṣāḥ |

a̱ntaḥ pa̍śyanti vṛji̱nota sā̱dhu sarva̱ṃ rāja̍bhyaḥ para̱mā ci̱danti̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 4

dhā̱raya̍nta ādi̱tyāso̱ jaga̱t sthā de̱vā viśva̍sya̱ bhuva̍nasya go̱pāḥ |

dī̱rghādhi̍yo̱ rakṣa̍māṇā asu̱rya̍mṛ̱tāvā̍na̱ścaya̍mānā ṛ̱ṇāni̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 5

vi̱dyāmā̍dityā̱ ava̍so vo a̱sya yada̍ryaman bha̱ya ā ci̍nmayo̱bhu |

yu̱ṣmāka̍ṃ mitrāvaruṇā̱ praṇī̍tau̱ pari̱ śvabhre̍va duri̱tāni̍ vṛjyām

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 6

su̱go hi vo̍ aryamanmitra̱ panthā̍ anṛkṣa̱ro va̍ruṇa sā̱dhurasti̍ |

tenā̍dityā̱ adhi̍ vocatā no̱ yaccha̍tā no duṣpari̱hantu̱ śarma̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 7

pipa̍rtu no̱ adi̍tī̱ rāja̍pu̱trā 'ti̱ dveṣā̍ṃsyarya̱mā su̱gebhi̍: |

bṛ̱hanmi̱trasya̱ varu̍ṇasya̱ śarmopa̍ syāma puru̱vīrā̱ ari̍ṣṭāḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 8

ti̱sro bhūmī̍rdhāraya̱n trīm̐ru̱ta dyūn trīṇi̍ vra̱tā vi̱dathe̍ a̱ntare̍ṣām |

ṛ̱tenā̍dityā̱ mahi̍ vo mahi̱tvaṃ tada̍ryaman varuṇa mitra̱ cāru̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 9

trī ro̍ca̱nā di̱vyā dhā̍rayanta hira̱ṇyayā̱: śuca̍yo̱ dhāra̍pūtāḥ |

asva̍pnajo animi̱ṣā ada̍bdhā uru̱śaṃsā̍ ṛ̱jave̱ martyā̍ya

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 10

tvaṃ viśve̍ṣāṃ varuṇāsi̱ rājā̱ ye ca̍ de̱vā a̍sura̱ ye ca̱ martā̍: |

śa̱taṃ no̍ rāsva śa̱rado̍ vi̱cakṣe̱ 'śyāmāyū̍ṃṣi̱ sudhi̍tāni̱ pūrvā̍

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 11

na da̍kṣi̱ṇā vi ci̍kite̱ na sa̱vyā na prā̱cīna̍mādityā̱ nota pa̱ścā |

pā̱kyā̍ cid vasavo dhī̱ryā̍ cid yu̱ṣmānī̍to̱ abha̍ya̱ṃ jyoti̍raśyām

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 12

yo rāja̍bhya ṛta̱nibhyo̍ da̱dāśa̱ yaṃ va̱rdhaya̍nti pu̱ṣṭaya̍śca̱ nityā̍: |

sa re̱vān yā̍ti pratha̱mo rathe̍na vasu̱dāvā̍ vi̱dathe̍ṣu praśa̱staḥ

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 13

śuci̍ra̱paḥ sū̱yava̍sā̱ ada̍bdha̱ upa̍ kṣeti vṛ̱ddhava̍yāḥ su̱vīra̍: |

naki̱ṣṭaṃ ghna̱ntyanti̍to̱ na dū̱rād ya ā̍di̱tyānā̱ṃ bhava̍ti̱ praṇī̍tau

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 14

u̱rva̍śyā̱mabha̍ya̱ṃ jyoti̍rindra̱ mā no̍ dī̱rghā a̱bhi na̍śa̱ntami̍srāḥ

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 15

u̱bhā kṣayā̍vā̱jaya̍n yāti pṛ̱tsūbhāvardhau̍ bhavataḥ sā̱dhū a̍smai

---

## Verse 16

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 16

a̱śvīva̱ tām̐ ati̍ yeṣa̱ṃ rathe̱nāri̍ṣṭā u̱rāvā śarma̍ntsyāma
