# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 2

**13 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| agniḥ|jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 1

ya̱jñena̍ vardhata jā̱tave̍dasama̱gniṃ ya̍jadhvaṃ ha̱viṣā̱ tanā̍ gi̱rā |
sa̱mi̱dhā̱naṃ su̍pra̱yasa̱ṃ sva̍rṇaraṃ dyu̱kṣaṃ hotā̍raṃ vṛ̱jane̍ṣu dhū̱rṣada̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 2

a̱bhi tvā̱ naktī̍ru̱ṣaso̍ vavāśi̱re 'gne̍ va̱tsaṃ na svasa̍reṣu dhe̱nava̍: |

di̱va i̱veda̍ra̱tirmānu̍ṣā yu̱gā kṣapo̍ bhāsi puruvāra sa̱ṃyata̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 3

taṃ de̱vā bu̱dhne raja̍saḥ su̱daṃsa̍saṃ di̱vaspṛ̍thi̱vyora̍ra̱tiṃ nye̍rire |

ratha̍miva̱ vedya̍ṃ śu̱kraśo̍ciṣama̱gniṃ mi̱traṃ na kṣi̱tiṣu̍ pra̱śaṃsya̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 4

tamu̱kṣamā̍ṇa̱ṃ raja̍si̱ sva ā dame̍ ca̱ndrami̍va su̱ruca̍ṃ hvā̱ra ā da̍dhuḥ |

pṛśnyā̍: pata̱raṃ ci̱taya̍ntama̱kṣabhi̍: pā̱tho na pā̱yuṃ jana̍sī u̱bhe anu̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 5

sa hotā̱ viśva̱ṃ pari̍ bhūtvadhva̱raṃ tamu̍ ha̱vyairmanu̍ṣa ṛñjate gi̱rā |

hi̱ri̱śi̱pro vṛ̍dhasā̱nāsu̱ jarbhu̍ra̱d dyaurna stṛbhi̍ścitaya̱d roda̍sī̱ anu̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 6

sa no̍ re̱vat sa̍midhā̱naḥ sva̱staye̍ saṃdada̱svān ra̱yima̱smāsu̍ dīdihi |

ā na̍: kṛṇuṣva suvi̱tāya̱ roda̍sī̱ agne̍ ha̱vyā manu̍ṣo deva vī̱taye̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 7

dā no̍ agne bṛha̱to dāḥ sa̍ha̱sriṇo̍ du̱ro na vāja̱ṃ śrutyā̱ apā̍ vṛdhi |

prācī̱ dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī brahma̍ṇā kṛdhi̱ sva1rṇa śu̱kramu̱ṣaso̱ vi di̍dyutaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 8

sa i̍dhā̱na u̱ṣaso̱ rāmyā̱ anu̱ sva1rṇa dī̍dedaru̱ṣeṇa̍ bhā̱nunā̍ |

hotrā̍bhira̱gnirmanu̍ṣaḥ svadhva̱ro rājā̍ vi̱śāmati̍thi̱ścāru̍rā̱yave̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 9

e̱vā no̍ agne a̱mṛte̍ṣu pūrvya̱ dhīṣpī̍pāya bṛ̱had di̍veṣu̱ mānu̍ṣā |

duhā̍nā dhe̱nurvṛ̱jane̍ṣu kā̱rave̱ tmanā̍ śa̱tina̍ṃ puru̱rūpa̍mi̱ṣaṇi̍

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 10

va̱yama̍gne̱ arva̍tā vā su̱vīrya̱ṃ brahma̍ṇā vā citayemā̱ janā̱m̐ ati̍ |

a̱smāka̍ṃ dyu̱mnamadhi̱ pañca̍ kṛ̱ṣṭiṣū̱ccā sva1rṇa śu̍śucīta du̱ṣṭara̍m

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 11

sa no̍ bodhi sahasya pra̱śaṃsyo̱ yasmi̍n tsujā̱tā i̱ṣaya̍nta sū̱raya̍: |

yama̍gne ya̱jñamu̍pa̱yanti̍ vā̱jino̱ nitye̍ to̱ke dī̍di̱vāṃsa̱ṃ sve dame̍

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 12

u̱bhayā̍so jātavedaḥ syāma te sto̱tāro̍ agne sū̱raya̍śca̱ śarma̍ṇi |

vasvo̍ rā̱yaḥ pu̍ruśca̱ndrasya̱ bhūya̍saḥ pra̱jāva̍taḥ svapa̱tyasya̍ śagdhi naḥ

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 13

ye sto̱tṛbhyo̱ goa̍grā̱maśva̍peśasa̱magne̍ rā̱timu̍pasṛ̱janti̍ sū̱raya̍: |

a̱smāñca̱ tāṃśca̱ pra hi neṣi̱ vasya̱ ā bṛ̱had va̍dema vi̱dathe̍ su̱vīrā̍:
