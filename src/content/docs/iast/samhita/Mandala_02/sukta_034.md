# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 34

**15 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| marut| jagatī, 15 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 1

dhā̱rā̱va̱rā ma̱ruto̍ dhṛ̱ṣṇvo̍jaso mṛ̱gā na bhī̱māstavi̍ṣībhira̱rcina̍: |
a̱gnayo̱ na śu̍śucā̱nā ṛ̍jī̱ṣiṇo̱ bhṛmi̱ṃ dhama̍nto̱ apa̱ gā a̍vṛṇvata

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 2

dyāvo̱ na stṛbhi̍ścitayanta khā̱dino̱ vya1bhriyā̱ na dyu̍tayanta vṛ̱ṣṭaya̍: |

ru̱dro yad vo̍ maruto rukmavakṣaso̱ vṛṣāja̍ni̱ pṛśnyā̍: śu̱kra ūdha̍ni

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 3

u̱kṣante̱ aśvā̱m̐ atyā̍m̐ ivā̱jiṣu̍ na̱dasya̱ karṇai̍sturayanta ā̱śubhi̍: |

hira̍ṇyaśiprā maruto̱ davi̍dhvataḥ pṛ̱kṣaṃ yā̍tha̱ pṛṣa̍tībhiḥ samanyavaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 4

pṛ̱kṣe tā viśvā̱ bhuva̍nā vavakṣire mi̱trāya̍ vā̱ sada̱mā jī̱radā̍navaḥ |

pṛṣa̍daśvāso anava̱bhrarā̍dhasa ṛji̱pyāso̱ na va̱yune̍ṣu dhū̱rṣada̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 5

indha̍nvabhirdhe̱nubhī̍ ra̱pśadū̍dhabhiradhva̱smabhi̍: pa̱thibhi̍rbhrājadṛṣṭayaḥ |

ā ha̱ṃsāso̱ na svasa̍rāṇi gantana̱ madho̱rmadā̍ya marutaḥ samanyavaḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 6

ā no̱ brahmā̍ṇi marutaḥ samanyavo na̱rāṃ na śaṃsa̱: sava̍nāni gantana |

aśvā̍miva pipyata dhe̱numūdha̍ni̱ kartā̱ dhiya̍ṃ jari̱tre vāja̍peśasam

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 7

taṃ no̍ dāta maruto vā̱jina̱ṃ ratha̍ āpā̱naṃ brahma̍ ci̱taya̍d di̱vedi̍ve |

iṣa̍ṃ sto̱tṛbhyo̍ vṛ̱jane̍ṣu kā̱rave̍ sa̱niṃ me̱dhāmari̍ṣṭaṃ du̱ṣṭara̱ṃ saha̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 8

yad yu̱ñjate̍ ma̱ruto̍ ru̱kmava̍kṣa̱so 'śvā̱n rathe̍ṣu̱ bhaga̱ ā su̱dāna̍vaḥ |

dhe̱nurna śiśve̱ svasa̍reṣu pinvate̱ janā̍ya rā̱taha̍viṣe ma̱hīmiṣa̍m

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 9

yo no̍ maruto vṛ̱katā̍ti̱ martyo̍ ri̱purda̱dhe va̍savo̱ rakṣa̍tā ri̱ṣaḥ |

va̱rtaya̍ta̱ tapu̍ṣā ca̱kriyā̱bhi tamava̍ rudrā a̱śaso̍ hantanā̱ vadha̍:

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 10

ci̱traṃ tad vo̍ maruto̱ yāma̍ cekite̱ pṛśnyā̱ yadūdha̱rapyā̱payo̍ du̱huḥ |

yad vā̍ ni̱de nava̍mānasya rudriyāstri̱taṃ jarā̍ya jura̱tāma̍dābhyāḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 11

tān vo̍ ma̱ho ma̱ruta̍ eva̱yāvno̱ viṣṇo̍re̱ṣasya̍ prabhṛ̱the ha̍vāmahe |

hira̍ṇyavarṇān kaku̱hān ya̱tasru̍co brahma̱ṇyanta̱: śaṃsya̱ṃ rādha̍ īmahe

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 12

te daśa̍gvāḥ pratha̱mā ya̱jñamū̍hire̱ te no̍ hinvantū̱ṣaso̱ vyu̍ṣṭiṣu |

u̱ṣā na rā̱mīra̍ru̱ṇairapo̍rṇute ma̱ho jyoti̍ṣā śuca̱tā goa̍rṇasā

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 13

te kṣo̱ṇībhi̍raru̱ṇebhi̱rnāñjibhī̍ ru̱drā ṛ̱tasya̱ sada̍neṣu vāvṛdhuḥ |

ni̱megha̍mānā̱ atye̍na̱ pāja̍sā suśca̱ndraṃ varṇa̍ṃ dadhire su̱peśa̍sam

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 14

tām̐ i̍yā̱no mahi̱ varū̍thamū̱taya̱ upa̱ ghede̱nā nama̍sā gṛṇīmasi |

tri̱to na yān pañca̱ hotṝ̍na̱bhiṣṭa̍ya āva̱varta̱dava̍rāñca̱kriyāva̍se

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 15

yayā̍ ra̱dhraṃ pā̱raya̱thātyaṃho̱ yayā̍ ni̱do mu̱ñcatha̍ vandi̱tāra̍m |

a̱rvācī̱ sā ma̍ruto̱ yā va̍ ū̱tiro ṣu vā̱śreva̍ suma̱tirji̍gātu
