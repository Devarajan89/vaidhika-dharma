# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 38

**11 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| savitā|triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 1

udu̱ ṣya de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā sa̱vāya̍ śaśvatta̱maṃ tada̍pā̱ vahni̍rasthāt |
nū̱naṃ de̱vebhyo̱ vi hi dhāti̱ ratna̱mathābha̍jad vī̱tiho̍traṃ sva̱stau

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 2

viśva̍sya̱ hi śru̱ṣṭaye̍ de̱va ū̱rdhvaḥ pra bā̱havā̍ pṛ̱thupā̍ṇi̱: sisa̍rti |

āpa̍ścidasya vra̱ta ā nimṛ̍grā a̱yaṃ ci̱d vāto̍ ramate̱ pari̍jman

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 3

ā̱śubhi̍ści̱dyān vi mu̍cāti nū̱namarī̍rama̱data̍mānaṃ ci̱deto̍: |

a̱hyarṣū̍ṇāṃ ci̱nnya̍yām̐ avi̱ṣyāmanu̍ vra̱taṃ sa̍vi̱turmokyāgā̍t

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 4

puna̱: sama̍vya̱d vita̍ta̱ṃ vaya̍ntī ma̱dhyā karto̱rnya̍dhā̱cchakma̱ dhīra̍: |

ut saṃhāyā̍sthā̱d vyṛ1tūm̐ra̍dardhara̱rama̍tiḥ savi̱tā de̱va āgā̍t

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 5

nānaukā̍ṃsi̱ duryo̱ viśva̱māyu̱rvi ti̍ṣṭhate prabha̱vaḥ śoko̍ a̱gneḥ |

jyeṣṭha̍ṃ mā̱tā sū̱nave̍ bhā̱gamādhā̱danva̍sya̱ keta̍miṣi̱taṃ sa̍vi̱trā

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 6

sa̱māva̍varti̱ viṣṭhi̍to jigī̱ṣurviśve̍ṣā̱ṃ kāma̱ścara̍tāma̱mābhū̍t |

śaśvā̱m̐ apo̱ vikṛ̍taṃ hi̱tvyāgā̱danu̍ vra̱taṃ sa̍vi̱turdaivya̍sya

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 7

tvayā̍ hi̱tamapya̍ma̱psu bhā̱gaṃ dhanvānvā mṛ̍ga̱yaso̱ vi ta̍sthuḥ |

vanā̍ni̱ vibhyo̱ naki̍rasya̱ tāni̍ vra̱tā de̱vasya̍ savi̱turmi̍nanti

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 8

yā̱drā̱dhyaṃ1 varu̍ṇo̱ yoni̱mapya̱mani̍śitaṃ ni̱miṣi̱ jarbhu̍rāṇaḥ |

viśvo̍ mārtā̱ṇḍo vra̱jamā pa̱śurgā̍tstha̱ stha̱śo janmā̍ni savi̱tā vyāka̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 9

na yasyendro̱ varu̍ṇo̱ na mi̱tro vra̱tama̍rya̱mā na mi̱nanti̍ ru̱draḥ |

nārā̍taya̱stami̱daṃ sva̱sti hu̱ve de̱vaṃ sa̍vi̱tāra̱ṃ namo̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 10

bhaga̱ṃ dhiya̍ṃ vā̱jaya̍nta̱: pura̍ṃdhi̱ṃ narā̱śaṃso̱ gnāspati̍rno avyāḥ |

ā̱ye vā̱masya̍ saṃga̱the ra̍yī̱ṇāṃ pri̱yā de̱vasya̍ savi̱tuḥ syā̍ma

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 11

a̱smabhya̱ṃ tad di̱vo a̱dbhyaḥ pṛ̍thi̱vyāstvayā̍ da̱ttaṃ kāmya̱ṃ rādha̱ ā gā̍t |

śaṃ yat sto̱tṛbhya̍ ā̱paye̱ bhavā̍tyuru̱śaṃsā̍ya savitarjari̱tre
