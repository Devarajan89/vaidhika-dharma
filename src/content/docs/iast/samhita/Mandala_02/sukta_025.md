# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 25

**5 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| brahmaṇaspatiḥ| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 1

indhā̍no a̱gniṃ va̍navad vanuṣya̱taḥ kṛ̱tabra̍hmā śūśuvad rā̱taha̍vya̱ it |
jā̱tena̍ jā̱tamati̱ sa pra sa̍rsṛte̱ yaṃya̱ṃ yuja̍ṃ kṛṇu̱te brahma̍ṇa̱spati̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 2

vī̱rebhi̍rvī̱rān va̍navad vanuṣya̱to gobhī̍ ra̱yiṃ pa̍pratha̱d bodha̍ti̱ tmanā̍ |

to̱kaṃ ca̱ tasya̱ tana̍yaṃ ca vardhate̱ yaṃya̱ṃ yuja̍ṃ kṛṇu̱te brahma̍ṇa̱spati̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 3

sindhu̱rna kṣoda̱: śimī̍vām̐ ṛghāya̱to vṛṣe̍va̱ vadhrī̍m̐ra̱bhi va̱ṣṭyoja̍sā |

a̱gneri̍va̱ prasi̍ti̱rnāha̱ varta̍ve̱ yaṃya̱ṃ yuja̍ṃ kṛṇu̱te brahma̍ṇa̱spati̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 4

tasmā̍ arṣanti di̱vyā a̍sa̱ścata̱: sa satva̍bhiḥ pratha̱mo goṣu̍ gacchati |

ani̍bhṛṣṭataviṣirha̱ntyoja̍sā̱ yaṃya̱ṃ yuja̍ṃ kṛṇu̱te brahma̍ṇa̱spati̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 5

tasmā̱ id viśve̍ dhunayanta̱ sindha̱vo 'cchi̍drā̱ śarma̍ dadhire pu̱rūṇi̍ |

de̱vānā̍ṃ su̱mne su̱bhaga̱: sa e̍dhate̱ yaṃya̱ṃ yuja̍ṃ kṛṇu̱te brahma̍ṇa̱spati̍:
