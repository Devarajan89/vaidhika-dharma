# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 30

**5  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| indra: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 1

ā no̍ deva̱ śava̍sā yāhi śuṣmi̱nbhavā̍ vṛ̱dha i̍ndra rā̱yo a̱sya |
ma̱he nṛ̱mṇāya̍ nṛpate suvajra̱ mahi̍ kṣa̱trāya̱ pauṃsyā̍ya śūra

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 2

hava̍nta u tvā̱ havya̱ṃ vivā̍ci ta̱nūṣu̱ śūrā̱: sūrya̍sya sā̱tau |

tvaṃ viśve̍ṣu̱ senyo̱ jane̍ṣu̱ tvaṃ vṛ̱trāṇi̍ randhayā su̱hantu̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 3

ahā̱ yadi̍ndra su̱dinā̍ vyu̱cchāndadho̱ yatke̱tumu̍pa̱maṃ sa̱matsu̍ |

nya1gniḥ sī̍da̱dasu̍ro̱ na hotā̍ huvā̱no atra̍ su̱bhagā̍ya de̱vān

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 4

va̱yaṃ te ta̍ indra̱ ye ca̍ deva̱ stava̍nta śūra̱ dada̍to ma̱ghāni̍ |

yacchā̍ sū̱ribhya̍ upa̱maṃ varū̍thaṃ svā̱bhuvo̍ jara̱ṇāma̍śnavanta

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 5

vo̱cemedindra̍ṃ ma̱ghavā̍namenaṃ ma̱ho rā̱yo rādha̍so̱ yaddada̍nnaḥ |

yo arca̍to̱ brahma̍kṛti̱mavi̍ṣṭho yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
