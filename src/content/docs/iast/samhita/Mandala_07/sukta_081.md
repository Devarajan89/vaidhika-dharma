# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 81

**6  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| uṣasa: |pragātha:=(viṣamā bṛhatī, samā satobṛhatī )|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 1

pratyu̍ adarśyāya̱tyu1cchantī̍ duhi̱tā di̱vaḥ |
apo̱ mahi̍ vyayati̱ cakṣa̍se̱ tamo̱ jyoti̍ṣkṛṇoti sū̱narī̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 2

udu̱sriyā̍: sṛjate̱ sūrya̱: sacā̍m̐ u̱dyannakṣa̍tramarci̱vat |

tavedu̍ṣo̱ vyuṣi̱ sūrya̍sya ca̱ saṃ bha̱ktena̍ gamemahi

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 3

prati̍ tvā duhitardiva̱ uṣo̍ jī̱rā a̍bhutsmahi |

yā vaha̍si pu̱ru spā̱rhaṃ va̍nanvati̱ ratna̱ṃ na dā̱śuṣe̱ maya̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 4

tasyā̍ste ratna̱bhāja̍ īmahe va̱yaṃ syāma̍ mā̱turna sū̱nava̍:

## Verse 5
