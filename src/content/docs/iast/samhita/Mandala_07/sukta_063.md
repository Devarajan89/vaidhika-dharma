# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 63

**6 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| 1-4 sūrya:, 5 sūrya – mitrāvaruṇā:, 6 mitrāvaruṇau aryamā ca | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 63 | **Verse:** 1

udve̍ti su̱bhago̍ vi̱śvaca̍kṣā̱: sādhā̍raṇa̱: sūryo̱ mānu̍ṣāṇām |
cakṣu̍rmi̱trasya̱ varu̍ṇasya de̱vaścarme̍va̱ yaḥ sa̱mavi̍vya̱ktamā̍ṃsi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 63 | **Verse:** 2

udve̍ti prasavī̱tā janā̍nāṃ ma̱hānke̱tura̍rṇa̱vaḥ sūrya̍sya |

sa̱mā̱naṃ ca̱kraṃ pa̍ryā̱vivṛ̍tsa̱nyade̍ta̱śo vaha̍ti dhū̱rṣu yu̱ktaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 63 | **Verse:** 3

vi̱bhrāja̍māna u̱ṣasā̍mu̱pasthā̍dre̱bhairude̍tyanuma̱dyamā̍naḥ |

e̱ṣa me̍ de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā ca̍cchanda̱ yaḥ sa̍mā̱naṃ na pra̍mi̱nāti̱ dhāma̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 63 | **Verse:** 4

di̱vo ru̱kma u̍ru̱cakṣā̱ ude̍ti dū̱rea̍rthasta̱raṇi̱rbhrāja̍mānaḥ |

nū̱naṃ janā̱: sūrye̍ṇa̱ prasū̍tā̱ aya̱nnarthā̍ni kṛ̱ṇava̱nnapā̍ṃsi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 63 | **Verse:** 5

yatrā̍ ca̱krura̱mṛtā̍ gā̱tuma̍smai śye̱no na dīya̱nnanve̍ti̱ pātha̍: |

prati̍ vā̱ṃ sūra̱ udi̍te vidhema̱ namo̍bhirmitrāvaruṇo̱ta ha̱vyaiḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 63 | **Verse:** 6

nū mi̱tro varu̍ṇo arya̱mā na̱stmane̍ to̱kāya̱ vari̍vo dadhantu |

su̱gā no̱ viśvā̍ su̱pathā̍ni santu yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
