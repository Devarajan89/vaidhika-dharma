# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 54

**3 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| vāstoṣpati:| triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 1

vāsto̍ṣpate̱ prati̍ jānīhya̱smāntsvā̍ve̱śo a̍namī̱vo bha̍vā naḥ |
yattvema̍he̱ prati̱ tanno̍ juṣasva̱ śaṃ no̍ bhava dvi̱pade̱ śaṃ catu̍ṣpade

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 2

vāsto̍ṣpate pra̱tara̍ṇo na edhi gaya̱sphāno̱ gobhi̱raśve̍bhirindo |

a̱jarā̍saste sa̱khye syā̍ma pi̱teva̍ pu̱trānprati̍ no juṣasva

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 3

vāsto̍ṣpate śa̱gmayā̍ sa̱ṃsadā̍ te sakṣī̱mahi̍ ra̱ṇvayā̍ gātu̱matyā̍ |

pā̱hi kṣema̍ u̱ta yoge̱ vara̍ṃ no yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
