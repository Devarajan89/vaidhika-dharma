# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 3

**10 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| agni: |  triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 1

a̱gniṃ vo̍ de̱vama̱gnibhi̍: sa̱joṣā̱ yaji̍ṣṭhaṃ dū̱tama̍dhva̱re kṛ̍ṇudhvam |
yo martye̍ṣu̱ nidhru̍viṝ̱tāvā̱ tapu̍rmūrdhā ghṛ̱tānna̍: pāva̱kaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 2

protha̱daśvo̱ na yava̍se'vi̱ṣyanya̱dā ma̱haḥ sa̱ṃvara̍ṇā̱dvyasthā̍t |

āda̍sya̱ vāto̱ anu̍ vāti śo̱ciradha̍ sma te̱ vraja̍naṃ kṛ̱ṣṇama̍sti

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 3

udyasya̍ te̱ nava̍jātasya̱ vṛṣṇo'gne̱ cara̍ntya̱jarā̍ idhā̱nāḥ |

acchā̱ dyāma̍ru̱ṣo dhū̱ma e̍ti̱ saṃ dū̱to a̍gna̱ īya̍se̱ hi de̱vān

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 4

vi yasya̍ te pṛthi̱vyāṃ pājo̱ aśre̍ttṛ̱ṣu yadannā̍ sa̱mavṛ̍kta̱ jambhai̍: |

sene̍va sṛ̱ṣṭā prasi̍tiṣṭa eti̱ yava̱ṃ na da̍sma ju̱hvā̍ vivekṣi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 5

tamiddo̱ṣā tamu̱ṣasi̱ yavi̍ṣṭhama̱gnimatya̱ṃ na ma̍rjayanta̱ nara̍: |

ni̱śiśā̍nā̱ ati̍thimasya̱ yonau̍ dī̱dāya̍ śo̱cirāhu̍tasya̱ vṛṣṇa̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 6

su̱sa̱ṃdṛkte̍ svanīka̱ pratī̍ka̱ṃ vi yadru̱kmo na roca̍sa upā̱ke |

di̱vo na te̍ tanya̱ture̍ti̱ śuṣma̍ści̱tro na sūra̱: prati̍ cakṣi bhā̱num

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 7

yathā̍ va̱: svāhā̱gnaye̱ dāśe̍ma̱ parīl̤ā̍bhirghṛ̱tava̍dbhiśca ha̱vyaiḥ |

tebhi̍rno agne̱ ami̍tai̱rmaho̍bhiḥ śa̱taṃ pū̱rbhirāya̍sībhi̱rni pā̍hi

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 8

yā vā̍ te̱ santi̍ dā̱śuṣe̱ adhṛ̍ṣṭā̱ giro̍ vā̱ yābhi̍rnṛ̱vatī̍ruru̱ṣyāḥ |

tābhi̍rnaḥ sūno sahaso̱ ni pā̍hi̱ smatsū̱rīñja̍ri̱tṝñjā̍tavedaḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 9

niryatpū̱teva̱ svadhi̍ti̱: śuci̱rgātsvayā̍ kṛ̱pā ta̱nvā̱3 roca̍mānaḥ |

ā yo mā̱troru̱śenyo̱ jani̍ṣṭa deva̱yajyā̍ya su̱kratu̍: pāva̱kaḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 10

e̱tā no̍ agne̱ saubha̍gā didī̱hyapi̱ kratu̍ṃ su̱ceta̍saṃ vatema |

viśvā̍ sto̱tṛbhyo̍ gṛṇa̱te ca̍ santu yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
