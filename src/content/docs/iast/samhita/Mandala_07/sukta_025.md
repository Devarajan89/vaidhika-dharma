# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 25

**6 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| indra: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 1

ā te̍ ma̱ha i̍ndro̱tyu̍gra̱ sama̍nyavo̱ yatsa̱mara̍nta̱ senā̍: |
patā̍ti di̱dyunnarya̍sya bā̱hvormā te̱ mano̍ viṣva̱drya1gvi cā̍rīt

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 2

ni du̱rga i̍ndra śnathihya̱mitrā̍na̱bhi ye no̱ martā̍so a̱manti̍ |

ā̱re taṃ śaṃsa̍ṃ kṛṇuhi nini̱tsorā no̍ bhara sa̱ṃbhara̍ṇa̱ṃ vasū̍nām

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 3

śa̱taṃ te̍ śiprinnū̱taya̍: su̱dāse̍ sa̱hasra̱ṃ śaṃsā̍ u̱ta rā̱tira̍stu |

ja̱hi vadha̍rva̱nuṣo̱ martya̍syā̱'sme dyu̱mnamadhi̱ ratna̍ṃ ca dhehi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 4

tvāva̍to̱ hī̍ndra̱ kratve̱ asmi̱ tvāva̍to'vi̱tuḥ śū̍ra rā̱tau |

viśvedahā̍ni taviṣīva ugra̱m̐ oka̍: kṛṇuṣva harivo̱ na ma̍rdhīḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 5

kutsā̍ e̱te harya̍śvāya śū̱ṣamindre̱ saho̍ de̱vajū̍tamiyā̱nāḥ |

sa̱trā kṛ̍dhi su̱hanā̍ śūra vṛ̱trā va̱yaṃ taru̍trāḥ sanuyāma̱ vāja̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 6

e̱vā na̍ indra̱ vārya̍sya pūrdhi̱ pra te̍ ma̱hīṃ su̍ma̱tiṃ ve̍vidāma |

iṣa̍ṃ pinva ma̱ghava̍dbhyaḥ su̱vīrā̍ṃ yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
