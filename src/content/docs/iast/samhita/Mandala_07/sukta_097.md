# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 97

**10  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| 1 indra:, 2,4-8 bṛhaspati:, 3,9 indrābrahmaṇaspatī, 10 indrābṛhaspatī | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 97 | **Verse:** 1

ya̱jñe di̱vo nṛ̱ṣada̍ne pṛthi̱vyā naro̱ yatra̍ deva̱yavo̱ mada̍nti |
indrā̍ya̱ yatra̱ sava̍nāni su̱nve gama̱nmadā̍ya pratha̱maṃ vaya̍śca

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 97 | **Verse:** 2

ā daivyā̍ vṛṇīma̱he'vā̍ṃsi̱ bṛha̱spati̍rno maha̱ ā sa̍khāyaḥ |

yathā̱ bhave̍ma mī̱l̤huṣe̱ anā̍gā̱ yo no̍ dā̱tā pa̍rā̱vata̍: pi̱teva̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 97 | **Verse:** 3

tamu̱ jyeṣṭha̱ṃ nama̍sā ha̱virbhi̍: su̱śeva̱ṃ brahma̍ṇa̱spati̍ṃ gṛṇīṣe |

indra̱ṃ śloko̱ mahi̱ daivya̍: siṣaktu̱ yo brahma̍ṇo de̱vakṛ̍tasya̱ rājā̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 97 | **Verse:** 4

sa ā no̱ yoni̍ṃ sadatu̱ preṣṭho̱ bṛha̱spati̍rvi̱śvavā̍ro̱ yo asti̍ |

kāmo̍ rā̱yaḥ su̱vīrya̍sya̱ taṃ dā̱tparṣa̍nno̱ ati̍ sa̱ścato̱ ari̍ṣṭān

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 97 | **Verse:** 5

tamā no̍ a̱rkama̱mṛtā̍ya̱ juṣṭa̍mi̱me dhā̍sura̱mṛtā̍saḥ purā̱jāḥ |

śuci̍krandaṃ yaja̱taṃ pa̱styā̍nā̱ṃ bṛha̱spati̍mana̱rvāṇa̍ṃ huvema

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 97 | **Verse:** 6

taṃ śa̱gmāso̍ aru̱ṣāso̱ aśvā̱ bṛha̱spati̍ṃ saha̱vāho̍ vahanti |

saha̍ści̱dyasya̱ nīla̍vatsa̱dhastha̱ṃ nabho̱ na rū̱pama̍ru̱ṣaṃ vasā̍nāḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 97 | **Verse:** 7

sa hi śuci̍: śa̱tapa̍tra̱: sa śu̱ndhyurhira̍ṇyavāśīriṣi̱raḥ sva̱rṣāḥ |

bṛha̱spati̱: sa svā̍ve̱śa ṛ̱ṣvaḥ pu̱rū sakhi̍bhya āsu̱tiṃ kari̍ṣṭhaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 97 | **Verse:** 8

de̱vī de̱vasya̱ roda̍sī̱ jani̍trī̱ bṛha̱spati̍ṃ vāvṛdhaturmahi̱tvā |

da̱kṣāyyā̍ya dakṣatā sakhāya̱: kara̱dbrahma̍ṇe su̱tarā̍ sugā̱dhā

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 97 | **Verse:** 9

i̱yaṃ vā̍ṃ brahmaṇaspate suvṛ̱ktirbrahmendrā̍ya va̱jriṇe̍ akāri |

a̱vi̱ṣṭaṃ dhiyo̍ jigṛ̱taṃ pura̍ṃdhīrjaja̱stama̱ryo va̱nuṣā̱marā̍tīḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 97 | **Verse:** 10

bṛha̍spate yu̱vamindra̍śca̱ vasvo̍ di̱vyasye̍śāthe u̱ta pārthi̍vasya |

dha̱ttaṃ ra̱yiṃ stu̍va̱te kī̱raye̍ cidyū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
