# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 39

**7 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| viśve devā: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 1

ū̱rdhvo a̱gniḥ su̍ma̱tiṃ vasvo̍ aśretpratī̱cī jū̱rṇirde̱vatā̍timeti |
bhe̱jāte̱ adrī̍ ra̱thye̍va̱ panthā̍mṛ̱taṃ hotā̍ na iṣi̱to ya̍jāti

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 2

pra vā̍vṛje supra̱yā ba̱rhire̍ṣā̱mā vi̱śpatī̍va̱ bīri̍ṭa iyāte |

vi̱śāma̱ktoru̱ṣasa̍: pū̱rvahū̍tau vā̱yuḥ pū̱ṣā sva̱staye̍ ni̱yutvā̍n

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 3

jma̱yā atra̱ vasa̍vo ranta de̱vā u̱rāva̱ntari̍kṣe marjayanta śu̱bhrāḥ |

a̱rvākpa̱tha u̍rujrayaḥ kṛṇudhva̱ṃ śrotā̍ dū̱tasya̍ ja̱gmuṣo̍ no a̱sya

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 4

te hi ya̱jñeṣu̍ ya̱jñiyā̍sa̱ ūmā̍: sa̱dhastha̱ṃ viśve̍ a̱bhi santi̍ de̱vāḥ |

tām̐ a̍dhva̱ra u̍śa̱to ya̍kṣyagne śru̱ṣṭī bhaga̱ṃ nāsa̍tyā̱ pura̍ṃdhim

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 5

āgne̱ giro̍ di̱va ā pṛ̍thi̱vyā mi̱traṃ va̍ha̱ varu̍ṇa̱mindra̍ma̱gnim |

ārya̱maṇa̱madi̍ti̱ṃ viṣṇu̍meṣā̱ṃ sara̍svatī ma̱ruto̍ mādayantām

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 6

ra̱re ha̱vyaṃ ma̱tibhi̍rya̱jñiyā̍nā̱ṃ nakṣa̱tkāma̱ṃ martyā̍nā̱masi̍nvan |

dhātā̍ ra̱yima̍vida̱syaṃ sa̍dā̱sāṃ sa̍kṣī̱mahi̱ yujye̍bhi̱rnu de̱vaiḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 7

nū roda̍sī a̱bhiṣṭu̍te̱ vasi̍ṣṭhaiṝ̱tāvā̍no̱ varu̍ṇo mi̱tro a̱gniḥ |

yaccha̍ntu ca̱ndrā u̍pa̱maṃ no̍ a̱rkaṃ yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
