# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 84

**5  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| indrāvaruṇau | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 1

ā vā̍ṃ rājānāvadhva̱re va̍vṛtyāṃ ha̱vyebhi̍rindrāvaruṇā̱ namo̍bhiḥ |
pra vā̍ṃ ghṛ̱tācī̍ bā̱hvordadhā̍nā̱ pari̱ tmanā̱ viṣu̍rūpā jigāti

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 2

yu̱vo rā̱ṣṭraṃ bṛ̱hadi̍nvati̱ dyauryau se̱tṛbhi̍rara̱jjubhi̍: sinī̱thaḥ |

pari̍ no̱ hel̤o̱ varu̍ṇasya vṛjyā u̱ruṃ na̱ indra̍: kṛṇavadu lo̱kam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 3

kṛ̱taṃ no̍ ya̱jñaṃ vi̱dathe̍ṣu̱ cāru̍ṃ kṛ̱taṃ brahmā̍ṇi sū̱riṣu̍ praśa̱stā |

upo̍ ra̱yirde̱vajū̍to na etu̱ pra ṇa̍: spā̱rhābhi̍rū̱tibhi̍stiretam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 4

a̱sme i̍ndrāvaruṇā vi̱śvavā̍raṃ ra̱yiṃ dha̍tta̱ṃ vasu̍mantaṃ puru̱kṣum |

pra ya ā̍di̱tyo anṛ̍tā mi̱nātyami̍tā̱ śūro̍ dayate̱ vasū̍ni

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 5

i̱yamindra̱ṃ varu̍ṇamaṣṭa me̱ gīḥ prāva̍tto̱ke tana̍ye̱ tūtu̍jānā |

su̱ratnā̍so de̱vavī̍tiṃ gamema yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
