# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 101

**6  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| (vṛṣṭikāma ), kumāra āgneyo vā |parjanya:|  triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 1

ti̱sro vāca̱: pra va̍da̱ jyoti̍ragrā̱ yā e̱taddu̱hre ma̍dhudo̱ghamūdha̍: |
sa va̱tsaṃ kṛ̱ṇvangarbha̱moṣa̍dhīnāṃ sa̱dyo jā̱to vṛ̍ṣa̱bho ro̍ravīti

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 2

yo vardha̍na̱ oṣa̍dhīnā̱ṃ yo a̱pāṃ yo viśva̍sya̱ jaga̍to de̱va īśe̍ |

sa tri̱dhātu̍ śara̱ṇaṃ śarma̍ yaṃsattri̱vartu̱ jyoti̍: svabhi̱ṣṭya1sme

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 3

sta̱rīru̍ tva̱dbhava̍ti̱ sūta̍ u tvadyathāva̱śaṃ ta̱nva̍ṃ cakra e̱ṣaḥ |

pi̱tuḥ paya̱: prati̍ gṛbhṇāti mā̱tā tena̍ pi̱tā va̍rdhate̱ tena̍ pu̱traḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 4

yasmi̱nviśvā̍ni̱ bhuva̍nāni ta̱sthusti̱sro dyāva̍stre̱dhā sa̱srurāpa̍: |

traya̱: kośā̍sa upa̱seca̍nāso̱ madhva̍: ścotantya̱bhito̍ vira̱pśam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 5

i̱daṃ vaca̍: pa̱rjanyā̍ya sva̱rāje̍ hṛ̱do a̱stvanta̍ra̱ṃ tajju̍joṣat |

ma̱yo̱bhuvo̍ vṛ̱ṣṭaya̍: santva̱sme su̍pippa̱lā oṣa̍dhīrde̱vago̍pāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 6

sa re̍to̱dhā vṛ̍ṣa̱bhaḥ śaśva̍tīnā̱ṃ tasmi̍nnā̱tmā jaga̍tasta̱sthuṣa̍śca |

tanma̍ ṛ̱taṃ pā̍tu śa̱taśā̍radāya yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
