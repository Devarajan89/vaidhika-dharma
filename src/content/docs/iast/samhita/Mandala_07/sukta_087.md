# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 87

**7  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| varuṇa:| triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 1

rada̍tpa̱tho varu̍ṇa̱: sūryā̍ya̱ prārṇā̍ṃsi samu̱driyā̍ na̱dīnā̍m |
sargo̱ na sṛ̱ṣṭo arva̍tīṝtā̱yañca̱kāra̍ ma̱hīra̱vanī̱raha̍bhyaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 2

ā̱tmā te̱ vāto̱ raja̱ ā na̍vīnotpa̱śurna bhūrṇi̱ryava̍se sasa̱vān |

a̱ntarma̱hī bṛ̍ha̱tī roda̍sī̱me viśvā̍ te̱ dhāma̍ varuṇa pri̱yāṇi̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 3

pari̱ spaśo̱ varu̍ṇasya̱ smadi̍ṣṭā u̱bhe pa̍śyanti̱ roda̍sī su̱meke̍ |

ṛ̱tāvā̍naḥ ka̱vayo̍ ya̱jñadhī̍rā̱: prace̍taso̱ ya i̱ṣaya̍nta̱ manma̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 4

u̱vāca̍ me̱ varu̍ṇo̱ medhi̍rāya̱ triḥ sa̱pta nāmāghnyā̍ bibharti |

vi̱dvānpa̱dasya̱ guhyā̱ na vo̍cadyu̱gāya̱ vipra̱ upa̍rāya̱ śikṣa̍n

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 5

ti̱sro dyāvo̱ nihi̍tā a̱ntara̍sminti̱sro bhūmī̱rupa̍rā̱: ṣaḍvi̍dhānāḥ |

gṛtso̱ rājā̱ varu̍ṇaścakra e̱taṃ di̱vi pre̱ṅkhaṃ hi̍ra̱ṇyaya̍ṃ śu̱bhe kam

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 6

ava̱ sindhu̱ṃ varu̍ṇo̱ dyauri̍va sthāddra̱pso na śve̱to mṛ̱gastuvi̍ṣmān |

ga̱mbhī̱raśa̍ṃso̱ raja̍so vi̱māna̍: supā̱rakṣa̍traḥ sa̱to a̱sya rājā̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 7

yo mṛ̱l̤ayā̍ti ca̱kruṣe̍ ci̱dāgo̍ va̱yaṃ syā̍ma̱ varu̍ṇe̱ anā̍gāḥ |

anu̍ vra̱tānyadi̍teṝ̱dhanto̍ yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
