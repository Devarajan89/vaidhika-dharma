# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 51

**3 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| ādityā: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 1

ā̱di̱tyānā̱mava̍sā̱ nūta̍nena sakṣī̱mahi̱ śarma̍ṇā̱ śaṃta̍mena |
a̱nā̱gā̱stve a̍diti̱tve tu̱rāsa̍ i̱maṃ ya̱jñaṃ da̍dhatu̱ śroṣa̍māṇāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 2

ā̱di̱tyāso̱ adi̍tirmādayantāṃ mi̱tro a̍rya̱mā varu̍ṇo̱ raji̍ṣṭhāḥ |

a̱smāka̍ṃ santu̱ bhuva̍nasya go̱pāḥ piba̍ntu̱ soma̱mava̍se no a̱dya

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 3

ā̱di̱tyā viśve̍ ma̱ruta̍śca̱ viśve̍ de̱vāśca̱ viśva̍ ṛ̱bhava̍śca̱ viśve̍ |

indro̍ a̱gnira̱śvinā̍ tuṣṭuvā̱nā yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
