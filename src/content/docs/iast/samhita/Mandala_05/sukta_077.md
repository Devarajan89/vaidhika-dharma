# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 77

**5 bhaumo'triḥ| aśvinau| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 77 | **Verse:** 1

prā̱ta̱ryāvā̍ṇā pratha̱mā ya̍jadhvaṃ pu̱rā gṛdhrā̱dara̍ruṣaḥ pibātaḥ |
prā̱tarhi ya̱jñama̱śvinā̍ da̱dhāte̱ pra śa̍ṃsanti ka̱vaya̍: pūrva̱bhāja̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 77 | **Verse:** 2

prā̱tarya̍jadhvama̱śvinā̍ hinota̱ na sā̱yama̍sti deva̱yā aju̍ṣṭam |

u̱tānyo a̱smad ya̍jate̱ vi cāva̱: pūrva̍:pūrvo̱ yaja̍māno̱ vanī̍yān

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 77 | **Verse:** 3

hira̍ṇyatva̱ṅmadhu̍varṇo ghṛ̱tasnu̱: pṛkṣo̱ vaha̱nnā ratho̍ vartate vām |

mano̍javā aśvinā̱ vāta̍raṃhā̱ yenā̍tiyā̱tho du̍ri̱tāni̱ viśvā̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 77 | **Verse:** 4

yo bhūyi̍ṣṭha̱ṃ nāsa̍tyābhyāṃ vi̱veṣa̱ cani̍ṣṭhaṃ pi̱tvo rara̍te vibhā̱ge |

sa to̱kama̍sya pīpara̱cchamī̍bhi̱ranū̍rdhvabhāsa̱: sada̱mit tu̍turyāt

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 77 | **Verse:** 5

sama̱śvino̱rava̍sā̱ nūta̍nena mayo̱bhuvā̍ su̱praṇī̍tī gamema |

ā no̍ ra̱yiṃ va̍hata̱mota vī̱rānā viśvā̍nyamṛtā̱ saubha̍gāni
