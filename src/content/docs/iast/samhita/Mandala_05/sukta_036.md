# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 36

**6 prabhūvasurāṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ | triṣṭup, 3jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 1

sa ā ga̍ma̱dindro̱ yo vasū̍nā̱ṃ cike̍ta̱d dātu̱ṃ dāma̍no rayī̱ṇām |
dha̱nva̱ca̱ro na vaṃsa̍gastṛṣā̱ṇaśca̍kamā̱naḥ pi̍batu du̱gdhama̱ṃśum

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 2

ā te̱ hanū̍ harivaḥ śūra̱ śipre̱ ruha̱t somo̱ na parva̍tasya pṛ̱ṣṭhe |

anu̍ tvā rāja̱nnarva̍to̱ na hi̱nvan gī̱rbhirma̍dema puruhūta̱ viśve̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 3

ca̱kraṃ na vṛ̱ttaṃ pu̍ruhūta vepate̱ mano̍ bhi̱yā me̱ ama̍te̱rida̍drivaḥ |

rathā̱dadhi̍ tvā jari̱tā sa̍dāvṛdha ku̱vinnu sto̍ṣanmaghavan purū̱vasu̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 4

e̱ṣa grāve̍va jari̱tā ta̍ i̱ndreya̍rti̱ vāca̍ṃ bṛ̱hadā̍śuṣā̱ṇaḥ |

pra sa̱vyena̍ maghava̱n yaṃsi̍ rā̱yaḥ pra da̍kṣi̱ṇiddha̍rivo̱ mā vi ve̍naḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 5

vṛṣā̍ tvā̱ vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ vardhatu̱ dyaurvṛṣā̱ vṛṣa̍bhyāṃ vahase̱ hari̍bhyām |

sa no̱ vṛṣā̱ vṛṣa̍rathaḥ suśipra̱ vṛṣa̍krato̱ vṛṣā̍ vajri̱n bhare̍ dhāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 6

yo rohi̍tau vā̱jinau̍ vā̱jinī̍vān tri̱bhiḥ śa̱taiḥ saca̍mānā̱vadi̍ṣṭa |

yūne̱ sama̍smai kṣi̱tayo̍ namantāṃ śru̱tara̍thāya maruto duvo̱yā
