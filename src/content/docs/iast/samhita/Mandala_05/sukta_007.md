# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 7

**10 iṣa ātreyaḥ| agniḥ| anuṣṭup, 10 paṃktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 1

varṣi̍ṣṭhāya kṣitī̱nāmū̱rjo naptre̱ saha̍svate
