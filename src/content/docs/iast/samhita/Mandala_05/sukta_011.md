# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 11

**6 sutaṃbhara ātreyaḥ| agniḥ | jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 1

jana̍sya go̱pā a̍janiṣṭa̱ jāgṛ̍vira̱gniḥ su̱dakṣa̍: suvi̱tāya̱ navya̍se |
ghṛ̱tapra̍tīko bṛha̱tā di̍vi̱spṛśā̍ dyu̱mad vi bhā̍ti bhara̱tebhya̱: śuci̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 2

ya̱jñasya̍ ke̱tuṃ pra̍tha̱maṃ pu̱rohi̍tama̱gniṃ nara̍striṣadha̱sthe samī̍dhire |

indre̍ṇa de̱vaiḥ sa̱ratha̱ṃ sa ba̱rhiṣi̱ sīda̱nni hotā̍ ya̱jathā̍ya su̱kratu̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 3

asa̍mmṛṣṭo jāyase mā̱troḥ śuci̍rma̱ndraḥ ka̱viruda̍tiṣṭho vi̱vasva̍taḥ |

ghṛ̱tena̍ tvāvardhayannagna āhuta dhū̱maste̍ ke̱tura̍bhavad di̱vi śri̱taḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 4

a̱gnirno̍ ya̱jñamupa̍ vetu sādhu̱yā 'gniṃ naro̱ vi bha̍rante gṛ̱hegṛ̍he |

a̱gnirdū̱to a̍bhavaddhavya̱vāha̍no̱'gniṃ vṛ̍ṇā̱nā vṛ̍ṇate ka̱vikra̍tum

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 5

tubhye̱dama̍gne̱ madhu̍mattama̱ṃ vaca̱stubhya̍ṃ manī̱ṣā i̱yama̍stu̱ śaṃ hṛ̱de |

tvāṃ gira̱: sindhu̍mivā̱vanī̍rma̱hīrā pṛ̍ṇanti̱ śava̍sā va̱rdhaya̍nti ca

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 6
