# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 56

**9 śyāvāśva ātreyaḥ| marutaḥ| bṛhatī, 3, 7 satobṛhatī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 1

agne̱ śardha̍nta̱mā ga̱ṇaṃ pi̱ṣṭaṃ ru̱kmebhi̍ra̱ñjibhi̍: |
viśo̍ a̱dya ma̱rutā̱mava̍ hvaye di̱vaści̍d roca̱nādadhi̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 2

yathā̍ ci̱nmanya̍se hṛ̱dā tadinme̍ jagmurā̱śasa̍: |

ye te̱ nedi̍ṣṭha̱ṃ hava̍nānyā̱gama̱n tān va̍rdha bhī̱masa̍ṃdṛśaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 3

mī̱l̤ahuṣma̍tīva pṛthi̱vī parā̍hatā̱ mada̍ntyetya̱smadā |

ṛkṣo̱ na vo̍ maruta̱: śimī̍vā̱m̐ amo̍ du̱dhro gauri̍va bhīma̱yuḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 4

ni ye ri̱ṇantyoja̍sā̱ vṛthā̱ gāvo̱ na du̱rdhura̍: |

aśmā̍naṃ cit svaryaṃ1 parva̍taṃ gi̱riṃ pra cyā̍vayanti̱ yāma̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 5

ut ti̍ṣṭha nū̱name̍ṣā̱ṃ stomai̱: samu̍kṣitānām |

ma̱rutā̍ṃ puru̱tama̱mapū̍rvya̱ṃ gavā̱ṃ sarga̍miva hvaye

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 6

yu̱ṅgdhvaṃ hyaru̍ṣī̱ rathe̍ yu̱ṅgdhvaṃ rathe̍ṣu ro̱hita̍: |

yu̱ṅgdhvaṃ harī̍ aji̱rā dhu̱ri vol̤aha̍ve̱ vahi̍ṣṭhā dhu̱ri vol̤aha̍ve

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 7

u̱ta sya vā̱jya̍ru̱ṣastu̍vi̱ṣvaṇi̍ri̱ha sma̍ dhāyi darśa̱taḥ |

mā vo̱ yāme̍ṣu marutaści̱raṃ ka̍ra̱t pra taṃ rathe̍ṣu codata

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 8

ratha̱ṃ nu māru̍taṃ va̱yaṃ śra̍va̱syumā hu̍vāmahe |

ā yasmi̍n ta̱sthau su̱raṇā̍ni̱ bibhra̍tī̱ sacā̍ ma̱rutsu̍ roda̱sī

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 9

taṃ va̱: śardha̍ṃ rathe̱śubha̍ṃ tve̱ṣaṃ pa̍na̱syumā hu̍ve |

yasmi̱n tsujā̍tā su̱bhagā̍ mahī̱yate̱ sacā̍ ma̱rutsu̍ mīl̤ahu̱ṣī
