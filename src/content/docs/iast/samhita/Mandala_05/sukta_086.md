# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 86

**bhaumo'triḥ|indrāgnī| anuṣṭup, 6 virāṭ pūrvā|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 1

indrā̍gnī̱ yamava̍tha u̱bhā vāje̍ṣu̱ martya̍m | dṛ̱l̤ahā ci̱t sa pra bhe̍dati dyu̱mnā vāṇī̍riva tri̱taḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 2

yā pṛta̍nāsu du̱ṣṭarā̱ yā vāje̍ṣu śra̱vāyyā̍ | yā pañca̍ carṣa̱ṇīra̱bhī̍ndrā̱gnī tā ha̍vāmahe

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 3

tayo̱ridama̍va̱cchava̍sti̱gmā di̱dyunma̱ghono̍: | prati̱ druṇā̱ gabha̍styo̱rgavā̍ṃ vṛtra̱ghna eṣa̍te

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 4

tā vā̱meṣe̱ rathā̍nāmindrā̱gnī ha̍vāmahe | patī̍ tu̱rasya̱ rādha̍so vi̱dvāṃsā̱ girva̍ṇastamā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 5

tā vṛ̱dhantā̱vanu̱ dyūn martā̍ya de̱vāva̱dabhā̍ | arha̍ntā cit pu̱ro da̱dheṃ'śe̍va de̱vāvarva̍te

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 6

e̱vendrā̱gnibhyā̱mahā̍vi ha̱vyaṃ śū̱ṣya̍ṃ ghṛ̱taṃ na pū̱tamadri̍bhiḥ |

tā sū̱riṣu̱ śravo̍ bṛ̱hadra̱yiṃ gṛ̱ṇatsu̍ didhṛta̱miṣa̍ṃ gṛ̱ṇatsu̍ didhṛtam
