# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 8

**7 iṣa ātreyaḥ| agniḥ| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 1

tvāma̍gna ṛtā̱yava̱: samī̍dhire pra̱tnaṃ pra̱tnāsa̍ ū̱taye̍ sahaskṛta |
pu̱ru̱śca̱ndraṃ ya̍ja̱taṃ vi̱śvadhā̍yasa̱ṃ damū̍nasaṃ gṛ̱hapa̍ti̱ṃ vare̍ṇyam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 2

tvāma̍gne̱ ati̍thiṃ pū̱rvyaṃ viśa̍: śo̱ciṣke̍śaṃ gṛ̱hapa̍ti̱ṃ ni ṣe̍dire |

bṛ̱hatke̍tuṃ puru̱rūpa̍ṃ dhana̱spṛta̍ṃ su̱śarmā̍ṇa̱ṃ svava̍saṃ jara̱dviṣa̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 3

tvāma̍gne̱ mānu̍ṣīrīl̤ate̱ viśo̍ hotrā̱vida̱ṃ vivi̍ciṃ ratna̱dhāta̍mam |

guhā̱ santa̍ṃ subhaga vi̱śvada̍rśataṃ tuviṣva̱ṇasa̍ṃ su̱yaja̍ṃ ghṛta̱śriya̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 4

tvāma̍gne dharṇa̱siṃ vi̱śvadhā̍ va̱yaṃ gī̱rbhirgṛ̱ṇanto̱ nama̱sopa̍ sedima |

sa no̍ juṣasva samidhā̱no a̍ṅgiro de̱vo marta̍sya ya̱śasā̍ sudī̱tibhi̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 5

tvama̍gne puru̱rūpo̍ vi̱śevi̍śe̱ vayo̍ dadhāsi pra̱tnathā̍ puruṣṭuta |

pu̱rūṇyannā̱ saha̍sā̱ vi rā̍jasi̱ tviṣi̱: sā te̍ titviṣā̱ṇasya̱ nādhṛṣe̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 6

tvāma̍gne samidhā̱naṃ ya̍viṣṭhya de̱vā dū̱taṃ ca̍krire havya̱vāha̍nam |

u̱ru̱jraya̍saṃ ghṛ̱tayo̍ni̱māhu̍taṃ tve̱ṣaṃ cakṣu̍rdadhire coda̱yanma̍ti

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 7

tvāma̍gne pra̱diva̱ āhu̍taṃ ghṛ̱taiḥ su̍mnā̱yava̍: suṣa̱midhā̱ samī̍dhire |

sa vā̍vṛdhā̱na oṣa̍dhībhirukṣi̱to̱3'bhi jrayā̍ṃsi̱ pārthi̍vā̱ vi ti̍ṣṭhase
