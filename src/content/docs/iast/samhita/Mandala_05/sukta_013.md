# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 13

**6 sutaṃbhara ātreyaḥ| agniḥ | gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 1

arca̍ntastvā havāma̱he'rca̍nta̱: sami̍dhīmahi | agne̱ arca̍nta ū̱taye̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 2

a̱gneḥ stoma̍ṃ manāmahe si̱dhrama̱dya di̍vi̱spṛśa̍: | de̱vasya̍ draviṇa̱syava̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 3

a̱gnirju̍ṣata no̱ giro̱ hotā̱ yo mānu̍ṣe̱ṣvā | sa ya̍kṣa̱d daivya̱ṃ jana̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 4

tvama̍gne sa̱prathā̍ asi̱ juṣṭo̱ hotā̱ vare̍ṇyaḥ | tvayā̍ ya̱jñaṃ vi ta̍nvate

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 5

tvāma̍gne vāja̱sāta̍ma̱ṃ viprā̍ vardhanti̱ suṣṭu̍tam | sa no̍ rāsva su̱vīrya̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 6
