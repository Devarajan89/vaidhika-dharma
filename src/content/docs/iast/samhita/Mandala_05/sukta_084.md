# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 84

**3 bhaumo'triḥ| pṛthivī| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 1

bal̤i̱tthā parva̍tānāṃ khi̱draṃ bi̍bharṣi pṛthivi | pra yā bhūmi̍ṃ pravatvati ma̱hnā ji̱noṣi̍ mahini

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 2

stomā̍sastvā vicāriṇi̱ prati̍ ṣṭobhantya̱ktubhi̍: | pra yā vāja̱ṃ na heṣa̍ntaṃ pe̱rumasya̍syarjuni
