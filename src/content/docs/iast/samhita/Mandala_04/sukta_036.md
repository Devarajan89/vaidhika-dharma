# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 36

**9 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| ṛbhavaḥ|jagatī, 9 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 1

a̱na̱śvo jā̱to a̍nabhī̱śuru̱kthyo̱3 ratha̍strica̱kraḥ pari̍ vartate̱ raja̍: |
ma̱hat tadvo̍ de̱vya̍sya pra̱vāca̍na̱ṃ dyāmṛ̍bhavaḥ pṛthi̱vīṃ yacca̱ puṣya̍tha

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 2

ratha̱ṃ ye ca̱kruḥ su̱vṛta̍ṃ su̱ceta̱so'vi̍hvaranta̱ṃ mana̍sa̱spari̱ dhyayā̍ |

tām̐ ū̱ nva1sya sava̍nasya pī̱taya̱ ā vo̍ vājā ṛbhavo vedayāmasi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 3

tad vo̍ vājā ṛbhavaḥ supravāca̱naṃ de̱veṣu̍ vibhvo abhavanmahitva̱nam |

jivrī̱ yat santā̍ pi̱tarā̍ sanā̱jurā̱ puna̱ryuvā̍nā ca̱rathā̍ya̱ takṣa̍tha

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 4

eka̱ṃ vi ca̍kra cama̱saṃ catu̍rvaya̱ṃ niścarma̍ṇo̱ gāma̍riṇīta dhī̱tibhi̍: |

athā̍ de̱veṣva̍mṛta̱tvamā̍naśa śru̱ṣṭī vā̍jā ṛbhava̱stad va̍ u̱kthya̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 5

ṛ̱bhu̱to ra̱yiḥ pra̍tha̱maśra̍vastamo̱ vāja̍śrutāso̱ yamajī̍jana̱n nara̍: |

vi̱bhva̱ta̱ṣṭo vi̱dathe̍ṣu pra̱vācyo̱ yaṃ de̍vā̱so'va̍thā̱ sa vica̍rṣaṇiḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 6

sa vā̱jyarvā̱ sa ṛṣi̍rvaca̱syayā̱ sa śūro̱ astā̱ pṛta̍nāsu du̱ṣṭara̍: |

sa rā̱yaspoṣa̱ṃ sa su̱vīrya̍ṃ dadhe̱ yaṃ vājo̱ vibhvā̍m̐ ṛ̱bhavo̱ yamāvi̍ṣuḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 7

śreṣṭha̍ṃ va̱: peśo̱ adhi̍ dhāyi darśa̱taṃ stomo̍ vājā ṛbhava̱staṃ ju̍juṣṭana |

dhīrā̍so̱ hi ṣṭhā ka̱vayo̍ vipa̱ścita̱stān va̍ e̱nā brahma̱ṇā ve̍dayāmasi

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 8

yū̱yama̱smabhya̍ṃ dhi̱ṣaṇā̍bhya̱spari̍ vi̱dvāṃso̱ viśvā̱ naryā̍ṇi̱ bhoja̍nā |

dyu̱manta̱ṃ vāja̱ṃ vṛṣa̍śuṣmamutta̱mamā no̍ ra̱yimṛ̍bhavastakṣa̱tā vaya̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 9

i̱ha pra̱jāmi̱ha ra̱yiṃ rarā̍ṇā i̱ha śravo̍ vī̱rava̍t takṣatā naḥ |

yena̍ va̱yaṃ ci̱taye̱mātya̱nyān taṃ vāja̍ṃ ci̱tramṛ̍bhavo dadā naḥ
