# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 55

**10 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| viśve devāḥ| triṣṭup 8-10 gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 1

ko va̍strā̱tā va̍sava̱: ko va̍rū̱tā dyāvā̍bhūmī adite̱ trāsī̍thāṃ naḥ |
sahī̍yaso varuṇa mitra̱ martā̱t ko vo̍'dhva̱re vari̍vo dhāti devāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 2

pra ye dhāmā̍ni pū̱rvyāṇyarcā̱n vi yadu̱cchān vi̍yo̱tāro̱ amū̍rāḥ |

vi̱dhā̱tāro̱ vi te da̍dhu̱raja̍srā ṛ̱tadhī̍tayo rurucanta da̱smāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 3

pra pa̱styā̱3madi̍ti̱ṃ sindhu̍ma̱rkaiḥ sva̱stimī̍l̤e sa̱khyāya̍ de̱vīm |

u̱bhe yathā̍ no̱ aha̍nī ni̱pāta̍ u̱ṣāsā̱naktā̍ karatā̱mada̍bdhe

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 4

vya̍rya̱mā varu̍ṇaśceti̱ panthā̍mi̱ṣaspati̍: suvi̱taṃ gā̱tuma̱gniḥ |

indrā̍viṣṇū nṛ̱vadu̱ ṣu stavā̍nā̱ śarma̍ no yanta̱mama̍va̱d varū̍tham

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 5

pāt pati̱rjanyā̱daṃha̍so no mi̱tro mi̱triyā̍du̱ta na̍ uruṣyet

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 6

sa̱mu̱draṃ na sa̱ṃcara̍ṇe sani̱ṣyavo̍ gha̱rmasva̍raso na̱dyo̱3 apa̍ vran

## Verse 7

na̱hi mi̱trasya̱ varu̍ṇasya dhā̱simarhā̍masi pra̱miya̱ṃ sānva̱gneḥ

## Verse 8
