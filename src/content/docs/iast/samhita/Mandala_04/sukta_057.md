# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 57

**8 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| 1-3  kṣetrapatiḥ, 4 śunaḥ, 5, 8 śunāsīrau, 6-7 sītā| anuṣṭup, 5 pura uṣṇik, 2, 3, 8 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 1

kṣetra̍sya̱ pati̍nā va̱yaṃ hi̱tene̍va jayāmasi |
gāmaśva̍ṃ poṣayi̱tnvā sa no̍ mṛl̤ātī̱dṛśe̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 2

kṣetra̍sya pate̱ madhu̍mantamū̱rmiṃ dhe̱nuri̍va̱ payo̍ a̱smāsu̍ dhukṣva |

ma̱dhu̱ścuta̍ṃ ghṛ̱tami̍va̱ supū̍tamṛ̱tasya̍ na̱: pata̍yo mṛl̤ayantu

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 3

madhu̍matī̱roṣa̍dhī̱rdyāva̱ āpo̱ madhu̍manno bhavatva̱ntari̍kṣam |

kṣetra̍sya̱ pati̱rmadhu̍mān no a̱stvari̍ṣyanto̱ anve̍naṃ carema

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 4

śu̱naṃ vā̱hāḥ śu̱naṃ nara̍: śu̱naṃ kṛ̍ṣatu̱ lāṅga̍lam |

śu̱naṃ va̍ra̱trā ba̍dhyantāṃ śu̱namaṣṭrā̱mudi̍ṅgaya

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 5

śunā̍sīrāvi̱māṃ vāca̍ṃ juṣethā̱ṃ yad di̱vi ca̱krathu̱: paya̍: | tene̱māmupa̍ siñcatam

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 6

## Verse 7

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 8
