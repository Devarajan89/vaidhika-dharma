# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 46

**7 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| indravāyū, 1 vāyuḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 1

agra̍ṃ pibā̱ madhū̍nāṃ su̱taṃ vā̍yo̱ divi̍ṣṭiṣu | tvaṃ hi pū̍rva̱pā asi̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 2

śa̱tenā̍ no a̱bhiṣṭi̍bhirni̱yutvā̱m̐ indra̍sārathiḥ | vāyo̍ su̱tasya̍ tṛmpatam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 3

ā vā̍ṃ sa̱hasra̱ṃ hara̍ya̱ indra̍vāyū a̱bhi praya̍: | vaha̍ntu̱ soma̍pītaye

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 4

ratha̱ṃ hira̍ṇyavandhura̱mindra̍vāyū svadhva̱ram | ā hi sthātho̍ divi̱spṛśa̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 5

rathe̍na pṛthu̱pāja̍sā dā̱śvāṃsa̱mupa̍ gacchatam | indra̍vāyū i̱hā ga̍tam

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 6

indra̍vāyū a̱yaṃ su̱tastaṃ de̱vebhi̍: sa̱joṣa̍sā | piba̍taṃ dā̱śuṣo̍ gṛ̱he

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 7

i̱ha pra̱yāṇa̍mastu vā̱mindra̍vāyū vi̱moca̍nam | i̱ha vā̱ṃ soma̍pītaye
