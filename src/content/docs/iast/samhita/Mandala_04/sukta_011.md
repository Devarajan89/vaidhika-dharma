# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 11

**6 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| agniḥ|triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 1

bha̱draṃ te̍ agne sahasi̱nnanī̍kamupā̱ka ā ro̍cate̱ sūrya̍sya |
ruśa̍d dṛ̱śe da̍dṛśe nakta̱yā ci̱darū̍kṣitaṃ dṛ̱śa ā rū̱pe anna̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 2

vi ṣā̍hyagne gṛṇa̱te ma̍nī̱ṣāṃ khaṃ vepa̍sā tuvijāta̱ stavā̍naḥ |

viśve̍bhi̱ryad vā̱vana̍: śukra de̱vaistanno̍ rāsva sumaho̱ bhūri̱ manma̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 3

tvada̍gne̱ kāvyā̱ tvanma̍nī̱ṣāstvadu̱kthā jā̍yante̱ rādhyā̍ni |

tvade̍ti̱ dravi̍ṇaṃ vī̱rape̍śā i̱tthādhi̍ye dā̱śuṣe̱ martyā̍ya

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 4

tvad vā̱jī vā̍jambha̱ro vihā̍yā abhiṣṭi̱kṛjjā̍yate sa̱tyaśu̍ṣmaḥ |

tvad ra̱yirde̱vajū̍to mayo̱bhustvadā̱śurjū̍ju̱vām̐ a̍gne̱ arvā̍

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 5

dve̱ṣo̱yuta̱mā vi̍vāsanti dhī̱bhirdamū̍nasaṃ gṛ̱hapa̍ti̱mamū̍ram
