# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 21

**11 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 1

ā yā̱tvindro'va̍sa̱ upa̍ na i̱ha stu̱taḥ sa̍dha̱māda̍stu̱ śūra̍: |
vā̱vṛ̱dhā̱nastavi̍ṣī̱ryasya̍ pū̱rvīrdyaurna kṣa̱trama̱bhibhū̍ti̱ puṣyā̍t

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 2

tasyedi̱ha sta̍vatha̱ vṛṣṇyā̍ni tuvidyu̱mnasya̍ tuvi̱rādha̍so̱ nṝn |

yasya̱ kratu̍rvida̱thyo̱3 na sa̱mrāṭ sā̱hvāntaru̍tro a̱bhyasti̍ kṛ̱ṣṭīḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 3

ā yā̱tvindro̍ di̱va ā pṛ̍thi̱vyā ma̱kṣū sa̍mu̱drādu̱ta vā̱ purī̍ṣāt |

sva̍rṇarā̱dava̍se no ma̱rutvā̍n parā̱vato̍ vā̱ sada̍nādṛ̱tasya̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 4

sthū̱rasya̍ rā̱yo bṛ̍ha̱to ya īśe̱ tamu̍ ṣṭavāma vi̱dathe̱ṣvindra̍m |

yo vā̱yunā̱ jaya̍ti̱ goma̍tīṣu̱ pra dhṛ̍ṣṇu̱yā naya̍ti̱ vasyo̱ accha̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 5

upa̱ yo namo̱ nama̍si stabhā̱yanniya̍rti̱ vāca̍ṃ ja̱naya̱n yaja̍dhyai |

ṛ̱ñja̱sā̱naḥ pu̍ru̱vāra̍ u̱kthairendra̍ṃ kṛṇvīta̱ sada̍neṣu̱ hotā̍

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 6

ā du̱roṣā̍: pā̱styasya̱ hotā̱ yo no̍ ma̱hān tsa̱ṃvara̍ṇeṣu̱ vahni̍:

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 7

guhā̱ yadī̍mauśi̱jasya̱ gohe̱ pra yad dhi̱ye prāya̍se̱ madā̍ya

## Verse 8

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 9

kā te̱ niṣa̍tti̱: kimu̱ no ma̍matsi̱ kiṃ nodu̍du harṣase̱ dāta̱vā u̍

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 10
