# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 41

**11 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| indrāvaruṇau| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 1

indrā̱ ko vā̍ṃ varuṇā su̱mnamā̍pa̱ stomo̍ ha̱viṣmā̍m̐ a̱mṛto̱ na hotā̍ |
yo vā̍ṃ hṛ̱di kratu̍mām̐ a̱smadu̱ktaḥ pa̱sparśa̍dindrāvaruṇā̱ nama̍svān

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 2

indrā̍ ha̱ yo varu̍ṇā ca̱kra ā̱pī de̱vau marta̍: sa̱khyāya̱ praya̍svān |

sa ha̍nti vṛ̱trā sa̍mi̱theṣu̱ śatrū̱navo̍bhirvā ma̱hadbhi̱: sa pra śṛ̍ṇve

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 3

indrā̍ ha̱ ratna̱ṃ varu̍ṇā̱ dheṣṭhe̱tthā nṛbhya̍: śaśamā̱nebhya̱stā |

yadī̱ sakhā̍yā sa̱khyāya̱ somai̍: su̱tebhi̍: supra̱yasā̍ mā̱dayai̍te

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 4

indrā̍ yu̱vaṃ va̍ruṇā di̱dyuma̍smi̱nnoji̍ṣṭhamugrā̱ ni va̍dhiṣṭa̱ṃ vajra̍m |

yo no̍ du̱revo̍ vṛ̱kati̍rda̱bhīti̱stasmi̍n mimāthāma̱bhibhū̱tyoja̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 5

indrā̍ yu̱vaṃ va̍ruṇā bhū̱tama̱syā dhi̱yaḥ pre̱tārā̍ vṛṣa̱bheva̍ dhe̱noḥ |

sā no̍ duhīya̱d yava̍seva ga̱tvī sa̱hasra̍dhārā̱ paya̍sā ma̱hī gauḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 6

to̱ke hi̱te tana̍ya u̱rvarā̍su̱ sūro̱ dṛśī̍ke̱ vṛṣa̍ṇaśca̱ pauṃsye̍ |

indrā̍ no̱ atra̱ varu̍ṇā syātā̱mavo̍bhirda̱smā pari̍takmyāyām

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 7

yu̱vāmiddhyava̍se pū̱rvyāya̱ pari̱ prabhū̍tī ga̱viṣa̍: svāpī |

vṛ̱ṇī̱mahe̍ sa̱khyāya̍ pri̱yāya̱ śūrā̱ maṃhi̍ṣṭhā pi̱tare̍va śa̱mbhū

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 8

tā vā̱ṃ dhiyo'va̍se vāja̱yantī̍rā̱jiṃ na ja̍gmuryuva̱yūḥ su̍dānū |

śri̱ye na gāva̱ upa̱ soma̍masthu̱rindra̱ṃ giro̱ varu̍ṇaṃ me manī̱ṣāḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 9

i̱mā indra̱ṃ varu̍ṇaṃ me manī̱ṣā agma̱nnupa̱ dravi̍ṇami̱cchamā̍nāḥ |

upe̍masthurjo̱ṣṭāra̍ iva̱ vasvo̍ ra̱ghvīri̍va̱ śrava̍so̱ bhikṣa̍māṇāḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 10

aśvya̍sya̱ tmanā̱ rathya̍sya pu̱ṣṭernitya̍sya rā̱yaḥ pata̍yaḥ syāma |

tā ca̍krā̱ṇā ū̱tibhi̱rnavya̍sībhirasma̱trā rāyo̍ ni̱yuta̍: sacantām

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 11

ā no̍ bṛhantā bṛha̱tībhi̍rū̱tī indra̍ yā̱taṃ va̍ruṇa̱ vāja̍sātau |

yad di̱dyava̱: pṛta̍nāsu pra̱krīl̤ā̱n tasya̍ vāṃ syāma sani̱tāra̍ ā̱jeḥ
