# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 38

**10 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| dadhikrā, dyāvāpṛthivī| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 1

u̱to hi vā̍ṃ dā̱trā santi̱ pūrvā̱ yā pū̱rubhya̍stra̱sada̍syurnito̱śe |
kṣe̱trā̱sāṃ da̍dathururvarā̱sāṃ gha̱naṃ dasyu̍bhyo a̱bhibhū̍timu̱gram

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 2

u̱ta vā̱jina̍ṃ puruni̱ṣṣidhvā̍naṃ dadhi̱krāmu̍ dadathurvi̱śvakṛ̍ṣṭim |

ṛ̱ji̱pyaṃ śye̱naṃ pru̍ṣi̱tapsu̍mā̱śuṃ ca̱rkṛtya̍ma̱ryo nṛ̱pati̱ṃ na śūra̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 3

yaṃ sī̱manu̍ pra̱vate̍va̱ drava̍nta̱ṃ viśva̍: pū̱rurmada̍ti̱ harṣa̍māṇaḥ |

pa̱ḍbhirgṛdhya̍ntaṃ medha̱yuṃ na śūra̍ṃ ratha̱tura̱ṃ vāta̍miva̱ dhraja̍ntam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 4

yaḥ smā̍rundhā̱no gadhyā̍ sa̱matsu̱ sanu̍tara̱ścara̍ti̱ goṣu̱ gaccha̍n |

ā̱virṛ̍jīko vi̱dathā̍ ni̱cikya̍t tiro a̍ra̱tiṃ paryāpa̍ ā̱yoḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 5

u̱ta smai̍naṃ vastra̱mathi̱ṃ na tā̱yumanu̍ krośanti kṣi̱tayo̱ bhare̍ṣu |

nī̱cāya̍māna̱ṃ jasu̍ri̱ṃ na śye̱naṃ śrava̱ścācchā̍ paśu̱macca̍ yū̱tham

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 6

u̱ta smā̍su pratha̱maḥ sa̍ri̱ṣyan ni ve̍veti̱ śreṇi̍bhī̱ rathā̍nām |

sraja̍ṃ kṛṇvā̱no janyo̱ na śubhvā̍ re̱ṇuṃ reri̍hat ki̱raṇa̍ṃ dada̱śvān

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 7

u̱ta sya vā̱jī sahu̍rirṛ̱tāvā̱ śuśrū̍ṣamāṇasta̱nvā̍ sama̱rye |

tura̍ṃ ya̱tīṣu̍ tu̱raya̍nnṛji̱pyo'dhi̍ bhru̱voḥ ki̍rate re̱ṇumṛ̱ñjan

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 8

u̱ta smā̍sya tanya̱tori̍va̱ dyorṛ̍ghāya̱to a̍bhi̱yujo̍ bhayante |

ya̱dā sa̱hasra̍ma̱bhi ṣī̱mayo̍dhīd du̱rvartu̍: smā bhavati bhī̱ma ṛ̱ñjan

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 9

u̱ta smā̍sya panayanti̱ janā̍ jū̱tiṃ kṛ̍ṣṭi̱pro a̱bhibhū̍timā̱śoḥ |

u̱taina̍māhuḥ sami̱the vi̱yanta̱: parā̍ dadhi̱krā a̍sarat sa̱hasrai̍:

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 10

ā da̍dhi̱krāḥ śava̍sā̱ pañca̍ kṛ̱ṣṭīḥ sūrya̍ iva̱ jyoti̍ṣā̱pasta̍tāna |

sa̱ha̱sra̱sāḥ śa̍ta̱sā vā̱jyarvā̍ pṛ̱ṇaktu̱ madhvā̱ sami̱mā vacā̍ṃsi
