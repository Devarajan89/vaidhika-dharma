# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 26

**7 vāmadevo gautamaḥ, 1-3 indro vā| 1-3 indraḥ, ātmā vā, 4-7 śyenaḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 1

a̱haṃ manu̍rabhava̱ṃ sūrya̍ścā̱haṃ ka̱kṣīvā̱m̐ ṛṣi̍rasmi̱ vipra̍: |
a̱haṃ kutsa̍mārjune̱yaṃ nyṛ̍ñje̱'haṃ ka̱viru̱śanā̱ paśya̍tā mā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 2

a̱hama̱po a̍nayaṃ vāvaśā̱nā mama̍ de̱vāso̱ anu̱ keta̍māyan

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 3

śa̱ta̱ta̱maṃ ve̱śya̍ṃ sa̱rvatā̍tā̱ divo̍dāsamatithi̱gvaṃ yadāva̍m

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 4

a̱ca̱krayā̱ yat sva̱dhayā̍ supa̱rṇo ha̱vyaṃ bhara̱nmana̍ve de̱vaju̍ṣṭam
