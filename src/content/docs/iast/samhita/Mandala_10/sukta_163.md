# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 163

**6 vivṛhā kāśyapaḥ| yakṣmanāśanam| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 1

a̱kṣībhyā̍ṃ te̱ nāsi̍kābhyā̱ṃ karṇā̍bhyā̱ṃ chubu̍kā̱dadhi̍ | yakṣma̍ṃ śīrṣa̱ṇya̍ṃ ma̱stiṣkā̍jji̱hvāyā̱ vi vṛ̍hāmi te

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 2

grī̱vābhya̍sta u̱ṣṇihā̍bhya̱: kīka̍sābhyo anū̱kyā̍t | yakṣma̍ṃ doṣa̱ṇya1maṃsā̍bhyāṃ bā̱hubhyā̱ṃ vi vṛ̍hāmi te

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 3

ā̱ntrebhya̍ste̱ gudā̍bhyo vani̱ṣṭhorhṛda̍yā̱dadhi̍ | yakṣma̱ṃ mata̍snābhyāṃ ya̱knaḥ plā̱śibhyo̱ vi vṛ̍hāmi te

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 4

ū̱rubhyā̍ṃ te aṣṭhī̱vadbhyā̱ṃ pārṣṇi̍bhyā̱ṃ prapa̍dābhyām | yakṣma̱ṃ śroṇi̍bhyā̱ṃ bhāsa̍dā̱dbhaṃsa̍so̱ vi vṛ̍hāmi te

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 5

meha̍nādvana̱ṃkara̍ṇā̱lloma̍bhyaste na̱khebhya̍: | yakṣma̱ṃ sarva̍smādā̱tmana̱stami̱daṃ vi vṛ̍hāmi te

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 6

aṅgā̍daṅgā̱llomno̍lomno jā̱taṃ parva̍ṇiparvaṇi | yakṣma̱ṃ sarva̍smādā̱tmana̱stami̱daṃ vi vṛ̍hāmi te
