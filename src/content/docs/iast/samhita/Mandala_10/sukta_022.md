# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 22

**15 aindro vimadaḥ prājāpatyo vā, vāsukro vasukṛdvā| indraḥ| purastādbṛhatī 5,7,9 anuṣṭup, 15 triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 1

kuha̍ śru̱ta indra̱: kasmi̍nna̱dya jane̍ mi̱tro na śrū̍yate | ṛṣī̍ṇāṃ vā̱ yaḥ kṣaye̱ guhā̍ vā̱ carkṛ̍ṣe gi̱rā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 2

i̱ha śru̱ta indro̍ a̱sme a̱dya stave̍ va̱jryṛcī̍ṣamaḥ | mi̱tro na yo jane̱ṣvā yaśa̍śca̱kre asā̱myā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 3

ma̱ho yaspati̱: śava̍so̱ asā̱myā ma̱ho nṛ̱mṇasya̍ tūtu̱jiḥ | bha̱rtā vajra̍sya dhṛ̱ṣṇoḥ pi̱tā pu̱trami̍va pri̱yam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 4

yu̱jā̱no aśvā̱ vāta̍sya̱ dhunī̍ de̱vo de̱vasya̍ vajrivaḥ | syantā̍ pa̱thā vi̱rukma̍tā sṛjā̱naḥ sto̱ṣyadhva̍naḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 5

tvaṃ tyā ci̱dvāta̱syāśvāgā̍ ṛ̱jrā tmanā̱ vaha̍dhyai | yayo̍rde̱vo na martyo̍ ya̱ntā naki̍rvi̱dāyya̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 6

adha̱ gmanto̱śanā̍ pṛcchate vā̱ṃ kada̍rthā na̱ ā gṛ̱ham | ā ja̍gmathuḥ parā̱kāddi̱vaśca̱ gmaśca̱ martya̍m

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 7

ā na̍ indra pṛkṣase̱'smāka̱ṃ brahmodya̍tam | tattvā̍ yācāma̱he'va̱: śuṣṇa̱ṃ yaddhannamā̍nuṣam

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 8

a̱ka̱rmā dasyu̍ra̱bhi no̍ ama̱ntura̱nyavra̍to̱ amā̍nuṣaḥ | tvaṃ tasyā̍mitraha̱nvadha̍rdā̱sasya̍ dambhaya

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 9

tvaṃ na̍ indra śūra̱ śūrai̍ru̱ta tvotā̍so ba̱rhaṇā̍ | pu̱ru̱trā te̱ vi pū̱rtayo̱ nava̍nta kṣo̱ṇayo̍ yathā

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 10

tvaṃ tānvṛ̍tra̱hatye̍ codayo̱ nṝnkā̍rpā̱ṇe śū̍ra vajrivaḥ | guhā̱ yadī̍ kavī̱nāṃ vi̱śāṃ nakṣa̍traśavasām

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 11

ma̱kṣū tā ta̍ indra dā̱nāpna̍sa ākṣā̱ṇe śū̍ra vajrivaḥ | yaddha̱ śuṣṇa̍sya da̱mbhayo̍ jā̱taṃ viśva̍ṃ sa̱yāva̍bhiḥ
