# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 57

**6 bandhu: śrutabandhurviprabandhurgaupāyanāḥ|viśvedevāḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 1

mā pra gā̍ma pa̱tho va̱yaṃ mā ya̱jñādi̍ndra so̱mina̍: | mānta sthu̍rno̱ arā̍tayaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 2

yo ya̱jñasya̍ pra̱sādha̍na̱stantu̍rde̱veṣvāta̍taḥ | tamāhu̍taṃ naśīmahi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 3

mano̱ nvā hu̍vāmahe nārāśa̱ṃsena̱ some̍na | pi̱tṝ̱ṇāṃ ca̱ manma̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 4

ā ta̍ etu̱ mana̱: puna̱: kratve̱ dakṣā̍ya jī̱vase̍ | jyokca̱ sūrya̍ṃ dṛ̱śe

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 5

puna̍rnaḥ pitaro̱ mano̱ dadā̍tu̱ daivyo̱ jana̍: | jī̱vaṃ vrāta̍ṃ sacemahi

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 6

va̱yaṃ so̍ma vra̱te tava̱ mana̍sta̱nūṣu̱ bibhra̍taḥ | pra̱jāva̍ntaḥ sacemahi
