# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 35

**14 luśo dhānākaḥ| viśvedevāḥ | jagatī,13-14 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 1

abu̍dhramu̱ tya indra̍vanto a̱gnayo̱ jyoti̱rbhara̍nta u̱ṣaso̱ vyu̍ṣṭiṣu |
ma̱hī dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī ce̍tatā̱mapo̱'dyā de̱vānā̱mava̱ ā vṛ̍ṇīmahe

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 2

di̱vaspṛ̍thi̱vyorava̱ ā vṛ̍ṇīmahe mā̱tṝntsindhū̱nparva̍tāñcharya̱ṇāva̍taḥ |

a̱nā̱gā̱stvaṃ sūrya̍mu̱ṣāsa̍mīmahe bha̱draṃ soma̍: suvā̱no a̱dyā kṛ̍ṇotu naḥ

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 3

u̱ṣā u̱cchantyapa̍ bādhatāma̱ghaṃ sva̱stya1gniṃ sa̍midhā̱namī̍mahe

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 4

ā̱re ma̱nyuṃ du̍rvi̱datra̍sya dhīmahi sva̱stya1gniṃ sa̍midhā̱namī̍mahe
