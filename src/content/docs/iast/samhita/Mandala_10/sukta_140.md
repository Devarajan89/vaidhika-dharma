# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 140

**6 agniḥ pāvakaḥ| agniḥ| satobṛhatī, 1-2 viṣṭārapaṃktiḥ, 6 upariṣṭājjyotiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 140 | **Verse:** 1

agne̱ tava̱ śravo̱ vayo̱ mahi̍ bhrājante a̱rcayo̍ vibhāvaso |
bṛha̍dbhāno̱ śava̍sā̱ vāja̍mu̱kthyaṃ1 dadhā̍si dā̱śuṣe̍ kave

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 140 | **Verse:** 2

pā̱va̱kava̍rcāḥ śu̱krava̍rcā̱ anū̍navarcā̱ udi̍yarṣi bhā̱nunā̍ |

pu̱tro mā̱tarā̍ vi̱cara̱nnupā̍vasi pṛ̱ṇakṣi̱ roda̍sī u̱bhe

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 140 | **Verse:** 3

ūrjo̍ napājjātavedaḥ suśa̱stibhi̱rmanda̍sva dhī̱tibhi̍rhi̱taḥ |

tve iṣa̱: saṃ da̍dhu̱rbhūri̍varpasaści̱trota̍yo vā̱majā̍tāḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 140 | **Verse:** 4

i̱ra̱jyanna̍gne prathayasva ja̱ntubhi̍ra̱sme rāyo̍ amartya |

sa da̍rśa̱tasya̱ vapu̍ṣo̱ vi rā̍jasi pṛ̱ṇakṣi̍ sāna̱siṃ kratu̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 140 | **Verse:** 5

i̱ṣka̱rtāra̍madhva̱rasya̱ prace̍tasa̱ṃ kṣaya̍nta̱ṃ rādha̍so ma̱haḥ |

rā̱tiṃ vā̱masya̍ su̱bhagā̍ṃ ma̱hīmiṣa̱ṃ dadhā̍si sāna̱siṃ ra̱yim

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 140 | **Verse:** 6
