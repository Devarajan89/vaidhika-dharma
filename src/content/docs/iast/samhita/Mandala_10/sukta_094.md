# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 94

**14 arbudaḥ kādraveyaḥ sarpaḥ| grāvāṇaḥ| jagatī, 5, 7, 14 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 1

praite va̍dantu̱ pra va̱yaṃ va̍dāma̱ grāva̍bhyo̱ vāca̍ṃ vadatā̱ vada̍dbhyaḥ |
yada̍drayaḥ parvatāḥ sā̱kamā̱śava̱: śloka̱ṃ ghoṣa̱ṃ bhara̱thendrā̍ya so̱mina̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 2

e̱te va̍danti śa̱tava̍tsa̱hasra̍vada̱bhi kra̍ndanti̱ hari̍tebhirā̱sabhi̍: |

vi̱ṣṭvī grāvā̍ṇaḥ su̱kṛta̍: sukṛ̱tyayā̱ hotu̍ści̱tpūrve̍ havi̱radya̍māśata

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 3

e̱te va̍da̱ntyavi̍danna̱nā madhu̱ nyū̍ṅkhayante̱ adhi̍ pa̱kva āmi̍ṣi |

vṛ̱kṣasya̱ śākhā̍maru̱ṇasya̱ bapsa̍ta̱ste sūbha̍rvā vṛṣa̱bhāḥ prema̍rāviṣuḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 4

bṛ̱hadva̍danti madi̱reṇa̍ ma̱ndinendra̱ṃ krośa̍nto'vidanna̱nā madhu̍ |

sa̱ṃrabhyā̱ dhīrā̱: svasṛ̍bhiranartiṣurāgho̱ṣaya̍ntaḥ pṛthi̱vīmu̍pa̱bdibhi̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 5

su̱pa̱rṇā vāca̍makra̱topa̱ dyavyā̍kha̱re kṛṣṇā̍ iṣi̱rā a̍nartiṣuḥ |

nya1ṅni ya̱ntyupa̍rasya niṣkṛ̱taṃ pu̱rū reto̍ dadhire sūrya̱śvita̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 6

u̱grā i̍va pra̱vaha̍ntaḥ sa̱māya̍muḥ sā̱kaṃ yu̱ktā vṛṣa̍ṇo̱ bibhra̍to̱ dhura̍: |

yacchva̱santo̍ jagrasā̱nā arā̍viṣuḥ śṛ̱ṇva e̍ṣāṃ pro̱thatho̱ arva̍tāmiva

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 7

daśā̍vanibhyo̱ daśa̍kakṣyebhyo̱ daśa̍yoktrebhyo̱ daśa̍yojanebhyaḥ |

daśā̍bhīśubhyo arcatā̱jare̍bhyo̱ daśa̱ dhuro̱ daśa̍ yu̱ktā vaha̍dbhyaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 8

ta ū̍ su̱tasya̍ so̱myasyāndha̍so̱ṃ'śoḥ pī̱yūṣa̍ṃ pratha̱masya̍ bhejire

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 9

tebhi̍rdu̱gdhaṃ pa̍pi̱vāntso̱myaṃ madhvindro̍ vardhate̱ pratha̍te vṛṣā̱yate̍

## Verse 13
