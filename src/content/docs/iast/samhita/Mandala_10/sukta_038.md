# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 38

**5 muṣkavānindraḥ| indraḥ| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 1

a̱sminna̍ indra pṛtsu̱tau yaśa̍svati̱ śimī̍vati̱ kranda̍si̱ prāva̍ sā̱taye̍ |
yatra̱ goṣā̍tā dhṛṣi̱teṣu̍ khā̱diṣu̱ viṣva̱kpata̍nti di̱dyavo̍ nṛ̱ṣāhye̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 2

sa na̍: kṣu̱manta̱ṃ sada̍ne̱ vyū̍rṇuhi̱ goa̍rṇasaṃ ra̱yimi̍ndra śra̱vāyya̍m |

syāma̍ te̱ jaya̍taḥ śakra me̱dino̱ yathā̍ va̱yamu̱śmasi̱ tadva̍so kṛdhi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 3

yo no̱ dāsa̱ āryo̍ vā puruṣṭu̱tā'de̍va indra yu̱dhaye̱ cike̍tati |

a̱smābhi̍ṣṭe su̱ṣahā̍: santu̱ śatra̍va̱stvayā̍ va̱yaṃ tānva̍nuyāma saṃga̱me

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 4

yo da̱bhrebhi̱rhavyo̱ yaśca̱ bhūri̍bhi̱ryo a̱bhīke̍ varivo̱vinnṛ̱ṣāhye̍ |

taṃ vi̍khā̱de sasni̍ma̱dya śru̱taṃ nara̍ma̱rvāñca̱mindra̱mava̍se karāmahe

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 5

sva̱vṛja̱ṃ hi tvāma̱hami̍ndra śu̱śravā̍nānu̱daṃ vṛ̍ṣabha radhra̱coda̍nam |

pra mu̍ñcasva̱ pari̱ kutsā̍di̱hā ga̍hi̱ kimu̱ tvāvā̍nmu̱ṣkayo̍rba̱ddha ā̍sate
