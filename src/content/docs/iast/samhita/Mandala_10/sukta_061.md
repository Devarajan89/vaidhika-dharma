# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 61

**27 nābhānediṣṭho  mānavaḥ| viśve devāḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 1

i̱dami̱tthā raudra̍ṃ gū̱rtava̍cā̱ brahma̱ kratvā̱ śacyā̍ma̱ntarā̱jau |
krā̱ṇā yada̍sya pi̱tarā̍ maṃhane̱ṣṭhāḥ parṣa̍tpa̱kthe aha̱nnā sa̱pta hotṝ̍n
