# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 49

**11 vaikuṇṭha indraḥ| indraḥ| jagatīḥ, 2-11 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 1

a̱haṃ dā̍ṃ gṛṇa̱te pūrvya̱ṃ vasva̱haṃ brahma̍ kṛṇava̱ṃ mahya̱ṃ vardha̍nam |
a̱haṃ bhu̍va̱ṃ yaja̍mānasya codi̱tāya̍jvanaḥ sākṣi̱ viśva̍smi̱nbhare̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 2

māṃ dhu̱rindra̱ṃ nāma̍ de̱vatā̍ di̱vaśca̱ gmaścā̱pāṃ ca̍ ja̱ntava̍: |

a̱haṃ harī̱ vṛṣa̍ṇā̱ vivra̍tā ra̱ghū a̱haṃ vajra̱ṃ śava̍se dhṛ̱ṣṇvā da̍de

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 3

a̱hamatka̍ṃ ka̱vaye̍ śiśnatha̱ṃ hathai̍ra̱haṃ kutsa̍māvamā̱bhirū̱tibhi̍: |

a̱haṃ śuṣṇa̍sya̱ śnathi̍tā̱ vadha̍ryama̱ṃ na yo ra̱ra ārya̱ṃ nāma̱ dasya̍ve

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 4

a̱haṃ pi̱teva̍ veta̱sūm̐ra̱bhiṣṭa̍ye̱ tugra̱ṃ kutsā̍ya̱ smadi̍bhaṃ ca randhayam |

a̱haṃ bhu̍va̱ṃ yaja̍mānasya rā̱jani̱ pra yadbhare̱ tuja̍ye̱ na pri̱yādhṛṣe̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 5

a̱haṃ ra̍ndhaya̱ṃ mṛga̍yaṃ śru̱tarva̍ṇe̱ yanmāji̍hīta va̱yunā̍ ca̱nānu̱ṣak |

a̱haṃ ve̱śaṃ na̱mramā̱yave̍'karama̱haṃ savyā̍ya̱ paḍgṛ̍bhimarandhayam

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 6

a̱haṃ sa yo nava̍vāstvaṃ bṛ̱hadra̍tha̱ṃ saṃ vṛ̱treva̱ dāsa̍ṃ vṛtra̱hāru̍jam |

yadva̱rdhaya̍ntaṃ pra̱thaya̍ntamānu̱ṣagdū̱re pā̱re raja̍so roca̱nāka̍ram

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 7

a̱haṃ sūrya̍sya̱ pari̍ yāmyā̱śubhi̱: praita̱śebhi̱rvaha̍māna̱ oja̍sā |

yanmā̍ sā̱vo manu̍ṣa̱ āha̍ ni̱rṇija̱ ṛdha̍kkṛṣe̱ dāsa̱ṃ kṛtvya̱ṃ hathai̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 8

a̱haṃ sa̍pta̱hā nahu̍ṣo̱ nahu̍ṣṭara̱: prāśrā̍vaya̱ṃ śava̍sā tu̱rvaśa̱ṃ yadu̍m |

a̱haṃ nya1nyaṃ saha̍sā̱ saha̍skara̱ṃ nava̱ vrādha̍to nava̱tiṃ ca̍ vakṣayam

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 9

a̱haṃ sa̱pta sra̱vato̍ dhāraya̱ṃ vṛṣā̍ dravi̱tnva̍: pṛthi̱vyāṃ sī̱rā adhi̍ |

a̱hamarṇā̍ṃsi̱ vi ti̍rāmi su̱kratu̍ryu̱dhā vi̍da̱ṃ mana̍ve gā̱tumi̱ṣṭaye̍

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 10

a̱haṃ tadā̍su dhāraya̱ṃ yadā̍su̱ na de̱vaśca̱na tvaṣṭādhā̍raya̱druśa̍t |

spā̱rhaṃ gavā̱mūdha̍ssu va̱kṣaṇā̱svā madho̱rmadhu̱ śvātrya̱ṃ soma̍mā̱śira̍m

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 11

e̱vā de̱vām̐ indro̍ vivye̱ nṝnpra cyau̱tnena̍ ma̱ghavā̍ sa̱tyarā̍dhāḥ |

viśvettā te̍ harivaḥ śacīvo̱'bhi tu̱rāsa̍: svayaśo gṛṇanti
