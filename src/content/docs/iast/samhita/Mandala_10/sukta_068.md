# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 68

**12 ayāsya āṃgirasaḥ| bṛhaspati:| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 1

u̱da̱pruto̱ na vayo̱ rakṣa̍māṇā̱ vāva̍dato a̱bhriya̍syeva̱ ghoṣā̍: |
gi̱ri̱bhrajo̱ normayo̱ mada̍nto̱ bṛha̱spati̍ma̱bhya1rkā a̍nāvan

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 2

saṃ gobhi̍rāṅgira̱so nakṣa̍māṇo̱ bhaga̍ i̱veda̍rya̱maṇa̍ṃ nināya |

jane̍ mi̱tro na dampa̍tī anakti̱ bṛha̍spate vā̱jayā̱śūm̐ri̍vā̱jau

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 3

sā̱dhva̱ryā a̍ti̱thinī̍riṣi̱rā: spā̱rhāḥ su̱varṇā̍ anava̱dyarū̍pāḥ |

bṛha̱spati̱: parva̍tebhyo vi̱tūryā̱ nirgā ū̍pe̱ yava̍miva sthi̱vibhya̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 4

ā̱pru̱ṣā̱yanmadhu̍na ṛ̱tasya̱ yoni̍mavakṣi̱panna̱rka u̱lkāmi̍va̱ dyoḥ |

bṛha̱spati̍ru̱ddhara̱nnaśma̍no̱ gā bhūmyā̍ u̱dneva̱ vi tvaca̍ṃ bibheda

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 5

apa̱ jyoti̍ṣā̱ tamo̍ a̱ntari̍kṣādu̱dnaḥ śīpā̍lamiva̱ vāta̍ ājat |

bṛha̱spati̍ranu̱mṛśyā̍ va̱lasyā̱'bhrami̍va̱ vāta̱ ā ca̍kra̱ ā gāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 6

ya̱dā va̱lasya̱ pīya̍to̱ jasu̱ṃ bhedbṛha̱spati̍ragni̱tapo̍bhira̱rkaiḥ |

da̱dbhirna ji̱hvā pari̍viṣṭa̱māda̍dā̱virni̱dhīm̐ra̍kṛṇodu̱sriyā̍ṇām

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 7

bṛha̱spati̱rama̍ta̱ hi tyadā̍sā̱ṃ nāma̍ sva̱rīṇā̱ṃ sada̍ne̱ guhā̱ yat |

ā̱ṇḍeva̍ bhi̱ttvā śa̍ku̱nasya̱ garbha̱mudu̱sriyā̱: parva̍tasya̱ tmanā̍jat

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 8

aśnāpi̍naddha̱ṃ madhu̱ parya̍paśya̱nmatsya̱ṃ na dī̱na u̱dani̍ kṣi̱yanta̍m |

niṣṭajja̍bhāra cama̱saṃ na vṛ̱kṣādbṛha̱spati̍rvira̱veṇā̍ vi̱kṛtya̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 9

soṣāma̍vinda̱tsa sva1: so a̱gniṃ so a̱rkeṇa̱ vi ba̍bādhe̱ tamā̍ṃsi |

bṛha̱spati̱rgova̍puṣo va̱lasya̱ nirma̱jjāna̱ṃ na parva̍ṇo jabhāra

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 10

hi̱meva̍ pa̱rṇā mu̍ṣi̱tā vanā̍ni̱ bṛha̱spati̍nākṛpayadva̱lo gāḥ |

a̱nā̱nu̱kṛ̱tyama̍pu̱naśca̍kāra̱ yātsūryā̱māsā̍ mi̱tha u̱ccarā̍taḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 11

a̱bhi śyā̱vaṃ na kṛśa̍nebhi̱raśva̱ṃ nakṣa̍trebhiḥ pi̱taro̱ dyāma̍piṃśan |

rātryā̱ṃ tamo̱ ada̍dhu̱rjyoti̱raha̱nbṛha̱spati̍rbhi̱nadadri̍ṃ vi̱dadgāḥ

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 12

i̱dama̍karma̱ namo̍ abhri̱yāya̱ yaḥ pū̱rvīranvā̱nona̍vīti |

bṛha̱spati̱: sa hi gobhi̱: so aśvai̱: sa vī̱rebhi̱: sa nṛbhi̍rno̱ vayo̍ dhāt
