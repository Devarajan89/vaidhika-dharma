# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 33

**9kavaṣa ailūṣaḥ| 1 viśvedevāḥ, 2-3 indraḥ, 4-5 kuruśravaṇastrāsadasyavaḥ, 6-9 upamaśravā maitrātithiḥ| 1 triṣṭup , pragāthaḥ = ( 2 bṛhatī, 3 satobṛhatī ),4-9 gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 1

pra mā̍ yuyujre pra̱yujo̱ janā̍nā̱ṃ vahā̍mi sma pū̱ṣaṇa̱manta̍reṇa |
viśve̍ de̱vāso̱ adha̱ māma̍rakṣandu̱:śāsu̱rāgā̱diti̱ ghoṣa̍ āsīt

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 2

saṃ mā̍ tapantya̱bhita̍: sa̱patnī̍riva̱ parśa̍vaḥ | ni bā̍dhate̱ ama̍tirna̱gnatā̱ jasu̱rverna ve̍vīyate ma̱tiḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 3

mūṣo̱ na śi̱śnā vya̍danti mā̱dhya̍ sto̱tāra̍ṃ te śatakrato | sa̱kṛtsu no̍ maghavannindra mṛl̤a̱yā'dhā̍ pi̱teva̍ no bhava

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 4

ku̱ru̱śrava̍ṇamāvṛṇi̱ rājā̍na̱ṃ trāsa̍dasyavam | maṃhi̍ṣṭhaṃ vā̱ghatā̱mṛṣi̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 5

yasya̍ mā ha̱rito̱ rathe̍ ti̱sro vaha̍nti sādhu̱yā | stavai̍ sa̱hasra̍dakṣiṇe

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 6

yasya̱ prasvā̍daso̱ gira̍ upa̱maśra̍vasaḥ pi̱tuḥ | kṣetra̱ṃ na ra̱ṇvamū̱cuṣe̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 7

adhi̍ putropamaśravo̱ napā̍nmitrātitherihi | pi̱tuṣṭe̍ asmi vandi̱tā

## Verse 8

## Verse 9
