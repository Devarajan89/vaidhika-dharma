# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 115

**9 vārṣṭihavya upastutaḥ| agniḥ|jagatī, 8 triṣṭup, 9 śakkarī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 115 | **Verse:** 1

ci̱tra icchiśo̱staru̍ṇasya va̱kṣatho̱ na yo mā̱tarā̍va̱pyeti̱ dhāta̍ve |
a̱nū̱dhā yadi̱ jīja̍na̱dadhā̍ ca̱ nu va̱vakṣa̍ sa̱dyo mahi̍ dū̱tyaṃ1 cara̍n

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 115 | **Verse:** 2

a̱gnirha̱ nāma̍ dhāyi̱ danna̱pasta̍ma̱: saṃ yo vanā̍ yu̱vate̱ bhasma̍nā da̱tā |

a̱bhi̱pra̱murā̍ ju̱hvā̍ svadhva̱ra i̱no na protha̍māno̱ yava̍se̱ vṛṣā̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 115 | **Verse:** 3

taṃ vo̱ viṃ na dru̱ṣada̍ṃ de̱vamandha̍sa̱ indu̱ṃ protha̍ntaṃ pra̱vapa̍ntamarṇa̱vam |

ā̱sā vahni̱ṃ na śo̱ciṣā̍ vira̱pśina̱ṃ mahi̍vrata̱ṃ na sa̱raja̍nta̱madhva̍naḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 115 | **Verse:** 4

vi yasya̍ te jrayasā̱nasyā̍jara̱ dhakṣo̱rna vātā̱: pari̱ santyacyu̍tāḥ |

ā ra̱ṇvāso̱ yuyu̍dhayo̱ na sa̍tva̱naṃ tri̱taṃ na̍śanta̱ pra śi̱ṣanta̍ i̱ṣṭaye̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 115 | **Verse:** 5

sa ida̱gniḥ kaṇva̍tama̱: kaṇva̍sakhā̱'ryaḥ para̱syānta̍rasya̱ taru̍ṣaḥ |

a̱gniḥ pā̍tu gṛṇa̱to a̱gniḥ sū̱rīna̱gnirda̍dātu̱ teṣā̱mavo̍ naḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 115 | **Verse:** 6

vā̱jinta̍māya̱ sahya̍se supitrya tṛ̱ṣu cyavā̍no̱ anu̍ jā̱tave̍dase |

a̱nu̱dre ci̱dyo dhṛ̍ṣa̱tā vara̍ṃ sa̱te ma̱hinta̍māya̱ dhanva̱neda̍viṣya̱te

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 115 | **Verse:** 7

e̱vāgnirmartai̍: sa̱ha sū̱ribhi̱rvasu̍: ṣṭave̱ saha̍saḥ sū̱naro̱ nṛbhi̍: |

mi̱trāso̱ na ye sudhi̍tā ṛtā̱yavo̱ dyāvo̱ na dyu̱mnaira̱bhi santi̱ mānu̍ṣān

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 115 | **Verse:** 8

ūrjo̍ napātsahasāva̱nniti̍ tvopastu̱tasya̍ vandate̱ vṛṣā̱ vāk |

tvāṃ sto̍ṣāma̱ tvayā̍ su̱vīrā̱ drāghī̍ya̱ āyu̍: prata̱raṃ dadhā̍nāḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 115 | **Verse:** 9

iti̍ tvāgne vṛṣṭi̱havya̍sya pu̱trā u̍pastu̱tāsa̱ ṛṣa̍yo'vocan |

tām̐śca̍ pā̱hi gṛ̍ṇa̱taśca̍ sū̱rīnvaṣa̱ḍvaṣa̱l̤ityū̱rdhvāso̍ anakṣa̱nnamo̱ nama̱ ityū̱rdhvāso̍ anakṣan
