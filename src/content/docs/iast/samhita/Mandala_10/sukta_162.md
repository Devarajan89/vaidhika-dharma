# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 162

**6 brāhmo rakṣohā| rakṣohā| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 162 | **Verse:** 1

brahma̍ṇā̱gniḥ sa̍ṃvidā̱no ra̍kṣo̱hā bā̍dhatāmi̱taḥ | amī̍vā̱ yaste̱ garbha̍ṃ du̱rṇāmā̱ yoni̍mā̱śaye̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 162 | **Verse:** 2

yaste̱ garbha̱mamī̍vā du̱rṇāmā̱ yoni̍mā̱śaye̍ | a̱gniṣṭaṃ brahma̍ṇā sa̱ha niṣkra̱vyāda̍manīnaśat

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 162 | **Verse:** 3

yaste̱ hanti̍ pa̱taya̍ntaṃ niṣa̱tsnuṃ yaḥ sa̍rīsṛ̱pam | jā̱taṃ yaste̱ jighā̍ṃsati̱ tami̱to nā̍śayāmasi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 162 | **Verse:** 4

yasta̍ ū̱rū vi̱hara̍tyanta̱rā dampa̍tī̱ śaye̍ | yoni̱ṃ yo a̱ntarā̱rel̤hi̱ tami̱to nā̍śayāmasi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 162 | **Verse:** 5

yastvā̱ bhrātā̱ pati̍rbhū̱tvā jā̱ro bhū̱tvā ni̱padya̍te | pra̱jāṃ yaste̱ jighā̍ṃsati̱ tami̱to nā̍śayāmasi

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 162 | **Verse:** 6

yastvā̱ svapne̍na̱ tama̍sā mohayi̱tvā ni̱padya̍te | pra̱jāṃ yaste̱ jighā̍ṃsati̱ tami̱to nā̍śayāmasi
