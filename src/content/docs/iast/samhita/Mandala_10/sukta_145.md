# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 145

**6 indrāṇī|sapatnībādhanam (upaniṣat) | anuṣṭup , 6 paṃktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 145 | **Verse:** 1

i̱māṃ kha̍nā̱myoṣa̍dhiṃ vī̱rudha̱ṃ bala̍vattamām | yayā̍ sa̱patnī̱ṃ bādha̍te̱ yayā̍ saṃvi̱ndate̱ pati̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 145 | **Verse:** 2

uttā̍naparṇe̱ subha̍ge̱ deva̍jūte̱ saha̍svati | sa̱patnī̍ṃ me̱ parā̍ dhama̱ pati̍ṃ me̱ keva̍laṃ kuru

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 145 | **Verse:** 3

utta̍rā̱hamu̍ttara̱ utta̱redutta̍rābhyaḥ | athā̍ sa̱patnī̱ yā mamā'dha̍rā̱ sādha̍rābhyaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 145 | **Verse:** 4

na̱hya̍syā̱ nāma̍ gṛ̱bhṇāmi̱ no a̱sminra̍mate̱ jane̍ | parā̍me̱va pa̍rā̱vata̍ṃ sa̱patnī̍ṃ gamayāmasi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 145 | **Verse:** 5

a̱hama̍smi̱ saha̍mā̱nā'tha̱ tvama̍si sāsa̱hiḥ | u̱bhe saha̍svatī bhū̱tvī sa̱patnī̍ṃ me sahāvahai

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 145 | **Verse:** 6

upa̍ te'dhā̱ṃ saha̍mānāma̱bhi tvā̍dhā̱ṃ sahī̍yasā |

māmanu̱ pra te̱ mano̍ va̱tsaṃ gauri̍va dhāvatu pa̱thā vāri̍va dhāvatu
