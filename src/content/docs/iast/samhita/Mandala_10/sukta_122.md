# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 122

**8 citramahā vāsiṣṭhaḥ | agniḥ| jagatīḥ 1,5 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 1

vasu̱ṃ na ci̱trama̍hasaṃ gṛṇīṣe vā̱maṃ śeva̱mati̍thimadviṣe̱ṇyam |
sa rā̍sate śu̱rudho̍ vi̱śvadhā̍yaso̱'gnirhotā̍ gṛ̱hapa̍tiḥ su̱vīrya̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 2

ju̱ṣā̱ṇo a̍gne̱ prati̍ harya me̱ vaco̱ viśvā̍ni vi̱dvānva̱yunā̍ni sukrato |

ghṛta̍nirṇi̱gbrahma̍ṇe gā̱tumera̍ya̱ tava̍ de̱vā a̍janaya̱nnanu̍ vra̱tam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 3

sa̱pta dhāmā̍ni pari̱yannama̍rtyo̱ dāśa̍ddā̱śuṣe̍ su̱kṛte̍ māmahasva |

su̱vīre̍ṇa ra̱yiṇā̍gne svā̱bhuvā̱ yasta̱ āna̍ṭ sa̱midhā̱ taṃ ju̍ṣasva

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 4

ya̱jñasya̍ ke̱tuṃ pra̍tha̱maṃ pu̱rohi̍taṃ ha̱viṣma̍nta īl̤ate sa̱pta vā̱jina̍m |

śṛ̱ṇvanta̍ma̱gniṃ ghṛ̱tapṛ̍ṣṭhamu̱kṣaṇa̍ṃ pṛ̱ṇanta̍ṃ de̱vaṃ pṛ̍ṇa̱te su̱vīrya̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 5

tvaṃ dū̱taḥ pra̍tha̱mo vare̍ṇya̱: sa hū̱yamā̍no a̱mṛtā̍ya matsva |

tvāṃ ma̍rjayanma̱ruto̍ dā̱śuṣo̍ gṛ̱he tvāṃ stome̍bhi̱rbhṛga̍vo̱ vi ru̍rucuḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 6

iṣa̍ṃ du̱hantsu̱dughā̍ṃ vi̱śvadhā̍yasaṃ yajña̱priye̱ yaja̍mānāya sukrato |

agne̍ ghṛ̱tasnu̱striṝ̱tāni̱ dīdya̍dva̱rtirya̱jñaṃ pa̍ri̱yantsu̍kratūyase

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 7

tvāmida̱syā u̱ṣaso̱ vyu̍ṣṭiṣu dū̱taṃ kṛ̍ṇvā̱nā a̍yajanta̱ mānu̍ṣāḥ |

tvāṃ de̱vā ma̍ha̱yāyyā̍ya vāvṛdhu̱rājya̍magne nimṛ̱janto̍ adhva̱re

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 8

ni tvā̱ vasi̍ṣṭhā ahvanta vā̱jina̍ṃ gṛ̱ṇanto̍ agne vi̱dathe̍ṣu ve̱dhasa̍: |

rā̱yaspoṣa̱ṃ yaja̍māneṣu dhāraya yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
