# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 30

**15 kavaṣa ailūṣaḥ|āpaḥ, apāṃ na pāt vā| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 1

ma̱hīṃ mi̱trasya̱ varu̍ṇasya dhā̱siṃ pṛ̍thu̱jraya̍se rīradhā suvṛ̱ktim

## Verse 2
