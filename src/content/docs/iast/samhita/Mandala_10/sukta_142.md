# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 142

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 142 | **Verse:** 1

a̱yama̍gne jari̱tā tve a̍bhū̱dapi̱ saha̍saḥ sūno na̱hya1nyadastyāpya̍m |

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 142 | **Verse:** 2

## Verse 3
