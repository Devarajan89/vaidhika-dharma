# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 102

**12  mudgalo bhārmyaśvaḥ| drughaṇaḥ, indro vā| triṣṭup, 1,3, 12 bṛhatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 1

pra te̱ ratha̍ṃ mithū̱kṛta̱mindro̍'vatu dhṛṣṇu̱yā |
a̱sminnā̱jau pu̍ruhūta śra̱vāyye̍ dhanabha̱kṣeṣu̍ no'va

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 2

utsma̱ vāto̍ vahati̱ vāso̍'syā̱ adhi̍ratha̱ṃ yadaja̍yatsa̱hasra̍m |

ra̱thīra̍bhūnmudga̱lānī̱ gavi̍ṣṭau̱ bhare̍ kṛ̱taṃ vya̍cedindrase̱nā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 3

a̱ntarya̍ccha̱ jighā̍ṃsato̱ vajra̍mindrābhi̱dāsa̍taḥ |

dāsa̍sya vā maghava̱nnārya̍sya vā sanu̱tarya̍vayā va̱dham

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 4

u̱dno hra̱dama̍piba̱jjarhṛ̍ṣāṇa̱: kūṭa̍ṃ sma tṛ̱ṃhada̱bhimā̍timeti |

pra mu̱ṣkabhā̍ra̱: śrava̍ i̱cchamā̍no'ji̱raṃ bā̱hū a̍bhara̱tsiṣā̍san

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 5

nya̍krandayannupa̱yanta̍ ena̱mame̍hayanvṛṣa̱bhaṃ madhya̍ ā̱jeḥ |

tena̱ sūbha̍rvaṃ śa̱tava̍tsa̱hasra̱ṃ gavā̱ṃ mudga̍laḥ pra̱dhane̍ jigāya

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 6

ka̱karda̍ve vṛṣa̱bho yu̱kta ā̍sī̱davā̍vacī̱tsāra̍thirasya ke̱śī |

dudhe̍ryu̱ktasya̱ drava̍taḥ sa̱hāna̍sa ṛ̱cchanti̍ ṣmā ni̱ṣpado̍ mudga̱lānī̍m

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 7

u̱ta pra̱dhimuda̍hannasya vi̱dvānupā̍yuna̱gvaṃsa̍ga̱matra̱ śikṣa̍n |

indra̱ udā̍va̱tpati̱maghnyā̍nā̱mara̍ṃhata̱ padyā̍bhiḥ ka̱kudmā̍n

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 8

śu̱nama̍ṣṭrā̱vya̍caratkapa̱rdī va̍ra̱trāyā̱ṃ dārvā̱nahya̍mānaḥ |

nṛ̱mṇāni̍ kṛ̱ṇvanba̱have̱ janā̍ya̱ gāḥ pa̍spaśā̱nastavi̍ṣīradhatta

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 9

i̱maṃ taṃ pa̍śya vṛṣa̱bhasya̱ yuñja̱ṃ kāṣṭhā̍yā̱ madhye̍ drugha̱ṇaṃ śayā̍nam |

yena̍ ji̱gāya̍ śa̱tava̍tsa̱hasra̱ṃ gavā̱ṃ mudga̍laḥ pṛta̱nājye̍ṣu

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 10

ā̱re a̱ghā ko nvi1tthā da̍darśa̱ yaṃ yu̱ñjanti̱ tamvā sthā̍payanti |

nāsmai̱ tṛṇa̱ṃ noda̱kamā bha̍ra̱ntyutta̍ro dhu̱ro va̍hati pra̱dedi̍śat

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 11

pa̱ri̱vṛ̱kteva̍ pati̱vidya̍māna̱ṭ pīpyā̍nā̱ kūca̍kreṇeva si̱ñcan |

e̱ṣai̱ṣyā̍ cidra̱thyā̍ jayema suma̱ṅgala̱ṃ sina̍vadastu sā̱tam

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 12

tvaṃ viśva̍sya̱ jaga̍ta̱ścakṣu̍rindrāsi̱ cakṣu̍ṣaḥ | vṛṣā̱ yadā̱jiṃ vṛṣa̍ṇā̱ siṣā̍sasi co̱daya̱nvadhri̍ṇā yu̱jā
