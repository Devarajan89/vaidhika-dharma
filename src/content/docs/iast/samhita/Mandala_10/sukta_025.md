# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 25

**11 aindro vimadaḥ prājāpatyo vā, vāsukro vasukṛdvā| somaḥ| āstārapaṃktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 1

bha̱draṃ no̱ api̍ vātaya̱ mano̱ dakṣa̍mu̱ta kratu̍m |
adhā̍ te sa̱khye andha̍so̱ vi vo̱ made̱ raṇa̱ngāvo̱ na yava̍se̱ viva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 2

hṛ̱di̱spṛśa̍sta āsate̱ viśve̍ṣu soma̱ dhāma̍su |

adhā̱ kāmā̍ i̱me mama̱ vi vo̱ made̱ vi ti̍ṣṭhante vasū̱yavo̱ viva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 3

u̱ta vra̱tāni̍ soma te̱ prāhaṃ mi̍nāmi pā̱kyā̍ |

adhā̍ pi̱teva̍ sū̱nave̱ vi vo̱ made̍ mṛ̱l̤ā no̍ a̱bhi ci̍dva̱dhādviva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 4

samu̱ pra ya̍nti dhī̱taya̱: sargā̍so'va̱tām̐ i̍va |

kratu̍ṃ naḥ soma jī̱vase̱ vi vo̱ made̍ dhā̱rayā̍ cama̱sām̐ i̍va̱ viva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 5

tava̱ tye so̍ma̱ śakti̍bhi̱rnikā̍māso̱ vyṛ̍ṇvire |

gṛtsa̍sya̱ dhīrā̍sta̱vaso̱ vi vo̱ made̍ vra̱jaṃ goma̍ntama̱śvina̱ṃ viva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 6

pa̱śuṃ na̍: soma rakṣasi puru̱trā viṣṭhi̍ta̱ṃ jaga̍t |

sa̱mākṛ̍ṇoṣi jī̱vase̱ vi vo̱ made̱ viśvā̍ sa̱mpaśya̱nbhuva̍nā̱ viva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 7

tvaṃ na̍: soma vi̱śvato̍ go̱pā adā̍bhyo bhava | sedha̍ rāja̱nnapa̱ sridho̱ vi vo̱ made̱ mā no̍ du̱:śaṃsa̍ īśatā̱ viva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 8

tvaṃ na̍: soma su̱kratu̍rvayo̱dheyā̍ya jāgṛhi | kṣe̱tra̱vitta̍ro̱ manu̍ṣo̱ vi vo̱ made̍ dru̱ho na̍: pā̱hyaṃha̍so̱ viva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 9

tvaṃ no̍ vṛtrahanta̱mendra̍syendo śi̱vaḥ sakhā̍ | yatsī̱ṃ hava̍nte sami̱the vi vo̱ made̱ yudhya̍mānāsto̱kasā̍tau̱ viva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 10

a̱yaṃ gha̱ sa tu̱ro mada̱ indra̍sya vardhata pri̱yaḥ | a̱yaṃ ka̱kṣīva̍to ma̱ho vi vo̱ made̍ ma̱tiṃ vipra̍sya vardhaya̱dviva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 11

a̱yaṃ viprā̍ya dā̱śuṣe̱ vājā̍m̐ iyarti̱ goma̍taḥ | a̱yaṃ sa̱ptabhya̱ ā vara̱ṃ vi vo̱ made̱ prāndhaṃ śro̱ṇaṃ ca̍ tāriṣa̱dviva̍kṣase
