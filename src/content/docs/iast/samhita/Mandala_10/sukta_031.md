# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 31

**11 kavaṣa ailūṣaḥ|viśve devāḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 1

ā no̍ de̱vānā̱mupa̍ vetu̱ śaṃso̱ viśve̍bhistu̱rairava̍se̱ yaja̍traḥ |
tebhi̍rva̱yaṃ su̍ṣa̱khāyo̍ bhavema̱ tara̍nto̱ viśvā̍ duri̱tā syā̍ma

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 2

pari̍ ci̱nmarto̱ dravi̍ṇaṃ mamanyādṛ̱tasya̍ pa̱thā nama̱sā vi̍vāset |

u̱ta svena̱ kratu̍nā̱ saṃ va̍deta̱ śreyā̍ṃsa̱ṃ dakṣa̱ṃ mana̍sā jagṛbhyāt

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 3

adhā̍yi dhī̱tirasa̍sṛgra̱maṃśā̍stī̱rthe na da̱smamupa̍ ya̱ntyūmā̍: |

a̱bhyā̍naśma suvi̱tasya̍ śū̱ṣaṃ nave̍daso a̱mṛtā̍nāmabhūma

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 4

nitya̍ścākanyā̱tsvapa̍ti̱rdamū̍nā̱ yasmā̍ u de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā ja̱jāna̍ |

bhago̍ vā̱ gobhi̍rarya̱mema̍najyā̱tso a̍smai̱ cāru̍śchadayadu̱ta syā̍t

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 5

i̱yaṃ sā bhū̍yā u̱ṣasā̍miva̱ kṣā yaddha̍ kṣu̱manta̱: śava̍sā sa̱māya̍n |

a̱sya stu̱tiṃ ja̍ri̱turbhikṣa̍māṇā̱ ā na̍: śa̱gmāsa̱ upa̍ yantu̱ vājā̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 6

a̱syede̱ṣā su̍ma̱tiḥ pa̍prathā̱nā'bha̍vatpū̱rvyā bhūma̍nā̱ gauḥ |

a̱sya sanī̍l̤ā̱ asu̍rasya̱ yonau̍ samā̱na ā bhara̍ṇe̱ bibhra̍māṇāḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 7

kiṃ svi̱dvana̱ṃ ka u̱ sa vṛ̱kṣa ā̍sa̱ yato̱ dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī ni̍ṣṭata̱kṣuḥ |

sa̱ṃta̱sthā̱ne a̱jare̍ i̱taū̍tī̱ ahā̍ni pū̱rvīru̱ṣaso̍ jaranta

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 8

naitāva̍de̱nā pa̱ro a̱nyada̍styu̱kṣā sa dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī bi̍bharti |

tvaca̍ṃ pa̱vitra̍ṃ kṛṇuta sva̱dhāvā̱nyadī̱ṃ sūrya̱ṃ na ha̱rito̱ vaha̍nti

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 9

ste̱go na kṣāmatye̍ti pṛ̱thvīṃ miha̱ṃ na vāto̱ vi ha̍ vāti̱ bhūma̍ |

mi̱tro yatra̱ varu̍ṇo a̱jyamā̍no̱'gnirvane̱ na vyasṛ̍ṣṭa̱ śoka̍m

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 10

sta̱rīryatsūta̍ sa̱dyo a̱jyamā̍nā̱ vyathi̍ravya̱thīḥ kṛ̍ṇuta̱ svago̍pā |

pu̱tro yatpūrva̍: pi̱trorjani̍ṣṭa śa̱myāṃ gaurja̍gāra̱ yaddha̍ pṛ̱cchān

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 11

u̱ta kaṇva̍ṃ nṛ̱ṣada̍: pu̱tramā̍huru̱ta śyā̱vo dhana̱māda̍tta vā̱jī |

pra kṛ̱ṣṇāya̱ ruśa̍dapinva̱todha̍ṝ̱tamatra̱ naki̍rasmā apīpet
