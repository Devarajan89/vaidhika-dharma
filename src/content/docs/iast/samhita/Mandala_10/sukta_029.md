# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 29

**8 aindro vasukra:| indraḥ|triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 1

vane̱ na vā̱ yo nya̍dhāyi cā̱kañchuci̍rvā̱ṃ stomo̍ bhuraṇāvajīgaḥ |
yasyedindra̍: puru̱dine̍ṣu̱ hotā̍ nṛ̱ṇāṃ naryo̱ nṛta̍maḥ kṣa̱pāvā̍n

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 2

pra te̍ a̱syā u̱ṣasa̱: prāpa̍rasyā nṛ̱tau syā̍ma̱ nṛta̍masya nṛ̱ṇām |

anu̍ tri̱śoka̍: śa̱tamāva̍ha̱nnṝnkutse̍na̱ ratho̱ yo asa̍tsasa̱vān

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 3

kaste̱ mada̍ indra̱ rantyo̍ bhū̱dduro̱ giro̍ a̱bhyu1gro vi dhā̍va |

kadvāho̍ a̱rvāgupa̍ mā manī̱ṣā ā tvā̍ śakyāmupa̱maṃ rādho̱ annai̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 4

kadu̍ dyu̱mnami̍ndra̱ tvāva̍to̱ nṝnkayā̍ dhi̱yā ka̍rasa̱ya kanna̱ āga̍n |

mi̱tro na sa̱tya u̍rugāya bhṛ̱tyā anne̍ samasya̱ yadasa̍nmanī̱ṣāḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 5

prera̍ya̱ sūro̱ artha̱ṃ na pā̱raṃ ye a̍sya̱ kāma̍ṃ jani̱dhā i̍va̱ gman |

gira̍śca̱ ye te̍ tuvijāta pū̱rvīrnara̍ indra prati̱śikṣa̱ntyannai̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 6

mātre̱ nu te̱ sumi̍te indra pū̱rvī dyaurma̱jmanā̍ pṛthi̱vī kāvye̍na |

varā̍ya te ghṛ̱tava̍ntaḥ su̱tāsa̱: svādma̍nbhavantu pī̱taye̱ madhū̍ni

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 7

ā madhvo̍ asmā asica̱nnama̍tra̱mindrā̍ya pū̱rṇaṃ sa hi sa̱tyarā̍dhāḥ |

sa vā̍vṛdhe̱ vari̍ma̱nnā pṛ̍thi̱vyā a̱bhi kratvā̱ narya̱: pauṃsyai̍śca

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 8

vyā̍na̱l̤indra̱: pṛta̍nā̱: svojā̱ āsmai̍ yatante sa̱khyāya̍ pū̱rvīḥ |

ā smā̱ ratha̱ṃ na pṛta̍nāsu tiṣṭha̱ yaṃ bha̱drayā̍ suma̱tyā co̱dayā̍se
