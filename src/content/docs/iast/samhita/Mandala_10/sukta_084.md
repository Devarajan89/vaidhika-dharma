# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 84

**7  manyustāpasaḥ| manyuḥ| jagatī,1-3 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 1

tvayā̍ manyo sa̱ratha̍māru̱janto̱ harṣa̍māṇāso dhṛṣi̱tā ma̍rutvaḥ |
ti̱gmeṣa̍va̱ āyu̍dhā sa̱ṃśiśā̍nā a̱bhi pra ya̍ntu̱ naro̍ a̱gnirū̍pāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 2

a̱gniri̍va manyo tviṣi̱taḥ sa̍hasva senā̱nīrna̍: sahure hū̱ta e̍dhi |

ha̱tvāya̱ śatrū̱nvi bha̍jasva̱ veda̱ ojo̱ mimā̍no̱ vi mṛdho̍ nudasva

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 3

saha̍sva manyo a̱bhimā̍tima̱sme ru̱janmṛ̱ṇanpra̍mṛ̱ṇanprehi̱ śatrū̍n |

u̱graṃ te̱ pājo̍ na̱nvā ru̍rudhre va̱śī vaśa̍ṃ nayasa ekaja̱ tvam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 4

eko̍ bahū̱nāma̍si manyavīl̤i̱to viśa̍ṃviśaṃ yu̱dhaye̱ saṃ śi̍śādhi |

akṛ̍ttaru̱ktvayā̍ yu̱jā va̱yaṃ dyu̱manta̱ṃ ghoṣa̍ṃ vija̱yāya̍ kṛṇmahe

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 5

vi̱je̱ṣa̱kṛdindra̍ ivānavabra̱vo̱3'smāka̍ṃ manyo adhi̱pā bha̍ve̱ha |

pri̱yaṃ te̱ nāma̍ sahure gṛṇīmasi vi̱dmā tamutsa̱ṃ yata̍ āba̱bhūtha̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 6

ābhū̍tyā saha̱jā va̍jra sāyaka̱ saho̍ bibharṣyabhibhūta̱ utta̍ram |

kratvā̍ no manyo sa̱ha me̱dye̍dhi mahādha̱nasya̍ puruhūta sa̱ṃsṛji̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 7

saṃsṛ̍ṣṭa̱ṃ dhana̍mu̱bhaya̍ṃ sa̱mākṛ̍tama̱smabhya̍ṃ dattā̱ṃ varu̍ṇaśca ma̱nyuḥ |

bhiya̱ṃ dadhā̍nā̱ hṛda̍yeṣu̱ śatra̍va̱: parā̍jitāso̱ apa̱ ni la̍yantām
