# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 114

**10 vairūpaḥ sadhriḥ, tāpaso dharmo vā| viśve devāḥ|triṣṭup, 4 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 114 | **Verse:** 1

gha̱rmā sama̍ntā tri̱vṛta̱ṃ vyā̍patu̱stayo̱rjuṣṭi̍ṃ māta̱riśvā̍ jagāma |
di̱vaspayo̱ didhi̍ṣāṇā aveṣanvi̱durde̱vāḥ sa̱hasā̍mānama̱rkam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 114 | **Verse:** 2

ti̱sro de̱ṣṭrāya̱ niṝ̍tī̱rupā̍sate dīrgha̱śruto̱ vi hi jā̱nanti̱ vahna̍yaḥ |

tāsā̱ṃ ni ci̍kyuḥ ka̱vayo̍ ni̱dāna̱ṃ pare̍ṣu̱ yā guhye̍ṣu vra̱teṣu̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 114 | **Verse:** 3

catu̍ṣkapardā yuva̱tiḥ su̱peśā̍ ghṛ̱tapra̍tīkā va̱yunā̍ni vaste |

tasyā̍ṃ supa̱rṇā vṛṣa̍ṇā̱ ni ṣe̍datu̱ryatra̍ de̱vā da̍dhi̱re bhā̍ga̱dheya̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 114 | **Verse:** 4

eka̍: supa̱rṇaḥ sa sa̍mu̱dramā vi̍veśa̱ sa i̱daṃ viśva̱ṃ bhuva̍na̱ṃ vi ca̍ṣṭe |

taṃ pāke̍na̱ mana̍sāpaśya̱manti̍ta̱staṃ mā̱tā re̍l̤hi̱ sa u̍ rel̤hi mā̱tara̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 114 | **Verse:** 5

su̱pa̱rṇaṃ viprā̍: ka̱vayo̱ vaco̍bhi̱reka̱ṃ santa̍ṃ bahu̱dhā ka̍lpayanti |

chandā̍ṃsi ca̱ dadha̍to adhva̱reṣu̱ grahā̱ntsoma̍sya mimate̱ dvāda̍śa

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 114 | **Verse:** 6

ṣa̱ṭtri̱ṃśām̐śca̍ ca̱tura̍: ka̱lpaya̍nta̱śchandā̍ṃsi ca̱ dadha̍ta ādvāda̱śam |

ya̱jñaṃ vi̱māya̍ ka̱vayo̍ manī̱ṣa ṛ̍ksā̱mābhyā̱ṃ pra ratha̍ṃ vartayanti

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 114 | **Verse:** 7

catu̍rdaśā̱nye ma̍hi̱māno̍ asya̱ taṃ dhīrā̍ vā̱cā pra ṇa̍yanti sa̱pta |

āpnā̍naṃ tī̱rthaṃ ka i̱ha pra vo̍ca̱dyena̍ pa̱thā pra̱piba̍nte su̱tasya̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 114 | **Verse:** 8

sa̱ha̱sra̱dhā pa̍ñcada̱śānyu̱kthā yāva̱ddyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī tāva̱dittat |

sa̱ha̱sra̱dhā ma̍hi̱māna̍: sa̱hasra̱ṃ yāva̱dbrahma̱ viṣṭhi̍ta̱ṃ tāva̍tī̱ vāk

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 114 | **Verse:** 9

kaśchanda̍sā̱ṃ yoga̱mā ve̍da̱ dhīra̱: ko dhiṣṇyā̱ṃ prati̱ vāca̍ṃ papāda |

kamṛ̱tvijā̍maṣṭa̱maṃ śūra̍māhu̱rharī̱ indra̍sya̱ ni ci̍kāya̱ kaḥ svi̍t

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 114 | **Verse:** 10

bhūmyā̱ anta̱ṃ paryeke̍ caranti̱ ratha̍sya dhū̱rṣu yu̱ktāso̍ asthuḥ |

śrama̍sya dā̱yaṃ vi bha̍jantyebhyo ya̱dā ya̱mo bhava̍ti ha̱rmye hi̱taḥ
