# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 159

**6 paulomī śacī| śacī (ātmānaṃ tuṣṭāva)| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 159 | **Verse:** 1

uda̱sau sūryo̍ agā̱duda̱yaṃ mā̍ma̱ko bhaga̍: | a̱haṃ tadvi̍dva̱lā pati̍ma̱bhya̍sākṣi viṣāsa̱hiḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 159 | **Verse:** 2

a̱haṃ ke̱tura̱haṃ mū̱rdhā'hamu̱grā vi̱vāca̍nī | mamedanu̱ kratu̱ṃ pati̍: sehā̱nāyā̍ u̱pāca̍ret

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 159 | **Verse:** 3

mama̍ pu̱trāḥ śa̍tru̱haṇo'tho̍ me duhi̱tā vi̱rāṭ | u̱tāhama̍smi saṃja̱yā patyau̍ me̱ śloka̍ utta̱maḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 159 | **Verse:** 4

yenendro̍ ha̱viṣā̍ kṛ̱tvyabha̍vaddyu̱mnyu̍tta̱maḥ | i̱daṃ tada̍kri devā asapa̱tnā kilā̍bhuvam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 159 | **Verse:** 5

a̱sa̱pa̱tnā sa̍patna̱ghnī jaya̍ntyabhi̱bhūva̍rī | āvṛ̍kṣama̱nyāsā̱ṃ varco̱ rādho̱ asthe̍yasāmiva

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 159 | **Verse:** 6

sama̍jaiṣami̱mā a̱haṃ sa̱patnī̍rabhi̱bhūva̍rī | yathā̱hama̱sya vī̱rasya̍ vi̱rājā̍ni̱ jana̍sya ca
