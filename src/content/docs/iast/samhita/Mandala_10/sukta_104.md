# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 104

**11 aṣṭako vaiśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 1

asā̍vi̱ soma̍: puruhūta̱ tubhya̱ṃ hari̍bhyāṃ ya̱jñamupa̍ yāhi̱ tūya̍m |
tubhya̱ṃ giro̱ vipra̍vīrā iyā̱nā da̍dhanvi̱ra i̍ndra̱ pibā̍ su̱tasya̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 2

a̱psu dhū̱tasya̍ hariva̱: pibe̱ha nṛbhi̍: su̱tasya̍ ja̱ṭhara̍ṃ pṛṇasva |

mi̱mi̱kṣuryamadra̍ya indra̱ tubhya̱ṃ tebhi̍rvardhasva̱ mada̍mukthavāhaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 3

progrāṃ pī̱tiṃ vṛṣṇa̍ iyarmi sa̱tyāṃ pra̱yai su̱tasya̍ haryaśva̱ tubhya̍m |

indra̱ dhenā̍bhiri̱ha mā̍dayasva dhī̱bhirviśvā̍bhi̱: śacyā̍ gṛṇā̱naḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 4

ū̱tī śa̍cīva̱stava̍ vī̱rye̍ṇa̱ vayo̱ dadhā̍nā u̱śija̍ ṛta̱jñāḥ |

pra̱jāva̍dindra̱ manu̍ṣo duro̱ṇe ta̱sthurgṛ̱ṇanta̍: sadha̱mādyā̍saḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 5

praṇī̍tibhiṣṭe haryaśva su̱ṣṭoḥ su̍ṣu̱mnasya̍ puru̱ruco̱ janā̍saḥ |

maṃhi̍ṣṭhāmū̱tiṃ vi̱tire̱ dadhā̍nā sto̱tāra̍ indra̱ tava̍ sū̱nṛtā̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 6

upa̱ brahmā̍ṇi harivo̱ hari̍bhyā̱ṃ soma̍sya yāhi pī̱taye̍ su̱tasya̍ |

indra̍ tvā ya̱jñaḥ kṣama̍māṇamānaḍdā̱śvām̐ a̍syadhva̱rasya̍ prake̱taḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 7

sa̱hasra̍vājamabhimāti̱ṣāha̍ṃ su̱tera̍ṇaṃ ma̱ghavā̍naṃ suvṛ̱ktim |

upa̍ bhūṣanti̱ giro̱ apra̍tīta̱mindra̍ṃ nama̱syā ja̍ri̱tuḥ pa̍nanta

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 8

sa̱ptāpo̍ de̱vīḥ su̱raṇā̱ amṛ̍ktā̱ yābhi̱: sindhu̱mata̍ra indra pū̱rbhit |

na̱va̱tiṃ sro̱tyā nava̍ ca̱ srava̍ntīrde̱vebhyo̍ gā̱tuṃ manu̍ṣe ca vindaḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 9

a̱po ma̱hīra̱bhiśa̍steramu̱ñco'jā̍garā̱svadhi̍ de̱va eka̍: |

indra̱ yāstvaṃ vṛ̍tra̱tūrye̍ ca̱kartha̱ tābhi̍rvi̱śvāyu̍sta̱nva̍ṃ pupuṣyāḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 10

vī̱reṇya̱: kratu̱rindra̍: suśa̱stiru̱tāpi̱ dhenā̍ puruhū̱tamī̍ṭṭe |

ārda̍yadvṛ̱tramakṛ̍ṇodu lo̱kaṃ sa̍sā̱he śa̱kraḥ pṛta̍nā abhi̱ṣṭiḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 11

śu̱naṃ hu̍vema ma̱ghavā̍na̱mindra̍ma̱sminbhare̱ nṛta̍ma̱ṃ vāja̍sātau |

śṛ̱ṇvanta̍mu̱gramū̱taye̍ sa̱matsu̱ ghnanta̍ṃ vṛ̱trāṇi̍ sa̱ṃjita̱ṃ dhanā̍nām
