# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 180

**3 jaya aindraḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 180 | **Verse:** 1

pra sa̍sāhiṣe puruhūta̱ śatrū̱ñjyeṣṭha̍ste̱ śuṣma̍ i̱ha rā̱tira̍stu |
indrā bha̍ra̱ dakṣi̍ṇenā̱ vasū̍ni̱ pati̱: sindhū̍nāmasi re̱vatī̍nām

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 180 | **Verse:** 2

mṛ̱go na bhī̱maḥ ku̍ca̱ro gi̍ri̱ṣṭhāḥ pa̍rā̱vata̱ ā ja̍ganthā̱ para̍syāḥ |

sṛ̱kaṃ sa̱ṃśāya̍ pa̱vimi̍ndra ti̱gmaṃ vi śatrū̍ntāl̤hi̱ vi mṛdho̍ nudasva

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 180 | **Verse:** 3

indra̍ kṣa̱trama̱bhi vā̱mamojo'jā̍yathā vṛṣabha carṣaṇī̱nām |

apā̍nudo̱ jana̍mamitra̱yanta̍mu̱ruṃ de̱vebhyo̍ akṛṇoru lo̱kam
