# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 44

**11 kṛṣṇa āṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ| jagatī,1-3,10-11 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 1

ā yā̱tvindra̱: svapa̍ti̱rmadā̍ya̱ yo dharma̍ṇā tūtujā̱nastuvi̍ṣmān |
pra̱tva̱kṣā̱ṇo ati̱ viśvā̱ sahā̍ṃsyapā̱reṇa̍ maha̱tā vṛṣṇye̍na

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 2

śībha̍ṃ rājantsu̱pathā yā̍hya̱rvāṅvardhā̍ma te pa̱puṣo̱ vṛṣṇyā̍ni

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 3

pratva̍kṣasaṃ vṛṣa̱bhaṃ sa̱tyaśu̍ṣma̱mema̍sma̱trā sa̍dha̱mādo̍ vahantu

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 4

oja̍: kṛṣva̱ saṃ gṛ̍bhāya̱ tve apyaso̱ yathā̍ keni̱pānā̍mi̱no vṛ̱dhe

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 5

tvamī̍śiṣe̱ sāsminnā sa̍tsi ba̱rhiṣya̍nādhṛ̱ṣyā tava̱ pātrā̍ṇi̱ dharma̍ṇā

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 6

na ye śe̱kurya̱jñiyā̱ṃ nāva̍mā̱ruha̍mī̱rmaiva te nya̍viśanta̱ kepa̍yaḥ

## Verse 7
