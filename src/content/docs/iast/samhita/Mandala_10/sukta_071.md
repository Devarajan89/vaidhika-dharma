# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 71

**11 bṛhaspatirāṃgirasaḥ| jñānam| triṣṭup ,9 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 1

bṛha̍spate pratha̱maṃ vā̱co agra̱ṃ yatpraira̍ta nāma̱dheya̱ṃ dadhā̍nāḥ |
yade̍ṣā̱ṃ śreṣṭha̱ṃ yada̍ri̱pramāsī̍tpre̱ṇā tade̍ṣā̱ṃ nihi̍ta̱ṃ guhā̱viḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 2

saktu̍miva̱ tita̍unā pu̱nanto̱ yatra̱ dhīrā̱ mana̍sā̱ vāca̱makra̍ta |

atrā̱ sakhā̍yaḥ sa̱khyāni̍ jānate bha̱draiṣā̍ṃ la̱kṣmīrnihi̱tādhi̍ vā̱ci

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 3

ya̱jñena̍ vā̱caḥ pa̍da̱vīya̍māya̱ntāmanva̍vinda̱nnṛṣi̍ṣu̱ pravi̍ṣṭām |

tāmā̱bhṛtyā̱ vya̍dadhuḥ puru̱trā tāṃ sa̱pta re̱bhā a̱bhi saṃ na̍vante

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 4

u̱ta tva̱: paśya̱nna da̍darśa̱ vāca̍mu̱ta tva̍: śṛ̱ṇvanna śṛ̍ṇotyenām |

u̱to tva̍smai ta̱nvaṃ1 vi sa̍sre jā̱yeva̱ patya̍ uśa̱tī su̱vāsā̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 5

u̱ta tva̍ṃ sa̱khye sthi̱rapī̍tamāhu̱rnaina̍ṃ hinva̱ntyapi̱ vāji̍neṣu |

adhe̍nvā carati mā̱yayai̱ṣa vāca̍ṃ śuśru̱vām̐ a̍pha̱lāma̍pu̱ṣpām

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 6

yasti̱tyāja̍ saci̱vida̱ṃ sakhā̍ya̱ṃ na tasya̍ vā̱cyapi̍ bhā̱go a̍sti |

yadī̍ṃ śṛ̱ṇotyala̍kaṃ śṛṇoti na̱hi pra̱veda̍ sukṛ̱tasya̱ panthā̍m

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 7

a̱kṣa̱ṇvanta̱: karṇa̍vanta̱: sakhā̍yo manoja̱veṣvasa̍mā babhūvuḥ |

ā̱da̱ghnāsa̍ upaka̱kṣāsa̍ u tve hra̱dā i̍va̱ snātvā̍ u tve dadṛśre

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 8

hṛ̱dā ta̱ṣṭeṣu̱ mana̍so ja̱veṣu̱ yadbrā̍hma̱ṇāḥ sa̱ṃyaja̍nte̱ sakhā̍yaḥ |

atrāha̍ tva̱ṃ vi ja̍hurve̱dyābhi̱roha̍brahmāṇo̱ vi ca̍rantyu tve

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 9

i̱me ye nārvāṅna pa̱raścara̍nti̱ na brā̍hma̱ṇāso̱ na su̱teka̍rāsaḥ |

ta e̱te vāca̍mabhi̱padya̍ pā̱payā̍ si̱rīstantra̍ṃ tanvate̱ apra̍jajñayaḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 10

sarve̍ nandanti ya̱śasāga̍tena sabhāsā̱hena̱ sakhyā̱ sakhā̍yaḥ |

ki̱lbi̱ṣa̱spṛtpi̍tu̱ṣaṇi̱rhye̍ṣā̱mara̍ṃ hi̱to bhava̍ti̱ vāji̍nāya

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 11

ṛ̱cāṃ tva̱: poṣa̍māste pupu̱ṣvāngā̍ya̱traṃ tvo̍ gāyati̱ śakva̍rīṣu |

bra̱hmā tvo̱ vada̍ti jātavi̱dyāṃ ya̱jñasya̱ mātrā̱ṃ vi mi̍mīta u tvaḥ
