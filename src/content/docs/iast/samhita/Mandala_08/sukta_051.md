# Maṇḍala 8 — Sūktam 51

**10 śruṣṭigu: kāṇva:| indra: | pragātha:= ( viṣamā bṛhatī, samā satobṛhatī ) |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 8 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 1

yathā̱ manau̱ sāṃva̍raṇau̱ soma̍mi̱ndrāpi̍baḥ su̱tam | nīpā̍tithau maghava̱nmedhyā̍tithau̱ puṣṭi̍gau̱ śruṣṭi̍gau̱ sacā̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 8 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 2

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 8 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 3

## Verse 4

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 8 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 5

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 8 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 6

## Verse 7

## Verse 8
