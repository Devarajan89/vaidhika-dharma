# Maṇḍala 8 — Sūktam 55

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 8 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 1

bhūrīdindra̍sya vī̱ryaṃ1 vyakhya̍ma̱bhyāya̍ti | rādha̍ste dasyave vṛka

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 8 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 2
