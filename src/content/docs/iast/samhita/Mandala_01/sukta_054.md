# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 54

**11 savya āṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ| jagatī, 6, 8-9, 11 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 1

mā no̍ a̱smin ma̍ghavan pṛ̱tsvaṃha̍si na̱hi te̱ anta̱: śava̍saḥ parī̱ṇaśe̍ |
akra̍ndayo na̱dyo̱ 3 roru̍va̱d vanā̍ ka̱thā na kṣo̱ṇīrbhi̱yasā̱ samā̍rata

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 2

arcā̍ śa̱krāya̍ śā̱kine̱ śacī̍vate śṛ̱ṇvanta̱mindra̍ṃ ma̱haya̍nna̱bhi ṣṭu̍hi |

yo dhṛ̱ṣṇunā̱ śava̍sā̱ roda̍sī u̱bhe vṛṣā̍ vṛṣa̱tvā vṛ̍ṣa̱bho nyṛ̱ñjate̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 3

arcā̍ di̱ve bṛ̍ha̱te śū̱ṣyaṃ 1 vaca̱: svakṣa̍tra̱ṃ yasya̍ dhṛṣa̱to dhṛ̱ṣanmana̍: |

bṛ̱hacchra̍vā̱ asu̍ro ba̱rhaṇā̍ kṛ̱taḥ pu̱ro hari̍bhyāṃ vṛṣa̱bho ratho̱ hi ṣaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 4

tvaṃ di̱vo bṛ̍ha̱taḥ sānu̍ kopa̱yo 'va̱ tmanā̍ dhṛṣa̱tā śamba̍raṃ bhinat |

yanmā̱yino̍ vra̱ndino̍ ma̱ndinā̍ dhṛ̱ṣacchi̱tāṃ gabha̍stima̱śani̍ṃ pṛta̱nyasi̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 5

ni yad vṛ̱ṇakṣi̍ śvasa̱nasya̍ mū̱rdhani̱ śuṣṇa̍sya cid vra̱ndino̱ roru̍va̱d vanā̍ |

prā̱cīne̍na̱ mana̍sā ba̱rhaṇā̍vatā̱ yada̱dyā ci̍tkṛ̱ṇava̱: kastvā̱ pari̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 6

tvamā̍vitha̱ narya̍ṃ tu̱rvaśa̱ṃ yadu̱ṃ tvaṃ tu̱rvīti̍ṃ va̱yya̍ṃ śatakrato |

tvaṃ ratha̱meta̍śa̱ṃ kṛtvye̱ dhane̱ tvaṃ puro̍ nava̱tiṃ da̍mbhayo̱ nava̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 7

sa ghā̱ rājā̱ satpa̍tiḥ śūśuva̱jjano̍ rā̱taha̍vya̱: prati̱ yaḥ śāsa̱minva̍ti |

u̱kthā vā̱ yo a̍bhigṛ̱ṇāti̱ rādha̍sā̱ dānu̍rasmā̱ upa̍rā pinvate di̱vaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 8

asa̍maṃ kṣa̱tramasa̍mā manī̱ṣā pra so̍ma̱pā apa̍sā santu̱ neme̍ |

ye ta̍ indra da̱duṣo̍ va̱rdhaya̍nti̱ mahi̍ kṣa̱traṃ sthavi̍ra̱ṃ vṛṣṇya̍ṃ ca

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 9

tubhyede̱te ba̍hu̱lā adri̍dugdhāścamū̱ṣada̍ścama̱sā i̍ndra̱pānā̍: |

vya̍śnuhi ta̱rpayā̱ kāma̍meṣā̱mathā̱ mano̍ vasu̱deyā̍ya kṛṣva

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 10

a̱pāma̍tiṣṭhaddha̱ruṇa̍hvara̱ṃ tamo̱ 'ntarvṛ̱trasya̍ ja̱ṭhare̍ṣu̱ parva̍taḥ |

a̱bhīmindro̍ na̱dyo̍ va̱vriṇā̍ hi̱tā viśvā̍ anu̱ṣṭhāḥ pra̍va̱ṇeṣu̍ jighnate

## Verse 11
