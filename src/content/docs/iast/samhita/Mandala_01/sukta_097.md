# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 97

**8 kutsa āṅgirasaḥ| agniḥ, śuciragnirvā| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 97 | **Verse:** 1

apa̍ na̱: śośu̍cada̱ghamagne̍ śuśu̱gdhyā ra̱yim | apa̍ na̱: śośu̍cada̱gham

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 97 | **Verse:** 2

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 97 | **Verse:** 3
