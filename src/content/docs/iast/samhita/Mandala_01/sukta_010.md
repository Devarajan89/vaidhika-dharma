# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 10

**12 madhucchandā vaiśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 1

gāya̍nti tvā gāya̱triṇo'rca̍ntya̱rkama̱rkiṇa̍:|
bra̱hmāṇa̍stvā śatakrata̱ ud va̱ṃśami̍va yemire

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 2

yat sāno̱: sānu̱māru̍ha̱d bhūryaspa̍ṣṭa̱ kartva̍m|

tadindro̱ artha̍ṃ cetati yū̱thena̍ vṛ̱ṣṇire̍jati

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 3

yu̱kṣvā hi ke̱śinā̱ harī̱ vṛṣa̍ṇā kakṣya̱prā|

athā̍ na indra somapā gi̱rāmupa̍śrutiṃ cara

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 4

ehi̱ stomā̍m̐ a̱bhi sva̍rā̱ 'bhi gṛ̍ṇī̱hyā ru̍va|

brahma̍ ca no vaso̱ sacendra̍ ya̱jñaṃ ca̍ vardhaya

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 5

u̱kthamindrā̍ya̱ śaṃsya̱ṃ vardha̍naṃ puruni̱ṣṣidhe̍|

śa̱kro yathā̍ su̱teṣu̍ ṇo rā̱raṇa̍t sa̱khyeṣu̍ ca

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 6

tamit sa̍khi̱tva ī̍mahe̱ taṃ rā̱ye taṃ su̱vīrye̍|

sa śa̱kra u̱ta na̍: śaka̱dindro̱ vasu̱ daya̍mānaḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 7

su̱vi̱vṛta̍ṃ suni̱raja̱mindra̱ tvādā̍ta̱midyaśa̍:|

gavā̱mapa̍ vra̱jaṃ vṛ̍dhi kṛṇu̱ṣva rādho̍ adrivaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 8

na̱hi tvā̱ roda̍sī u̱bhe ṛ̍ghā̱yamā̍ṇa̱minva̍taḥ|

jeṣa̱: sva̍rvatīra̱paḥ saṃ gā a̱smabhya̍ṃ dhūnuhi

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 9

āśru̍tkarṇa śru̱dhī hava̱ṃ nū ci̍ddadhiṣva me̱ gira̍:|

indra̱ stoma̍mi̱maṃ mama̍ kṛ̱ṣvā yu̱jaści̱danta̍ram

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 10

vi̱dmā hi tvā̱ vṛṣa̍ntama̱ṃ vāje̍ṣu havana̱śruta̍m|

vṛṣa̍ntamasya hūmaha ū̱tiṃ sa̍hasra̱sāta̍mām

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 11

ā tū na̍ indra kauśika mandasā̱naḥ su̱taṃ pi̍ba|

navya̱māyu̱: pra sū ti̍ra kṛ̱dhī sa̍hasra̱sāmṛṣi̍m

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 12

pari̍ tvā girvaṇo̱ gira̍ i̱mā bha̍vantu vi̱śvata̍: |

vṛ̱ddhāyu̱manu̱ vṛddha̍yo̱ juṣṭā̍ bhavantu̱ juṣṭa̍yaḥ
