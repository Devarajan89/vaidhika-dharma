# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 151

**9 dīrghatamā aucathyaḥ| 1 mitraḥ, 2-9 mitrāvaruṇau| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 151 | **Verse:** 1

mi̱traṃ na yaṃ śimyā̱ goṣu̍ ga̱vyava̍: svā̱dhyo̍ vi̱dathe̍ a̱psu jīja̍nan |
are̍jetā̱ṃ roda̍sī̱ pāja̍sā gi̱rā prati̍ pri̱yaṃ ya̍ja̱taṃ ja̱nuṣā̱mava̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 151 | **Verse:** 2

yaddha̱ tyad vā̍ṃ purumī̱l̤ahasya̍ so̱mina̱: pra mi̱trāso̱ na da̍dhi̱re svā̱bhuva̍: |

adha̱ kratu̍ṃ vidataṃ gā̱tumarca̍ta u̱ta śru̍taṃ vṛṣaṇā pa̱styā̍vataḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 151 | **Verse:** 3

ā vā̍ṃ bhūṣan kṣi̱tayo̱ janma̱ roda̍syoḥ pra̱vācya̍ṃ vṛṣaṇā̱ dakṣa̍se ma̱he |

yadī̍mṛ̱tāya̱ bhara̍tho̱ yadarva̍te̱ pra hotra̍yā̱ śimyā̍ vītho adhva̱ram

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 151 | **Verse:** 4

pra sā kṣi̱tira̍sura̱ yā mahi̍ pri̱ya ṛtā̍vānāvṛ̱tamā gho̍ṣatho bṛ̱hat |

yu̱vaṃ di̱vo bṛ̍ha̱to dakṣa̍mā̱bhuva̱ṃ gāṃ na dhu̱ryupa̍ yuñjāthe a̱paḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 151 | **Verse:** 5

ma̱hī atra̍ mahi̱nā vāra̍mṛṇvatho 're̱ṇava̱stuja̱ ā sadma̍n dhe̱nava̍: |

svara̍nti̱ tā u̍pa̱ratā̍ti̱ sūrya̱mā ni̱mruca̍ u̱ṣasa̍stakva̱vīri̍va

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 151 | **Verse:** 6

ā vā̍mṛ̱tāya̍ ke̱śinī̍ranūṣata̱ mitra̱ yatra̱ varu̍ṇa gā̱tumarca̍thaḥ |

ava̱ tmanā̍ sṛ̱jata̱ṃ pinva̍ta̱ṃ dhiyo̍ yu̱vaṃ vipra̍sya̱ manma̍nāmirajyathaḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 151 | **Verse:** 7

yo vā̍ṃ ya̱jñaiḥ śa̍śamā̱no ha̱ dāśa̍ti ka̱virhotā̱ yaja̍ti manma̱sādha̍naḥ |

upāha̱ taṃ gaccha̍tho vī̱tho a̍dhva̱ramacchā̱ gira̍: suma̱tiṃ ga̍ntamasma̱yū

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 151 | **Verse:** 8

yu̱vāṃ ya̱jñaiḥ pra̍tha̱mā gobhi̍rañjata̱ ṛtā̍vānā̱ mana̍so̱ na prayu̍ktiṣu |

bhara̍nti vā̱ṃ manma̍nā sa̱ṃyatā̱ giro 'dṛ̍pyatā̱ mana̍sā re̱vadā̍śāthe

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 151 | **Verse:** 9

re̱vad vayo̍ dadhāthe re̱vadā̍śāthe̱ narā̍ mā̱yābhi̍ri̱taū̍ti̱ māhi̍nam |

na vā̱ṃ dyāvo'ha̍bhi̱rnota sindha̍vo̱ na de̍va̱tvaṃ pa̱ṇayo̱ nāna̍śurma̱gham
