# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 138

**4 parucchepo daivodāsiḥ| pūṣā| atyaṣṭiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 138 | **Verse:** 1

prapra̍ pū̱ṣṇastu̍vijā̱tasya̍ śasyate mahi̱tvama̍sya ta̱vaso̱ na ta̍ndate sto̱trama̍sya̱ na ta̍ndate |
arcā̍mi sumna̱yanna̱hamantyū̍tiṃ mayo̱bhuva̍m |
viśva̍sya̱ yo mana̍ āyuyu̱ve ma̱kho de̱va ā̍yuyu̱ve ma̱khaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 138 | **Verse:** 2

pra hi tvā̍ pūṣannaji̱raṃ na yāma̍ni̱ stome̍bhiḥ kṛ̱ṇva ṛ̱ṇavo̱ yathā̱ mṛdha̱ uṣṭro̱ na pī̍paro̱ mṛdha̍: |

hu̱ve yat tvā̍ mayo̱bhuva̍ṃ de̱vaṃ sa̱khyāya̱ martya̍: |

a̱smāka̍māṅgū̱ṣān dyu̱mnina̍skṛdhi̱ vāje̍ṣu dyu̱mnina̍skṛdhi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 138 | **Verse:** 3

yasya̍ te pūṣan tsa̱khye vi̍pa̱nyava̱: kratvā̍ ci̱t santo'va̍sā bubhujri̱ra iti̱ kratvā̍ bubhujri̱re |

tāmanu̍ tvā̱ navī̍yasīṃ ni̱yuta̍ṃ rā̱ya ī̍mahe |

ahe̍l̤amāna uruśaṃsa̱ sarī̍ bhava̱ vāje̍vāje̱ sarī̍ bhava

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 138 | **Verse:** 4

a̱syā ū̱ ṣu ṇa̱ upa̍ sā̱taye̍ bhu̱vo 'he̍l̤amāno rari̱vām̐ a̍jāśva śravasya̱tāma̍jāśva |

o ṣu tvā̍ vavṛtīmahi̱ stome̍bhirdasma sā̱dhubhi̍: |

na̱hi tvā̍ pūṣannati̱manya̍ āghṛṇe̱ na te̍ sa̱khyama̍pahnu̱ve
