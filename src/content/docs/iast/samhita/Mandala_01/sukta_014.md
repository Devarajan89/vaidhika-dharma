# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 14

**12 medhātithiḥ kāṇvaḥ| viśve devāḥ (viśvairdevaiḥ sahito'gniḥ),  3 indravāyubṛhaspatimitrāgnipūṣabhagādityamarudgaṇāḥ, 10 viśvadevāgnīndravāyumitradhāmāni, 11 agniḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 1

aibhi̍ragne̱ duvo̱ giro̱ viśve̍bhi̱: soma̍pītaye | de̱vebhi̍ryāhi̱ yakṣi̍ ca

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 2

ā tvā̱ kaṇvā̍ ahūṣata gṛ̱ṇanti̍ vipra te̱ dhiya̍: | de̱vebhi̍ragna̱ ā ga̍hi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 3

i̱ndra̱vā̱yū bṛha̱spati̍ṃ mi̱trāgniṃ pū̱ṣaṇa̱ṃ bhaga̍m | ā̱di̱tyān māru̍taṃ ga̱ṇam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 4

pra vo̍ bhriyanta̱ inda̍vo matsa̱rā mā̍dayi̱ṣṇava̍: | dra̱psā madhva̍ścamū̱ṣada̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 5

īl̤a̍te̱ tvāma̍va̱syava̱: kaṇvā̍so vṛ̱ktaba̍rhiṣaḥ | ha̱viṣma̍nto ara̱ṃkṛta̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 6

ghṛ̱tapṛ̍ṣṭhā mano̱yujo̱ ye tvā̱ vaha̍nti̱ vahna̍yaḥ | ā de̱vāntsoma̍pītaye

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 7

tān yaja̍trām̐ ṛtā̱vṛdho 'gne̱ patnī̍vataskṛdhi | madhva̍: sujihva pāyaya

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 8

ye yaja̍trā̱ ya īḍyā̱s te te̍ pibantu ji̱hvayā̍ | madho̍ragne̱ vaṣa̍ṭkṛti

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 9

ākī̱ṃ sūrya̍sya roca̱nād viśvā̍nde̱vām̐ u̍ṣa̱rbudha̍: | vipro̱ hote̱ha va̍kṣati

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 10

viśve̍bhiḥ so̱myaṃ madhvagna̱ indre̍ṇa vā̱yunā̍ | pibā̍ mi̱trasya̱ dhāma̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 11

tvaṃ hotā̱ manu̍rhi̱to 'gne̍ ya̱jñeṣu̍ sīdasi | semaṃ no̍ adhva̱raṃ ya̍ja

## Verse 12
