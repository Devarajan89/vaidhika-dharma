# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 2

**9 madhucchandā vaiśvāmitraḥ| 1-3 vāyuḥ, 4-6 indra-vāyu, 7-9 mitrā-varuṇau| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 1
