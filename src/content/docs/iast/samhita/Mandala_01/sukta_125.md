# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 125

**7 kakṣīvān dairghatamasa auśijaḥ| svanayasya dānastutiḥ| triṣṭup, 4-5 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 125 | **Verse:** 1

prā̱tā ratna̍ṃ prāta̱ritvā̍ dadhāti̱ taṃ ci̍ki̱tvān pra̍ti̱gṛhyā̱ ni dha̍tte |
tena̍ pra̱jāṃ va̱rdhaya̍māna̱ āyū̍ rā̱yaspoṣe̍ṇa sacate su̱vīra̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 125 | **Verse:** 2

su̱gura̍sat suhira̱ṇyaḥ svaśvo̍ bṛ̱hada̍smai̱ vaya̱ indro̍ dadhāti |

yastvā̱yanta̱ṃ vasu̍nā prātaritvo mu̱kṣīja̍yeva̱ padi̍mutsi̱nāti̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 125 | **Verse:** 3

āya̍ma̱dya su̱kṛta̍ṃ prā̱tari̱cchanni̱ṣṭeḥ pu̱traṃ vasu̍matā̱ rathe̍na |

a̱ṃśoḥ su̱taṃ pā̍yaya matsa̱rasya̍ kṣa̱yadvī̍raṃ vardhaya sū̱nṛtā̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 125 | **Verse:** 4

upa̍ kṣaranti̱ sindha̍vo mayo̱bhuva̍ ījā̱naṃ ca̍ ya̱kṣyamā̍ṇaṃ ca dhe̱nava̍: |

pṛ̱ṇanta̍ṃ ca̱ papu̍riṃ ca śrava̱syavo̍ ghṛ̱tasya̱ dhārā̱ upa̍ yanti vi̱śvata̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 125 | **Verse:** 5

nāka̍sya pṛ̱ṣṭhe adhi̍ tiṣṭhati śri̱to yaḥ pṛ̱ṇāti̱ sa ha̍ de̱veṣu̍ gacchati |

tasmā̱ āpo̍ ghṛ̱tama̍rṣanti̱ sindha̍va̱stasmā̍ i̱yaṃ dakṣi̍ṇā pinvate̱ sadā̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 125 | **Verse:** 6

dakṣi̍ṇāvatā̱midi̱māni̍ ci̱trā dakṣi̍ṇāvatāṃ di̱vi sūryā̍saḥ |

dakṣi̍ṇāvanto a̱mṛta̍ṃ bhajante̱ dakṣi̍ṇāvanta̱: pra ti̍ranta̱ āyu̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 125 | **Verse:** 7

mā pṛ̱ṇanto̱ duri̍ta̱mena̱ āra̱n mā jā̍riṣuḥ sū̱raya̍: suvra̱tāsa̍: |

a̱nyasteṣā̍ṃ pari̱dhira̍stu̱ kaści̱dapṛ̍ṇantama̱bhi saṃ ya̍ntu̱ śokā̍:
