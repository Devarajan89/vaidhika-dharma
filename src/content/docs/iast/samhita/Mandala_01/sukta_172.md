# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 172

**3 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ | marutaḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 172 | **Verse:** 1

ci̱tro vo̍'stu̱ yāma̍ści̱tra ū̱tī su̍dānavaḥ | maru̍to̱ ahi̍bhānavaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 172 | **Verse:** 2

ā̱re sā va̍: sudānavo̱ maru̍ta ṛñja̱tī śaru̍: | ā̱re aśmā̱ yamasya̍tha

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 172 | **Verse:** 3

tṛ̱ṇa̱ska̱ndasya̱ nu viśa̱: pari̍ vṛṅkta sudānavaḥ | ū̱rdhvān na̍ḥ karta jī̱vase̍
