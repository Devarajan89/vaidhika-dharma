# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 108

**13 kutsa āṅgirasaḥ| indrāgnī| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 1

ya i̍ndrāgnī ci̱trata̍mo̱ ratho̍ vāma̱bhi viśvā̍ni̱ bhuva̍nāni̱ caṣṭe̍ |
tenā yā̍taṃ sa̱ratha̍ṃ tasthi̱vāṃsāthā̱ soma̍sya pibataṃ su̱tasya̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 2

yāva̍di̱daṃ bhuva̍na̱ṃ viśva̱mastyu̍ru̱vyacā̍ vari̱matā̍ gabhī̱ram |

tāvā̍m̐ a̱yaṃ pāta̍ve̱ somo̍ a̱stvara̍mindrāgnī̱ mana̍se yu̱vabhyā̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 3

ca̱krāthe̱ hi sa̱dhrya 1 ṅnāma̍ bha̱draṃ sa̍dhrīcī̱nā vṛ̍trahaṇā u̱ta stha̍: |

tāvi̍ndrāgnī sa̱dhrya̍ñcā ni̱ṣadyā̱ vṛṣṇa̱: soma̍sya vṛṣa̱ṇā vṛ̍ṣethām

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 4

sami̍ddheṣva̱gniṣvā̍najā̱nā ya̱tasru̍cā ba̱rhiru̍ tistirā̱ṇā |

tī̱vraiḥ somai̱: pari̍ṣiktebhira̱rvāgendrā̍gnī saumana̱sāya̍ yātam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 5

yānī̍ndrāgnī ca̱krathu̍rvī̱ryā̍ṇi̱ yāni̍ rū̱pāṇyu̱ta vṛṣṇyā̍ni |

yā vā̍ṃ pra̱tnāni̍ sa̱khyā śi̱vāni̱ tebhi̱: soma̍sya pibataṃ su̱tasya̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 6

yadabra̍vaṃ pratha̱maṃ vā̍ṃ vṛṇā̱no̱ 3 'yaṃ somo̱ asu̍rairno vi̱havya̍: |

tāṃ sa̱tyāṃ śra̱ddhāma̱bhyā hi yā̱tamathā̱ soma̍sya pibataṃ su̱tasya̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 7

yadi̍ndrāgnī̱ mada̍tha̱: sve du̍ro̱ṇe yad bra̱hmaṇi̱ rāja̍ni vā yajatrā |

ata̱: pari̍ vṛṣaṇā̱vā hi yā̱tamathā̱ soma̍sya pibataṃ su̱tasya̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 8

ata̱: pari̍ vṛṣaṇā̱vā hi yā̱tamathā̱ soma̍sya pibataṃ su̱tasya̍
