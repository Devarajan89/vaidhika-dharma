# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 50

**13 praskaṇvaḥ kāṇvaḥ| sūryaḥ (11-13 rogaghnya upaniṣadaḥ, 13 antyo'rdharcaḥ dviṣad ghnaśca)| gāyatrī, 10-13 anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 1

udu̱ tyaṃ jā̱tave̍dasaṃ de̱vaṃ va̍hanti ke̱tava̍: | dṛ̱śe viśvā̍ya̱ sūrya̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 2

apa̱ tye tā̱yavo̍ yathā̱ nakṣa̍trā yantya̱ktubhi̍: | sūrā̍ya vi̱śvaca̍kṣase

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 3

adṛ̍śramasya ke̱tavo̱ vi ra̱śmayo̱ janā̱m̐ anu̍ | bhrāja̍nto a̱gnayo̍ yathā

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 4

ta̱raṇi̍rvi̱śvada̍rśato jyoti̱ṣkṛda̍si sūrya | viśva̱mā bhā̍si roca̱nam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 5

pra̱tyaṅ de̱vānā̱ṃ viśa̍: pra̱tyaṅṅude̍ṣi̱ mānu̍ṣān | pra̱tyaṅ viśva̱ṃ sva̍rdṛ̱śe

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 6

yenā̍ pāvaka̱ cakṣa̍sā bhura̱ṇyanta̱ṃ janā̱m̐ anu̍ | tvaṃ va̍ruṇa̱ paśya̍si

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 7

vi dyāme̍ṣi̱ raja̍spṛ̱thvahā̱ mimā̍no a̱ktubhi̍: | paśya̱ñjanmā̍ni sūrya

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 8

sa̱pta tvā̍ ha̱rito̱ rathe̱ vaha̍nti deva sūrya | śo̱ciṣke̍śaṃ vicakṣaṇa

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 9

ayu̍kta sa̱pta śu̱ndhyuva̱: sūro̱ ratha̍sya na̱ptya̍: | tābhi̍ryāti̱ svayu̍ktibhiḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 10

udva̱yaṃ tama̍sa̱spari̱ jyoti̱ṣpaśya̍nta̱ utta̍ram | de̱vaṃ de̍va̱trā sūrya̱maga̍nma̱ jyoti̍rutta̱mam

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 11

u̱dyanna̱dya mi̍tramaha ā̱roha̱nnutta̍rā̱ṃ diva̍m | hṛ̱dro̱gaṃ mama̍ sūrya hari̱māṇa̍ṃ ca nāśaya

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 12

śuke̍ṣu me hari̱māṇa̍ṃ ropa̱ṇākā̍su dadhmasi | atho̍ hāridra̱veṣu̍ me hari̱māṇa̱ṃ ni da̍dhmasi

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 13

uda̍gāda̱yamā̍di̱tyo viśve̍na̱ saha̍sā sa̱ha | dvi̱ṣanta̱ṃ mahya̍ṃ ra̱ndhaya̱n mo  a̱haṃ dvi̍ṣa̱te ra̍dham
