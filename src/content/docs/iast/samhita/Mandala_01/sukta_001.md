# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 1

**9 madhucchandā vaiśvāmitraḥ | agniḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 1

a̱gnimī̍l̤e pu̱rohi̍taṃ ya̱jñasya̍ de̱vamṛ̱tvija̍m| hotā̍raṃ ratna̱dhāta̍mam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 2

a̱gniḥ pūrve̍bhi̱rṛṣi̍bhi̱rīḍyo̱ nūta̍nairu̱ta| sa de̱vām̐ eha va̍kṣati

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 3

a̱gninā̍ ra̱yima̍śnava̱t poṣa̍me̱va di̱vedi̍ve| ya̱śasa̍ṃ vī̱rava̍ttamam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 4

agne̱ yaṃ ya̱jñama̍dhva̱raṃ vi̱śvata̍: pari̱bhūrasi̍| sa id de̱veṣu̍ gacchati

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 5

a̱gnirhotā̍ ka̱vikra̍tuḥ sa̱tyaści̱traśra̍vastamaḥ| de̱vo de̱vebhi̱rā ga̍mat

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 6

yada̱ṅga dā̱śuṣe̱ tvamagne̍ bha̱draṃ ka̍ri̱ṣyasi̍| tavet tat sa̱tyama̍ṅgiraḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 7

upa̍ tvāgne di̱vedi̍ve̱ doṣā̍vastardhi̱yā va̱yam| namo̱ bhara̍nta̱ ema̍si

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 8

rāja̍ntamadhva̱rāṇā̍ṃ go̱pāmṛ̱tasya̱ dīdi̍vim| vardha̍māna̱ṃ sve dame̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 9

sa na̍: pi̱teva̍ sū̱nave 'gne̍ sūpāya̱no bha̍va| saca̍svā naḥ sva̱staye̍
