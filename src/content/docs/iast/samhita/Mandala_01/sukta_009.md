# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 9

**10 madhucchandā vaiśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 1

indrehi̱ matsyandha̍so̱ viśve̍bhiḥ soma̱parva̍bhiḥ| ma̱hām̐ a̍bhi̱ṣṭiroja̍sā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 2

eme̍naṃ sṛjatā su̱te ma̱ndimindrā̍ya ma̱ndine̍| cakri̱ṃ viśvā̍ni̱ cakra̍ye

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 3

matsvā̍ suśipra ma̱ndibhi̱: stome̍bhirviśvacarṣaṇe| sacai̱ṣu sava̍ne̱ṣvā

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 4

asṛ̍gramindra te̱ gira̱: prati̱ tvāmuda̍hāsata| ajo̍ṣā vṛṣa̱bhaṃ pati̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 5

saṃ co̍daya ci̱trama̱rvāg rādha̍ indra̱ vare̍ṇyam| asa̱dit te̍ vi̱bhu pra̱bhu

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 6

a̱smāntsu tatra̍ coda̱yendra̍ rā̱ye rabha̍svataḥ| tuvi̍dyumna̱ yaśa̍svataḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 7

saṃ goma̍dindra̱ vāja̍vada̱sme pṛ̱thu śravo̍ bṛ̱hat| vi̱śvāyu̍rdhe̱hyakṣi̍tam

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 8

a̱sme dhe̍hi̱ śravo̍ bṛ̱had dyu̱mnaṃ sa̍hasra̱sāta̍mam| indra̱ tā ra̱thinī̱riṣa̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 9

vaso̱rindra̱ṃ vasu̍patiṃ gī̱rbhirgṛ̱ṇanta̍ ṛ̱gmiya̍m| homa̱ gantā̍ramū̱taye̍

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 10

su̱te su̍te̱ nyo̍kase bṛ̱had bṛ̍ha̱ta eda̱riḥ| indrā̍ya śū̱ṣama̍rcati
