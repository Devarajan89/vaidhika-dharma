# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 117

**25 kakṣīvān dairghatamasa auśijaḥ| aśvinau| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 117 | **Verse:** 1

madhva̱: soma̍syāśvinā̱ madā̍ya pra̱tno hotā vi̍vāsate vām |
ba̱rhiṣma̍tī rā̱tirviśri̍tā̱ gīri̱ṣā yā̍taṃ nāsa̱tyopa̱ vājai̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 117 | **Verse:** 2

yo vā̍maśvinā̱ mana̍so̱ javī̍yā̱n ratha̱: svaśvo̱ viśa̍ ā̱jigā̍ti |

yena̱ gaccha̍thaḥ su̱kṛto̍ duro̱ṇaṃ tena̍ narā va̱rtira̱smabhya̍ṃ yātam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 117 | **Verse:** 3

mi̱nantā̱ dasyo̱raśi̍vasya mā̱yā a̍nupū̱rvaṃ vṛ̍ṣaṇā co̱daya̍ntā

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 117 | **Verse:** 4

aśva̱ṃ na gū̱hlama̍śvinā du̱revī̱rṛṣi̍ṃ narā vṛṣaṇā re̱bhama̱psu |

saṃ taṃ ri̍ṇītho̱ vipru̍ta̱ṃ daṃso̍bhi̱rna vā̍ṃ jūryanti pū̱rvyā kṛ̱tāni̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 117 | **Verse:** 5

su̱ṣu̱pvāṃsa̱ṃ na nirṛ̍teru̱pasthe̱ sūrya̱ṃ na da̍srā̱ tama̍si kṣi̱yanta̍m |

śu̱bhe ru̱kmaṃ na da̍rśa̱taṃ nikhā̍ta̱mudū̍pathuraśvinā̱ vanda̍nāya

## Verse 6
