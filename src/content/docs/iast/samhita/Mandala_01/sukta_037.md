# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 37

**15 kaṇvo ghauraḥ| marutaḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 1

krī̱l̤aṃ va̱: śardho̱ māru̍tamana̱rvāṇa̍ṃ rathe̱śubha̍m | kaṇvā̍ a̱bhi pra gā̍yata

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 2

ye pṛṣa̍tībhirṛ̱ṣṭibhi̍: sā̱kaṃ vāśī̍bhira̱ñjibhi̍: | ajā̍yanta̱ svabhā̍navaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 3

i̱heva̍ śṛṇva eṣā̱ṃ kaśā̱ haste̍ṣu̱ yad vadā̍n | ni yāma̍ñci̱tramṛ̍ñjate

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 4

pra va̱: śardhā̍ya̱ ghṛṣva̍ye tve̱ṣadyu̍mnāya śu̱ṣmiṇe̍ | de̱vatta̱ṃ brahma̍ gāyata

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 5

pra śa̍ṃsā̱ goṣvaghnya̍ṃ krī̱l̤aṃ yacchardho̱ māru̍tam | jambhe̱ rasa̍sya vāvṛdhe

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 6

ko vo̱ varṣi̍ṣṭha̱ ā na̍ro di̱vaśca̱ gmaśca̍ dhūtayaḥ | yatsī̱manta̱ṃ na dhū̍nu̱tha

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 7

ni vo̱ yāmā̍ya̱ mānu̍ṣo da̱dhra u̱grāya̍ ma̱nyave̍ | jihī̍ta̱ parva̍to gi̱riḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 8

yeṣā̱majme̍ṣu pṛthi̱vī ju̍ju̱rvām̐ i̍va vi̱śpati̍: | bhi̱yā yāme̍ṣu̱ reja̍te

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 9

sthi̱raṃ hi jāna̍meṣā̱ṃ vayo̍ mā̱turnire̍tave | yat sī̱manu̍ dvi̱tā śava̍:

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 10

udu̱ tye sū̱navo̱ gira̱: kāṣṭhā̱ ajme̍ṣvatnata | vā̱śrā a̍bhi̱jñu yāta̍ve

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 11

tyaṃ ci̍d ghā dī̱rghaṃ pṛ̱thuṃ mi̱ho napā̍ta̱mamṛ̍dhram | pra cyā̍vayanti̱ yāma̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 12

maru̍to̱ yaddha̍ vo̱ bala̱ṃ janā̍m̐ acucyavītana | gi̱rīm̐ra̍cucyavītana

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 13

yaddha̱ yānti̍ ma̱ruta̱: saṃ ha̍ bruva̱te'dhva̱nnā | śṛ̱ṇoti̱ kaści̍deṣām

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 14

pra yā̍ta̱ śībha̍mā̱śubhi̱: santi̱ kaṇve̍ṣu vo̱ duva̍: | tatro̱ ṣu mā̍dayādhvai

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 15

asti̱ hi ṣmā̱ madā̍ya va̱: smasi̍ ṣmā va̱yame̍ṣām | viśva̍ṃ ci̱dāyu̍rjī̱vase̍
