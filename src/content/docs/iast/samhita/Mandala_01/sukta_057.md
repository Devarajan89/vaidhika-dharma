# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 57

**6 savya āṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 1

pra maṃhi̍ṣṭhāya bṛha̱te bṛ̱hadra̍ye sa̱tyaśu̍ṣmāya ta̱vase̍ ma̱tiṃ bha̍re |
a̱pāmi̍va prava̱ṇe yasya̍ du̱rdhara̱ṃ rādho̍ vi̱śvāyu̱ śava̍se̱ apā̍vṛtam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 2

adha̍ te̱ viśva̱manu̍ hāsadi̱ṣṭaya̱ āpo̍ ni̱mneva̱ sava̍nā ha̱viṣma̍taḥ |

yat parva̍te̱ na sa̱maśī̍ta harya̱ta indra̍sya̱ vajra̱: śnathi̍tā hira̱ṇyaya̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 3

a̱smai bhī̱māya̱ nama̍sā̱ sama̍dhva̱ra uṣo̱ na śu̍bhra̱ ā bha̍rā̱ panī̍yase |

yasya̱ dhāma̱ śrava̍se̱ nāme̍ndri̱yaṃ jyoti̱rakā̍ri ha̱rito̱ nāya̍se

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 4

i̱me ta̍ indra̱ te va̱yaṃ pu̍ruṣṭuta̱ ye tvā̱rabhya̱ carā̍masi prabhūvaso |

na̱hi tvada̱nyo gi̍rvaṇo̱ gira̱: sagha̍t kṣo̱ṇīri̍va̱ prati̍ no harya̱ tad vaca̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 5

bhūri̍ ta indra vī̱ryaṃ 1 tava̍ smasya̱sya sto̱turma̍ghava̱n kāma̱mā pṛ̍ṇa |

anu̍ te̱ dyaurbṛ̍ha̱tī vī̱rya̍ṃ mama i̱yaṃ ca̍ te pṛthi̱vī ne̍ma̱ oja̍se

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 6

tvaṃ tami̍ndra̱ parva̍taṃ ma̱hāmu̱ruṃ vajre̍ṇa vajrin parva̱śaśca̍kartitha |

avā̍sṛjo̱ nivṛ̍tā̱: sarta̱vā a̱paḥ sa̱trā viśva̍ṃ dadhiṣe̱ keva̍la̱ṃ saha̍:
