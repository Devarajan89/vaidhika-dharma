# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 55

**8 savya āṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 1

di̱vaści̍dasya vari̱mā vi pa̍pratha̱ indra̱ṃ na ma̱hnā pṛ̍thi̱vī ca̱na prati̍ |
bhī̱mastuvi̍ṣmāñcarṣa̱ṇibhya̍ āta̱paḥ śiśī̍te̱ vajra̱ṃ teja̍se̱ na vaṃsa̍gaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 2

so a̍rṇa̱vo na na̱dya̍: samu̱driya̱: prati̍ gṛbhṇāti̱ viśri̍tā̱ varī̍mabhiḥ |

indra̱: soma̍sya pī̱taye̍ vṛṣāyate sa̱nāt sa yu̱dhma oja̍sā panasyate

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 3

tvaṃ tami̍ndra̱ parva̍ta̱ṃ na bhoja̍se ma̱ho nṛ̱mṇasya̱ dharma̍ṇāmirajyasi |

pra vī̱rye̍ṇa de̱vatāti̍ cekite̱ viśva̍smā u̱graḥ karma̍ṇe pu̱rohi̍taḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 4

sa id vane̍ nama̱syubhi̍rvacasyate̱ cāru̱ jane̍ṣu prabruvā̱ṇa i̍ndri̱yam |

vṛṣā̱ chandu̍rbhavati harya̱to vṛṣā̱ kṣeme̍ṇa̱ dhenā̍ṃ ma̱ghavā̱ yadinva̍ti

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 5

sa inma̱hāni̍ sami̱thāni̍ ma̱jmanā̍ kṛ̱ṇoti̍ yu̱dhma oja̍sā̱ jane̍bhyaḥ |

adhā̍ ca̱na śrad da̍dhati̱ tviṣī̍mata̱ indrā̍ya̱ vajra̍ṃ ni̱ghani̍ghnate va̱dham

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 6

sa hi śra̍va̱syuḥ sada̍nāni kṛ̱trimā̍ kṣma̱yā vṛ̍dhā̱na oja̍sā vinā̱śaya̍n |

jyotī̍ṃṣi kṛ̱ṇvanna̍vṛ̱kāṇi̱ yajya̱ve 'va̍ su̱kratu̱: sarta̱vā a̱paḥ sṛ̍jat

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 7

dā̱nāya̱ mana̍: somapāvannastu te̱ 'rvāñcā̱ harī̍ vandanaśru̱dā kṛ̍dhi |

yami̍ṣṭhāsa̱: sāra̍thayo̱ ya i̍ndra te̱ na tvā̱ ketā̱ ā da̍bhnuvanti̱ bhūrṇa̍yaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 8

apra̍kṣita̱ṃ vasu̍ bibharṣi̱ hasta̍yo̱raṣā̍hl̤a̱ṃ saha̍sta̱nvi̍ śru̱to da̍dhe |

āvṛ̍tāso'va̱tāso̱ na ka̱rtṛbhi̍sta̱nūṣu̍ te̱ krata̍va indra̱ bhūra̍yaḥ
