# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 25

**21 ājīgartiḥ śunaḥ śepaḥ sa kṛtrimo vaiśvāmitro devarātaḥ| varuṇaḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 1

yacci̱ddhi te̱ viśo̍ yathā̱ pra de̍va varuṇa vra̱tam| mi̱nī̱masi̱ dyavi̍dyavi

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 2

## Verse 4

## Verse 6
