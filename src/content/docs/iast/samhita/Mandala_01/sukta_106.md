# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 106

**7 kutsa āṅgirasaḥ| viśve devāḥ| jagatī, 7 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 1

indra̍ṃ mi̱traṃ varu̍ṇama̱gnimū̱taye̱ māru̍ta̱ṃ śardho̱ adi̍tiṃ havāmahe |
ratha̱ṃ na du̱rgād va̍savaḥ sudānavo̱ viśva̍smānno̱ aṃha̍so̱ niṣpi̍partana

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 2

ta ā̍dityā̱ ā ga̍tā sa̱rvatā̍taye bhū̱ta de̍vā vṛtra̱tūrye̍ṣu śa̱mbhuva̍: |

ratha̱ṃ na du̱rgād va̍savaḥ sudānavo̱ viśva̍smānno̱ aṃha̍so̱ niṣpi̍partana

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 3

ava̍ntu naḥ pi̱tara̍: supravāca̱nā u̱ta de̱vī de̱vapu̍tre ṛtā̱vṛdhā̍ |

ratha̱ṃ na du̱rgād va̍savaḥ sudānavo̱ viśva̍smānno̱ aṃha̍so̱ niṣpi̍partana

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 4

narā̱śaṃsa̍ṃ vā̱jina̍ṃ vā̱jaya̍nni̱ha kṣa̱yadvī̍raṃ pū̱ṣaṇa̍ṃ su̱mnairī̍mahe |

ratha̱ṃ na du̱rgād va̍savaḥ sudānavo̱ viśva̍smānno̱ aṃha̍so̱ niṣpi̍partana

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 5

bṛha̍spate̱ sada̱minna̍: su̱gaṃ kṛ̍dhi̱ śaṃ yoryat te̱ manu̍rhita̱ṃ tadī̍mahe |

ratha̱ṃ na du̱rgād va̍savaḥ sudānavo̱ viśva̍smānno̱ aṃha̍so̱ niṣpi̍partana

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 6

indra̱ṃ kutso̍ vṛtra̱haṇa̱ṃ śacī̱pati̍ṃ kā̱ṭe nibā̍hla̱ ṛṣi̍rahvadū̱taye̍ |

ratha̱ṃ na du̱rgād va̍savaḥ sudānavo̱ viśva̍smānno̱ aṃha̍so̱ niṣpi̍partana

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 7

tanno̍ mi̱tro varu̍ṇo māmahantā̱madi̍ti̱: sindhu̍: pṛthi̱vī u̱ta dyauḥ
