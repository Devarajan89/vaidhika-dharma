# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 80

**16 gotamo rāhūgaṇaḥ| indraḥ, 16 indraḥ, (atharvā, manuḥ dadhyaṅ ca)| paṃktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 80 | **Verse:** 1

i̱tthā hi soma̱ inmade̍ bra̱hmā ca̱kāra̱ vardha̍nam |
śavi̍ṣṭha vajri̱nnoja̍sā pṛthi̱vyā niḥ śa̍śā̱ ahi̱marca̱nnanu̍ sva̱rājya̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 80 | **Verse:** 2

yenā̍ vṛ̱traṃ nira̱dbhyo ja̱ghantha̍ vajri̱nnoja̱sārca̱nnanu̍ sva̱rājya̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 80 | **Verse:** 3

indra̍ nṛ̱mṇaṃ hi te̱ śavo̱ hano̍ vṛ̱traṃ jayā̍ a̱po 'rca̱nnanu̍ sva̱rājya̍m

## Verse 4

## Verse 5
