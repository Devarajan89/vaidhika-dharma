# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 61

**16 nodhā gautamaḥ| indraḥ|triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 1

a̱smā idu̱ pra ta̱vase̍ tu̱rāya̱ prayo̱ na ha̍rmi̱ stoma̱ṃ māhi̍nāya |
ṛcī̍ṣamā̱yādhri̍gava̱ oha̱mindrā̍ya̱ brahmā̍ṇi rā̱tata̍mā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 2

a̱smā idu̱ praya̍ iva̱ pra ya̍ṃsi̱ bharā̍myāṅgū̱ṣaṃ bādhe̍ suvṛ̱kti |

indrā̍ya hṛ̱dā mana̍sā manī̱ṣā pra̱tnāya̱ patye̱ dhiyo̍ marjayanta

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 3

a̱smā idu̱ tyamu̍pa̱maṃ sva̱rṣāṃ bharā̍myāṅgū̱ṣamā̱sye̍na |

maṃhi̍ṣṭha̱maccho̍ktibhirmatī̱nāṃ su̍vṛ̱ktibhi̍: sū̱riṃ vā̍vṛ̱dhadhyai̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 4

a̱smā idu̱ stoma̱ṃ saṃ hi̍nomi̱ ratha̱ṃ na taṣṭe̍va̱ tatsi̍nāya |

gira̍śca̱ girvā̍hase suvṛ̱ktīndrā̍ya viśvami̱nvaṃ medhi̍rāya

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 5

a̱smā idu̱ sapti̍miva śrava̱syendrā̍yā̱rkaṃ ju̱hvā̱ 3 sama̍ñje |

vī̱raṃ dā̱nauka̍saṃ va̱ndadhyai̍ pu̱rāṃ gū̱rtaśra̍vasaṃ da̱rmāṇa̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 6

a̱smā idu̱ tvaṣṭā̍ takṣa̱d vajra̱ṃ svapa̍stamaṃ sva̱ryaṃ 1 raṇā̍ya |

vṛ̱trasya̍ cid vi̱dadyena̱ marma̍ tu̱jannīśā̍nastuja̱tā ki̍ye̱dhāḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 7

a̱syedu̍ mā̱tuḥ sava̍neṣu sa̱dyo ma̱haḥ pi̱tuṃ pa̍pi̱vāñcārvannā̍ |

mu̱ṣā̱yad viṣṇu̍: paca̱taṃ sahī̍yā̱n vidhya̍d varā̱haṃ ti̱ro adri̱mastā̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 8

a̱smā idu̱ gnāści̍d de̱vapa̍tnī̱rindrā̍yā̱rkama̍hi̱hatya̍ ūvuḥ |

pari̱ dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī ja̍bhra u̱rvī nāsya̱ te ma̍hi̱māna̱ṃ pari̍ ṣṭaḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 9

a̱syede̱va pra ri̍rice mahi̱tvaṃ di̱vaspṛ̍thi̱vyāḥ parya̱ntari̍kṣāt |

sva̱rāl̤indro̱ dama̱ ā vi̱śvagū̍rtaḥ sva̱rirama̍tro vavakṣe̱ raṇā̍ya

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 10

a̱syede̱va śava̍sā śu̱ṣanta̱ṃ vi vṛ̍śca̱d vajre̍ṇa vṛ̱tramindra̍: |

gā na vrā̱ṇā a̱vanī̍ramuñcada̱bhi śravo̍ dā̱vane̱ sace̍tāḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 11

a̱syedu̍ tve̱ṣasā̍ ranta̱ sindha̍va̱: pari̱ yad vajre̍ṇa sī̱maya̍cchat |

ī̱śā̱na̱kṛd dā̱śuṣe̍ daśa̱syan tu̱rvīta̍ye gā̱dhaṃ tu̱rvaṇi̍: kaḥ

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 12

a̱smā idu̱ pra bha̍rā̱ tūtu̍jāno vṛ̱trāya̱ vajra̱mīśā̍naḥ kiye̱dhāḥ |

gorna parva̱ vi ra̍dā tira̱śceṣya̱nnarṇā̍ṃsya̱pāṃ ca̱radhyai̍

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 13

a̱syedu̱ pra brū̍hi pū̱rvyāṇi̍ tu̱rasya̱ karmā̍ṇi̱ navya̍ u̱kthaiḥ |

yu̱dhe yadi̍ṣṇā̱na āyu̍dhānyṛghā̱yamā̍ṇo niri̱ṇāti̱ śatrū̍n

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 14

a̱syedu̍ bhi̱yā gi̱raya̍śca dṛ̱hlā dyāvā̍ ca̱ bhūmā̍ ja̱nuṣa̍stujete |

upo̍ ve̱nasya̱ jogu̍vāna o̱ṇiṃ sa̱dyo bhu̍vad vī̱ryā̍ya no̱dhāḥ

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 15

a̱smā idu̱ tyadanu̍ dāyyeṣā̱meko̱ yad va̱vne bhūre̱rīśā̍naḥ |

praita̍śa̱ṃ sūrye̍ paspṛdhā̱naṃ sauva̍śvye̱ suṣvi̍māva̱dindra̍:

---

## Verse 16

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 16

e̱vā te̍ hāriyojanā suvṛ̱ktīndra̱ brahmā̍ṇi̱ gota̍māso akran |

aiṣu̍ vi̱śvape̍śasa̱ṃ dhiya̍ṃ dhāḥ prā̱tarma̱kṣū dhi̱yāva̍surjagamyāt
