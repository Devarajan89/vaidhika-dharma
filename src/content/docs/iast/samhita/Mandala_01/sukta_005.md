# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 5

**10 madhucchandā vaiśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 1

ā tvetā̱ ni ṣī̍da̱tendra̍ma̱bhi pra gā̍yata| sakhā̍ya̱: stoma̍vāhasaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 2

pu̱rū̱tama̍ṃ purū̱ṇāmīśā̍na̱ṃ vāryā̍ṇām| indra̱ṃ some̱ sacā̍ su̱te

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 3

sa ghā̍ no̱ yoga̱ ā bhu̍va̱t sa rā̱ye sa pura̍ṃdhyām| gama̱dvāje̍bhi̱rā sa na̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 4

yasya̍ sa̱ṃsthe na vṛ̱ṇvate̱ harī̍ sa̱matsu̱ śatra̍vaḥ| tasmā̱ indrā̍ya gāyata

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 5

su̱ta̱pāvne̍ su̱tā i̱me śuca̍yo yanti vī̱taye̍| somā̍so̱ dadhyā̍śiraḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 6

tvaṃ su̱tasya̍ pī̱taye̍ sa̱dyo vṛ̱ddho a̍jāyathāḥ| indra̱ jyaiṣṭhyā̍ya sukrato

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 7

ā tvā̍ viśantvā̱śava̱: somā̍sa indra girvaṇaḥ| śaṃ te̍ santu̱ prace̍tase

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 8

tvāṃ stomā̍ avīvṛdha̱n tvāmu̱kthā śa̍takrato| tvāṃ va̍rdhantu no̱ gira̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 9

akṣi̍totiḥ sanedi̱maṃ vāja̱mindra̍: saha̱sriṇa̍m| yasmi̱n viśvā̍ni̱ pauṃsyā̍

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 10

mā no̱ martā̍ a̱bhi dru̍hanta̱nūnā̍mindra girvaṇaḥ| īśā̍no yavayā va̱dham
