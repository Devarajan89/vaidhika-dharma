# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 141

**13 dīrghatamā aucathyaḥ| agniḥ| jagatī, 12-13 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 1

bal̤i̱tthā tad vapu̍ṣe dhāyi darśa̱taṃ de̱vasya̱ bharga̱: saha̍so̱ yato̱ jani̍ |
yadī̱mupa̱ hvara̍te̱ sādha̍te ma̱tirṛ̱tasya̱ dhenā̍ anayanta sa̱sruta̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 2

pṛ̱kṣo vapu̍: pitu̱mān nitya̱ ā śa̍ye dvi̱tīya̱mā sa̱ptaśi̍vāsu mā̱tṛṣu̍ |

tṛ̱tīya̍masya vṛṣa̱bhasya̍ do̱hase̱ daśa̍pramatiṃ janayanta̱ yoṣa̍ṇaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 3

niryadī̍ṃ bu̱dhnānma̍hi̱ṣasya̱ varpa̍sa īśā̱nāsa̱: śava̍sā̱ kranta̍ sū̱raya̍: |

yadī̱manu̍ pra̱divo̱ madhva̍ ādha̱ve guhā̱ santa̍ṃ māta̱riśvā̍ mathā̱yati̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 4

pra yat pi̱tuḥ pa̍ra̱mānnī̱yate̱ paryā pṛ̱kṣudho̍ vī̱rudho̱ daṃsu̍ rohati |

u̱bhā yada̍sya ja̱nuṣa̱ṃ yadinva̍ta̱ ādid yavi̍ṣṭho abhavad ghṛ̱ṇā śuci̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 5

ādinmā̱tṝrāvi̍śa̱d yāsvā śuci̱rahi̍ṃsyamāna urvi̱yā vi vā̍vṛdhe |

anu̱ yat pūrvā̱ aru̍hat sanā̱juvo̱ ni navya̍sī̱ṣvava̍rāsu dhāvate

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 6

ādiddhotā̍raṃ vṛṇate̱ divi̍ṣṭiṣu̱ bhaga̍miva papṛcā̱nāsa̍ ṛñjate |

de̱vān yat kratvā̍ ma̱jmanā̍ puruṣṭu̱to marta̱ṃ śaṃsa̍ṃ vi̱śvadhā̱ veti̱ dhāya̍se

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 7

vi yadasthā̍d yaja̱to vāta̍codito hvā̱ro na vakvā̍ ja̱raṇā̱ anā̍kṛtaḥ |

tasya̱ patma̍n da̱kṣuṣa̍: kṛ̱ṣṇaja̍ṃhasa̱: śuci̍janmano̱ raja̱ ā vya̍dhvanaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 8

ratho̱ na yā̱taḥ śikva̍bhiḥ kṛ̱to dyāmaṅge̍bhiraru̱ṣebhi̍rīyate |

āda̍sya̱ te kṛ̱ṣṇāso̍ dakṣi sū̱raya̱: śūra̍syeva tve̱ṣathā̍dīṣate̱ vaya̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 9

tvayā̱ hya̍gne̱ varu̍ṇo dhṛ̱tavra̍to mi̱traḥ śā̍śa̱dre a̍rya̱mā su̱dāna̍vaḥ |

yat sī̱manu̱ kratu̍nā vi̱śvathā̍ vi̱bhura̱rānna ne̱miḥ pa̍ri̱bhūrajā̍yathāḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 10

tvama̍gne śaśamā̱nāya̍ sunva̱te ratna̍ṃ yaviṣṭha de̱vatā̍timinvasi |

taṃ tvā̱ nu navya̍ṃ sahaso yuvan va̱yaṃ bhaga̱ṃ na kā̱re ma̍hiratna dhīmahi

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 11

a̱sme ra̱yiṃ na svartha̱ṃ damū̍nasa̱ṃ bhaga̱ṃ dakṣa̱ṃ na pa̍pṛcāsi dharṇa̱sim |

ra̱śmīm̐ri̍va̱ yo yama̍ti̱ janma̍nī u̱bhe de̱vānā̱ṃ śaṃsa̍mṛ̱ta ā ca̍ su̱kratu̍:

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 12

u̱ta na̍: su̱dyotmā̍ jī̱rāśvo̱ hotā̍ ma̱ndraḥ śṛ̍ṇavacca̱ndrara̍thaḥ |

sa no̍ neṣa̱nneṣa̍tamai̱ramū̍ro̱ 'gnirvā̱maṃ su̍vi̱taṃ vasyo̱ accha̍

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 13

astā̍vya̱gniḥ śimī̍vadbhira̱rkaiḥ sāmrā̍jyāya prata̱raṃ dadhā̍naḥ |

a̱mī ca̱ ye ma̱ghavā̍no va̱yaṃ ca̱ miha̱ṃ na sūro̱ ati̱ niṣṭa̍tanyuḥ
