# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 71

**10 parāśaraḥ śāktyaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 1

upa̱ pra ji̍nvannuśa̱tīru̱śanta̱ṃ pati̱ṃ na nitya̱ṃ jana̍ya̱: sanī̍l̤āḥ |
svasā̍ra̱: śyāvī̱maru̍ṣīmajuṣrañ ci̱tramu̱cchantī̍mu̱ṣasa̱ṃ na gāva̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 2

vī̱l̤u ci̍d dṛ̱hlā pi̱taro̍ na u̱kthairadri̍ṃ ruja̱nnaṅgi̍raso̱ rave̍ṇa |

ca̱krurdi̱vo bṛ̍ha̱to gā̱tuma̱sme aha̱: sva̍rvividuḥ ke̱tumu̱srāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 3

dadha̍nnṛ̱taṃ dha̱naya̍nnasya dhī̱timādida̱ryo di̍dhi̱ṣvo̱ 3 vibhṛ̍trāḥ |

atṛ̍ṣyantīra̱paso̍ ya̱ntyacchā̍ de̱vāñjanma̱ praya̍sā va̱rdhaya̍ntīḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 4

mathī̱d yadī̱ṃ vibhṛ̍to māta̱riśvā̍ gṛ̱hegṛ̍he śye̱to jenyo̱ bhūt |

ādī̱ṃ rājñe̱ na sahī̍yase̱ sacā̱ sannā dū̱tyaṃ 1 bhṛga̍vāṇo vivāya

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 5

ma̱he yat pi̱tra ī̱ṃ rasa̍ṃ di̱ve karava̍ tsarat pṛśa̱nya̍ściki̱tvān |

sṛ̱jadastā̍ dhṛṣa̱tā di̱dyuma̍smai̱ svāyā̍ṃ de̱vo du̍hi̱tari̱ tviṣi̍ṃ dhāt

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 6

sva ā yastubhya̱ṃ dama̱ ā vi̱bhāti̱ namo̍ vā̱ dāśā̍duśa̱to anu̱ dyūn |

vardho̍ agne̱ vayo̍ asya dvi̱barhā̱ yāsa̍d rā̱yā sa̱ratha̱ṃ yaṃ ju̱nāsi̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 7

a̱gniṃ viśvā̍ a̱bhi pṛkṣa̍: sacante samu̱draṃ na sra̱vata̍: sa̱pta ya̱hvīḥ |

na jā̱mibhi̱rvi ci̍kite̱ vayo̍ no vi̱dā de̱veṣu̱ prama̍tiṃ ciki̱tvān

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 8

ā yadi̱ṣe nṛ̱pati̱ṃ teja̱ āna̱ṭ chuci̱ reto̱ niṣi̍kta̱ṃ dyaura̱bhīke̍ |

a̱gniḥ śardha̍manava̱dyaṃ yuvā̍naṃ svā̱dhya̍ṃ janayat sū̱daya̍cca

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 9

mano̱ na yo'dhva̍naḥ sa̱dya etyeka̍: sa̱trā sūro̱ vasva̍ īśe |

rājā̍nā mi̱trāvaru̍ṇā supā̱ṇī goṣu̍ pri̱yama̱mṛta̱ṃ rakṣa̍māṇā

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 10

mā no̍ agne sa̱khyā pitryā̍ṇi̱ pra ma̍rṣiṣṭhā a̱bhi vi̱duṣka̱viḥ san |

nabho̱ na rū̱paṃ ja̍ri̱mā mi̍nāti pu̱rā tasyā̍ a̱bhiśa̍ste̱radhī̍hi
