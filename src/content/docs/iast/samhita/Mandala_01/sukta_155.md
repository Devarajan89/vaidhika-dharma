# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 155

**6 dīrghatamā aucathyaḥ| viṣṇuḥ|1-3 indrāviṣṇū| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 155 | **Verse:** 1

pra va̱: pānta̱mandha̍so dhiyāya̱te ma̱he śūrā̍ya̱ viṣṇa̍ve cārcata |
yā sānu̍ni̱ parva̍tānā̱madā̍bhyā ma̱hasta̱sthatu̱rarva̍teva sā̱dhunā̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 155 | **Verse:** 2

tve̱ṣami̱tthā sa̱mara̍ṇa̱ṃ śimī̍vato̱rindrā̍viṣṇū suta̱pā vā̍muruṣyati |

yā martyā̍ya pratidhī̱yamā̍na̱mit kṛ̱śāno̱rastu̍rasa̱nāmu̍ru̱ṣyatha̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 155 | **Verse:** 3

tā ī̍ṃ vardhanti̱ mahya̍sya̱ pauṃsya̱ṃ ni mā̱tarā̍ nayati̱ reta̍se bhu̱je |

dadhā̍ti pu̱tro'va̍ra̱ṃ para̍ṃ pi̱turnāma̍ tṛ̱tīya̱madhi̍ roca̱ne di̱vaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 155 | **Verse:** 4

tatta̱dida̍sya̱ pauṃsya̍ṃ gṛṇīmasī̱nasya̍ trā̱tura̍vṛ̱kasya̍ mī̱l̤ahuṣa̍: |

yaḥ pārthi̍vāni tri̱bhirid vigā̍mabhiru̱ru krami̍ṣṭorugā̱yāya̍ jī̱vase̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 155 | **Verse:** 5

dve ida̍sya̱ krama̍ṇe sva̱rdṛśo̍ 'bhi̱khyāya̱ martyo̍ bhuraṇyati |

tṛ̱tīya̍masya̱ naki̱rā da̍dharṣati̱ vaya̍śca̱na pa̱taya̍ntaḥ pata̱triṇa̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 155 | **Verse:** 6

ca̱turbhi̍: sā̱kaṃ na̍va̱tiṃ ca̱ nāma̍bhiśca̱kraṃ na vṛ̱ttaṃ vyatī̍m̐ravīvipat |

bṛ̱haccha̍rīro vi̱mimā̍na̱ ṛkva̍bhi̱ryuvāku̍māra̱: pratye̍tyāha̱vam
