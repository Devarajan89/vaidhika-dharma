# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 123

**13 kakṣīvān dairghatamasa auśijaḥ| uṣāḥ | triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 123 | **Verse:** 1

pṛ̱thū ratho̱ dakṣi̍ṇāyā ayo̱jyaina̍ṃ de̱vāso̍ a̱mṛtā̍so asthuḥ |
kṛ̱ṣṇāduda̍sthāda̱ryā̱ 3 vihā̍yā̱ściki̍tsantī̱ mānu̍ṣāya̱ kṣayā̍ya

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 123 | **Verse:** 2

pūrvā̱ viśva̍smā̱d bhuva̍nādabodhi̱ jaya̍ntī̱ vāja̍ṃ bṛha̱tī sanu̍trī |

u̱ccā vya̍khyad yuva̱tiḥ pu̍na̱rbhūroṣā a̍gan pratha̱mā pū̱rvahū̍tau

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 123 | **Verse:** 3

yada̱dya bhā̱gaṃ vi̱bhajā̍si̱ nṛbhya̱ uṣo̍ devi martya̱trā su̍jāte |

de̱vo no̱ atra̍ savi̱tā damū̍nā̱ anā̍gaso vocati̱ sūryā̍ya

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 123 | **Verse:** 4

gṛ̱haṃgṛ̍hamaha̱nā yā̱tyacchā̍ di̱vedi̍ve̱ adhi̱ nāmā̱ dadhā̍nā |

siṣā̍santī dyota̱nā śaśva̱dāgā̱dagra̍magra̱mid bha̍jate̱ vasū̍nām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 123 | **Verse:** 5

bhaga̍sya̱ svasā̱ varu̍ṇasya jā̱miruṣa̍: sūnṛte pratha̱mā ja̍rasva |

pa̱ścā sa da̍ghyā̱ yo a̱ghasya̍ dhā̱tā jaye̍ma̱ taṃ dakṣi̍ṇayā̱ rathe̍na

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 123 | **Verse:** 6

udī̍ratāṃ sū̱nṛtā̱ ut pura̍ndhī̱ruda̱gnaya̍: śuśucā̱nāso̍ asthuḥ |

spā̱rhā vasū̍ni̱ tama̱sāpa̍gūhla̱viṣkṛ̍ṇvantyu̱ṣaso̍ vibhā̱tīḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 123 | **Verse:** 7

apā̱nyadetya̱bhya 1 nyade̍ti̱ viṣu̍rūpe̱ aha̍nī̱ saṃ ca̍rete |

pa̱ri̱kṣito̱stamo̍ a̱nyā guhā̍ka̱radyau̍du̱ṣāḥ śośu̍catā̱ rathe̍na

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 123 | **Verse:** 8

sa̱dṛśī̍ra̱dya sa̱dṛśī̱ridu̱ śvo dī̱rghaṃ sa̍cante̱ varu̍ṇasya̱ dhāma̍ |

a̱na̱va̱dyāstri̱ṃśata̱ṃ yoja̍nā̱nyekai̍kā̱ kratu̱ṃ pari̍ yanti sa̱dyaḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 123 | **Verse:** 9

jā̱na̱tyahna̍: pratha̱masya̱ nāma̍ śu̱krā kṛ̱ṣṇāda̍janiṣṭa śvitī̱cī |

ṛ̱tasya̱ yoṣā̱ na mi̍nāti̱ dhāmāha̍raharniṣkṛ̱tamā̱cara̍ntī

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 123 | **Verse:** 10

ka̱nye̍va ta̱nvā̱ 3 śāśa̍dānā̱m̐ eṣi̍ devi de̱vamiya̍kṣamāṇam |

sa̱ṃsmaya̍mānā yuva̱tiḥ pu̱rastā̍dā̱virvakṣā̍ṃsi kṛṇuṣe vibhā̱tī

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 123 | **Verse:** 11

su̱sa̱ṃkā̱śā mā̱tṛmṛ̍ṣṭeva̱ yoṣā̱vista̱nva̍ṃ kṛṇuṣe dṛ̱śe kam |

bha̱drā tvamu̍ṣo vita̱raṃ vyu̍ccha̱ na tatte̍ a̱nyā u̱ṣaso̍ naśanta

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 123 | **Verse:** 12

aśvā̍vatī̱rgoma̍tīrvi̱śvavā̍rā̱ yata̍mānā ra̱śmibhi̱: sūrya̍sya |

parā̍ ca̱ yanti̱ puna̱rā ca̍ yanti bha̱drā nāma̱ vaha̍mānā u̱ṣāsa̍:

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 123 | **Verse:** 13

ṛ̱tasya̍ ra̱śmima̍nu̱yaccha̍mānā bha̱drambha̍dra̱ṃ kratu̍ma̱smāsu̍ dhehi |

uṣo̍ no a̱dya su̱havā̱ vyu̍cchā̱smāsu̱ rāyo̍ ma̱ghava̍tsu ca syuḥ
