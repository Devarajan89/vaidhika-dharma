# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 127

**11 parucchepo daivodāsiḥ | agniḥ| atyaṣṭiḥ, 6 atidhṛtiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 1

a̱gniṃ hotā̍raṃ manye̱ dāsva̍nta̱ṃ vasu̍ṃ sū̱nuṃ saha̍so jā̱tave̍dasa̱ṃ vipra̱ṃ na jā̱tave̍dasam |
ya ū̱rdhvayā̍ svadhva̱ro de̱vo de̱vācyā̍ kṛ̱pā |
ghṛ̱tasya̱ vibhrā̍ṣṭi̱manu̍ vaṣṭi śo̱ciṣā̱ ''juhvā̍nasya sa̱rpiṣa̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 2

yaji̍ṣṭhaṃ tvā̱ yaja̍mānā huvema̱ jyeṣṭha̱maṅgi̍rasāṃ vipra̱ manma̍bhi̱rvipre̍bhiḥ śukra̱ manma̍bhiḥ |

pari̍jmānamiva̱ dyāṃ hotā̍raṃ carṣaṇī̱nām |

śo̱ciṣke̍śa̱ṃ vṛṣa̍ṇa̱ṃ yami̱mā viśa̱: prāva̍ntu jū̱taye̱ viśa̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 3

sa hi pu̱rū ci̱doja̍sā vi̱rukma̍tā̱ dīdyā̍no̱ bhava̍ti druhaṃta̱raḥ pa̍ra̱śurna dru̍haṃta̱raḥ |

vī̱l̤u ci̱d yasya̱ samṛ̍tau̱ śruva̱d vane̍va̱ yat sthi̱ram |

ni̱ṣṣaha̍māṇo yamate̱ nāya̍te dhanvā̱sahā̱ nāya̍te

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 4

dṛ̱hlā ci̍dasmā̱ anu̍ du̱ryathā̍ vi̱de teji̍ṣṭhābhira̱raṇi̍bhirdā̱ṣṭyava̍se̱ 'gnaye̍ dā̱ṣṭyava̍se |

pra yaḥ pu̱rūṇi̱ gāha̍te̱ takṣa̱d vane̍va śo̱ciṣā̍ |

sthi̱rā ci̱dannā̱ ni ri̍ṇā̱tyoja̍sā̱ ni sthi̱rāṇi̍ ci̱doja̍sā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 5

tama̍sya pṛ̱kṣamupa̍rāsu dhīmahi̱ nakta̱ṃ yaḥ su̱darśa̍taro̱ divā̍tarā̱daprā̍yuṣe̱ divā̍tarāt |

āda̱syāyu̱rgrabha̍ṇavad vī̱l̤u śarma̱ na sū̱nave̍ |

bha̱ktamabha̍kta̱mavo̱ vyanto̍ a̱jarā̍ a̱gnayo̱ vyanto̍ a̱jarā̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 6

sa hi śardho̱ na māru̍taṃ tuvi̱ṣvaṇi̱rapna̍svatīṣū̱rvarā̍svi̱ṣṭani̱rārta̍nāsvi̱ṣṭani̍: |

āda̍ddha̱vyānyā̍da̱dirya̱jñasya̍ ke̱tura̱rhaṇā̍ |

adha̍ smāsya̱ harṣa̍to̱ hṛṣī̍vato̱ viśve̍ juṣanta̱ panthā̱ṃ nara̍: śu̱bhe na panthā̍m

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 7

dvi̱tā yadī̍ṃ kī̱stāso̍ a̱bhidya̍vo nama̱syanta̍ upa̱voca̍nta̱ bhṛga̍vo ma̱thnanto̍ dā̱śā bhṛga̍vaḥ |

a̱gnirī̍śe̱ vasū̍nā̱ṃ śuci̱ryo dha̱rṇire̍ṣām |

pri̱yām̐ a̍pi̱dhīm̐rva̍niṣīṣṭa̱ medhi̍ra̱ ā va̍niṣīṣṭa̱ medhi̍raḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 8

viśvā̍sāṃ tvā vi̱śāṃ pati̍ṃ havāmahe̱ sarvā̍sāṃ samā̱naṃ dampa̍tiṃ bhu̱je sa̱tyagi̍rvāhasaṃ bhu̱je |

ati̍thi̱ṃ mānu̍ṣāṇāṃ pi̱turna yasyā̍sa̱yā |

a̱mī ca̱ viśve̍ a̱mṛtā̍sa̱ ā vayo̍ ha̱vyā de̱veṣvā vaya̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 9

tvama̍gne̱ saha̍sā̱ saha̍ntamaḥ śu̱ṣminta̍mo jāyase de̱vatā̍taye ra̱yirna de̱vatā̍taye |

śu̱ṣminta̍mo̱ hi te̱ mado̍ dyu̱mninta̍ma u̱ta kratu̍: |

adha̍ smā te̱ pari̍ carantyajara śruṣṭī̱vāno̱ nāja̍ra

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 10

pra vo̍ ma̱he saha̍sā̱ saha̍svata uṣa̱rbudhe̍ paśu̱ṣe nāgnaye̱ stomo̍ babhūtva̱gnaye̍ |

prati̱ yadī̍ṃ ha̱viṣmā̱n viśvā̍su̱ kṣāsu̱ jogu̍ve |

agre̍ re̱bho na ja̍rata ṛṣū̱ṇāṃ jūrṇi̱rhota̍ ṛṣū̱ṇām

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 11

sa no̱ nedi̍ṣṭha̱ṃ dadṛ̍śāna̱ ā bha̱rāgne̍ de̱vebhi̱: saca̍nāḥ suce̱tunā̍ ma̱ho rā̱yaḥ su̍ce̱tunā̍ |

mahi̍ śaviṣṭha naskṛdhi sa̱ṃcakṣe̍ bhu̱je a̱syai |

mahi̍ sto̱tṛbhyo̍ maghavan tsu̱vīrya̱ṃ mathī̍ru̱gro na śava̍sā
