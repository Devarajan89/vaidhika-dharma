# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 53

**11 savya āṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ| jagatī, 10-11 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 1

nyū̱ 3 ṣu vāca̱ṃ pra ma̱he bha̍rāmahe̱ gira̱ indrā̍ya̱ sada̍ne vi̱vasva̍taḥ |
nū ci̱ddhi ratna̍ṃ sasa̱tāmi̱vāvi̍da̱nna du̍ṣṭu̱tirdra̍viṇo̱deṣu̍ śasyate

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 2

śi̱kṣā̱na̱raḥ pra̱divo̱ akā̍makarśana̱: sakhā̱ sakhi̍bhya̱stami̱daṃ gṛ̍ṇīmasi

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 3

ata̍: sa̱ṃgṛbhyā̍bhibhūta̱ ā bha̍ra̱ mā tvā̍ya̱to ja̍ri̱tuḥ kāma̍mūnayīḥ

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 4

e̱bhirdyubhi̍: su̱manā̍ e̱bhirindu̍bhirnirundhā̱no ama̍ti̱ṃ gobhi̍ra̱śvinā̍ |

indre̍ṇa̱ dasyu̍ṃ da̱raya̍nta̱ indu̍bhiryu̱tadve̍ṣasa̱: sami̱ṣā ra̍bhemahi

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 5

sami̍ndra rā̱yā sami̱ṣā ra̍bhemahi̱ saṃ vāje̍bhiḥ puruśca̱ndraira̱bhidyu̍bhiḥ |

saṃ de̱vyā prama̍tyā vī̱raśu̍ṣmayā̱ goa̍gra̱yāśvā̍vatyā rabhemahi

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 6

yat kā̱rave̱ daśa̍ vṛ̱trāṇya̍pra̱ti ba̱rhiṣma̍te̱ ni sa̱hasrā̍ṇi ba̱rhaya̍:

## Verse 7

namyā̱ yadi̍ndra̱ sakhyā̍ parā̱vati̍ niba̱rhayo̱ namu̍ci̱ṃ nāma̍ mā̱yina̍m
