# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 88

**6 gotamo rāhūgaṇaḥ| marutaḥ| triṣṭup, 1, 6 prastārapaṃktiḥ, 5 virāḍ rupā|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 1

ā vi̱dyunma̍dbhirmarutaḥ sva̱rkai rathe̍bhiryāta ṛṣṭi̱madbhi̱raśva̍parṇaiḥ |
ā varṣi̍ṣṭhayā na i̱ṣā vayo̱ na pa̍ptatā sumāyāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 2

te̍'ru̱ṇebhi̱rvara̱mā pi̱śaṅgai̍: śu̱bhe kaṃ yā̍nti ratha̱tūrbhi̱raśvai̍: |

ru̱kmo na ci̱traḥ svadhi̍tīvān pa̱vyā ratha̍sya jaṅghananta̱ bhūma̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 3

śri̱ye kaṃ vo̱ adhi̍ ta̱nūṣu̱ vāśī̍rme̱dhā vanā̱ na kṛ̍ṇavanta ū̱rdhvā |

yu̱ṣmabhya̱ṃ kaṃ ma̍rutaḥ sujātāstuvidyu̱mnāso̍ dhanayante̱ adri̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 4

ahā̍ni̱ gṛdhrā̱: paryā va̱ āgu̍ri̱māṃ dhiya̍ṃ vārkā̱ryāṃ ca̍ de̱vīm |

brahma̍ kṛ̱ṇvanto̱ gota̍māso a̱rkairū̱rdhvaṃ nu̍nudra utsa̱dhiṃ piba̍dhyai

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 5

e̱tat tyanna yoja̍namaceti sa̱svarha̱ yanma̍ruto̱ gota̍mo vaḥ |

paśya̱n hira̍ṇyacakrā̱nayo̍daṃṣṭrān vi̱dhāva̍to va̱rāhū̍n

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 6

e̱ṣā syā vo̍ maruto'nubha̱rtrī prati̍ ṣṭobhati vā̱ghato̱ na vāṇī̍ |

asto̍bhaya̱d vṛthā̍sā̱manu̍ sva̱dhāṃ gabha̍styoḥ
