# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 147

**5 dīrghatamā aucathyaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 147 | **Verse:** 1

ka̱thā te̍ agne śu̱caya̍nta ā̱yorda̍dā̱śurvāje̍bhirāśuṣā̱ṇāḥ |
u̱bhe yat to̱ke tana̍ye̱ dadhā̍nā ṛ̱tasya̱ sāma̍n ra̱ṇaya̍nta de̱vāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 147 | **Verse:** 2

bodhā̍ me a̱sya vaca̍so yaviṣṭha̱ maṃhi̍ṣṭhasya̱ prabhṛ̍tasya svadhāvaḥ |

pīya̍ti tvo̱ anu̍ tvo gṛṇāti va̱ndāru̍ste ta̱nva̍ṃ vande agne

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 147 | **Verse:** 3

ye pā̱yavo̍ māmate̱yaṃ te̍ agne̱ paśya̍nto a̱ndhaṃ du̍ri̱tādara̍kṣan |

ra̱rakṣa̱ tān tsu̱kṛto̍ vi̱śvave̍dā̱ dipsa̍nta̱ id ri̱pavo̱ nāha̍ debhuḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 147 | **Verse:** 4

yo no̍ agne̱ ara̍rivām̐ aghā̱yura̍rātī̱vā ma̱rcaya̍ti dva̱yena̍ |

mantro̍ gu̱ruḥ puna̍rastu̱ so a̍smā̱ anu̍ mṛkṣīṣṭa ta̱nva̍ṃ duru̱ktaiḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 147 | **Verse:** 5

u̱ta vā̱ yaḥ sa̍hasya pravi̱dvān marto̱ marta̍ṃ ma̱rcaya̍ti dva̱yena̍ |

ata̍: pāhi stavamāna stu̱vanta̱magne̱ māki̍rno duri̱tāya̍ dhāyīḥ
