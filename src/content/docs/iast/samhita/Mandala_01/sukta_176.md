# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 176

**6 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ | indraḥ| anuṣṭup, 6 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 176 | **Verse:** 1

matsi̍ no̱ vasya̍ iṣṭaya̱ indra̍mindo̱ vṛṣā vi̍śa |
ṛ̱ghā̱yamā̍ṇa invasi̱ śatru̱manti̱ na vi̍ndasi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 176 | **Verse:** 2

tasmi̱nnā ve̍śayā̱ giro̱ ya eka̍ścarṣaṇī̱nām |

anu̍ sva̱dhā yamu̱pyate̱ yava̱ṃ na carkṛ̍ṣa̱d vṛṣā̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 176 | **Verse:** 3

yasya̱ viśvā̍ni̱ hasta̍yo̱: pañca̍ kṣitī̱nāṃ vasu̍ |

spā̱śaya̍sva̱ yo a̍sma̱dhrugdi̱vyevā̱śani̍rjahi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 176 | **Verse:** 4

asu̍nvantaṃ samaṃ jahi dū̱ṇāśa̱ṃ yo na te̱ maya̍: |

a̱smabhya̍masya̱ veda̍naṃ da̱ddhi sū̱riści̍dohate

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 176 | **Verse:** 5

āvo̱ yasya̍ dvi̱barha̍so̱'rkeṣu̍ sānu̱ṣagasa̍t |

ā̱jāvindra̍syendo̱ prāvo̱ vāje̍ṣu vā̱jina̍m

## Verse 6
