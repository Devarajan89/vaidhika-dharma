# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 94

**16 kutsa āṅgirasaḥ| agniḥ(jātavedāḥ), 8 (trayaḥ pādāḥ) devāḥ, 16 uttarārdhasya agniḥ, mitravaruṇāditisindhu pṛthivīdyāvo vā| jagatī, 15-16 triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 1

i̱maṃ stoma̱marha̍te jā̱tave̍dase̱ ratha̍miva̱ saṃ ma̍hemā manī̱ṣayā̍ |
bha̱drā hi na̱: prama̍tirasya sa̱ṃsadyagne̍ sa̱khye mā ri̍ṣāmā va̱yaṃ tava̍
