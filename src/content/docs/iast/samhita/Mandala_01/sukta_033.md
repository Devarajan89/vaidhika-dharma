# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 33

**15 hiraṇyastūpa āṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 1

etāyā̱mopa̍ ga̱vyanta̱ indra̍ma̱smāka̱ṃ su prama̍tiṃ vāvṛdhāti |
a̱nā̱mṛ̱ṇaḥ ku̱vidāda̱sya rā̱yo gavā̱ṃ keta̱ṃ para̍mā̱varja̍te naḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 2

upeda̱haṃ dha̍na̱dāmapra̍tīta̱ṃ juṣṭā̱ṃ na śye̱no va̍sa̱tiṃ pa̍tāmi |

indra̍ṃ nama̱syannu̍pa̱mebhi̍ra̱rkairyaḥ sto̱tṛbhyo̱ havyo̱ asti̱ yāma̍n

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 3

ni sarva̍sena iṣu̱dhīm̐ra̍sakta̱ sama̱ryo gā a̍jati̱ yasya̱ vaṣṭi̍ |

co̱ṣkū̱yamā̍ṇa indra̱ bhūri̍ vā̱maṃ mā pa̱ṇirbhū̍ra̱smadadhi̍ pravṛddha

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 4

vadhī̱rhi dasyu̍ṃ dha̱nina̍ṃ gha̱nena̱m̐ eka̱ścara̍nnupaśā̱kebhi̍rindra |

dhano̱radhi̍ viṣu̱ṇakte vyā̍ya̱nnaya̍jvānaḥ sana̱kāḥ preti̍mīyuḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 5

parā̍ cicchī̱rṣā va̍vṛju̱sta i̱ndrā'ya̍jvāno̱ yajva̍bhi̱: spardha̍mānāḥ |

pra yad di̱vo ha̍rivaḥ sthātarugra̱  nira̍vra̱tām̐ a̍dhamo̱ roda̍syoḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 6

ayu̍yutsannanava̱dyasya̱ senā̱mayā̍tayanta kṣi̱tayo̱ nava̍gvāḥ |

vṛ̱ṣā̱yudho̱ na vadhra̍yo̱ nira̍ṣṭāḥ pra̱vadbhi̱rindrā̍cci̱taya̍nta āyan

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 8

tvame̱tān ru̍da̱to jakṣa̍ta̱ścāyo̍dhayo̱ raja̍sa indra pā̱re |

avā̍daho di̱va ā dasyu̍mu̱ccā pra su̍nva̱taḥ stu̍va̱taḥ śaṃsa̍māvaḥ || 7

ca̱krā̱ṇāsa̍: parī̱ṇaha̍ṃ pṛthi̱vyā hira̍ṇyena ma̱ṇinā̱ śumbha̍mānāḥ |

na hi̍nvā̱nāsa̍stitiru̱sta indra̱ṃ pari̱ spaśo̍ adadhā̱t sūrye̍ṇa

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 9

pari̱ yadi̍ndra̱ roda̍sī u̱bhe abu̍bhojīrmahi̱nā vi̱śvata̍: sīm |

ama̍nyamānām̐ a̱bhi manya̍mānai̱rnirbra̱hmabhi̍radhamo̱ dasyu̍mindra

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 10

na ye di̱vaḥ pṛ̍thi̱vyā anta̍mā̱purna mā̱yābhi̍rdhana̱dāṃ pa̱ryabhū̍van |

yuja̱ṃ vajra̍ṃ vṛṣa̱bhaśca̍kra̱ indro̱ nirjyoti̍ṣā̱ tama̍so̱ gā a̍dukṣat

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 11

sa̱dhrī̱cīne̍na̱ mana̍sā̱ tamindra̱ oji̍ṣṭhena̱ hanma̍nāhanna̱bhi dyūn

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 12

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 13

## Verse 14
