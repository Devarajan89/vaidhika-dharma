# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 16

**9 medhātithiḥkāṇvaḥ| indraḥ|gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 1

ā tvā̍ vahantu̱ hara̍yo̱ vṛṣa̍ṇa̱ṃ soma̍pītaye | indra̍ tvā̱ sūra̍cakṣasaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 2

i̱mā dhā̱nā ghṛ̍ta̱snuvo̱ harī̍ i̱hopa̍ vakṣataḥ | indra̍ṃ su̱khata̍me̱ rathe̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 3

indra̍ṃ prā̱tarha̍vāmaha̱ indra̍ṃ praya̱tya̍dhva̱re | indra̱ṃ soma̍sya pī̱taye̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 4

upa̍ naḥ su̱tamā ga̍hi̱ hari̍bhirindra ke̱śibhi̍: | su̱te hi tvā̱ havā̍mahe

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 5

semaṃ na̱: stoma̱mā ga̱hyupe̱daṃ sava̍naṃ su̱tam | gau̱ro na tṛ̍ṣi̱taḥ pi̍ba

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 6

i̱me somā̍sa̱ inda̍vaḥ su̱tāso̱ adhi̍ ba̱rhiṣi̍ | tām̐ i̍ndra̱ saha̍se piba

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 7

a̱yaṃ te̱ stomo̍ agri̱yo hṛ̍di̱spṛga̍stu̱ śaṃta̍maḥ | athā̱ soma̍ṃ su̱taṃ pi̍ba

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 8

viśva̱mitsava̍naṃ su̱tamindro̱ madā̍ya gacchati | vṛ̱tra̱hā soma̍pītaye

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 9

semaṃ na̱: kāma̱mā pṛ̍ṇa̱ gobhi̱raśvai̍: śatakrato | stavā̍ma tvā svā̱dhya̍:
