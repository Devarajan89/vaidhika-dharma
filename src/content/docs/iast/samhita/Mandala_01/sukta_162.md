# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 162

**22 dīrghatamā aucathyaḥ| aśvaḥ| triṣṭup, 3-6 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 162 | **Verse:** 1

mā no̍ mi̱tro varu̍ṇo arya̱māyurindra̍ ṛbhu̱kṣā ma̱ruta̱: pari̍ khyan |
yad vā̱jino̍ de̱vajā̍tasya̱ sapte̍: prava̱kṣyāmo̍ vi̱dathe̍ vī̱ryā̍ṇi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 162 | **Verse:** 2

suprā̍ṅa̱jo memya̍d viśvarū̍pa indrāpū̱ṣṇoḥ pri̱yamapye̍ti̱ pātha̍:

a̱bhi̱priya̱ṃ yatpu̍ro̱l̤āśa̱marva̍tā̱ tvaṣṭede̍naṃ sauśrava̱sāya̍ jinvati
