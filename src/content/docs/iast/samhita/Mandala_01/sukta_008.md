# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 8

**10 madhucchandā vaiśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 1

endra̍ sāna̱siṃ ra̱yiṃ sa̱jitvā̍naṃ sadā̱saha̍m| varṣi̍ṣṭhamū̱taye̍ bhara

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 2

ni yena̍ muṣṭiha̱tyayā̱ ni vṛ̱trā ru̱ṇadhā̍mahai| tvotā̍so̱ nyarva̍tā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 3

indra̱ tvotā̍sa̱ ā va̱yaṃ vajra̍ṃ gha̱nā da̍dīmahi| jaye̍ma̱ saṃ yu̱dhi spṛdha̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 4

va̱yaṃ śūre̍bhi̱rastṛ̍bhi̱rindra̱ tvayā̍ yu̱jā va̱yam| sā̱sa̱hyāma̍ pṛtanya̱taḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 5

ma̱hām̐ indra̍: pa̱raśca̱ nu ma̍hi̱tvama̍stu va̱jriṇe̍| dyaurna pra̍thi̱nā śava̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 6

sa̱mo̱he vā̱ ya āśa̍ta̱ nara̍sto̱kasya̱ sani̍tau| viprā̍so vā dhiyā̱yava̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 7

yaḥ ku̱kṣiḥ so̍ma̱pāta̍maḥ samu̱dra i̍va̱ pinva̍te| u̱rvīrāpo̱ na kā̱kuda̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 8

e̱vā hya̍sya sū̱nṛtā̍ vira̱pśī goma̍tī ma̱hī| pa̱kvā śākhā̱ na dā̱śuṣe̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 9

e̱vā hi te̱ vibhū̍taya ū̱taya̍ indra̱ māva̍te| sa̱dyaści̱t santi̍ dā̱śuṣe̍

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 10

e̱vā hya̍sya̱ kāmyā̱ stoma̍ u̱kthaṃ ca̱ śaṃsyā̍| indrā̍ya̱ soma̍pītaye
