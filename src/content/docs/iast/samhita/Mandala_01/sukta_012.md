# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 12

**12 medhātithiḥ kāṇvaḥ| agniḥ,6 prathamapādasya (nirmathyāhavanīyau) agnī| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 1

a̱gniṃ dū̱taṃ vṛ̍ṇīmahe̱ hotā̍raṃ vi̱śvave̍dasam | a̱sya ya̱jñasya̍ su̱kratu̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 2

a̱gnima̍gni̱ṃ havī̍mabhi̱: sadā̍ havanta vi̱śpati̍m | ha̱vya̱vāha̍ṃ purupri̱yam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 3

agne̍ de̱vām̐ i̱hā va̍ha jajñā̱no vṛ̱ktaba̍rhiṣe | asi̱ hotā̍ na̱ īḍya̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 4

tām̐ u̍śa̱to vi bo̍dhaya̱ yada̍gne̱ yāsi̍ dū̱tya̍m | de̱vairā sa̍tsi ba̱rhiṣi̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 5

ghṛtā̍havana dīdiva̱: prati̍ ṣma̱ riṣa̍to daha | agne̱ tvaṃ ra̍kṣa̱svina̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 6

a̱gninā̱gniḥ sami̍dhyate ka̱virgṛ̱hapa̍ti̱ryuvā̍ | ha̱vya̱vāḍ ju̱hvā̍syaḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 7

ka̱vima̱gnimupa̍ stuhi sa̱tyadha̍rmāṇamadhva̱re | de̱vama̍mīva̱cāta̍nam

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 8

yastvāma̍gne ha̱viṣpa̍tirdū̱taṃ de̍va sapa̱ryati̍ | tasya̍ sma prāvi̱tā bha̍va

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 9

yo a̱gniṃ de̱vavī̍taye ha̱viṣmā̍m̐ ā̱vivā̍sati | tasmai̍ pāvaka mṛl̤aya

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 10

sa na̍: pāvaka dīdi̱vo 'gne̍ de̱vām̐ i̱hā va̍ha | upa̍ ya̱jñaṃ ha̱viśca̍ naḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 11

sa na̱: stavā̍na̱ ā bha̍ra gāya̱treṇa̱ navī̍yasā | ra̱yiṃ vī̱rava̍tī̱miṣa̍m

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 12

agne̍ śu̱kreṇa̍ śo̱ciṣā̱ viśvā̍bhirde̱vahū̍tibhiḥ | i̱maṃ stoma̍ṃ juṣasva naḥ
