# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 158

**6 dīrghatamā aucathyaḥ| aśvinau|triṣṭup, 6 anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 158 | **Verse:** 1

vasū̍ ru̱drā pu̍ru̱mantū̍ vṛ̱dhantā̍ daśa̱syata̍ṃ no vṛṣaṇāva̱bhiṣṭau̍ |
dasrā̍ ha̱ yad rekṇa̍ auca̱thyo vā̱ṃ pra yat sa̱srāthe̱ aka̍vābhirū̱tī

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 158 | **Verse:** 2

ko vā̍ṃ dāśat suma̱taye̍ cida̱syai vasū̱ yad dhethe̱ nama̍sā pa̱de goḥ |

ji̱gṛ̱tama̱sme re̱vatī̱: pura̍ṃdhīḥ kāma̱preṇe̍va̱ mana̍sā̱ cara̍ntā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 158 | **Verse:** 3

yu̱kto ha̱ yad vā̍ṃ tau̱gryāya̍ pe̱rurvi madhye̱ arṇa̍so̱ dhāyi̍ pa̱jraḥ |

upa̍ vā̱mava̍: śara̱ṇaṃ ga̍meya̱ṃ śūro̱ nājma̍ pa̱taya̍dbhi̱revai̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 158 | **Verse:** 4

upa̍stutirauca̱thyamu̍ruṣye̱nmā māmi̱me pa̍ta̱triṇī̱ vi du̍gdhām |

mā māmedho̱ daśa̍tayaści̱to dhā̱k pra yad vā̍ṃ ba̱ddhastmani̱ khāda̍ti̱ kṣām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 158 | **Verse:** 5

na mā̍ garan na̱dyo̍ mā̱tṛta̍mā dā̱sā yadī̱ṃ susa̍mubdhama̱vādhu̍: |

śiro̱ yada̍sya traita̱no vi̱takṣa̍t sva̱yaṃ dā̱sa uro̱ aṃsā̱vapi̍ gdha

## Verse 6
