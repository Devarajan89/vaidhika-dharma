# Aitareya āraṇyakam — Sūcī

The Aitareya Āraṇyakam has five āraṇyakas and eighteen adhyāyas. Āraṇyaka 2 adhyāyas 4–6 are the **Aitareya Upaniṣad** — follow those links rather than duplicating the text here.

Source: [MIU Vedic Reserve — Aitareya Āraṇyakam (PDF)](https://vedicreserve.miu.edu/aranyaka/aitareya_aranyaka.pdf)
