oṃ bha̱draṃ karṇe̍bhiḥ śṛṇu̱yāma̍ devāḥ | bha̱draṃ pa̍śyemā̱kṣabhi̱ryaja̍trāḥ |<br />
sthi̱rairaṅgai̎stuṣṭu̱vāgͫ sa̍sta̱nūbhi̍ḥ | vyaśe̍ma de̱vahi̍ta̱ṃ yadāyu̍: ||<br />
sva̱sti na̱ indro̍ vṛ̱ddhaśra̍vāḥ | sva̱sti na̍ḥ pū̱ṣā vi̱śvave̍dāḥ |<br />
sva̱sti na̱stārkṣyo̱ ari̍ṣṭanemiḥ | sva̱sti no̱ bṛha̱spati̍rdadhātu ||<br />
oṃ śānti̱: śānti̱: śānti̍ḥ ||

**|| atha gaṇapatyatharvaśīrṣam vyā̎khyāsyā̱ma: ||**

oṃ nama̍ste ga̱ṇapa̍taye | tvame̱va pra̱tyakṣa̱ṃ tattva̍masi |<br />
tvame̱va ke̱vala̱ṃ kartā̍'si | tvame̱va ke̱vala̱ṃ dhartā̍'si |<br />
tvame̱va ke̱vala̱ṃ hartā̍'si | tvameva sarvaṃ khalvida̍ṃ brahmā̱si |<br />
tvaṃ sākṣādātmā̍'si ni̱tyam || 1||

ṛ̍taṃ va̱cmi | sa̍tyaṃ va̱cmi || 2||

ava̍ tva̱ṃ mām | ava̍ va̱ktāram̎ | ava̍ śro̱tāram̎ |<br />
ava̍ dā̱tāram̎ | ava̍ dhā̱tāram̎ | avānūcānama̍va śi̱ṣyam |<br />
ava̍ pa̱ścāttā̎t | ava̍ pu̱rastā̎t |<br />
avotta̱rāttā̎t |ava̍ dakṣi̱ṇāttā̎t |<br />
ava̍ co̱rdhvāttā̎t | avādha̱rāttā̎t |<br />
sarvato māṃ pāhi pāhi̍ sama̱ntāt || 3||

tvaṃ vāṅmaya̍stvaṃ cinma̱yaḥ | tvamānandamaya̍stvaṃ brahma̱mayaḥ |<br />
tvaṃ saccidānandā'dvi̍tīyo̱'si |tvaṃ pra̱tyakṣa̱ṃ brahmā̍si |<br />
tvaṃ jñānamayo vijñāna̍mayo̱'si || 4||

sarvaṃ jagadidaṃ tva̍tto jā̱yate | sarvaṃ jagadidaṃ tva̍ttasti̱ṣṭhati |<br />
sarvaṃ jagadidaṃ tvayi la̍yame̱ṣyati | sarvaṃ jagadidaṃ tvayi̍ pratye̱ti |<br />
tvaṃ bhūmirāpo'nalo'ni̍lo na̱bhaḥ | tvaṃ catvāri vā̎kpadā̱ni || 5 ||

tvaṃ gu̱ṇatra̍yātī̱taḥ | tvaṃ avasthātra̍yātī̱taḥ | tvaṃ de̱hatra̍yātī̱taḥ |<br />
tvaṃ kā̱latra̍yātī̱taḥ | tvaṃ mūlādhārasthito̍'si ni̱tyam |<br />
tvaṃ śaktitra̍yātma̱kaḥ |tvāṃ yogino dhyāya̍nti ni̱tyam |<br />
tvaṃ brahmā tvaṃ viṣṇustvaṃ rudrastvamindrastvamagnistvaṃ
vāyustvaṃ sūryastvaṃ candramāstvaṃ brahma̱ bhūrbhuva̱: suva̱rom || 6||

ga̱ṇādī̎ṃ pūrva̍muccā̱rya̱ va̱rṇādī̎ṃ stada̱nanta̍ram | anusvāraḥ pa̍rata̱raḥ |<br />
ardhe̎ndula̱sitam | tāre̍ṇa ṛ̱ddham | etattava manu̍svarū̱pam |<br />
gakāraḥ pū̎rvarū̱pam | akāro madhya̍marū̱pam |<br />
anusvāraścā̎ntyarū̱pam | bindurutta̍rarū̱pam | nāda̍: sandhā̱nam |<br />
sagͫhi̍tā sa̱ndhiḥ | saiṣā gaṇe̍śavi̱dyā |<br />
gaṇa̍ka ṛ̱ṣiḥ | nicṛdgāya̍trīccha̱ndaḥ |<br />
śrī mahā gaṇapati̍rdeva̱tā |<br />
**oṃ gaṃ gaṇapataye̱ nama̍: || 7 ||**

e̱ka̱da̱ntāya̍ vi̱dmahe̍ vakratu̱ṇḍāya̍ dhīmahi |
tanno̍ dantī praco̱dayā̎t || 8||

e̱ka̱da̱ntaṃ ca̍turha̱sta̱ṃ pā̱śama̍ṅkuśa̱dhāri̍ṇam |<br />
rada̍ṃ ca̱ vara̍daṃ ha̱stai̱rbi̱bhrāṇa̍ṃ mūṣa̱kadhva̍jam |<br />
rakta̍ṃ la̱mboda̍raṃ śū̱rpa̱ka̱rṇaka̍ṃ rakta̱vāsa̍sam |<br />
rakta̍ga̱ndhānu̍liptā̱ṅga̱ṃ ra̱ktapu̍ṣpaiḥ su̱pūji̍tam |<br />
bhaktā̍nu̱kampi̍naṃ de̱va̱ṃ ja̱gatkā̍raṇa̱macyu̍tam |<br />
āvi̍rbhū̱taṃ ca̍ sṛṣṭyā̱dau̱ pra̱kṛte̎: puru̱ṣātpa̍ram |<br />
eva̍ṃ dhyā̱yati̍ yo ni̱tya̱ṃ sa yogī̍ yogi̱nāṃ va̍raḥ || 9||

namo vrātapataye namo gaṇapataye namaḥ pramathapataye
namaste astu lambodarāyaikadantāya vighnavināśine
śivasutāya śrīvaradamūrtaye̍ namo̱ nama̍: || 10||

etadatharvaśīrṣa̍ṃ yo'dhī̱te | sa brahmabhūyā̍ya ka̱lpate |<br />
sa sarvavighnai̎rna bā̱dhyate |sa sarvatra sukha̍medha̱te |<br />
sa pañcamahāpāpā̎t pramu̱cyate |<br />
sā̱yama̍dhīyā̱no̱ di̱vasakṛtaṃ pāpa̍ṃ nāśa̱yati |<br />
prā̱tara̍dhīyā̱no̱ rātrikṛtaṃ pāpa̍ṃ nāśa̱yati |<br />
sā̱yaṃ prā̱taḥ pra̍yuñjā̱no̱ pā̱po'pā̍po bha̱vati |<br />
sarvatrādhīyāno'pavi̍ghno bha̱vati |<br />
dharmārthakāmamokṣa̍ṃ ca vi̱ndati | idamatharvaśīrṣamaśiṣyāya̍ na de̱yam |<br />
yo yadi mohā̎ddā̱syati | sa pāpī̍yān bha̱vati |<br />
sahasrāvartanādyaṃ yaṃ kāma̍madhī̱te | taṃ tamane̍na sā̱dhayet || 11||<br />

anena gaṇapatima̍bhiṣi̱ñcati | sa vā̎gmī bha̱vati |<br />
caturthyāmana̍śnan ja̱pati | sa vidyā̍vān bha̱vati |<br />
ityatharva̍ṇavā̱kyam | bra̱hmādyā̱vara̍ṇaṃ vi̱dyāt |<br />
na bibheti kadā̍cane̱ti || 12||

yo dūrvāṅku̍rairya̱jati | sa vaiśravaṇopa̍mo bha̱vati |<br />
yo lā̍jairya̱jati | sa yaśo̍vān bha̱vati | sa medhā̍vān bha̱vati |<br />
yo modakasahasre̍ṇa ya̱jati | sa vāñchitaphalama̍vāpno̱ti |<br />
yaḥ sājya sami̍dbhirya̱jati | sa sarvaṃ labhate sa sa̍rvaṃ la̱bhate || 13||

aṣṭau brāhmaṇān samyag grā̍hayi̱tvā | sūryavarca̍svī bha̱vati |<br />
sūryagrahe ma̍hāna̱dyā̱ṃ pra̱timāsannidhau̍ vā ja̱ptvā | si̱ddhama̍ntro bha̱vati |<br />
mahāvighnā̎t pramu̱cyate | mahādoṣā̎t pramu̱cyate |<br />
mahāpāpā̎t pramu̱cyate | mahāpratyavāyā̎t pramu̱cyate |<br />
sa sarvavidbhavati sa sarva̍vidbha̱vati |<br />
ya e̱vaṃ veda̍ | ityu̍pa̱niṣa̍t || 14||

oṃ bha̱draṃ karṇe̍bhiḥ śṛṇu̱yāma̍ devāḥ | bha̱draṃ pa̍śyemā̱kṣabhi̱ryaja̍trāḥ |<br />
sthi̱rairaṅgai̎stuṣṭu̱vāgͫ sa̍sta̱nūbhi̍ḥ | vyaśe̍ma de̱vahi̍ta̱ṃ yadāyu̍: ||<br />
sva̱sti na̱ indro̍ vṛ̱ddhaśra̍vāḥ | sva̱sti na̍ḥ pū̱ṣā vi̱śvave̍dāḥ |<br />
sva̱sti na̱stārkṣyo̱ ari̍ṣṭanemiḥ | sva̱sti no̱ bṛha̱spati̍rdadhātu ||<br /><br />

**oṃ śānti̱: śānti̱: śānti̍ḥ**
