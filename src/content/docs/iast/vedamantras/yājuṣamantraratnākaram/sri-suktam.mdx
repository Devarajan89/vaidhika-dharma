oṃ || hira̍ṇyavarṇā̱ṃ hari̍ṇīṃ su̱varṇa̍raja̱tasra̍jām |<br />
ca̱ndrāṃ hi̱raṇma̍yīṃ la̱kṣmīṃ jāta̍vedo ma̱ āva̍ha || 1 ||<br />

tāṃ ma̱ āva̍ha̱ jāta̍vedo la̱kṣmīmana̍pagā̱minī̍m |<br />
yasyā̱ṃ hira̍ṇyaṃ vi̱ndeya̱ṃ gāmaśva̱ṃ puru̍ṣāna̱ham || 2 ||<br />

a̱śva̱pū̱rvāṃ ra̍thama̱dhyāṃ ha̱stinā̍dapra̱bodhi̍nīm |<br />
śriya̍ṃ de̱vīmupa̍hvaye̱ śrīrmā̍de̱vīrju̍ṣatām || 3 ||<br />

kā̱ṃ so̱smi̱tāṃ hira̍ṇyaprākārāmā̱rdrāṃ jvala̍ntīṃ tṛ̱ptāṃ ta̱rpaya̍ntīm |<br />
pa̱dme̱ sthi̱tāṃ pa̱dmava̍rṇā̱ṃ tāmi̱hopa̍hvaye̱ śriya̍m || 4 ||<br />

ca̱ndrāṃ pra̍bhā̱sāṃ ya̱śasā̱ jvala̍ntī̱ṃ śriya̍ṃ lo̱ke de̱vaju̍ṣṭāmudā̱rām |<br />
tāṃ pa̱dminī̍mī̱ṃ śara̍ṇama̱haṃ prapa̍dye 'la̱kṣmīrme̍ naśyatā̱ṃ tvāṃ vṛ̍ṇe || 5 ||<br />

ā̱di̱tyava̍rṇe̱ tapa̱so'dhi̍jā̱to vana̱spati̱stava̍ vṛ̱kṣo'tha bi̱lvaḥ |<br />
tasya̱ phalā̍ni̱ tapa̱sā nu̍dantu mā̱yānta̍rā̱ yāśca̍ bā̱hyā a̍la̱kṣmīḥ || 6 ||<br />

upai̍tu̱ māṃ de̍vasa̱khaḥ kī̱rtiśca̱ maṇi̍nā sa̱ha |<br />
prā̱du̱rbhū̱to'smi̍ rāṣṭre̱'smin kī̱rtimṛ̍ddhiṃ da̱dātu̍ me || 7 ||<br />

kṣutpi̍pā̱sāma̍lāṃ jye̱ṣṭhāmala̱kṣmīṃ nā̍śayā̱myaham |<br />
abhū̍ti̱masa̍mṛddhi̱ṃ ca sarvā̱ṃ nirṇu̍da me̱ gṛhā̍t || 8 ||<br />

ga̱ṃdha̱dvā̱rāṃ du̍rādha̱rṣā̱ṃ ni̱tyapu̍ṣṭāṃ karī̱ṣiṇī̍m |<br />
ī̱śvarī̍ṃ sarva̍bhūtā̱nā̱ṃ tāmi̱hopa̍hvaye̱ śriya̍m || 9 ||<br />

mana̍sa̱ḥ kāma̱mākū̍tiṃ vā̱caḥ sa̱tyama̍śīmahi |<br />
pa̱śū̱nāṃ rūpa̍manna̱sya mayi̱ śrīḥ śra̍yatā̱ṃ yaśa̍ḥ || 10 ||<br />

karda̍me̱na pra̍jābhū̱tā ma̱yi sa̍ṃbhava̱ karda̍ma |<br />
śriya̍ṃ vā̱saya̍ me ku̱le mā̱tara̍ṃ padma̱māli̍nīm || 11 ||

āpa̍ḥ sṛ̱jantu̍ sni̱gdhā̱ni ciklī̍ta̱ vasa̍ me gṛ̱he |<br />
ni ca̍ de̱vīṃ mā̱tara̱ṃ śriya̍ṃ vā̱saya̍ me ku̱le || 12 ||<br />

ā̱rdrāṃ pu̱ṣkari̍ṇīṃ pu̱ṣṭi̱ṃ su̱varṇā̍ṃ hema̱māli̍nīm |<br />
sū̱ryāṃ hi̱raṇma̍yīṃ la̱kṣmīṃ jāta̍vedo ma̱ āva̍ha || 13 ||<br />

ā̱rdrāṃ ya̱ḥ kari̍ṇīṃ ya̱ṣṭi̱ṃ pi̱ṅgalā̍ṃ padma̱māli̍nīm |<br />
ca̱ndrāṃ hi̱raṇma̍yīṃ la̱kṣmī̱ṃ jāta̍vedo ma̱ āva̍ha || 14||<br />

tāṃ ma̱ āva̍ha̱ jāta̍vedo la̱kṣmīmana̍pa gā̱minī̍m |<br />
yasyā̱ṃ hira̍ṇya̱ṃ prabhū̍ta̱ṃ gāvo̍ dā̱syo'śvā̍nvi̱ndeya̱ṃ puru̍ṣāna̱ham || 15 ||<br />

**(ma̱hā̱de̱vyai ca̍ vi̱dmahe̍ viṣṇupa̱tnyai ca̍ dhīmahī |<br />
tanno̍ lakṣmīḥ praco̱dayā̍t || 16 ||)** <br />

## śrīsūktaśeṣaḥ

yaḥ śuci̱ praya̍to bhū̱tvā ju̱huyā̍dājya̱manva̍ham | sūkta̍ṃ pa̱ñcada̍śarca̱ṃ ca śrīkāma̍: sata̱taṃ ja̍pet |<br />
pa̱dmā̱na̱ne pa̍dma ū̱rū pa̱dmākṣī̍ padma̱sambha̍ve| tanme̍ bha̱jasi̍ pa̱dmā̱kṣī̱ ye̱na saukhya̍ṃ la̱bhāmya̍ham | <br />
a̱śva̱dāyī̍ godā̱yī dha̱nadā̍yī ma̱hādha̍ne | dhana̍ṃ me̱ juṣa̍tāṃ de̱vi sa̱rvakāmā̎ṃśca̱ dehi̍ me |<br />
padmā̍na̱ne pa̍hma̱vipa̍dmapa̱tre̱ padya̍priye̱ padma̱dalā̍yatā̱kṣi | viśva̍priye̱ viṣṇu mano̍'nukū̱le tvatpā̍dapa̱dmaṃ mayi̱ sanni̍dhatsva |<br />
pu̱tra̱pau̱tra dha̍naṃ dhā̱nyaṃ ha̱styaśvā̍diga̱ve ra̍tham | pra̱jānā̱ṃ bhava̍si mā̱tā̱ ā̱yuṣma̍ntaṃ ka̱rotu̍ me |<br />
dhana̍ me̱gnirdhana̍ṃ vā̱yurdhana̱ṃ sūryo̍ dhana̱ṃ vasu̍: | dhana̱mindro̱ bṛha̱spati̱rvaru̍ṇa̱ṃ dhana̱mastu̍ te |<br />
vaina̍teya̱ soma̍ṃ piba̱ soma̍ṃ pibatu vṛtra̱hā | soma̱ṃ dhana̍sya sā̱mino̱ mahya̱ṃ dadā̍tu so̱mina̍ḥ |<br /> 
na krodho̱ na ca̍ mātsa̱rya̱ṃ na̱ lobho̍ nāśu̱bhāma̍tiḥ | bhava̍nti̱ kṛta̍puṇyā̱nā̱ṃ bha̱ktānāṃ śrīsū̎ktaṃ ja̱pet |<br /> 
sarasijanilaye saro̍ja̱ha̱ste̱ dhavalatarāṃ śukaga̱ndhamā̎lyaśo̱bhe |<br />
bhagavati harivallabhe̍ mano̱jñe̱ tribhuvanabhūtikari prasī̍da ma̱hyam |<br /> 
viṣṇu̍pa̱tnīṃ kṣa̍māṃ de̱vī̱ṃ mā̱dhavī̎ṃ mādha̱pri̍yām | lakṣmī̍ṃ pri̱yasa̍khīṃ de̱vī̱ṃ na̱māmya̍cyuta̱valla̍bhām |<br /> 
ma̱hā̱la̱kṣmyai ca̍ vi̱dmahe̍ viṣṇupa̱tnyai ca̍ dhīmahi | tanno̍ lakṣmīḥ praco̱dayā̎t |<br /> 
śrīvarca̍sva̱māyu̍ṣya̱māro̎gya̱māvi̍dhā̱cchobha̍mānaṃ mahī̱yate̎ | dhā̱nyaṃ dha̱naṃ pa̱śuṃ ba̱hupu̍tralā̱bhaṃ śa̱tasa̍ṃvatsa̱raṃ dī̱rghamāyu̍:
