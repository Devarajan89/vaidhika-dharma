namo̍ astu sa̱rpebhyo̱ ye ke ca̍ pṛthi̱vīmanu̍ |<br />
ye a̱ntari̍kṣe̱ ye di̱vi tebhya̍ḥ sa̱rpebhyo̱ nama̍ḥ ||

ye̍'do ro̍ca̱ne di̱vo ye vā̱ sūrya̍sya ra̱śmiṣu̍ |<br />
yeṣā̍ma̱psu sada̍ḥ kṛ̱taṃ tebhya̍ḥ sa̱rpebhyo̱ nama̍ḥ ||

yā iṣa̍vo yātu̱dhānā̍nā̱ṃ ye vā̱ vana̱spatī̱g̱ͫranu̍ |<br />
ye vā̍ va̱ṭeṣu̱ śera̍te̱ tebhya̍ḥ sa̱rpebhyo̱ nama̍ḥ ||

i̱dagͫ sa̱rpebhyo̍ ha̱vira̍stu̱ juṣṭa̎m |<br />
ā̱śre̱ṣā yeṣā̍manu̱yanti̱ ceta̍ḥ ||

ye a̱ntari̍kṣaṃ pṛthi̱vīṃ kṣi̱yanti̍ |
te na̍ḥ sa̱rpāso̱ hava̱māga̍miṣṭhāḥ |<br />
ye ro̍ca̱ne sūrya̱syāpi̍ sa̱rpāḥ |
ye diva̍ṃ de̱vīmanu̍ sa̱ṃcara̍nti |<br />
yeṣā̍māśre̱ṣā a̍nu̱yanti̱ kāma̎m |
tebhya̍ḥ sa̱rpebhyo̱ madhu̍majjuhomi |

ni̱ghṛṣvai̍rasa̱māyu̍taiḥ | kālairharitva̍māpa̱nnaiḥ | <br />
indrāyā̍hi sa̱hasra̍yuk | a̱gnirvi̱bhrāṣṭi̍vasanaḥ | <br />
vā̱yuḥ śveta̍sikadru̱kaḥ | sa̱ṃva̱tsa̱ro vi̍ṣū̱varṇai̎ḥ |<br />
nityā̱ste'nuca̍rāsta̱va | subrahmaṇyogͫ subrahmaṇyogͫ su̍brahma̱ṇyom ||
