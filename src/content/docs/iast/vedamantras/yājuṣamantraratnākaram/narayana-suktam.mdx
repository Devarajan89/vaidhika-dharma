vi̱śvata̱ḥ para̍mānni̱tya̱ṃ vi̱śvaṃ nā̍rāya̱ṇagͫ ha̍rim | viśva̍me̱vedaṃ puru̍ṣa̱stadviśva̱mupa̍jīvati |<br />
pati̱ṃ viśva̍syā̱'̱'̱tmeśva̍ra̱g̱ͫ śāśva̍tagͫ śi̱vama̍cyutam | nā̱rāya̱ṇaṃ ma̍hājñe̱ya̱ṃ vi̱śvātmā̍naṃ pa̱rāya̍ṇam |<br />
nā̱rāya̱ṇapa̍ro jyo̱ti̱rā̱tmā nā̍rāya̱ṇaḥ pa̍raḥ | nā̱rāya̱ṇa pa̍raṃ bra̱hma̱ ta̱ttvaṃ nā̍rāya̱ṇaḥ pa̍raḥ |<br />
nā̱rāya̱ṇapa̍ro dhyā̱tā̱ dhyā̱naṃ nā̍rāya̱ṇaḥ pa̍raḥ | yacca̍ ki̱ñcijja̍gat sa̱rva̱ṃ dṛ̱śyate̎ śrūya̱te'pi̍ vā ||<br />

anta̍rba̱hiśca̍ tat sa̱rva̱ṃ vyā̱pya nā̍rāya̱ṇaḥ sthi̍taḥ | ana̍nta̱mavya̍yaṃ ka̱vigͫ sa̍mu̱dre'nta̍ṃ vi̱śvaśa̍mbhuvam |<br />
pa̱dma̱ko̱śa pra̍tīkā̱śa̱g̱ͫ hṛ̱daya̍ṃ cāpya̱dhomu̍kham | adho̍ ni̱ṣṭyā vi̍tasyā̱nte̱ nā̱bhyāmu̍pari̱ tiṣṭha̍ti |<br />
jvā̱la̱mā̱lāku̍laṃ bhā̱tī̱ vi̱śvasyā̍''yata̱naṃ ma̍hat | santa̍tagͫ śi̱lābhi̍stu̱ lamba̍tyākośa̱sanni̍bham |<br />
tasyānte̍ suṣi̱ragͫ sū̱kṣmaṃ tasmi̎n sa̱rvaṃ prati̍ṣṭhitam | tasya̱ madhye̍ ma̱hāna̍gnirvi̱śvārci̍rvi̱śvato̍mukhaḥ |<br />
so'gra̍bhu̱gvibha̍janti̱ṣṭha̱nnāhā̍ramaja̱raḥ ka̱viḥ| ti̱rya̱gū̱rdhvama̍dhaḥ śā̱yī̱ ra̱śmaya̍stasya̱ santa̍tā | <br />
sa̱ṃtā̱paya̍ti svaṃ de̱hamāpā̍datala̱masta̍kaḥ | tasya̱ madhye̱ vahni̍śikhā a̱ṇīyo̎rdhvā vya̱vasthi̍taḥ |<br /> nī̱lato̍yada̍madhya̱sthā̱dvi̱dyulle̍kheva̱ bhāsva̍rā | nī̱vāra̱śūka̍vatta̱nvī̱ pī̱tā bhā̎svatya̱ṇūpa̍mā |<br />
tasyā̎ḥ śikhā̱yā ma̍dhye pa̱ramā̎tmā vya̱vasthi̍taḥ | sa brahma̱ sa śiva̱ḥ sa hari̱ḥ sendra̱ḥ so'kṣa̍raḥ para̱maḥ sva̱rāṭ ||<br />
ṛ̱tagͫ sa̱tyaṃ pa̍raṃ bra̱hma̱ pu̱ruṣa̍ṃ kṛṣṇa̱piṅga̍lam | ū̱rdhvare̍taṃ vi̍rūpā̱kṣa̱ṃ vi̱śvarū̍pāya̱ vai namo̱ nama̍ḥ |<br />

nā̱rā̱ya̱ṇāya̍ vi̱dmahe̍ vāsude̱vāya̍ dhīmahi | tanno̍ viṣṇuḥ praco̱dayā̎t |<br />
