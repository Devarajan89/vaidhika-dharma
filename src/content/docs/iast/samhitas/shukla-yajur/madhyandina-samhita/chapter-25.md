<a id="mantra-1"></a>

śāda̍ṃ da̱dbhirava̍kāṃ dantamūlai̱rmṛda̱ṃ barsvai̍ste̱gāndaṣṭrā̍bhyā sara̍svatyā agraji̱hvaṃ ji̱hvāyā̍ utsā̱dama̍vakra̱ndena̱ tālu̱ vāja hanu̍bhyāma̱pa ā̱sye̱na̱ vṛṣa̍ṇamā̱ṇḍābhyā̍mādi̱tyām̐ śmaśru̍bhi̱: panthā̍naṃ bhrū̱bhyāṃ dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī varto̍bhyāṃ vi̱dyuta̍ṃ ka̱nīna̍kābhyā śu̱klāya̱ svāhā̍ kṛ̱ṣṇāya̱ svāhā̱ pāryā̍ṇi̱ pakṣmā̍ṇyavā̱ryā̱ i̱kṣavo̍'vā̱ryā̱ṇi̱ pakṣmā̍ṇi̱ pāryā̍ i̱kṣava̍: ||25.1||

<a id="mantra-2"></a>
