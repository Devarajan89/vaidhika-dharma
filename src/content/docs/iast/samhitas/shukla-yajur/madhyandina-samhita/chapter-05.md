ya̱thā̱ya̱thaṃ nau̍ vratapate vra̱tānyanu̍ me dī̱kṣāṃ dī̱kṣāpa̍ti̱ramastānu̱ tapa̱stapa̍spatiḥ ||5.40||

atya̱nyām̐2  agā̱ṃ nānyām̐2  upā̍gāma̱rvāk tvā̱ pare̱bhyo'vi̍daṃ pa̱ro'va̍rebhyaḥ |
taṃ tvā̍ juṣāmahe deva vanaspate devaya̱jyāyai̍ de̱vāstvā̍ devaya̱jyāyai̍ juṣantā̱ṃ viṣṇa̍ve tvā |
oṣa̍dhe̱ trāya̍sva svadhi̍te̱ maina  hisīḥ ||5.42||
