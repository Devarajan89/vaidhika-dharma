<a id="mantra-1"></a>

vāgoja̍: sa̱haujo̱ mayi̍ prāṇāpā̱nau ||36.1||

---

<a id="mantra-2"></a>

yanme̍ chi̱draṃ cakṣu̍ṣo̱ hṛda̍yasya̱ mana̍so̱ vāti̍tṛṇṇa̱ṃ bṛha̱spati̍rme̱ tadda̍dhātu |
śaṃ no̍ bhavatu̱ bhuva̍nasya̱ yaspati̍: ||36.2||

---

<a id="mantra-3"></a>

bhūrbhuva̱: sva̱: tatsa̍vi̱turvare̍ṇya̱ṃ bhargo̍ de̱vasya̍ dhīmahi |
dhiyo̱ yo na̍: praco̱dayā̍t ||36.3||

---

<a id="mantra-4"></a>

kayā̍ naści̱tra ā bhu̍vadū̱tī sa̱dāvṛ̍dha̱: sakhā̍ | kayā̱ śaci̍ṣṭhayā vṛ̱tā ||36.4||

---

<a id="mantra-5"></a>

kastvā̍ sa̱tyo madā̍nā̱ṃ mahi̍ṣṭho matsa̱dandha̍saḥ | dṛ̱ḍhā ci̍dā̱ruje̱ vasu̍ ||36.5||

---

<a id="mantra-6"></a>

a̱bhī ṣu  ṇa̱: sakhī̍nāmavi̱tā ja̍ritṝ̱ṇām | śa̱taṃ bha̍vāsyū̱tibhi̍: ||36.6||

---

<a id="mantra-7"></a>

kayā̱ tvaṃ na ū̱tyābhi pra ma̍ndase vṛṣan | kayā̍ sto̱tṛbhya̱ ā bha̍ra ||36.7||

---

<a id="mantra-8"></a>

indro̱ viśva̍sya rājati | śaṃ no̍ astu dvi̱pade̱ śaṃ catu̍ṣpade ||36.8||

---

<a id="mantra-9"></a>

śaṃ no̍ mi̱tra: śaṃ varu̍ṇa̱: śaṃ no̍ bhavatvarya̱mā |
śaṃ na̱ indro̱ bṛha̱spati̱: śaṃ no̱ viṣṇu̍rurukra̱maḥ ||36.9||

---

<a id="mantra-10"></a>

śaṃ no̱ vāta̍: pavatā śaṃ na̍stapatu̱ sūrya̍: |
śaṃ na̱: kani̍kradadde̱vaḥ pa̱rjanyo̍ a̱bhi va̍rṣatu ||36.10||

---

<a id="mantra-11"></a>

ahā̍ni̱ śaṃ bhava̍ntu na̱: śa rātrī̱: prati̍ dhīyatām |
śaṃ na̍ indrā̱gnī bha̍vatā̱mavo̍bhi̱: śaṃ na̱ indrā̱varu̍ṇā rā̱taha̍vyā |
śaṃ na̍ indrāpū̱ṣaṇā̱ vāja̍sātau̱ śamindrā̱somā̍ suvi̱tāya̱ śaṃ yoḥ ||36.11||

---

<a id="mantra-12"></a>

śaṃ no̍ de̱vīra̱bhiṣṭa̍ya̱ āpo̍ bhavantu pī̱taye̍ |
śaṃ yora̱bhi sra̍vantu naḥ ||36.12||

---

<a id="mantra-13"></a>

syo̱nā pṛ̍thivi no bhavānṛkṣa̱rā ni̱veśa̍nī |
yacchā̍ na̱: śarma̍ sa̱prathā̍: ||36.13||

---

<a id="mantra-14"></a>

āpo̱ hi ṣṭhā ma̍yo̱bhuva̱stā na̍ ū̱rje da̍dhātana | ma̱he raṇā̍ya̱ cakṣa̍se ||36.14||

---

<a id="mantra-15"></a>

yo va̍: śi̱vata̍mo̱ rasa̱stasya̍ bhājayate̱ha na̍: | u̱śa̱tīri̍va mā̱tara̍: ||36.15||

---

<a id="mantra-16"></a>

tasmā̱ ara̍ṃ gamāma vo̱ yasya̱ kṣayā̍ya̱ jinva̍tha | āpo̍ ja̱naya̍thā ca naḥ ||36.16||

---

<a id="mantra-17"></a>

dyau: śānti̍ra̱ntari̍kṣa śānti̍: pṛthi̱vī śānti̱rāpa̱: śānti̱roṣa̍dhaya̱: śānti̍: |
vana̱spata̍ya̱: śānti̱rviśve̍ de̱vāḥ śānti̱rbrahma̱ śānti̱: sarva śānti̱: śānti̍re̱va śānti̱: sā mā̱ śānti̍redhi ||36.17||

---

<a id="mantra-18"></a>

dṛte̱ dṛha̍ mā mi̱trasya̍ mā̱ cakṣu̍ṣā̱ sarvā̍ṇi bhū̱tāni̱ samī̍kṣantām |
mi̱trasyā̱haṃ cakṣu̍ṣā̱ sarvā̍ṇi bhū̱tāni̱ samī̍kṣe |
mi̱trasya̱ cakṣu̍ṣā̱ samī̍kṣāmahe ||36.18||

---

<a id="mantra-19"></a>

<a id="mantra-21"></a>

<a id="mantra-22"></a>
