<a id="mantra-1"></a>

tade̱va śu̱kraṃ tadbrahma̱ tā āpa̱: sa pra̱jāpa̍tiḥ ||32.1||

---

<a id="mantra-2"></a>

sarve̍ nime̱ṣā ja̍jñire vi̱dyuta̱: puru̍ṣā̱dadhi̍ |
nai̍namū̱rdhvaṃ na ti̱ryañca̱ṃ na madhye̱ pari̍ jagrabhat ||32.2||

---

<a id="mantra-3"></a>

na tasya̍ prati̱mā a̍sti̱ yasya̱ nāma̍ ma̱hadyaśa̍: |
hi̱ra̱ṇya̱ga̱rbha itye̱ṣaḥ  mā mā̍ hisī̱ditye̱ṣā yasmā̱nna jā̱ta itye̱ṣa: ||32.3||

---

<a id="mantra-4"></a>

e̱ṣo ha̍ de̱vaḥ pra̱diśo'nu̱ sarvā̱: pūrvo̍ ha jā̱taḥ sa u̱ garbhe̍ a̱ntaḥ |
sa e̱va jā̱taḥ sa ja̍ni̱ṣyamā̍ṇaḥ pra̱tyaṅ janā̍stiṣṭhati sa̱rvato̍mukhaḥ ||32.4||

---

<a id="mantra-5"></a>

yasmā̍jjā̱taṃ na pu̱rā kiṃ canai̱va ya ā̍ba̱bhūva̱ bhuva̍nāni̱ viśvā̍ |
pra̱jāpa̍tiḥ pra̱jayā̍ sararā̱ṇastrīṇi̱ jyotīṣi sacate̱ sa ṣo̍ḍa̱śī ||32.5||

---

<a id="mantra-6"></a>

yena̱ dyauru̱grā pṛ̍thi̱vī ca̍ dṛ̱ḍhā yena̱ sva̱ stabhi̱taṃ yena̱ nāka̍: |
yo a̱ntari̍kṣe̱ raja̍so vi̱māna̱: kasmai̍ de̱vāya̍ ha̱viṣā̍ vidhema ||32.6||

---

<a id="mantra-7"></a>

yaṃ kranda̍sī̱ ava̍sā tastabhā̱ne a̱bhyaikṣe̍tā̱ṃ mana̍sā̱ reja̍māne |
yatrādhi̱ sūra̱ udi̍to vi̱bhāti̱ kasmai̍ de̱vāya̍ ha̱viṣā̍ vidhema |
āpo̍ ha̱ yadbṛ̍ha̱tīryaści̱dāpa̍: ||32.7||

---

<a id="mantra-8"></a>

ve̱nastatpa̍śya̱nnihi̍ta̱ṃ guhā̱ sadyatra̱ viśva̱ṃ bhava̱tyeka̍nīḍam |
tasmi̍nni̱da saṃ ca̱ vi cai̍ti̱ sarva sa ota̱: prota̍śca vi̱bhūḥ pra̱jāsu̍ ||32.8||

---

<a id="mantra-9"></a>

pra tadvo̍ceda̱mṛta̱ṃ nu vi̱dvān ga̍ndha̱rvo dhāma̱ vibhṛ̍ta̱ṃ guhā̱ sat |
trīṇi̍ pa̱dāni̱ nihi̍tā̱ guhā̍sya̱ yastāni̱ veda̱ sa pi̱tuḥ pi̱tā'sa̍t ||32.9||

---

<a id="mantra-10"></a>

sa no̱ bandhu̍rjani̱tā sa vi̍dhā̱tā dhāmā̍ni veda̱ bhuva̍nāni̱ viśvā̍ |
yatra̍ de̱vā a̱mṛta̍mānaśā̱nāstṛ̱tīye̱ dhāma̍nna̱dhyaira̍yanta ||32.10||

---

<a id="mantra-11"></a>

pa̱rītya̍ bhū̱tāni̍ pa̱rītya̍ lo̱kān pa̱rītya̱ sarvā̍: pra̱diśo̱ diśa̍śca |
u̱pa̱sthāya̍ prathama̱jāmṛ̱tasyā̱tmanā̱''tmāna̍ma̱bhi saṃ vi̍veśa ||32.11||

---

<a id="mantra-12"></a>

pari̱ dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī sa̱dya i̱tvā pari̍ lo̱kān pari̱ diśa̱: pari̱ sva̱: |
ṛ̱tasya̱ tantu̱ṃ vita̍taṃ vi̱cṛtya̱ tada̍paśya̱ttada̍bhava̱ttadā̍sīt ||32.12||

---

<a id="mantra-13"></a>

sada̍sa̱spati̱madbhu̍taṃ pri̱yamindra̍sya̱ kāmya̍m | sa̱niṃ me̱dhāma̍yāsiṣasvāhā̍ ||32.13||

---

<a id="mantra-14"></a>

yāṃ me̱dhāṃ de̍vaga̱ṇāḥ pi̱tara̍śco̱pāsa̍te |
tayā̱ māma̱dya me̱dhayā'gne̍ me̱dhāvi̍naṃ kuru̱ svāhā̍ ||32.14||

---

<a id="mantra-15"></a>

me̱dhāṃ me̱ varu̍ṇo dadātu me̱dhāma̱gniḥ pra̱jāpa̍tiḥ |
me̱dhāmindra̍śca vā̱yuśca̍ me̱dhāṃ dhā̱tā da̍dātu me̱ svāhā̍ ||32.15||

---

<a id="mantra-16"></a>

i̱daṃ me̱ brahma̍ ca kṣa̱traṃ co̱bhe śriya̍maśnutām |
mayi̍ de̱vā da̍dhatu̱ śriya̱mutta̍mā̱ṃ tasyai̍ te̱ svāhā̍ ||32.16||
