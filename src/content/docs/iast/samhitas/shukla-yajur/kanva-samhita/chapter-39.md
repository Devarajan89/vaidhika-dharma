<a id="mantra-1"></a>

svāhā̍ prā̱ṇebhya̱ḥ sādhi̍patikebhyaḥ pṛthi̱vyai svāhā̱gnaye̱ svāhā̱ntari̍kṣāya̱ svāhā̍ vā̱yave̱ svāhā̍
di̱ve svāhā̱ sūryā̍ya̱ svāhā̍ | di̱gbhyaḥ svāhā̍ ca̱ndrāya̱ svāhā̱ nakṣa̍trebhya̱ḥ svāhā̱dbhyaḥ svāhā̱
varu̍ṇāya̱ svāhā̍ | nābhyai̱ svāhā̍ pū̱tāya̱ svāhā̍ ||39.1||

---

<a id="mantra-2"></a>

vā̱ce svāhā̍ prā̱ṇāya̱ svāhā̍ prā̱ṇāya̱ svāhā̍ |
cakṣu̍ṣe̱ svāhā̱ cakṣu̍ṣe̱ svāhā̱ śrotrā̍ya̱ svāhā̱ śrotrā̍ya̱ svāhā̍ ||39.2||

---

<a id="mantra-3"></a>

mana̍sa̱ḥ kāma̱mākū̍tiṃ vā̱caḥ sa̱tyama̍śīya |
pa̱śū̱nā  rū̱pamanna̍sya̱ raso̱ yaśa̱ḥ śrīḥ śra̍yatā̱ṃ mayi̱ svāhā̍ ||39.3||

---

<a id="mantra-4"></a>

pra̱jāpa̍tiḥ saṃbhri̱yamā̍ṇaḥ sa̱mrāṭ saṃbhṛ̍to vaiśvade̱vaḥ sa̍ sa̱nno gha̱rmaḥ pravṛ̍kta̱steja̱ udya̍ta āśvi̱naḥ
paya̍syānī̱yamā̍ne pū̱ṣā vi̍ṣya̱ndamā̍ne māru̱taḥ kvatha̍n |
mai̱traḥ śara̍si saṃtā̱yyamā̍ne vāya̱vyo̍ hri̱yamā̍ṇa āgne̱yo hū̱yamā̍no̱ vāgghu̱taḥ ||39.4||

---

<a id="mantra-5"></a>

sa̱vi̱tā pra̍tha̱me'ha̍nna̱gnirdvi̱tīye̍ vā̱yustṛ̱tīya̍ ādi̱tyaśca̍tu̱rthe
ca̱ndramā̍ḥ pañca̱ma ṛ̱tuḥ ṣa̱ṣṭhe ma̱ruta̍ḥ sapta̱me bṛ̍ha̱spati̍raṣṭa̱me |
mi̱tro na̍va̱me varu̍ṇo daśa̱ma indra̍ ekāda̱śe viśve̍ de̱vā dvā̍da̱śe ||39.5||

---

<a id="mantra-6"></a>

u̱graśca̍ bhī̱maśca̱ dhvānta̍śca̱ dhuni̍śca | sā̱sa̱hvā ścā̍bhiyu̱gvā ca̍ vi̱kṣipa̱ḥ svāhā̍ ||39.6||

---

<a id="mantra-7"></a>

a̱gni  hṛda̍yenā̱śani̍  hṛdayā̱greṇa̍ paśu̱pati̍ṃ kṛtsna̱hṛda̍yena bha̱vaṃ ya̱knā |
śa̱rvaṃ mata̍snābhyā̱mīśā̍naṃ ma̱nyunā̍ mahāde̱vama̍ntaḥparśa̱vyeno̱graṃ
de̱vaṃ va̍ni̱ṣṭhunā̍ vasiṣṭha̱hanu̱ḥ śiṅgī̍ni ko̱śyābhyā̍m ||39.7||

---

<a id="mantra-8"></a>

u̱gram̐ lohi̍tena mi̱tra  sau̍vratyena ru̱draṃ daurvra̍tye̱nendra̍ṃ prakrī̱l̤ena̍ ma̱ruto̱ bale̍na sā̱dhyānpra̱mudā̍ |
bha̱vasya̱ kaṇṭhya̍  ru̱drasyā̍ntaḥpā̱rśvyaṃ ma̍hāde̱vasya̱ yakṛ̍ccha̱rvasya̍ vani̱ṣṭhuḥ pa̍śu̱pate̍ḥ purī̱tat ||39.8||

---

<a id="mantra-9"></a>

loma̍bhya̱ḥ svāhā̱ loma̍bhya̱ḥ svāhā̍ tva̱ce svāhā̍ tva̱ce svāhā̱ lohi̍tāya̱ svāhā̱ lohi̍tāya̱ svāhā̱ meda̍se̱ svāhā̱ meda̍se̱ svāhā̍ |
mā̱sebhya̱ḥ svāhā̍ mā̱sebhya̱ḥ svāhā̱ snāva̍bhya̱ḥ svāhā̱ snāva̍bhya̱ḥ svāhā̱sthabhya̱ḥ svāhā̱sthabhya̱ḥ svāhā̍ ma̱jjabhya̱ḥ svāhā̍ ma̱jjabhya̱ḥ svāhā̍ |
reta̍se̱ svāhā̍ pā̱yave̱ svāhā̍ ||39.9||

---

<a id="mantra-10"></a>

ā̱yā̱sāya̱ svāhā̍ prāyā̱sāya̱ svāhā̍ saṃyā̱sāya̱ svāhā̍ viyā̱sāya̱ svāho̍dyā̱sāya̱ svāhā̍ |
śu̱ce svāhā̱ śoca̍te̱ svāhā̱ śokā̍ya̱ svāhā̍ ||39.10||

---

<a id="mantra-11"></a>

tapa̍se̱ svāhā̱ tapya̍mānāya̱ svāhā̍ ta̱ptāya̱ svāhā̍ |
niṣkṛ̍tyai̱ svāhā̱ prāya̍ścittyai̱ svāhā̍ bheṣa̱jāya̱ svāhā̍ ||39.11||

---

<a id="mantra-12"></a>

ya̱māya̱ svāhānta̍kāya̱ svāhā̍ mṛ̱tyave̱ svāhā̱ |
bra̱hma̱ha̱tyāyai̱ svāhā̱ viśve̍bhyo de̱vebhya̱ḥ svāhā̱ dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vībhyā̱  svāhā̍ ||39.12||
