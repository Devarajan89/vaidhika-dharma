ca̱tura̍ e̱tadvai

stanā̎nvra̱tamupai̱tyatha̱ trīnatha̱ dvāvathaika̍me̱tadvai su̍jagha̱naṃ nāma̍ vra̱taṃ
ta̍pa̱syṛ̍ suva̱rgya̍matho̱ praiva jā̍yate pra̱jayā̍ pa̱śubhi̍ryavā̱gū
rā̍ja̱nya̍sya vra̱taṃ krū̱reva̱ vai ya̍vā̱gūḥ krū̱ra i̍va
27 rāja̱nyo̍ vajra̍sya rū̱pṛ samṛ̍ddhyā ā̱mikṣā̱ vaiśya̍sya pākaya̱jñasya̍

vā e̱ṣa yaddī̎kṣi̱to yada̍sya̱ payo̎ vra̱taṃ bhava̍tyā̱tmāna̍me̱va tadva̍rdhayati̱
trivra̍to̱ vai manu̍rāsī̱ddvivra̍tā̱ asu̍rā̱ eka̍vratā
28 de̱vāḥ prā̱tarma̱dhyaṃdi̍ne sā̱yaṃ tanmano̎rvra̱tamā̍sītpākaya̱jñasya̍ rū̱paṃ
puṣṭyai̎ prā̱taśca̍ sā̱yaṃ cāsu̍rāṇāṃ nirma̱dhyaṃ kṣu̱dho rū̱paṃ tata̱ste
parā̍bhavanma̱dhyaṃdi̍ne madhyarā̱tre de̱vānā̱ṃ tata̱ste̍'bhavanthsuva̱rgaṃ

bhava̍tyā̱tmanā̱

vimi̱taṃ yaddī̎kṣi̱to dī̎kṣitavimi̱tātpra̱vase̱dyathā̱ yone̱rgarbha̱ḥ skanda̍ti
tā̱dṛge̱va tanna pra̍vasta̱vya̍mā̱tmano̍ gopī̱thāyai̱ṣa vai vyā̱ghraḥ ku̍lago̱po
yada̱gnistasmā̱dyaddī̎kṣi̱taḥ pra̱vase̱thsa e̍namīśva̱ro̍'nū̱tthāya̱
hanto̱rna pra̍vasta̱vya̍mā̱tmano̱ guptyai̍ dakṣiṇa̱taḥ śa̍ya e̱tadvai
yaja̍mānasyā̱yata̍na̱gg̱ sva e̱vāyata̍ne śaye̱'gnima̍bhyā̱vṛtya̍ śaye de̱vatā̍
e̱va ya̱jñama̍bhyā̱vṛtya̍ śaye ||6.2.5||

na̍meda̱bhi su̍va̱rgaṃ lo̱kaṃ ja̍ye̱ditye̱tadvai pu̱roha̍virdeva̱yaja̍na̱ṃ
yasya̱ hotā̎ prātaranuvā̱kama̍nubru̱vanna̱gnima̱pa ā̍di̱tyama̱bhi
vi̱paśya̱tyupai̍na̱mutta̍ro ya̱jño na̍matya̱bhi su̍va̱rgaṃ lo̱kaṃ ja̍yatyā̱pte
de̍va̱yaja̍ne yājaye̱dbhrātṛ̍vyavanta̱ṃ panthā̎ṃ vādhispa̱ṟśaye̎tka̱rtaṃ vā̱
yāva̱nnāna̍se̱ yāta̱ vai
31 na rathā̍yai̱tadvā ā̱ptaṃ de̍va̱yaja̍namā̱pnotye̱va bhrātṛ̍vya̱ṃ naina̱ṃ
bhrātṛ̍vya āpno̱tyeko̎nnate deva̱ yaja̍ne yājayetpa̱śukā̍ma̱meko̎nnatā̱dvai
de̍va̱yaja̍nā̱daṃgi̍rasaḥ pa̱śūna̍sṛjantānta̱rā sa̍dohavirdhā̱ne u̍nna̱tagg
syā̍de̱tadvā eko̎nnataṃ deva̱yaja̍naṃ paśu̱māne̱va bha̍vati̱ tryu̍nnate deva̱yaja̍ne
yājayethsuva̱rgakā̍ma̱ṃ tryu̍nnatā̱dvai de̍va̱yaja̍nā̱daṃgi̍rasaḥ suva̱rgaṃ
lo̱kamā̍yannanta̱rāha̍va̱nīya̍ṃ ca̱ havi̱rdhāna̍ṃ co
32 nna̱tagg syā̍danta̱rā ha̍vi̱rdhāna̍ṃ ca̱ sada̍ścānta̱rā sada̍śca̱ gārha̍patyaṃ
cai̱tadvai tryu̍nnataṃ deva̱yaja̍nṛ suva̱rgame̱va lo̱kame̍ti̱ prati̍ṣṭhite
deva̱yaja̍ne yājayetprati̱ṣṭhākā̍mame̱tadvai prati̍ṣṭhitaṃ deva̱yaja̍na̱ṃ
yathsa̱rvata̍ḥ sa̱maṃ pratye̱va ti̍ṣṭhati̱ yatrā̱nyā a̍nyā̱ oṣa̍dhayo̱
vyati̍ṣaktā̱ḥ syustadyā̍jayetpa̱śukā̍mame̱tadvai pa̍śū̱nṛ rū̱pṛ
rū̱peṇai̱vāsmai̍ pa̱śū

kā̱maye̍ta̱ nirṛ̍tyāsya ya̱jñaṃ grā̍hayeya̱mitye̱tadvai nirṛ̍tigṛhītaṃ
deva̱yaja̍na̱ṃ yathsa̱dṛśyai̍ sa̱tyā̍ ṛ̱kṣaṃ nirṛ̍tyai̱vāsya̍ ya̱jñaṃ

gārha̍patyāde̱tadvai vyāvṛ̍ttaṃ deva̱yaja̍na̱ṃ vi pā̱pmanā̱ bhrātṛ̍vye̱ṇā
va̍rtate̱ naina̱ṃ pātre̱ na talpe̍ mīmṛsante kā̱rye̍ deva̱yaja̍ne
yājaye̱dbhūti̍kāmaṃ kā̱ṟyo̍ vai puru̍ṣo̱ bhava̍tye̱va ||6.2.6||

kṛ̱tvobhayā̍nanta̱rāpa̱kramyāti̍ṣṭhadu̱ru pra̍thasvo̱ru te̍ ya̱jñapa̍tiḥ
prathatā̱mityā̍ha̱ yaja̍māname̱va pra̱jayā̍ pa̱śubhi̍ḥ prathayati dhru̱vā
37 'sīti̱ sṛ ha̍nti̱ dhṛtyai̍ de̱vebhya̍ḥ śundhasva
de̱vebhya̍ḥ śuṃbha̱svetyava̍ co̱kṣati̱ pra ca̍ kirati̱ śuddhyā̍
indragho̱ṣastvā̱ vasu̍bhiḥ pu̱rastā̎tpā̱tvityā̍ha di̱gbhya e̱vainā̱ṃ prokṣa̍ti
de̱vāgścedu̍ttarave̱diru̱pāva̍vartī̱haiva vi ja̍yāmahā̱ ityasu̍rā̱ vajra̍mu̱dyatya̍

tadda̍kṣiṇa̱ta u̍ttarave̱dyai ni na̍ye̱dyade̱va tatra̍ krū̱raṃ tattena̍ śamayati̱ yaṃ

sṛ̱hīra̍si suprajā̱vani̱ḥ svāheti̱ te pra̱jāma̍vindanta̱ te pra̱jāṃ vi̱ttvā
40 'kā̍mayanta pa̱śūn, vi̍ndema̱hīti̱ te̍'juhavuḥ sṛ̱hīra̍si
rāyaspoṣa̱vani̱ḥ svāheti̱ te pa̱śūna̍vindanta̱ te pa̱śūn, vi̱ttvākā̍mayanta
prati̱ṣṭhāṃ vi̍ndema̱hīti̱ te̍'juhavuḥ sṛ̱hīra̍syāditya̱vani̱ḥ svāheti̱ ta
i̱māṃ pra̍ti̱ṣṭhāma̍vindanta̱ ta i̱māṃ pra̍ti̱ṣṭhāṃ vi̱ttvākā̍mayanta de̱vatā̍
ā̱śiṣa̱ upe̍yā̱meti̱ te̍'juhavuḥ sṛ̱hīra̱syā va̍ha de̱vānde̍vaya̱te
41 yaja̍mānāya̱ svāheti̱ te de̱vatā̍ ā̱śiṣa̱ upā̍ya̱npañca̱ kṛtvo̱
vyāghā̍rayati̱ pañcā̎kṣarā pa̱ṅktiḥ pāṅkto̍ ya̱jño ya̱jñame̱vāva̍
ruṃdhe'kṣṇa̱yā vyāghā̍rayati̱ tasmā̍dakṣṇa̱yā pa̱śavo'ṅgā̍ni̱ pra ha̍ranti̱
prati̍ṣṭhityai bhū̱tebhya̱stveti̱ sruca̱mudgṛ̍hṇāti̱ ya e̱va de̱vā bhū̱tāsteṣā̱ṃ
tadbhā̍ga̱dheya̱ṃ tāne̱va tena̍ prīṇāti̱ pautu̍dravānpari̱dhīnpari̍ dadhātye̱ṣāṃ
42 lo̱kānā̱ṃ vidhṛ̍tyā a̱gnestrayo̱ jyāyṛ̍so̱ bhrāta̍ra āsa̱nte de̱vebhyo̍
ha̱vyaṃ vaha̍nta̱ḥ prāmī̍yanta̱ so̎'gnira̍bibhedi̱tthaṃ vāva sya ārti̱māri̍ṣya̱tīti̱
sa nilā̍yata̱ sa yāṃ vana̱spati̱ṣvava̍sa̱ttāṃ pūtu̍drau̱ yāmoṣa̍dhīṣu̱ tṛ
su̍gandhi̱teja̍ne̱ yāṃ pa̱śuṣu̱ tāṃ petva̍syānta̱rā śaṛṅge̱ taṃ de̱vatā̱ḥ
praiṣa̍maiccha̱ntamanva̍vinda̱ntama̍bruva̱
43 nnupa̍ na̱ ā va̍rtasva ha̱vyaṃ no̍ va̱heti̱ so̎'bravī̱dvara̍ṃ vṛṇai̱
yade̱va gṛ̍hī̱tasyāhu̍tasya bahiḥ pari̱dhi skandā̱ttanme̱ bhrātṛ̍ṇāṃ
bhāga̱dheya̍masa̱diti̱ tasmā̱dyadgṛ̍hī̱tasyāhu̍tasya bahiḥ pari̱dhi skanda̍ti̱
teṣā̱ṃ tadbhā̍ga̱dheya̱ṃ tāne̱va tena̍ prīṇāti̱ so̍'manyatāstha̱nvanto̍ me̱
pūrve̱ bhrāta̍ra̱ḥ prāme̍ṣatā̱sthāni̍ śātayā̱ iti̱ sa yānya̱

tadgulgu̍lu̱ yade̱tānthsa̍ṃbhā̱rānthsa̱ṃbhara̍tya̱gnime̱va tathsaṃbha̍ratya̱gneḥ
purī̍ṣama̱sītyā̍hā̱gnerhye̍tatpurī̍ṣa̱ṃ yathsa̍ṃbhā̱rā atho̱ khalvā̍hure̱te

e̱vaine̍ karoti sāvitri̱yarcā hu̱tvā ha̍vi̱rdhāne̱ pra va̍rtayati savi̱tṛpra̍sūta
e̱vaine̱ pra va̍rtayati̱ varu̍ṇo̱ vā e̱ṣa du̱rvāgu̍bha̱yato̍ ba̱ddho yadakṣa̱ḥ
sa yadu̱thsarje̱dyaja̍mānasya gṛ̱hāna̱bhyuthsa̍rjethsu̱vāgde̍va̱ duryṛ̱
ā va̱detyā̍ha gṛ̱hā vai duryā̱ḥ śāntyai̱ patnyu
46 pā̍nakti̱ patnī̱ hi sarva̍sya mi̱traṃ mi̍tra̱tvāya̱ yadvai patnī̍ ya̱jñasya̍
ka̱roti̍ mithu̱naṃ tadatho̱ patni̍yā e̱vaiṣa ya̱jñasyā̎nvāra̱ṃbhona̍vacchittyai̱
vartma̍nā̱ vā a̱nvitya̍ ya̱jñṛ rakṣṛ̍si jighṛsanti
vaiṣṇa̱vībhyā̍mṛ̱gbhyāṃ vartma̍norjuhoti ya̱jño vai viṣṇu̍rya̱jñāde̱va
rakṣā̱g̱syapa̍ hanti̱ yada̍dhva̱ryura̍na̱gnāvāhu̍tiṃ juhu̱yāda̱ndho̎'dhva̱ryuḥ
syā̱drakṣṛ̍si ya̱jñṛ ha̍nyu̱ṟ
47 hira̍ṇyamu̱pāsya̍ juhotyagni̱vatye̱va ju̍hoti̱ nāndho̎'dhva̱ryurbhava̍ti̱ na
ya̱jñṛ rakṣṛ̍si ghnanti̱ prācī̱ preta̍madhva̱raṃ ka̱lpaya̍ntī̱ ityā̍ha
suva̱rgame̱vaine̍ lo̱kaṃ ga̍maya̱tyatra̍ ramethā̱ṃ varṣma̍npṛthi̱vyā ityā̍ha̱
