sambhṛ̍tasambhāra̱ ityā̍hu̱rna sa̱mbhṛtyā̎ḥ sambhā̱rā na yaju̍ḥ karta̱vya̍mityatho̱

itya̍gniho̱traṃ ju̍hoti̱ yatra̍yatrai̱vāsya̱ nya̍kta̱ṃ tata̍
9 e̱vaina̱mava̍ rundhe vīra̱hā vā e̱ṣa de̱vānā̱ṃ yo̎'gnimu̍dvā̱saya̍te̱ tasya̱
