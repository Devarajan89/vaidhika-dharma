prāṅi̍va̱ hi su̍va̱rgo
4 lo̱kaḥ sara̍svatyā yāntye̱ṣa vai de̍va̱yāna̱ḥ panthā̱stame̱vānvāro̍hantyā̱krośa̍nto
yā̱ntyava̍rtime̱vānyasmi̍nprati̱ṣajya̍ prati̱ṣṭhāṃ ga̍cchanti ya̱dā
