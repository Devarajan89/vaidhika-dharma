1 de̱vasya̍ tvā savi̱tuḥ pra̍sa̱ve̎'śvino̎rbā̱hubhyā̎ṃ pū̱ṣṇo
hastā̎bhyā̱māda̱de'bhri̍rasi̱ nāri̍rasi̱ pari̍likhitṛ̱ rakṣa̱ḥ
pari̍likhitā̱ arā̍taya i̱dama̱hṛ rakṣa̍so grī̱vā api̍ kṛntāmi̱ yo̎'smān
dveṣṭi̱ yaṃ ca̍ va̱yaṃ dvi̱ṣma i̱dama̍sya grī̱vā api̍ kṛntāmi di̱ve
tvā̱'ntari̍kṣāya tvā pṛthi̱vyai tvā̱ śundha̍tāṃ lo̱kaḥ pi̍tṛ̱ṣada̍no̱
yavo̍'si ya̱vayā̱smaddveṣo̍
2 ya̱vayārā̍tīḥ pitṛ̱ṇṛ sada̍nama̱syuddivagg̍ stabhā̱nā'ntari̍kṣaṃ pṛṇa
pṛthi̱vīṃ dṛ̍ha dyutā̱nastvā̍ māru̱to mi̍notu mi̱trāvaru̍ṇayordhru̱veṇa̱
dharma̍ṇā brahma̱vani̍ṃ tvā kṣatra̱vanṛ̍ suprajā̱vanṛ̍ rāyaspoṣa̱vani̱ṃ
paryū̍hāmi̱ brahma̍ dṛha kṣa̱traṃ dṛ̍ha pra̱jāṃ dṛ̍ha
rā̱yaspoṣa̍ṃ dṛha ghṛ̱tena̍ dyāvāpṛthivī̱ ā pṛ̍ṇethā̱mindra̍sya̱
sado̍'si viśvaja̱nasya̍ chā̱yā pari̍ tvā girvaṇo̱ gira̍ i̱mā bha̍vantu
vi̱śvato̍ vṛ̱ddhāyu̱manu̱ vṛddha̍yo̱ juṣṭā̍ bhavantu̱ juṣṭa̍ya̱ indra̍sya̱
syūra̱sīndra̍sya dhru̱vama̍syai̱ndrama̱sīndrā̍ya tvā || dveṣa̍ i̱mā a̱ṣṭāda̍śa
ca ||1.3.1||

3 ra̱kṣo̱haṇo̍ valaga̱hano̍ vaiṣṇa̱vānkha̍nāmī̱dama̱haṃ taṃ va̍la̱gamudva̍pāmi̱
yaṃ na̍ḥ samā̱no yamasa̍māno nica̱khāne̱dame̍na̱madha̍raṃ karomi̱ yo na̍ḥ samā̱no
yo'sa̍māno'rātī̱yati̍ gāya̱treṇa̱ chanda̱sā'va̍bāḍho vala̱gaḥ kimatra̍ bha̱draṃ
tannau̍ sa̱ha vi̱rāḍa̍si sapatna̱hā sa̱mrāḍa̍si bhrātṛvya̱hā sva̱rāḍa̍syabhimāti̱hā
vi̍śvā̱rāḍa̍si̱ viśvā̍sāṃ nā̱ṣṭrāṇṛ̍ ha̱ntā
4 ra̍kṣo̱haṇo̍ valaga̱hana̱ḥ prokṣā̍mi vaiṣṇa̱vān ra̍kṣo̱haṇo̍ valaga̱hano'va̍
nayāmi vaiṣṇa̱vān, yavo̍'si ya̱vayā̱smaddveṣo̍ ya̱vayārā̍tī rakṣo̱haṇo̍
valaga̱hano'va̍ stṛṇāmi vaiṣṇa̱vān ra̍kṣo̱haṇo̍ valaga̱hano̱'bhi ju̍homi
vaiṣṇa̱vān ra̍kṣo̱haṇau̍ valaga̱hanā̱vupa̍ dadhāmi vaiṣṇa̱vī ra̍kṣo̱haṇau̍
valaga̱hanau̱ paryū̍hāmi vaiṣṇa̱vī ra̍kṣo̱haṇau̍ valaga̱hanau̱ pari̍ stṛṇāmi
vaiṣṇa̱vī ra̍kṣo̱haṇau̍ valaga̱hanau̍ vaiṣṇa̱vī bṛ̱hanna̍si bṛ̱hadgrā̍vā

varū̍tha̱gg̱ svāhā̍ juṣā̱ṇo a̱pturājya̍sya vetu̱ svāhā̱'yaṃ no̍ a̱gnirvari̍vaḥ
kṛṇotva̱yaṃ mṛdha̍ḥ pu̱ra e̍tu prabhi̱ndann | a̱yṛ śatrū̎ñjayatu̱
jarhṛ̍ṣāṇo̱'yaṃ vāja̍ṃ jayatu̱ vāja̍sātau || u̱ru vi̍ṣṇo̱ vi kra̍masvo̱ru

7 de̱vānā̍meti niṣkṛ̱tamṛ̱tasya̱ yoni̍mā̱sada̱madi̍tyā̱ḥ sado̱'syadi̍tyā̱ḥ

nirvaru̍ṇasya̱ pāśā̱t suva̍ra̱bhi
8 vi khye̍ṣaṃ vaiśvāna̱raṃ jyoti̱ragne̎ vratapate̱ tvaṃ vra̱tānā̎ṃ vra̱tapa̍tirasi̱
yā mama̍ ta̱nūstvayyabhū̍di̱yṛ sā mayi̱ yā tava̍ ta̱nūrmayyabhū̍de̱ṣā sā tvayi̍
yathāya̱thaṃ nau̎ vratapate vra̱tino̎rvra̱tāni̍ || gā̱tu̱vida̱bhyeka̍trṛśacca ||1.3.4||

viṣṇo̎ḥ para̱maṃ pa̱damava̍ bhāti̱ bhūre̎ḥ || viṣṇo̱ḥ karmā̍ṇi paśyata̱ yato̎
vra̱tāni̍ paspa̱śe | indra̍sya̱ yujya̱ḥ sakhā̎ || tadviṣṇo̎ḥ para̱maṃ pa̱dṛ

dṛha kṣa̱ttraṃ dṛ̍ha pra̱jāṃ dṛ̍ha rā̱yaspoṣa̍ṃ dṛha
