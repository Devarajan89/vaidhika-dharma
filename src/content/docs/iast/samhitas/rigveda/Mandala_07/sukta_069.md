# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 69

**8 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| aśvinau | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 1

ā vā̱ṃ ratho̱ roda̍sī badbadhā̱no hi̍ra̱ṇyayo̱ vṛṣa̍bhiryā̱tvaśvai̍: |
ghṛ̱tava̍rtaniḥ pa̱vibhī̍ rucā̱na i̱ṣāṃ vo̱l̤hā nṛ̱pati̍rvā̱jinī̍vān

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 2

sa pa̍prathā̱no a̱bhi pañca̱ bhūmā̍ trivandhu̱ro mana̱sā yā̍tu yu̱ktaḥ |

viśo̱ yena̱ gaccha̍tho deva̱yantī̱: kutrā̍ ci̱dyāma̍maśvinā̱ dadhā̍nā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 3

svaśvā̍ ya̱śasā yā̍tama̱rvāgdasrā̍ ni̱dhiṃ madhu̍mantaṃ pibāthaḥ |

vi vā̱ṃ ratho̍ va̱dhvā̱3 yāda̍mā̱no'ntā̍ndi̱vo bā̍dhate varta̱nibhyā̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 4

yu̱voḥ śriya̱ṃ pari̱ yoṣā̍vṛṇīta̱ sūro̍ duhi̱tā pari̍takmyāyām |

yadde̍va̱yanta̱mava̍tha̱: śacī̍bhi̱: pari̍ ghra̱ṃsamo̱manā̍ vā̱ṃ vayo̍ gāt

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 5

yo ha̱ sya vā̍ṃ rathirā̱ vasta̍ u̱srā ratho̍ yujā̱naḥ pa̍ri̱yāti̍ va̱rtiḥ |

tena̍ na̱: śaṃ yoru̱ṣaso̱ vyu̍ṣṭau̱ nya̍śvinā vahataṃ ya̱jñe a̱smin

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 6

narā̍ gau̱reva̍ vi̱dyuta̍ṃ tṛṣā̱ṇā'smāka̍ma̱dya sava̱nopa̍ yātam |

pu̱ru̱trā hi vā̍ṃ ma̱tibhi̱rhava̍nte̱ mā vā̍ma̱nye ni ya̍mandeva̱yanta̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 7

yu̱vaṃ bhu̱jyumava̍viddhaṃ samu̱dra udū̍hathu̱rarṇa̍so̱ asri̍dhānaiḥ |

pa̱ta̱tribhi̍raśra̱maira̍vya̱thibhi̍rda̱ṃsanā̍bhiraśvinā pā̱raya̍ntā

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 8

nū me̱ hava̱mā śṛ̍ṇutaṃ yuvānā yāsi̱ṣṭaṃ va̱rtira̍śvinā̱virā̍vat |

dha̱ttaṃ ratnā̍ni̱ jara̍taṃ ca sū̱rīnyū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
