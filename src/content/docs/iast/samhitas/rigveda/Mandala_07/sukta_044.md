# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 44

**5 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| dadhikā:, 1 dadhikāśvyuṣo' gnibhagendraviṣṇupūṣabrahmaṇaspatyādityadyāvāpṛthivyāpa:| triṣṭup ,1 jagatī|**

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 1

da̱dhi̱krāṃ va̍: pratha̱mama̱śvino̱ṣasa̍ma̱gniṃ sami̍ddha̱ṃ bhaga̍mū̱taye̍ huve |
indra̱ṃ viṣṇu̍ṃ pū̱ṣaṇa̱ṃ brahma̍ṇa̱spati̍mādi̱tyāndyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī a̱paḥ sva̍:

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 2

il̤ā̍ṃ de̱vīṃ ba̱rhiṣi̍ sā̱daya̍nto̱'śvinā̱ viprā̍ su̱havā̍ huvema

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 3
