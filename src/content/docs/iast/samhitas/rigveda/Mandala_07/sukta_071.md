# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 71

**6  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| aśvinau | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 1

apa̱ svasu̍ru̱ṣaso̱ nagji̍hīte ri̱ṇakti̍ kṛ̱ṣṇīra̍ru̱ṣāya̱ panthā̍m |
aśvā̍maghā̱ goma̍ghā vāṃ huvema̱ divā̱ nakta̱ṃ śaru̍ma̱smadyu̍yotam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 2

u̱pāyā̍taṃ dā̱śuṣe̱ martyā̍ya̱ rathe̍na vā̱mama̍śvinā̱ vaha̍ntā |

yu̱yu̱tama̱smadani̍rā̱mamī̍vā̱ṃ divā̱ nakta̍ṃ mādhvī̱ trāsī̍thāṃ naḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 3

ā vā̱ṃ ratha̍mava̱masyā̱ṃ vyu̍ṣṭau sumnā̱yavo̱ vṛṣa̍ṇo vartayantu |

syūma̍gabhastimṛta̱yugbhi̱raśvai̱rāśvi̍nā̱ vasu̍mantaṃ vahethām

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 4

yo vā̱ṃ ratho̍ nṛpatī̱ asti̍ vo̱l̤hā tri̍vandhu̱ro vasu̍mām̐ u̱srayā̍mā |

ā na̍ e̱nā nā̍sa̱tyopa̍ yātama̱bhi yadvā̍ṃ vi̱śvapsnyo̱ jigā̍ti

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 5

yu̱vaṃ cyavā̍naṃ ja̱raso̍'mumukta̱ṃ ni pe̱dava̍ ūhathurā̱śumaśva̍m |

niraṃha̍sa̱stama̍saḥ sparta̱matri̱ṃ ni jā̍hu̱ṣaṃ śi̍thi̱re dhā̍tama̱ntaḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 6

i̱yaṃ ma̍nī̱ṣā i̱yama̍śvinā̱ gīri̱māṃ su̍vṛ̱ktiṃ vṛ̍ṣaṇā juṣethām |

i̱mā brahmā̍ṇi yuva̱yūnya̍gmanyū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
