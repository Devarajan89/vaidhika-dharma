# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 65

**5 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| mitrāvaruṇau | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 65 | **Verse:** 1

prati̍ vā̱ṃ sūra̱ udi̍te sū̱ktairmi̱traṃ hu̍ve̱ varu̍ṇaṃ pū̱tada̍kṣam |
yayo̍rasu̱rya1makṣi̍ta̱ṃ jyeṣṭha̱ṃ viśva̍sya̱ yāma̍nnā̱citā̍ jiga̱tnu

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 65 | **Verse:** 2

tā hi de̱vānā̱masu̍rā̱ tāva̱ryā tā na̍: kṣi̱tīḥ ka̍ratamū̱rjaya̍ntīḥ |

a̱śyāma̍ mitrāvaruṇā va̱yaṃ vā̱ṃ dyāvā̍ ca̱ yatra̍ pī̱paya̱nnahā̍ ca

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 65 | **Verse:** 3

tā bhūri̍pāśā̱vanṛ̍tasya̱ setū̍ dura̱tyetū̍ ri̱pave̱ martyā̍ya |

ṛ̱tasya̍ mitrāvaruṇā pa̱thā vā̍ma̱po na nā̱vā du̍ri̱tā ta̍rema

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 65 | **Verse:** 4

ā no̍ mitrāvaruṇā ha̱vyaju̍ṣṭiṃ ghṛ̱tairgavyū̍timukṣata̱mil̤ā̍bhiḥ |

prati̍ vā̱matra̱ vara̱mā janā̍ya pṛṇī̱tamu̱dno di̱vyasya̱ cāro̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 65 | **Verse:** 5

e̱ṣa stomo̍ varuṇa mitra̱ tubhya̱ṃ soma̍: śu̱kro na vā̱yave̍'yāmi |

a̱vi̱ṣṭaṃ dhiyo̍ jigṛ̱taṃ pura̍ṃdhīryū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
