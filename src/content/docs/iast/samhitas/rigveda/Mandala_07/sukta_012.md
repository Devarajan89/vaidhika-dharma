# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 12

**3 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| agni: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 1

aga̍nma ma̱hā nama̍sā̱ yavi̍ṣṭha̱ṃ yo dī̱dāya̱ sami̍ddha̱: sve du̍ro̱ṇe |
ci̱trabhā̍nu̱ṃ roda̍sī a̱ntaru̱rvī svā̍hutaṃ vi̱śvata̍: pra̱tyañca̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 2

sa ma̱hnā viśvā̍ duri̱tāni̍ sā̱hvāna̱gniḥ ṣṭa̍ve̱ dama̱ ā jā̱tave̍dāḥ |

sa no̍ rakṣiṣadduri̱tāda̍va̱dyāda̱smāngṛ̍ṇa̱ta u̱ta no̍ ma̱ghona̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 3

tvaṃ varu̍ṇa u̱ta mi̱tro a̍gne̱ tvāṃ va̍rdhanti ma̱tibhi̱rvasi̍ṣṭhāḥ |

tve vasu̍ suṣaṇa̱nāni̍ santu yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
