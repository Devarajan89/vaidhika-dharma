# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 11

**5 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| agni: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 1

ma̱hām̐ a̍syadhva̱rasya̍ prake̱to na ṛ̱te tvada̱mṛtā̍ mādayante |
ā viśve̍bhiḥ sa̱ratha̍ṃ yāhi de̱vairnya̍gne̱ hotā̍ pratha̱maḥ sa̍de̱ha

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 2

tvāmī̍l̤ate aji̱raṃ dū̱tyā̍ya ha̱viṣma̍nta̱: sada̱minmānu̍ṣāsaḥ |

yasya̍ de̱vairāsa̍do ba̱rhira̱gne'hā̍nyasmai su̱dinā̍ bhavanti

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 3

triści̍da̱ktoḥ pra ci̍kitu̱rvasū̍ni̱ tve a̱ntardā̱śuṣe̱ martyā̍ya |

ma̱nu̱ṣvada̍gna i̱ha ya̍kṣi de̱vānbhavā̍ no dū̱to a̍bhiśasti̱pāvā̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 4

a̱gnirī̍śe bṛha̱to a̍dhva̱rasyā̱'gnirviśva̍sya ha̱viṣa̍: kṛ̱tasya̍ |

kratu̱ṃ hya̍sya̱ vasa̍vo ju̱ṣantā'thā̍ de̱vā da̍dhire havya̱vāha̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 5

āgne̍ vaha havi̱radyā̍ya de̱vānindra̍jyeṣṭhāsa i̱ha mā̍dayantām |

i̱maṃ ya̱jñaṃ di̱vi de̱veṣu̍ dhehi yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
