# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 93

**8  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| indrāgnī| triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 1

śuci̱ṃ nu stoma̱ṃ nava̍jātama̱dyendrā̍gnī vṛtrahaṇā ju̱ṣethā̍m |
u̱bhā hi vā̍ṃ su̱havā̱ joha̍vīmi̱ tā vāja̍ṃ sa̱dya u̍śa̱te dheṣṭhā̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 2

tā sā̍na̱sī śa̍vasānā̱ hi bhū̱taṃ sā̍ka̱ṃvṛdhā̱ śava̍sā śūśu̱vāṃsā̍ |

kṣaya̍ntau rā̱yo yava̍sasya̱ bhūre̍: pṛ̱ṅktaṃ vāja̍sya̱ sthavi̍rasya̱ ghṛṣve̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 3

upo̍ ha̱ yadvi̱datha̍ṃ vā̱jino̱ gurdhī̱bhirviprā̱: prama̍timi̱cchamā̍nāḥ |

arva̍nto̱ na kāṣṭhā̱ṃ nakṣa̍māṇā indrā̱gnī johu̍vato̱ nara̱ste

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 4

gī̱rbhirvipra̱: prama̍timi̱cchamā̍na̱ īṭṭe̍ ra̱yiṃ ya̱śasa̍ṃ pūrva̱bhāja̍m |

indrā̍gnī vṛtrahaṇā suvajrā̱ pra no̱ navye̍bhistirataṃ de̱ṣṇaiḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 5

saṃ yanma̱hī mi̍tha̱tī spardha̍māne tanū̱rucā̱ śūra̍sātā̱ yatai̍te |

ade̍vayuṃ vi̱dathe̍ deva̱yubhi̍: sa̱trā ha̍taṃ soma̱sutā̱ jane̍na

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 6

i̱māmu̱ ṣu soma̍suti̱mupa̍ na̱ endrā̍gnī saumana̱sāya̍ yātam |

nū ci̱ddhi pa̍rima̱mnāthe̍ a̱smānā vā̱ṃ śaśva̍dbhirvavṛtīya̱ vājai̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 7

so a̍gna e̱nā nama̍sā̱ sami̱ddho'cchā̍ mi̱traṃ varu̍ṇa̱mindra̍ṃ voceḥ |

yatsī̱māga̍ścakṛ̱mā tatsu mṛ̍l̤a̱ tada̍rya̱mādi̍tiḥ śiśrathantu

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 8

e̱tā a̍gna āśuṣā̱ṇāsa̍ i̱ṣṭīryu̱voḥ sacā̱bhya̍śyāma̱ vājā̍n |

mendro̍ no̱ viṣṇu̍rma̱ruta̱: pari̍ khyanyū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
