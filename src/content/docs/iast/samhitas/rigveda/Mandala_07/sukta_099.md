# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 99

**7  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| viṣṇu:, 4-6 indrāviṣṇū | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 1

pa̱ro mātra̍yā ta̱nvā̍ vṛdhāna̱ na te̍ mahi̱tvamanva̍śnuvanti |
u̱bhe te̍ vidma̱ raja̍sī pṛthi̱vyā viṣṇo̍ deva̱ tvaṃ pa̍ra̱masya̍ vitse

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 2

na te̍ viṣṇo̱ jāya̍māno̱ na jā̱to deva̍ mahi̱mnaḥ para̱manta̍māpa |

uda̍stabhnā̱ nāka̍mṛ̱ṣvaṃ bṛ̱hanta̍ṃ dā̱dhartha̱ prācī̍ṃ ka̱kubha̍ṃ pṛthi̱vyāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 3

irā̍vatī dhenu̱matī̱ hi bhū̱taṃ sū̍yava̱sinī̱ manu̍ṣe daśa̱syā |

vya̍stabhnā̱ roda̍sī viṣṇave̱te dā̱dhartha̍ pṛthi̱vīma̱bhito̍ ma̱yūkhai̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 4

u̱ruṃ ya̱jñāya̍ cakrathuru lo̱kaṃ ja̱naya̍ntā̱ sūrya̍mu̱ṣāsa̍ma̱gnim |

dāsa̍sya cidvṛṣaśi̱prasya̍ mā̱yā ja̱ghnathu̍rnarā pṛta̱nājye̍ṣu

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 5

indrā̍viṣṇū dṛṃhi̱tāḥ śamba̍rasya̱ nava̱ puro̍ nava̱tiṃ ca̍ śnathiṣṭam |

śa̱taṃ va̱rcina̍: sa̱hasra̍ṃ ca sā̱kaṃ ha̱tho a̍pra̱tyasu̍rasya vī̱rān

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 6

i̱yaṃ ma̍nī̱ṣā bṛ̍ha̱tī bṛ̱hanto̍rukra̱mā ta̱vasā̍ va̱rdhaya̍ntī |

ra̱re vā̱ṃ stoma̍ṃ vi̱dathe̍ṣu viṣṇo̱ pinva̍ta̱miṣo̍ vṛ̱jane̍ṣvindra

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 7

vaṣa̍ṭ te viṣṇavā̱sa ā kṛ̍ṇomi̱ tanme̍ juṣasva śipiviṣṭa ha̱vyam |

vardha̍ntu tvā suṣṭu̱tayo̱ giro̍ me yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
