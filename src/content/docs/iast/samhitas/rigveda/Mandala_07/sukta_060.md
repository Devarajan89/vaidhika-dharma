# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 60

**12 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| 1 sūrya: , 2-12 mitrāvaruṇau | triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 1

yada̱dya sū̍rya̱ bravo'nā̍gā u̱dyanmi̱trāya̱ varu̍ṇāya sa̱tyam |
va̱yaṃ de̍va̱trādi̍te syāma̱ tava̍ pri̱yāso̍ aryamangṛ̱ṇanta̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 2

e̱ṣa sya mi̍trāvaruṇā nṛ̱cakṣā̍ u̱bhe ude̍ti̱ sūryo̍ a̱bhi jman |

viśva̍sya sthā̱turjaga̍taśca go̱pā ṛ̱ju marte̍ṣu vṛji̱nā ca̱ paśya̍n

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 3

ayu̍kta sa̱pta ha̱rita̍: sa̱dhasthā̱dyā ī̱ṃ vaha̍nti̱ sūrya̍ṃ ghṛ̱tācī̍: |

dhāmā̍ni mitrāvaruṇā yu̱vāku̱: saṃ yo yū̱theva̱ jani̍māni̱ caṣṭe̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 4

udvā̍ṃ pṛ̱kṣāso̱ madhu̍manto asthu̱rā sūryo̍ aruhacchu̱kramarṇa̍: |

yasmā̍ ādi̱tyā adhva̍no̱ rada̍nti mi̱tro a̍rya̱mā varu̍ṇaḥ sa̱joṣā̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 5

i̱me ce̱tāro̱ anṛ̍tasya̱ bhūre̍rmi̱tro a̍rya̱mā varu̍ṇo̱ hi santi̍ |

i̱ma ṛ̱tasya̍ vāvṛdhurduro̱ṇe śa̱gmāsa̍: pu̱trā adi̍te̱rada̍bdhāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 6

i̱me mi̱tro varu̍ṇo dū̱l̤abhā̍so'ce̱tasa̍ṃ ciccitayanti̱ dakṣai̍: |

api̱ kratu̍ṃ su̱ceta̍sa̱ṃ vata̍ntasti̱raści̱daṃha̍: su̱pathā̍ nayanti

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 7

i̱me di̱vo ani̍miṣā pṛthi̱vyāści̍ki̱tvāṃso̍ ace̱tasa̍ṃ nayanti |

pra̱vrā̱je ci̍nna̱dyo̍ gā̱dhama̍sti pā̱raṃ no̍ a̱sya vi̍ṣpi̱tasya̍ parṣan

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 8

yadgo̱pāva̱dadi̍ti̱: śarma̍ bha̱draṃ mi̱tro yaccha̍nti̱ varu̍ṇaḥ su̱dāse̍ |

tasmi̱nnā to̱kaṃ tana̍ya̱ṃ dadhā̍nā̱ mā ka̍rma deva̱hel̤a̍naṃ turāsaḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 9

ava̱ vedi̱ṃ hotrā̍bhiryajeta̱ ripa̱: kāści̍dvaruṇa̱dhruta̱: saḥ |

pari̱ dveṣo̍bhirarya̱mā vṛ̍ṇaktū̱ruṃ su̱dāse̍ vṛṣaṇā u lo̱kam

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 10

sa̱svaści̱ddhi samṛ̍tistve̱ṣye̍ṣāmapī̱cye̍na̱ saha̍sā̱ saha̍nte |

yu̱ṣmadbhi̱yā vṛ̍ṣaṇo̱ reja̍mānā̱ dakṣa̍sya cinmahi̱nā mṛ̱l̤atā̍ naḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 11

yo brahma̍ṇe suma̱timā̱yajā̍te̱ vāja̍sya sā̱tau pa̍ra̱masya̍ rā̱yaḥ |

sīkṣa̍nta ma̱nyuṃ ma̱ghavā̍no a̱rya u̱ru kṣayā̍ya cakrire su̱dhātu̍

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 12

i̱yaṃ de̍va pu̱rohi̍tiryu̱vabhyā̍ṃ ya̱jñeṣu̍ mitrāvaruṇāvakāri |

viśvā̍ni du̱rgā pi̍pṛtaṃ ti̱ro no̍ yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
