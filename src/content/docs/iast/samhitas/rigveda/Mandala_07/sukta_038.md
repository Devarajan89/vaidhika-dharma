# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 38

**8 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| 1-6 savitā , 6 uttarārdhasya bhago vā , 7-8  vājina: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 1

udu̱ ṣya de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā ya̍yāma hira̱ṇyayī̍ma̱mati̱ṃ yāmaśi̍śret |
nū̱naṃ bhago̱ havyo̱ mānu̍ṣebhi̱rvi yo ratnā̍ purū̱vasu̱rdadhā̍ti

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 2

udu̍ tiṣṭha savitaḥ śru̱dhya1sya hira̍ṇyapāṇe̱ prabhṛ̍tāvṛ̱tasya̍ |

vyu1rvīṃ pṛ̱thvīma̱mati̍ṃ sṛjā̱na ā nṛbhyo̍ marta̱bhoja̍naṃ suvā̱naḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 3

api̍ ṣṭu̱taḥ sa̍vi̱tā de̱vo a̍stu̱ yamā ci̱dviśve̱ vasa̍vo gṛ̱ṇanti̍ |

sa na̱: stomā̍nnama̱sya1ścano̍ dhā̱dviśve̍bhiḥ pātu pā̱yubhi̱rni sū̱rīn

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 4

a̱bhi yaṃ de̱vyadi̍tirgṛ̱ṇāti̍ sa̱vaṃ de̱vasya̍ savi̱turju̍ṣā̱ṇā |

a̱bhi sa̱mrājo̱ varu̍ṇo gṛṇantya̱bhi mi̱trāso̍ arya̱mā sa̱joṣā̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 5

a̱bhi ye mi̱tho va̱nuṣa̱: sapa̍nte rā̱tiṃ di̱vo rā̍ti̱ṣāca̍: pṛthi̱vyāḥ |

ahi̍rbu̱dhnya̍ u̱ta na̍: śṛṇotu̱ varū̱tryeka̍dhenubhi̱rni pā̍tu

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 6

anu̱ tanno̱ jāspati̍rmaṃsīṣṭa̱ ratna̍ṃ de̱vasya̍ savi̱turi̍yā̱naḥ |

bhaga̍mu̱gro'va̍se̱ joha̍vīti̱ bhaga̱manu̍gro̱ adha̍ yāti̱ ratna̍m

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 7

śaṃ no̍ bhavantu vā̱jino̱ have̍ṣu de̱vatā̍tā mi̱tadra̍vaḥ sva̱rkāḥ |

ja̱mbhaya̱nto'hi̱ṃ vṛka̱ṃ rakṣā̍ṃsi̱ sane̍mya̱smadyu̍yava̱nnamī̍vāḥ

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 8
