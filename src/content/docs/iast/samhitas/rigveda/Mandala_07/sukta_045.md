# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 45

**4 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| savitā | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 1

ā de̱vo yā̍tu savi̱tā su̱ratno̍'ntarikṣa̱prā vaha̍māno̱ aśvai̍: |
haste̱ dadhā̍no̱ naryā̍ pu̱rūṇi̍ nive̱śaya̍ñca prasu̱vañca̱ bhūma̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 2

uda̍sya bā̱hū śi̍thi̱rā bṛ̱hantā̍ hira̱ṇyayā̍ di̱vo antā̍m̐ anaṣṭām |

nū̱naṃ so a̍sya mahi̱mā pa̍niṣṭa̱ sūra̍ścidasmā̱ anu̍ dādapa̱syām

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 3

sa ghā̍ no de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā sa̱hāvā ''sā̍viṣa̱dvasu̍pati̱rvasū̍ni |

vi̱śraya̍māṇo a̱mati̍murū̱cīṃ ma̍rta̱bhoja̍na̱madha̍ rāsate naḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 4

i̱mā gira̍: savi̱tāra̍ṃ suji̱hvaṃ pū̱rṇaga̍bhastimīl̤ate supā̱ṇim |

ci̱traṃ vayo̍ bṛ̱hada̱sme da̍dhātu yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
