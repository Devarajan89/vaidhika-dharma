# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 31

**12 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| indra: |gāyatrī, 10-12  virāṭ |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 1

pra va̱ indrā̍ya̱ māda̍na̱ṃ harya̍śvāya gāyata | sakhā̍yaḥ soma̱pāvne̍

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 2

śaṃsedu̱kthaṃ su̱dāna̍va u̱ta dyu̱kṣaṃ yathā̱ nara̍: | ca̱kṛ̱mā sa̱tyarā̍dhase

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 3

## Verse 4

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 5
