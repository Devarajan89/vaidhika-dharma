# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 10

**5 | maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| agni: | triṣṭup**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 1

u̱ṣo na jā̱raḥ pṛ̱thu pājo̍ aśre̱ddavi̍dyuta̱ddīdya̱cchośu̍cānaḥ |
vṛṣā̱ hari̱: śuci̱rā bhā̍ti bhā̱sā dhiyo̍ hinvā̱na u̍śa̱tīra̍jīgaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 2

sva1rṇa vasto̍ru̱ṣasā̍maroci ya̱jñaṃ ta̍nvā̱nā u̱śijo̱ na manma̍ |

a̱gnirjanmā̍ni de̱va ā vi vi̱dvāndra̱vaddū̱to de̍va̱yāvā̱ vani̍ṣṭhaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 3

acchā̱ giro̍ ma̱tayo̍ deva̱yantī̍ra̱gniṃ ya̍nti̱ dravi̍ṇa̱ṃ bhikṣa̍māṇāḥ |

su̱sa̱ṃdṛśa̍ṃ su̱pratī̍ka̱ṃ svañca̍ṃ havya̱vāha̍mara̱tiṃ mānu̍ṣāṇām

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 4

indra̍ṃ no agne̱ vasu̍bhiḥ sa̱joṣā̍ ru̱draṃ ru̱drebhi̱rā va̍hā bṛ̱hanta̍m |

ā̱di̱tyebhi̱radi̍tiṃ vi̱śvaja̍nyā̱ṃ bṛha̱spati̱mṛkva̍bhirvi̱śvavā̍ram

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 5

ma̱ndraṃ hotā̍ramu̱śijo̱ yavi̍ṣṭhama̱gniṃ viśa̍ īl̤ate adhva̱reṣu̍ |

sa hi kṣapā̍vā̱m̐ abha̍vadrayī̱ṇāmata̍ndro dū̱to ya̱jathā̍ya de̱vān
