# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 88

**7  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| varuṇa:, ( 7 pāśavimocanī) | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 1

pra śu̱ndhyuva̱ṃ varu̍ṇāya̱ preṣṭhā̍ṃ ma̱tiṃ va̍siṣṭha mī̱l̤huṣe̍ bharasva |
ya ī̍ma̱rvāñca̱ṃ kara̍te̱ yaja̍traṃ sa̱hasrā̍magha̱ṃ vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ bṛ̱hanta̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 2

adhā̱ nva̍sya sa̱ṃdṛśa̍ṃ jaga̱nvāna̱gneranī̍ka̱ṃ varu̍ṇasya maṃsi |

sva1ryadaśma̍nnadhi̱pā u̱ andho̱'bhi mā̱ vapu̍rdṛ̱śaye̍ ninīyāt

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 3

ā yadru̱hāva̱ varu̍ṇaśca̱ nāva̱ṃ pra yatsa̍mu̱dramī̱rayā̍va̱ madhya̍m |

adhi̱ yada̱pāṃ snubhi̱ścarā̍va̱ pra pre̱ṅkha ī̍ṅkhayāvahai śu̱bhe kam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 4

vasi̍ṣṭhaṃ ha̱ varu̍ṇo nā̱vyādhā̱dṛṣi̍ṃ cakāra̱ svapā̱ maho̍bhiḥ |

sto̱tāra̱ṃ vipra̍: sudina̱tve ahnā̱ṃ yānnu dyāva̍sta̱tana̱nyādu̱ṣāsa̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 5

kva1 tyāni̍ nau sa̱khyā ba̍bhūvu̱: sacā̍vahe̱ yada̍vṛ̱kaṃ pu̱rā ci̍t |

bṛ̱hanta̱ṃ māna̍ṃ varuṇa svadhāvaḥ sa̱hasra̍dvāraṃ jagamā gṛ̱haṃ te̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 6

ya ā̱pirnityo̍ varuṇa pri̱yaḥ santvāmāgā̍ṃsi kṛ̱ṇava̱tsakhā̍ te |

mā ta̱ ena̍svanto yakṣinbhujema ya̱ndhi ṣmā̱ vipra̍: stuva̱te varū̍tham

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 7

dhru̱vāsu̍ tvā̱su kṣi̱tiṣu̍ kṣi̱yanto̱ vya1smatpāśa̱ṃ varu̍ṇo mumocat |

avo̍ vanvā̱nā adi̍teru̱pasthā̍dyū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
