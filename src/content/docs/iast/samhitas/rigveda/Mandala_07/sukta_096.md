# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 96

**6  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| sarasvatī, 4-6 sarasvān  | 1-2= pragātha: (1 bṛhatī, 2 satobṛhatī ), 3 prastārapaṃkti:, 4-6 gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 1

bṛ̱hadu̍ gāyiṣe̱ vaco̍'su̱ryā̍ na̱dīnā̍m |
sara̍svatī̱minma̍hayā suvṛ̱ktibhi̱: stomai̍rvasiṣṭha̱ roda̍sī

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 2

u̱bhe yatte̍ mahi̱nā śu̍bhre̱ andha̍sī adhikṣi̱yanti̍ pū̱rava̍: |

sā no̍ bodhyavi̱trī ma̱rutsa̍khā̱ coda̱ rādho̍ ma̱ghonā̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 3

bha̱dramidbha̱drā kṛ̍ṇava̱tsara̍sva̱tyaka̍vārī cetati vā̱jinī̍vatī |

gṛ̱ṇā̱nā ja̍madagni̱vatstu̍vā̱nā ca̍ vasiṣṭha̱vat

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 4

ja̱nī̱yanto̱ nvagra̍vaḥ putrī̱yanta̍: su̱dāna̍vaḥ | sara̍svantaṃ havāmahe

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 5

ye te̍ sarasva ū̱rmayo̱ madhu̍manto ghṛta̱ścuta̍: | tebhi̍rno'vi̱tā bha̍va

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 6

pī̱pi̱vāṃsa̱ṃ sara̍svata̱: stana̱ṃ yo vi̱śvada̍rśataḥ | bha̱kṣī̱mahi̍ pra̱jāmiṣa̍m
