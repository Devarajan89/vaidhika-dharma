# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 46

**4 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:|rūdra:| jagatī, 4 triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 1

i̱mā ru̱drāya̍ sthi̱radha̍nvane̱ gira̍: kṣi̱preṣa̍ve de̱vāya̍ sva̱dhāvne̍ |
aṣā̍l̤hāya̱ saha̍mānāya ve̱dhase̍ ti̱gmāyu̍dhāya bharatā śṛ̱ṇotu̍ naḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 2

sa hi kṣaye̍ṇa̱ kṣamya̍sya̱ janma̍na̱: sāmrā̍jyena di̱vyasya̱ ceta̍ti |

ava̱nnava̍ntī̱rupa̍ no̱ dura̍ścarānamī̱vo ru̍dra̱ jāsu̍ no bhava

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 3

yā te̍ di̱dyudava̍sṛṣṭā di̱vaspari̍ kṣma̱yā cara̍ti̱ pari̱ sā vṛ̍ṇaktu naḥ |

sa̱hasra̍ṃ te svapivāta bheṣa̱jā mā na̍sto̱keṣu̱ tana̍yeṣu rīriṣaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 4

mā no̍ vadhī rudra̱ mā parā̍ dā̱ mā te̍ bhūma̱ prasi̍tau hīl̤i̱tasya̍ |

ā no̍ bhaja ba̱rhiṣi̍ jīvaśa̱ṃse yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
