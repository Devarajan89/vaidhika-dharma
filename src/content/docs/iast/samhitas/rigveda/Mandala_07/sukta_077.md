# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 77

**6  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| uṣasa: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 77 | **Verse:** 1

upo̍ ruruce yuva̱tirna yoṣā̱ viśva̍ṃ jī̱vaṃ pra̍su̱vantī̍ ca̱rāyai̍ |
abhū̍da̱gniḥ sa̱midhe̱ mānu̍ṣāṇā̱maka̱rjyoti̱rbādha̍mānā̱ tamā̍ṃsi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 77 | **Verse:** 2

viśva̍ṃ pratī̱cī sa̱prathā̱ uda̍sthā̱druśa̱dvāso̱ bibhra̍tī śu̱krama̍śvait |

hira̍ṇyavarṇā su̱dṛśī̍kasaṃdṛ̱ggavā̍ṃ mā̱tā ne̱tryahnā̍maroci

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 77 | **Verse:** 3

de̱vānā̱ṃ cakṣu̍: su̱bhagā̱ vaha̍ntī śve̱taṃ naya̍ntī su̱dṛśī̍ka̱maśva̍m |

u̱ṣā a̍darśi ra̱śmibhi̱rvya̍ktā ci̱trāma̍ghā̱ viśva̱manu̱ prabhū̍tā

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 77 | **Verse:** 4

anti̍vāmā dū̱re a̱mitra̍muccho̱rvīṃ gavyū̍ti̱mabha̍yaṃ kṛdhī naḥ |

yā̱vaya̱ dveṣa̱ ā bha̍rā̱ vasū̍ni co̱daya̱ rādho̍ gṛṇa̱te ma̍ghoni

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 77 | **Verse:** 5

a̱sme śreṣṭhe̍bhirbhā̱nubhi̱rvi bhā̱hyuṣo̍ devi prati̱rantī̍ na̱ āyu̍: |

iṣa̍ṃ ca no̱ dadha̍tī viśvavāre̱ goma̱daśvā̍va̱dratha̍vacca̱ rādha̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 77 | **Verse:** 6

yāṃ tvā̍ divo duhitarva̱rdhaya̱ntyuṣa̍: sujāte ma̱tibhi̱rvasi̍ṣṭhāḥ |

sāsmāsu̍ dhā ra̱yimṛ̱ṣvaṃ bṛ̱hanta̍ṃ yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
