# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 94

**12  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| indrāgnī|gāyatrī, 12  anuṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 1

i̱yaṃ vā̍ma̱sya manma̍na̱ indrā̍gnī pū̱rvyastu̍tiḥ | a̱bhrādvṛ̱ṣṭiri̍vājani

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 2

śṛ̱ṇu̱taṃ ja̍ri̱turhava̱mindrā̍gnī̱ vana̍ta̱ṃ gira̍: | ī̱śā̱nā pi̍pyata̱ṃ dhiya̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 3

mā pā̍pa̱tvāya̍ no na̱rendrā̍gnī̱ mābhiśa̍staye | mā no̍ rīradhataṃ ni̱de

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 4

indre̍ a̱gnā namo̍ bṛ̱hatsu̍vṛ̱ktimera̍yāmahe | dhi̱yā dhenā̍ ava̱syava̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 5

tā hi śaśva̍nta̱ īl̤a̍ta i̱tthā viprā̍sa ū̱taye̍ | sa̱bādho̱ vāja̍sātaye

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 6

tā vā̍ṃ gī̱rbhirvi̍pa̱nyava̱: praya̍svanto havāmahe | me̱dhasā̍tā sani̱ṣyava̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 7

indrā̍gnī̱ ava̱sā ga̍tama̱smabhya̍ṃ carṣaṇīsahā | mā no̍ du̱:śaṃsa̍ īśata

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 8

mā kasya̍ no̱ ara̍ruṣo dhū̱rtiḥ praṇa̱ṅmartya̍sya | indrā̍gnī̱ śarma̍ yacchatam

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 9

goma̱ddhira̍ṇyava̱dvasu̱ yadvā̱maśvā̍va̱dīma̍he | indrā̍gnī̱ tadva̍nemahi

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 10

yatsoma̱ ā su̱te nara̍ indrā̱gnī ajo̍havuḥ | saptī̍vantā sapa̱ryava̍:

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 94 | **Verse:** 11

u̱kthebhi̍rvṛtra̱hanta̍mā̱ yā ma̍ndā̱nā ci̱dā gi̱rā | ā̱ṅgū̱ṣairā̱vivā̍sataḥ
