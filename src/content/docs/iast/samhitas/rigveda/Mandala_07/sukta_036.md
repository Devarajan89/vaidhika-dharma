# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 36

**9 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| viśve devā: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 1

pra brahmai̍tu̱ sada̍nādṛ̱tasya̱ vi ra̱śmibhi̍: sasṛje̱ sūryo̱ gāḥ |
vi sānu̍nā pṛthi̱vī sa̍sra u̱rvī pṛ̱thu pratī̍ka̱madhyedhe̍ a̱gniḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 2

i̱māṃ vā̍ṃ mitrāvaruṇā suvṛ̱ktimiṣa̱ṃ na kṛ̍ṇve asurā̱ navī̍yaḥ |

i̱no vā̍ma̱nyaḥ pa̍da̱vīrada̍bdho̱ jana̍ṃ ca mi̱tro ya̍tati bruvā̱ṇaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 3

ā vāta̍sya̱ dhraja̍to ranta i̱tyā apī̍payanta dhe̱navo̱ na sūdā̍: |

ma̱ho di̱vaḥ sada̍ne̱ jāya̍mā̱no'ci̍kradadvṛṣa̱bhaḥ sasmi̱nnūdha̍n

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 4

gi̱rā ya e̱tā yu̱naja̱ddharī̍ ta̱ indra̍ pri̱yā su̱rathā̍ śūra dhā̱yū |

pra yo ma̱nyuṃ riri̍kṣato mi̱nātyā su̱kratu̍marya̱maṇa̍ṃ vavṛtyām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 5

yaja̍nte asya sa̱khyaṃ vaya̍śca nama̱svina̱: sva ṛ̱tasya̱ dhāma̍n |

vi pṛkṣo̍ bābadhe̱ nṛbhi̱: stavā̍na i̱daṃ namo̍ ru̱drāya̱ preṣṭha̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 6

ā yatsā̱kaṃ ya̱śaso̍ vāvaśā̱nāḥ sara̍svatī sa̱ptathī̱ sindhu̍mātā |

yāḥ su̱ṣvaya̍nta su̱dughā̍: sudhā̱rā a̱bhi svena̱ paya̍sā̱ pīpyā̍nāḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 7

u̱ta tye no̍ ma̱ruto̍ mandasā̱nā dhiya̍ṃ to̱kaṃ ca̍ vā̱jino̍'vantu |

mā na̱: pari̍ khya̱dakṣa̍rā̱ cara̱ntyavī̍vṛdha̱nyujya̱ṃ te ra̱yiṃ na̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 8

pra vo̍ ma̱hīma̱rama̍tiṃ kṛṇudhva̱ṃ pra pū̱ṣaṇa̍ṃ vida̱thyaṃ1 na vī̱ram |

bhaga̍ṃ dhi̱yo̍'vi̱tāra̍ṃ no a̱syāḥ sā̱tau vāja̍ṃ rāti̱ṣāca̱ṃ pura̍ṃdhim

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 9

acchā̱yaṃ vo̍ maruta̱: śloka̍ e̱tvacchā̱ viṣṇu̍ṃ niṣikta̱pāmavo̍bhiḥ |

u̱ta pra̱jāyai̍ gṛṇa̱te vayo̍ dhuryū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
