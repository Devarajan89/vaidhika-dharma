# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 7

**7  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| agni: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 1

pra vo̍ de̱vaṃ ci̍tsahasā̱nama̱gnimaśva̱ṃ na vā̱jina̍ṃ hiṣe̱ namo̍bhiḥ |
bhavā̍ no dū̱to a̍dhva̱rasya̍ vi̱dvāntmanā̍ de̱veṣu̍ vivide mi̱tadru̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 2

ā yā̍hyagne pa̱thyā̱3 anu̱ svā ma̱ndro de̱vānā̍ṃ sa̱khyaṃ ju̍ṣā̱ṇaḥ |

ā sānu̱ śuṣmai̍rna̱daya̍npṛthi̱vyā jambhe̍bhi̱rviśva̍mu̱śadha̱gvanā̍ni

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 3

prā̱cīno̍ ya̱jñaḥ sudhi̍ta̱ṃ hi ba̱rhiḥ prī̍ṇī̱te a̱gnirī̍l̤i̱to na hotā̍ |

ā mā̱tarā̍ vi̱śvavā̍re huvā̱no yato̍ yaviṣṭha jajñi̱ṣe su̱śeva̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 4

sa̱dyo a̍dhva̱re ra̍thi̱raṃ ja̍nanta̱ mānu̍ṣāso̱ vice̍taso̱ ya e̍ṣām |

vi̱śāma̍dhāyi vi̱śpati̍rduro̱ṇe̱3'gnirma̱ndro madhu̍vacā ṛ̱tāvā̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 5

asā̍di vṛ̱to vahni̍rājaga̱nvāna̱gnirbra̱hmā nṛ̱ṣada̍ne vidha̱rtā |

dyauśca̱ yaṃ pṛ̍thi̱vī vā̍vṛ̱dhāte̱ ā yaṃ hotā̱ yaja̍ti vi̱śvavā̍ram

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 6

e̱te dyu̱mnebhi̱rviśva̱māti̍ranta̱ mantra̱ṃ ye vāra̱ṃ naryā̱ ata̍kṣan |

pra ye viśa̍sti̱ranta̱ śroṣa̍māṇā̱ ā ye me̍ a̱sya dīdha̍yannṛ̱tasya̍

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 7
