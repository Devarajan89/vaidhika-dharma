# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 59

**12 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| 1-11 maruta:, 12 rūdra: ( mṛtyuvimocinī ṛk ) | pragātha: = ( viṣamā bṛhatī, samā satobṛhatī ), 7-8, 9-11 gāyatrī, 12 anuṣṭup  |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 1

yaṃ trāya̍dhva i̱dami̍da̱ṃ devā̍so̱ yaṃ ca̱ naya̍tha| tasmā̍ agne̱ varu̍ṇa̱ mitrārya̍ma̱nmaru̍ta̱: śarma̍ yacchata

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 2

## Verse 4
