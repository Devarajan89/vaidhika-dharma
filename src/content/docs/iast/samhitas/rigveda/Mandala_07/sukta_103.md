# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 103

**10 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:|maṇḍūkā: { parjanya: } | triṣṭup , 1 anuṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 1

sa̱ṃva̱tsa̱raṃ śa̍śayā̱nā brā̍hma̱ṇā vra̍tacā̱riṇa̍: |
vāca̍ṃ pa̱rjanya̍jinvitā̱ṃ pra ma̱ṇḍūkā̍ avādiṣuḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 2

di̱vyā āpo̍ a̱bhi yade̍na̱māya̱ndṛti̱ṃ na śuṣka̍ṃ sara̱sī śayā̍nam |

gavā̱maha̱ na mā̱yurva̱tsinī̍nāṃ ma̱ṇḍūkā̍nāṃ va̱gnuratrā̱ same̍ti

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 3

yadī̍menām̐ uśa̱to a̱bhyava̍rṣīttṛ̱ṣyāva̍taḥ prā̱vṛṣyāga̍tāyām |

a̱khkha̱lī̱kṛtyā̍ pi̱tara̱ṃ na pu̱tro a̱nyo a̱nyamupa̱ vada̍ntameti

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 4

a̱nyo a̱nyamanu̍ gṛbhṇātyenora̱pāṃ pra̍sa̱rge yadama̍ndiṣātām |

ma̱ṇḍūko̱ yada̱bhivṛ̍ṣṭa̱: kani̍ṣka̱npṛśni̍: sampṛ̱ṅkte hari̍tena̱ vāca̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 5

yade̍ṣāma̱nyo a̱nyasya̱ vāca̍ṃ śā̱ktasye̍va̱ vada̍ti̱ śikṣa̍māṇaḥ |

sarva̱ṃ tade̍ṣāṃ sa̱mṛdhe̍va̱ parva̱ yatsu̱vāco̱ vada̍tha̱nādhya̱psu

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 6

gomā̍yu̱reko̍ a̱jamā̍yu̱reka̱: pṛśni̱reko̱ hari̍ta̱ eka̍ eṣām |

sa̱mā̱naṃ nāma̱ bibhra̍to̱ virū̍pāḥ puru̱trā vāca̍ṃ pipiśu̱rvada̍ntaḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 7

brā̱hma̱ṇāso̍ atirā̱tre na some̱ saro̱ na pū̱rṇama̱bhito̱ vada̍ntaḥ |

sa̱ṃva̱tsa̱rasya̱ tadaha̱: pari̍ ṣṭha̱ yanma̍ṇḍūkāḥ prāvṛ̱ṣīṇa̍ṃ ba̱bhūva̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 8

brā̱hma̱ṇāsa̍: so̱mino̱ vāca̍makrata̱ brahma̍ kṛ̱ṇvanta̍: parivatsa̱rīṇa̍m |

a̱dhva̱ryavo̍ gha̱rmiṇa̍: siṣvidā̱nā ā̱virbha̍vanti̱ guhyā̱ na ke ci̍t

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 9

de̱vahi̍tiṃ jugupurdvāda̱śasya̍ ṛ̱tuṃ naro̱ na pra mi̍nantye̱te |

sa̱ṃva̱tsa̱re prā̱vṛṣyāga̍tāyāṃ ta̱ptā gha̱rmā a̍śnuvate visa̱rgam

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 10

gomā̍yuradāda̱jamā̍yuradā̱tpṛśni̍radā̱ddhari̍to no̱ vasū̍ni |

gavā̍ṃ ma̱ṇḍūkā̱ dada̍taḥ śa̱tāni̍ sahasrasā̱ve pra ti̍ranta̱ āyu̍:
