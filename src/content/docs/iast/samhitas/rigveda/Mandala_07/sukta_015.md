# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 15

**15 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| agni: |gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 1

u̱pa̱sadyā̍ya mī̱l̤huṣa̍ ā̱sye̍ juhutā ha̱viḥ | yo no̱ nedi̍ṣṭha̱māpya̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 2

yaḥ pañca̍ carṣa̱ṇīra̱bhi ni̍ṣa̱sāda̱ dame̍dame | ka̱virgṛ̱hapa̍ti̱ryuvā̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 3

sa no̱ vedo̍ a̱mātya̍ma̱gnī ra̍kṣatu vi̱śvata̍: | u̱tāsmānpā̱tvaṃha̍saḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 4

nava̱ṃ nu stoma̍ma̱gnaye̍ di̱vaḥ śye̱nāya̍ jījanam | vasva̍: ku̱vidva̱nāti̍ naḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 5

spā̱rhā yasya̱ śriyo̍ dṛ̱śe ra̱yirvī̱rava̍to yathā | agre̍ ya̱jñasya̱ śoca̍taḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 6

semāṃ ve̍tu̱ vaṣa̍ṭkṛtima̱gnirju̍ṣata no̱ gira̍: | yaji̍ṣṭho havya̱vāha̍naḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 7

ni tvā̍ nakṣya viśpate dyu̱manta̍ṃ deva dhīmahi | su̱vīra̍magna āhuta

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 8

kṣapa̍ u̱sraśca̍ dīdihi sva̱gnaya̱stvayā̍ va̱yam | su̱vīra̱stvama̍sma̱yuḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 9

upa̍ tvā sā̱taye̱ naro̱ viprā̍so yanti dhī̱tibhi̍: | upākṣa̍rā saha̱sriṇī̍

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 10

a̱gnī rakṣā̍ṃsi sedhati śu̱kraśo̍ci̱rama̍rtyaḥ | śuci̍: pāva̱ka īḍya̍:

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 11

sa no̱ rādhā̱ṃsyā bha̱reśā̍naḥ sahaso yaho | bhaga̍śca dātu̱ vārya̍m

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 12

tvama̍gne vī̱rava̱dyaśo̍ de̱vaśca̍ savi̱tā bhaga̍: | diti̍śca dāti̱ vārya̍m

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 13

agne̱ rakṣā̍ ṇo̱ aṃha̍sa̱: prati̍ ṣma deva̱ rīṣa̍taḥ | tapi̍ṣṭhaira̱jaro̍ daha

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 14

adhā̍ ma̱hī na̱ āya̱syanā̍dhṛṣṭo̱ nṛpī̍taye | pūrbha̍vā śa̱tabhu̍jiḥ

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 15

tvaṃ na̍: pā̱hyaṃha̍so̱ doṣā̍vastaraghāya̱taḥ | divā̱ nakta̍madābhya
