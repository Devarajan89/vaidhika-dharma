# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 16

**12 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| agni: | pragātha: (=viṣamā bṛhatī , samā satobṛhatī )  |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 1

e̱nā vo̍ a̱gniṃ nama̍so̱rjo napā̍ta̱mā hu̍ve | pri̱yaṃ ceti̍ṣṭhamara̱tiṃ sva̍dhva̱raṃ viśva̍sya dū̱tama̱mṛta̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 2

sa yo̍jate aru̱ṣā vi̱śvabho̍jasā̱ sa du̍drava̱tsvā̍hutaḥ |

su̱brahmā̍ ya̱jñaḥ su̱śamī̱ vasū̍nāṃ de̱vaṃ rādho̱ janā̍nām

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 3

uda̍sya śo̱cira̍sthādā̱juhvā̍nasya mī̱l̤huṣa̍: |

uddhū̱māso̍ aru̱ṣāso̍ divi̱spṛśa̱: sama̱gnimi̍ndhate̱ nara̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 4

taṃ tvā̍ dū̱taṃ kṛ̍ṇmahe ya̱śasta̍maṃ de̱vām̐ ā vī̱taye̍ vaha |

viśvā̍ sūno sahaso marta̱bhoja̍nā̱ rāsva̱ tadyattvema̍he

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 5

tvama̍gne gṛ̱hapa̍ti̱stvaṃ hotā̍ no adhva̱re | tvaṃ potā̍ viśvavāra̱ prace̍tā̱ yakṣi̱ veṣi̍ ca̱ vārya̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 6

kṛ̱dhi ratna̱ṃ yaja̍mānāya sukrato̱ tvaṃ hi ra̍tna̱dhā asi̍ |

ā na̍ ṛ̱te śi̍śīhi̱ viśva̍mṛ̱tvija̍ṃ su̱śaṃso̱ yaśca̱ dakṣa̍te

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 7

tve a̍gne svāhuta pri̱yāsa̍: santu sū̱raya̍: | ya̱ntāro̱ ye ma̱ghavā̍no̱ janā̍nāmū̱rvāndaya̍nta̱ gonā̍m

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 8

yeṣā̱mil̤ā̍ ghṛ̱taha̍stā duro̱ṇa ām̐ api̍ prā̱tā ni̱ṣīda̍ti |

tām̐strā̍yasva sahasya dru̱ho ni̱do yacchā̍ na̱: śarma̍ dīrgha̱śrut

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 9

sa ma̱ndrayā̍ ca ji̱hvayā̱ vahni̍rā̱sā vi̱duṣṭa̍raḥ | agne̍ ra̱yiṃ ma̱ghava̍dbhyo na̱ ā va̍ha ha̱vyadā̍tiṃ ca sūdaya

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 10

ye rādhā̍ṃsi̱ dada̱tyaśvyā̍ ma̱ghā kāme̍na̱ śrava̍so ma̱haḥ | tām̐ aṃha̍saḥ pipṛhi pa̱rtṛbhi̱ṣṭvaṃ śa̱taṃ pū̱rbhirya̍viṣṭhya

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 11

de̱vo vo̍ draviṇo̱dāḥ pū̱rṇāṃ vi̍vaṣṭyā̱sica̍m | udvā̍ si̱ñcadhva̱mupa̍ vā pṛṇadhva̱mādidvo̍ de̱va o̍hate

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 12

taṃ hotā̍ramadhva̱rasya̱ prace̍tasa̱ṃ vahni̍ṃ de̱vā a̍kṛṇvata | dadhā̍ti̱ ratna̍ṃ vidha̱te su̱vīrya̍ma̱gnirjanā̍ya dā̱śuṣe̍
