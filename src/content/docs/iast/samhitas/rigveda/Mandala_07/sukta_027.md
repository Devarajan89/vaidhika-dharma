# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 27

**5  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| indra: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 1

indra̱ṃ naro̍ ne̱madhi̍tā havante̱ yatpāryā̍ yu̱naja̍te̱ dhiya̱stāḥ |
śūro̱ nṛṣā̍tā̱ śava̍saścakā̱na ā goma̍ti vra̱je bha̍jā̱ tvaṃ na̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 2

ya i̍ndra̱ śuṣmo̍ maghavante̱ asti̱ śikṣā̱ sakhi̍bhyaḥ puruhūta̱ nṛbhya̍: |

tvaṃ hi dṛ̱l̤hā ma̍ghava̱nvice̍tā̱ apā̍ vṛdhi̱ pari̍vṛta̱ṃ na rādha̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 3

indro̱ rājā̱ jaga̍taścarṣaṇī̱nāmadhi̱ kṣami̱ viṣu̍rūpa̱ṃ yadasti̍ |

tato̍ dadāti dā̱śuṣe̱ vasū̍ni̱ coda̱drādha̱ upa̍stutaścida̱rvāk

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 4

nū ci̍nna̱ indro̍ ma̱ghavā̱ sahū̍tī dā̱no vāja̱ṃ ni ya̍mate na ū̱tī |

anū̍nā̱ yasya̱ dakṣi̍ṇā pī̱pāya̍ vā̱maṃ nṛbhyo̍ a̱bhivī̍tā̱ sakhi̍bhyaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 5

nū i̍ndra rā̱ye vari̍vaskṛdhī na̱ ā te̱ mano̍ vavṛtyāma ma̱ghāya̍ |

goma̱daśvā̍va̱dratha̍va̱dvyanto̍ yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
