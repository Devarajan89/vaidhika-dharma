# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 90

**7  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| vāyu:, 5 -7 indravāyū| triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 90 | **Verse:** 1

pra vī̍ra̱yā śuca̍yo dadrire vāmadhva̱ryubhi̱rmadhu̍mantaḥ su̱tāsa̍: |
vaha̍ vāyo ni̱yuto̍ yā̱hyacchā̱ pibā̍ su̱tasyāndha̍so̱ madā̍ya

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 90 | **Verse:** 2

ī̱śā̱nāya̱ prahu̍ti̱ṃ yasta̱ āna̱ṭ śuci̱ṃ soma̍ṃ śucipā̱stubhya̍ṃ vāyo |

kṛ̱ṇoṣi̱ taṃ martye̍ṣu praśa̱staṃ jā̱tojā̍to jāyate vā̱jya̍sya

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 90 | **Verse:** 3

rā̱ye nu yaṃ ja̱jñatū̱ roda̍sī̱me rā̱ye de̱vī dhi̱ṣaṇā̍ dhāti de̱vam |

adha̍ vā̱yuṃ ni̱yuta̍: saścata̱ svā u̱ta śve̱taṃ vasu̍dhitiṃ nire̱ke

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 90 | **Verse:** 4

u̱cchannu̱ṣasa̍: su̱dinā̍ ari̱prā u̱ru jyoti̍rvividu̱rdīdhyā̍nāḥ |

gavya̍ṃ cidū̱rvamu̱śijo̱ vi va̍vru̱steṣā̱manu̍ pra̱diva̍: sasru̱rāpa̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 90 | **Verse:** 5

te sa̱tyena̱ mana̍sā̱ dīdhyā̍nā̱: svena̍ yu̱ktāsa̱: kratu̍nā vahanti |

indra̍vāyū vīra̱vāha̱ṃ ratha̍ṃ vāmīśā̱nayo̍ra̱bhi pṛkṣa̍: sacante

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 90 | **Verse:** 6

ī̱śā̱nāso̱ ye dadha̍te̱ sva̍rṇo̱ gobhi̱raśve̍bhi̱rvasu̍bhi̱rhira̍ṇyaiḥ |

indra̍vāyū sū̱rayo̱ viśva̱māyu̱rarva̍dbhirvī̱raiḥ pṛta̍nāsu sahyuḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 90 | **Verse:** 7

arva̍nto̱ na śrava̍so̱ bhikṣa̍māṇā indravā̱yū su̍ṣṭu̱tibhi̱rvasi̍ṣṭhāḥ |

vā̱ja̱yanta̱: svava̍se huvema yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
