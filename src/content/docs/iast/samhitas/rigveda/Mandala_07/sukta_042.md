# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 42

**6 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| viśve devā: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 1

pra bra̱hmāṇo̱ aṅgi̍raso nakṣanta̱ pra kra̍nda̱nurna̍bha̱nya̍sya vetu |
pra dhe̱nava̍ uda̱pruto̍ navanta yu̱jyātā̱madrī̍ adhva̱rasya̱ peśa̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 2

su̱gaste̍ agne̱ sana̍vitto̱ adhvā̍ yu̱kṣvā su̱te ha̱rito̍ ro̱hita̍śca |

ye vā̱ sadma̍nnaru̱ṣā vī̍ra̱vāho̍ hu̱ve de̱vānā̱ṃ jani̍māni sa̱ttaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 3

samu̍ vo ya̱jñaṃ ma̍haya̱nnamo̍bhi̱: pra hotā̍ ma̱ndro ri̍rica upā̱ke |

yaja̍sva̱ su pu̍rvaṇīka de̱vānā ya̱jñiyā̍ma̱rama̍tiṃ vavṛtyāḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 4

ya̱dā vī̱rasya̍ re̱vato̍ duro̱ṇe syo̍na̱śīrati̍thirā̱cike̍tat |

suprī̍to a̱gniḥ sudhi̍to̱ dama̱ ā sa vi̱śe dā̍ti̱ vārya̱miya̍tyai

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 5

i̱maṃ no̍ agne adhva̱raṃ ju̍ṣasva ma̱rutsvindre̍ ya̱śasa̍ṃ kṛdhī naḥ |

ā naktā̍ ba̱rhiḥ sa̍datāmu̱ṣāso̱śantā̍ mi̱trāvaru̍ṇā yaje̱ha

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 6

e̱vāgniṃ sa̍ha̱syaṃ1 vasi̍ṣṭho rā̱yaskā̍mo vi̱śvapsnya̍sya staut |

iṣa̍ṃ ra̱yiṃ pa̍pratha̱dvāja̍ma̱sme yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
