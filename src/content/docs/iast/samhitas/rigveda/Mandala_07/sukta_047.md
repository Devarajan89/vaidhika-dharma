# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 47

**4 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:|āpa:| triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 1

āpo̱ yaṃ va̍: pratha̱maṃ de̍va̱yanta̍ indra̱pāna̍mū̱rmimakṛ̍ṇvate̱l̤aḥ |
taṃ vo̍ va̱yaṃ śuci̍mari̱prama̱dya ghṛ̍ta̱pruṣa̱ṃ madhu̍mantaṃ vanema

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 2

tamū̱rmimā̍po̱ madhu̍mattamaṃ vo̱'pāṃ napā̍davatvāśu̱hemā̍ |

yasmi̱nnindro̱ vasu̍bhirmā̱dayā̍te̱ tama̍śyāma deva̱yanto̍ vo a̱dya

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 3

śa̱tapa̍vitrāḥ sva̱dhayā̱ mada̍ntīrde̱vīrde̱vānā̱mapi̍ yanti̱ pātha̍: |

tā indra̍sya̱ na mi̍nanti vra̱tāni̱ sindhu̍bhyo ha̱vyaṃ ghṛ̱tava̍jjuhota

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 4

yāḥ sūryo̍ ra̱śmibhi̍rāta̱tāna̱ yābhya̱ indro̱ ara̍dadgā̱tumū̱rmim |

te si̍ndhavo̱ vari̍vo dhātanā no yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
