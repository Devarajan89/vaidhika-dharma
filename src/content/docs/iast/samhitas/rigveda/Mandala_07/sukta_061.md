# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 61

**7  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| mitrāvaruṇau | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 1

udvā̱ṃ cakṣu̍rvaruṇa su̱pratī̍kaṃ de̱vayo̍reti̱ sūrya̍stata̱nvān |
a̱bhi yo viśvā̱ bhuva̍nāni̱ caṣṭe̱ sa ma̱nyuṃ martye̱ṣvā ci̍keta

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 2

pra vā̱ṃ sa mi̍trāvaruṇāvṛ̱tāvā̱ vipro̱ manmā̍ni dīrgha̱śrudi̍yarti |

yasya̱ brahmā̍ṇi sukratū̱ avā̍tha̱ ā yatkratvā̱ na śa̱rada̍: pṛ̱ṇaithe̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 3

prorormi̍trāvaruṇā pṛthi̱vyāḥ pra di̱va ṛ̱ṣvādbṛ̍ha̱taḥ su̍dānū |

spaśo̍ dadhāthe̱ oṣa̍dhīṣu vi̱kṣvṛdha̍gya̱to ani̍miṣa̱ṃ rakṣa̍māṇā

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 4

śaṃsā̍ mi̱trasya̱ varu̍ṇasya̱ dhāma̱ śuṣmo̱ roda̍sī badbadhe mahi̱tvā |

aya̱nmāsā̱ aya̍jvanāma̱vīrā̱: pra ya̱jñama̍nmā vṛ̱jana̍ṃ tirāte

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 5

amū̍rā̱ viśvā̍ vṛṣaṇāvi̱mā vā̱ṃ na yāsu̍ ci̱traṃ dadṛ̍śe̱ na ya̱kṣam |

druha̍: sacante̱ anṛ̍tā̱ janā̍nā̱ṃ na vā̍ṃ ni̱ṇyānya̱cite̍ abhūvan

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 6

samu̍ vāṃ ya̱jñaṃ ma̍haya̱ṃ namo̍bhirhu̱ve vā̍ṃ mitrāvaruṇā sa̱bādha̍: |

pra vā̱ṃ manmā̍nyṛ̱case̱ navā̍ni kṛ̱tāni̱ brahma̍ jujuṣanni̱māni̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 7

i̱yaṃ de̍va pu̱rohi̍tiryu̱vabhyā̍ṃ ya̱jñeṣu̍ mitrāvaruṇāvakāri |

viśvā̍ni du̱rgā pi̍pṛtaṃ ti̱ro no̍ yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
