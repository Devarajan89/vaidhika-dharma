# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 19

**11 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| indra: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 1

yasti̱gmaśṛ̍ṅgo vṛṣa̱bho na bhī̱ma eka̍: kṛ̱ṣṭīścyā̱vaya̍ti̱ pra viśvā̍: |
yaḥ śaśva̍to̱ adā̍śuṣo̱ gaya̍sya praya̱ntāsi̱ suṣvi̍tarāya̱ veda̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 2

tvaṃ ha̱ tyadi̍ndra̱ kutsa̍māva̱: śuśrū̍ṣamāṇasta̱nvā̍ sama̱rye |

dāsa̱ṃ yacchuṣṇa̱ṃ kuya̍va̱ṃ nya̍smā̱ ara̍ndhaya ārjune̱yāya̱ śikṣa̍n

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 3

tvaṃ dhṛ̍ṣṇo dhṛṣa̱tā vī̱taha̍vya̱ṃ prāvo̱ viśvā̍bhirū̱tibhi̍: su̱dāsa̍m |

pra pauru̍kutsiṃ tra̱sada̍syumāva̱: kṣetra̍sātā vṛtra̱hatye̍ṣu pū̱rum

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 4

tvaṃ nṛbhi̍rnṛmaṇo de̱vavī̍tau̱ bhūrī̍ṇi vṛ̱trā ha̍ryaśva haṃsi |

tvaṃ ni dasyu̱ṃ cumu̍ri̱ṃ dhuni̱ṃ cā'svā̍payo da̱bhīta̍ye su̱hantu̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 5

tava̍ cyau̱tnāni̍ vajrahasta̱ tāni̱ nava̱ yatpuro̍ nava̱tiṃ ca̍ sa̱dyaḥ |

ni̱veśa̍ne śatata̱māvi̍veṣī̱raha̍ñca vṛ̱traṃ namu̍cimu̱tāha̍n

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 6

sanā̱ tā ta̍ indra̱ bhoja̍nāni rā̱taha̍vyāya dā̱śuṣe̍ su̱dāse̍ |

vṛṣṇe̍ te̱ harī̱ vṛṣa̍ṇā yunajmi̱ vyantu̱ brahmā̍ṇi puruśāka̱ vāja̍m

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 7

mā te̍ a̱syāṃ sa̍hasāva̱npari̍ṣṭāva̱ghāya̍ bhūma harivaḥ parā̱dai |

trāya̍sva no'vṛ̱kebhi̱rvarū̍thai̱stava̍ pri̱yāsa̍: sū̱riṣu̍ syāma

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 8

pri̱yāsa̱ itte̍ maghavanna̱bhiṣṭau̱ naro̍ madema śara̱ṇe sakhā̍yaḥ |

ni tu̱rvaśa̱ṃ ni yādva̍ṃ śiśīhyatithi̱gvāya̱ śaṃsya̍ṃ kari̱ṣyan

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 9

sa̱dyaści̱nnu te ma̍ghavanna̱bhiṣṭau̱ nara̍: śaṃsantyuktha̱śāsa̍ u̱kthā |

ye te̱ have̍bhi̱rvi pa̱ṇīm̐radā̍śanna̱smānvṛ̍ṇīṣva̱ yujyā̍ya̱ tasmai̍

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 10

e̱te stomā̍ na̱rāṃ nṛ̍tama̱ tubhya̍masma̱drya̍ñco̱ dada̍to ma̱ghāni̍ |

teṣā̍mindra vṛtra̱hatye̍ śi̱vo bhū̱: sakhā̍ ca̱ śūro̍'vi̱tā ca̍ nṛ̱ṇām

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 11

nū i̍ndra śūra̱ stava̍māna ū̱tī brahma̍jūtasta̱nvā̍ vāvṛdhasva |

upa̍ no̱ vājā̍nmimī̱hyupa̱ stīnyū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
