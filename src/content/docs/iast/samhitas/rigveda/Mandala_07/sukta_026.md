# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 26

**5 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| indra: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 1

na soma̱ indra̱masu̍to mamāda̱ nābra̍hmāṇo ma̱ghavā̍naṃ su̱tāsa̍: |
tasmā̍ u̱kthaṃ ja̍naye̱ yajjujo̍ṣannṛ̱vannavī̍yaḥ śṛ̱ṇava̱dyathā̍ naḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 2

u̱kthau̍kthe̱ soma̱ indra̍ṃ mamāda nī̱thenī̍the ma̱ghavā̍naṃ su̱tāsa̍: |

yadī̍ṃ sa̱bādha̍: pi̱tara̱ṃ na pu̱trāḥ sa̍mā̱nada̍kṣā̱ ava̍se̱ hava̍nte

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 3

ca̱kāra̱ tā kṛ̱ṇava̍nnū̱nama̱nyā yāni̍ bru̱vanti̍ ve̱dhasa̍: su̱teṣu̍ |

janī̍riva̱ pati̱reka̍: samā̱no ni mā̍mṛje̱ pura̱ indra̱: su sarvā̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 4

e̱vā tamā̍huru̱ta śṛ̍ṇva̱ indra̱ eko̍ vibha̱ktā ta̱raṇi̍rma̱ghānā̍m |

mi̱tha̱stura̍ ū̱tayo̱ yasya̍ pū̱rvīra̱sme bha̱drāṇi̍ saścata pri̱yāṇi̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 5

e̱vā vasi̍ṣṭha̱ indra̍mū̱taye̱ nṝnkṛ̍ṣṭī̱nāṃ vṛ̍ṣa̱bhaṃ su̱te gṛ̍ṇāti |

sa̱ha̱sriṇa̱ upa̍ no māhi̱ vājā̍nyū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
