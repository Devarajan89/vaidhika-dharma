# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 14

**3  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| agni: | triṣṭup , 1 bṛhatī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 1

sa̱midhā̍ jā̱tave̍dase de̱vāya̍ de̱vahū̍tibhiḥ |
ha̱virbhi̍: śu̱kraśo̍ciṣe nama̱svino̍ va̱yaṃ dā̍śemā̱gnaye̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 2

va̱yaṃ te̍ agne sa̱midhā̍ vidhema va̱yaṃ dā̍śema suṣṭu̱tī ya̍jatra |

va̱yaṃ ghṛ̱tenā̍dhvarasya hotarva̱yaṃ de̍va ha̱viṣā̍ bhadraśoce

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 3

ā no̍ de̱vebhi̱rupa̍ de̱vahū̍ti̱magne̍ yā̱hi vaṣa̍ṭkṛtiṃ juṣā̱ṇaḥ |

tubhya̍ṃ de̱vāya̱ dāśa̍taḥ syāma yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
