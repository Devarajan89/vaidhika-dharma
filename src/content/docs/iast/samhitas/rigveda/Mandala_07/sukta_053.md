# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 53

**3 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| dyāvāpṛthivī|triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 1

pra dyāvā̍ ya̱jñaiḥ pṛ̍thi̱vī namo̍bhiḥ sa̱bādha̍ īl̤e bṛha̱tī yaja̍tre |
te ci̱ddhi pūrve̍ ka̱vayo̍ gṛ̱ṇanta̍: pu̱ro ma̱hī da̍dhi̱re de̱vapu̍tre

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 2

pra pū̍rva̱je pi̱tarā̱ navya̍sībhirgī̱rbhiḥ kṛ̍ṇudhva̱ṃ sada̍ne ṛ̱tasya̍ |

ā no̍ dyāvāpṛthivī̱ daivye̍na̱ jane̍na yāta̱ṃ mahi̍ vā̱ṃ varū̍tham

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 3

u̱to hi vā̍ṃ ratna̱dheyā̍ni̱ santi̍ pu̱rūṇi̍ dyāvāpṛthivī su̱dāse̍ |

a̱sme dha̍tta̱ṃ yadasa̱daskṛ̍dhoyu yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
