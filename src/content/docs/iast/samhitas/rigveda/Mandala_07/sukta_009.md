# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 9

**6 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| agni: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 1

abo̍dhi jā̱ra u̱ṣasā̍mu̱pasthā̱ddhotā̍ ma̱ndraḥ ka̱vita̍maḥ pāva̱kaḥ |
dadhā̍ti ke̱tumu̱bhaya̍sya ja̱ntorha̱vyā de̱veṣu̱ dravi̍ṇaṃ su̱kṛtsu̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 2

sa su̱kratu̱ryo vi dura̍: paṇī̱nāṃ pu̍nā̱no a̱rkaṃ pu̍ru̱bhoja̍saṃ naḥ |

hotā̍ ma̱ndro vi̱śāṃ damū̍nāsti̱rastamo̍ dadṛśe rā̱myāṇā̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 3

ci̱trabhā̍nuru̱ṣasā̍ṃ bhā̱tyagre̱'pāṃ garbha̍: pra̱sva1 ā vi̍veśa

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 4

su̱sa̱ṃdṛśā̍ bhā̱nunā̱ yo vi̱bhāti̱ prati̱ gāva̍: samidhā̱naṃ bu̍dhanta

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 5

## Verse 6
