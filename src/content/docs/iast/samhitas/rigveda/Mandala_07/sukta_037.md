# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 37

**8  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| viśve devā: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 1

ā vo̱ vāhi̍ṣṭho vahatu sta̱vadhyai̱ ratho̍ vājā ṛbhukṣaṇo̱ amṛ̍ktaḥ |
a̱bhi tri̍pṛ̱ṣṭhaiḥ sava̍neṣu̱ somai̱rmade̍ suśiprā ma̱habhi̍: pṛṇadhvam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 2

yū̱yaṃ ha̱ ratna̍ṃ ma̱ghava̍tsu dhattha sva̱rdṛśa̍ ṛbhukṣaṇo̱ amṛ̍ktam |

saṃ ya̱jñeṣu̍ svadhāvantaḥ pibadhva̱ṃ vi no̱ rādhā̍ṃsi ma̱tibhi̍rdayadhvam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 3

u̱voci̍tha̱ hi ma̍ghavande̱ṣṇaṃ ma̱ho arbha̍sya̱ vasu̍no vibhā̱ge |

u̱bhā te̍ pū̱rṇā vasu̍nā̱ gabha̍stī̱ na sū̱nṛtā̱ ni ya̍mate vasa̱vyā̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 4

tvami̍ndra̱ svaya̍śā ṛbhu̱kṣā vājo̱ na sā̱dhurasta̍me̱ṣyṛkvā̍ |

va̱yaṃ nu te̍ dā̱śvāṃsa̍: syāma̱ brahma̍ kṛ̱ṇvanto̍ harivo̱ vasi̍ṣṭhāḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 5

sani̍tāsi pra̱vato̍ dā̱śuṣe̍ ci̱dyābhi̱rvive̍ṣo haryaśva dhī̱bhiḥ |

va̱va̱nmā nu te̱ yujyā̍bhirū̱tī ka̱dā na̍ indra rā̱ya ā da̍śasyeḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 6

vā̱saya̍sīva ve̱dhasa̱stvaṃ na̍: ka̱dā na̍ indra̱ vaca̍so bubodhaḥ |

asta̍ṃ tā̱tyā dhi̱yā ra̱yiṃ su̱vīra̍ṃ pṛ̱kṣo no̱ arvā̱ nyu̍hīta vā̱jī

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 7

a̱bhi yaṃ de̱vī niṝ̍tiści̱dīśe̱ nakṣa̍nta̱ indra̍ṃ śa̱rada̍: su̱pṛkṣa̍: |

upa̍ triba̱ndhurja̱rada̍ṣṭime̱tyasva̍veśa̱ṃ yaṃ kṛ̱ṇava̍nta̱ martā̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 8

ā no̱ rādhā̍ṃsi savita sta̱vadhyā̱ ā rāyo̍ yantu̱ parva̍tasya rā̱tau | sa

dā̍ no di̱vyaḥ pā̱yuḥ si̍ṣaktu yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
