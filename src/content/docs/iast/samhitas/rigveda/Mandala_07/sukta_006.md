# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 6

**7 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| vaiśvānaro'gni: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 1

pra sa̱mrājo̱ asu̍rasya̱ praśa̍stiṃ pu̱ṃsaḥ kṛ̍ṣṭī̱nāma̍nu̱mādya̍sya |
indra̍syeva̱ pra ta̱vasa̍skṛ̱tāni̱ vande̍ dā̱ruṃ vanda̍māno vivakmi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 2

ka̱viṃ ke̱tuṃ dhā̱siṃ bhā̱numadre̍rhi̱nvanti̱ śaṃ rā̱jyaṃ roda̍syoḥ |

pu̱ra̱ṃda̱rasya̍ gī̱rbhirā vi̍vāse̱'gnervra̱tāni̍ pū̱rvyā ma̱hāni̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 3

nya̍kra̱tūngra̱thino̍ mṛ̱dhravā̍caḥ pa̱ṇīm̐ra̍śra̱ddhām̐ a̍vṛ̱dhām̐ a̍ya̱jñān |

prapra̱ tāndasyū̍m̐ra̱gnirvi̍vāya̱ pūrva̍ścakā̱rāpa̍rā̱m̐ aya̍jyūn

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 4

yo a̍pā̱cīne̱ tama̍si̱ mada̍ntī̱: prācī̍śca̱kāra̱ nṛta̍ma̱: śacī̍bhiḥ |

tamīśā̍na̱ṃ vasvo̍ a̱gniṃ gṛ̍ṇī̱ṣe'nā̍nataṃ da̱maya̍ntaṃ pṛta̱nyūn

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 5

sa ni̱rudhyā̱ nahu̍ṣo ya̱hvo a̱gnirviśa̍ścakre bali̱hṛta̱: saho̍bhiḥ

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 6
