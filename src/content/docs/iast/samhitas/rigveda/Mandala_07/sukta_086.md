# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 86

**8  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| varuṇa: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 1

dhīrā̱ tva̍sya mahi̱nā ja̱nūṃṣi̱ vi yasta̱stambha̱ roda̍sī cidu̱rvī |
pra nāka̍mṛ̱ṣvaṃ nu̍nude bṛ̱hanta̍ṃ dvi̱tā nakṣa̍traṃ pa̱pratha̍cca̱ bhūma̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 2

u̱ta svayā̍ ta̱nvā̱3 saṃ va̍de̱ tatka̱dā nva1ntarvaru̍ṇe bhuvāni |

kiṃ me̍ ha̱vyamahṛ̍ṇāno juṣeta ka̱dā mṛ̍l̤ī̱kaṃ su̱manā̍ a̱bhi khya̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 3

pṛ̱cche tadeno̍ varuṇa di̱dṛkṣūpo̍ emi ciki̱tuṣo̍ vi̱pṛccha̍m |

sa̱mā̱naminme̍ ka̱vaya̍ścidāhura̱yaṃ ha̱ tubhya̱ṃ varu̍ṇo hṛṇīte

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 4

kimāga̍ āsa varuṇa̱ jyeṣṭha̱ṃ yatsto̱tāra̱ṃ jighā̍ṃsasi̱ sakhā̍yam |

pra tanme̍ voco dūl̤abha svadhā̱vo'va̍ tvāne̱nā nama̍sā tu̱ra i̍yām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 5

ava̍ dru̱gdhāni̱ pitryā̍ sṛjā̱ no'va̱ yā va̱yaṃ ca̍kṛ̱mā ta̱nūbhi̍: |

ava̍ rājanpaśu̱tṛpa̱ṃ na tā̱yuṃ sṛ̱jā va̱tsaṃ na dāmno̱ vasi̍ṣṭham

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 6

na sa svo dakṣo̍ varuṇa̱ dhruti̱: sā surā̍ ma̱nyurvi̱bhīda̍ko̱ aci̍ttiḥ |

asti̱ jyāyā̱nkanī̍yasa upā̱re svapna̍śca̱nedanṛ̍tasya prayo̱tā

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 7

ara̍ṃ dā̱so na mī̱l̤huṣe̍ karāṇya̱haṃ de̱vāya̱ bhūrṇa̱ye'nā̍gāḥ |

ace̍tayada̱cito̍ de̱vo a̱ryo gṛtsa̍ṃ rā̱ye ka̱vita̍ro junāti

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 8

a̱yaṃ su tubhya̍ṃ varuṇa svadhāvo hṛ̱di stoma̱ upa̍śritaścidastu |

śaṃ na̱: kṣeme̱ śamu̱ yoge̍ no astu yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
