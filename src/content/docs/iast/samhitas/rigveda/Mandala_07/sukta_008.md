# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 8

**7  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| agni: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 1

i̱ndhe rājā̱ sama̱ryo namo̍bhi̱ryasya̱ pratī̍ka̱māhu̍taṃ ghṛ̱tena̍ |
naro̍ ha̱vyebhi̍rīl̤ate sa̱bādha̱ āgniragra̍ u̱ṣasā̍maśoci

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 2

a̱yamu̱ ṣya suma̍hām̐ avedi̱ hotā̍ ma̱ndro manu̍ṣo ya̱hvo a̱gniḥ |

vi bhā a̍kaḥ sasṛjā̱naḥ pṛ̍thi̱vyāṃ kṛ̱ṣṇapa̍vi̱roṣa̍dhībhirvavakṣe

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 3

kayā̍ no agne̱ vi va̍saḥ suvṛ̱ktiṃ kāmu̍ sva̱dhāmṛ̍ṇavaḥ śa̱syamā̍naḥ |

ka̱dā bha̍vema̱ pata̍yaḥ sudatra rā̱yo va̱ntāro̍ du̱ṣṭara̍sya sā̱dhoḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 4

praprā̱yama̱gnirbha̍ra̱tasya̍ śṛṇve̱ vi yatsūryo̱ na roca̍te bṛ̱hadbhāḥ |

a̱bhi yaḥ pū̱ruṃ pṛta̍nāsu ta̱sthau dyu̍tā̱no daivyo̱ ati̍thiḥ śuśoca

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 5

asa̱nnittve ā̱hava̍nāni̱ bhūri̱ bhuvo̱ viśve̍bhiḥ su̱manā̱ anī̍kaiḥ |

stu̱taści̍dagne śṛṇviṣe gṛṇā̱naḥ sva̱yaṃ va̍rdhasva ta̱nva̍ṃ sujāta

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 6

i̱daṃ vaca̍: śata̱sāḥ saṃsa̍hasra̱muda̱gnaye̍ janiṣīṣṭa dvi̱barhā̍: |

śaṃ yatsto̱tṛbhya̍ ā̱paye̱ bhavā̍ti dyu̱mada̍mīva̱cāta̍naṃ rakṣo̱hā

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 7

nū tvāma̍gna īmahe̱ vasi̍ṣṭhā īśā̱naṃ sū̍no sahaso̱ vasū̍nām |

iṣa̍ṃ sto̱tṛbhyo̍ ma̱ghava̍dbhya ānaḍyū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
