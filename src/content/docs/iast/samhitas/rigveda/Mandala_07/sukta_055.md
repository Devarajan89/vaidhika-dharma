# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 55

**8 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| vāstoṣpati:, 2- 8 indra:( 2-8 prasvāpinī upaniṣad ) |1 gāyatrī, 2-4 upariṣṭādbṛhatī, 5-8 anuṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 1

a̱mī̱va̱hā vā̍stoṣpate̱ viśvā̍ rū̱pāṇyā̍vi̱śan | sakhā̍ su̱śeva̍ edhi naḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 2

yada̍rjuna sārameya da̱taḥ pi̍śaṅga̱ yaccha̍se | vī̍va bhrājanta ṛ̱ṣṭaya̱ upa̱ srakve̍ṣu̱ bapsa̍to̱ ni ṣu sva̍pa

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 3

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 4

## Verse 5
