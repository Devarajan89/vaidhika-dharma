# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 68

**9 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| aśvinau|virāṭ, 8-9   triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 1

ā śu̍bhrā yātamaśvinā̱ svaśvā̱ giro̍ dasrā jujuṣā̱ṇā yu̱vāko̍: | ha̱vyāni̍ ca̱ prati̍bhṛtā vī̱taṃ na̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 2

pra vā̱mandhā̍ṃsi̱ madyā̍nyasthu̱rara̍ṃ gantaṃ ha̱viṣo̍ vī̱taye̍ me | ti̱ro a̱ryo hava̍nāni śru̱taṃ na̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 3

pra vā̱ṃ ratho̱ mano̍javā iyarti ti̱ro rajā̍ṃsyaśvinā śa̱toti̍: | a̱smabhya̍ṃ sūryāvasū iyā̱naḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 4

a̱yaṃ ha̱ yadvā̍ṃ deva̱yā u̱ adri̍rū̱rdhvo viva̍kti soma̱sudyu̱vabhyā̍m | ā va̱lgū vipro̍ vavṛtīta ha̱vyaiḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 5

ci̱traṃ ha̱ yadvā̱ṃ bhoja̍na̱ṃ nvasti̱ nyatra̍ye̱ mahi̍ṣvantaṃ yuyotam | yo vā̍mo̱māna̱ṃ dadha̍te pri̱yaḥ san

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 6

u̱ta tyadvā̍ṃ jura̱te a̍śvinā bhū̱ccyavā̍nāya pra̱tītya̍ṃ havi̱rde | adhi̱ yadvarpa̍ i̱taū̍ti dha̱tthaḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 7

u̱ta tyaṃ bhu̱jyuma̍śvinā̱ sakhā̍yo̱ madhye̍ jahurdu̱revā̍saḥ samu̱dre | nirī̍ṃ parṣa̱darā̍vā̱ yo yu̱vāku̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 8

vṛkā̍ya ci̱jjasa̍mānāya śaktamu̱ta śru̍taṃ śa̱yave̍ hū̱yamā̍nā |

yāva̱ghnyāmapi̍nvatama̱po na sta̱rya̍ṃ ciccha̱ktya̍śvinā̱ śacī̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 9

e̱ṣa sya kā̱rurja̍rate sū̱ktairagre̍ budhā̱na u̱ṣasā̍ṃ su̱manmā̍ |

i̱ṣā taṃ va̍rdhada̱ghnyā payo̍bhiryū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
