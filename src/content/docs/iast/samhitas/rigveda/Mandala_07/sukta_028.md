# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 28

**5  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| indra: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 1

brahmā̍ ṇa i̱ndropa̍ yāhi vi̱dvāna̱rvāñca̍ste̱ hara̍yaḥ santu yu̱ktāḥ |
viśve̍ ci̱ddhi tvā̍ vi̱hava̍nta̱ martā̍ a̱smāka̱micchṛ̍ṇuhi viśvaminva

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 2

hava̍ṃ ta indra mahi̱mā vyā̍na̱ḍbrahma̱ yatpāsi̍ śavasi̱nnṛṣī̍ṇām |

ā yadvajra̍ṃ dadhi̱ṣe hasta̍ ugra gho̱raḥ sankratvā̍ janiṣṭhā̱ aṣā̍l̤haḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 3

tava̱ praṇī̍tīndra̱ johu̍vānā̱ntsaṃ yannṝnna roda̍sī ni̱netha̍ |

ma̱he kṣa̱trāya̱ śava̍se̱ hi ja̱jñe'tū̍tujiṃ ci̱ttūtu̍jiraśiśnat

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 4

e̱bhirna̍ i̱ndrāha̍bhirdaśasya durmi̱trāso̱ hi kṣi̱taya̱: pava̍nte |

prati̱ yaccaṣṭe̱ anṛ̍tamane̱nā ava̍ dvi̱tā varu̍ṇo mā̱yī na̍: sāt

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 5

vo̱cemedindra̍ṃ ma̱ghavā̍namenaṃ ma̱ho rā̱yo rādha̍so̱ yaddada̍nnaḥ |

yo arca̍to̱ brahma̍kṛti̱mavi̍ṣṭho yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
