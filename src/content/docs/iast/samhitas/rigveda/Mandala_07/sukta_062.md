# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 62

**6  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| 1-3 sūrya:, 4-6 mitrāvaruṇau | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 1

utsūryo̍ bṛ̱hada̱rcīṃṣya̍śretpu̱ru viśvā̱ jani̍ma̱ mānu̍ṣāṇām |
sa̱mo di̱vā da̍dṛśe̱ roca̍māna̱: kratvā̍ kṛ̱taḥ sukṛ̍taḥ ka̱rtṛbhi̍rbhūt

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 2

sa sū̍rya̱ prati̍ pu̱ro na̱ udgā̍ e̱bhiḥ stome̍bhireta̱śebhi̱revai̍: |

pra no̍ mi̱trāya̱ varu̍ṇāya vo̱co'nā̍gaso arya̱mṇe a̱gnaye̍ ca

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 3

vi na̍: sa̱hasra̍ṃ śu̱rudho̍ radantvṛ̱tāvā̍no̱ varu̍ṇo mi̱tro a̱gniḥ |

yaccha̍ntu ca̱ndrā u̍pa̱maṃ no̍ a̱rkamā na̱: kāma̍ṃ pūpurantu̱ stavā̍nāḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 4

dyāvā̍bhūmī adite̱ trāsī̍thāṃ no̱ ye vā̍ṃ ja̱jñuḥ su̱jani̍māna ṛṣve |

mā hel̤e̍ bhūma̱ varu̍ṇasya vā̱yormā mi̱trasya̍ pri̱yata̍masya nṛ̱ṇām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 5

pra bā̱havā̍ sisṛtaṃ jī̱vase̍ na̱ ā no̱ gavyū̍timukṣataṃ ghṛ̱tena̍ |

ā no̱ jane̍ śravayataṃ yuvānā śru̱taṃ me̍ mitrāvaruṇā̱ have̱mā

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 6

nū mi̱tro varu̍ṇo arya̱mā na̱stmane̍ to̱kāya̱ vari̍vo dadhantu |

su̱gā no̱ viśvā̍ su̱pathā̍ni santu yū̱yaṃ pā̍ta sva̱stibhi̱: sadā̍ naḥ
