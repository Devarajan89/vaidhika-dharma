# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 89

**5  maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| varuṇa: | gāyatrī, 5 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 1

mo ṣu va̍ruṇa mṛ̱nmaya̍ṃ gṛ̱haṃ rā̍janna̱haṃ ga̍mam | mṛ̱l̤ā su̍kṣatra mṛ̱l̤aya̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 2

yademi̍ prasphu̱ranni̍va̱ dṛti̱rna dhmā̱to a̍drivaḥ | mṛ̱l̤ā su̍kṣatra mṛ̱l̤aya̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 3

kratva̍: samaha dī̱natā̍ pratī̱paṃ ja̍gamā śuce | mṛ̱l̤ā su̍kṣatra mṛ̱l̤aya̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 4

a̱pāṃ madhye̍ tasthi̱vāṃsa̱ṃ tṛṣṇā̍vidajjari̱tāra̍m | mṛ̱l̤ā su̍kṣatra mṛ̱l̤aya̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 5

yatkiṃ ce̱daṃ va̍ruṇa̱ daivye̱ jane̍'bhidro̱haṃ ma̍nu̱ṣyā̱3ścarā̍masi |

aci̍ttī̱ yattava̱ dharmā̍ yuyopi̱ma mā na̱stasmā̱dena̍so deva rīriṣaḥ
