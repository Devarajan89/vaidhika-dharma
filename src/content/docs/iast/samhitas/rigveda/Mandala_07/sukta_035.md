# Maṇḍala 7 — Sūktam 35

**15 maitrāvaruṇirvasiṣṭha:| viśve devā:| triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 1

śaṃ na̍ indrā̱gnī bha̍vatā̱mavo̍bhi̱: śaṃ na̱ indrā̱varu̍ṇā rā̱taha̍vyā |
śamindrā̱somā̍ suvi̱tāya̱ śaṃ yoḥ śaṃ na̱ indrā̍pū̱ṣaṇā̱ vāja̍sātau

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 2

śaṃ no̱ bhaga̱: śamu̍ na̱: śaṃso̍ astu̱ śaṃ na̱: pura̍ṃdhi̱: śamu̍ santu̱ rāya̍: |

śaṃ na̍: sa̱tyasya̍ su̱yama̍sya̱ śaṃsa̱: śaṃ no̍ arya̱mā pu̍rujā̱to a̍stu

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 3

śaṃ no̍ dhā̱tā śamu̍ dha̱rtā no̍ astu̱ śaṃ na̍ urū̱cī bha̍vatu sva̱dhābhi̍: |

śaṃ roda̍sī bṛha̱tī śaṃ no̱ adri̱: śaṃ no̍ de̱vānā̍ṃ su̱havā̍ni santu

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 4

śaṃ no̍ a̱gnirjyoti̍ranīko astu̱ śaṃ no̍ mi̱trāvaru̍ṇāva̱śvinā̱ śam |

śaṃ na̍: su̱kṛtā̍ṃ sukṛ̱tāni̍ santu̱ śaṃ na̍ iṣi̱ro a̱bhi vā̍tu̱ vāta̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 5

śaṃ no̱ dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī pū̱rvahū̍tau̱ śama̱ntari̍kṣaṃ dṛ̱śaye̍ no astu |

śaṃ na̱ oṣa̍dhīrva̱nino̍ bhavantu̱ śaṃ no̱ raja̍sa̱spati̍rastu ji̱ṣṇuḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 6

śaṃ na̱ indro̱ vasu̍bhirde̱vo a̍stu̱ śamā̍di̱tyebhi̱rvaru̍ṇaḥ su̱śaṃsa̍: |

śaṃ no̍ ru̱dro ru̱drebhi̱rjalā̍ṣa̱: śaṃ na̱stvaṣṭā̱ gnābhi̍ri̱ha śṛ̍ṇotu

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 7

śaṃ na̱ somo̍ bhavatu̱ brahma̱ śaṃ na̱: śaṃ no̱ grāvā̍ṇa̱: śamu̍ santu ya̱jñāḥ |

śaṃ na̱: svarū̍ṇāṃ mi̱tayo̍ bhavantu̱ śaṃ na̍: pra̱sva1: śamva̍stu̱ vedi̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 8

śaṃ na̱: sūrya̍ uru̱cakṣā̱ ude̍tu̱ śaṃ na̱ścata̍sraḥ pra̱diśo̍ bhavantu |

śaṃ na̱: parva̍tā dhru̱vayo̍ bhavantu̱ śaṃ na̱: sindha̍va̱: śamu̍ sa̱ntvāpa̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 9

śaṃ no̱ adi̍tirbhavatu vra̱tebhi̱: śaṃ no̍ bhavantu ma̱ruta̍: sva̱rkāḥ |

śaṃ no̱ viṣṇu̱: śamu̍ pū̱ṣā no̍ astu̱ śaṃ no̍ bha̱vitra̱ṃ śamva̍stu vā̱yuḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 10

śaṃ no̍ de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā trāya̍māṇa̱: śaṃ no̍ bhavantū̱ṣaso̍ vibhā̱tīḥ |

śaṃ na̍: pa̱rjanyo̍ bhavatu pra̱jābhya̱: śaṃ na̱: kṣetra̍sya̱ pati̍rastu śa̱mbhuḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 11

śama̍bhi̱ṣāca̱: śamu̍ rāti̱ṣāca̱: śaṃ no̍ di̱vyāḥ pārthi̍vā̱: śaṃ no̱ apyā̍:

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 7 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 12

śaṃ na̍ ṛ̱bhava̍: su̱kṛta̍: su̱hastā̱: śaṃ no̍ bhavantu pi̱taro̱ have̍ṣu

## Verse 13

śṛ̱ṇvantu̍ no di̱vyāḥ pārthi̍vāso̱ gojā̍tā u̱ta ye ya̱jñiyā̍saḥ
