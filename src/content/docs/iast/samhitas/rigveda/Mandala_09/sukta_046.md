# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 46

**6 ayāsya āṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 1

asṛ̍grande̱vavī̍ta̱ye'tyā̍sa̱: kṛtvyā̍ iva | kṣara̍ntaḥ parvatā̱vṛdha̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 2

pari̍ṣkṛtāsa̱ inda̍vo̱ yoṣe̍va̱ pitryā̍vatī | vā̱yuṃ somā̍ asṛkṣata

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 3

e̱te somā̍sa̱ inda̍va̱: praya̍svantaśca̱mū su̱tāḥ | indra̍ṃ vardhanti̱ karma̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 4

ā dhā̍vatā suhastyaḥ śu̱krā gṛ̍bhṇīta ma̱nthinā̍ | gobhi̍: śrīṇīta matsa̱ram

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 5

sa pa̍vasva dhanaṃjaya praya̱ntā rādha̍so ma̱haḥ | a̱smabhya̍ṃ soma gātu̱vit

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 6

e̱taṃ mṛ̍janti̱ marjya̱ṃ pava̍māna̱ṃ daśa̱ kṣipa̍: | indrā̍ya matsa̱raṃ mada̍m
