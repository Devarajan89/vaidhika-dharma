# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 60

**4 avatsāra: kāśyapaḥ | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |3 purauṣṇika |**

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 1

pra gā̍ya̱treṇa̍ gāyata̱ pava̍māna̱ṃ vica̍rṣaṇim | indu̍ṃ sa̱hasra̍cakṣasam

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 2
