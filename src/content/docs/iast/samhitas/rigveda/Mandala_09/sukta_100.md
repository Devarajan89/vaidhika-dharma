# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 100

**9 rebhasūnū kāśyapau | pavamāna: soma: | anuṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 100 | **Verse:** 1

a̱bhī na̍vante a̱druha̍: pri̱yamindra̍sya̱ kāmya̍m | va̱tsaṃ na pūrva̱ āyu̍ni jā̱taṃ ri̍hanti mā̱tara̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 100 | **Verse:** 2

pu̱nā̱na i̍nda̱vā bha̍ra̱ soma̍ dvi̱barha̍saṃ ra̱yim | tvaṃ vasū̍ni puṣyasi̱ viśvā̍ni dā̱śuṣo̍ gṛ̱he

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 100 | **Verse:** 3

tvaṃ dhiya̍ṃ mano̱yuja̍ṃ sṛ̱jā vṛ̱ṣṭiṃ na ta̍nya̱tuḥ | tvaṃ vasū̍ni̱ pārthi̍vā di̱vyā ca̍ soma puṣyasi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 100 | **Verse:** 4

pari̍ te ji̱gyuṣo̍ yathā̱ dhārā̍ su̱tasya̍ dhāvati | raṃha̍māṇā̱ vya1vyaya̱ṃ vāra̍ṃ vā̱jīva̍ sāna̱siḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 100 | **Verse:** 5

kratve̱ dakṣā̍ya naḥ kave̱ pava̍sva soma̱ dhāra̍yā | indrā̍ya̱ pāta̍ve su̱to mi̱trāya̱ varu̍ṇāya ca

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 100 | **Verse:** 6

pava̍sva vāja̱sāta̍maḥ pa̱vitre̱ dhāra̍yā su̱taḥ | indrā̍ya soma̱ viṣṇa̍ve de̱vebhyo̱ madhu̍mattamaḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 100 | **Verse:** 7

tvāṃ ri̍hanti mā̱taro̱ hari̍ṃ pa̱vitre̍ a̱druha̍: | va̱tsaṃ jā̱taṃ na dhe̱nava̱: pava̍māna̱ vidha̍rmaṇi

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 100 | **Verse:** 8

pava̍māna̱ mahi̱ śrava̍ści̱trebhi̍ryāsi ra̱śmibhi̍: | śardha̱ntamā̍ṃsi jighnase̱ viśvā̍ni dā̱śuṣo̍ gṛ̱he

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 100 | **Verse:** 9

tvaṃ dyāṃ ca̍ mahivrata pṛthi̱vīṃ cāti̍ jabhriṣe | prati̍ drā̱pima̍muñcathā̱: pava̍māna mahitva̱nā
