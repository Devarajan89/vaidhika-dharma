# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 35

**6 prabhūvasurāṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 1

ā na̍: pavasva̱ dhāra̍yā̱ pava̍māna ra̱yiṃ pṛ̱thum | yayā̱ jyoti̍rvi̱dāsi̍ naḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 2

indo̍ samudramīṅkhaya̱ pava̍sva viśvamejaya | rā̱yo dha̱rtā na̱ oja̍sā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 3

tvayā̍ vī̱reṇa̍ vīravo̱'bhi ṣyā̍ma pṛtanya̱taḥ | kṣarā̍ ṇo a̱bhi vārya̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 4

pra vāja̱mindu̍riṣyati̱ siṣā̍sanvāja̱sā ṛṣi̍: | vra̱tā vi̍dā̱na āyu̍dhā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 5

taṃ gī̱rbhirvā̍camīṅkha̱yaṃ pu̍nā̱naṃ vā̍sayāmasi | soma̱  jana̍sya̱ gopa̍tim

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 6

viśvo̱ yasya̍ vra̱te jano̍ dā̱dhāra̱ dharma̍ṇa̱spate̍: | pu̱nā̱nasya̍ pra̱bhūva̍soḥ
