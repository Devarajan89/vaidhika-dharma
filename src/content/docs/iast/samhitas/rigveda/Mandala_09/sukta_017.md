# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 17

**8   kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 1

pra ni̱mnene̍va̱ sindha̍vo̱ ghnanto̍ vṛ̱trāṇi̱ bhūrṇa̍yaḥ | somā̍ asṛgramā̱śava̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 2

a̱bhi su̍vā̱nāsa̱ inda̍vo vṛ̱ṣṭaya̍: pṛthi̱vīmi̍va | indra̱ṃ somā̍so akṣaran

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 3

atyū̍rmirmatsa̱ro mada̱: soma̍: pa̱vitre̍ arṣati | vi̱ghnanrakṣā̍ṃsi deva̱yuḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 4

ā ka̱laśe̍ṣu dhāvati pa̱vitre̱ pari̍ ṣicyate | u̱kthairya̱jñeṣu̍ vardhate

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 5

ati̱ trī so̍ma roca̱nā roha̱nna bhrā̍jase̱ diva̍m | i̱ṣṇantsūrya̱ṃ na co̍dayaḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 6

a̱bhi viprā̍ anūṣata mū̱rdhanya̱jñasya̍ kā̱rava̍: | dadhā̍nā̱ścakṣa̍si pri̱yam

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 7

tamu̍ tvā vā̱jina̱ṃ naro̍ dhī̱bhirviprā̍ ava̱syava̍: | mṛ̱janti̍ de̱vatā̍taye

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 8

madho̱rdhārā̱manu̍ kṣara tī̱vraḥ sa̱dhastha̱māsa̍daḥ | cāru̍ṝ̱tāya̍ pī̱taye̍
