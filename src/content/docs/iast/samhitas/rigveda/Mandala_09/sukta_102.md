# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 102

**8 trita āptya: | pavamāna: soma: | uṣṇik |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 1

krā̱ṇā śiśu̍rma̱hīnā̍ṃ hi̱nvannṛ̱tasya̱ dīdhi̍tim | viśvā̱ pari̍ pri̱yā bhu̍va̱dadha̍ dvi̱tā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 2

upa̍ tri̱tasya̍ pā̱ṣyo̱3rabha̍kta̱ yadguhā̍ pa̱dam | ya̱jñasya̍ sa̱pta dhāma̍bhi̱radha̍ pri̱yam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 3

trīṇi̍ tri̱tasya̱ dhāra̍yā pṛ̱ṣṭheṣvera̍yā ra̱yim | mimī̍te asya̱ yoja̍nā̱ vi su̱kratu̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 4

ja̱jñā̱naṃ sa̱pta mā̱taro̍ ve̱dhāma̍śāsata śri̱ye | a̱yaṃ dhru̱vo ra̍yī̱ṇāṃ cike̍ta̱ yat

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 5

a̱sya vra̱te sa̱joṣa̍so̱ viśve̍ de̱vāso̍ a̱druha̍: | spā̱rhā bha̍vanti̱ ranta̍yo ju̱ṣanta̱ yat

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 6

yamī̱ garbha̍mṛtā̱vṛdho̍ dṛ̱śe cāru̱majī̍janan | ka̱viṃ maṃhi̍ṣṭhamadhva̱re pu̍ru̱spṛha̍m

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 7

sa̱mī̱cī̱ne a̱bhi tmanā̍ ya̱hvī ṛ̱tasya̍ mā̱tarā̍ | ta̱nvā̱nā ya̱jñamā̍nu̱ṣagyada̍ñja̱te

---

## Verse 8

kratvā̍ śu̱krebhi̍ra̱kṣabhi̍ṝ̱ṇorapa̍ vra̱jaṃ di̱vaḥ | hi̱nvannṛ̱tasya̱ dīdhi̍ti̱ṃ prādhva̱re
