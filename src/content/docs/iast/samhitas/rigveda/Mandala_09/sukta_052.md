# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 52

**5 ucathya āṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 1

pari̍ dyu̱kṣaḥ sa̱nadra̍yi̱rbhara̱dvāja̍ṃ no̱ andha̍sā | su̱vā̱no a̍rṣa pa̱vitra̱ ā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 2

tava̍ pra̱tnebhi̱radhva̍bhi̱ravyo̱ vāre̱ pari̍ pri̱yaḥ | sa̱hasra̍dhāro yā̱ttanā̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 3

ca̱rurna yastamī̍ṅkha̱yendo̱ na dāna̍mīṅkhaya | va̱dhairva̍dhasnavīṅkhaya

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 4

ni śuṣma̍mindaveṣā̱ṃ puru̍hūta̱ janā̍nām | yo a̱smām̐ ā̱dide̍śati

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 5

śa̱taṃ na̍ inda ū̱tibhi̍: sa̱hasra̍ṃ vā̱ śucī̍nām | pava̍sva maṃha̱yadra̍yiḥ
