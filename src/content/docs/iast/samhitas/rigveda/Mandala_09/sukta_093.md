# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 93

**5 nodhā gautama: | pavamāna: soma: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 1

sā̱ka̱mukṣo̍ marjayanta̱ svasā̍ro̱ daśa̱ dhīra̍sya dhī̱tayo̱ dhanu̍trīḥ |
hari̱: parya̍drava̱jjāḥ sūrya̍sya̱ droṇa̍ṃ nanakṣe̱ atyo̱ na vā̱jī

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 2

saṃ mā̱tṛbhi̱rna śiśu̍rvāvaśā̱no vṛṣā̍ dadhanve puru̱vāro̍ a̱dbhiḥ |

maryo̱ na yoṣā̍ma̱bhi ni̍ṣkṛ̱taṃ yantsaṃ ga̍cchate ka̱laśa̍ u̱sriyā̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 3

u̱ta pra pi̍pya̱ ūdha̱raghnyā̍yā̱ indu̱rdhārā̍bhiḥ sacate sume̱dhāḥ |

mū̱rdhāna̱ṃ gāva̱: paya̍sā ca̱mūṣva̱bhi śrī̍ṇanti̱ vasu̍bhi̱rna ni̱ktaiḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 4

sa no̍ de̱vebhi̍: pavamāna ra̱dendo̍ ra̱yima̱śvina̍ṃ vāvaśā̱naḥ |

ra̱thi̱rā̱yatā̍muśa̱tī pura̍ṃdhirasma̱drya1gā dā̱vane̱ vasū̍nām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 5

nū no̍ ra̱yimupa̍ māsva nṛ̱vanta̍ṃ punā̱no vā̱tāpya̍ṃ vi̱śvaśca̍ndram |

pra va̍ndi̱turi̍ndo tā̱ryāyu̍: prā̱tarma̱kṣū dhi̱yāva̍surjagamyāt
