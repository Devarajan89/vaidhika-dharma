# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 5

**11   kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 1

sami̍ddho vi̱śvata̱spati̱: pava̍māno̱ vi rā̍jati | prī̱ṇanvṛṣā̱ kani̍kradat

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 2

tanū̱napā̱tpava̍māna̱: śṛṅge̱ śiśā̍no arṣati | a̱ntari̍kṣeṇa̱ rāra̍jat

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 3

ī̱l̤enya̱: pava̍māno ra̱yirvi rā̍jati dyu̱mān | madho̱rdhārā̍bhi̱roja̍sā

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 5
