# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 91

**6 kaśyapo mārīca: | pavamāna: soma: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 91 | **Verse:** 1

asa̍rji̱ vakvā̱ rathye̱ yathā̱jau dhi̱yā ma̱notā̍ pratha̱mo ma̍nī̱ṣī |
daśa̱ svasā̍ro̱ adhi̱ sāno̱ avye'ja̍nti̱ vahni̱ṃ sada̍nā̱nyaccha̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 91 | **Verse:** 2

vī̱tī jana̍sya di̱vyasya̍ ka̱vyairadhi̍ suvā̱no na̍hu̱ṣye̍bhi̱rindu̍: |

pra yo nṛbhi̍ra̱mṛto̱ martye̍bhirmarmṛjā̱no'vi̍bhi̱rgobhi̍ra̱dbhiḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 91 | **Verse:** 3

vṛṣā̱ vṛṣṇe̱ roru̍vada̱ṃśura̍smai̱ pava̍māno̱ ruśa̍dīrte̱ payo̱ goḥ |

sa̱hasra̱mṛkvā̍ pa̱thibhi̍rvaco̱vida̍dhva̱smabhi̱: sūro̱ aṇva̱ṃ vi yā̍ti

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 91 | **Verse:** 4

ru̱jā dṛ̱l̤hā ci̍dra̱kṣasa̱: sadā̍ṃsi punā̱na i̍nda ūrṇuhi̱ vi vājā̍n |

vṛ̱ścopari̍ṣṭāttuja̱tā va̱dhena̱ ye anti̍ dū̱rādu̍panā̱yame̍ṣām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 91 | **Verse:** 5

sa pra̍tna̱vannavya̍se viśvavāra sū̱ktāya̍ pa̱thaḥ kṛ̍ṇuhi̱ prāca̍: |

ye du̱:ṣahā̍so va̱nuṣā̍ bṛ̱hanta̱stām̐ste̍ aśyāma purukṛtpurukṣo

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 91 | **Verse:** 6

śaṃ na̱: kṣetra̍mu̱ru jyotī̍ṃṣi soma̱ jyoṅna̱: sūrya̍ṃ dṛ̱śaye̍ rirīhi
