# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 53

**4 avatsāra: kāśyapaḥ | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 1

utte̱ śuṣmā̍so asthū̱ rakṣo̍ bhi̱ndanto̍ adrivaḥ | nu̱dasva̱ yāḥ pa̍ri̱spṛdha̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 2

a̱yā ni̍ja̱ghniroja̍sā rathasa̱ṃge dhane̍ hi̱te | stavā̱ abi̍bhyuṣā hṛ̱dā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 3

asya̍ vra̱tāni̱ nādhṛṣe̱ pava̍mānasya dū̱ḍhyā̍ | ru̱ja yastvā̍ pṛta̱nyati̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 4

taṃ hi̍nvanti mada̱cyuta̱ṃ hari̍ṃ na̱dīṣu̍ vā̱jina̍m | indu̱mindrā̍ya matsa̱ram
