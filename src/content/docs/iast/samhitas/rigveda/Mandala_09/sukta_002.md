# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 2

**10 medhātithi: kāṇva: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 1

pava̍sva deva̱vīrati̍ pa̱vitra̍ṃ soma̱ raṃhyā̍ | indra̍mindo̱ vṛṣā vi̍śa

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 2

ā va̍cyasva̱ mahi̱ psaro̱ vṛṣe̍ndo dyu̱mnava̍ttamaḥ | ā yoni̍ṃ dharṇa̱siḥ sa̍daḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 3

adhu̍kṣata pri̱yaṃ madhu̱ dhārā̍ su̱tasya̍ ve̱dhasa̍: | a̱po va̍siṣṭa su̱kratu̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 4

ma̱hānta̍ṃ tvā ma̱hīranvāpo̍ arṣanti̱ sindha̍vaḥ | yadgobhi̍rvāsayi̱ṣyase̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 5

sa̱mu̱dro a̱psu mā̍mṛje viṣṭa̱mbho dha̱ruṇo̍ di̱vaḥ | soma̍: pa̱vitre̍ asma̱yuḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 6

aci̍krada̱dvṛṣā̱ hari̍rma̱hānmi̱tro na da̍rśa̱taḥ | saṃ sūrye̍ṇa rocate

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 7

gira̍sta inda̱ oja̍sā marmṛ̱jyante̍ apa̱syuva̍: | yābhi̱rmadā̍ya̱ śumbha̍se

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 8

taṃ tvā̱ madā̍ya̱ ghṛṣva̍ya u lokakṛ̱tnumī̍mahe | tava̱ praśa̍stayo ma̱hīḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 9

a̱smabhya̍mindavindra̱yurmadhva̍: pavasva̱ dhāra̍yā | pa̱rjanyo̍ vṛṣṭi̱mām̐ i̍va

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 10

go̱ṣā i̍ndo nṛ̱ṣā a̍syaśva̱sā vā̍ja̱sā u̱ta | ā̱tmā ya̱jñasya̍ pū̱rvyaḥ
