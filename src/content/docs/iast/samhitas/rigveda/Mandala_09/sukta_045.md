# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 45

**6 ayāsya āṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 1

sa pa̍vasva̱ madā̍ya̱ kaṃ nṛ̱cakṣā̍ de̱vavī̍taye | inda̱vindrā̍ya pī̱taye̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 2

sa no̍ arṣā̱bhi dū̱tyaṃ1 tvamindrā̍ya tośase | de̱vāntsakhi̍bhya̱ ā vara̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 3

u̱ta tvāma̍ru̱ṇaṃ va̱yaṃ gobhi̍rañjmo̱ madā̍ya̱ kam | vi no̍ rā̱ye duro̍ vṛdhi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 4

atyū̍ pa̱vitra̍makramīdvā̱jī dhura̱ṃ na yāma̍ni | indu̍rde̱veṣu̍ patyate

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 5

samī̱ sakhā̍yo asvara̱nvane̱ krīl̤a̍nta̱matya̍vim | indu̍ṃ nā̱vā a̍nūṣata

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 6

tayā̍ pavasva̱ dhāra̍yā̱ yayā̍ pī̱to vi̱cakṣa̍se | indo̍ sto̱tre su̱vīrya̍m
