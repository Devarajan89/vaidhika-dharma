# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 28

**6 priyamedha āṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 1

e̱ṣa vā̱jī hi̱to nṛbhi̍rviśva̱vinmana̍sa̱spati̍: | avyo̱ vāra̱ṃ vi dhā̍vati

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 2

e̱ṣa pa̱vitre̍ akṣara̱tsomo̍ de̱vebhya̍: su̱taḥ | viśvā̱ dhāmā̍nyāvi̱śan

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 3

e̱ṣa de̱vaḥ śu̍bhāya̱te'dhi̱ yonā̱vama̍rtyaḥ | vṛ̱tra̱hā de̍va̱vīta̍maḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 4

e̱ṣa vṛṣā̱ kani̍kradadda̱śabhi̍rjā̱mibhi̍rya̱taḥ | a̱bhi droṇā̍ni dhāvati

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 5

e̱ṣa sūrya̍marocaya̱tpava̍māno̱ vica̍rṣaṇiḥ | viśvā̱ dhāmā̍ni viśva̱vit

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 6

e̱ṣa śu̱ṣmyadā̍bhya̱: soma̍: punā̱no a̍rṣati | de̱vā̱vīra̍ghaśaṃsa̱hā
