# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 50

**5 ucathya āṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 1

utte̱ śuṣmā̍sa īrate̱ sindho̍rū̱rmeri̍va sva̱naḥ | vā̱ṇasya̍ codayā pa̱vim

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 2

pra̱sa̱ve ta̱ udī̍rate ti̱sro vāco̍ makha̱syuva̍: | yadavya̱ eṣi̱ sāna̍vi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 3

avyo̱ vāre̱ pari̍ pri̱yaṃ hari̍ṃ hinva̱ntyadri̍bhiḥ | pava̍mānaṃ madhu̱ścuta̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 4

ā pa̍vasva madintama pa̱vitra̱ṃ dhāra̍yā kave | a̱rkasya̱ yoni̍mā̱sada̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 5

sa pa̍vasva madintama̱ gobhi̍rañjā̱no a̱ktubhi̍: | inda̱vindrā̍ya pī̱taye̍
