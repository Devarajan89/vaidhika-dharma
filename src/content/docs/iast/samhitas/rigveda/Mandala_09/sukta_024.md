# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 24

**7   kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 24 | **Verse:** 1

pra somā̍so adhanviṣu̱: pava̍mānāsa̱ inda̍vaḥ | śrī̱ṇā̱nā a̱psu mṛ̍ñjata

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 24 | **Verse:** 2

a̱bhi gāvo̍ adhanviṣu̱rāpo̱ na pra̱vatā̍ ya̱tīḥ | pu̱nā̱nā indra̍māśata

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 24 | **Verse:** 3

pra pa̍vamāna dhanvasi̱ somendrā̍ya̱ pāta̍ve | nṛbhi̍rya̱to vi nī̍yase

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 24 | **Verse:** 4

tvaṃ so̍ma nṛ̱māda̍na̱: pava̍sva carṣaṇī̱sahe̍ | sasni̱ryo a̍nu̱mādya̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 24 | **Verse:** 5

indo̱ yadadri̍bhiḥ su̱taḥ pa̱vitra̍ṃ pari̱dhāva̍si | ara̱mindra̍sya̱ dhāmne̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 24 | **Verse:** 6

pava̍sva vṛtrahantamo̱kthebhi̍ranu̱mādya̍: | śuci̍: pāva̱ko adbhu̍taḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 24 | **Verse:** 7

śuci̍: pāva̱ka u̍cyate̱ soma̍: su̱tasya̱ madhva̍: | de̱vā̱vīra̍ghaśaṃsa̱hā
