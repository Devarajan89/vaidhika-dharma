# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 23

**7   kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 1

somā̍ asṛgramā̱śavo̱ madho̱rmada̍sya̱ dhāra̍yā | a̱bhi viśvā̍ni̱ kāvyā̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 2

anu̍ pra̱tnāsa̍ ā̱yava̍: pa̱daṃ navī̍yo akramuḥ | ru̱ce ja̍nanta̱ sūrya̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 3

ā pa̍vamāna no bharā̱'ryo adā̍śuṣo̱ gaya̍m | kṛ̱dhi pra̱jāva̍tī̱riṣa̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 4

a̱bhi somā̍sa ā̱yava̱: pava̍nte̱ madya̱ṃ mada̍m | a̱bhi kośa̍ṃ madhu̱ścuta̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 5

somo̍ arṣati dharṇa̱sirdadhā̍na indri̱yaṃ rasa̍m | su̱vīro̍ abhiśasti̱pāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 6

indrā̍ya soma pavase de̱vebhya̍: sadha̱mādya̍: | indo̱ vāja̍ṃ siṣāsasi

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 7

a̱sya pī̱tvā madā̍nā̱mindro̍ vṛ̱trāṇya̍pra̱ti | ja̱ghāna̍ ja̱ghana̍cca̱ nu
