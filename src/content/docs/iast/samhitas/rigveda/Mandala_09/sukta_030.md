# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 30

**6 bindurāṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 1

pra dhārā̍ asya śu̱ṣmiṇo̱ vṛthā̍ pa̱vitre̍ akṣaran | pu̱nā̱no vāca̍miṣyati

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 2

indu̍rhiyā̱naḥ so̱tṛbhi̍rmṛ̱jyamā̍na̱: kani̍kradat | iya̍rti va̱gnumi̍ndri̱yam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 3

ā na̱: śuṣma̍ṃ nṛ̱ṣāhya̍ṃ vī̱rava̍ntaṃ puru̱spṛha̍m | pava̍sva soma̱ dhāra̍yā

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 4

pra somo̱ ati̱ dhāra̍yā̱ pava̍māno asiṣyadat | a̱bhi droṇā̍nyā̱sada̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 5

a̱psu tvā̱ madhu̍mattama̱ṃ hari̍ṃ hinva̱ntyadri̍bhiḥ | inda̱vindrā̍ya pī̱taye̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 6

su̱notā̱ madhu̍mattama̱ṃ soma̱mindrā̍ya va̱jriṇe̍ | cāru̱ṃ śardhā̍ya matsa̱ram
