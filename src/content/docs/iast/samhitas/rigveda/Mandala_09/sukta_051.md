# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 51

**5 ucathya āṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 1

adhva̍ryo̱ adri̍bhiḥ su̱taṃ soma̍ṃ pa̱vitra̱ ā sṛ̍ja | pu̱nī̱hīndrā̍ya̱ pāta̍ve

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 2

di̱vaḥ pī̱yūṣa̍mutta̱maṃ soma̱mindrā̍ya va̱jriṇe̍ | su̱notā̱ madhu̍mattamam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 3

tava̱ tya i̍ndo̱ andha̍so de̱vā madho̱rvya̍śnate | pava̍mānasya ma̱ruta̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 4

tvaṃ hi so̍ma va̱rdhaya̍ntsu̱to madā̍ya̱ bhūrṇa̍ye | vṛṣa̍ntsto̱tāra̍mū̱taye̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 5

a̱bhya̍rṣa vicakṣaṇa pa̱vitra̱ṃ dhāra̍yā su̱taḥ | a̱bhi vāja̍mu̱ta śrava̍:
