# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 57

**4 avatsāra: kāśyapaḥ | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 1

pra te̱ dhārā̍ asa̱ścato̍ di̱vo na ya̍nti vṛ̱ṣṭaya̍: | acchā̱ vāja̍ṃ saha̱sriṇa̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 2

a̱bhi pri̱yāṇi̱ kāvyā̱ viśvā̱ cakṣā̍ṇo arṣati | hari̍stuñjā̱na āyu̍dhā

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 3

sa ma̍rmṛjā̱na ā̱yubhi̱ribho̱ rāje̍va suvra̱taḥ | śye̱no na vaṃsu̍ ṣīdati

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 4

sa no̱ viśvā̍ di̱vo vasū̱to pṛ̍thi̱vyā adhi̍ | pu̱nā̱na i̍nda̱vā bha̍ra
