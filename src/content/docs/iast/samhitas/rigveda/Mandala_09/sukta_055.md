# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 55

**4 avatsāra: kāśyapaḥ | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 1

yava̍ṃyavaṃ no̱ andha̍sā pu̱ṣṭampu̍ṣṭa̱ṃ pari̍ srava | soma̱ viśvā̍ ca̱ saubha̍gā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 2

indo̱ yathā̱ tava̱ stavo̱ yathā̍ te jā̱tamandha̍saḥ | ni ba̱rhiṣi̍ pri̱ye sa̍daḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 3

u̱ta no̍ go̱vida̍śva̱vitpava̍sva so̱māndha̍sā | ma̱kṣūta̍mebhi̱raha̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 4

yo ji̱nāti̱ na jīya̍te̱ hanti̱ śatru̍ma̱bhītya̍ | sa pa̍vasva sahasrajit
