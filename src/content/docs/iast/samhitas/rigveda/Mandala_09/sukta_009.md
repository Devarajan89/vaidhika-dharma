# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 9

**9 kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 1

pari̍ pri̱yā di̱vaḥ ka̱virvayā̍ṃsi na̱ptyo̍rhi̱taḥ | su̱vā̱no yā̍ti ka̱vikra̍tuḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 2

prapra̱ kṣayā̍ya̱ panya̍se̱ janā̍ya̱ juṣṭo̍ a̱druhe̍ | vī̱tya̍rṣa̱ cani̍ṣṭhayā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 3

sa sū̱nurmā̱tarā̱ śuci̍rjā̱to jā̱te a̍rocayat | ma̱hānma̱hī ṛ̍tā̱vṛdhā̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 4

sa sa̱pta dhī̱tibhi̍rhi̱to na̱dyo̍ ajinvada̱druha̍: | yā eka̱makṣi̍ vāvṛ̱dhuḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 5

tā a̱bhi santa̱mastṛ̍taṃ ma̱he yuvā̍na̱mā da̍dhuḥ | indu̍mindra̱ tava̍ vra̱te

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 6

a̱bhi vahni̱rama̍rtyaḥ sa̱pta pa̍śyati̱ vāva̍hiḥ | krivi̍rde̱vīra̍tarpayat

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 7

avā̱ kalpe̍ṣu naḥ puma̱stamā̍ṃsi soma̱ yodhyā̍ | tāni̍ punāna jaṅghanaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 8

nū navya̍se̱ navī̍yase sū̱ktāya̍ sādhayā pa̱thaḥ | pra̱tna̱vadro̍cayā̱ ruca̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 9

pava̍māna̱ mahi̱ śravo̱ gāmaśva̍ṃ rāsi vī̱rava̍t | sanā̍ me̱dhāṃ sanā̱ sva̍:
