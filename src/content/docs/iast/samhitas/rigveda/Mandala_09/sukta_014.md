# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 14

**8   kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 1

pari̱ prāsi̍ṣyadatka̱viḥ sindho̍rū̱rmāvadhi̍ śri̱taḥ | kā̱raṃ bibhra̍tpuru̱spṛha̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 2

gi̱rā yadī̱ saba̍ndhava̱: pañca̱ vrātā̍ apa̱syava̍: | pa̱ri̱ṣkṛ̱ṇvanti̍ dharṇa̱sim

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 3

āda̍sya śu̱ṣmiṇo̱ rase̱ viśve̍ de̱vā a̍matsata | yadī̱ gobhi̍rvasā̱yate̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 4

ni̱ri̱ṇā̱no vi dhā̍vati̱ jaha̱ccharyā̍ṇi̱ tānvā̍ | atrā̱ saṃ ji̍ghnate yu̱jā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 5

na̱ptībhi̱ryo vi̱vasva̍taḥ śu̱bhro na mā̍mṛ̱je yuvā̍ | gāḥ kṛ̍ṇvā̱no na ni̱rṇija̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 6

ati̍ śri̱tī ti̍ra̱ścatā̍ ga̱vyā ji̍gā̱tyaṇvyā̍ | va̱gnumi̍yarti̱ yaṃ vi̱de

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 7

a̱bhi kṣipa̱: sama̍gmata ma̱rjaya̍ntīri̱ṣaspati̍m | pṛ̱ṣṭhā gṛ̍bhṇata vā̱jina̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 8

pari̍ di̱vyāni̱ marmṛ̍śa̱dviśvā̍ni soma̱ pārthi̍vā | vasū̍ni yāhyasma̱yuḥ
