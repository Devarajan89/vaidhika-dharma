# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 74

**9 kakṣīvān dairghatamasa: | pavamāna: soma: |jagatī , 8 triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 1

śiśu̱rna jā̱to'va̍ cakrada̱dvane̱ sva1ryadvā̱jya̍ru̱ṣaḥ siṣā̍sati |
di̱vo reta̍sā sacate payo̱vṛdhā̱ tamī̍mahe suma̱tī śarma̍ sa̱pratha̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 2

di̱vo yaḥ ska̱mbho dha̱ruṇa̱: svā̍tata̱ āpū̍rṇo a̱ṃśuḥ pa̱ryeti̍ vi̱śvata̍: |

seme ma̱hī roda̍sī yakṣadā̱vṛtā̍ samīcī̱ne dā̍dhāra̱ samiṣa̍: ka̱viḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 3

mahi̱ psara̱: sukṛ̍taṃ so̱myaṃ madhū̱rvī gavyū̍ti̱radi̍teṝ̱taṃ ya̱te |

īśe̱ yo vṛ̱ṣṭeri̱ta u̱sriyo̱ vṛṣā̱'pāṃ ne̱tā ya i̱taū̍tiṝ̱gmiya̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 4

ā̱tma̱nvannabho̍ duhyate ghṛ̱taṃ paya̍ ṛ̱tasya̱ nābhi̍ra̱mṛta̱ṃ vi jā̍yate |

sa̱mī̱cī̱nāḥ su̱dāna̍vaḥ prīṇanti̱ taṃ naro̍ hi̱tamava̍ mehanti̱ pera̍vaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 5

arā̍vīda̱ṃśuḥ saca̍māna ū̱rmiṇā̍ devā̱vyaṃ1 manu̍ṣe pinvati̱ tvaca̍m |

dadhā̍ti̱ garbha̱madi̍teru̱pastha̱ ā yena̍ to̱kaṃ ca̱ tana̍yaṃ ca̱ dhāma̍he

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 6

sa̱hasra̍dhā̱re'va̱ tā a̍sa̱ścata̍stṛ̱tīye̍ santu̱ raja̍si pra̱jāva̍tīḥ |

cata̍sro̱ nābho̱ nihi̍tā a̱vo di̱vo ha̱virbha̍rantya̱mṛta̍ṃ ghṛta̱ścuta̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 7

śve̱taṃ rū̱paṃ kṛ̍ṇute̱ yatsiṣā̍sati̱ somo̍ mī̱ḍhvām̐ asu̍ro veda̱ bhūma̍naḥ |

dhi̱yā śamī̍ sacate̱ sema̱bhi pra̱vaddi̱vaskava̍ndha̱mava̍ darṣadu̱driṇa̍m

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 8

adha̍ śve̱taṃ ka̱laśa̱ṃ gobhi̍ra̱ktaṃ kārṣma̱nnā vā̱jya̍kramītsasa̱vān |

ā hi̍nvire̱ mana̍sā deva̱yanta̍: ka̱kṣīva̍te śa̱tahi̍māya̱ gonā̍m

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 9

a̱dbhiḥ so̍ma papṛcā̱nasya̍ te̱ raso'vyo̱ vāra̱ṃ vi pa̍vamāna dhāvati |

sa mṛ̱jyamā̍naḥ ka̱vibhi̍rmadintama̱ svada̱svendrā̍ya pavamāna pī̱taye̍
