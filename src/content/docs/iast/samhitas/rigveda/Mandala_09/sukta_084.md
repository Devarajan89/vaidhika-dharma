# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 84

**5 vācya: prajāpati: | pavamāna: soma: |jagatī  |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 1

pava̍sva deva̱māda̍no̱ vica̍rṣaṇira̱psā indrā̍ya̱ varu̍ṇāya vā̱yave̍ |
kṛ̱dhī no̍ a̱dya vari̍vaḥ svasti̱madu̍rukṣi̱tau gṛ̍ṇīhi̱ daivya̱ṃ jana̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 2

ā yasta̱sthau bhuva̍nā̱nyama̍rtyo̱ viśvā̍ni̱ soma̱: pari̱ tānya̍rṣati |

kṛ̱ṇvantsa̱ṃcṛta̍ṃ vi̱cṛta̍ma̱bhiṣṭa̍ya̱ indu̍: siṣaktyu̱ṣasa̱ṃ na sūrya̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 3

ā yo gobhi̍: sṛ̱jyata̱ oṣa̍dhī̱ṣvā de̱vānā̍ṃ su̱mna i̱ṣaya̱nnupā̍vasuḥ |

ā vi̱dyutā̍ pavate̱ dhāra̍yā su̱ta indra̱ṃ somo̍ mā̱daya̱ndaivya̱ṃ jana̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 4

e̱ṣa sya soma̍: pavate sahasra̱jiddhi̍nvā̱no vāca̍miṣi̱rāmu̍ṣa̱rbudha̍m |

indu̍: samu̱dramudi̍yarti vā̱yubhi̱rendra̍sya̱ hārdi̍ ka̱laśe̍ṣu sīdati

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 5

a̱bhi tyaṃ gāva̱: paya̍sā payo̱vṛdha̱ṃ soma̍ṃ śrīṇanti ma̱tibhi̍: sva̱rvida̍m |

dha̱na̱ṃja̱yaḥ pa̍vate̱ kṛtvyo̱ raso̱ vipra̍: ka̱viḥ kāvye̍nā̱ sva̍rcanāḥ
