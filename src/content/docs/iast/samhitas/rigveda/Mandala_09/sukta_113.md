# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 113

**11 kaśyapo mārīca: | pavamāna: soma: | paṅkti: |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 113 | **Verse:** 1

śa̱rya̱ṇāva̍ti̱ soma̱mindra̍: pibatu vṛtra̱hā |
bala̱ṃ dadhā̍na ā̱tmani̍ kari̱ṣyanvī̱rya̍ṃ ma̱hadindrā̍yendo̱ pari̍ srava

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 113 | **Verse:** 2

ā pa̍vasva diśāṃ pata ārjī̱kātso̍ma mīḍhvaḥ |

ṛ̱ta̱vā̱kena̍ sa̱tyena̍ śra̱ddhayā̱ tapa̍sā su̱ta indrā̍yendo̱ pari̍ srava

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 113 | **Verse:** 3

pa̱rjanya̍vṛddhaṃ mahi̱ṣaṃ taṃ sūrya̍sya duhi̱tābha̍rat |

taṃ ga̍ndha̱rvāḥ pratya̍gṛbhṇa̱ntaṃ some̱ rasa̱māda̍dhu̱rindrā̍yendo̱ pari̍ srava

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 113 | **Verse:** 4

ṛ̱taṃ vada̍nnṛtadyumna sa̱tyaṃ vada̍ntsatyakarman |

śra̱ddhāṃ vada̍ntsoma rājandhā̱trā so̍ma̱ pari̍ṣkṛta̱ indrā̍yendo̱ pari̍ srava

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 113 | **Verse:** 5

sa̱tyamu̍grasya bṛha̱taḥ saṃ sra̍vanti saṃsra̱vāḥ |

saṃ ya̍nti ra̱sino̱ rasā̍: punā̱no brahma̍ṇā hara̱ indrā̍yendo̱ pari̍ srava

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 113 | **Verse:** 6

yatra̍ bra̱hmā pa̍vamāna chanda̱syā̱ṃ3 vāca̱ṃ vada̍n |

grāvṇā̱ some̍ mahī̱yate̱ some̍nāna̱ndaṃ ja̱naya̱nnindrā̍yendo̱ pari̍ srava

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 113 | **Verse:** 7

yatra̱ jyoti̱raja̍sra̱ṃ yasmi̍m̐llo̱ke sva̍rhi̱tam |

tasmi̱nmāṃ dhe̍hi pavamānā̱'mṛte̍ lo̱ke akṣi̍ta̱ indrā̍yendo̱ pari̍ srava

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 113 | **Verse:** 8

yatra̱ rājā̍ vaivasva̱to yatrā̍va̱rodha̍naṃ di̱vaḥ |

yatrā̱mūrya̱hvatī̱rāpa̱statra̱ māma̱mṛta̍ṃ kṛ̱dhīndrā̍yendo̱ pari̍ srava

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 113 | **Verse:** 9

yatrā̍nukā̱maṃ cara̍ṇaṃ trinā̱ke tri̍di̱ve di̱vaḥ |

lo̱kā yatra̱ jyoti̍ṣmanta̱statra̱ māma̱mṛta̍ṃ kṛ̱dhīndrā̍yendo̱ pari̍ srava

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 113 | **Verse:** 10

yatra̱ kāmā̍ nikā̱māśca̱ yatra̍ bra̱dhnasya̍ vi̱ṣṭapa̍m |

sva̱dhā ca̱ yatra̱ tṛpti̍śca̱ tatra̱ māma̱mṛta̍ṃ kṛ̱dhīndrā̍yendo̱ pari̍ srava

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 113 | **Verse:** 11

yatrā̍na̱ndāśca̱ modā̍śca̱ muda̍: pra̱muda̱ āsa̍te |

kāma̍sya̱ yatrā̱ptāḥ kāmā̱statra̱ māma̱mṛta̍ṃ kṛ̱dhīndrā̍yendo̱ pari̍ srava
