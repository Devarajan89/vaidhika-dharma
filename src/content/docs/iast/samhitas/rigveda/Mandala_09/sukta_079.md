# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 79

**5 kavirbhārgava: | pavamāna: soma: |jagatī  |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 1

a̱co̱daso̍ no dhanva̱ntvinda̍va̱: pra su̍vā̱nāso̍ bṛ̱haddi̍veṣu̱ hara̍yaḥ |
vi ca̱ naśa̍nna i̱ṣo arā̍tayo̱'ryo na̍śanta̱ sani̍ṣanta no̱ dhiya̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 2

pra ṇo̍ dhanva̱ntvinda̍vo mada̱cyuto̱ dhanā̍ vā̱ yebhi̱rarva̍to junī̱masi̍ |

ti̱ro marta̍sya̱ kasya̍ ci̱tpari̍hvṛtiṃ va̱yaṃ dhanā̍ni vi̱śvadhā̍ bharemahi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 3

u̱ta svasyā̱ arā̍tyā a̱rirhi ṣa u̱tānyasyā̱ arā̍tyā̱ vṛko̱ hi ṣaḥ |

dhanva̱nna tṛṣṇā̱ sama̍rīta̱ tām̐ a̱bhi soma̍ ja̱hi pa̍vamāna durā̱dhya̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 4

di̱vi te̱ nābhā̍ para̱mo ya ā̍da̱de pṛ̍thi̱vyāste̍ ruruhu̱: sāna̍vi̱ kṣipa̍: |

adra̍yastvā bapsati̱ goradhi̍ tva̱cya1psu tvā̱ hastai̍rduduhurmanī̱ṣiṇa̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 5

e̱vā ta̍ indo su̱bhva̍ṃ su̱peśa̍sa̱ṃ rasa̍ṃ tuñjanti pratha̱mā a̍bhi̱śriya̍: |

nida̍ṃnidaṃ pavamāna̱ ni tā̍riṣa ā̱viste̱ śuṣmo̍ bhavatu pri̱yo mada̍:
