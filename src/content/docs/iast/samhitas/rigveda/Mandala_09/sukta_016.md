# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 16

**8   kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 1

pra te̍ so̱tāra̍ o̱ṇyo̱3 rasa̱ṃ madā̍ya̱ ghṛṣva̍ye | sargo̱ na ta̱ktyeta̍śaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 2

kratvā̱ dakṣa̍sya ra̱thya̍ma̱po vasā̍na̱mandha̍sā | go̱ṣāmaṇve̍ṣu saścima

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 3

## Verse 4

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 5
