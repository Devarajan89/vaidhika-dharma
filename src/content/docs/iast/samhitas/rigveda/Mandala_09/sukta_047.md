# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 47

**5 kavirbhārgava: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 1

a̱yā soma̍: sukṛ̱tyayā̍ ma̱haści̍da̱bhya̍vardhata | ma̱ndā̱na udvṛ̍ṣāyate

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 2

kṛ̱tānīda̍sya̱ kartvā̱ ceta̍nte dasyu̱tarha̍ṇā | ṛ̱ṇā ca̍ dhṛ̱ṣṇuśca̍yate

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 3

ātsoma̍ indri̱yo raso̱ vajra̍: sahasra̱sā bhu̍vat | u̱kthaṃ yada̍sya̱ jāya̍te

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 4

sva̱yaṃ ka̱virvi̍dha̱rtari̱ viprā̍ya̱ ratna̍micchati | yadī̍ marmṛ̱jyate̱ dhiya̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 5

si̱ṣā̱satū̍ rayī̱ṇāṃ vāje̱ṣvarva̍tāmiva | bhare̍ṣu ji̱gyuṣā̍masi
