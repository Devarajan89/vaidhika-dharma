# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 11

**9   kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 1

upā̍smai gāyatā nara̱: pava̍mānā̱yenda̍ve | a̱bhi de̱vām̐ iya̍kṣate

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 2

a̱bhi te̱ madhu̍nā̱ payo'tha̍rvāṇo aśiśrayuḥ | de̱vaṃ de̱vāya̍ deva̱yu

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 3

sa na̍: pavasva̱ śaṃ gave̱ śaṃ janā̍ya̱ śamarva̍te | śaṃ rā̍ja̱nnoṣa̍dhībhyaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 4

ba̱bhrave̱ nu svata̍vase'ru̱ṇāya̍ divi̱spṛśe̍ | somā̍ya gā̱thama̍rcata

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 5

hasta̍cyutebhi̱radri̍bhiḥ su̱taṃ soma̍ṃ punītana | madhā̱vā dhā̍vatā̱ madhu̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 6

nama̱sedupa̍ sīdata da̱dhneda̱bhi śrī̍ṇītana | indu̱mindre̍ dadhātana

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 7

a̱mi̱tra̱hā vica̍rṣaṇi̱: pava̍sva soma̱ śaṃ gave̍ | de̱vebhyo̍ anukāma̱kṛt

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 8

indrā̍ya soma̱ pāta̍ve̱ madā̍ya̱ pari̍ ṣicyase | ma̱na̱ścinmana̍sa̱spati̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 9

pava̍māna su̱vīrya̍ṃ ra̱yiṃ so̍ma rirīhi naḥ | inda̱vindre̍ṇa no yu̱jā
