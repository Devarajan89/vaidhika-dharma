# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 4

**10 hiraṇyastūpa āṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 1

sanā̍ ca soma̱ jeṣi̍ ca̱ pava̍māna̱ mahi̱ śrava̍: | athā̍ no̱ vasya̍saskṛdhi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 2

sanā̱ jyoti̱: sanā̱ sva1rviśvā̍ ca soma̱ saubha̍gā | athā̍ no̱ vasya̍saskṛdhi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 3

sanā̱ dakṣa̍mu̱ta kratu̱mapa̍ soma̱ mṛdho̍ jahi | athā̍ no̱ vasya̍saskṛdhi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 4

pavī̍tāraḥ punī̱tana̱ soma̱mindrā̍ya̱ pāta̍ve | athā̍ no̱ vasya̍saskṛdhi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 5

tvaṃ sūrye̍ na̱ ā bha̍ja̱ tava̱ kratvā̱ tavo̱tibhi̍: | athā̍ no̱ vasya̍saskṛdhi

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 6

tava̱ kratvā̱ tavo̱tibhi̱rjyokpa̍śyema̱ sūrya̍m | athā̍ no̱ vasya̍saskṛdhi

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 8
