# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 6

**9 kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 1

ma̱ndrayā̍ soma̱ dhāra̍yā̱ vṛṣā̍ pavasva deva̱yuḥ | avyo̱ vāre̍ṣvasma̱yuḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 2

a̱bhi tyaṃ madya̱ṃ mada̱minda̱vindra̱ iti̍ kṣara | a̱bhi vā̱jino̱ arva̍taḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 3

a̱bhi tyaṃ pū̱rvyaṃ mada̍ṃ suvā̱no a̍rṣa pa̱vitra̱ ā | a̱bhi vāja̍mu̱ta śrava̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 4

anu̍ dra̱psāsa̱ inda̍va̱ āpo̱ na pra̱vatā̍saran | pu̱nā̱nā indra̍māśata

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 5

yamatya̍miva vā̱jina̍ṃ mṛ̱janti̱ yoṣa̍ṇo̱ daśa̍ | vane̱ krīl̤a̍nta̱matya̍vim

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 6

taṃ gobhi̱rvṛṣa̍ṇa̱ṃ rasa̱ṃ madā̍ya de̱vavī̍taye | su̱taṃ bharā̍ya̱ saṃ sṛ̍ja

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 7

de̱vo de̱vāya̱ dhāra̱yendrā̍ya pavate su̱taḥ | payo̱ yada̍sya pī̱paya̍t

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 8

ā̱tmā ya̱jñasya̱ raṃhyā̍ suṣvā̱ṇaḥ pa̍vate su̱taḥ | pra̱tnaṃ ni pā̍ti̱ kāvya̍m

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 9

e̱vā pu̍nā̱na i̍ndra̱yurmada̍ṃ madiṣṭha vī̱taye̍ | guhā̍ ciddadhiṣe̱ gira̍:
