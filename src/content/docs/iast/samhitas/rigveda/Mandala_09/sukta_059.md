# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 59

**4 avatsāra: kāśyapaḥ | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 1

pava̍sva go̱jida̍śva̱jidvi̍śva̱jitso̍ma raṇya̱jit | pra̱jāva̱dratna̱mā bha̍ra

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 2

||

pava̍svā̱dbhyo adā̍bhya̱: pava̱svauṣa̍dhībhyaḥ | pava̍sva dhi̱ṣaṇā̍bhyaḥ
