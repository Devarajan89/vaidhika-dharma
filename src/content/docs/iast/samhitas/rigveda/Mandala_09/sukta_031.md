# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 31

**6 gotamo rāhūgaṇa:| pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 1

pra somā̍saḥ svā̱dhya1: pava̍mānāso akramuḥ | ra̱yiṃ kṛ̍ṇvanti̱ ceta̍nam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 2

di̱vaspṛ̍thi̱vyā adhi̱ bhave̍ndo dyumna̱vardha̍naḥ | bhavā̱ vājā̍nā̱ṃ pati̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 3

tubhya̱ṃ vātā̍ abhi̱priya̱stubhya̍marṣanti̱ sindha̍vaḥ | soma̱ vardha̍nti te̱ maha̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 4

ā pyā̍yasva̱ same̍tu te vi̱śvata̍: soma̱ vṛṣṇya̍m | bhavā̱ vāja̍sya saṃga̱the

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 5

tubhya̱ṃ gāvo̍ ghṛ̱taṃ payo̱ babhro̍ dudu̱hre akṣi̍tam | varṣi̍ṣṭhe̱ adhi̱ sāna̍vi

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 6

svā̱yu̱dhasya̍ te sa̱to bhuva̍nasya pate va̱yam | indo̍ sakhi̱tvamu̍śmasi
