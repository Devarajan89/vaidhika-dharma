# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 98

**12 ambarīṣo vārṣāgira:, ṛjiśvā bhāradvājaśca | pavamāna: soma: |anuṣṭup , 11 bṛhatī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 98 | **Verse:** 1

a̱bhi no̍ vāja̱sāta̍maṃ ra̱yima̍rṣa puru̱spṛha̍m | indo̍ sa̱hasra̍bharṇasaṃ tuvidyu̱mnaṃ vi̍bhvā̱saha̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 98 | **Verse:** 2

pari̱ ṣya su̍vā̱no a̱vyaya̱ṃ rathe̱ na varmā̍vyata | indu̍ra̱bhi druṇā̍ hi̱to hi̍yā̱no dhārā̍bhirakṣāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 98 | **Verse:** 3

pari̱ ṣya su̍vā̱no a̍kṣā̱ indu̱ravye̱ mada̍cyutaḥ | dhārā̱ ya ū̱rdhvo a̍dhva̱re bhrā̱jā naiti̍ gavya̱yuḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 98 | **Verse:** 4

sa hi tvaṃ de̍va̱ śaśva̍te̱ vasu̱ martā̍ya dā̱śuṣe̍ | indo̍ saha̱sriṇa̍ṃ ra̱yiṃ śa̱tātmā̍naṃ vivāsasi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 98 | **Verse:** 5

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 98 | **Verse:** 6

---

## Verse 7
