# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 110

**12 tryaruṇastraivṛṣṇa:,trasadasyu: paurukutsya: | pavamāna: soma: |1-3 pipīlikamadhyā anuṣṭup , 4-9 ūrdhvabṛhatī,10-12 virāṭ |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 1

paryū̱ ṣu pra dha̍nva̱ vāja̍sātaye̱ pari̍ vṛ̱trāṇi̍ sa̱kṣaṇi̍: | dvi̱ṣasta̱radhyā̍ ṛṇa̱yā na̍ īyase

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 2

anu̱ hi tvā̍ su̱taṃ so̍ma̱ madā̍masi ma̱he sa̍marya̱rājye̍ | vājā̍m̐ a̱bhi pa̍vamāna̱ pra gā̍hase

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 3

ajī̍jano̱ hi pa̍vamāna̱ sūrya̍ṃ vi̱dhāre̱ śakma̍nā̱ paya̍: | gojī̍rayā̱ raṃha̍māṇa̱: pura̍ṃdhyā

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 4

ajī̍jano amṛta̱ martye̱ṣvām̐ ṛ̱tasya̱ dharma̍nna̱mṛta̍sya̱ cāru̍ṇaḥ | sadā̍saro̱ vāja̱macchā̱ sani̍ṣyadat

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 5

a̱bhya̍bhi̱ hi śrava̍sā ta̱tardi̱thotsa̱ṃ na kaṃ ci̍jjana̱pāna̱makṣi̍tam | śaryā̍bhi̱rna bhara̍māṇo̱ gabha̍styoḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 6

ādī̱ṃ ke ci̱tpaśya̍mānāsa̱ āpya̍ṃ vasu̱ruco̍ di̱vyā a̱bhya̍nūṣata | vāra̱ṃ na de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā vyū̍rṇute

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 7

tve so̍ma pratha̱mā vṛ̱ktaba̍rhiṣo ma̱he vājā̍ya̱ śrava̍se̱ dhiya̍ṃ dadhuḥ | sa tvaṃ no̍ vīra vī̱ryā̍ya codaya

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 8

di̱vaḥ pī̱yūṣa̍ṃ pū̱rvyaṃ yadu̱kthya̍ṃ ma̱ho gā̱hāddi̱va ā nira̍dhukṣata | indra̍ma̱bhi jāya̍māna̱ṃ sama̍svaran

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 9

adha̱ yadi̱me pa̍vamāna̱ roda̍sī i̱mā ca̱ viśvā̱ bhuva̍nā̱bhi ma̱jmanā̍ |

yū̱the na ni̱:ṣṭhā vṛ̍ṣa̱bho vi ti̍ṣṭhase

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 10

soma̍: punā̱no a̱vyaye̱ vāre̱ śiśu̱rna krīl̤a̱npava̍māno akṣāḥ | sa̱hasra̍dhāraḥ śa̱tavā̍ja̱ indu̍:

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 11
