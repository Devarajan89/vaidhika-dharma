# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 92

**6 kaśyapo mārīca: | pavamāna: soma: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 1

pari̍ suvā̱no hari̍ra̱ṃśuḥ pa̱vitre̱ ratho̱ na sa̍rji sa̱naye̍ hiyā̱naḥ |
āpa̱cchloka̍mindri̱yaṃ pū̱yamā̍na̱: prati̍ de̱vām̐ a̍juṣata̱ prayo̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 2

acchā̍ nṛ̱cakṣā̍ asaratpa̱vitre̱ nāma̱ dadhā̍naḥ ka̱vira̍sya̱ yonau̍ |

sīda̱nhote̍va̱ sada̍ne ca̱mūṣūpe̍magma̱nnṛṣa̍yaḥ sa̱pta viprā̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 3

pra su̍me̱dhā gā̍tu̱vidvi̱śvade̍va̱: soma̍: punā̱naḥ sada̍ eti̱ nitya̍m |

bhuva̱dviśve̍ṣu̱ kāvye̍ṣu̱ rantā'nu̱ janā̍nyatate̱ pañca̱ dhīra̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 4

tava̱ tye so̍ma pavamāna ni̱ṇye viśve̍ de̱vāstraya̍ ekāda̱śāsa̍: |

daśa̍ sva̱dhābhi̱radhi̱ sāno̱ avye̍ mṛ̱janti̍ tvā na̱dya̍: sa̱pta ya̱hvīḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 5

tannu sa̱tyaṃ pava̍mānasyāstu̱ yatra̱ viśve̍ kā̱rava̍: sa̱ṃnasa̍nta |

jyoti̱ryadahne̱ akṛ̍ṇodu lo̱kaṃ prāva̱nmanu̱ṃ dasya̍ve kara̱bhīka̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 6

pari̱ sadme̍va paśu̱mānti̱ hotā̱ rājā̱ na sa̱tyaḥ sami̍tīriyā̱naḥ |

soma̍: punā̱naḥ ka̱laśā̍m̐ ayāsī̱tsīda̍nmṛ̱go na ma̍hi̱ṣo vane̍ṣu
