# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 44

**6 ayāsya āṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 1

pra ṇa̍ indo ma̱he tana̍ ū̱rmiṃ na bibhra̍darṣasi | a̱bhi de̱vām̐ a̱yāsya̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 2

ma̱tī ju̱ṣṭo dhi̱yā hi̱taḥ somo̍ hinve parā̱vati̍ | vipra̍sya̱ dhāra̍yā ka̱viḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 3

a̱yaṃ de̱veṣu̱ jāgṛ̍viḥ su̱ta e̍ti pa̱vitra̱ ā | somo̍ yāti̱ vica̍rṣaṇiḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 4

sa na̍: pavasva vāja̱yuśca̍krā̱ṇaścāru̍madhva̱ram | ba̱rhiṣmā̱m̐ ā vi̍vāsati

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 5

sa no̱ bhagā̍ya vā̱yave̱ vipra̍vīraḥ sa̱dāvṛ̍dhaḥ | somo̍ de̱veṣvā ya̍mat

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 6

sa no̍ a̱dya vasu̍ttaye kratu̱vidgā̍tu̱vitta̍maḥ | vāja̍ṃ jeṣi̱ śravo̍ bṛ̱hat
