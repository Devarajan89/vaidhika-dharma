# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 48

**5 kavirbhārgava: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 1

taṃ tvā̍ nṛ̱mṇāni̱ bibhra̍taṃ sa̱dhasthe̍ṣu ma̱ho di̱vaḥ | cāru̍ṃ sukṛ̱tyaye̍mahe

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 2

saṃvṛ̍ktadhṛṣṇumu̱kthya̍ṃ ma̱hāma̍hivrata̱ṃ mada̍m | śa̱taṃ puro̍ ruru̱kṣaṇi̍m

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 3

ata̍stvā ra̱yima̱bhi rājā̍naṃ sukrato di̱vaḥ | su̱pa̱rṇo a̍vya̱thirbha̍rat

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 4

viśva̍smā̱ itsva̍rdṛ̱śe sādhā̍raṇaṃ raja̱stura̍m | go̱pāmṛ̱tasya̱ virbha̍rat

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 5

adhā̍ hinvā̱na i̍ndri̱yaṃ jyāyo̍ mahi̱tvamā̍naśe | a̱bhi̱ṣṭi̱kṛdvica̍rṣaṇiḥ
