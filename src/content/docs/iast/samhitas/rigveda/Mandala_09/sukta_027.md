# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 27

**6 nṛmedha āṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 1

e̱ṣa ka̱vira̱bhiṣṭu̍taḥ pa̱vitre̱ adhi̍ tośate | pu̱nā̱no ghnannapa̱ sridha̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 2

e̱ṣa indrā̍ya vā̱yave̍ sva̱rjitpari̍ ṣicyate | pa̱vitre̍ dakṣa̱sādha̍naḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 3

e̱ṣa nṛbhi̱rvi nī̍yate di̱vo mū̱rdhā vṛṣā̍ su̱taḥ | somo̱ vane̍ṣu viśva̱vit

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 4

e̱ṣa ga̱vyura̍cikrada̱tpava̍māno hiraṇya̱yuḥ | indu̍: satrā̱jidastṛ̍taḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 5

e̱ṣa sūrye̍ṇa hāsate̱ pava̍māno̱ adhi̱ dyavi̍ | pa̱vitre̍ matsa̱ro mada̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 6

e̱ṣa śu̱ṣmya̍siṣyadada̱ntari̍kṣe̱ vṛṣā̱ hari̍: | pu̱nā̱na indu̱rindra̱mā
