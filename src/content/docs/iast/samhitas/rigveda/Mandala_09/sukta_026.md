# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 26

**6 idhmavāho dārḍhacyuta:  | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 1

tama̍mṛkṣanta vā̱jina̍mu̱pasthe̱ adi̍te̱radhi̍ | viprā̍so̱ aṇvyā̍ dhi̱yā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 2

taṃ gāvo̍ a̱bhya̍nūṣata sa̱hasra̍dhāra̱makṣi̍tam | indu̍ṃ dha̱rtāra̱mā di̱vaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 3

taṃ ve̱dhāṃ me̱dhayā̍hya̱npava̍māna̱madhi̱ dyavi̍ | dha̱rṇa̱siṃ bhūri̍dhāyasam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 4

tama̍hyanbhu̱rijo̍rdhi̱yā sa̱ṃvasā̍naṃ vi̱vasva̍taḥ | pati̍ṃ vā̱co adā̍bhyam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 5

taṃ sānā̱vadhi̍ jā̱mayo̱ hari̍ṃ hinva̱ntyadri̍bhiḥ | ha̱rya̱taṃ bhūri̍cakṣasam

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 6

taṃ tvā̍ hinvanti ve̱dhasa̱: pava̍māna girā̱vṛdha̍m | inda̱vindrā̍ya matsa̱ram
