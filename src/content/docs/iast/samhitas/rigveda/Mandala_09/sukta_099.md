# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 99

**8 rebhasūnū  kāśyapau | pavamāna: soma: |anuṣṭup ,1 bṛhatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 1

ā ha̍rya̱tāya̍ dhṛ̱ṣṇave̱ dhanu̍stanvanti̱ pauṃsya̍m |
śu̱krāṃ va̍ya̱ntyasu̍rāya ni̱rṇija̍ṃ vi̱pāmagre̍ mahī̱yuva̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 2

adha̍ kṣa̱pā pari̍ṣkṛto̱ vājā̍m̐ a̱bhi pra gā̍hate | yadī̍ vi̱vasva̍to̱ dhiyo̱ hari̍ṃ hi̱nvanti̱ yāta̍ve

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 3

tama̍sya marjayāmasi̱ mado̱ ya i̍ndra̱pāta̍maḥ | yaṃ gāva̍ ā̱sabhi̍rda̱dhuḥ pu̱rā nū̱naṃ ca̍ sū̱raya̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 4

taṃ gātha̍yā purā̱ṇyā pu̍nā̱nama̱bhya̍nūṣata | u̱to kṛ̍panta dhī̱tayo̍ de̱vānā̱ṃ nāma̱ bibhra̍tīḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 5

tamu̱kṣamā̍ṇama̱vyaye̱ vāre̍ punanti dharṇa̱sim | dū̱taṃ na pū̱rvaci̍ttaya̱ ā śā̍sate manī̱ṣiṇa̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 6

sa pu̍nā̱no ma̱dinta̍ma̱: soma̍śca̱mūṣu̍ sīdati | pa̱śau na reta̍ ā̱dadha̱tpati̍rvacasyate dhi̱yaḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 7

sa mṛ̍jyate su̱karma̍bhirde̱vo de̱vebhya̍: su̱taḥ | vi̱de yadā̍su saṃda̱dirma̱hīra̱po vi gā̍hate

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 8

su̱ta i̍ndo pa̱vitra̱ ā nṛbhi̍rya̱to vi nī̍yase | indrā̍ya matsa̱rinta̍maśca̱mūṣvā ni ṣī̍dasi
