# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 40

**6 bṛhanmatirāṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 1

pu̱nā̱no a̍kramīda̱bhi viśvā̱ mṛdho̱ vica̍rṣaṇiḥ | śu̱mbhanti̱ vipra̍ṃ dhī̱tibhi̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 2

ā yoni̍maru̱ṇo ru̍ha̱dgama̱dindra̱ṃ vṛṣā̍ su̱taḥ | dhru̱ve sada̍si sīdati

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 3

nū no̍ ra̱yiṃ ma̱hāmi̍ndo̱'smabhya̍ṃ soma vi̱śvata̍: | ā pa̍vasva saha̱sriṇa̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 4

viśvā̍ soma pavamāna dyu̱mnānī̍nda̱vā bha̍ra | vi̱dāḥ sa̍ha̱sriṇī̱riṣa̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 5

sa na̍: punā̱na ā bha̍ra ra̱yiṃ sto̱tre su̱vīrya̍m | ja̱ri̱turva̍rdhayā̱ gira̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 6

pu̱nā̱na i̍nda̱vā bha̍ra̱ soma̍ dvi̱barha̍saṃ ra̱yim | vṛṣa̍nnindo na u̱kthya̍m
