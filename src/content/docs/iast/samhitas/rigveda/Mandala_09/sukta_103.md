# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 103

**6 dvita āptya:  | pavamāna: soma: | uṣṇik |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 1

pra pu̍nā̱nāya̍ ve̱dhase̱ somā̍ya̱ vaca̱ udya̍tam | bhṛ̱tiṃ na bha̍rā ma̱tibhi̱rjujo̍ṣate

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 2

pari̱ vārā̍ṇya̱vyayā̱ gobhi̍rañjā̱no a̍rṣati | trī ṣa̱dhasthā̍ punā̱naḥ kṛ̍ṇute̱ hari̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 3

pari̱ kośa̍ṃ madhu̱ścuta̍ma̱vyaye̱ vāre̍ arṣati | a̱bhi vāṇī̱ṝṣī̍ṇāṃ sa̱pta nū̍ṣata

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 4

pari̍ ṇe̱tā ma̍tī̱nāṃ vi̱śvade̍vo̱ adā̍bhyaḥ | soma̍: punā̱naśca̱mvo̍rviśa̱ddhari̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 5

pari̱ daivī̱ranu̍ sva̱dhā indre̍ṇa yāhi sa̱ratha̍m | pu̱nā̱no vā̱ghadvā̱ghadbhi̱rama̍rtyaḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 6

pari̱ sapti̱rna vā̍ja̱yurde̱vo de̱vebhya̍: su̱taḥ | vyā̱na̱śiḥ pava̍māno̱ vi dhā̍vati
