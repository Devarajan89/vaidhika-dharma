# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 41

**6 medhyātithi: kāṇva: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 1

pra ye gāvo̱ na bhūrṇa̍yastve̱ṣā a̱yāso̱ akra̍muḥ | ghnanta̍: kṛ̱ṣṇāmapa̱ tvaca̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 2

su̱vi̱tasya̍ manāma̱he'ti̱ setu̍ṃ durā̱vya̍m | sā̱hvāṃso̱ dasyu̍mavra̱tam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 3

śṛ̱ṇve vṛ̱ṣṭeri̍va sva̱naḥ pava̍mānasya śu̱ṣmiṇa̍: | cara̍nti vi̱dyuto̍ di̱vi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 4

ā pa̍vasva ma̱hīmiṣa̱ṃ goma̍dindo̱ hira̍ṇyavat | aśvā̍va̱dvāja̍vatsu̱taḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 5

sa pa̍vasva vicarṣaṇa̱ ā ma̱hī roda̍sī pṛṇa | u̱ṣāḥ sūryo̱ na ra̱śmibhi̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 6

pari̍ ṇaḥ śarma̱yantyā̱ dhāra̍yā soma vi̱śvata̍: | sarā̍ ra̱seva̍ vi̱ṣṭapa̍m
