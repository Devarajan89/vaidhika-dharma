# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 72

**9 harimanta āṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: |jagatī  |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 1

hari̍ṃ mṛjantyaru̱ṣo na yu̍jyate̱ saṃ dhe̱nubhi̍: ka̱laśe̱ somo̍ ajyate |
udvāca̍mī̱raya̍ti hi̱nvate̍ ma̱tī pu̍ruṣṭu̱tasya̱ kati̍ citpari̱priya̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 2

sā̱kaṃ va̍danti ba̱havo̍ manī̱ṣiṇa̱ indra̍sya̱ soma̍ṃ ja̱ṭhare̱ yadā̍du̱huḥ |

yadī̍ mṛ̱janti̱ suga̍bhastayo̱ nara̱: sanī̍l̤ābhirda̱śabhi̱: kāmya̱ṃ madhu̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 3

ara̍mamāṇo̱ atye̍ti̱ gā a̱bhi sūrya̍sya pri̱yaṃ du̍hi̱tusti̱ro rava̍m |

anva̍smai̱ joṣa̍mabharadvinaṃgṛ̱saḥ saṃ dva̱yībhi̱: svasṛ̍bhiḥ kṣeti jā̱mibhi̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 4

nṛdhū̍to̱ adri̍ṣuto ba̱rhiṣi̍ pri̱yaḥ pati̱rgavā̍ṃ pra̱diva̱ indu̍ṝ̱tviya̍: |

pura̍ṃdhivā̱nmanu̍ṣo yajña̱sādha̍na̱: śuci̍rdhi̱yā pa̍vate̱ soma̍ indra te

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 5

nṛbā̱hubhyā̍ṃ codi̱to dhāra̍yā su̱to̍'nuṣva̱dhaṃ pa̍vate̱ soma̍ indra te |

āprā̱: kratū̱ntsama̍jairadhva̱re ma̱tīrverna dru̱ṣacca̱mvo̱3rāsa̍da̱ddhari̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 6

a̱ṃśuṃ du̍hanti sta̱naya̍nta̱makṣi̍taṃ ka̱viṃ ka̱vayo̱'paso̍ manī̱ṣiṇa̍: |

samī̱ gāvo̍ ma̱tayo̍ yanti sa̱ṃyata̍ ṛ̱tasya̱ yonā̱ sada̍ne puna̱rbhuva̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 7

nābhā̍ pṛthi̱vyā dha̱ruṇo̍ ma̱ho di̱vo̱3'pāmū̱rmau sindhu̍ṣva̱ntaru̍kṣi̱taḥ |

indra̍sya̱ vajro̍ vṛṣa̱bho vi̱bhūva̍su̱: somo̍ hṛ̱de pa̍vate̱ cāru̍ matsa̱raḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 8

sa tū pa̍vasva̱ pari̱ pārthi̍va̱ṃ raja̍: sto̱tre śikṣa̍nnādhūnva̱te ca̍ sukrato |

mā no̱ nirbhā̱gvasu̍naḥ sādana̱spṛśo̍ ra̱yiṃ pi̱śaṅga̍ṃ bahu̱laṃ va̍sīmahi

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 9

ā tū na̍ indo śa̱tadā̱tvaśvya̍ṃ sa̱hasra̍dātu paśu̱maddhira̍ṇyavat |

upa̍ māsva bṛha̱tī re̱vatī̱riṣo'dhi̍ sto̱trasya̍ pavamāna no gahi
