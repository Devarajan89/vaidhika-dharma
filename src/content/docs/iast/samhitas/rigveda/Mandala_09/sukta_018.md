# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 18

**7   kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 1

pari̍ suvā̱no gi̍ri̱ṣṭhāḥ pa̱vitre̱ somo̍ akṣāḥ | made̍ṣu sarva̱dhā a̍si

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 2

tvaṃ vipra̱stvaṃ ka̱virmadhu̱ pra jā̱tamandha̍saḥ | made̍ṣu sarva̱dhā a̍si

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 3

tava̱ viśve̍ sa̱joṣa̍so de̱vāsa̍: pī̱timā̍śata | made̍ṣu sarva̱dhā a̍si

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 4

ā yo viśvā̍ni̱ vāryā̱ vasū̍ni̱ hasta̍yorda̱dhe | made̍ṣu sarva̱dhā a̍si

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 5

ya i̱me roda̍sī ma̱hī saṃ mā̱tare̍va̱ doha̍te | made̍ṣu sarva̱dhā a̍si

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 6

pari̱ yo roda̍sī u̱bhe sa̱dyo vāje̍bhi̱rarṣa̍ti | made̍ṣu sarva̱dhā a̍si

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 7

sa śu̱ṣmī ka̱laśe̱ṣvā pu̍nā̱no a̍cikradat | made̍ṣu sarva̱dhā a̍si
