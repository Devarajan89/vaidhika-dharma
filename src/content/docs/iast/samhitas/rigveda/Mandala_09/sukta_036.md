# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 36

**6 prabhūvasurāṅgirasa: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 1

asa̍rji̱ rathyo̍ yathā pa̱vitre̍ ca̱mvo̍: su̱taḥ | kārṣma̍nvā̱jī nya̍kramīt

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 2

sa vahni̍: soma̱ jāgṛ̍vi̱: pava̍sva deva̱vīrati̍ | a̱bhi kośa̍ṃ madhu̱ścuta̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 3

sa no̱ jyotī̍ṃṣi pūrvya̱ pava̍māna̱ vi ro̍caya | kratve̱ dakṣā̍ya no hinu

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 4

śu̱mbhamā̍na ṛtā̱yubhi̍rmṛ̱jyamā̍no̱ gabha̍styoḥ | pava̍te̱ vāre̍ a̱vyaye̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 5

sa viśvā̍ dā̱śuṣe̱ vasu̱ somo̍ di̱vyāni̱ pārthi̍vā | pava̍tā̱māntari̍kṣyā

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 6

ā di̱vaspṛ̱ṣṭhama̍śva̱yurga̍vya̱yuḥ so̍ma rohasi | vī̱ra̱yuḥ śa̍vasaspate
