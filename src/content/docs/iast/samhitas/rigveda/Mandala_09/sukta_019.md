# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 19

**7   kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 1

yatso̍ma ci̱tramu̱kthya̍ṃ di̱vyaṃ pārthi̍va̱ṃ vasu̍ | tanna̍: punā̱na ā bha̍ra

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 2

yu̱vaṃ hi sthaḥ sva̍rpatī̱ indra̍śca soma̱ gopa̍tī | ī̱śā̱nā pi̍pyata̱ṃ dhiya̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 3

vṛṣā̍ punā̱na ā̱yuṣu̍ sta̱naya̱nnadhi̍ ba̱rhiṣi̍ | hari̱: sanyoni̱māsa̍dat

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 4

avā̍vaśanta dhī̱tayo̍ vṛṣa̱bhasyādhi̱ reta̍si | sū̱norva̱tsasya̍ mā̱tara̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 5

ku̱vidvṛ̍ṣa̱ṇyantī̍bhyaḥ punā̱no garbha̍mā̱dadha̍t | yāḥ śu̱kraṃ du̍ha̱te paya̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 6

upa̍ śikṣāpata̱sthuṣo̍ bhi̱yasa̱mā dhe̍hi̱ śatru̍ṣu | pava̍māna vi̱dā ra̱yim

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 7

ni śatro̍: soma̱ vṛṣṇya̱ṃ ni śuṣma̱ṃ ni vaya̍stira | dū̱re vā̍ sa̱to anti̍ vā
