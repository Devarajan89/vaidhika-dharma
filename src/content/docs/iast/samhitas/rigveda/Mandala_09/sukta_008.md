# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 8

**9 kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 1

e̱te somā̍ a̱bhi pri̱yamindra̍sya̱ kāma̍makṣaran | vardha̍nto asya vī̱rya̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 2

pu̱nā̱nāsa̍ścamū̱ṣado̱ gaccha̍nto vā̱yuma̱śvinā̍ | te no̍ dhāntu su̱vīrya̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 3

indra̍sya soma̱ rādha̍se punā̱no hārdi̍ codaya | ṛ̱tasya̱ yoni̍mā̱sada̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 4

mṛ̱janti̍ tvā̱ daśa̱ kṣipo̍ hi̱nvanti̍ sa̱pta dhī̱taya̍: | anu̱ viprā̍ amādiṣuḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 5

de̱vebhya̍stvā̱ madā̍ya̱ kaṃ sṛ̍jā̱namati̍ me̱ṣya̍: | saṃ gobhi̍rvāsayāmasi

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 6
