# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 12

**9   kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 1

somā̍ asṛgra̱minda̍vaḥ su̱tā ṛ̱tasya̱ sāda̍ne | indrā̍ya̱ madhu̍mattamāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 2

a̱bhi viprā̍ anūṣata̱ gāvo̍ va̱tsaṃ na mā̱tara̍: | indra̱ṃ soma̍sya pī̱taye̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 3

ma̱da̱cyutkṣe̍ti̱ sāda̍ne̱ sindho̍rū̱rmā vi̍pa̱ścit | somo̍ gau̱rī adhi̍ śri̱taḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 4

di̱vo nābhā̍ vicakṣa̱ṇo'vyo̱ vāre̍ mahīyate | somo̱ yaḥ su̱kratu̍: ka̱viḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 5

yaḥ soma̍: ka̱laśe̱ṣvām̐ a̱ntaḥ pa̱vitra̱ āhi̍taḥ | tamindu̱: pari̍ ṣasvaje

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 6

pra vāca̱mindu̍riṣyati samu̱drasyādhi̍ vi̱ṣṭapi̍ | jinva̱nkośa̍ṃ madhu̱ścuta̍m

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 7

nitya̍stotro̱ vana̱spati̍rdhī̱nāma̱ntaḥ sa̍ba̱rdugha̍: | hi̱nvā̱no mānu̍ṣā yu̱gā

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 8

a̱bhi pri̱yā di̱vaspa̱dā somo̍ hinvā̱no a̍rṣati | vipra̍sya̱ dhāra̍yā ka̱viḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 9

ā pa̍vamāna dhāraya ra̱yiṃ sa̱hasra̍varcasam | a̱sme i̍ndo svā̱bhuva̍m
