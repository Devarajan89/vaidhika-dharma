# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 80

**5  vasurbhāradvāja: | pavamāna: soma: |jagatī  |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 80 | **Verse:** 1

soma̍sya̱ dhārā̍ pavate nṛ̱cakṣa̍sa ṛ̱tena̍ de̱vānha̍vate di̱vaspari̍ |
bṛha̱spate̍ ra̱vathe̍nā̱ vi di̍dyute samu̱drāso̱ na sava̍nāni vivyacuḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 80 | **Verse:** 2

yaṃ tvā̍ vājinna̱ghnyā a̱bhyanū̍ṣa̱tā'yo̍hata̱ṃ yoni̱mā ro̍hasi dyu̱mān |

ma̱ghonā̱māyu̍: prati̱ranmahi̱ śrava̱ indrā̍ya soma pavase̱ vṛṣā̱ mada̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 80 | **Verse:** 3

endra̍sya ku̱kṣā pa̍vate ma̱dinta̍ma̱ ūrja̱ṃ vasā̍na̱: śrava̍se suma̱ṅgala̍: |

pra̱tyaṅsa viśvā̱ bhuva̍nā̱bhi pa̍prathe̱ krīl̤a̱nhari̱ratya̍: syandate̱ vṛṣā̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 80 | **Verse:** 4

taṃ tvā̍ de̱vebhyo̱ madhu̍mattama̱ṃ nara̍: sa̱hasra̍dhāraṃ duhate̱ daśa̱ kṣipa̍: |

nṛbhi̍: soma̱ pracyu̍to̱ grāva̍bhiḥ su̱to viśvā̍nde̱vām̐ ā pa̍vasvā sahasrajit

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 80 | **Verse:** 5

taṃ tvā̍ ha̱stino̱ madhu̍manta̱madri̍bhirdu̱hantya̱psu vṛ̍ṣa̱bhaṃ daśa̱ kṣipa̍: |

indra̍ṃ soma mā̱daya̱ndaivya̱ṃ jana̱ṃ sindho̍rivo̱rmiḥ pava̍māno arṣasi
