# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 3

**10 ājīgarti: śuna: śepa :, kṛtrimo vaiśvāmitro devarāta: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 1

e̱ṣa de̱vo ama̍rtyaḥ parṇa̱vīri̍va dīyati | a̱bhi droṇā̍nyā̱sada̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 2

e̱ṣa de̱vo vi̱pā kṛ̱to'ti̱ hvarā̍ṃsi dhāvati | pava̍māno̱ adā̍bhyaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 3

e̱ṣa de̱vo vi̍pa̱nyubhi̱: pava̍māna ṛtā̱yubhi̍: | hari̱rvājā̍ya mṛjyate

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 4

e̱ṣa viśvā̍ni̱ vāryā̱ śūro̱ yanni̍va̱ satva̍bhiḥ | pava̍mānaḥ siṣāsati

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 5

e̱ṣa de̱vo ra̍tharyati̱ pava̍māno daśasyati | ā̱viṣkṛ̍ṇoti vagva̱num

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 6

e̱ṣa viprai̍ra̱bhiṣṭu̍to̱'po de̱vo vi gā̍hate | dadha̱dratnā̍ni dā̱śuṣe̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 7

e̱ṣa diva̱ṃ vi dhā̍vati ti̱ro rajā̍ṃsi̱ dhāra̍yā | pava̍māna̱: kani̍kradat

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 8

e̱ṣa diva̱ṃ vyāsa̍ratti̱ro rajā̱ṃsyaspṛ̍taḥ | pava̍mānaḥ svadhva̱raḥ

## Verse 9
