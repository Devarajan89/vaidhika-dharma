# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 70

**reṇurvaiśvāmitra: | pavamāna: soma: |jagatī ,10 triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 70 | **Verse:** 1

trira̍smai sa̱pta dhe̱navo̍ duduhre sa̱tyāmā̱śira̍ṃ pū̱rvye vyo̍mani |
ca̱tvārya̱nyā bhuva̍nāni ni̱rṇije̱ cārū̍ṇi cakre̱ yadṛ̱tairava̍rdhata

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 70 | **Verse:** 2

sa bhikṣa̍māṇo a̱mṛta̍sya̱ cāru̍ṇa u̱bhe dyāvā̱ kāvye̍nā̱ vi śa̍śrathe |

teji̍ṣṭhā a̱po ma̱ṃhanā̱ pari̍ vyata̱ yadī̍ de̱vasya̱ śrava̍sā̱ sado̍ vi̱duḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 70 | **Verse:** 3

te a̍sya santu ke̱tavo'mṛ̍tya̱vo'dā̍bhyāso ja̱nuṣī̍ u̱bhe anu̍ |

yebhi̍rnṛ̱mṇā ca̍ de̱vyā̍ ca puna̱ta ādidrājā̍naṃ ma̱nanā̍ agṛbhṇata

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 70 | **Verse:** 4

sa mṛ̱jyamā̍no da̱śabhi̍: su̱karma̍bhi̱: pra ma̍dhya̱māsu̍ mā̱tṛṣu̍ pra̱me sacā̍ |

vra̱tāni̍ pā̱no a̱mṛta̍sya̱ cāru̍ṇa u̱bhe nṛ̱cakṣā̱ anu̍ paśyate̱ viśau̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 70 | **Verse:** 5

sa ma̍rmṛjā̱na i̍ndri̱yāya̱ dhāya̍sa̱ obhe a̱ntā roda̍sī harṣate hi̱taḥ |

vṛṣā̱ śuṣme̍ṇa bādhate̱ vi du̍rma̱tīrā̱dedi̍śānaḥ śarya̱heva̍ śu̱rudha̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 70 | **Verse:** 6

sa mā̱tarā̱ na dadṛ̍śāna u̱sriyo̱ nāna̍dadeti ma̱rutā̍miva sva̱naḥ |

jā̱nannṛ̱taṃ pra̍tha̱maṃ yatsva̍rṇara̱ṃ praśa̍staye̱ kama̍vṛṇīta su̱kratu̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 70 | **Verse:** 7

ru̱vati̍ bhī̱mo vṛ̍ṣa̱bhasta̍vi̱ṣyayā̱ śṛṅge̱ śiśā̍no̱ hari̍ṇī vicakṣa̱ṇaḥ |

ā yoni̱ṃ soma̱: sukṛ̍ta̱ṃ ni ṣī̍dati ga̱vyayī̱ tvagbha̍vati ni̱rṇiga̱vyayī̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 70 | **Verse:** 8

śuci̍: punā̱nasta̱nva̍mare̱pasa̱mavye̱ hari̱rnya̍dhāviṣṭa̱ sāna̍vi |

juṣṭo̍ mi̱trāya̱ varu̍ṇāya vā̱yave̍ tri̱dhātu̱ madhu̍ kriyate su̱karma̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 70 | **Verse:** 9

pava̍sva soma de̱vavī̍taye̱ vṛṣendra̍sya̱ hārdi̍ soma̱dhāna̱mā vi̍śa |

pu̱rā no̍ bā̱dhāddu̍ri̱tāti̍ pāraya kṣetra̱viddhi diśa̱ āhā̍ vipṛccha̱te

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 70 | **Verse:** 10

hi̱to na sapti̍ra̱bhi vāja̍ma̱rṣendra̍syendo ja̱ṭhara̱mā pa̍vasva |

nā̱vā na sindhu̱mati̍ parṣi vi̱dvāñchūro̱ na yudhya̱nnava̍ no ni̱daḥ spa̍:
