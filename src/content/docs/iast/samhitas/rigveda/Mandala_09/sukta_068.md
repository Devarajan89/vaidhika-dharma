# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 68

**10vatsaprirbhālandana: | pavamāna: soma: |jagatī ,10 triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 1

pra de̱vamacchā̱ madhu̍manta̱ inda̱vo'si̍ṣyadanta̱ gāva̱ ā na dhe̱nava̍: |
ba̱rhi̱ṣado̍ vaca̱nāva̍nta̱ ūdha̍bhiḥ pari̱sruta̍mu̱sriyā̍ ni̱rṇija̍ṃ dhire

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 2

sa roru̍vada̱bhi pūrvā̍ acikradadupā̱ruha̍: śra̱thaya̍ntsvādate̱ hari̍: |

ti̱raḥ pa̱vitra̍ṃ pari̱yannu̱ru jrayo̱ ni śaryā̍ṇi dadhate de̱va ā vara̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 3

vi yo ma̱me ya̱myā̍ saṃya̱tī mada̍: sāka̱ṃvṛdhā̱ paya̍sā pinva̱dakṣi̍tā |

ma̱hī a̍pā̱re raja̍sī vi̱vevi̍dadabhi̱vraja̱nnakṣi̍ta̱ṃ pāja̱ ā da̍de

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 4

sa mā̱tarā̍ vi̱cara̍nvā̱jaya̍nna̱paḥ pra medhi̍raḥ sva̱dhayā̍ pinvate pa̱dam |

a̱ṃśuryave̍na pipiśe ya̱to nṛbhi̱: saṃ jā̱mibhi̱rnasa̍te̱ rakṣa̍te̱ śira̍:

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 5

yūnā̍ ha̱ santā̍ pratha̱maṃ vi ja̍jñatu̱rguhā̍ hi̱taṃ jani̍ma̱ nema̱mudya̍tam

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 6

taṃ ma̍rjayanta su̱vṛdha̍ṃ na̱dīṣvām̐ u̱śanta̍ma̱ṃśuṃ pa̍ri̱yanta̍mṛ̱gmiya̍m

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 7

tvāṃ mṛ̍janti̱ daśa̱ yoṣa̍ṇaḥ su̱taṃ soma̱ ṛṣi̍bhirma̱tibhi̍rdhī̱tibhi̍rhi̱tam |

avyo̱ vāre̍bhiru̱ta de̱vahū̍tibhi̱rnṛbhi̍rya̱to vāja̱mā da̍rṣi sā̱taye̍

## Verse 8

yo dhāra̍yā̱ madhu̍mām̐ ū̱rmiṇā̍ di̱va iya̍rti̱ vāca̍ṃ rayi̱ṣāl̤ama̍rtyaḥ
