# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 21

**7   kāśyapo'sito devalo vā | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 1

e̱te dhā̍va̱ntīnda̍va̱: somā̱ indrā̍ya̱ ghṛṣva̍yaḥ | ma̱tsa̱rāsa̍: sva̱rvida̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 2

pra̱vṛ̱ṇvanto̍ abhi̱yuja̱: suṣva̍ye varivo̱vida̍: | sva̱yaṃ sto̱tre va̍ya̱skṛta̍:

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 3

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 4

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 5

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 6
