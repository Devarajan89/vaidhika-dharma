# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 34

**6 trita āptya: | pavamāna: soma: | gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 1

pra su̍vā̱no dhāra̍yā̱ tanendu̍rhinvā̱no a̍rṣati | ru̱jaddṛ̱l̤hā vyoja̍sā

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 2

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 4

## Verse 5
