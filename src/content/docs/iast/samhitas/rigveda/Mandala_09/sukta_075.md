# Maṇḍala 9 — Sūktam 75

**5 kavirbhārgava: | pavamāna: soma: |jagatī  |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 1

a̱bhi pri̱yāṇi̍ pavate̱ cano̍hito̱ nāmā̍ni ya̱hvo adhi̱ yeṣu̱ vardha̍te |
ā sūrya̍sya bṛha̱to bṛ̱hannadhi̱ ratha̱ṃ viṣva̍ñcamaruhadvicakṣa̱ṇaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 2

ṛ̱tasya̍ ji̱hvā pa̍vate̱ madhu̍ pri̱yaṃ va̱ktā pati̍rdhi̱yo a̱syā adā̍bhyaḥ |

dadhā̍ti pu̱traḥ pi̱trora̍pī̱cyaṃ1 nāma̍ tṛ̱tīya̱madhi̍ roca̱ne di̱vaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 3

ava̍ dyutā̱naḥ ka̱laśā̍m̐ acikrada̱nnṛbhi̍ryemā̱naḥ kośa̱ ā hi̍ra̱ṇyaye̍ |

a̱bhīmṛ̱tasya̍ do̱hanā̍ anūṣa̱tā'dhi̍ tripṛ̱ṣṭha u̱ṣaso̱ vi rā̍jati

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 9 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 4

romā̱ṇyavyā̍ sa̱mayā̱ vi dhā̍vati̱ madho̱rdhārā̱ pinva̍mānā di̱vedi̍ve
