# Maṇḍala 8 — Sūktam 14

**15 goṣūktyaśvasūktinau kāṇvāyanau | indra: | gāyatrī  |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 8 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 1

yadi̍ndrā̱haṃ yathā̱ tvamīśī̍ya̱ vasva̱ eka̱ it | sto̱tā me̱ goṣa̍khā syāt

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 8 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 2
