# Maṇḍala 8 — Sūktam 84

**9 uśanā kāvya: | agni: |gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 8 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 1

preṣṭha̍ṃ vo̱ ati̍thiṃ stu̱ṣe mi̱trami̍va pri̱yam | a̱gniṃ ratha̱ṃ na vedya̍m

**Mandala:** 8 | **Sukta:** 84 | **Verse:** 2
