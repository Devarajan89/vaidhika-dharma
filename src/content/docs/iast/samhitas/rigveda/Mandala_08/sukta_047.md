# Maṇḍala 8 — Sūktam 47

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 8 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 1

yamā̍dityā a̱bhi dru̱ho rakṣa̍thā̱ nema̱ghaṃ na̍śadane̱haso̍ va ū̱taya̍: su̱ūtayo̍ va ū̱taya̍:
