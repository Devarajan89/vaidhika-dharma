# Maṇḍala 8 — Sūktam 73

**18 gopavana ātreya: saptavadhrirvā | aśvinau |gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 8 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 1

udī̍rāthāmṛtāya̱te yu̱ñjāthā̍maśvinā̱ ratha̍m | anti̱ ṣadbhū̍tu vā̱mava̍:

---

**Mandala:** 8 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 2
