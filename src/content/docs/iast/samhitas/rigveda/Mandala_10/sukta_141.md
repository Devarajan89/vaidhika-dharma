# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 141

**6 agnistāpasaḥ| viśvedevāḥ| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 1

agne̱ acchā̍ vade̱ha na̍: pra̱tyaṅna̍: su̱manā̍ bhava | pra no̍ yaccha viśaspate dhana̱dā a̍si na̱stvam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 2

pra no̍ yacchatvarya̱mā pra bhaga̱: pra bṛha̱spati̍: | pra de̱vāḥ prota sū̱nṛtā̍ rā̱yo de̱vī da̍dātu naḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 3

soma̱ṃ rājā̍na̱mava̍se̱'gniṃ gī̱rbhirha̍vāmahe | ā̱di̱tyānviṣṇu̱ṃ sūrya̍ṃ bra̱hmāṇa̍ṃ ca̱ bṛha̱spati̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 4

i̱ndra̱vā̱yū bṛha̱spati̍ṃ su̱have̱ha ha̍vāmahe | yathā̍ na̱: sarva̱ ijjana̱: saṃga̍tyāṃ su̱manā̱ asa̍t

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 5

a̱rya̱maṇa̱ṃ bṛha̱spati̱mindra̱ṃ dānā̍ya codaya | vāta̱ṃ viṣṇu̱ṃ sara̍svatīṃ savi̱tāra̍ṃ ca vā̱jina̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 141 | **Verse:** 6

tvaṃ no̍ agne a̱gnibhi̱rbrahma̍ ya̱jñaṃ ca̍ vardhaya | tvaṃ no̍ de̱vatā̍taye rā̱yo dānā̍ya codaya
