# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 185

**3 satyadhṛtirvāruṇiḥ| ādityaḥ (svastyayanam)| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 185 | **Verse:** 1

mahi̍ trī̱ṇāmavo̍'stu dyu̱kṣaṃ mi̱trasyā̍rya̱mṇaḥ | du̱rā̱dharṣa̱ṃ varu̍ṇasya

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 185 | **Verse:** 2

na̱hi teṣā̍ma̱mā ca̱na nādhva̍su vāra̱ṇeṣu̍ | īśe̍ ri̱pura̱ghaśa̍ṃsaḥ

## Verse 3
