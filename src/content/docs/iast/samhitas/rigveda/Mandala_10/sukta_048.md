# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 48

**vaikuṇṭha indraḥ| indraḥ| jagatīḥ, 7, 10-11 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 1

a̱haṃ bhu̍va̱ṃ vasu̍naḥ pū̱rvyaspati̍ra̱haṃ dhanā̍ni̱ saṃ ja̍yāmi̱ śaśva̍taḥ |
māṃ ha̍vante pi̱tara̱ṃ na ja̱ntavo̱'haṃ dā̱śuṣe̱ vi bha̍jāmi̱ bhoja̍nam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 2

a̱hamindro̱ rodho̱ vakṣo̱ atha̍rvaṇastri̱tāya̱ gā a̍janaya̱mahe̱radhi̍ |

a̱haṃ dasyu̍bhya̱: pari̍ nṛ̱mṇamā da̍de go̱trā śikṣa̍ndadhī̱ce mā̍ta̱riśva̍ne

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 3

mahya̱ṃ tvaṣṭā̱ vajra̍matakṣadāya̱saṃ mayi̍ de̱vāso̍'vṛja̱nnapi̱ kratu̍m |

mamānī̍ka̱ṃ sūrya̍syeva du̱ṣṭara̱ṃ māmārya̍nti kṛ̱tena̱ kartve̍na ca

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 4

a̱hame̱taṃ ga̱vyaya̱maśvya̍ṃ pa̱śuṃ pu̍rī̱ṣiṇa̱ṃ sāya̍kenā hira̱ṇyaya̍m |

pu̱rū sa̱hasrā̱ ni śi̍śāmi dā̱śuṣe̱ yanmā̱ somā̍sa u̱kthino̱ ama̍ndiṣuḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 5

a̱hamindro̱ na parā̍ jigya̱ iddhana̱ṃ na mṛ̱tyave'va̍ tasthe̱ kadā̍ ca̱na |

soma̱minmā̍ su̱nvanto̍ yācatā̱ vasu̱ na me̍ pūravaḥ sa̱khye ri̍ṣāthana

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 6

a̱hame̱tāñchāśva̍sato̱ dvādvendra̱ṃ ye vajra̍ṃ yu̱dhaye'kṛ̍ṇvata |

ā̱hvaya̍mānā̱m̐ ava̱ hanma̍nāhanaṃ dṛ̱l̤hā vada̱nnana̍masyurnama̱svina̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 7

a̱bhī̱3dameka̱meko̍ asmi ni̱ṣṣāl̤a̱bhī dvā kimu̱ traya̍: karanti |

khale̱ na pa̱rṣānprati̍ hanmi̱ bhūri̱ kiṃ mā̍ nindanti̱ śatra̍vo'ni̱ndrāḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 8

a̱haṃ gu̱ṅgubhyo̍ atithi̱gvamiṣka̍ra̱miṣa̱ṃ na vṛ̍tra̱tura̍ṃ vi̱kṣu dhā̍rayam |

yatpa̍rṇaya̱ghna u̱ta vā̍ karañja̱he prāhaṃ ma̱he vṛ̍tra̱hatye̱ aśu̍śravi

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 9

pra me̱ namī̍ sā̱pya i̱ṣe bhu̱je bhū̱dgavā̱meṣe̍ sa̱khyā kṛ̍ṇuta dvi̱tā |

di̱dyuṃ yada̍sya sami̱theṣu̍ ma̱ṃhaya̱mādide̍na̱ṃ śaṃsya̍mu̱kthya̍ṃ karam

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 10

pra nema̍smindadṛśe̱ somo̍ a̱ntargo̱pā nema̍mā̱vira̱sthā kṛ̍ṇoti |

sa ti̱gmaśṛ̍ṅgaṃ vṛṣa̱bhaṃ yuyu̍tsandru̱hasta̍sthau bahu̱le ba̱ddho a̱ntaḥ

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 11

te mā̍ bha̱drāya̱ śava̍se tatakṣu̱rapa̍rājita̱mastṛ̍ta̱maṣā̍l̤ham
