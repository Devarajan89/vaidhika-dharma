# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 117

**9 bhikṣurāṃgirasaḥ| dhanānnadānaṃ| triṣṭup , 1-2 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 117 | **Verse:** 1

na vā u̍ de̱vāḥ kṣudha̱midva̱dhaṃ da̍duru̱tāśi̍ta̱mupa̍ gacchanti mṛ̱tyava̍: |
u̱to ra̱yiḥ pṛ̍ṇa̱to nopa̍ dasyatyu̱tāpṛ̍ṇanmarḍi̱tāra̱ṃ na vi̍ndate

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 117 | **Verse:** 2

ya ā̱dhrāya̍ cakamā̱nāya̍ pi̱tvo'nna̍vā̱ntsanra̍phi̱tāyo̍paja̱gmuṣe̍ |

sthi̱raṃ mana̍: kṛṇu̱te seva̍te pu̱roto ci̱tsa ma̍rḍi̱tāra̱ṃ na vi̍ndate

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 117 | **Verse:** 3

sa idbho̱jo yo gṛ̱have̱ dadā̱tyanna̍kāmāya̱ cara̍te kṛ̱śāya̍ |

ara̍masmai bhavati̱ yāma̍hūtā u̱tāpa̱rīṣu̍ kṛṇute̱ sakhā̍yam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 117 | **Verse:** 4

na sa sakhā̱ yo na dadā̍ti̱ sakhye̍ sacā̱bhuve̱ saca̍mānāya pi̱tvaḥ |

apā̍smā̱tpreyā̱nna tadoko̍ asti pṛ̱ṇanta̍ma̱nyamara̍ṇaṃ cidicchet

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 117 | **Verse:** 5

pṛ̱ṇī̱yādinnādha̍mānāya̱ tavyā̱ndrāghī̍yāṃsa̱manu̍ paśyeta̱ panthā̍m |

o hi varta̍nte̱ rathye̍va ca̱krā'nyama̍nya̱mupa̍ tiṣṭhanta̱ rāya̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 117 | **Verse:** 6

mogha̱manna̍ṃ vindate̱ apra̍cetāḥ sa̱tyaṃ bra̍vīmi va̱dha itsa tasya̍ |

nārya̱maṇa̱ṃ puṣya̍ti̱ no sakhā̍ya̱ṃ keva̍lāgho bhavati kevalā̱dī

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 117 | **Verse:** 7

kṛ̱ṣannitphāla̱ āśi̍taṃ kṛṇoti̱ yannadhvā̍na̱mapa̍ vṛṅkte ca̱ritrai̍: |

vada̍nbra̱hmāva̍dato̱ vanī̍yānpṛ̱ṇannā̱pirapṛ̍ṇantama̱bhi ṣyā̍t

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 117 | **Verse:** 8

eka̍pā̱dbhūyo̍ dvi̱pado̱ vi ca̍krame dvi̱pāttri̱pāda̍ma̱bhye̍ti pa̱ścāt |

catu̍ṣpādeti dvi̱padā̍mabhisva̱re sa̱mpaśya̍npa̱ṅktīru̍pa̱tiṣṭha̍mānaḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 117 | **Verse:** 9

sa̱mau ci̱ddhastau̱ na sa̱maṃ vi̍viṣṭaḥ sammā̱tarā̍ ci̱nna sa̱maṃ du̍hāte |

ya̱mayo̍ści̱nna sa̱mā vī̱ryā̍ṇi jñā̱tī ci̱tsantau̱ na sa̱maṃ pṛ̍ṇītaḥ
