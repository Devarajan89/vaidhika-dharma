# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 176

**4 sūnurārbhavaḥ| 1 ṛbhavaḥ, 2-4 agniḥ| anuṣṭup , 2 gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 176 | **Verse:** 1

pra sū̱nava̍ ṛbhū̱ṇāṃ bṛ̱hanna̍vanta vṛ̱janā̍ | kṣāmā̱ ye vi̱śvadhā̍ya̱so'śna̍ndhe̱nuṃ na mā̱tara̍m

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 176 | **Verse:** 2
