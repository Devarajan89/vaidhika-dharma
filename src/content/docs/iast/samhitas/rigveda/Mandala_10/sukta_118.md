# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 118

**9 urukṣaya āmahīyavaḥ| rakṣohā'gniḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 118 | **Verse:** 1

agne̱ haṃsi̱ nya1triṇa̱ṃ dīdya̱nmartye̱ṣvā | sve kṣaye̍ śucivrata

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 118 | **Verse:** 2

utti̍ṣṭhasi̱ svā̍huto ghṛ̱tāni̱ prati̍ modase | yattvā̱ sruca̍: sa̱masthi̍ran

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 118 | **Verse:** 3

sa āhu̍to̱ vi ro̍cate̱'gnirī̱l̤enyo̍ gi̱rā | sru̱cā pratī̍kamajyate

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 118 | **Verse:** 4

ghṛ̱tenā̱gniḥ sama̍jyate̱ madhu̍pratīka̱ āhu̍taḥ | roca̍māno vi̱bhāva̍suḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 118 | **Verse:** 5

jara̍māṇa̱: sami̍dhyase de̱vebhyo̍ havyavāhana | taṃ tvā̍ havanta̱ martyā̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 118 | **Verse:** 6

taṃ ma̍rtā̱ ama̍rtyaṃ ghṛ̱tenā̱gniṃ sa̍paryata | adā̍bhyaṃ gṛ̱hapa̍tim

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 118 | **Verse:** 7

adā̍bhyena śo̱ciṣā'gne̱ rakṣa̱stvaṃ da̍ha | go̱pā ṛ̱tasya̍ dīdihi

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 118 | **Verse:** 8

sa tvama̍gne̱ pratī̍kena̱ pratyo̍ṣa yātudhā̱nya̍: | u̱ru̱kṣaye̍ṣu̱ dīdya̍t

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 118 | **Verse:** 9

taṃ tvā̍ gī̱rbhiru̍ru̱kṣayā̍ havya̱vāha̱ṃ samī̍dhire | yaji̍ṣṭha̱ṃ mānu̍ṣe̱ jane̍
