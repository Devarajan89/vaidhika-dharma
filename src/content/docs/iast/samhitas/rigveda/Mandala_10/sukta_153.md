# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 153

**5 devajāmaya indramātaraḥ| indraḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 153 | **Verse:** 1

ī̱ṅkhaya̍ntīrapa̱syuva̱ indra̍ṃ jā̱tamupā̍sate | bhe̱jā̱nāsa̍: su̱vīrya̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 153 | **Verse:** 2

tvami̍ndra̱ balā̱dadhi̱ saha̍so jā̱ta oja̍saḥ | tvaṃ vṛ̍ṣa̱nvṛṣeda̍si

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 153 | **Verse:** 3

tvami̍ndrāsi vṛtra̱hā vya1ntari̍kṣamatiraḥ | uddyāma̍stabhnā̱ oja̍sā

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 153 | **Verse:** 4

tvami̍ndra sa̱joṣa̍sama̱rkaṃ bi̍bharṣi bā̱hvoḥ | vajra̱ṃ śiśā̍na̱ oja̍sā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 153 | **Verse:** 5

tvami̍ndrābhi̱bhūra̍si̱ viśvā̍ jā̱tānyoja̍sā | sa viśvā̱ bhuva̱ ābha̍vaḥ
