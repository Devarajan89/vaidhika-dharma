# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 131

**7 sukīrtiḥ kākṣīvat| indraḥ,4-5 aśvinau| triṣṭup , 4 anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 131 | **Verse:** 1

apa̱ prāca̍ indra̱ viśvā̍m̐ a̱mitrā̱napāpā̍co abhibhūte nudasva |
apodī̍co̱ apa̍ śūrādha̱rāca̍ u̱rau yathā̱ tava̱ śarma̱nmade̍ma

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 131 | **Verse:** 2

ku̱vida̱ṅga yava̍manto̱ yava̍ṃ ci̱dyathā̱ dāntya̍nupū̱rvaṃ vi̱yūya̍ |

i̱hehai̍ṣāṃ kṛṇuhi̱ bhoja̍nāni̱ ye ba̱rhiṣo̱ namo̍vṛkti̱ṃ na ja̱gmuḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 131 | **Verse:** 3

na̱hi sthūryṛ̍tu̱thā yā̱tamasti̱ nota śravo̍ vivide saṃga̱meṣu̍ |

ga̱vyanta̱ indra̍ṃ sa̱khyāya̱ viprā̍ aśvā̱yanto̱ vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ vā̱jaya̍ntaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 131 | **Verse:** 4

yu̱vaṃ su̱rāma̍maśvinā̱ namu̍cāvāsu̱re sacā̍ | vi̱pi̱pā̱nā śu̍bhaspatī̱ indra̱ṃ karma̍svāvatam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 131 | **Verse:** 5

pu̱trami̍va pi̱tarā̍va̱śvino̱bhendrā̱vathu̱: kāvyai̍rda̱ṃsanā̍bhiḥ |

yatsu̱rāma̱ṃ vyapi̍ba̱: śacī̍bhi̱: sara̍svatī tvā maghavannabhiṣṇak

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 131 | **Verse:** 6

indra̍: su̱trāmā̱ svavā̱m̐ avo̍bhiḥ sumṛl̤ī̱ko bha̍vatu vi̱śvave̍dāḥ |

bādha̍tā̱ṃ dveṣo̱ abha̍yaṃ kṛṇotu su̱vīrya̍sya̱ pata̍yaḥ syāma

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 131 | **Verse:** 7

tasya̍ va̱yaṃ su̍ma̱tau ya̱jñiya̱syā'pi̍ bha̱dre sau̍mana̱se syā̍ma |

sa su̱trāmā̱ svavā̱m̐ indro̍ a̱sme ā̱rācci̱ddveṣa̍: sanu̱taryu̍yotu
