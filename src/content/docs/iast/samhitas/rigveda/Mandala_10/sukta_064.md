# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 64

**17 gayaḥ plātaḥ| viśve devāḥ|jagatī, 12,16,17 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 1

ka̱thā de̱vānā̍ṃ kata̱masya̱ yāma̍ni su̱mantu̱ nāma̍ śṛṇva̱tāṃ ma̍nāmahe |
ko mṛ̍l̤āti kata̱mo no̱ maya̍skaratkata̱ma ū̱tī a̱bhyā va̍vartati

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 2

kra̱tū̱yanti̱ krata̍vo hṛ̱tsu dhī̱tayo̱ vena̍nti ve̱nāḥ pa̱taya̱ntyā diśa̍: |

na ma̍rḍi̱tā vi̍dyate a̱nya e̍bhyo de̱veṣu̍ me̱ adhi̱ kāmā̍ ayaṃsata

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 3

narā̍ vā̱ śaṃsa̍ṃ pū̱ṣaṇa̱mago̍hyama̱gniṃ de̱veddha̍ma̱bhya̍rcase gi̱rā |

sūryā̱māsā̍ ca̱ndrama̍sā ya̱maṃ di̱vi tri̱taṃ vāta̍mu̱ṣasa̍ma̱ktuma̱śvinā̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 4

ka̱thā ka̱vistu̍vī̱ravā̱nkayā̍ gi̱rā bṛha̱spati̍rvāvṛdhate suvṛ̱ktibhi̍: |

a̱ja eka̍pātsu̱have̍bhi̱ṝkva̍bhi̱rahi̍: śṛṇotu bu̱dhnyo̱3 havī̍mani

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 5

dakṣa̍sya vādite̱ janma̍ni vra̱te rājā̍nā mi̱trāvaru̱ṇā vi̍vāsasi |

atū̍rtapanthāḥ puru̱ratho̍ arya̱mā sa̱ptaho̍tā̱ viṣu̍rūpeṣu̱ janma̍su

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 6

te no̱ arva̍nto havana̱śruto̱ hava̱ṃ viśve̍ śṛṇvantu vā̱jino̍ mi̱tadra̍vaḥ |

sa̱ha̱sra̱sā me̱dhasā̍tāviva̱ tmanā̍ ma̱ho ye dhana̍ṃ sami̱theṣu̍ jabhri̱re

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 7

pra vo̍ vā̱yuṃ ra̍tha̱yuja̱ṃ pura̍ṃdhi̱ṃ stomai̍: kṛṇudhvaṃ sa̱khyāya̍ pū̱ṣaṇa̍m |

te hi de̱vasya̍ savi̱tuḥ savī̍mani̱ kratu̱ṃ saca̍nte sa̱cita̱: sace̍tasaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 8

triḥ sa̱pta sa̱srā na̱dyo̍ ma̱hīra̱po vana̱spatī̱nparva̍tām̐ a̱gnimū̱taye̍ |

kṛ̱śānu̱mastṝ̍nti̱ṣya̍ṃ sa̱dhastha̱ ā ru̱draṃ ru̱dreṣu̍ ru̱driya̍ṃ havāmahe

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 9

sara̍svatī sa̱rayu̱: sindhu̍rū̱rmibhi̍rma̱ho ma̱hīrava̱sā ya̍ntu̱ vakṣa̍ṇīḥ |

de̱vīrāpo̍ mā̱tara̍: sūdayi̱tnvo̍ ghṛ̱tava̱tpayo̱ madhu̍manno arcata

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 10

u̱ta mā̱tā bṛ̍haddi̱vā śṛ̍ṇotu na̱stvaṣṭā̍ de̱vebhi̱rjani̍bhiḥ pi̱tā vaca̍: |

ṛ̱bhu̱kṣā vājo̱ ratha̱spati̱rbhago̍ ra̱ṇvaḥ śaṃsa̍: śaśamā̱nasya̍ pātu naḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 11

ra̱ṇvaḥ saṃdṛ̍ṣṭau pitu̱mām̐ i̍va̱ kṣayo̍ bha̱drā ru̱drāṇā̍ṃ ma̱rutā̱mupa̍stutiḥ |

## Verse 13

## Verse 14
