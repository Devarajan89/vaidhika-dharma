# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 28

**(12) 1 indrasnuṣā vasukrapatnī ṛṣikā, 2,6,8,10,12 indra ṛṣiḥ, 3,4,5,7,9,11 aindro vasukra: ṛṣiḥ| 2,6,8,10,12 aindro vasukro devatā, 1,3,4,5,7,9,11 indro devatā| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 1

viśvo̱ hya1nyo a̱rirā̍ja̱gāma̱ mamedaha̱ śvaśu̍ro̱ nā ja̍gāma |
ja̱kṣī̱yāddhā̱nā u̱ta soma̍ṃ papīyā̱tsvā̍śita̱: puna̱rasta̍ṃ jagāyāt

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 2

sa roru̍vadvṛṣa̱bhasti̱gmaśṛ̍ṅgo̱ varṣma̍ntasthau̱ vari̍ma̱nnā pṛ̍thi̱vyāḥ |

viśve̍ṣvenaṃ vṛ̱jane̍ṣu pāmi̱ yo me̍ ku̱kṣī su̱taso̍maḥ pṛ̱ṇāti̍

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 3

paca̍nti te vṛṣa̱bhām̐ atsi̱ teṣā̍ṃ pṛ̱kṣeṇa̱ yanma̍ghavanhū̱yamā̍naḥ

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 4

lo̱pā̱śaḥ si̱ṃhaṃ pra̱tyañca̍matsāḥ kro̱ṣṭā va̍rā̱haṃ nira̍takta̱ kakṣā̍t

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 5

tvaṃ no̍ vi̱dvām̐ ṛ̍tu̱thā vi vo̍co̱ yamardha̍ṃ te maghavankṣe̱myā dhūḥ

## Verse 6

pu̱rū sa̱hasrā̱ ni śi̍śāmi sā̱kama̍śa̱truṃ hi mā̱ jani̍tā ja̱jāna̍

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 7
