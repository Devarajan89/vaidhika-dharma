# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 26

**9 aindro vimadaḥ, prājāpatyo vā, vāsukro vasukṛdvā|pūṣā| anuṣṭup ,1,4 uṣṇik|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 1

pra hyacchā̍ manī̱ṣā spā̱rhā yanti̍ ni̱yuta̍: | pra da̱srā ni̱yudra̍thaḥ pū̱ṣā a̍viṣṭu̱ māhi̍naḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 2

yasya̱ tyanma̍hi̱tvaṃ vā̱tāpya̍ma̱yaṃ jana̍: | vipra̱ ā va̍ṃsaddhī̱tibhi̱ścike̍ta suṣṭutī̱nām

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 3

sa ve̍da suṣṭutī̱nāmindu̱rna pū̱ṣā vṛṣā̍ | a̱bhi psura̍: pruṣāyati vra̱jaṃ na̱ ā pru̍ṣāyati

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 4

ma̱ṃsī̱mahi̍ tvā va̱yama̱smāka̍ṃ deva pūṣan | ma̱tī̱nāṃ ca̱ sādha̍na̱ṃ viprā̍ṇāṃ cādha̱vam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 5

pratya̍rdhirya̱jñānā̍maśvaha̱yo rathā̍nām | ṛṣi̱: sa yo manu̍rhito̱ vipra̍sya yāvayatsa̱khaḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 6

ā̱dhīṣa̍māṇāyā̱: pati̍: śu̱cāyā̍śca śu̱casya̍ ca | vā̱so̱vā̱yo'vī̍nā̱mā vāsā̍ṃsi̱ marmṛ̍jat

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 7

i̱no vājā̍nā̱ṃ pati̍ri̱naḥ pu̍ṣṭī̱nāṃ sakhā̍ | pra śmaśru̍ harya̱to dū̍dho̱dvi vṛthā̱ yo adā̍bhyaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 8

ā te̱ ratha̍sya pūṣanna̱jā dhura̍ṃ vavṛtyuḥ | viśva̍syā̱rthina̱: sakhā̍ sano̱jā ana̍pacyutaḥ
