# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 67

**12 ayāsya āṃgirasaḥ| bṛhaspati:| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 1

i̱māṃ dhiya̍ṃ sa̱ptaśī̍rṣṇīṃ pi̱tā na̍ ṛ̱tapra̍jātāṃ bṛha̱tīma̍vindat |
tu̱rīya̍ṃ svijjanayadvi̱śvaja̍nyo̱'yāsya̍ u̱kthamindrā̍ya̱ śaṃsa̍n

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 2

ṛ̱taṃ śaṃsa̍nta ṛ̱ju dīdhyā̍nā di̱vaspu̱trāso̱ asu̍rasya vī̱rāḥ |

vipra̍ṃ pa̱damaṅgi̍raso̱ dadhā̍nā ya̱jñasya̱ dhāma̍ pratha̱maṃ ma̍nanta

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 3

ha̱ṃsairi̍va̱ sakhi̍bhi̱rvāva̍dadbhiraśma̱nmayā̍ni̱ naha̍nā̱ vyasya̍n |

bṛha̱spati̍rabhi̱kani̍krada̱dgā u̱ta prāstau̱ducca̍ vi̱dvām̐ a̍gāyat

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 4

a̱vo dvābhyā̍ṃ pa̱ra eka̍yā̱ gā guhā̱ tiṣṭha̍ntī̱ranṛ̍tasya̱ setau̍ |

bṛha̱spati̱stama̍si̱ jyoti̍ri̱cchannudu̱srā āka̱rvi hi ti̱sra āva̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 5

vi̱bhidyā̱ pura̍ṃ śa̱yathe̱mapā̍cī̱ṃ nistrīṇi̍ sā̱kamu̍da̱dhera̍kṛntat |

bṛha̱spati̍ru̱ṣasa̱ṃ sūrya̱ṃ gāma̱rkaṃ vi̍veda sta̱naya̍nniva̱ dyauḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 6

indro̍ va̱laṃ ra̍kṣi̱tāra̱ṃ dughā̍nāṃ ka̱reṇe̍va̱ vi ca̍kartā̱ rave̍ṇa |

svedā̍ñjibhirā̱śira̍mi̱cchamā̱no'ro̍dayatpa̱ṇimā gā a̍muṣṇāt

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 7

sa ī̍ṃ sa̱tyebhi̱: sakhi̍bhiḥ śu̱cadbhi̱rgodhā̍yasa̱ṃ vi dha̍na̱saira̍dardaḥ |

brahma̍ṇa̱spati̱rvṛṣa̍bhirva̱rāhai̍rgha̱rmasve̍debhi̱rdravi̍ṇa̱ṃ vyā̍naṭ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 8

te sa̱tyena̱ mana̍sā̱ gopa̍ti̱ṃ gā i̍yā̱nāsa̍ iṣaṇayanta dhī̱bhiḥ |

bṛha̱spati̍rmi̱thoa̍vadyapebhi̱rudu̱sriyā̍ asṛjata sva̱yugbhi̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 9

taṃ va̱rdhaya̍nto ma̱tibhi̍: śi̱vābhi̍: si̱ṃhami̍va̱ nāna̍dataṃ sa̱dhasthe̍ |

bṛha̱spati̱ṃ vṛṣa̍ṇa̱ṃ śūra̍sātau̱ bhare̍bhare̱ anu̍ madema ji̱ṣṇum

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 10

ya̱dā vāja̱masa̍nadvi̱śvarū̍pa̱mā dyāmaru̍kṣa̱dutta̍rāṇi̱ sadma̍ |

bṛha̱spati̱ṃ vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ va̱rdhaya̍nto̱ nānā̱ santo̱ bibhra̍to̱ jyoti̍rā̱sā

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 11

sa̱tyāmā̱śiṣa̍ṃ kṛṇutā vayo̱dhai kī̱riṃ ci̱ddhyava̍tha̱ svebhi̱revai̍: |

pa̱ścā mṛdho̱ apa̍ bhavantu̱ viśvā̱stadro̍dasī śṛṇutaṃ viśvami̱nve

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 12

indro̍ ma̱hnā ma̍ha̱to a̍rṇa̱vasya̱ vi mū̱rdhāna̍mabhinadarbu̱dasya̍ |

aha̱nnahi̱mari̍ṇātsa̱pta sindhū̍nde̱vairdyā̍vāpṛthivī̱ prāva̍taṃ naḥ
