# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 164

**5 pracetā āṃgirasaḥ| du:svapnanāśanam| anuṣṭup , 3 triṣṭup, 5 paṃktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 164 | **Verse:** 1

ape̍hi manasaspa̱te'pa̍ krāma pa̱raśca̍ra | pa̱ro niṝ̍tyā̱ ā ca̍kṣva bahu̱dhā jīva̍to̱ mana̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 164 | **Verse:** 2

bha̱draṃ vai vara̍ṃ vṛṇate bha̱draṃ yu̍ñjanti̱ dakṣi̍ṇam | bha̱draṃ vai̍vasva̱te cakṣu̍rbahu̱trā jīva̍to̱ mana̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 164 | **Verse:** 3

yadā̱śasā̍ ni̱:śasā̍bhi̱śaso̍pāri̱ma jāgra̍to̱ yatsva̱panta̍: |

a̱gnirviśvā̱nyapa̍ duṣkṛ̱tānyaju̍ṣṭānyā̱re a̱smadda̍dhātu

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 164 | **Verse:** 4

yadi̍ndra brahmaṇaspate'bhidro̱haṃ carā̍masi | prace̍tā na āṅgira̱so dvi̍ṣa̱tāṃ pā̱tvaṃha̍saḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 164 | **Verse:** 5

ajai̍ṣmā̱dyāsa̍nāma̱ cābhū̱mānā̍gaso va̱yam |

jā̱gra̱tsva̱pnaḥ sa̍ṃka̱lpaḥ pā̱po yaṃ dvi̱ṣmastaṃ sa ṛ̍cchatu̱ yo no̱ dveṣṭi̱ tamṛ̍cchatu
