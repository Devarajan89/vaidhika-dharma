# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 161

**5 prājāpatyo yakṣmanāśanaḥ|indrāgnī, rājayakṣmaghnaṃ vā| triṣṭup , 5 anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 1

mu̱ñcāmi̍ tvā ha̱viṣā̱ jīva̍nāya̱ kama̍jñātaya̱kṣmādu̱ta rā̍jaya̱kṣmāt |
grāhi̍rja̱grāha̱ yadi̍ vai̱tade̍na̱ṃ tasyā̍ indrāgnī̱ pra mu̍muktamenam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 2

yadi̍ kṣi̱tāyu̱ryadi̍ vā̱ pare̍to̱ yadi̍ mṛ̱tyora̍nti̱kaṃ nī̍ta e̱va |

tamā ha̍rāmi̱ niṝ̍teru̱pasthā̱daspā̍rṣamenaṃ śa̱taśā̍radāya

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 3

sa̱ha̱srā̱kṣeṇa̍ śa̱taśā̍radena śa̱tāyu̍ṣā ha̱viṣāhā̍rṣamenam |

śa̱taṃ yathe̱maṃ śa̱rado̱ nayā̱tīndro̱ viśva̍sya duri̱tasya̍ pā̱ram

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 4

śa̱taṃ jī̍va śa̱rado̱ vardha̍mānaḥ śa̱taṃ he̍ma̱ntāñcha̱tamu̍ vasa̱ntān |

śa̱tami̍ndrā̱gnī sa̍vi̱tā bṛha̱spati̍: śa̱tāyu̍ṣā ha̱viṣe̱maṃ puna̍rduḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 5

āhā̍rṣa̱ṃ tvāvi̍daṃ tvā̱ puna̱rāgā̍: punarnava | sarvā̍ṅga̱ sarva̍ṃ te̱ cakṣu̱: sarva̱māyu̍śca te'vidam
