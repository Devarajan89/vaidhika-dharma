# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 32

**9 kavaṣa ailūṣaḥ| indraḥ| jagatī, 6-9 triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 1

pra su gmantā̍ dhiyasā̱nasya̍ sa̱kṣaṇi̍ va̱rebhi̍rva̱rām̐ a̱bhi ṣu pra̱sīda̍taḥ |
a̱smāka̱mindra̍ u̱bhaya̍ṃ jujoṣati̱ yatso̱myasyāndha̍so̱ bubo̍dhati

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 2

vī̍ndra yāsi di̱vyāni̍ roca̱nā vi pārthi̍vāni̱ raja̍sā puruṣṭuta |

ye tvā̱ vaha̍nti̱ muhu̍radhva̱rām̐ upa̱ te su va̍nvantu vagva̱nām̐ a̍rā̱dhasa̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 3

tadinme̍ chantsa̱dvapu̍ṣo̱ vapu̍ṣṭaraṃ pu̱tro yajjāna̍ṃ pi̱trora̱dhīya̍ti |

jā̱yā pati̍ṃ vahati va̱gnunā̍ su̱matpu̱ṃsa idbha̱dro va̍ha̱tuḥ pari̍ṣkṛtaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 4

taditsa̱dhastha̍ma̱bhi cāru̍ dīdhaya̱ gāvo̱ yacchāsa̍nvaha̱tuṃ na dhe̱nava̍: |

mā̱tā yanmantu̍ryū̱thasya̍ pū̱rvyā'bhi vā̱ṇasya̍ sa̱ptadhā̍tu̱rijjana̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 5

pra vo'cchā̍ ririce deva̱yuṣpa̱dameko̍ ru̱drebhi̍ryāti tu̱rvaṇi̍: |

ja̱rā vā̱ yeṣva̱mṛte̍ṣu dā̱vane̱ pari̍ va̱ ūme̍bhyaḥ siñcatā̱ madhu̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 6

ni̱dhī̱yamā̍na̱mapa̍gūl̤hama̱psu pra me̍ de̱vānā̍ṃ vrata̱pā u̍vāca |

indro̍ vi̱dvām̐ anu̱ hi tvā̍ ca̱cakṣa̱ tenā̱hama̍gne̱ anu̍śiṣṭa̱ āgā̍m

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 7

akṣe̍travitkṣetra̱vida̱ṃ hyaprā̱ṭ sa praiti̍ kṣetra̱vidānu̍śiṣṭaḥ |

e̱tadvai bha̱drama̍nu̱śāsa̍nasyo̱ta sru̱tiṃ vi̍ndatyañja̱sīnā̍m

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 8

a̱dyedu̱ prāṇī̱dama̍manni̱māhā'pī̍vṛto adhayanmā̱turūdha̍: |

eme̍namāpa jari̱mā yuvā̍na̱mahe̍l̤a̱nvasu̍: su̱manā̍ babhūva

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 9

e̱tāni̍ bha̱drā ka̍laśa kriyāma̱ kuru̍śravaṇa̱ dada̍to ma̱ghāni̍ |

dā̱na idvo̍ maghavāna̱: so a̍stva̱yaṃ ca̱ somo̍ hṛ̱di yaṃ bibha̍rmi
