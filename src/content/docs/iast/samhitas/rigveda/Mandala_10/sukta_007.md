# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 7

**7 trita āptyaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 1

sva̱sti no̍ di̱vo a̍gne pṛthi̱vyā vi̱śvāyu̍rdhehi ya̱jathā̍ya deva |
sace̍mahi̱ tava̍ dasma prake̱tairu̍ru̱ṣyā ṇa̍ u̱rubhi̍rdeva̱ śaṃsai̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 2

i̱mā a̍gne ma̱taya̱stubhya̍ṃ jā̱tā gobhi̱raśvai̍ra̱bhi gṛ̍ṇanti̱ rādha̍: |

ya̱dā te̱ marto̱ anu̱ bhoga̱māna̱ḍvaso̱ dadhā̍no ma̱tibhi̍: sujāta

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 3

a̱gniṃ ma̍nye pi̱tara̍ma̱gnimā̱pima̱gniṃ bhrāta̍ra̱ṃ sada̱mitsakhā̍yam |

a̱gneranī̍kaṃ bṛha̱taḥ sa̍paryaṃ di̱vi śu̱kraṃ ya̍ja̱taṃ sūrya̍sya

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 4

si̱dhrā a̍gne̱ dhiyo̍ a̱sme sanu̍trī̱ryaṃ trāya̍se̱ dama̱ ā nitya̍hotā |

ṛ̱tāvā̱ sa ro̱hida̍śvaḥ puru̱kṣurdyubhi̍rasmā̱ aha̍bhirvā̱mama̍stu

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 5

dyubhi̍rhi̱taṃ mi̱trami̍va pra̱yoga̍ṃ pra̱tnamṛ̱tvija̍madhva̱rasya̍ jā̱ram |

bā̱hubhyā̍ma̱gnimā̱yavo̍'jananta vi̱kṣu hotā̍ra̱ṃ nya̍sādayanta

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 6

sva̱yaṃ ya̍jasva di̱vi de̍va de̱vānkiṃ te̱ pāka̍: kṛṇava̱dapra̍cetāḥ |

yathāya̍ja ṛ̱tubhi̍rdeva de̱vāne̱vā ya̍jasva ta̱nva̍ṃ sujāta

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 7

bhavā̍ no agne'vi̱tota go̱pā bhavā̍ vaya̱skṛdu̱ta no̍ vayo̱dhāḥ |

rāsvā̍ ca naḥ sumaho ha̱vyadā̍ti̱ṃ trāsvo̱ta na̍sta̱nvo̱3 apra̍yucchan
