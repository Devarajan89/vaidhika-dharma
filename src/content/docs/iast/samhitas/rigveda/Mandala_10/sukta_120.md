# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 120

**9 ātharvaṇo bṛhahivaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 120 | **Verse:** 1

tadidā̍sa̱ bhuva̍neṣu̱ jyeṣṭha̱ṃ yato̍ ja̱jña u̱grastve̱ṣanṛ̍mṇaḥ |
sa̱dyo ja̍jñā̱no ni ri̍ṇāti̱ śatrū̱nanu̱ yaṃ viśve̱ mada̱ntyūmā̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 120 | **Verse:** 2

vā̱vṛ̱dhā̱naḥ śava̍sā̱ bhūryo̍jā̱: śatru̍rdā̱sāya̍ bhi̱yasa̍ṃ dadhāti |

avya̍nacca vya̱nacca̱ sasni̱ saṃ te̍ navanta̱ prabhṛ̍tā̱ made̍ṣu

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 120 | **Verse:** 3

svā̱doḥ svādī̍yaḥ svā̱dunā̍ sṛjā̱ sama̱daḥ su madhu̱ madhu̍nā̱bhi yo̍dhīḥ

## Verse 4

## Verse 5
