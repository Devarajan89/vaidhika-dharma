# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 89

**18  reṇurvaiśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ, 5 indrāsomau| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 1

indra̍ṃ stavā̱ nṛta̍ma̱ṃ yasya̍ ma̱hnā vi̍babā̱dhe ro̍ca̱nā vi jmo antā̍n |
ā yaḥ pa̱prau ca̍rṣaṇī̱dhṛdvaro̍bhi̱: pra sindhu̍bhyo riricā̱no ma̍hi̱tvā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 2

sa sūrya̱: paryu̱rū varā̱ṃsyendro̍ vavṛtyā̱drathye̍va ca̱krā |

ati̍ṣṭhantamapa̱syaṃ1 na sarga̍ṃ kṛ̱ṣṇā tamā̍ṃsi̱ tviṣyā̍ jaghāna

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 3

sa̱mā̱nama̍smā̱ ana̍pāvṛdarca kṣma̱yā di̱vo asa̍ma̱ṃ brahma̱ navya̍m |

vi yaḥ pṛ̱ṣṭheva̱ jani̍mānya̱rya indra̍ści̱kāya̱ na sakhā̍yamī̱ṣe

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 4

indrā̍ya̱ giro̱ ani̍śitasargā a̱paḥ prera̍ya̱ṃ saga̍rasya bu̱dhnāt |

yo akṣe̍ṇeva ca̱kriyā̱ śacī̍bhi̱rviṣva̍kta̱stambha̍ pṛthi̱vīmu̱ta dyām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 5

āpā̍ntamanyustṛ̱pala̍prabharmā̱ dhuni̱: śimī̍vā̱ñcharu̍mām̐ ṛjī̱ṣī |

somo̱ viśvā̍nyata̱sā vanā̍ni̱ nārvāgindra̍ṃ prati̱mānā̍ni debhuḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 6

na yasya̱ dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī na dhanva̱ nāntari̍kṣa̱ṃ nādra̍ya̱: somo̍ akṣāḥ |

yada̍sya ma̱nyura̍dhinī̱yamā̍naḥ śṛ̱ṇāti̍ vī̱l̤u ru̱jati̍ sthi̱rāṇi̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 7

ja̱ghāna̍ vṛ̱traṃ svadhi̍ti̱rvane̍va ru̱roja̱ puro̱ ara̍da̱nna sindhū̍n |

bi̱bheda̍ gi̱riṃ nava̱minna ku̱mbhamā gā indro̍ akṛṇuta sva̱yugbhi̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 8

tvaṃ ha̱ tyadṛ̍ṇa̱yā i̍ndra̱ dhīro̱'sirna parva̍ vṛji̱nā śṛ̍ṇāsi |

pra ye mi̱trasya̱ varu̍ṇasya̱ dhāma̱ yuja̱ṃ na janā̍ mi̱nanti̍ mi̱tram

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 9

pra ye mi̱traṃ prārya̱maṇa̍ṃ du̱revā̱: pra sa̱ṃgira̱: pra varu̍ṇaṃ mi̱nanti̍ |

nya1mitre̍ṣu va̱dhami̍ndra̱ tumra̱ṃ vṛṣa̱nvṛṣā̍ṇamaru̱ṣaṃ śi̍śīhi

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 10

indro̍ di̱va indra̍ īśe pṛthi̱vyā indro̍ a̱pāmindra̱ itparva̍tānām |

indro̍ vṛ̱dhāmindra̱ inmedhi̍rāṇā̱mindra̱: kṣeme̱ yoge̱ havya̱ indra̍:

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 11

prāktubhya̱ indra̱: pra vṛ̱dho aha̍bhya̱: prāntari̍kṣā̱tpra sa̍mu̱drasya̍ dhā̱seḥ |

pra vāta̍sya̱ pratha̍sa̱: pra jmo antā̱tpra sindhu̍bhyo ririce̱ pra kṣi̱tibhya̍:

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 12

pra śośu̍catyā u̱ṣaso̱ na ke̱tura̍si̱nvā te̍ vartatāmindra he̱tiḥ |

aśme̍va vidhya di̱va ā sṛ̍jā̱nastapi̍ṣṭhena̱ heṣa̍sā̱ drogha̍mitrān

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 13

anvaha̱ māsā̱ anvidvanā̱nyanvoṣa̍dhī̱ranu̱ parva̍tāsaḥ |

anvindra̱ṃ roda̍sī vāvaśā̱ne anvāpo̍ ajihata̱ jāya̍mānam

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 14

karhi̍ svi̱tsā ta̍ indra ce̱tyāsa̍da̱ghasya̱ yadbhi̱nado̱ rakṣa̱ eṣa̍t |

mi̱tra̱kruvo̱ yacchasa̍ne̱ na gāva̍: pṛthi̱vyā ā̱pṛga̍mu̱yā śaya̍nte

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 15

śa̱trū̱yanto̍ a̱bhi ye na̍stata̱sre mahi̱ vrādha̍nta oga̱ṇāsa̍ indra |

a̱ndhenā̱mitrā̱stama̍sā sacantāṃ sujyo̱tiṣo̍ a̱ktava̱stām̐ a̱bhi ṣyu̍:

---

## Verse 16

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 16

pu̱rūṇi̱ hi tvā̱ sava̍nā̱ janā̍nā̱ṃ brahmā̍ṇi̱ manda̍ngṛṇa̱tāmṛṣī̍ṇām |

i̱māmā̱ghoṣa̱nnava̍sā̱ sahū̍tiṃ ti̱ro viśvā̱m̐ arca̍to yāhya̱rvāṅ

---

## Verse 17

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 17

e̱vā te̍ va̱yami̍ndra bhuñjatī̱nāṃ vi̱dyāma̍ sumatī̱nāṃ navā̍nām |

vi̱dyāma̱ vasto̱rava̍sā gṛ̱ṇanto̍ vi̱śvāmi̍trā u̱ta ta̍ indra nū̱nam

---

## Verse 18

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 18

śu̱naṃ hu̍vema ma̱ghavā̍na̱mindra̍ma̱sminbhare̱ nṛta̍ma̱ṃ vāja̍sātau |

śṛ̱ṇvanta̍mu̱gramū̱taye̍ sa̱matsu̱ ghnanta̍ṃ vṛ̱trāṇi̍ sa̱ṃjita̱ṃ dhanā̍nām
