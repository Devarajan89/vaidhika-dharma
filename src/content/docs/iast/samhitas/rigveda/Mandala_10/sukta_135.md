# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 135

**7 kumāro yāmāyanaḥ| yamaḥ| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 135 | **Verse:** 1

yasmi̍nvṛ̱kṣe su̍palā̱śe de̱vaiḥ sa̱mpiba̍te ya̱maḥ | atrā̍ no vi̱śpati̍: pi̱tā pu̍rā̱ṇām̐ anu̍ venati

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 135 | **Verse:** 2

pu̱rā̱ṇām̐ a̍nu̱vena̍nta̱ṃ cara̍ntaṃ pā̱payā̍mu̱yā | a̱sū̱yanna̱bhya̍cākaśa̱ṃ tasmā̍ aspṛhaya̱ṃ puna̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 135 | **Verse:** 3

yaṃ ku̍māra̱ nava̱ṃ ratha̍maca̱kraṃ mana̱sākṛ̍ṇoḥ | eke̍ṣaṃ vi̱śvata̱: prāñca̱mapa̍śya̱nnadhi̍ tiṣṭhasi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 135 | **Verse:** 4

yaṃ ku̍māra̱ prāva̍rtayo̱ ratha̱ṃ vipre̍bhya̱spari̍ | taṃ sāmānu̱ prāva̍rtata̱ sami̱to nā̱vyāhi̍tam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 135 | **Verse:** 5

kaḥ ku̍mā̱rama̍janaya̱dratha̱ṃ ko nira̍vartayat | kaḥ svi̱ttada̱dya no̍ brūyādanu̱deyī̱ yathābha̍vat

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 135 | **Verse:** 6

yathābha̍vadanu̱deyī̱ tato̱ agra̍majāyata | pu̱rastā̍dbu̱dhna āta̍taḥ pa̱ścānni̱raya̍ṇaṃ kṛ̱tam

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 135 | **Verse:** 7

i̱daṃ ya̱masya̱ sāda̍naṃ devamā̱naṃ yadu̱cyate̍ | i̱yama̍sya dhamyate nā̱l̤īra̱yaṃ gī̱rbhiḥ pari̍ṣkṛtaḥ
