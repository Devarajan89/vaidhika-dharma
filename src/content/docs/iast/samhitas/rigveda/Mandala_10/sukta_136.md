# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 136

**1 jūtiḥ, 2 vātajūtiḥ, 3 viprajūtiḥ, 4 vṛṣāṇakaḥ, 5 karikrataḥ, 6 etaśaḥ 7 ṛṣyaśrṛṃgaḥ (ete vātaraśanā munayaḥ)| keśinaḥ = agni-sūrya-vāyavaḥ| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 136 | **Verse:** 1

ke̱śya1gniṃ ke̱śī vi̱ṣaṃ ke̱śī bi̍bharti̱ roda̍sī | ke̱śī viśva̱ṃ sva̍rdṛ̱śe ke̱śīdaṃ jyoti̍rucyate

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 136 | **Verse:** 2

muna̍yo̱ vāta̍raśanāḥ pi̱śaṅgā̍ vasate̱ malā̍ | vāta̱syānu̱ dhrāji̍ṃ yanti̱ yadde̱vāso̱ avi̍kṣata

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 136 | **Verse:** 3

unma̍ditā̱ maune̍yena̱ vātā̱m̐ ā ta̍sthimā va̱yam | śarī̱reda̱smāka̍ṃ yū̱yaṃ martā̍so a̱bhi pa̍śyatha

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 136 | **Verse:** 4

a̱ntari̍kṣeṇa patati̱ viśvā̍ rū̱pāva̱cāka̍śat | muni̍rde̱vasya̍devasya̱ saukṛ̍tyāya̱ sakhā̍ hi̱taḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 136 | **Verse:** 5

vāta̱syāśvo̍ vā̱yoḥ sakhātho̍ de̱veṣi̍to̱ muni̍: | u̱bhau sa̍mu̱drāvā kṣe̍ti̱ yaśca̱ pūrva̍ u̱tāpa̍raḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 136 | **Verse:** 6

a̱psa̱rasā̍ṃ gandha̱rvāṇā̍ṃ mṛ̱gāṇā̱ṃ cara̍ṇe̱ cara̍n | ke̱śī keta̍sya vi̱dvāntsakhā̍ svā̱durma̱dinta̍maḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 136 | **Verse:** 7

vā̱yura̍smā̱ upā̍manthatpi̱naṣṭi̍ smā kunanna̱mā | ke̱śī vi̱ṣasya̱ pātre̍ṇa̱ yadru̱dreṇāpi̍batsa̱ha
