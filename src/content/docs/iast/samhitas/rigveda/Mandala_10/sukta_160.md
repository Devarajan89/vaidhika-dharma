# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 160

**5 pūraṇo vaiśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 160 | **Verse:** 1

tī̱vrasyā̱bhiva̍yaso a̱sya pā̍hi sarvara̱thā vi harī̍ i̱ha mu̍ñca |
indra̱ mā tvā̱ yaja̍mānāso a̱nye ni rī̍rama̱ntubhya̍mi̱me su̱tāsa̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 160 | **Verse:** 2

tubhya̍ṃ su̱tāstubhya̍mu̱ sotvā̍sa̱stvāṃ gira̱: śvātryā̱ ā hva̍yanti |

indre̱dama̱dya sava̍naṃ juṣā̱ṇo viśva̍sya vi̱dvām̐ i̱ha pā̍hi̱ soma̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 160 | **Verse:** 3

ya u̍śa̱tā mana̍sā̱ soma̍masmai sarvahṛ̱dā de̱vakā̍maḥ su̱noti̍ |

na gā indra̱stasya̱ parā̍ dadāti praśa̱stamiccāru̍masmai kṛṇoti

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 160 | **Verse:** 4

anu̍spaṣṭo bhavatye̱ṣo a̍sya̱ yo a̍smai re̱vānna su̱noti̱ soma̍m |

nira̍ra̱tnau ma̱ghavā̱ taṃ da̍dhāti brahma̱dviṣo̍ ha̱ntyanā̍nudiṣṭaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 160 | **Verse:** 5

a̱śvā̱yanto̍ ga̱vyanto̍ vā̱jaya̍nto̱ havā̍mahe̱ tvopa̍ganta̱vā u̍ |

ā̱bhūṣa̍ntaste suma̱tau navā̍yāṃ va̱yami̍ndra tvā śu̱naṃ hu̍vema
