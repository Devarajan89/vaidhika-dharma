# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 83

**7  manyustāpasaḥ| manyuḥ| triṣṭup , 1 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 1

yaste̍ ma̱nyo'vi̍dhadvajra sāyaka̱ saha̱ oja̍: puṣyati̱ viśva̍mānu̱ṣak |
sā̱hyāma̱ dāsa̱mārya̱ṃ tvayā̍ yu̱jā saha̍skṛtena̱ saha̍sā̱ saha̍svatā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 2

ma̱nyurindro̍ ma̱nyure̱vāsa̍ de̱vo ma̱nyurhotā̱ varu̍ṇo jā̱tave̍dāḥ |

ma̱nyuṃ viśa̍ īl̤ate̱ mānu̍ṣī̱ryāḥ pā̱hi no̍ manyo̱ tapa̍sā sa̱joṣā̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 3

a̱bhī̍hi manyo ta̱vasa̱stavī̍yā̱ntapa̍sā yu̱jā vi ja̍hi̱ śatrū̍n |

a̱mi̱tra̱hā vṛ̍tra̱hā da̍syu̱hā ca̱ viśvā̱ vasū̱nyā bha̍rā̱ tvaṃ na̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 4

tvaṃ hi ma̍nyo a̱bhibhū̍tyojāḥ svaya̱mbhūrbhāmo̍ abhimātiṣā̱haḥ |

vi̱śvaca̍rṣaṇi̱: sahu̍ri̱: sahā̍vāna̱smāsvoja̱: pṛta̍nāsu dhehi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 5

a̱bhā̱gaḥ sannapa̱ pare̍to asmi̱ tava̱ kratvā̍ tavi̱ṣasya̍ pracetaḥ |

taṃ tvā̍ manyo akra̱turji̍hīl̤ā̱haṃ svā ta̱nūrba̍la̱deyā̍ya̱ mehi̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 6

a̱yaṃ te̍ a̱smyupa̱ mehya̱rvāṅpra̍tīcī̱naḥ sa̍hure viśvadhāyaḥ |

manyo̍ vajrinna̱bhi māmā va̍vṛtsva̱ hanā̍va̱ dasyū̍m̐ru̱ta bo̍dhyā̱peḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 7

a̱bhi prehi̍ dakṣiṇa̱to bha̍vā̱ me'dhā̍ vṛ̱trāṇi̍ jaṅghanāva̱ bhūri̍ |

ju̱homi̍ te dha̱ruṇa̱ṃ madhvo̱ agra̍mu̱bhā u̍pā̱ṃśu pra̍tha̱mā pi̍bāva
