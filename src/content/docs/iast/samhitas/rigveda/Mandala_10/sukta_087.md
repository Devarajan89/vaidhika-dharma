# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 87

**25 pāyurbhāradvājaḥ|rakṣohāgniḥ|triṣṭup , 22-25 anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 1

ra̱kṣo̱haṇa̍ṃ vā̱jina̱mā ji̍gharmi mi̱traṃ prathi̍ṣṭha̱mupa̍ yāmi̱ śarma̍ |
śiśā̍no a̱gniḥ kratu̍bhi̱: sami̍ddha̱: sa no̱ divā̱ sa ri̱ṣaḥ pā̍tu̱ nakta̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 2

ayo̍daṃṣṭro a̱rciṣā̍ yātu̱dhānā̱nupa̍ spṛśa jātaveda̱: sami̍ddhaḥ |

ā ji̱hvayā̱ mūra̍devānrabhasva kra̱vyādo̍ vṛ̱ktvyapi̍ dhatsvā̱san

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 3

u̱bhobha̍yāvi̱nnupa̍ dhehi̱ daṃṣṭrā̍ hi̱ṃsraḥ śiśā̱no'va̍ra̱ṃ para̍ṃ ca |

u̱tāntari̍kṣe̱ pari̍ yāhi rāja̱ñjambhai̱: saṃ dhe̍hya̱bhi yā̍tu̱dhānā̍n

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 4

ya̱jñairiṣū̍: sa̱ṃnama̍māno agne vā̱cā śa̱lyām̐ a̱śani̍bhirdihā̱naḥ |

tābhi̍rvidhya̱ hṛda̍ye yātu̱dhānā̍npratī̱co bā̱hūnprati̍ bhaṅdhyeṣām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 5

agne̱ tvaca̍ṃ yātu̱dhāna̍sya bhindhi hi̱ṃsrāśani̱rhara̍sā hantvenam |

pra parvā̍ṇi jātavedaḥ śṛṇīhi kra̱vyātkra̍vi̱ṣṇurvi ci̍notu vṛ̱kṇam

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 6

yatre̱dānī̱ṃ paśya̍si jātaveda̱stiṣṭha̍ntamagna u̱ta vā̱ cara̍ntam |

yadvā̱ntari̍kṣe pa̱thibhi̱: pata̍nta̱ṃ tamastā̍ vidhya̱ śarvā̱ śiśā̍naḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 7

u̱tāla̍bdhaṃ spṛṇuhi jātaveda ālebhā̱nādṛ̱ṣṭibhi̍ryātu̱dhānā̍t |

agne̱ pūrvo̱ ni ja̍hi̱ śośu̍cāna ā̱māda̱: kṣviṅkā̱stama̍da̱ntvenī̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 8

i̱ha pra brū̍hi yata̱maḥ so a̍gne̱ yo yā̍tu̱dhāno̱ ya i̱daṃ kṛ̱ṇoti̍ |

tamā ra̍bhasva sa̱midhā̍ yaviṣṭha nṛ̱cakṣa̍sa̱ścakṣu̍ṣe randhayainam

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 9

tī̱kṣṇenā̍gne̱ cakṣu̍ṣā rakṣa ya̱jñaṃ prāñca̱ṃ vasu̍bhya̱: pra ṇa̍ya pracetaḥ |

hi̱ṃsraṃ rakṣā̍ṃsya̱bhi śośu̍cāna̱ṃ mā tvā̍ dabhanyātu̱dhānā̍ nṛcakṣaḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 10

nṛ̱cakṣā̱ rakṣa̱: pari̍ paśya vi̱kṣu tasya̱ trīṇi̱ prati̍ śṛṇī̱hyagrā̍ |

tasyā̍gne pṛ̱ṣṭīrhara̍sā śṛṇīhi tre̱dhā mūla̍ṃ yātu̱dhāna̍sya vṛśca

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 11

triryā̍tu̱dhāna̱: prasi̍tiṃ ta etvṛ̱taṃ yo a̍gne̱ anṛ̍tena̱ hanti̍ |

tama̱rciṣā̍ sphū̱rjaya̍ñjātavedaḥ sama̱kṣame̍naṃ gṛṇa̱te ni vṛ̍ṅdhi

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 12

tada̍gne̱ cakṣu̱: prati̍ dhehi re̱bhe śa̍phā̱ruja̱ṃ yena̱ paśya̍si yātu̱dhāna̍m |

a̱tha̱rva̱vajjyoti̍ṣā̱ daivye̍na sa̱tyaṃ dhūrva̍ntama̱cita̱ṃ nyo̍ṣa

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 13

yada̍gne a̱dya mi̍thu̱nā śapā̍to̱ yadvā̱castṛ̱ṣṭaṃ ja̱naya̍nta re̱bhāḥ |

ma̱nyormana̍saḥ śara̱vyā̱3 jāya̍te̱ yā tayā̍ vidhya̱ hṛda̍ye yātu̱dhānā̍n

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 14

parā̍ śṛṇīhi̱ tapa̍sā yātu̱dhānā̱nparā̍gne̱ rakṣo̱ hara̍sā śṛṇīhi |

parā̱rciṣā̱ mūra̍devāñchṛṇīhi̱ parā̍su̱tṛpo̍ a̱bhi śośu̍cānaḥ

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 15

parā̱dya de̱vā vṛ̍ji̱naṃ śṛ̍ṇantu pra̱tyage̍naṃ śa̱pathā̍ yantu tṛ̱ṣṭāḥ |

vā̱cāste̍na̱ṃ śara̍va ṛcchantu̱ marma̱nviśva̍syaitu̱ prasi̍tiṃ yātu̱dhāna̍:

---

## Verse 16

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 16

yaḥ pauru̍ṣeyeṇa kra̱viṣā̍ sama̱ṅkte yo aśvye̍na pa̱śunā̍ yātu̱dhāna̍: |

yo a̱ghnyāyā̱ bhara̍ti kṣī̱rama̍gne̱ teṣā̍ṃ śī̱rṣāṇi̱ hara̱sāpi̍ vṛśca

---

## Verse 17

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 17

sa̱ṃva̱tsa̱rīṇa̱ṃ paya̍ u̱sriyā̍yā̱stasya̱ māśī̍dyātu̱dhāno̍ nṛcakṣaḥ |

pī̱yūṣa̍magne yata̱mastitṛ̍psā̱ttaṃ pra̱tyañca̍ma̱rciṣā̍ vidhya̱ marma̍n

---

## Verse 18

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 18

vi̱ṣaṃ gavā̍ṃ yātu̱dhānā̍: piba̱ntvā vṛ̍ścyantā̱madi̍taye du̱revā̍: |

parai̍nānde̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā da̍dātu̱ parā̍ bhā̱gamoṣa̍dhīnāṃ jayantām

---

## Verse 19

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 19

sa̱nāda̍gne mṛṇasi yātu̱dhānā̱nna tvā̱ rakṣā̍ṃsi̱ pṛta̍nāsu jigyuḥ |

anu̍ daha sa̱hamū̍rānkra̱vyādo̱ mā te̍ he̱tyā mu̍kṣata̱ daivyā̍yāḥ

---

## Verse 20

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 20

tvaṃ no̍ agne adha̱rāduda̍ktā̱ttvaṃ pa̱ścādu̱ta ra̍kṣā pu̱rastā̍t |

prati̱ te te̍ a̱jarā̍sa̱stapi̍ṣṭhā a̱ghaśa̍ṃsa̱ṃ śośu̍cato dahantu

---

## Verse 21

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 21

pa̱ścātpu̱rastā̍dadha̱rāduda̍ktātka̱viḥ kāvye̍na̱ pari̍ pāhi rājan |

sakhe̱ sakhā̍yama̱jaro̍ jari̱mṇe'gne̱ martā̱m̐ ama̍rtya̱stvaṃ na̍:

## Verse 22

## Verse 23
