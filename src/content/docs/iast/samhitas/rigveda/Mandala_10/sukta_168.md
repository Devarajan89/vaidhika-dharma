# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 168

**4 anilo vātāyanaḥ| vāyuḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 168 | **Verse:** 1

vāta̍sya̱ nu ma̍hi̱māna̱ṃ ratha̍sya ru̱janne̍ti sta̱naya̍nnasya̱ ghoṣa̍: |
di̱vi̱spṛgyā̍tyaru̱ṇāni̍ kṛ̱ṇvannu̱to e̍ti pṛthi̱vyā re̱ṇumasya̍n

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 168 | **Verse:** 2

saṃ prera̍te̱ anu̱ vāta̍sya vi̱ṣṭhā aina̍ṃ gacchanti̱ sama̍na̱ṃ na yoṣā̍: |

tābhi̍: sa̱yuksa̱ratha̍ṃ de̱va ī̍yate̱'sya viśva̍sya̱ bhuva̍nasya̱ rājā̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 168 | **Verse:** 3

a̱ntari̍kṣe pa̱thibhi̱rīya̍māno̱ na ni vi̍śate kata̱macca̱nāha̍: |

a̱pāṃ sakhā̍ prathama̱jā ṛ̱tāvā̱ kva̍ svijjā̱taḥ kuta̱ ā ba̍bhūva

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 168 | **Verse:** 4

ā̱tmā de̱vānā̱ṃ bhuva̍nasya̱ garbho̍ yathāva̱śaṃ ca̍rati de̱va e̱ṣaḥ |

ghoṣā̱ ida̍sya śṛṇvire̱ na rū̱paṃ tasmai̱ vātā̍ya ha̱viṣā̍ vidhema
