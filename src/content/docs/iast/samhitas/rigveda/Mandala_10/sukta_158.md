# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 158

**5 cakṣuḥ sauryaḥ| sūryaḥ| gāyatrī, 2 svarāṭ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 158 | **Verse:** 1

sūryo̍ no di̱vaspā̍tu̱ vāto̍ a̱ntari̍kṣāt | a̱gnirna̱: pārthi̍vebhyaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 158 | **Verse:** 2

joṣā̍ savita̱ryasya̍ te̱ hara̍: śa̱taṃ sa̱vām̐ arha̍ti | pā̱hi no̍ di̱dyuta̱: pata̍ntyāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 158 | **Verse:** 3

cakṣu̍rno de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā cakṣu̍rna u̱ta parva̍taḥ | cakṣu̍rdhā̱tā da̍dhātu naḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 158 | **Verse:** 4

cakṣu̍rno dhehi̱ cakṣu̍ṣe̱ cakṣu̍rvi̱khyai ta̱nūbhya̍: | saṃ ce̱daṃ vi ca̍ paśyema

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 158 | **Verse:** 5

su̱sa̱ṃdṛśa̍ṃ tvā va̱yaṃ prati̍ paśyema sūrya | vi pa̍śyema nṛ̱cakṣa̍saḥ
