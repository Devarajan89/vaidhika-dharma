# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 18

**14 saṃkusuko yāmāyanaḥ| 1-4 mṛtyuḥ,5 dhātā, 6 tvaṣṭā,7-14 pitṛmedhaḥ,14 prajāpatirvā| triṣṭup , 11 prastārapaṃktiḥ, 13 jagatī 14 anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 1

para̍ṃ mṛtyo̱ anu̱ pare̍hi̱ panthā̱ṃ yaste̱ sva ita̍ro deva̱yānā̍t |
cakṣu̍ṣmate śṛṇva̱te te̍ bravīmi̱ mā na̍: pra̱jāṃ rī̍riṣo̱ mota vī̱rān

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 2

mṛ̱tyoḥ pa̱daṃ yo̱paya̍nto̱ yadaita̱ drāghī̍ya̱ āyu̍: prata̱raṃ dadhā̍nāḥ |

ā̱pyāya̍mānāḥ pra̱jayā̱ dhane̍na śu̱ddhāḥ pū̱tā bha̍vata yajñiyāsaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 3

prāñco̍ agāma nṛ̱taye̱ hasā̍ya̱ drāghī̍ya̱ āyu̍: prata̱raṃ dadhā̍nāḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 4

i̱maṃ jī̱vebhya̍: pari̱dhiṃ da̍dhāmi̱ maiṣā̱ṃ nu gā̱dapa̍ro̱ artha̍me̱tam |

śa̱taṃ jī̍vantu śa̱rada̍: purū̱cīra̱ntarmṛ̱tyuṃ da̍dhatā̱ṃ parva̍tena

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 5

yathā̱ na pūrva̱mapa̍ro̱ jahā̍tye̱vā dhā̍ta̱rāyū̍ṃṣi kalpayaiṣām

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 6

i̱ha tvaṣṭā̍ su̱jani̍mā sa̱joṣā̍ dī̱rghamāyu̍: karati jī̱vase̍ vaḥ

## Verse 7
