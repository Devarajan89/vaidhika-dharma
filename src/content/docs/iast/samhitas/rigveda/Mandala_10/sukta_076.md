# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 76

**8 sarpa airāvato jaratkarṇaḥ| grāvāṇaḥ| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 76 | **Verse:** 1

ā va̍ ṛñjasa ū̱rjāṃ vyu̍ṣṭi̱ṣvindra̍ṃ ma̱ruto̱ roda̍sī anaktana |
u̱bhe yathā̍ no̱ aha̍nī sacā̱bhuvā̱ sada̍:sado variva̱syāta̍ u̱dbhidā̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 76 | **Verse:** 2

tadu̱ śreṣṭha̱ṃ sava̍naṃ sunota̱nā'tyo̱ na hasta̍yato̱ adri̍: so̱tari̍ |

vi̱daddhya1ryo a̱bhibhū̍ti̱ pauṃsya̍ṃ ma̱ho rā̱ye ci̍ttarute̱ yadarva̍taḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 76 | **Verse:** 3

tadiddhya̍sya̱ sava̍naṃ vi̱vera̱po yathā̍ pu̱rā mana̍ve gā̱tumaśre̍t |

goa̍rṇasi tvā̱ṣṭre aśva̍nirṇiji̱ prema̍dhva̱reṣva̍dhva̱rām̐ a̍śiśrayuḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 76 | **Verse:** 4

apa̍ hata ra̱kṣaso̍ bhaṅgu̱rāva̍ta skabhā̱yata̱ niṝ̍ti̱ṃ sedha̱tāma̍tim |

ā no̍ ra̱yiṃ sarva̍vīraṃ sunotana devā̱vya̍ṃ bharata̱ śloka̍madrayaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 76 | **Verse:** 5

di̱vaści̱dā vo'ma̍vattarebhyo vi̱bhvanā̍ cidā̱śva̍pastarebhyaḥ |

vā̱yości̱dā soma̍rabhastarebhyo̱'gneści̍darca pitu̱kṛtta̍rebhyaḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 76 | **Verse:** 6

bhu̱rantu̍ no ya̱śasa̱: sotvandha̍so̱ grāvā̍ṇo vā̱cā di̱vitā̍ di̱vitma̍tā |

naro̱ yatra̍ duha̱te kāmya̱ṃ madhvā̍gho̱ṣaya̍nto a̱bhito̍ mitha̱stura̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 76 | **Verse:** 7

su̱nvanti̱ soma̍ṃ rathi̱rāso̱ adra̍yo̱ nira̍sya̱ rasa̍ṃ ga̱viṣo̍ duhanti̱ te |

du̱hantyūdha̍rupa̱seca̍nāya̱ kaṃ naro̍ ha̱vyā na ma̍rjayanta ā̱sabhi̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 76 | **Verse:** 8

e̱te na̍ra̱: svapa̍so abhūtana̱ ya indrā̍ya sunu̱tha soma̍madrayaḥ |

vā̱maṃvā̍maṃ vo di̱vyāya̱ dhāmne̱ vasu̍vasu va̱: pārthi̍vāya sunva̱te
