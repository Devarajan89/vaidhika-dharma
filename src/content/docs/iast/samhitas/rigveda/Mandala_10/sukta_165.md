# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 165

**5 naiṛrtaḥ kapotaḥ|viśvedevāḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 1

devā̍: ka̱pota̍ iṣi̱to yadi̱cchandū̱to niṝ̍tyā i̱damā̍ja̱gāma̍ |
tasmā̍ arcāma kṛ̱ṇavā̍ma̱ niṣkṛ̍ti̱ṃ śaṃ no̍ astu dvi̱pade̱ śaṃ catu̍ṣpade

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 2

śi̱vaḥ ka̱pota̍ iṣi̱to no̍ astvanā̱gā de̍vāḥ śaku̱no gṛ̱heṣu̍ |

a̱gnirhi vipro̍ ju̱ṣatā̍ṃ ha̱virna̱: pari̍ he̱tiḥ pa̱kṣiṇī̍ no vṛṇaktu

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 3

he̱tiḥ pa̱kṣiṇī̱ na da̍bhātya̱smānā̱ṣṭryāṃ pa̱daṃ kṛ̍ṇute agni̱dhāne̍ |

śaṃ no̱ gobhya̍śca̱ puru̍ṣebhyaścāstu̱ mā no̍ hiṃsīdi̱ha de̍vāḥ ka̱pota̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 4

yadulū̍ko̱ vada̍ti mo̱ghame̱tadyatka̱pota̍: pa̱dama̱gnau kṛ̱ṇoti̍ |

yasya̍ dū̱taḥ prahi̍ta e̱ṣa e̱tattasmai̍ ya̱māya̱ namo̍ astu mṛ̱tyave̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 5

ṛ̱cā ka̱pota̍ṃ nudata pra̱ṇoda̱miṣa̱ṃ mada̍nta̱: pari̱ gāṃ na̍yadhvam |

sa̱ṃyo̱paya̍nto duri̱tāni̱ viśvā̍ hi̱tvā na̱ ūrja̱ṃ pra pa̍tā̱tpati̍ṣṭhaḥ
