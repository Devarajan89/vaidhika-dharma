# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 1

**7 trita āptyaḥ agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 1

agre̍ bṛ̱hannu̱ṣasā̍mū̱rdhvo a̍sthānnirjaga̱nvāntama̍so̱ jyoti̱ṣāgā̍t |
a̱gnirbhā̱nunā̱ ruśa̍tā̱ svaṅga̱ ā jā̱to viśvā̱ sadmā̍nyaprāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 2

sa jā̱to garbho̍ asi̱ roda̍syo̱ragne̱ cāru̱rvibhṛ̍ta̱ oṣa̍dhīṣu |

ci̱traḥ śiśu̱: pari̱ tamā̍ṃsya̱ktūnpra mā̱tṛbhyo̱ adhi̱ kani̍kradadgāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 3

viṣṇu̍ri̱tthā pa̍ra̱mama̍sya vi̱dvāñjā̱to bṛ̱hanna̱bhi pā̍ti tṛ̱tīya̍m |

ā̱sā yada̍sya̱ payo̱ akra̍ta̱ svaṃ sace̍taso a̱bhya̍rca̱ntyatra̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 4

ata̍ u tvā pitu̱bhṛto̱ jani̍trīrannā̱vṛdha̱ṃ prati̍ cara̱ntyannai̍: |

tā ī̱ṃ pratye̍ṣi̱ puna̍ra̱nyarū̍pā̱ asi̱ tvaṃ vi̱kṣu mānu̍ṣīṣu̱ hotā̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 5

hotā̍raṃ ci̱trara̍thamadhva̱rasya̍ ya̱jñasya̍yajñasya ke̱tuṃ ruśa̍ntam |

pratya̍rdhiṃ de̱vasya̍devasya ma̱hnā śri̱yā tva1gnimati̍thi̱ṃ janā̍nām

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 6

sa tu vastrā̱ṇyadha̱ peśa̍nāni̱ vasā̍no a̱gnirnābhā̍ pṛthi̱vyāḥ |

a̱ru̱ṣo jā̱taḥ pa̱da il̤ā̍yāḥ pu̱rohi̍to rājanyakṣī̱ha de̱vān

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 7

ā hi dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī a̍gna u̱bhe sadā̍ pu̱tro na mā̱tarā̍ ta̱tantha̍ |

pra yā̱hyaccho̍śa̱to ya̍vi̱ṣṭhā'thā va̍ha sahasye̱ha de̱vān
