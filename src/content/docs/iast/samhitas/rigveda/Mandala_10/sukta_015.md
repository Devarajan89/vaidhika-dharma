# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 15

**14 śaṅkho yāmāyanaḥ|pitaraḥ| triṣṭup ,11 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 1

udī̍ratā̱mava̍ra̱ utparā̍sa̱ unma̍dhya̱māḥ pi̱tara̍: so̱myāsa̍: |
asu̱ṃ ya ī̱yura̍vṛ̱kā ṛ̍ta̱jñāste no̍'vantu pi̱taro̱ have̍ṣu

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 2

i̱daṃ pi̱tṛbhyo̱ namo̍ astva̱dya ye pūrvā̍so̱ ya upa̍rāsa ī̱yuḥ |

ye pārthi̍ve̱ raja̱syā niṣa̍ttā̱ ye vā̍ nū̱naṃ su̍vṛ̱janā̍su vi̱kṣu

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 3

āhaṃ pi̱tṝntsu̍vi̱datrā̍m̐ avitsi̱ napā̍taṃ ca vi̱krama̍ṇaṃ ca̱ viṣṇo̍: |

ba̱rhi̱ṣado̱ ye sva̱dhayā̍ su̱tasya̱ bhaja̍nta pi̱tvasta i̱hāga̍miṣṭhāḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 4

barhi̍ṣadaḥ pitara ū̱tya1rvāgi̱mā vo̍ ha̱vyā ca̍kṛmā ju̱ṣadhva̍m |

ta ā ga̱tāva̍sā̱ śaṃta̍me̱nā'thā̍ na̱: śaṃ yora̍ra̱po da̍dhāta

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 5

upa̍hūtāḥ pi̱tara̍: so̱myāso̍ barhi̱ṣye̍ṣu ni̱dhiṣu̍ pri̱yeṣu̍ |

ta ā ga̍mantu̱ ta i̱ha śru̍va̱ntvadhi̍ bruvantu̱ te̍'vantva̱smān

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 6

ācyā̱ jānu̍ dakṣiṇa̱to ni̱ṣadye̱maṃ ya̱jñama̱bhi gṛ̍ṇīta̱ viśve̍ |

mā hi̍ṃsiṣṭa pitara̱: kena̍ cinno̱ yadva̱ āga̍: puru̱ṣatā̱ karā̍ma

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 7

āsī̍nāso aru̱ṇīnā̍mu̱pasthe̍ ra̱yiṃ dha̍tta dā̱śuṣe̱ martyā̍ya |

pu̱trebhya̍: pitara̱stasya̱ vasva̱: pra ya̍cchata̱ ta i̱horja̍ṃ dadhāta

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 8

ye na̱: pūrve̍ pi̱tara̍: so̱myāso̍'nūhi̱re so̍mapī̱thaṃ vasi̍ṣṭhāḥ |

tebhi̍rya̱maḥ sa̍ṃrarā̱ṇo ha̱vīṃṣyu̱śannu̱śadbhi̍: pratikā̱mama̍ttu

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 9

ye tā̍tṛ̱ṣurde̍va̱trā jeha̍mānā hotrā̱vida̱: stoma̍taṣṭāso a̱rkaiḥ |

āgne̍ yāhi suvi̱datre̍bhira̱rvāṅsa̱tyaiḥ ka̱vyaiḥ pi̱tṛbhi̍rgharma̱sadbhi̍:

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 10

ye sa̱tyāso̍ havi̱rado̍ havi̱ṣpā indre̍ṇa de̱vaiḥ sa̱ratha̱ṃ dadhā̍nāḥ |

āgne̍ yāhi sa̱hasra̍ṃ devava̱ndaiḥ parai̱: pūrvai̍: pi̱tṛbhi̍rgharma̱sadbhi̍:

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 11

agni̍ṣvāttāḥ pitara̱ eha ga̍cchata̱ sada̍:sadaḥ sadata supraṇītayaḥ |

a̱ttā ha̱vīṃṣi̱ praya̍tāni ba̱rhiṣyathā̍ ra̱yiṃ sarva̍vīraṃ dadhātana

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 12

tvama̍gna īl̤i̱to jā̍tave̱do'vā̍ḍḍha̱vyāni̍ sura̱bhīṇi̍ kṛ̱tvī |

prādā̍: pi̱tṛbhya̍: sva̱dhayā̱ te a̍kṣanna̱ddhi tvaṃ de̍va̱ praya̍tā ha̱vīṃṣi̍

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 13

ye ce̱ha pi̱taro̱ ye ca̱ neha yām̐śca̍ vi̱dma yām̐ u̍ ca̱ na pra̍vi̱dma |

tvaṃ ve̍ttha̱ yati̱ te jā̍tavedaḥ sva̱dhābhi̍rya̱jñaṃ sukṛ̍taṃ juṣasva

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 14

ye a̍gnida̱gdhā ye ana̍gnidagdhā̱ madhye̍ di̱vaḥ sva̱dhayā̍ mā̱daya̍nte |

tebhi̍: sva̱rāl̤asu̍nītime̱tāṃ ya̍thāva̱śaṃ ta̱nva̍ṃ kalpayasva
