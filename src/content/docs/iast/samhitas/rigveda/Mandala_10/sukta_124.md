# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 124

**9 agni:, 1,5-9 agni-varuṇa-somāḥ| 1agniḥ,2-4 agnerātmāḥ, 5,7-8 varuṇa:, 6 somaḥ, 9 indraḥ| triṣṭup 7 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 1

i̱maṃ no̍ agna̱ upa̍ ya̱jñamehi̱ pañca̍yāmaṃ tri̱vṛta̍ṃ sa̱ptata̍ntum |
aso̍ havya̱vāl̤u̱ta na̍: puro̱gā jyoge̱va dī̱rghaṃ tama̱ āśa̍yiṣṭhāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 2

ade̍vādde̱vaḥ pra̱catā̱ guhā̱ yanpra̱paśya̍māno amṛta̱tvame̍mi |

śi̱vaṃ yatsanta̱maśi̍vo̱ jahā̍mi̱ svātsa̱khyādara̍ṇī̱ṃ nābhi̍memi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 3

paśya̍nna̱nyasyā̱ ati̍thiṃ va̱yāyā̍ ṛ̱tasya̱ dhāma̱ vi mi̍me pu̱rūṇi̍ |

śaṃsā̍mi pi̱tre asu̍rāya̱ śeva̍mayajñi̱yādya̱jñiya̍ṃ bhā̱game̍mi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 4

ba̱hvīḥ samā̍ akarama̱ntara̍smi̱nnindra̍ṃ vṛṇā̱naḥ pi̱tara̍ṃ jahāmi |

a̱gniḥ somo̱ varu̍ṇa̱ste cya̍vante pa̱ryāva̍rdrā̱ṣṭraṃ tada̍vāmyā̱yan

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 5

nirmā̍yā u̱ tye asu̍rā abhūva̱ntvaṃ ca̍ mā varuṇa kā̱mayā̍se |

ṛ̱tena̍ rāja̱nnanṛ̍taṃ vivi̱ñcanmama̍ rā̱ṣṭrasyādhi̍patya̱mehi̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 6

i̱daṃ sva̍ri̱damidā̍sa vā̱mama̱yaṃ pra̍kā̱śa u̱rva1ntari̍kṣam |

hanā̍va vṛ̱traṃ ni̱rehi̍ soma ha̱viṣṭvā̱ santa̍ṃ ha̱viṣā̍ yajāma

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 7

ka̱viḥ ka̍vi̱tvā di̱vi rū̱pamāsa̍ja̱dapra̍bhūtī̱ varu̍ṇo̱ nira̱paḥ sṛ̍jat |

kṣema̍ṃ kṛṇvā̱nā jana̍yo̱ na sindha̍va̱stā a̍sya̱ varṇa̱ṃ śuca̍yo bharibhrati

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 8

tā a̍sya̱ jyeṣṭha̍mindri̱yaṃ sa̍cante̱ tā ī̱mā kṣe̍ti sva̱dhayā̱ mada̍ntīḥ |

tā ī̱ṃ viśo̱ na rājā̍naṃ vṛṇā̱nā bī̍bha̱tsuvo̱ apa̍ vṛ̱trāda̍tiṣṭhan

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 9

bī̱bha̱tsūnā̍ṃ sa̱yuja̍ṃ ha̱ṃsamā̍hura̱pāṃ di̱vyānā̍ṃ sa̱khye cara̍ntam |

a̱nu̱ṣṭubha̱manu̍ carcū̱ryamā̍ṇa̱mindra̱ṃ ni ci̍kyuḥ ka̱vayo̍ manī̱ṣā
