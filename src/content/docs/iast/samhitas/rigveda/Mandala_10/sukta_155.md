# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 155

**5 śirimbiṭho bhāradvājaḥ| alakṣmīghnam , 2-3 brahmaṇaspatiḥ, 5 viśvedevāḥ| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 155 | **Verse:** 1

arā̍yi̱ kāṇe̱ vika̍ṭe gi̱riṃ ga̍ccha sadānve | śi̱rimbi̍ṭhasya̱ satva̍bhi̱stebhi̍ṣṭvā cātayāmasi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 155 | **Verse:** 2

ca̱tto i̱taśca̱ttāmuta̱: sarvā̍ bhrū̱ṇānyā̱ruṣī̍ | a̱rā̱yya̍ṃ brahmaṇaspate̱ tīkṣṇa̍śṛṇgodṛ̱ṣanni̍hi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 155 | **Verse:** 3

a̱do yaddāru̱ plava̍te̱ sindho̍: pā̱re a̍pūru̱ṣam | tadā ra̍bhasva durhaṇo̱ tena̍ gaccha parasta̱ram

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 155 | **Verse:** 4

yaddha̱ prācī̱raja̍ga̱ntoro̍ maṇḍūradhāṇikīḥ | ha̱tā indra̍sya̱ śatra̍va̱: sarve̍ budbu̱dayā̍śavaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 155 | **Verse:** 5

parī̱me gāma̍neṣata̱ parya̱gnima̍hṛṣata | de̱veṣva̍krata̱ śrava̱: ka i̱mām̐ ā da̍dharṣati
