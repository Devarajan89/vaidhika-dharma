# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 171

**4 iṭo bhārgavaḥ| indraḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 171 | **Verse:** 1

tvaṃ tyami̱ṭato̱ ratha̱mindra̱ prāva̍: su̱tāva̍taḥ | aśṛ̍ṇoḥ so̱mino̱ hava̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 171 | **Verse:** 2

tvaṃ ma̱khasya̱ dodha̍ta̱: śiro'va̍ tva̱co bha̍raḥ | aga̍cchaḥ so̱mino̍ gṛ̱ham

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 171 | **Verse:** 3

tvaṃ tyami̍ndra̱ martya̍māstrabu̱dhnāya̍ ve̱nyam | muhu̍: śrathnā mana̱syave̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 171 | **Verse:** 4

tvaṃ tyami̍ndra̱ sūrya̍ṃ pa̱ścā santa̍ṃ pu̱raskṛ̍dhi | de̱vānā̍ṃ citti̱ro vaśa̍m
