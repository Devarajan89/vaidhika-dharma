# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 16

**14 damano yāmāyanaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup,11-14 anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 1

maina̍magne̱ vi da̍ho̱ mābhi śo̍co̱ māsya̱ tvaca̍ṃ cikṣipo̱ mā śarī̍ram |
ya̱dā śṛ̱taṃ kṛ̱ṇavo̍ jātave̱do'the̍mena̱ṃ pra hi̍ṇutātpi̱tṛbhya̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 2

śṛ̱taṃ ya̱dā kara̍si jātave̱do'the̍mena̱ṃ pari̍ dattātpi̱tṛbhya̍: |

ya̱dā gacchā̱tyasu̍nītime̱tāmathā̍ de̱vānā̍ṃ vaśa̱nīrbha̍vāti

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 3

sūrya̱ṃ cakṣu̍rgacchatu̱ vāta̍mā̱tmā dyāṃ ca̍ gaccha pṛthi̱vīṃ ca̱ dharma̍ṇā |

a̱po vā̍ gaccha̱ yadi̱ tatra̍ te hi̱tamoṣa̍dhīṣu̱ prati̍ tiṣṭhā̱ śarī̍raiḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 4

a̱jo bhā̱gastapa̍sā̱ taṃ ta̍pasva̱ taṃ te̍ śo̱cista̍patu̱ taṃ te̍ a̱rciḥ |

yāste̍ śi̱vāsta̱nvo̍ jātaveda̱stābhi̍rvahainaṃ su̱kṛtā̍mu lo̱kam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 5

ava̍ sṛja̱ puna̍ragne pi̱tṛbhyo̱ yasta̱ āhu̍ta̱ścara̍ti sva̱dhābhi̍: |

āyu̱rvasā̍na̱ upa̍ vetu̱ śeṣa̱: saṃ ga̍cchatāṃ ta̱nvā̍ jātavedaḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 6

yatte̍ kṛ̱ṣṇaḥ śa̍ku̱na ā̍tu̱toda̍ pipī̱laḥ sa̱rpa u̱ta vā̱ śvāpa̍daḥ |

a̱gniṣṭadvi̱śvāda̍ga̱daṃ kṛ̍ṇotu̱ soma̍śca̱ yo brā̍hma̱ṇām̐ ā̍vi̱veśa̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 7

a̱gnervarma̱ pari̱ gobhi̍rvyayasva̱ saṃ prorṇu̍ṣva̱ pīva̍sā̱ meda̍sā ca |

nettvā̍ dhṛ̱ṣṇurhara̍sā̱ jarhṛ̍ṣāṇo da̱dhṛgvi̍dha̱kṣyanpa̍rya̱ṅkhayā̍te

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 8

i̱mama̍gne cama̱saṃ mā vi ji̍hvaraḥ pri̱yo de̱vānā̍mu̱ta so̱myānā̍m |

e̱ṣa yaśca̍ma̱so de̍va̱pāna̱stasmi̍nde̱vā a̱mṛtā̍ mādayante

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 9

kra̱vyāda̍ma̱gniṃ pra hi̍ṇomi dū̱raṃ ya̱marā̍jño gacchatu ripravā̱haḥ |

i̱haivāyamita̍ro jā̱tave̍dā de̱vebhyo̍ ha̱vyaṃ va̍hatu prajā̱nan

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 10

yo a̱gniḥ kra̱vyātpra̍vi̱veśa̍ vo gṛ̱hami̱maṃ paśya̱nnita̍raṃ jā̱tave̍dasam |

taṃ ha̍rāmi pitṛya̱jñāya̍ de̱vaṃ sa gha̱rmami̍nvātpara̱me sa̱dhasthe̍

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 11
