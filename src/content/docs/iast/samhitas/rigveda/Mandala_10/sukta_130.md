# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 130

**7 yajñaḥ prājāpatyaḥ| bhāvavṛttam| triṣṭup , 1 jagatī|**

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 130 | **Verse:** 1

## Verse 2
