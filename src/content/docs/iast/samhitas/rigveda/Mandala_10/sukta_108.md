# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 108

**11 paṇayo'surāḥ| saramā devatā| 2,4,6,8,10-11 saramā devaśunī ṛṣikā| paṇayo devatā| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 1

kimi̱cchantī̍ sa̱ramā̱ predamā̍naḍdū̱re hyadhvā̱ jagu̍riḥ parā̱caiḥ |
kāsmehi̍ti̱: kā pari̍takmyāsītka̱thaṃ ra̱sāyā̍ atara̱: payā̍ṃsi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 2

indra̍sya dū̱tīri̍ṣi̱tā ca̍rāmi ma̱ha i̱cchantī̍ paṇayo ni̱dhīnva̍: |

a̱ti̱ṣkado̍ bhi̱yasā̱ tanna̍ āva̱ttathā̍ ra̱sāyā̍ atara̱ṃ payā̍ṃsi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 3

kī̱dṛṅṅindra̍: sarame̱ kā dṛ̍śī̱kā yasye̱daṃ dū̱tīrasa̍raḥ parā̱kāt |

ā ca̱ gacchā̍nmi̱trame̍nā dadhā̱mā'thā̱ gavā̱ṃ gopa̍tirno bhavāti

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 4

nāhaṃ taṃ ve̍da̱ dabhya̱ṃ dabha̱tsa yasye̱daṃ dū̱tīrasa̍raṃ parā̱kāt |

na taṃ gū̍hanti sra̱vato̍ gabhī̱rā ha̱tā indre̍ṇa paṇayaḥ śayadhve

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 5

i̱mā gāva̍: sarame̱ yā aiccha̱: pari̍ di̱vo antā̍ntsubhage̱ pata̍ntī |

kasta̍ enā̱ ava̍ sṛjā̱dayu̍dhvyu̱tāsmāka̱māyu̍dhā santi ti̱gmā

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 6

a̱se̱nyā va̍: paṇayo̱ vacā̍ṃsyaniṣa̱vyāsta̱nva̍: santu pā̱pīḥ |

adhṛ̍ṣṭo va̱ eta̱vā a̍stu̱ panthā̱ bṛha̱spati̍rva ubha̱yā na mṛ̍l̤āt

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 7

a̱yaṃ ni̱dhiḥ sa̍rame̱ adri̍budhno̱ gobhi̱raśve̍bhi̱rvasu̍bhi̱rnyṛ̍ṣṭaḥ |

rakṣa̍nti̱ taṃ pa̱ṇayo̱ ye su̍go̱pā reku̍ pa̱damala̍ka̱mā ja̍gantha

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 8

eha ga̍ma̱nnṛṣa̍ya̱: soma̍śitā a̱yāsyo̱ aṅgi̍raso̱ nava̍gvāḥ |

ta e̱tamū̱rvaṃ vi bha̍janta̱ gonā̱mathai̱tadvaca̍: pa̱ṇayo̱ vama̱nnit

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 9

e̱vā ca̱ tvaṃ sa̍rama āja̱gantha̱ prabā̍dhitā̱ saha̍sā̱ daivye̍na |

svasā̍raṃ tvā kṛṇavai̱ mā puna̍rgā̱ apa̍ te̱ gavā̍ṃ subhage bhajāma

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 10

nāhaṃ ve̍da bhrātṛ̱tvaṃ no sva̍sṛ̱tvamindro̍ vidu̱raṅgi̍rasaśca gho̱rāḥ |

gokā̍mā me acchadaya̱nyadāya̱mapāta̍ ita paṇayo̱ varī̍yaḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 108 | **Verse:** 11

dū̱rami̍ta paṇayo̱ varī̍ya̱ udgāvo̍ yantu mina̱tīṝ̱tena̍ |

bṛha̱spati̱ryā avi̍nda̱nnigū̍l̤hā̱: somo̱ grāvā̍ṇa̱ ṛṣa̍yaśca̱ viprā̍:
