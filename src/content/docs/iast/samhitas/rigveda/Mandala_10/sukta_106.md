# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 106

**11 bhūtāṃśaḥ kāśyapaḥ| aśvinau| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 1

u̱bhā u̍ nū̱naṃ tadida̍rthayethe̱ vi ta̍nvāthe̱ dhiyo̱ vastrā̱pase̍va |
sa̱dhrī̱cī̱nā yāta̍ve̱ prema̍jīgaḥ su̱dine̍va̱ pṛkṣa̱ ā ta̍ṃsayethe

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 2

u̱ṣṭāre̍va̱ pharva̍reṣu śrayethe prāyo̱geva̱ śvātryā̱ śāsu̱retha̍: |

dū̱teva̱ hi ṣṭho ya̱śasā̱ jane̍ṣu̱ māpa̍ sthātaṃ mahi̱ṣevā̍va̱pānā̍t

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 3

sā̱ka̱ṃyujā̍ śaku̱nasye̍va pa̱kṣā pa̱śveva̍ ci̱trā yaju̱rā ga̍miṣṭam |

a̱gniri̍va deva̱yordī̍di̱vāṃsā̱ pari̍jmāneva yajathaḥ puru̱trā

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 4

ā̱pī vo̍ a̱sme pi̱tare̍va pu̱trogreva̍ ru̱cā nṛ̱patī̍va tu̱ryai |

irye̍va pu̱ṣṭyai ki̱raṇe̍va bhu̱jyai śru̍ṣṭī̱vāne̍va̱ hava̱mā ga̍miṣṭam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 5

vaṃsa̍geva pūṣa̱ryā̍ śi̱mbātā̍ mi̱treva̍ ṛ̱tā śa̱tarā̱ śāta̍pantā |

vāje̍vo̱ccā vaya̍sā gharmye̱ṣṭhā meṣe̍ve̱ṣā sa̍pa̱ryā̱3 purī̍ṣā

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 6

sṛ̱ṇye̍va ja̱rbharī̍ tu̱rpharī̍tū naito̱śeva̍ tu̱rpharī̍ parpha̱rīkā̍ |

u̱da̱nya̱jeva̱ jema̍nā made̱rū tā me̍ ja̱rāyva̱jara̍ṃ ma̱rāyu̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 7

pa̱jreva̱ carca̍ra̱ṃ jāra̍ṃ ma̱rāyu̱ kṣadme̱vārthe̍ṣu tartarītha ugrā |

ṛ̱bhū nāpa̍tkharama̱jrā kha̱rajru̍rvā̱yurna pa̍rpharatkṣayadrayī̱ṇām

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 8

gha̱rmeva̱ madhu̍ ja̱ṭhare̍ sa̱nerū̱ bhage̍vitā tu̱rpharī̱ phāri̱vāra̍m |

pa̱ta̱reva̍ caca̱rā ca̱ndrani̍rṇi̱ṅmana̍ṛṅgā mana̱nyā̱3 na jagmī̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 9

bṛ̱hante̍va ga̱mbhare̍ṣu prati̱ṣṭhāṃ pāde̍va gā̱dhaṃ tara̍te vidāthaḥ |

karṇe̍va̱ śāsu̱ranu̱ hi smarā̱thoṃ'śe̍va no bhajataṃ ci̱tramapna̍:

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 10

ā̱ra̱ṅga̱reva̱ madhvera̍yethe sāra̱gheva̱ gavi̍ nī̱cīna̍bāre |

kī̱nāre̍va̱ sveda̍māsiṣvidā̱nā kṣāme̍vo̱rjā sū̍yava̱sātsa̍cethe

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 106 | **Verse:** 11

ṛ̱dhyāma̱ stoma̍ṃ sanu̱yāma̱ vāja̱mā no̱ mantra̍ṃ sa̱rathe̱hopa̍ yātam |

yaśo̱ na pa̱kvaṃ madhu̱ goṣva̱ntarā bhū̱tāṃśo̍ a̱śvino̱: kāma̍maprāḥ
