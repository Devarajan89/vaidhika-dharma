# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 52

**6 saucīko'gniḥ| viśvedevāḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 1

viśve̍ devāḥ śā̱stana̍ mā̱ yathe̱ha hotā̍ vṛ̱to ma̱navai̱ yanni̱ṣadya̍ |
pra me̍ brūta bhāga̱dheya̱ṃ yathā̍ vo̱ yena̍ pa̱thā ha̱vyamā vo̱ vahā̍ni

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 2

a̱haṃ hotā̱ nya̍sīda̱ṃ yajī̍yā̱nviśve̍ de̱vā ma̱ruto̍ mā junanti |

aha̍raharaśvi̱nādhva̍ryavaṃ vāṃ bra̱hmā sa̱midbha̍vati̱ sāhu̍tirvām

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 3

a̱yaṃ yo hotā̱ kiru̱ sa ya̱masya̱ kamapyū̍he̱ yatsa̍ma̱ñjanti̍ de̱vāḥ |

aha̍raharjāyate mā̱simā̱syathā̍ de̱vā da̍dhire havya̱vāha̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 4

māṃ de̱vā da̍dhire havya̱vāha̱mapa̍mluktaṃ ba̱hu kṛ̱cchrā cara̍ntam |

a̱gnirvi̱dvānya̱jñaṃ na̍: kalpayāti̱ pañca̍yāmaṃ tri̱vṛta̍ṃ sa̱ptata̍ntum

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 5

ā vo̍ yakṣyamṛta̱tvaṃ su̱vīra̱ṃ yathā̍ vo devā̱ vari̍va̱: karā̍ṇi |

ā bā̱hvorvajra̱mindra̍sya dheyā̱mathe̱mā viśvā̱: pṛta̍nā jayāti

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 6

trīṇi̍ śa̱tā trī sa̱hasrā̍ṇya̱gniṃ tri̱ṃśacca̍ de̱vā nava̍ cāsaparyan |

aukṣa̍nghṛ̱tairastṛ̍ṇanba̱rhira̍smā̱ ādiddhotā̍ra̱ṃ nya̍sādayanta
