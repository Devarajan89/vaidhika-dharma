# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 156

**5 keturāgneyaḥ| agniḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 156 | **Verse:** 1

a̱gniṃ hi̍nvantu no̱ dhiya̱: sapti̍mā̱śumi̍vā̱jiṣu̍ | tena̍ jeṣma̱ dhana̍ṃdhanam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 156 | **Verse:** 2

yayā̱ gā ā̱karā̍mahe̱ sena̍yāgne̱ tavo̱tyā | tāṃ no̍ hinva ma̱ghatta̍ye

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 156 | **Verse:** 3

āgne̍ sthū̱raṃ ra̱yiṃ bha̍ra pṛ̱thuṃ goma̍ntama̱śvina̍m | a̱ṅdhi khaṃ va̱rtayā̍ pa̱ṇim

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 156 | **Verse:** 4

agne̱ nakṣa̍trama̱jara̱mā sūrya̍ṃ rohayo di̱vi | dadha̱jjyoti̱rjane̍bhyaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 156 | **Verse:** 5

agne̍ ke̱turvi̱śāma̍si̱ preṣṭha̱: śreṣṭha̍ upastha̱sat | bodhā̍ sto̱tre vayo̱ dadha̍t
