# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 126

**8 śailūṣiḥ kulmalabarhiṣo, vāmadevyo'homugvā| viśvedevāḥ| upariṣṭādbṛhatī,8 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 126 | **Verse:** 1

na tamaṃho̱ na du̍ri̱taṃ devā̍so aṣṭa̱ martya̍m | sa̱joṣa̍so̱ yama̍rya̱mā mi̱tro naya̍nti̱ varu̍ṇo̱ ati̱ dviṣa̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 126 | **Verse:** 2

taddhi va̱yaṃ vṛ̍ṇī̱mahe̱ varu̍ṇa̱ mitrārya̍man | yenā̱ niraṃha̍so yū̱yaṃ pā̱tha ne̱thā ca̱ martya̱mati̱ dviṣa̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 126 | **Verse:** 3

te nū̱naṃ no̱'yamū̱taye̱ varu̍ṇo mi̱tro a̍rya̱mā | nayi̍ṣṭhā u no ne̱ṣaṇi̱ parṣi̍ṣṭhā u naḥ pa̱rṣaṇyati̱ dviṣa̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 126 | **Verse:** 4

yū̱yaṃ viśva̱ṃ pari̍ pātha̱ varu̍ṇo mi̱tro a̍rya̱mā | yu̱ṣmāka̱ṃ śarma̍ṇi pri̱ye syāma̍ supraṇīta̱yo'ti̱ dviṣa̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 126 | **Verse:** 5

ā̱di̱tyāso̱ ati̱ sridho̱ varu̍ṇo mi̱tro a̍rya̱mā | u̱graṃ ma̱rudbhī̍ ru̱draṃ hu̍ve̱mendra̍ma̱gniṃ sva̱staye'ti̱ dviṣa̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 126 | **Verse:** 6

netā̍ra ū̱ ṣu ṇa̍sti̱ro varu̍ṇo mi̱tro a̍rya̱mā | ati̱ viśvā̍ni duri̱tā rājā̍naścarṣaṇī̱nāmati̱ dviṣa̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 126 | **Verse:** 7

śu̱nama̱smabhya̍mū̱taye̱ varu̍ṇo mi̱tro a̍rya̱mā | śarma̍ yacchantu sa̱pratha̍ ādi̱tyāso̱ yadīma̍he̱ ati̱ dviṣa̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 126 | **Verse:** 8

yathā̍ ha̱ tyadva̍savo gau̱rya̍ṃ citpa̱di ṣi̱tāmamu̍ñcatā yajatrāḥ |

e̱vo ṣva1smanmu̍ñcatā̱ vyaṃha̱: pra tā̍ryagne prata̱raṃ na̱ āyu̍:
