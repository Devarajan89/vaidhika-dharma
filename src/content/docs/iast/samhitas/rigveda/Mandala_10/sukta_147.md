# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 147

**5 suvedāḥ śairīṣiḥ| indraḥ| jagatī, 5 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 147 | **Verse:** 1

śratte̍ dadhāmi pratha̱māya̍ ma̱nyave'ha̱nyadvṛ̱traṃ narya̍ṃ vi̱vera̱paḥ |
u̱bhe yattvā̱ bhava̍to̱ roda̍sī̱ anu̱ reja̍te̱ śuṣmā̍tpṛthi̱vī ci̍dadrivaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 147 | **Verse:** 2

tvaṃ mā̱yābhi̍ranavadya mā̱yina̍ṃ śravasya̱tā mana̍sā vṛ̱trama̍rdayaḥ |

tvāminnaro̍ vṛṇate̱ gavi̍ṣṭiṣu̱ tvāṃ viśvā̍su̱ havyā̱sviṣṭi̍ṣu

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 147 | **Verse:** 3

aiṣu̍ cākandhi puruhūta sū̱riṣu̍ vṛ̱dhāso̱ ye ma̍ghavannāna̱śurma̱gham |

arca̍nti to̱ke tana̍ye̱ pari̍ṣṭiṣu me̱dhasā̍tā vā̱jina̱mahra̍ye̱ dhane̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 147 | **Verse:** 4

sa innu rā̱yaḥ subhṛ̍tasya cākana̱nmada̱ṃ yo a̍sya̱ raṃhya̱ṃ cike̍tati |

tvāvṛ̍dho maghavandā̱śva̍dhvaro ma̱kṣū sa vāja̍ṃ bharate̱ dhanā̱ nṛbhi̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 147 | **Verse:** 5

tvaṃ śardhā̍ya mahi̱nā gṛ̍ṇā̱na u̱ru kṛ̍dhi maghavañcha̱gdhi rā̱yaḥ |

tvaṃ no̍ mi̱tro varu̍ṇo̱ na mā̱yī pi̱tvo na da̍sma dayase vibha̱ktā
