# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 183

**3 prajāvān prājāpatyaḥ| 1 yajamānaḥ, 2 yajamānapatnī, 3 hotrāśivaḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 183 | **Verse:** 1

apa̍śyaṃ tvā̱ mana̍sā̱ ceki̍tāna̱ṃ tapa̍so jā̱taṃ tapa̍so̱ vibhū̍tam |
i̱ha pra̱jāmi̱ha ra̱yiṃ rarā̍ṇa̱: pra jā̍yasva pra̱jayā̍ putrakāma

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 183 | **Verse:** 2

apa̍śyaṃ tvā̱ mana̍sā̱ dīdhyā̍nā̱ṃ svāyā̍ṃ ta̱nū ṛtvye̱ nādha̍mānām |

upa̱ māmu̱ccā yu̍va̱tirba̍bhūyā̱: pra jā̍yasva pra̱jayā̍ putrakāme

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 183 | **Verse:** 3

a̱haṃ garbha̍madadhā̱moṣa̍dhīṣva̱haṃ viśve̍ṣu̱ bhuva̍neṣva̱ntaḥ |

a̱haṃ pra̱jā a̍janayaṃ pṛthi̱vyāma̱haṃ jani̍bhyo apa̱rīṣu̍ pu̱trān
