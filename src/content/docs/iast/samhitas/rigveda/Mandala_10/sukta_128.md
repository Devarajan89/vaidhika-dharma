# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 128

**9 vihavya āṃgirasaḥ| viśvedevāḥ| triṣṭup , 9 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 128 | **Verse:** 1

mamā̍gne̱ varco̍ viha̱veṣva̍stu va̱yaṃ tvendhā̍nāsta̱nva̍ṃ puṣema |
mahya̍ṃ namantāṃ pra̱diśa̱ścata̍sra̱stvayādhya̍kṣeṇa̱ pṛta̍nā jayema

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 128 | **Verse:** 2

mama̍ de̱vā vi̍ha̱ve sa̍ntu̱ sarva̱ indra̍vanto ma̱ruto̱ viṣṇu̍ra̱gniḥ |

mamā̱ntari̍kṣamu̱rulo̍kamastu̱ mahya̱ṃ vāta̍: pavatā̱ṃ kāme̍ a̱smin

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 128 | **Verse:** 3

mayi̍ de̱vā dravi̍ṇa̱mā ya̍jantā̱ṃ mayyā̱śīra̍stu̱ mayi̍ de̱vahū̍tiḥ |

daivyā̱ hotā̍ro vanuṣanta̱ pūrve'ri̍ṣṭāḥ syāma ta̱nvā̍ su̱vīrā̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 128 | **Verse:** 4

mahya̍ṃ yajantu̱ mama̱ yāni̍ ha̱vyā''kū̍tiḥ sa̱tyā mana̍so me astu |

eno̱ mā ni gā̍ṃ kata̱macca̱nāhaṃ viśve̍ devāso̱ adhi̍ vocatā naḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 128 | **Verse:** 5

devī̍: ṣal̤urvīru̱ru na̍: kṛṇota̱ viśve̍ devāsa i̱ha vī̍rayadhvam |

mā hā̍smahi pra̱jayā̱ mā ta̱nūbhi̱rmā ra̍dhāma dviṣa̱te so̍ma rājan

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 128 | **Verse:** 6

agne̍ ma̱nyuṃ pra̍tinu̱danpare̍ṣā̱mada̍bdho go̱pāḥ pari̍ pāhi na̱stvam |

pra̱tyañco̍ yantu ni̱guta̱: puna̱ste̱3'maiṣā̍ṃ ci̱ttaṃ pra̱budhā̱ṃ vi ne̍śat

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 128 | **Verse:** 7

dhā̱tā dhā̍tṝ̱ṇāṃ bhuva̍nasya̱ yaspati̍rde̱vaṃ trā̱tāra̍mabhimātiṣā̱ham |

i̱maṃ ya̱jñama̱śvino̱bhā bṛha̱spati̍rde̱vāḥ pā̍ntu̱ yaja̍mānaṃ nya̱rthāt

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 128 | **Verse:** 8

u̱ru̱vyacā̍ no mahi̱ṣaḥ śarma̍ yaṃsada̱sminhave̍ puruhū̱taḥ pu̍ru̱kṣuḥ |

sa na̍: pra̱jāyai̍ haryaśva mṛl̤a̱yendra̱ mā no̍ rīriṣo̱ mā parā̍ dāḥ

## Verse 9
