# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 127

**8 kuśikaḥ saubharaḥ, rātrirvā bhāradvājī| rātriḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 1

rātrī̱ vya̍khyadāya̱tī pu̍ru̱trā de̱vya1kṣabhi̍: | viśvā̱ adhi̱ śriyo̍'dhita

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 2

orva̍prā̱ ama̍rtyā ni̱vato̍ de̱vyu1dvata̍: | jyoti̍ṣā bādhate̱ tama̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 3

niru̱ svasā̍ramaskṛto̱ṣasa̍ṃ de̱vyā̍ya̱tī | apedu̍ hāsate̱ tama̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 4

sā no̍ a̱dya yasyā̍ va̱yaṃ ni te̱ yāma̱nnavi̍kṣmahi | vṛ̱kṣe na va̍sa̱tiṃ vaya̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 5

ni grāmā̍so avikṣata̱ ni pa̱dvanto̱ ni pa̱kṣiṇa̍: | ni śye̱nāsa̍ścida̱rthina̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 6

yā̱vayā̍ vṛ̱kyaṃ1 vṛka̍ṃ ya̱vaya̍ ste̱namū̍rmye | athā̍ naḥ su̱tarā̍ bhava

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 7

upa̍ mā̱ pepi̍śa̱ttama̍: kṛ̱ṣṇaṃ vya̍ktamasthita | uṣa̍ ṛ̱ṇeva̍ yātaya

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 127 | **Verse:** 8

upa̍ te̱ gā i̱vāka̍raṃ vṛṇī̱ṣva du̍hitardivaḥ | rātri̱ stoma̱ṃ na ji̱gyuṣe̍
