# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 133

**7 sudāḥ paijavanaḥ| indraḥ|śakkarī, 4-6 mahāpaṃktiḥ, 7 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 133 | **Verse:** 1

pro ṣva̍smai purora̱thamindrā̍ya śū̱ṣama̍rcata |
a̱bhīke̍ cidu loka̱kṛtsa̱ṃge sa̱matsu̍ vṛtra̱hā'smāka̍ṃ bodhi codi̱tā nabha̍ntāmanya̱keṣā̍ṃ jyā̱kā adhi̱ dhanva̍su

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 133 | **Verse:** 2

tvaṃ sindhū̱m̐ravā̍sṛjo'dha̱rāco̱ aha̱nnahi̍m |

a̱śa̱truri̍ndra jajñiṣe̱ viśva̍ṃ puṣyasi̱ vārya̱ṃ taṃ tvā̱ pari̍ ṣvajāmahe̱ nabha̍ntāmanya̱keṣā̍ṃ jyā̱kā adhi̱ dhanva̍su

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 133 | **Verse:** 3

vi ṣu viśvā̱ arā̍tayo̱'ryo na̍śanta no̱ dhiya̍: |

astā̍si̱ śatra̍ve va̱dhaṃ yo na̍ indra̱ jighā̍ṃsati̱ yā te̍ rā̱tirda̱dirvasu̱ nabha̍ntāmanya̱keṣā̍ṃ jyā̱kā adhi̱ dhanva̍su

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 133 | **Verse:** 4

yo na̍ indrā̱bhito̱ jano̍ vṛkā̱yurā̱dide̍śati |

a̱dha̱spa̱daṃ tamī̍ṃ kṛdhi vibā̱dho a̍si sāsa̱hirnabha̍ntāmanya̱keṣā̍ṃ jyā̱kā adhi̱ dhanva̍su

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 133 | **Verse:** 5

yo na̍ indrābhi̱dāsa̍ti̱ sanā̍bhi̱ryaśca̱ niṣṭya̍: |

ava̱ tasya̱ bala̍ṃ tira ma̱hīva̱ dyauradha̱ tmanā̱ nabha̍ntāmanya̱keṣā̍ṃ jyā̱kā adhi̱ dhanva̍su

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 133 | **Verse:** 6

va̱yami̍ndra tvā̱yava̍: sakhi̱tvamā ra̍bhāmahe |

ṛ̱tasya̍ naḥ pa̱thā na̱yā'ti̱ viśvā̍ni duri̱tā nabha̍ntāmanya̱keṣā̍ṃ jyā̱kā adhi̱ dhanva̍su

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 133 | **Verse:** 7

a̱smabhya̱ṃ su tvami̍ndra̱ tāṃ śi̍kṣa̱ yā doha̍te̱ prati̱ vara̍ṃ jari̱tre |
