# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 174

**5 abhīvarta āṃgirasaḥ| rājā| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 174 | **Verse:** 1

a̱bhī̱va̱rtena̍ ha̱viṣā̱ yenendro̍ abhivāvṛ̱te | tenā̱smānbra̍hmaṇaspate̱'bhi rā̱ṣṭrāya̍ vartaya

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 174 | **Verse:** 2

a̱bhi̱vṛtya̍ sa̱patnā̍na̱bhi yā no̱ arā̍tayaḥ | a̱bhi pṛ̍ta̱nyanta̍ṃ tiṣṭhā̱'bhi yo na̍ ira̱syati̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 174 | **Verse:** 3

a̱bhi tvā̍ de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā'bhi somo̍ avīvṛtat | a̱bhi tvā̱ viśvā̍ bhū̱tānya̍bhīva̱rto yathāsa̍si

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 174 | **Verse:** 4

yenendro̍ ha̱viṣā̍ kṛ̱tvyabha̍vaddyu̱mnyu̍tta̱maḥ | i̱daṃ tada̍kri devā asapa̱tnaḥ kilā̍bhuvam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 174 | **Verse:** 5

a̱sa̱pa̱tnaḥ sa̍patna̱hā'bhirā̍ṣṭro viṣāsa̱hiḥ | yathā̱hame̍ṣāṃ bhū̱tānā̍ṃ vi̱rājā̍ni̱ jana̍sya ca
