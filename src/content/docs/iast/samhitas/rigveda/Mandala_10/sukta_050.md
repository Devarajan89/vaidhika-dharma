# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 50

**7 vaikuṇṭha indraḥ| indraḥ| jagatīḥ, 3,4 abhisāriṇī, 5 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 1

pra vo̍ ma̱he manda̍mānā̱yāndha̱so'rcā̍ vi̱śvāna̍rāya viśvā̱bhuve̍ |
indra̍sya̱ yasya̱ suma̍kha̱ṃ saho̱ mahi̱ śravo̍ nṛ̱mṇaṃ ca̱ roda̍sī sapa̱ryata̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 2

so ci̱nnu sakhyā̱ narya̍ i̱naḥ stu̱taśca̱rkṛtya̱ indro̱ māva̍te̱ nare̍ |

viśvā̍su dhū̱rṣu vā̍ja̱kṛtye̍ṣu satpate vṛ̱tre vā̱psva1bhi śū̍ra mandase

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 3

ke te nara̍ indra̱ ye ta̍ i̱ṣe ye te̍ su̱mnaṃ sa̍dha̱nya1miya̍kṣān |

ke te̱ vājā̍yāsu̱ryā̍ya hinvire̱ ke a̱psu svāsū̱rvarā̍su̱ pauṃsye̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 4

bhuva̱stvami̍ndra̱ brahma̍ṇā ma̱hānbhuvo̱ viśve̍ṣu̱ sava̍neṣu ya̱jñiya̍: |

bhuvo̱ nṝm̐ścyau̱tno viśva̍smi̱nbhare̱ jyeṣṭha̍śca̱ mantro̍ viśvacarṣaṇe

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 5

avā̱ nu ka̱ṃ jyāyā̍nya̱jñava̍naso ma̱hīṃ ta̱ omā̍trāṃ kṛ̱ṣṭayo̍ viduḥ |

aso̱ nu ka̍ma̱jaro̱ vardhā̍śca̱ viśvede̱tā sava̍nā tūtu̱mā kṛ̍ṣe

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 6

e̱tā viśvā̱ sava̍nā tūtu̱mā kṛ̍ṣe sva̱yaṃ sū̍no sahaso̱ yāni̍ dadhi̱ṣe |

varā̍ya te̱ pātra̱ṃ dharma̍ṇe̱ tanā̍ ya̱jño mantro̱ brahmodya̍ta̱ṃ vaca̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 7

ye te̍ vipra brahma̱kṛta̍: su̱te sacā̱ vasū̍nāṃ ca̱ vasu̍naśca dā̱vane̍ |

pra te su̱mnasya̱ mana̍sā pa̱thā bhu̍va̱nmade̍ su̱tasya̍ so̱myasyāndha̍saḥ
