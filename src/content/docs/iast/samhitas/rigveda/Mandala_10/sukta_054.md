# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 54

**6 bṛhaduktho vāmadevyaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 1

tāṃ su te̍ kī̱rtiṃ ma̍ghavanmahi̱tvā yattvā̍ bhī̱te roda̍sī̱ ahva̍yetām |
prāvo̍ de̱vām̐ āti̍ro̱ dāsa̱moja̍: pra̱jāyai̍ tvasyai̱ yadaśi̍kṣa indra

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 2

yadaca̍rasta̱nvā̍ vāvṛdhā̱no balā̍nīndra prabruvā̱ṇo jane̍ṣu |

mā̱yetsā te̱ yāni̍ yu̱ddhānyā̱hurnādya śatru̍ṃ na̱nu pu̱rā vi̍vitse

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 3

ka u̱ nu te̍ mahi̱mana̍: samasyā̱'smatpūrva̱ ṛṣa̱yo'nta̍māpuḥ |

yanmā̱tara̍ṃ ca pi̱tara̍ṃ ca sā̱kamaja̍nayathāsta̱nva1: svāyā̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 4

ca̱tvāri̍ te asu̱ryā̍ṇi̱ nāmā'dā̍bhyāni mahi̱ṣasya̍ santi |

tvama̱ṅga tāni̱ viśvā̍ni vitse̱ yebhi̱: karmā̍ṇi maghavañca̱kartha̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 5

tvaṃ viśvā̍ dadhiṣe̱ keva̍lāni̱ yānyā̱viryā ca̱ guhā̱ vasū̍ni |

kāma̱minme̍ maghava̱nmā vi tā̍rī̱stvamā̍jñā̱tā tvami̍ndrāsi dā̱tā

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 6

yo ada̍dhā̱jjyoti̍ṣi̱ jyoti̍ra̱ntaryo asṛ̍ja̱nmadhu̍nā̱ saṃ madhū̍ni |

adha̍ pri̱yaṃ śū̱ṣamindrā̍ya̱ manma̍ brahma̱kṛto̍ bṛ̱hadu̍kthādavāci
