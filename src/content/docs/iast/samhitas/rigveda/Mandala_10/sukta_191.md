# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 191

**4 saṃvanana āṅgirasaḥ| 1 agniḥ,2-4 saṃjñānam| anuṣṭup , 3 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 191 | **Verse:** 1

saṃsa̱midyu̍vase vṛṣa̱nnagne̱ viśvā̍nya̱rya ā | i̱l̤aspa̱de sami̍dhyase̱ sa no̱ vasū̱nyā bha̍ra

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 191 | **Verse:** 2

saṃ ga̍cchadhva̱ṃ saṃ va̍dadhva̱ṃ saṃ vo̱ manā̍ṃsi jānatām |  de̱vā bhā̱gaṃ yathā̱ pūrve̍ saṃjānā̱nā u̱pāsa̍te

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 191 | **Verse:** 3

sa̱mā̱no mantra̱: sami̍tiḥ samā̱nī sa̍mā̱naṃ mana̍: sa̱ha ci̱ttame̍ṣām |

sa̱mā̱naṃ mantra̍ma̱bhi ma̍ntraye vaḥ samā̱nena̍ vo ha̱viṣā̍ juhomi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 191 | **Verse:** 4

sa̱mā̱nī va̱ ākū̍tiḥ samā̱nā hṛda̍yāni vaḥ | sa̱mā̱nama̍stu vo̱ mano̱ yathā̍ va̱: susa̱hāsa̍ti
