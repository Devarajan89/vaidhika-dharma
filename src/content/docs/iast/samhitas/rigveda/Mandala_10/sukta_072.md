# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 72

**9 laukyo bṛhaspatiḥ, bṛhaspatirāṃgiraso vā, dākṣāyaṇī aditirvā| devāḥ| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 1

de̱vānā̱ṃ nu va̱yaṃ jānā̱ pra vo̍cāma vipa̱nyayā̍ | u̱ktheṣu̍ śa̱syamā̍neṣu̱ yaḥ paśyā̱dutta̍re yu̱ge

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 2

brahma̍ṇa̱spati̍re̱tā saṃ ka̱rmāra̍ ivādhamat | de̱vānā̍ṃ pū̱rvye yu̱ge'sa̍ta̱: sada̍jāyata

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 3

de̱vānā̍ṃ yu̱ge pra̍tha̱me'sa̍ta̱: sada̍jāyata | tadāśā̱ anva̍jāyanta̱ tadu̍ttā̱napa̍da̱spari̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 4

bhūrja̍jña uttā̱napa̍do bhu̱va āśā̍ ajāyanta | adi̍te̱rdakṣo̍ ajāyata̱ dakṣā̱dvadi̍ti̱: pari̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 5

adi̍ti̱rhyaja̍niṣṭa̱ dakṣa̱ yā du̍hi̱tā tava̍ | tāṃ de̱vā anva̍jāyanta bha̱drā a̱mṛta̍bandhavaḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 6

yadde̍vā a̱daḥ sa̍li̱le susa̍ṃrabdhā̱ ati̍ṣṭhata | atrā̍ vo̱ nṛtya̍tāmiva tī̱vro re̱ṇurapā̍yata

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 7

yadde̍vā̱ yata̍yo yathā̱ bhuva̍nā̱nyapi̍nvata | atrā̍ samu̱dra ā gū̱l̤hamā sūrya̍majabhartana

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 8

a̱ṣṭau pu̱trāso̱ adi̍te̱rye jā̱tāsta̱nva1spari̍ | de̱vām̐ upa̱ praitsa̱ptabhi̱: parā̍ mārtā̱ṇḍamā̍syat

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 9

sa̱ptabhi̍: pu̱trairadi̍ti̱rupa̱ praitpū̱rvyaṃ yu̱gam | pra̱jāyai̍ mṛ̱tyave̍ tva̱tpuna̍rmārtā̱ṇḍamābha̍rat
