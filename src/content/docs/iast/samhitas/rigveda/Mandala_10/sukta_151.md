# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 151

**śraddhā sūkta 5 śraddhā kāmāyanī|śradhā | anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 151 | **Verse:** 1

śra̱ddhayā̱gniḥ sami̍dhyate śra̱ddhayā̍ hūyate ha̱viḥ | śra̱ddhāṃ bhaga̍sya mū̱rdhani̱ vaca̱sā ve̍dayāmasi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 151 | **Verse:** 2

pri̱yaṃ śra̍ddhe̱ dada̍taḥ pri̱yaṃ śra̍ddhe̱ didā̍sataḥ | pri̱yaṃ bho̱jeṣu̱ yajva̍svi̱daṃ ma̍ udi̱taṃ kṛ̍dhi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 151 | **Verse:** 3

yathā̍ de̱vā asu̍reṣu śra̱ddhāmu̱greṣu̍ cakri̱re | e̱vaṃ bho̱jeṣu̱ yajva̍sva̱smāka̍mudi̱taṃ kṛ̍dhi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 151 | **Verse:** 4

śra̱ddhāṃ de̱vā yaja̍mānā vā̱yugo̍pā̱ upā̍sate | śra̱ddhāṃ hṛ̍da̱yya1yākū̍tyā śra̱ddhayā̍ vindate̱ vasu̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 151 | **Verse:** 5

śra̱ddhāṃ prā̱tarha̍vāmahe śra̱ddhāṃ ma̱dhyaṃdi̍na̱ṃ pari̍ | śra̱ddhāṃ sūrya̍sya ni̱mruci̱ śraddhe̱ śraddhā̍paye̱ha na̍:
