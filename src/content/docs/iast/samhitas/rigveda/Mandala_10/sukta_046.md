# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 46

**10 vatsaprirbhālandanaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 1

pra hotā̍ jā̱to ma̱hānna̍bho̱vinnṛ̱ṣadvā̍ sīdada̱pāmu̱pasthe̍ |
dadhi̱ryo dhāyi̱ sa te̱ vayā̍ṃsi ya̱ntā vasū̍ni vidha̱te ta̍nū̱pāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 2

i̱maṃ vi̱dhanto̍ a̱pāṃ sa̱dhasthe̍ pa̱śuṃ na na̱ṣṭaṃ pa̱dairanu̍ gman |

guhā̱ cata̍ntamu̱śijo̱ namo̍bhiri̱cchanto̱ dhīrā̱ bhṛga̍vo'vindan

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 3

i̱maṃ tri̱to bhūrya̍vindadi̱cchanvai̍bhūva̱so mū̱rdhanyaghnyā̍yāḥ |

sa śevṛ̍dho jā̱ta ā ha̱rmyeṣu̱ nābhi̱ryuvā̍ bhavati roca̱nasya̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 4

ma̱ndraṃ hotā̍ramu̱śijo̱ namo̍bhi̱: prāñca̍ṃ ya̱jñaṃ ne̱tāra̍madhva̱rāṇā̍m |

vi̱śāma̍kṛṇvannara̱tiṃ pā̍va̱kaṃ ha̍vya̱vāha̱ṃ dadha̍to̱ mānu̍ṣeṣu

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 5

pra bhū̱rjaya̍ntaṃ ma̱hāṃ vi̍po̱dhāṃ mū̱rā amū̍raṃ pu̱rāṃ da̱rmāṇa̍m |

naya̍nto̱ garbha̍ṃ va̱nāṃ dhiya̍ṃ dhu̱rhiri̍śmaśru̱ṃ nārvā̍ṇa̱ṃ dhana̍rcam

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 6

ni pa̱styā̍su tri̱taḥ sta̍bhū̱yanpari̍vīto̱ yonau̍ sīdada̱ntaḥ |

ata̍: sa̱ṃgṛbhyā̍ vi̱śāṃ damū̍nā̱ vidha̍rmaṇāya̱ntrairī̍yate̱ nṝn

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 7

a̱syājarā̍so da̱māma̱ritrā̍ a̱rcaddhū̍māso a̱gnaya̍: pāva̱kāḥ |

śvi̱tī̱caya̍: śvā̱trāso̍ bhura̱ṇyavo̍ vana̱rṣado̍ vā̱yavo̱ na somā̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 8

pra ji̱hvayā̍ bharate̱ vepo̍ a̱gniḥ pra va̱yunā̍ni̱ ceta̍sā pṛthi̱vyāḥ |

tamā̱yava̍: śu̱caya̍ntaṃ pāva̱kaṃ ma̱ndraṃ hotā̍raṃ dadhire̱ yaji̍ṣṭham

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 9

dyāvā̱ yama̱gniṃ pṛ̍thi̱vī jani̍ṣṭā̱māpa̱stvaṣṭā̱ bhṛga̍vo̱ yaṃ saho̍bhiḥ |

ī̱l̤enya̍ṃ pratha̱maṃ mā̍ta̱riśvā̍ de̱vāsta̍takṣu̱rmana̍ve̱ yaja̍tram

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 10

yaṃ tvā̍ de̱vā da̍dhi̱re ha̍vya̱vāha̍ṃ puru̱spṛho̱ mānu̍ṣāso̱ yaja̍tram |

sa yāma̍nnagne stuva̱te vayo̍ dhā̱: pra de̍va̱yanya̱śasa̱: saṃ hi pū̱rvīḥ
