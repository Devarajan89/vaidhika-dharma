# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 173

**6 dhruva āṃgirasaḥ| rājā| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 1

ā tvā̍hārṣama̱ntare̍dhi dhru̱vasti̱ṣṭhāvi̍cācaliḥ | viśa̍stvā̱ sarvā̍ vāñchantu̱ mā tvadrā̱ṣṭramadhi̍ bhraśat

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 2

i̱haivaidhi̱ māpa̍ cyoṣṭhā̱: parva̍ta i̱vāvi̍cācaliḥ | indra̍ ive̱ha dhru̱vasti̍ṣṭhe̱ha rā̱ṣṭramu̍ dhāraya

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 3

i̱mamindro̍ adīdharaddhru̱vaṃ dhru̱veṇa̍ ha̱viṣā̍ | tasmai̱ somo̱ adhi̍ brava̱ttasmā̍ u̱ brahma̍ṇa̱spati̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 4

dhru̱vā dyaurdhru̱vā pṛ̍thi̱vī dhru̱vāsa̱: parva̍tā i̱me | dhru̱vaṃ viśva̍mi̱daṃ jaga̍ddhru̱vo rājā̍ vi̱śāma̱yam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 5

dhru̱vaṃ te̱ rājā̱ varu̍ṇo dhru̱vaṃ de̱vo bṛha̱spati̍: | dhru̱vaṃ ta̱ indra̍ścā̱gniśca̍ rā̱ṣṭraṃ dhā̍rayatāṃ dhru̱vam

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 6

dhru̱vaṃ dhru̱veṇa̍ ha̱viṣā̱'bhi soma̍ṃ mṛśāmasi | atho̍ ta̱ indra̱: keva̍lī̱rviśo̍ bali̱hṛta̍skarat
