# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 5

**7 trita āptyaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 1

eka̍: samu̱dro dha̱ruṇo̍ rayī̱ṇāma̱smaddhṛ̱do bhūri̍janmā̱ vi ca̍ṣṭe |
siṣa̱ktyūdha̍rni̱ṇyoru̱pastha̱ utsa̍sya̱ madhye̱ nihi̍taṃ pa̱daṃ veḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 2

sa̱mā̱naṃ nī̱l̤aṃ vṛṣa̍ṇo̱ vasā̍nā̱: saṃ ja̍gmire mahi̱ṣā arva̍tībhiḥ |

ṛ̱tasya̍ pa̱daṃ ka̱vayo̱ ni pā̍nti̱ guhā̱ nāmā̍ni dadhire̱ parā̍ṇi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 3

ṛ̱tā̱yinī̍ mā̱yinī̱ saṃ da̍dhāte mi̱tvā śiśu̍ṃ jajñaturva̱rdhaya̍ntī |

viśva̍sya̱ nābhi̱ṃ cara̍to dhru̱vasya̍ ka̱veści̱ttantu̱ṃ mana̍sā vi̱yanta̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 4

ṛ̱tasya̱ hi va̍rta̱naya̱: sujā̍ta̱miṣo̱ vājā̍ya pra̱diva̱: saca̍nte |

a̱dhī̱vā̱saṃ roda̍sī vāvasā̱ne ghṛ̱tairannai̍rvāvṛdhāte̱ madhū̍nām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 5

sa̱pta svasṝ̱raru̍ṣīrvāvaśā̱no vi̱dvānmadhva̱ ujja̍bhārā dṛ̱śe kam |

a̱ntarye̍me a̱ntari̍kṣe purā̱jā i̱cchanva̱vrima̍vidatpūṣa̱ṇasya̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 6

sa̱pta ma̱ryādā̍: ka̱vaya̍statakṣu̱stāsā̱mekā̱mida̱bhya̍ṃhu̱ro gā̍t |

ā̱yorha̍ ska̱mbha u̍pa̱masya̍ nī̱l̤e pa̱thāṃ vi̍sa̱rge dha̱ruṇe̍ṣu tasthau

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 7

asa̍cca̱ sacca̍ para̱me vyo̍ma̱ndakṣa̍sya̱ janma̱nnadi̍teru̱pasthe̍ |

a̱gnirha̍ naḥ prathama̱jā ṛ̱tasya̱ pūrva̱ āyu̍ni vṛṣa̱bhaśca̍ dhe̱nuḥ
