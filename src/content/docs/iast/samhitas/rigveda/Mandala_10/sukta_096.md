# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 96

**13 barurāṃgirasaḥ, sarvaharirvā aindraḥ| hariḥ| jagatī, 12-13 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 1

pra te̍ ma̱he vi̱dathe̍ śaṃsiṣa̱ṃ harī̱ pra te̍ vanve va̱nuṣo̍ harya̱taṃ mada̍m |
ghṛ̱taṃ na yo hari̍bhi̱ścāru̱ seca̍ta̱ ā tvā̍ viśantu̱ hari̍varpasa̱ṃ gira̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 2

hari̱ṃ hi yoni̍ma̱bhi ye sa̱masva̍ranhi̱nvanto̱ harī̍ di̱vyaṃ yathā̱ sada̍: |

ā yaṃ pṛ̱ṇanti̱ hari̍bhi̱rna dhe̱nava̱ indrā̍ya śū̱ṣaṃ hari̍vantamarcata

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 3

so a̍sya̱ vajro̱ hari̍to̱ ya ā̍ya̱so hari̱rnikā̍mo̱ hari̱rā gabha̍styoḥ |

dyu̱mnī su̍śi̱pro hari̍manyusāyaka̱ indre̱ ni rū̱pā hari̍tā mimikṣire

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 4

di̱vi na ke̱turadhi̍ dhāyi harya̱to vi̱vyaca̱dvajro̱ hari̍to̱ na raṃhyā̍ |

tu̱dadahi̱ṃ hari̍śipro̱ ya ā̍ya̱saḥ sa̱hasra̍śokā abhavaddharimbha̱raḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 5

tvaṃtva̍maharyathā̱ upa̍stuta̱: pūrve̍bhirindra harikeśa̱ yajva̍bhiḥ |

tvaṃ ha̍ryasi̱ tava̱ viśva̍mu̱kthya1masā̍mi̱ rādho̍ harijāta harya̱tam

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 6

tā va̱jriṇa̍ṃ ma̱ndina̱ṃ stomya̱ṃ mada̱ indra̱ṃ rathe̍ vahato harya̱tā harī̍ |

pu̱rūṇya̍smai̱ sava̍nāni̱ harya̍ta̱ indrā̍ya̱ somā̱ hara̍yo dadhanvire

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 7

ara̱ṃ kāmā̍ya̱ hara̍yo dadhanvire sthi̱rāya̍ hinva̱nhara̍yo̱ harī̍ tu̱rā |

arva̍dbhi̱ryo hari̍bhi̱rjoṣa̱mīya̍te̱ so a̍sya̱ kāma̱ṃ hari̍vantamānaśe

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 8

hari̍śmaśāru̱rhari̍keśa āya̱sastu̍ra̱speye̱ yo ha̍ri̱pā ava̍rdhata |

arva̍dbhi̱ryo hari̍bhirvā̱jinī̍vasu̱rati̱ viśvā̍ duri̱tā pāri̍ṣa̱ddharī̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 9

sruve̍va̱ yasya̱ hari̍ṇī vipe̱tatu̱: śipre̱ vājā̍ya̱ hari̍ṇī̱ davi̍dhvataḥ |

pra yatkṛ̱te ca̍ma̱se marmṛ̍ja̱ddharī̍ pī̱tvā mada̍sya harya̱tasyāndha̍saḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 10

u̱ta sma̱ sadma̍ harya̱tasya̍ pa̱styo̱3ratyo̱ na vāja̱ṃ hari̍vām̐ acikradat |

ma̱hī ci̱ddhi dhi̱ṣaṇāha̍rya̱doja̍sā bṛ̱hadvayo̍ dadhiṣe harya̱taści̱dā

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 11

ā roda̍sī̱ harya̍māṇo mahi̱tvā navya̍ṃnavyaṃ haryasi̱ manma̱ nu pri̱yam |

pra pa̱stya̍masura harya̱taṃ gorā̱viṣkṛ̍dhi̱ hara̍ye̱ sūryā̍ya

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 12

ā tvā̍ ha̱ryanta̍ṃ pra̱yujo̱ janā̍nā̱ṃ rathe̍ vahantu̱ hari̍śipramindra |

pibā̱ yathā̱ prati̍bhṛtasya̱ madhvo̱ harya̍nya̱jñaṃ sa̍dha̱māde̱ daśo̍ṇim

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 13

apā̱: pūrve̍ṣāṃ harivaḥ su̱tānā̱matho̍ i̱daṃ sava̍na̱ṃ keva̍laṃ te |

ma̱ma̱ddhi soma̱ṃ madhu̍mantamindra sa̱trā vṛ̍ṣañja̱ṭhara̱ ā vṛ̍ṣasva
