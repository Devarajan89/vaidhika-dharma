# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 3

**7 trita āptyaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 1

i̱no rā̍jannara̱tiḥ sami̍ddho̱ raudro̱ dakṣā̍ya suṣu̱mām̐ a̍darśi |
ci̱kidvi bhā̍ti bhā̱sā bṛ̍ha̱tā'si̍knīmeti̱ ruśa̍tīma̱pāja̍n

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 2

kṛ̱ṣṇāṃ yadenī̍ma̱bhi varpa̍sā̱ bhūjja̱naya̱nyoṣā̍ṃ bṛha̱taḥ pi̱turjām |

ū̱rdhvaṃ bhā̱nuṃ sūrya̍sya stabhā̱yandi̱vo vasu̍bhirara̱tirvi bhā̍ti

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 3

bha̱dro bha̱drayā̱ saca̍māna̱ āgā̱tsvasā̍raṃ jā̱ro a̱bhye̍ti pa̱ścāt |

su̱pra̱ke̱tairdyubhi̍ra̱gnirvi̱tiṣṭha̱nruśa̍dbhi̱rvarṇai̍ra̱bhi rā̱mama̍sthāt

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 4

a̱sya yāmā̍so bṛha̱to na va̱gnūnindhā̍nā a̱gneḥ sakhyu̍: śi̱vasya̍ |

īḍya̍sya̱ vṛṣṇo̍ bṛha̱taḥ svāso̱ bhāmā̍so̱ yāma̍nna̱ktava̍ścikitre

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 5

sva̱nā na yasya̱ bhāmā̍sa̱: pava̍nte̱ roca̍mānasya bṛha̱taḥ su̱diva̍: |

jyeṣṭhe̍bhi̱ryasteji̍ṣṭhaiḥ krīl̤u̱madbhi̱rvarṣi̍ṣṭhebhirbhā̱nubhi̱rnakṣa̍ti̱ dyām

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 6

a̱sya śuṣmā̍so dadṛśā̱napa̍ve̱rjeha̍mānasya svanayanni̱yudbhi̍: |

pra̱tnebhi̱ryo ruśa̍dbhirde̱vata̍mo̱ vi rebha̍dbhirara̱tirbhāti̱ vibhvā̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 7

sa ā va̍kṣi̱ mahi̍ na̱ ā ca̍ satsi di̱vaspṛ̍thi̱vyora̍ra̱tiryu̍va̱tyoḥ |

a̱gniḥ su̱tuka̍: su̱tuke̍bhi̱raśvai̱ rabha̍svadbhī̱ rabha̍svā̱m̐ eha ga̍myāḥ
