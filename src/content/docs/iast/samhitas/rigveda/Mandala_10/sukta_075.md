# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 75

**9 sindhukṣit praiyamedhaḥ| nadyaḥ| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 1

pra su va̍ āpo mahi̱māna̍mutta̱maṃ kā̱rurvo̍cāti̱ sada̍ne vi̱vasva̍taḥ |
pra sa̱ptasa̍pta tre̱dhā hi ca̍kra̱muḥ pra sṛtva̍rīṇā̱mati̱ sindhu̱roja̍sā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 2

pra te̍'rada̱dvaru̍ṇo̱ yāta̍ve pa̱thaḥ sindho̱ yadvājā̍m̐ a̱bhyadra̍va̱stvam |

bhūmyā̱ adhi̍ pra̱vatā̍ yāsi̱ sānu̍nā̱ yade̍ṣā̱magra̱ṃ jaga̍tāmira̱jyasi̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 3

di̱vi sva̱no ya̍tate̱ bhūmyo̱parya̍na̱ntaṃ śuṣma̱mudi̍yarti bhā̱nunā̍ |

a̱bhrādi̍va̱ pra sta̍nayanti vṛ̱ṣṭaya̱: sindhu̱ryadeti̍ vṛṣa̱bho na roru̍vat

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 4

a̱bhi tvā̍ sindho̱ śiśu̱minna mā̱taro̍ vā̱śrā a̍rṣanti̱ paya̍seva dhe̱nava̍: |

rāje̍va̱ yudhvā̍ nayasi̱ tvamitsicau̱ yadā̍sā̱magra̍ṃ pra̱vatā̱mina̍kṣasi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 5

i̱maṃ me̍ gaṅge yamune sarasvati̱ śutu̍dri̱ stoma̍ṃ sacatā̱ paru̱ṣṇyā |

a̱si̱knyā ma̍rudvṛdhe vi̱tasta̱yā''rjī̍kīye śṛṇu̱hyā su̱ṣoma̍yā

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 6

tṛ̱ṣṭāma̍yā pratha̱maṃ yāta̍ve sa̱jūḥ su̱sartvā̍ ra̱sayā̍ śve̱tyā tyā |

tvaṃ si̍ndho̱ kubha̍yā goma̱tīṃ krumu̍ṃ meha̱tnvā sa̱ratha̱ṃ yābhi̱rīya̍se

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 7

ṛjī̱tyenī̱ ruśa̍tī mahi̱tvā pari̱ jrayā̍ṃsi bharate̱ rajā̍ṃsi |

ada̍bdhā̱ sindhu̍ra̱pasā̍ma̱pasta̱mā'śvā̱ na ci̱trā vapu̍ṣīva darśa̱tā

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 8

svaśvā̱ sindhu̍: su̱rathā̍ su̱vāsā̍ hira̱ṇyayī̱ sukṛ̍tā vā̱jinī̍vatī |

ūrṇā̍vatī yuva̱tiḥ sī̱lamā̍vatyu̱tādhi̍ vaste su̱bhagā̍ madhu̱vṛdha̍m

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 9

su̱khaṃ ratha̍ṃ yuyuje̱ sindhu̍ra̱śvina̱ṃ tena̱ vāja̍ṃ saniṣada̱sminnā̱jau |

ma̱hānhya̍sya mahi̱mā pa̍na̱syate'da̍bdhasya̱ svaya̍śaso vira̱pśina̍:
