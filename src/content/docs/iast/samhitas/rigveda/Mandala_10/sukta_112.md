# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 112

**10 vairūpo nabhaḥ prabhedanaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 112 | **Verse:** 1

indra̱ piba̍ pratikā̱maṃ su̱tasya̍ prātaḥsā̱vastava̱ hi pū̱rvapī̍tiḥ |
harṣa̍sva̱ hanta̍ve śūra̱ śatrū̍nu̱kthebhi̍ṣṭe vī̱ryā̱3 pra bra̍vāma

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 112 | **Verse:** 2

yaste̱ ratho̱ mana̍so̱ javī̍yā̱nendra̱ tena̍ soma̱peyā̍ya yāhi |

tūya̱mā te̱ hara̍ya̱: pra dra̍vantu̱ yebhi̱ryāsi̱ vṛṣa̍bhi̱rmanda̍mānaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 112 | **Verse:** 3

hari̍tvatā̱ varca̍sā̱ sūrya̍sya̱ śreṣṭhai̍ rū̱paista̱nva̍ṃ sparśayasva |

a̱smābhi̍rindra̱ sakhi̍bhirhuvā̱naḥ sa̍dhrīcī̱no mā̍dayasvā ni̱ṣadya̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 112 | **Verse:** 4

yasya̱ tyatte̍ mahi̱māna̱ṃ made̍ṣvi̱me ma̱hī roda̍sī̱ nāvi̍viktām |

tadoka̱ ā hari̍bhirindra yu̱ktaiḥ pri̱yebhi̍ryāhi pri̱yamanna̱maccha̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 112 | **Verse:** 5

yasya̱ śaśva̍tpapi̱vām̐ i̍ndra̱ śatrū̍nanānukṛ̱tyā raṇyā̍ ca̱kartha̍ |

sa te̱ pura̍ṃdhi̱ṃ tavi̍ṣīmiyarti̱ sa te̱ madā̍ya su̱ta i̍ndra̱ soma̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 112 | **Verse:** 6

i̱daṃ te̱ pātra̱ṃ sana̍vittamindra̱ pibā̱ soma̍me̱nā śa̍takrato |

pū̱rṇa ā̍hā̱vo ma̍di̱rasya̱ madhvo̱ yaṃ viśva̱ ida̍bhi̱harya̍nti de̱vāḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 112 | **Verse:** 7

vi hi tvāmi̍ndra puru̱dhā janā̍so hi̱tapra̍yaso vṛṣabha̱ hvaya̍nte |

a̱smāka̍ṃ te̱ madhu̍mattamānī̱mā bhu̍va̱ntsava̍nā̱ teṣu̍ harya

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 112 | **Verse:** 8

pra ta̍ indra pū̱rvyāṇi̱ pra nū̱naṃ vī̱ryā̍ vocaṃ pratha̱mā kṛ̱tāni̍ |

sa̱tī̱nama̍nyuraśrathāyo̱ adri̍ṃ suveda̱nāma̍kṛṇo̱rbrahma̍ṇe̱ gām

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 112 | **Verse:** 9

ni ṣu sī̍da gaṇapate ga̱ṇeṣu̱ tvāmā̍hu̱rvipra̍tamaṃ kavī̱nām |

na ṛ̱te tvatkri̍yate̱ kiṃ ca̱nāre ma̱hāma̱rkaṃ ma̍ghavañci̱trama̍rca

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 112 | **Verse:** 10

a̱bhi̱khyā no̍ maghava̱nnādha̍mānā̱ntsakhe̍ bo̱dhi va̍supate̱ sakhī̍nām |

raṇa̍ṃ kṛdhi raṇakṛtsatyaśu̱ṣmā'bha̍kte ci̱dā bha̍jā rā̱ye a̱smān
