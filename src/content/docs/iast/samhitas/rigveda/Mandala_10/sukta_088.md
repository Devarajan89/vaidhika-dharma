# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 88

**19 āṃgiraso mūrdhanvān , vāmadevyo vā| sūrya-vaiśvānaro'gniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 1

ha̱viṣpānta̍ma̱jara̍ṃ sva̱rvidi̍ divi̱spṛśyāhu̍ta̱ṃ juṣṭa̍ma̱gnau |
tasya̱ bharma̍ṇe̱ bhuva̍nāya de̱vā dharma̍ṇe̱ kaṃ sva̱dhayā̍ paprathanta

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 2

gī̱rṇaṃ bhuva̍na̱ṃ tama̱sāpa̍gūl̤hamā̱viḥ sva̍rabhavajjā̱te a̱gnau |

tasya̍ de̱vāḥ pṛ̍thi̱vī dyauru̱tāpo'ra̍ṇaya̱nnoṣa̍dhīḥ sa̱khye a̍sya

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 3

de̱vebhi̱rnvi̍ṣi̱to ya̱jñiye̍bhira̱gniṃ sto̍ṣāṇya̱jara̍ṃ bṛ̱hanta̍m |

yo bhā̱nunā̍ pṛthi̱vīṃ dyāmu̱temāmā̍ta̱tāna̱ roda̍sī a̱ntari̍kṣam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 4

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 88 | **Verse:** 5

mā̱yāmū̱ tu ya̱jñiyā̍nāme̱tāmapo̱ yattūrṇi̱ścara̍ti prajā̱nan

## Verse 7
