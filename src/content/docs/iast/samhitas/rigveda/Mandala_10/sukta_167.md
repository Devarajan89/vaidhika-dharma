# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 167

**4 viśvāmitra-jamadagnī| indraḥ, 3 soma-varuṇa-bṛhaspati-anumati-maghavat-dhātṛ-vidhātāraḥ| jagatī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 167 | **Verse:** 1

tubhye̱dami̍ndra̱ pari̍ ṣicyate̱ madhu̱ tvaṃ su̱tasya̍ ka̱laśa̍sya rājasi |
tvaṃ ra̱yiṃ pu̍ru̱vīrā̍mu naskṛdhi̱ tvaṃ tapa̍: pari̱tapyā̍jaya̱: sva̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 167 | **Verse:** 2

sva̱rjita̱ṃ mahi̍ mandā̱namandha̍so̱ havā̍mahe̱ pari̍ śa̱kraṃ su̱tām̐ upa̍ |

i̱maṃ no̍ ya̱jñami̱ha bo̱dhyā ga̍hi̱ spṛdho̱ jaya̍ntaṃ ma̱ghavā̍namīmahe

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 167 | **Verse:** 3

soma̍sya̱ rājño̱ varu̍ṇasya̱ dharma̍ṇi̱ bṛha̱spate̱ranu̍matyā u̱ śarma̍ṇi |

tavā̱hama̱dya ma̍ghava̱nnupa̍stutau̱ dhāta̱rvidhā̍taḥ ka̱laśā̍m̐ abhakṣayam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 167 | **Verse:** 4

prasū̍to bha̱kṣama̍karaṃ ca̱rāvapi̱ stoma̍ṃ ce̱maṃ pra̍tha̱maḥ sū̱rirunmṛ̍je |

su̱te sā̱tena̱ yadyāga̍maṃ vā̱ṃ prati̍ viśvāmitrajamadagnī̱ dame̍
