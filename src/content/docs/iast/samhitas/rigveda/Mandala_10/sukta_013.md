# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 13

**5 āṅgirhavirdhānaḥ| vivasvānādityo vā| havirdhāne| triṣṭup, 5 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 1

yu̱je vā̱ṃ brahma̍ pū̱rvyaṃ namo̍bhi̱rvi śloka̍ etu pa̱thye̍va sū̱reḥ |
śṛ̱ṇvantu̱ viśve̍ a̱mṛta̍sya pu̱trā ā ye dhāmā̍ni di̱vyāni̍ ta̱sthuḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 2

ya̱me i̍va̱ yata̍māne̱ yadaita̱ṃ pra vā̍ṃ bhara̱nmānu̍ṣā deva̱yanta̍: |

ā sī̍data̱ṃ svamu̍ lo̱kaṃ vidā̍ne svāsa̱sthe bha̍vata̱minda̍ve naḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 3

pañca̍ pa̱dāni̍ ru̱po anva̍roha̱ṃ catu̍ṣpadī̱manve̍mi vra̱tena̍ |

a̱kṣare̍ṇa̱ prati̍ mima e̱tāmṛ̱tasya̱ nābhā̱vadhi̱ saṃ pu̍nāmi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 4

de̱vebhya̱: kama̍vṛṇīta mṛ̱tyuṃ pra̱jāyai̱ kama̱mṛta̱ṃ nāvṛ̍ṇīta |

bṛha̱spati̍ṃ ya̱jñama̍kṛṇvata̱ ṛṣi̍ṃ pri̱yāṃ ya̱masta̱nvaṃ1 prāri̍recīt

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 5

sa̱pta kṣa̍ranti̱ śiśa̍ve ma̱rutva̍te pi̱tre pu̱trāso̱ apya̍vīvatannṛ̱tam |

u̱bhe ida̍syo̱bhaya̍sya rājata u̱bhe ya̍tete u̱bhaya̍sya puṣyataḥ
