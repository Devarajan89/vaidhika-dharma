# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 43

**11 kṛṣṇa āṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ| jagatī,10-11 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 1

acchā̍ ma̱ indra̍ṃ ma̱taya̍: sva̱rvida̍: sa̱dhrīcī̱rviśvā̍ uśa̱tīra̍nūṣata |
pari̍ ṣvajante̱ jana̍yo̱ yathā̱ pati̱ṃ marya̱ṃ na śu̱ndhyuṃ ma̱ghavā̍namū̱taye̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 2

na ghā̍ tva̱drigapa̍ veti me̱ mana̱stve itkāma̍ṃ puruhūta śiśraya |

rāje̍va dasma̱ ni ṣa̱do'dhi̍ ba̱rhiṣya̱smintsu some̍'va̱pāna̍mastu te

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 3

vi̱ṣū̱vṛdindro̱ ama̍teru̱ta kṣu̱dhaḥ sa idrā̱yo ma̱ghavā̱ vasva̍ īśate |

tasyedi̱me pra̍va̱ṇe sa̱pta sindha̍vo̱ vayo̍ vardhanti vṛṣa̱bhasya̍ śu̱ṣmiṇa̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 4

vayo̱ na vṛ̱kṣaṃ su̍palā̱śamāsa̍da̱ntsomā̍sa̱ indra̍ṃ ma̱ndina̍ścamū̱ṣada̍: |

praiṣā̱manī̍ka̱ṃ śava̍sā̱ davi̍dyutadvi̱datsva1rmana̍ve̱ jyoti̱rārya̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 5

kṛ̱taṃ na śva̱ghnī vi ci̍noti̱ deva̍ne sa̱ṃvarga̱ṃ yanma̱ghavā̱ sūrya̱ṃ jaya̍t |

na tatte̍ a̱nyo anu̍ vī̱rya̍ṃ śaka̱nna pu̍rā̱ṇo ma̍ghava̱nnota nūta̍naḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 6

viśa̍ṃviśaṃ ma̱ghavā̱ parya̍śāyata̱ janā̍nā̱ṃ dhenā̍ ava̱cāka̍śa̱dvṛṣā̍ |

yasyāha̍ śa̱kraḥ sava̍neṣu̱ raṇya̍ti̱ sa tī̱vraiḥ somai̍: sahate pṛtanya̱taḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 7

āpo̱ na sindhu̍ma̱bhi yatsa̱makṣa̍ra̱ntsomā̍sa̱ indra̍ṃ ku̱lyā i̍va hra̱dam |

vardha̍nti̱ viprā̱ maho̍ asya̱ sāda̍ne̱ yava̱ṃ na vṛ̱ṣṭirdi̱vyena̱ dānu̍nā

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 8

vṛṣā̱ na kru̱ddhaḥ pa̍taya̱draja̱:svā yo a̱ryapa̍tnī̱rakṛ̍ṇodi̱mā a̱paḥ |

sa su̍nva̱te ma̱ghavā̍ jī̱radā̍na̱ve'vi̍nda̱jjyoti̱rmana̍ve ha̱viṣma̍te

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 9

ujjā̍yatāṃ para̱śurjyoti̍ṣā sa̱ha bhū̱yā ṛ̱tasya̍ su̱dughā̍ purāṇa̱vat |

vi ro̍catāmaru̱ṣo bhā̱nunā̱ śuci̱: sva1rṇa śu̱kraṃ śu̍śucīta̱ satpa̍tiḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 10

gobhi̍ṣṭare̱māma̍tiṃ du̱revā̱ṃ yave̍na̱ kṣudha̍ṃ puruhūta̱ viśvā̍m |

va̱yaṃ rāja̍bhiḥ pratha̱mā dhanā̍nya̱smāke̍na vṛ̱jane̍nā jayema

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 11

bṛha̱spati̍rna̱: pari̍ pātu pa̱ścādu̱totta̍rasmā̱dadha̍rādaghā̱yoḥ |

indra̍: pu̱rastā̍du̱ta ma̍dhya̱to na̱: sakhā̱ sakhi̍bhyo̱ vari̍vaḥ kṛṇotu
