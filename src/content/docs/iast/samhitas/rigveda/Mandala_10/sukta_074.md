# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 74

**6  gaurivītiḥśāktyaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 1

vasū̍nāṃ vā carkṛṣa̱ iya̍kṣandhi̱yā vā̍ ya̱jñairvā̱ roda̍syoḥ |
arva̍nto vā̱ ye ra̍yi̱manta̍: sā̱tau va̱naṃa vā̱ ye su̱śruṇa̍ṃ su̱śruto̱ dhuḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 2

hava̍ eṣā̱masu̍ro nakṣata̱ dyāṃ śra̍vasya̱tā mana̍sā niṃsata̱ kṣām |

cakṣā̍ṇā̱ yatra̍ suvi̱tāya̍ de̱vā dyaurna vāre̍bhiḥ kṛ̱ṇava̍nta̱ svaiḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 3

i̱yame̍ṣāma̱mṛtā̍nā̱ṃ gīḥ sa̱rvatā̍tā̱ ye kṛ̱paṇa̍nta̱ ratna̍m |

dhiya̍ṃ ca ya̱jñaṃ ca̱ sādha̍nta̱ste no̍ dhāntu vasa̱vya1masā̍mi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 4

ā tatta̍ indrā̱yava̍: panantā̱'bhi ya ū̱rvaṃ goma̍nta̱ṃ titṛ̍tsān |

sa̱kṛ̱tsvaṃ1 ye pu̍rupu̱trāṃ ma̱hīṃ sa̱hasra̍dhārāṃ bṛha̱tīṃ dudu̍kṣan

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 5

śacī̍va̱ indra̱mava̍se kṛṇudhva̱manā̍nataṃ da̱maya̍ntaṃ pṛta̱nyūn |

ṛ̱bhu̱kṣaṇa̍ṃ ma̱ghavā̍naṃ suvṛ̱ktiṃ bhartā̱ yo vajra̱ṃ narya̍ṃ puru̱kṣuḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 6

yadvā̱vāna̍ puru̱tama̍ṃ purā̱ṣāl̤ā vṛ̍tra̱hendro̱ nāmā̍nyaprāḥ |

ace̍ti prā̱saha̱spati̱stuvi̍ṣmā̱nyadī̍mu̱śmasi̱ karta̍ve̱ kara̱ttat
