# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 119

**13 aindro laba:|ātmā (indraḥ) | gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 119 | **Verse:** 1

iti̱ vā iti̍ me̱ mano̱ gāmaśva̍ṃ sanuyā̱miti̍ | ku̱vitsoma̱syāpā̱miti̍

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 119 | **Verse:** 2
