# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 21

**8 aindro vimadaḥ, prājāpatyo vā, vāsukro vasukṛdvā| agniḥ| āstārapaṃktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 1

āgniṃ na svavṛ̍ktibhi̱rhotā̍raṃ tvā vṛṇīmahe |
ya̱jñāya̍ stī̱rṇaba̍rhiṣe̱ vi vo̱ made̍ śī̱raṃ pā̍va̱kaśo̍ciṣa̱ṃ viva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 2

tvāmu̱ te svā̱bhuva̍: śu̱mbhantyaśva̍rādhasaḥ |

veti̱ tvāmu̍pa̱seca̍nī̱ vi vo̱ mada̱ ṛjī̍tiragna̱ āhu̍ti̱rviva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 3

tve dha̱rmāṇa̍ āsate ju̱hūbhi̍: siñca̱tīri̍va |

kṛ̱ṣṇā rū̱pāṇyarju̍nā̱ vi vo̱ made̱ viśvā̱ adhi̱ śriyo̍ dhiṣe̱ viva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 4

yama̍gne̱ manya̍se ra̱yiṃ saha̍sāvannamartya |

tamā no̱ vāja̍sātaye̱ vi vo̱ made̍ ya̱jñeṣu̍ ci̱tramā bha̍rā̱ viva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 5

a̱gnirjā̱to atha̍rvaṇā vi̱dadviśvā̍ni̱ kāvyā̍ |

bhuva̍ddū̱to vi̱vasva̍to̱ vi vo̱ made̍ pri̱yo ya̱masya̱ kāmyo̱ viva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 6

tvāṃ ya̱jñeṣvī̍l̤a̱te'gne̍ praya̱tya̍dhva̱re |

tvaṃ vasū̍ni̱ kāmyā̱ vi vo̱ made̱ viśvā̍ dadhāsi dā̱śuṣe̱ viva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 7

tvāṃ ya̱jñeṣvṛ̱tvija̱ṃ cāru̍magne̱ ni ṣe̍dire |

ghṛ̱tapra̍tīka̱ṃ manu̍ṣo̱ vi vo̱ made̍ śu̱kraṃ ceti̍ṣṭhama̱kṣabhi̱rviva̍kṣase

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 8

agne̍ śu̱kreṇa̍ śo̱ciṣo̱ru pra̍thayase bṛ̱hat |

a̱bhi̱kranda̍nvṛṣāyase̱ vi vo̱ made̱ garbha̍ṃ dadhāsi jā̱miṣu̱ viva̍kṣase
