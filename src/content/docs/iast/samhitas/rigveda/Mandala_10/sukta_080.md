# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 80

**7 saucīko'gnirvaiśvānaro vā, saptirvājaṃbharo vā| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 80 | **Verse:** 1

a̱gniḥ sapti̍ṃ vājambha̱raṃ da̍dātya̱gnirvī̱raṃ śrutya̍ṃ karmani̱ṣṭhām |
a̱gnī roda̍sī̱ vi ca̍ratsama̱ñjanna̱gnirnārī̍ṃ vī̱raku̍kṣi̱ṃ pura̍ṃdhim

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 80 | **Verse:** 2

a̱gnerapna̍saḥ sa̱mida̍stu bha̱drā'gnirma̱hī roda̍sī̱ ā vi̍veśa |

a̱gnireka̍ṃ codayatsa̱matsva̱gnirvṛ̱trāṇi̍ dayate pu̱rūṇi̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 80 | **Verse:** 3

a̱gnirha̱ tyaṃ jara̍ta̱: karṇa̍māvā̱'gnira̱dbhyo nira̍daha̱jjarū̍tham |

a̱gniratri̍ṃ gha̱rma u̍ruṣyada̱ntara̱gnirnṛ̱medha̍ṃ pra̱jayā̍sṛja̱tsam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 80 | **Verse:** 4

a̱gnirdā̱ddravi̍ṇaṃ vī̱rape̍śā a̱gniṝṣi̱ṃ yaḥ sa̱hasrā̍ sa̱noti̍ |

a̱gnirdi̱vi ha̱vyamā ta̍tānā̱'gnerdhāmā̍ni̱ vibhṛ̍tā puru̱trā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 80 | **Verse:** 5

a̱gnimu̱kthaiṝṣa̍yo̱ vi hva̍yante̱'gniṃ naro̱ yāma̍ni bādhi̱tāsa̍: |

a̱gniṃ vayo̍ a̱ntari̍kṣe̱ pata̍nto̱'gniḥ sa̱hasrā̱ pari̍ yāti̱ gonā̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 80 | **Verse:** 6

a̱gniṃ viśa̍ īl̤ate̱ mānu̍ṣī̱ryā a̱gniṃ manu̍ṣo̱ nahu̍ṣo̱ vi jā̱tāḥ |

a̱gnirgāndha̍rvīṃ pa̱thyā̍mṛ̱tasyā̱'gnergavyū̍tirghṛ̱ta ā niṣa̍ttā

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 80 | **Verse:** 7

a̱gnaye̱ brahma̍ ṛ̱bhava̍statakṣura̱gniṃ ma̱hāma̍vocāmā suvṛ̱ktim |

agne̱ prāva̍ jari̱tāra̍ṃ yavi̱ṣṭhā'gne̱ mahi̱ dravi̍ṇa̱mā ya̍jasva
