# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 143

**6 atriḥ sāṃkhyaḥ| aśvinau| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 143 | **Verse:** 1

tyaṃ ci̱datri̍mṛta̱jura̱martha̱maśva̱ṃ na yāta̍ve | ka̱kṣīva̍nta̱ṃ yadī̱ punā̱ ratha̱ṃ na kṛ̍ṇu̱tho nava̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 143 | **Verse:** 2

tyaṃ ci̱daśva̱ṃ na vā̱jina̍mare̱ṇavo̱ yamatna̍ta | dṛ̱l̤haṃ gra̱nthiṃ na vi ṣya̍ta̱matri̱ṃ yavi̍ṣṭha̱mā raja̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 143 | **Verse:** 3

narā̱ daṃsi̍ṣṭhā̱vatra̍ye̱ śubhrā̱ siṣā̍sata̱ṃ dhiya̍: | athā̱ hi vā̍ṃ di̱vo na̍rā̱ puna̱ stomo̱ na vi̱śase̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 143 | **Verse:** 4

ci̱te tadvā̍ṃ surādhasā rā̱tiḥ su̍ma̱tira̍śvinā | ā yanna̱: sada̍ne pṛ̱thau sama̍ne̱ parṣa̍tho narā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 143 | **Verse:** 5

yu̱vaṃ bhu̱jyuṃ sa̍mu̱dra ā raja̍saḥ pā̱ra ī̍ṅkhi̱tam | yā̱tamacchā̍ pata̱tribhi̱rnāsa̍tyā sā̱taye̍ kṛtam

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 143 | **Verse:** 6

ā vā̍ṃ su̱mnaiḥ śa̱ṃyū i̍va̱ maṃhi̍ṣṭhā̱ viśva̍vedasā | sama̱sme bhū̍ṣataṃ na̱rotsa̱ṃ na pi̱pyuṣī̱riṣa̍:
