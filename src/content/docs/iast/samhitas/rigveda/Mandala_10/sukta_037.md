# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 37

**12 sauryo'bhitapāḥ| sūryaḥ| jagatī, 10 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 1

namo̍ mi̱trasya̱ varu̍ṇasya̱ cakṣa̍se ma̱ho de̱vāya̱ tadṛ̱taṃ sa̍paryata |
dū̱re̱dṛśe̍ de̱vajā̍tāya ke̱tave̍ di̱vaspu̱trāya̱ sūryā̍ya śaṃsata

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 2

sā mā̍ sa̱tyokti̱: pari̍ pātu vi̱śvato̱ dyāvā̍ ca̱ yatra̍ ta̱tana̱nnahā̍ni ca |

viśva̍ma̱nyanni vi̍śate̱ yadeja̍ti vi̱śvāhāpo̍ vi̱śvāhode̍ti̱ sūrya̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 3

na te̱ ade̍vaḥ pra̱divo̱ ni vā̍sate̱ yade̍ta̱śebhi̍: pata̱rai ra̍tha̱ryasi̍ |

prā̱cīna̍ma̱nyadanu̍ vartate̱ raja̱ uda̱nyena̱ jyoti̍ṣā yāsi sūrya

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 4

yena̍ sūrya̱ jyoti̍ṣā̱ bādha̍se̱ tamo̱ jaga̍cca̱ viśva̍mudi̱yarṣi̍ bhā̱nunā̍ |

tenā̱smadviśvā̱mani̍rā̱manā̍huti̱mapāmī̍vā̱mapa̍ du̱ṣṣvapnya̍ṃ suva

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 5

viśva̍sya̱ hi preṣi̍to̱ rakṣa̍si vra̱tamahe̍l̤ayannu̱ccara̍si sva̱dhā anu̍ |

yada̱dya tvā̍ sūryopa̱bravā̍mahai̱ taṃ no̍ de̱vā anu̍ maṃsīrata̱ kratu̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 6

taṃ no̱ dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī tanna̱ āpa̱ indra̍: śṛṇvantu ma̱ruto̱ hava̱ṃ vaca̍: |

mā śūne̍ bhūma̱ sūrya̍sya sa̱ṃdṛśi̍ bha̱draṃ jīva̍nto jara̱ṇāma̍śīmahi

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 7

vi̱śvāhā̍ tvā su̱mana̍saḥ su̱cakṣa̍saḥ pra̱jāva̍nto anamī̱vā anā̍gasaḥ |

u̱dyanta̍ṃ tvā mitramaho di̱vedi̍ve̱ jyogjī̱vāḥ prati̍ paśyema sūrya

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 8

mahi̱ jyoti̱rbibhra̍taṃ tvā vicakṣaṇa̱ bhāsva̍nta̱ṃ cakṣu̍ṣecakṣuṣe̱ maya̍: |

ā̱roha̍ntaṃ bṛha̱taḥ pāja̍sa̱spari̍ va̱yaṃ jī̱vāḥ prati̍ paśyema sūrya

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 9

yasya̍ te̱ viśvā̱ bhuva̍nāni ke̱tunā̱ pra cera̍te̱ ni ca̍ vi̱śante̍ a̱ktubhi̍: |

a̱nā̱gā̱stvena̍ harikeśa sū̱ryā'hnā̍hnā no̱ vasya̍sāvasya̱sodi̍hi

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 10

śaṃ no̍ bhava̱ cakṣa̍sā̱ śaṃ no̱ ahnā̱ śaṃ bhā̱nunā̱ śaṃ hi̱mā śaṃ ghṛ̱ṇena̍ |

yathā̱ śamadhva̱ñchamasa̍dduro̱ṇe tatsū̍rya̱ dravi̍ṇaṃ dhehi ci̱tram

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 11

a̱smāka̍ṃ devā u̱bhayā̍ya̱ janma̍ne̱ śarma̍ yacchata dvi̱pade̱ catu̍ṣpade |

a̱datpiba̍dū̱rjaya̍māna̱māśi̍ta̱ṃ tada̱sme śaṃ yora̍ra̱po da̍dhātana

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 12

yadvo̍ devāścakṛ̱ma ji̱hvayā̍ gu̱ru mana̍so vā̱ prayu̍tī deva̱hel̤a̍nam |

arā̍vā̱ yo no̍ a̱bhi du̍cchunā̱yate̱ tasmi̱ntadeno̍ vasavo̱ ni dhe̍tana
