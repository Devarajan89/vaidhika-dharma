# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 99

**12 vamro vaikhānasaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 1

kaṃ na̍ści̱trami̍ṣaṇyasi ciki̱tvānpṛ̍thu̱gmāna̍ṃ vā̱śraṃ vā̍vṛ̱dhadhyai̍ |
kattasya̱ dātu̱ śava̍so̱ vyu̍ṣṭau̱ takṣa̱dvajra̍ṃ vṛtra̱tura̱mapi̍nvat

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 2

sa hi dyu̱tā vi̱dyutā̱ veti̱ sāma̍ pṛ̱thuṃ yoni̍masura̱tvā sa̍sāda |

sa sanī̍l̤ebhiḥ prasahā̱no a̍sya̱ bhrātu̱rna ṛ̱te sa̱ptatha̍sya mā̱yāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 3

sa vāja̱ṃ yātāpa̍duṣpadā̱ yantsva̍rṣātā̱ pari̍ ṣadatsani̱ṣyan |

a̱na̱rvā yaccha̱tadu̍rasya̱ vedo̱ ghnañchi̱śnade̍vām̐ a̱bhi varpa̍sā̱ bhūt

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 4

sa ya̱hvyo̱3'vanī̱rgoṣvarvā ''ju̍hoti pradha̱nyā̍su̱ sasri̍: |

a̱pādo̱ yatra̱ yujyā̍so'ra̱thā dro̱ṇya̍śvāsa̱ īra̍te ghṛ̱taṃ vāḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 5

sa ru̱drebhi̱raśa̍stavāra̱ ṛbhvā̍ hi̱tvī gaya̍mā̱rea̍vadya̱ āgā̍t |

va̱mrasya̍ manye mithu̱nā viva̍vrī̱ anna̍ma̱bhītyā̍rodayanmuṣā̱yan

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 6

sa iddāsa̍ṃ tuvī̱rava̱ṃ pati̱rdanṣa̍l̤a̱kṣaṃ tri̍śī̱rṣāṇa̍ṃ damanyat |

a̱sya tri̱to nvoja̍sā vṛdhā̱no vi̱pā va̍rā̱hamayo̍agrayā han

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 7

sa druhva̍ṇe̱ manu̍ṣa ūrdhvasā̱na ā sā̍viṣadarśasā̱nāya̱ śaru̍m |

sa nṛta̍mo̱ nahu̍ṣo̱'smatsujā̍ta̱: puro̍'bhina̱darha̍ndasyu̱hatye̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 8

so a̱bhriyo̱ na yava̍sa uda̱nyankṣayā̍ya gā̱tuṃ vi̱danno̍ a̱sme |

upa̱ yatsīda̱dindu̱ṃ śarī̍raiḥ śye̱no'yo̍pāṣṭirhanti̱ dasyū̍n

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 9

sa vrādha̍taḥ śavasā̱nebhi̍rasya̱ kutsā̍ya̱ śuṣṇa̍ṃ kṛ̱paṇe̱ parā̍dāt |

a̱yaṃ ka̱vima̍nayaccha̱syamā̍na̱matka̱ṃ yo a̍sya̱ sani̍to̱ta nṛ̱ṇām

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 10

a̱yaṃ da̍śa̱syannarye̍bhirasya da̱smo de̱vebhi̱rvaru̍ṇo̱ na mā̱yī |

a̱yaṃ ka̱nīna̍ ṛtu̱pā a̍ve̱dyami̍mītā̱raru̱ṃ yaścatu̍ṣpāt

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 11

a̱sya stome̍bhirauśi̱ja ṛ̱jiśvā̍ vra̱jaṃ da̍rayadvṛṣa̱bheṇa̱ pipro̍: |

sutvā̱ yadya̍ja̱to dī̱daya̱dgīḥ pura̍ iyā̱no a̱bhi varpa̍sā̱ bhūt

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 99 | **Verse:** 12

e̱vā ma̱ho a̍sura va̱kṣathā̍ya vamra̱kaḥ pa̱ḍbhirupa̍ sarpa̱dindra̍m |

sa i̍yā̱naḥ ka̍rati sva̱stima̍smā̱ iṣa̱mūrja̍ṃ sukṣi̱tiṃ viśva̱mābhā̍:
