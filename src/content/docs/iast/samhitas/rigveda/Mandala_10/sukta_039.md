# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 39

**14 kākṣīvatī ghoṣā| aśvinau| jagatī, 14 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 1

yo vā̱ṃ pari̍jmā su̱vṛda̍śvinā̱ ratho̍ do̱ṣāmu̱ṣāso̱ havyo̍ ha̱viṣma̍tā |
śa̱śva̱tta̱māsa̱stamu̍ vāmi̱daṃ va̱yaṃ pi̱turna nāma̍ su̱hava̍ṃ havāmahe

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 2

co̱daya̍taṃ sū̱nṛtā̱: pinva̍ta̱ṃ dhiya̱ utpura̍ṃdhīrīrayata̱ṃ tadu̍śmasi |

ya̱śasa̍ṃ bhā̱gaṃ kṛ̍ṇutaṃ no aśvinā̱ soma̱ṃ na cāru̍ṃ ma̱ghava̍tsu naskṛtam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 3

a̱mā̱jura̍ścidbhavatho yu̱vaṃ bhago̍'nā̱śości̍davi̱tārā̍pa̱masya̍ cit |

a̱ndhasya̍ cinnāsatyā kṛ̱śasya̍ cidyu̱vāmidā̍hurbhi̱ṣajā̍ ru̱tasya̍ cit

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 4

yu̱vaṃ cyavā̍naṃ sa̱naya̱ṃ yathā̱ ratha̱ṃ puna̱ryuvā̍naṃ ca̱rathā̍ya takṣathuḥ |

niṣṭau̱gryamū̍hathura̱dbhyaspari̱ viśvettā vā̱ṃ sava̍neṣu pra̱vācyā̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 5

pu̱rā̱ṇā vā̍ṃ vī̱ryā̱3 pra bra̍vā̱ jane'tho̍ hāsathurbhi̱ṣajā̍ mayo̱bhuvā̍ |

tā vā̱ṃ nu navyā̱vava̍se karāmahe̱'yaṃ nā̍satyā̱ śrada̱riryathā̱ dadha̍t

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 6

i̱yaṃ vā̍mahve śṛṇu̱taṃ me̍ aśvinā pu̱trāye̍va pi̱tarā̱ mahya̍ṃ śikṣatam |

anā̍pi̱rajñā̍ asajā̱tyāma̍tiḥ pu̱rā tasyā̍ a̱bhiśa̍ste̱rava̍ spṛtam

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 7

yu̱vaṃ rathe̍na vima̱dāya̍ śu̱ndhyuva̱ṃ nyū̍hathuḥ purumi̱trasya̱ yoṣa̍ṇām |

yu̱vaṃ hava̍ṃ vadhrima̱tyā a̍gacchataṃ yu̱vaṃ suṣu̍tiṃ cakrathu̱: pura̍ṃdhaye

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 8

yu̱vaṃ vipra̍sya jara̱ṇāmu̍pe̱yuṣa̱: puna̍: ka̱lera̍kṛṇuta̱ṃ yuva̱dvaya̍: |

yu̱vaṃ vanda̍namṛśya̱dādudū̍pathuryu̱vaṃ sa̱dyo vi̱śpalā̱meta̍ve kṛthaḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 9

yu̱vamṛ̱bīsa̍mu̱ta ta̱ptamatra̍ya̱ oma̍nvantaṃ cakrathuḥ sa̱ptava̍dhraye

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 10

ca̱rkṛtya̍ṃ dadathurdrāva̱yatsa̍kha̱ṃ bhaga̱ṃ na nṛbhyo̱ havya̍ṃ mayo̱bhuva̍m

## Verse 13
