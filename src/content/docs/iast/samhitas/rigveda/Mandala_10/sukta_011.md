# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 11

**9 āṅgirhavirdhānaḥ| agniḥ| jagatī, 7-9  triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 1

vṛṣā̱ vṛṣṇe̍ duduhe̱ doha̍sā di̱vaḥ payā̍ṃsi ya̱hvo adi̍te̱radā̍bhyaḥ |
viśva̱ṃ sa ve̍da̱ varu̍ṇo̱ yathā̍ dhi̱yā sa ya̱jñiyo̍ yajatu ya̱jñiyā̍m̐ ṛ̱tūn

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 2

i̱ṣṭasya̱ madhye̱ adi̍ti̱rni dhā̍tu no̱ bhrātā̍ no jye̱ṣṭhaḥ pra̍tha̱mo vi vo̍cati

## Verse 3

## Verse 4
