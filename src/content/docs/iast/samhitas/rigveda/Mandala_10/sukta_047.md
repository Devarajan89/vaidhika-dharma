# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 47

**8 saptagurāṃgirasaḥ| vaikuṇṭha indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 1

ja̱gṛ̱bhmā te̱ dakṣi̍ṇamindra̱ hasta̍ṃ vasū̱yavo̍ vasupate̱ vasū̍nām |
vi̱dmā hi tvā̱ gopa̍tiṃ śūra̱ gonā̍ma̱smabhya̍ṃ ci̱traṃ vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ ra̱yiṃ dā̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 2

svā̱yu̱dhaṃ svava̍saṃ sunī̱thaṃ catu̍:samudraṃ dha̱ruṇa̍ṃ rayī̱ṇām |

ca̱rkṛtya̱ṃ śaṃsya̱ṃ bhūri̍vārama̱smabhya̍ṃ ci̱traṃ vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ ra̱yiṃ dā̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 3

su̱brahmā̍ṇaṃ de̱vava̍ntaṃ bṛ̱hanta̍mu̱ruṃ ga̍bhī̱raṃ pṛ̱thubu̍dhnamindra |

śru̱taṛ̍ṣimu̱grama̍bhimāti̱ṣāha̍ma̱smabhya̍ṃ ci̱traṃ vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ ra̱yiṃ dā̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 4

sa̱nadvā̍ja̱ṃ vipra̍vīra̱ṃ taru̍traṃ dhana̱spṛta̍ṃ śūśu̱vāṃsa̍ṃ su̱dakṣa̍m |

da̱syu̱hana̍ṃ pū̱rbhida̍mindra sa̱tyama̱smabhya̍ṃ ci̱traṃ vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ ra̱yiṃ dā̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 5

aśvā̍vantaṃ ra̱thina̍ṃ vī̱rava̍ntaṃ saha̱sriṇa̍ṃ śa̱tina̱ṃ vāja̍mindra |

bha̱dravrā̍ta̱ṃ vipra̍vīraṃ sva̱rṣāma̱smabhya̍ṃ ci̱traṃ vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ ra̱yiṃ dā̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 6

pra sa̱ptagu̍mṛ̱tadhī̍tiṃ sume̱dhāṃ bṛha̱spati̍ṃ ma̱tiracchā̍ jigāti |

ya ā̍ṅgira̱so nama̍sopa̱sadyo̱'smabhya̍ṃ ci̱traṃ vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ ra̱yiṃ dā̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 7

vanī̍vāno̱ mama̍ dū̱tāsa̱ indra̱ṃ stomā̍ścaranti suma̱tīri̍yā̱nāḥ |

hṛ̱di̱spṛśo̱ mana̍sā va̱cyamā̍nā a̱smabhya̍ṃ ci̱traṃ vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ ra̱yiṃ dā̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 8

yattvā̱ yāmi̍ da̱ddhi tanna̍ indra bṛ̱hanta̱ṃ kṣaya̱masa̍ma̱ṃ janā̍nām |

a̱bhi taddyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī gṛ̍ṇītāma̱smabhya̍ṃ ci̱traṃ vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ ra̱yiṃ dā̍:
