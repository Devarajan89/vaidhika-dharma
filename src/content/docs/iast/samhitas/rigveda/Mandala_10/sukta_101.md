# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 101

**12 budhaḥ saumyaḥ| viśve devā, ṛtvijo vā | triṣṭup 4, 6 gāyatrī, 5 bṛhatī, 9,12 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 1

udbu̍dhyadhva̱ṃ sama̍nasaḥ sakhāya̱: sama̱gnimi̍ndhvaṃ ba̱hava̱: sanī̍l̤āḥ |
da̱dhi̱krāma̱gnimu̱ṣasa̍ṃ ca de̱vīmindrā̍va̱to'va̍se̱ ni hva̍ye vaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 2

ma̱ndrā kṛ̍ṇudhva̱ṃ dhiya̱ ā ta̍nudhva̱ṃ nāva̍maritra̱para̍ṇīṃ kṛṇudhvam |

iṣkṛ̍ṇudhva̱māyu̱dhāra̍ṃ kṛṇudhva̱ṃ prāñca̍ṃ ya̱jñaṃ pra ṇa̍yatā sakhāyaḥ

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 3

gi̱rā ca̍ śru̱ṣṭiḥ sabha̍rā̱ asa̍nno̱ nedī̍ya̱ itsṛ̱ṇya̍: pa̱kvameyā̍t

## Verse 4

## Verse 8

pura̍: kṛṇudhva̱māya̍sī̱radhṛ̍ṣṭā̱ mā va̍: susroccama̱so dṛṃha̍tā̱ tam

## Verse 9
