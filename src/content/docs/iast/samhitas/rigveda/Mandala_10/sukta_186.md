# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 186

**3 vātāyana ulaḥ| vāyuḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 186 | **Verse:** 1

vāta̱ ā vā̍tu bheṣa̱jaṃ śa̱mbhu ma̍yo̱bhu no̍ hṛ̱de | pra ṇa̱ āyū̍ṃṣi tāriṣat

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 186 | **Verse:** 2

u̱ta vā̍ta pi̱tāsi̍ na u̱ta bhrāto̱ta na̱: sakhā̍ | sa no̍ jī̱vāta̍ve kṛdhi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 186 | **Verse:** 3

yada̱do vā̍ta te gṛ̱he̱3'mṛta̍sya ni̱dhirhi̱taḥ | tato̍ no dehi jī̱vase̍
