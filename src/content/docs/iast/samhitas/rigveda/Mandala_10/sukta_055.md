# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 55

**8 bṛhaduktho vāmadevyaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 1

dū̱re tannāma̱ guhya̍ṃ parā̱cairyattvā̍ bhī̱te ahva̍yetāṃ vayo̱dhai |
uda̍stabhnāḥ pṛthi̱vīṃ dyāma̱bhīke̱ bhrātu̍: pu̱trānma̍ghavantitviṣā̱ṇaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 2

ma̱hattannāma̱ guhya̍ṃ puru̱spṛgyena̍ bhū̱taṃ ja̱nayo̱ yena̱ bhavya̍m |

pra̱tnaṃ jā̱taṃ jyoti̱ryada̍sya pri̱yaṃ pri̱yāḥ sama̍viśanta̱ pañca̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 3

ā roda̍sī apṛṇā̱dota madhya̱ṃ pañca̍ de̱vām̐ ṛ̍tu̱śaḥ sa̱ptasa̍pta |

catu̍striṃśatā puru̱dhā vi ca̍ṣṭe̱ sarū̍peṇa̱ jyoti̍ṣā̱ vivra̍tena

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 4

yadu̍ṣa̱ auccha̍: pratha̱mā vi̱bhānā̱maja̍nayo̱ yena̍ pu̱ṣṭasya̍ pu̱ṣṭam |

yatte̍ jāmi̱tvamava̍ra̱ṃ para̍syā ma̱hanma̍ha̱tyā a̍sura̱tvameka̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 5

vi̱dhuṃ da̍drā̱ṇaṃ sama̍ne bahū̱nāṃ yuvā̍na̱ṃ santa̍ṃ pali̱to ja̍gāra |

de̱vasya̍ paśya̱ kāvya̍ṃ mahi̱tvā'dyā ma̱māra̱ sa hyaḥ samā̍na

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 6

śākma̍nā śā̱ko a̍ru̱ṇaḥ su̍pa̱rṇa ā yo ma̱haḥ śūra̍: sa̱nādanī̍l̤aḥ |

yacci̱keta̍ sa̱tyamittanna mogha̱ṃ vasu̍ spā̱rhamu̱ta jeto̱ta dātā̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 7

aibhi̍rdade̱ vṛṣṇyā̱ pauṃsyā̍ni̱ yebhi̱raukṣa̍dvṛtra̱hatyā̍ya va̱jrī |

ye karma̍ṇaḥ kri̱yamā̍ṇasya ma̱hna ṛ̍teka̱rmamu̱dajā̍yanta de̱vāḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 55 | **Verse:** 8

yu̱jā karmā̍ṇi ja̱naya̍nvi̱śvaujā̍ aśasti̱hā vi̱śvama̍nāsturā̱ṣāṭ |

pī̱tvī soma̍sya di̱va ā vṛ̍dhā̱naḥ śūro̱ niryu̱dhādha̍ma̱ddasyū̍n
