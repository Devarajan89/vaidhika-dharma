# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 138

**6 aṃga auravaḥ|indraḥ| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 138 | **Verse:** 1

tava̱ tya i̍ndra sa̱khyeṣu̱ vahna̍ya ṛ̱taṃ ma̍nvā̱nā vya̍dardirurva̱lam |
yatrā̍ daśa̱syannu̱ṣaso̍ ri̱ṇanna̱paḥ kutsā̍ya̱ manma̍nna̱hya̍śca da̱ṃsaya̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 138 | **Verse:** 2

avā̍sṛjaḥ pra̱sva̍: śva̱ñcayo̍ gi̱rīnudā̍ja u̱srā api̍bo̱ madhu̍ pri̱yam |

ava̍rdhayo va̱nino̍ asya̱ daṃsa̍sā śu̱śoca̱ sūrya̍ ṛ̱tajā̍tayā gi̱rā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 138 | **Verse:** 3

vi sūryo̱ madhye̍ amuca̱dratha̍ṃ di̱vo vi̱daddā̱sāya̍ prati̱māna̱mārya̍: |

dṛ̱l̤hāni̱ pipro̱rasu̍rasya mā̱yina̱ indro̱ vyā̍syaccakṛ̱vām̐ ṛ̱jiśva̍nā

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 138 | **Verse:** 4

anā̍dhṛṣṭāni dhṛṣi̱to vyā̍syanni̱dhīm̐rade̍vām̐ amṛṇada̱yāsya̍: |

mā̱seva̱ sūryo̱ vasu̱ purya̱mā da̍de gṛṇā̱naḥ śatrū̍m̐raśṛṇādvi̱rukma̍tā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 138 | **Verse:** 5

ayu̍ddhaseno vi̱bhvā̍ vibhinda̱tā dāśa̍dvṛtra̱hā tujyā̍ni tejate |

indra̍sya̱ vajrā̍dabibhedabhi̱śnatha̱: prākrā̍macchu̱ndhyūraja̍hādu̱ṣā ana̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 138 | **Verse:** 6

e̱tā tyā te̱ śrutyā̍ni̱ keva̍lā̱ yadeka̱ eka̱makṛ̍ṇoraya̱jñam |

mā̱sāṃ vi̱dhāna̍madadhā̱ adhi̱ dyavi̱ tvayā̱ vibhi̍nnaṃ bharati pra̱dhiṃ pi̱tā
