# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 182

**3 tapurmūrdhā bārhaspatyaḥ| bṛhaspatiḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 182 | **Verse:** 1

bṛha̱spati̍rnayatu du̱rgahā̍ ti̱raḥ puna̍rneṣada̱ghaśa̍ṃsāya̱ manma̍ |
kṣi̱padaśa̍sti̱mapa̍ durma̱tiṃ ha̱nnathā̍ kara̱dyaja̍mānāya̱ śaṃ yoḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 182 | **Verse:** 2

narā̱śaṃso̍ no'vatu prayā̱je śaṃ no̍ astvanuyā̱jo have̍ṣu |

kṣi̱padaśa̍sti̱mapa̍ durma̱tiṃ ha̱nnathā̍ kara̱dyaja̍mānāya̱ śaṃ yoḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 182 | **Verse:** 3

tapu̍rmūrdhā tapatu ra̱kṣaso̱ ye bra̍hma̱dviṣa̱: śara̍ve̱ hanta̱vā u̍ |

kṣi̱padaśa̍sti̱mapa̍ durma̱tiṃ ha̱nnathā̍ kara̱dyaja̍mānāya̱ śaṃ yoḥ
