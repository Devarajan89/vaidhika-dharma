# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 78

**8  syūmaraśmirbhārgavaḥ| marutaḥ| triṣṭup , 2,5-7 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 78 | **Verse:** 1

viprā̍so̱ na manma̍bhiḥ svā̱dhyo̍ devā̱vyo̱3 na ya̱jñaiḥ svapna̍saḥ |
rājā̍no̱ na ci̱trāḥ su̍sa̱ṃdṛśa̍: kṣitī̱nāṃ na maryā̍ are̱pasa̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 78 | **Verse:** 2

a̱gnirna ye bhrāja̍sā ru̱kmava̍kṣaso̱ vātā̍so̱ na sva̱yuja̍: sa̱dyaū̍tayaḥ |

pra̱jñā̱tāro̱ na jyeṣṭhā̍: sunī̱taya̍: su̱śarmā̍ṇo̱ na somā̍ ṛ̱taṃ ya̱te

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 78 | **Verse:** 3

vātā̍so̱ na ye dhuna̍yo jiga̱tnavo̍'gnī̱nāṃ na ji̱hvā vi̍ro̱kiṇa̍: |

varma̍ṇvanto̱ na yo̱dhāḥ śimī̍vantaḥ pitṝ̱ṇāṃ na śaṃsā̍: surā̱taya̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 78 | **Verse:** 4

va̱re̱yavo̱ na maryā̍ ghṛta̱pruṣo̍'bhisva̱rtāro̍ a̱rkaṃ na su̱ṣṭubha̍:

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 78 | **Verse:** 5

aśvā̍so̱ na ye jyeṣṭhā̍sa ā̱śavo̍ didhi̱ṣavo̱ na ra̱thya̍: su̱dāna̍vaḥ |

āpo̱ na ni̱mnairu̱dabhi̍rjiga̱tnavo̍ vi̱śvarū̍pā̱ aṅgi̍raso̱ na sāma̍bhiḥ

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 78 | **Verse:** 6

śi̱śūlā̱ na krī̱l̤aya̍: sumā̱taro̍ mahāgrā̱mo na yāma̍nnu̱ta tvi̱ṣā

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 78 | **Verse:** 7

## Verse 8
