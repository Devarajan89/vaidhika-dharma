# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 148

**5 pṛthurvainyaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 148 | **Verse:** 1

su̱ṣvā̱ṇāsa̍ indra stu̱masi̍ tvā sasa̱vāṃsa̍śca tuvinṛmṇa̱ vāja̍m |
ā no̍ bhara suvi̱taṃ yasya̍ cā̱kantmanā̱ tanā̍ sanuyāma̱ tvotā̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 148 | **Verse:** 2

ṛ̱ṣvastvami̍ndra śūra jā̱to dāsī̱rviśa̱: sūrye̍ṇa sahyāḥ |

guhā̍ hi̱taṃ guhya̍ṃ gū̱l̤hama̱psu bi̍bhṛ̱masi̍ pra̱srava̍ṇe̱ na soma̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 148 | **Verse:** 3

a̱ryo vā̱ giro̍ a̱bhya̍rca vi̱dvānṛṣī̍ṇā̱ṃ vipra̍: suma̱tiṃ ca̍kā̱naḥ |

te syā̍ma̱ ye ra̱ṇaya̍nta̱ somai̍re̱nota tubhya̍ṃ rathol̤ha bha̱kṣaiḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 148 | **Verse:** 4

i̱mā brahme̍ndra̱ tubhya̍ṃ śaṃsi̱ dā nṛbhyo̍ nṛ̱ṇāṃ śū̍ra̱ śava̍: |

tebhi̍rbhava̱ sakra̍tu̱ryeṣu̍ cā̱kannu̱ta trā̍yasva gṛṇa̱ta u̱ta stīn

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 148 | **Verse:** 5

śru̱dhī hava̍mindra śūra̱ pṛthyā̍ u̱ta sta̍vase ve̱nyasyā̱rkaiḥ |

ā yaste̱ yoni̍ṃ ghṛ̱tava̍nta̱masvā̍rū̱rmirna nimnairdra̍vayanta̱ vakvā̍:
