# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 6

**7 trita āptyaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 1

a̱yaṃ sa yasya̱ śarma̱nnavo̍bhira̱gneredha̍te jari̱tābhiṣṭau̍ |
jyeṣṭhe̍bhi̱ryo bhā̱nubhi̍ṝṣū̱ṇāṃ pa̱ryeti̱ pari̍vīto vi̱bhāvā̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 2

yo bhā̱nubhi̍rvi̱bhāvā̍ vi̱bhātya̱gnirde̱vebhi̍ṝ̱tāvāja̍sraḥ |

ā yo vi̱vāya̍ sa̱khyā sakhi̱bhyo'pa̍rihvṛto̱ atyo̱ na sapti̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 3

īśe̱ yo viśva̍syā de̱vavī̍te̱rīśe̍ vi̱śvāyu̍ru̱ṣaso̱ vyu̍ṣṭau | ā yasmi̍nma̱nā ha̱vīṃṣya̱gnāvari̍ṣṭarathaḥ ska̱bhnāti̍ śū̱ṣaiḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 4

śū̱ṣebhi̍rvṛ̱dho ju̍ṣā̱ṇo a̱rkairde̱vām̐ acchā̍ raghu̱patvā̍ jigāti |

ma̱ndro hotā̱ sa ju̱hvā̱3 yaji̍ṣṭha̱: sammi̍ślo a̱gnirā ji̍gharti de̱vān

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 5

tamu̱srāmindra̱ṃ na reja̍mānama̱gniṃ gī̱rbhirnamo̍bhi̱rā kṛ̍ṇudhvam |

ā yaṃ viprā̍so ma̱tibhi̍rgṛ̱ṇanti̍ jā̱tave̍dasaṃ ju̱hva̍ṃ sa̱hānā̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 6

saṃ yasmi̱nviśvā̱ vasū̍ni ja̱gmurvāje̱ nāśvā̱: saptī̍vanta̱ evai̍: |

a̱sme ū̱tīrindra̍vātatamā arvācī̱nā a̍gna̱ ā kṛ̍ṇuṣva

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 7

adhā̱ hya̍gne ma̱hnā ni̱ṣadyā̍ sa̱dyo ja̍jñā̱no havyo̍ ba̱bhūtha̍ |

taṃ te̍ de̱vāso̱ anu̱ keta̍māya̱nnadhā̍vardhanta pratha̱māsa̱ ūmā̍:
