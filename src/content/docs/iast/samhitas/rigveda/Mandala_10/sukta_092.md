# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 92

**15 śāryāto mānavaḥ| viśvedevāḥ| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 1

ya̱jñasya̍ vo ra̱thya̍ṃ vi̱śpati̍ṃ vi̱śāṃ hotā̍rama̱ktorati̍thiṃ vi̱bhāva̍sum |
śoca̱ñchuṣkā̍su̱ hari̍ṇīṣu̱ jarbhu̍ra̱dvṛṣā̍ ke̱turya̍ja̱to dyāma̍śāyata

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 2

i̱mama̍ñja̱spāmu̱bhaye̍ akṛṇvata dha̱rmāṇa̍ma̱gniṃ vi̱datha̍sya̱ sādha̍nam |

a̱ktuṃ na ya̱hvamu̱ṣasa̍: pu̱rohi̍ta̱ṃ tanū̱napā̍tamaru̱ṣasya̍ niṃsate

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 3

bal̤a̍sya nī̱thā vi pa̱ṇeśca̍ manmahe va̱yā a̍sya̱ prahu̍tā āsu̱ratta̍ve |

ya̱dā gho̱rāso̍ amṛta̱tvamāśa̱tādijjana̍sya̱ daivya̍sya carkiran

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 4

ṛ̱tasya̱ hi prasi̍ti̱rdyauru̱ru vyaco̱ namo̍ ma̱hya1rama̍ti̱: panī̍yasī |

indro̍ mi̱tro varu̍ṇa̱: saṃ ci̍kitri̱re'tho̱ bhaga̍: savi̱tā pū̱tada̍kṣasaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 5

pra ru̱dreṇa̍ ya̱yinā̍ yanti̱ sindha̍vasti̱ro ma̱hīma̱rama̍tiṃ dadhanvire |

yebhi̱: pari̍jmā pari̱yannu̱ru jrayo̱ vi roru̍vajja̱ṭhare̱ viśva̍mu̱kṣate̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 6

krā̱ṇā ru̱drā ma̱ruto̍ vi̱śvakṛ̍ṣṭayo di̱vaḥ śye̱nāso̱ asu̍rasya nī̱l̤aya̍: |

tebhi̍ścaṣṭe̱ varu̍ṇo mi̱tro a̍rya̱mendro̍ de̱vebhi̍rarva̱śebhi̱rarva̍śaḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 7

indre̱ bhuja̍ṃ śaśamā̱nāsa̍ āśata̱ sūro̱ dṛśī̍ke̱ vṛṣa̍ṇaśca̱ pauṃsye̍ |

pra ye nva̍syā̱rhaṇā̍ tatakṣi̱re yuja̱ṃ vajra̍ṃ nṛ̱ṣada̍neṣu kā̱rava̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 8

sūra̍ści̱dā ha̱rito̍ asya rīrama̱dindrā̱dā kaści̍dbhayata̱ja tavī̍yasaḥ |

bhī̱masya̱ vṛṣṇo̍ ja̱ṭharā̍dabhi̱śvaso̍ di̱vedi̍ve̱ sahu̍riḥ sta̱nnabā̍dhitaḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 9

stoma̍ṃ vo a̱dya ru̱drāya̱ śikva̍se kṣa̱yadvī̍rāya̱ nama̍sā didiṣṭana |

yebhi̍: śi̱vaḥ svavā̍m̐ eva̱yāva̍bhirdi̱vaḥ siṣa̍kti̱ svaya̍śā̱ nikā̍mabhiḥ

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 10

ya̱jñairatha̍rvā pratha̱mo vi dhā̍rayadde̱vā dakṣai̱rbhṛga̍va̱: saṃ ci̍kitrire

## Verse 11
