# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 121

**10 hiraṇyagarbhaḥ prājāpatya:| kaḥ(prajāpatiḥ)| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 1

hi̱ra̱ṇya̱ga̱rbhaḥ sama̍varta̱tāgre̍ bhū̱tasya̍ jā̱taḥ pati̱reka̍ āsīt |
sa dā̍dhāra pṛthi̱vīṃ dyāmu̱temāṃ kasmai̍ de̱vāya̍ ha̱viṣā̍ vidhema

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 2

ya ā̍tma̱dā ba̍la̱dā yasya̱ viśva̍ u̱pāsa̍te pra̱śiṣa̱ṃ yasya̍ de̱vāḥ |

yasya̍ chā̱yāmṛta̱ṃ yasya̍ mṛ̱tyuḥ kasmai̍ de̱vāya̍ ha̱viṣā̍ vidhema

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 3

yaḥ prā̍ṇa̱to ni̍miṣa̱to ma̍hi̱tvaika̱ idrājā̱ jaga̍to ba̱bhūva̍ |

ya īśe̍ a̱sya dvi̱pada̱ścatu̍ṣpada̱: kasmai̍ de̱vāya̍ ha̱viṣā̍ vidhema

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 4

yasye̱me hi̱mava̍nto mahi̱tvā yasya̍ samu̱draṃ ra̱sayā̍ sa̱hāhuḥ |

yasye̱māḥ pra̱diśo̱ yasya̍ bā̱hū kasmai̍ de̱vāya̍ ha̱viṣā̍ vidhema

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 5

yena̱ dyauru̱grā pṛ̍thi̱vī ca̍ dṛ̱l̤hā yena̱ sva̍ stabhi̱taṃ yena̱ nāka̍: |

yo a̱ntari̍kṣe̱ raja̍so vi̱māna̱: kasmai̍ de̱vāya̍ ha̱viṣā̍ vidhema

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 6

yaṃ kranda̍sī̱ ava̍sā tastabhā̱ne a̱bhyaikṣe̍tā̱ṃ mana̍sā̱ reja̍māne |

yatrādhi̱ sūra̱ udi̍to vi̱bhāti̱ kasmai̍ de̱vāya̍ ha̱viṣā̍ vidhema

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 7

āpo̍ ha̱ yadbṛ̍ha̱tīrviśva̱māya̱ngarbha̱ṃ dadhā̍nā ja̱naya̍ntīra̱gnim |

tato̍ de̱vānā̱ṃ sama̍varta̱tāsu̱reka̱: kasmai̍ de̱vāya̍ ha̱viṣā̍ vidhema

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 8

yaści̱dāpo̍ mahi̱nā pa̱ryapa̍śya̱ddakṣa̱ṃ dadhā̍nā ja̱naya̍ntīrya̱jñam |

yo de̱veṣvadhi̍ de̱va eka̱ āsī̱tkasmai̍ de̱vāya̍ ha̱viṣā̍ vidhema

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 9

mā no̍ hiṃsījjani̱tā yaḥ pṛ̍thi̱vyā yo vā̱ diva̍ṃ sa̱tyadha̍rmā ja̱jāna̍ |

yaścā̱paśca̱ndrā bṛ̍ha̱tīrja̱jāna̱ kasmai̍ de̱vāya̍ ha̱viṣā̍ vidhema

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 10

prajā̍pate̱ na tvade̱tānya̱nyo viśvā̍ jā̱tāni̱ pari̱ tā ba̍bhūva |

yatkā̍māste juhu̱mastanno̍ astu va̱yaṃ syā̍ma̱ pata̍yo rayī̱ṇām
