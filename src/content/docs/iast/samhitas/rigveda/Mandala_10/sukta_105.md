# Maṇḍala 10 — Sūktam 105

**11 kautso durmitraḥ sumitro vā| indraḥ| uṣṇik, 1 gāyatrī vā 2,7 pipīlikamadhyā, 11 triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 105 | **Verse:** 1

ka̱dā va̍so sto̱traṃ harya̍ta̱ āva̍ śma̱śā ru̍dha̱dvāḥ | dī̱rghaṃ su̱taṃ vā̱tāpyā̍ya

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 105 | **Verse:** 2

harī̱ yasya̍ su̱yujā̱ vivra̍tā̱ verarva̱ntānu̱ śepā̍ | u̱bhā ra̱jī na ke̱śinā̱ pati̱rdan

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 105 | **Verse:** 3

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 105 | **Verse:** 4

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 10 | **Sukta:** 105 | **Verse:** 5
