# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 1

**20 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| agniḥ, 2-5 agnīvaruṇau vā| triṣṭup, 1 agniḥ, 2 atijagatī, 3 dhṛtiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 1

tvāṃ hya̍gne̱ sada̱mitsa̍ma̱nyavo̍ de̱vāso̍ de̱vama̍ra̱tiṃ nye̍ri̱ra iti̱ kratvā̍ nyeri̱re |
ama̍rtyaṃ yajata̱ martye̱ṣvā de̱vamāde̍vaṃ janata̱ prace̍tasa̱ṃ viśva̱māde̍vaṃ janata̱ prace̍tasam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 2

sa bhrāta̍ra̱ṃ varu̍ṇamagna̱ ā va̍vṛtsva de̱vām̐ acchā̍ suma̱tī ya̱jñava̍nasa̱ṃ jyeṣṭha̍ṃ ya̱jñava̍nasam |

ṛ̱tāvā̍namādi̱tyaṃ ca̍rṣaṇī̱dhṛta̱ṃ rājā̍naṃ carṣaṇī̱dhṛta̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 3

sakhe̱ sakhā̍yama̱bhyā va̍vṛtsvā̱śuṃ na ca̱kraṃ rathye̍va̱ raṃhyā̱smabhya̍ṃ dasma̱ raṃhyā̍ |

agne̍ mṛl̤ī̱kaṃ varu̍ṇe̱ sacā̍ vido ma̱rutsu̍ vi̱śvabhā̍nuṣu |

to̱kāya̍ tu̱je śu̍śucāna̱ śaṃ kṛ̍dhya̱smabhya̍ṃ dasma̱ śaṃ kṛ̍dhi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 4

tvaṃ no̍ agne̱ varu̍ṇasya vi̱dvān de̱vasya̱ hel̤o'va̍ yāsisīṣṭhāḥ |

yaji̍ṣṭho̱ vahni̍tama̱: śośu̍cāno̱ viśvā̱ dveṣā̍ṃsi̱ pra mu̍mugdhya̱smat

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 5

sa tvaṃ no̍ agne'va̱mo bha̍vo̱tī nedi̍ṣṭho a̱syā u̱ṣaso̱ vyu̍ṣṭau |

ava̍ yakṣva no̱ varu̍ṇa̱ṃ rarā̍ṇo vī̱hi mṛ̍l̤ī̱kaṃ su̱havo̍ na edhi

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 6

a̱sya śreṣṭhā̍ su̱bhaga̍sya sa̱ṃdṛg de̱vasya̍ ci̱trata̍mā̱ martye̍ṣu |

śuci̍ ghṛ̱taṃ na ta̱ptamaghnyā̍yāḥ spā̱rhā de̱vasya̍ ma̱ṃhane̍va dhe̱noḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 7

trira̍sya̱ tā pa̍ra̱mā sa̍nti sa̱tyā spā̱rhā de̱vasya̱ jani̍mānya̱gneḥ |

a̱na̱nte a̱ntaḥ pari̍vīta̱ āgā̱cchuci̍: śu̱kro a̱ryo roru̍cānaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 8

sa dū̱to viśveda̱bhi va̍ṣṭi̱ sadmā̱ hotā̱ hira̍ṇyaratho̱ raṃsu̍jihvaḥ |

ro̱hida̍śvo vapu̱ṣyo̍ vi̱bhāvā̱ sadā̍ ra̱ṇvaḥ pi̍tu̱matī̍va sa̱ṃsat

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 9

sa ce̍taya̱nmanu̍ṣo ya̱jñaba̍ndhu̱: pra taṃ ma̱hyā ra̍śa̱nayā̍ nayanti |

sa kṣe̍tyasya̱ duryā̍su̱ sādha̍n de̱vo marta̍sya sadhani̱tvamā̍pa

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 10

sa tū no̍ a̱gnirna̍yatu prajā̱nannacchā̱ ratna̍ṃ de̱vabha̍kta̱ṃ yada̍sya |

dhi̱yā yad viśve̍ a̱mṛtā̱ akṛ̍ṇva̱n dyauṣpi̱tā ja̍ni̱tā sa̱tyamu̍kṣan

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 11

sa jā̍yata pratha̱maḥ pa̱styā̍su ma̱ho bu̱dhne raja̍so a̱sya yonau̍ |

a̱pāda̍śī̱rṣā gu̱hamā̍no̱ antā̱ ''yoyu̍vāno vṛṣa̱bhasya̍ nī̱l̤e

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 12

pra śardha̍ ārta pratha̱maṃ vi̍pa̱nyām̐ ṛ̱tasya̱ yonā̍ vṛṣa̱bhasya̍ nī̱l̤e |

spā̱rho yuvā̍ vapu̱ṣyo̍ vi̱bhāvā̍ sa̱pta pri̱yāso̍'janayanta̱ vṛṣṇe̍

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 13

a̱smāka̱matra̍ pi̱taro̍ manu̱ṣyā̍ a̱bhi pra se̍durṛ̱tamā̍śuṣā̱ṇāḥ |

aśma̍vrajāḥ su̱dughā̍ va̱vre a̱ntarudu̱srā ā̍jannu̱ṣaso̍ huvā̱nāḥ

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 14

te ma̍rmṛjata dadṛ̱vāṃso̱ adri̱ṃ tade̍ṣāma̱nye a̱bhito̱ vi vo̍can |

pa̱śvaya̍ntrāso a̱bhi kā̱rama̍rcan vi̱danta̱ jyoti̍ścakṛ̱panta̍ dhī̱bhiḥ

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 15

te ga̍vya̱tā mana̍sā dṛ̱dhramu̱bdhaṃ gā ye̍mā̱naṃ pari̱ ṣanta̱madri̍m |

dṛ̱l̤ahaṃ naro̱ vaca̍sā̱ daivye̍na vra̱jaṃ goma̍ntamu̱śijo̱ vi va̍vruḥ

---

## Verse 16

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 16

te ma̍nvata pratha̱maṃ nāma̍ dhe̱nostriḥ sa̱pta mā̱tuḥ pa̍ra̱māṇi̍ vindan |

tajjā̍na̱tīra̱bhya̍nūṣata̱ vrā ā̱virbhu̍vadaru̱ṇīrya̱śasā̱ goḥ

---

## Verse 17

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 17

neśa̱t tamo̱ dudhi̍ta̱ṃ roca̍ta̱ dyaurudde̱vyā u̱ṣaso̍ bhā̱nura̍rta |

ā sūryo̍ bṛha̱tasti̍ṣṭha̱dajrā̍m̐ ṛ̱ju marte̍ṣu vṛji̱nā ca̱ paśya̍n

---

## Verse 18

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 18

ādit pa̱ścā bu̍budhā̱nā vya̍khya̱nnādid ratna̍ṃ dhārayanta̱ dyubha̍ktam |

viśve̱ viśvā̍su̱ duryā̍su de̱vā mitra̍ dhi̱ye va̍ruṇa sa̱tyama̍stu

---

## Verse 19

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 19

acchā̍ voceya śuśucā̱nama̱gniṃ hotā̍raṃ vi̱śvabha̍rasa̱ṃ yaji̍ṣṭham |

śucyūdho̍ atṛṇa̱nna gavā̱mandho̱ na pū̱taṃ pari̍ṣiktama̱ṃśoḥ

---

## Verse 20

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 20

viśve̍ṣā̱madi̍tirya̱jñiyā̍nā̱ṃ viśve̍ṣā̱mati̍thi̱rmānu̍ṣāṇām |

a̱gnirde̱vānā̱mava̍ āvṛṇā̱naḥ su̍mṛl̤ī̱ko bha̍vatu jā̱tave̍dāḥ
