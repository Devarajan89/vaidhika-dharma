# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 49

**6 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| indrābṛhaspatī| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 1

i̱daṃ vā̍mā̱sye̍ ha̱viḥ pri̱yami̍ndrābṛhaspatī | u̱kthaṃ mada̍śca śasyate

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 2

a̱yaṃ vā̱ṃ pari̍ ṣicyate̱ soma̍ indrābṛhaspatī | cāru̱rmadā̍ya pī̱taye̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 3

ā na̍ indrābṛhaspatī gṛ̱hamindra̍śca gacchatam | so̱ma̱pā soma̍pītaye

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 4

a̱sme i̍ndrābṛhaspatī ra̱yiṃ dha̍ttaṃ śata̱gvina̍m | aśvā̍vantaṃ saha̱sriṇa̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 5

indrā̱bṛha̱spatī̍ va̱yaṃ su̱te gī̱rbhirha̍vāmahe | a̱sya soma̍sya pī̱taye̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 6

soma̍mindrābṛhaspatī̱ piba̍taṃ dā̱śuṣo̍ gṛ̱he | mā̱daye̍thā̱ṃ tado̍kasā
