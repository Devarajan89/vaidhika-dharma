# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 10

**8 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| agniḥ| pādapaṃktiḥ, 4, 6, 7, uṣṇigvā, 5 mahāpadapaṃktiḥ, 8 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 1

agne̱ tama̱dyā'śva̱ṃ na stomai̱: kratu̱ṃ na bha̱draṃ hṛ̍di̱spṛśa̍m | ṛ̱dhyāmā̍ ta̱ ohai̍:

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 2

adhā̱ hya̍gne̱ krato̍rbha̱drasya̱ dakṣa̍sya sā̱dhoḥ | ra̱thīrṛ̱tasya̍ bṛha̱to ba̱bhūtha̍

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 3

e̱bhirno̍ a̱rkairbhavā̍ no a̱rvāṅsva1rṇa jyoti̍: | agne̱ viśve̍bhiḥ su̱manā̱ anī̍kaiḥ

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 4

ā̱bhiṣṭe̍ a̱dya gī̱rbhirgṛ̱ṇanto'gne̱ dāśe̍ma | pra te̍ di̱vo na sta̍nayanti̱ śuṣmā̍:

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 5

## Verse 6

## Verse 7

kṛ̱taṃ ci̱ddhi ṣmā̱ sane̍mi̱ dveṣo'gna̍ i̱noṣi̱ martā̍t | i̱tthā yaja̍mānādṛtāvaḥ
