# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 48

**5 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| vāyuḥ| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 1

vi̱hi hotrā̱ avī̍tā̱ vipo̱ na rāyo̍ a̱ryaḥ |
vāya̱vā ca̱ndreṇa̱ rathe̍na yā̱hi su̱tasya̍ pī̱taye̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 2

ni̱ryu̱vā̱ṇo aśa̍stīrni̱yutvā̱m̐ indra̍sārathiḥ |

vāya̱vā ca̱ndreṇa̱ rathe̍na yā̱hi su̱tasya̍ pī̱taye̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 3

anu̍ kṛ̱ṣṇe vasu̍dhitī ye̱māte̍ vi̱śvape̍śasā |

vāya̱vā ca̱ndreṇa̱ rathe̍na yā̱hi su̱tasya̍ pī̱taye̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 4

vaha̍ntu tvā mano̱yujo̍ yu̱ktāso̍ nava̱tirnava̍ |

vāya̱vā ca̱ndreṇa̱ rathe̍na yā̱hi su̱tasya̍ pī̱taye̍

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 5

vāyo̍ śa̱taṃ harī̍ṇāṃ yu̱vasva̱ poṣyā̍ṇām |

u̱ta vā̍ te saha̱sriṇo̱ ratha̱ ā yā̍tu̱ pāja̍sā
