# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 50

**11 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| bṛhaspatiḥ, 10-11 indrābṛhaspatī | triṣṭup, 10 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 1

yasta̱stambha̱ saha̍sā̱ vi jmo antā̱n bṛha̱spati̍striṣadha̱stho rave̍ṇa |
taṃ pra̱tnāsa̱ ṛṣa̍yo̱ dīdhyā̍nāḥ pu̱ro viprā̍ dadhire ma̱ndraji̍hvam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 2

dhu̱neta̍yaḥ suprake̱taṃ mada̍nto̱ bṛha̍spate a̱bhi ye na̍stata̱sre |

pṛṣa̍ntaṃ sṛ̱pramada̍bdhamū̱rvaṃ bṛha̍spate̱ rakṣa̍tādasya̱ yoni̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 3

bṛha̍spate̱ yā pa̍ra̱mā pa̍rā̱vadata̱ ā ta̍ ṛta̱spṛśo̱ ni ṣe̍duḥ |

tubhya̍ṃ khā̱tā a̍va̱tā adri̍dugdhā̱ madhva̍: ścotantya̱bhito̍ vira̱pśam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 4

bṛha̱spati̍: pratha̱maṃ jāya̍māno ma̱ho jyoti̍ṣaḥ para̱me vyo̍man |

sa̱ptāsya̍stuvijā̱to rave̍ṇa̱ vi sa̱ptara̍śmiradhama̱t tamā̍ṃsi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 5

sa su̱ṣṭubhā̱ sa ṛkva̍tā ga̱ṇena̍ va̱laṃ ru̍roja phali̱gaṃ rave̍ṇa |

bṛha̱spati̍ru̱sriyā̍ havya̱sūda̱: kani̍krada̱d vāva̍śatī̱rudā̍jat

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 6

e̱vā pi̱tre vi̱śvade̍vāya̱ vṛṣṇe̍ ya̱jñairvi̍dhema̱ nama̍sā ha̱virbhi̍: |

bṛha̍spate supra̱jā vī̱rava̍nto va̱yaṃ syā̍ma̱ pata̍yo rayī̱ṇām

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 7

sa id rājā̱ prati̍janyāni̱ viśvā̱ śuṣme̍ṇa tasthāva̱bhi vī̱rye̍ṇa |

bṛha̱spati̱ṃ yaḥ subhṛ̍taṃ bi̱bharti̍ valgū̱yati̱ vanda̍te pūrva̱bhāja̍m

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 8

sa it kṣe̍ti̱ sudhi̍ta̱ oka̍si̱ sve tasmā̱ il̤ā̍ pinvate viśva̱dānī̍m |

tasmai̱ viśa̍: sva̱yame̱vā na̍mante̱ yasmi̍n bra̱hmā rāja̍ni̱ pūrva̱ eti̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 9

apra̍tīto jayati̱ saṃ dhanā̍ni̱ prati̍janyānyu̱ta yā saja̍nyā |

a̱va̱syave̱ yo vari̍vaḥ kṛ̱ṇoti̍ bra̱hmaṇe̱ rājā̱ tama̍vanti de̱vāḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 10

indra̍śca̱ soma̍ṃ pibataṃ bṛhaspate̱'smin ya̱jñe ma̍ndasā̱nā vṛ̍ṣaṇvasū |

ā vā̍ṃ viśa̱ntvinda̍vaḥ svā̱bhuvo̱'sme ra̱yiṃ sarva̍vīra̱ṃ ni ya̍cchatam

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 11

bṛha̍spata indra̱ vardha̍taṃ na̱: sacā̱ sā vā̍ṃ suma̱tirbhū̍tva̱sme |

a̱vi̱ṣṭaṃ dhiyo̍ jigṛ̱taṃ pura̍ṃdhīrjaja̱stama̱ryo va̱nuṣā̱marā̍tīḥ
