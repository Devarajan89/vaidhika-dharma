# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 27

**5 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| śyenaḥ, 5 indro vā| triṣṭup, 5 śakvarī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 1

garbhe̱ nu sannanve̍ṣāmavedama̱haṃ de̱vānā̱ṃ jani̍māni̱ viśvā̍ |
śa̱taṃ mā̱ pura̱ āya̍sīrarakṣa̱nnadha̍ śye̱no ja̱vasā̱ nira̍dīyam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 2

na ghā̱ sa māmapa̱ joṣa̍ṃ jabhārā̱'bhīmā̍sa̱ tvakṣa̍sā vī̱rye̍ṇa |

ī̱rmā pura̍ṃdhirajahā̱darā̍tīru̱ta vātā̍m̐ atara̱cchūśu̍vānaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 3

ava̱ yacchye̱no asva̍nī̱dadha̱ dyorvi yad yadi̱ vāta̍ ū̱huḥ pura̍ṃdhim |

sṛ̱jad yada̍smā̱ ava̍ ha kṣi̱pajjyāṃ kṛ̱śānu̱rastā̱ mana̍sā bhura̱ṇyan

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 4

ṛ̱ji̱pya ī̱mindrā̍vato̱ na bhu̱jyuṃ śye̱no ja̍bhāra bṛha̱to adhi̱ ṣṇoḥ |

a̱ntaḥ pa̍tat pata̱trya̍sya pa̱rṇamadha̱ yāma̍ni̱ prasi̍tasya̱ tad veḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 5

adha̍ śve̱taṃ ka̱laśa̱ṃ gobhi̍ra̱ktamā̍pipyā̱naṃ ma̱ghavā̍ śu̱kramandha̍: |

a̱dhva̱ryubhi̱: praya̍ta̱ṃ madhvo̱ agra̱mindro̱ madā̍ya̱ prati̍ dha̱t piba̍dhyai̱ śūro̱ madā̍ya̱ prati̍ dha̱t piba̍dhyai
