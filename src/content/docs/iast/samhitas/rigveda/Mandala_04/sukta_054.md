# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 54

**6 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| savitā|jagatī, 6 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 1

abhū̍d de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā vandyo̱ nu na̍ i̱dānī̱mahna̍ upa̱vācyo̱ nṛbhi̍: |
vi yo ratnā̱ bhaja̍ti māna̱vebhya̱: śreṣṭha̍ṃ no̱ atra̱ dravi̍ṇa̱ṃ yathā̱ dadha̍t

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 2

de̱vebhyo̱ hi pra̍tha̱maṃ ya̱jñiye̍bhyo'mṛta̱tvaṃ su̱vasi̍ bhā̱gamu̍tta̱mam |

ādid dā̱māna̍ṃ savita̱rvyū̍rṇuṣe'nūcī̱nā jī̍vi̱tā mānu̍ṣebhyaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 3

aci̍ttī̱ yacca̍kṛ̱mā daivye̱ jane̍ dī̱nairdakṣai̱: prabhū̍tī pūruṣa̱tvatā̍ |

de̱veṣu̍ ca savita̱rmānu̍ṣeṣu ca̱ tvaṃ no̱ atra̍ suvatā̱danā̍gasaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 4

na pra̱miye̍ savi̱turdaivya̍sya̱ tad yathā̱ viśva̱ṃ bhuva̍naṃ dhārayi̱ṣyati̍ |

yat pṛ̍thi̱vyā vari̍ma̱nnā sva̍ṅgu̱rirvarṣma̍n di̱vaḥ su̱vati̍ sa̱tyama̍sya̱ tat

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 5

indra̍jyeṣṭhān bṛ̱hadbhya̱: parva̍tebhya̱: kṣayā̍m̐ ebhyaḥ suvasi pa̱styā̍vataḥ |

yathā̍yathā pa̱taya̍nto viyemi̱ra e̱vaiva ta̍sthuḥ savitaḥ sa̱vāya̍ te

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 54 | **Verse:** 6

indro̱ dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī sindhu̍ra̱dbhirā̍di̱tyairno̱ adi̍ti̱: śarma̍ yaṃsat
