# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 44

**7 purumīl̤ahājamīl̤ahau sauhotrau| aśvinau| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 1

taṃ vā̱ṃ ratha̍ṃ va̱yama̱dyā hu̍vema pṛthu̱jraya̍maśvinā̱ saṃga̍ti̱ṃ goḥ |
yaḥ sū̱ryāṃ vaha̍ti vandhurā̱yurgirvā̍hasaṃ puru̱tama̍ṃ vasū̱yum

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 2

yu̱vaṃ śriya̍maśvinā de̱vatā̱ tāṃ divo̍ napātā vanatha̱: śacī̍bhiḥ |

yu̱vorvapu̍ra̱bhi pṛkṣa̍: sacante̱ vaha̍nti̱ yat ka̍ku̱hāso̱ rathe̍ vām

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 3

ko vā̍ma̱dyā ka̍rate rā̱taha̍vya ū̱taye̍ vā suta̱peyā̍ya vā̱rkaiḥ |

ṛ̱tasya̍ vā va̱nuṣe̍ pū̱rvyāya̱ namo̍ yemā̱no a̍śvi̱nā va̍vartat

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 4

hi̱ra̱ṇyaye̍na purubhū̱ rathe̍ne̱maṃ ya̱jñaṃ nā̍sa̱tyopa̍ yātam |

pibā̍tha̱ inmadhu̍naḥ so̱myasya̱ dadha̍tho̱ ratna̍ṃ vidha̱te janā̍ya

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 5

ā no̍ yātaṃ di̱vo acchā̍ pṛthi̱vyā hi̍ra̱ṇyaye̍na su̱vṛtā̱ rathe̍na |

mā vā̍ma̱nye ni ya̍man deva̱yanta̱: saṃ yad da̱de nābhi̍: pū̱rvyā vā̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 6

nū no̍ ra̱yiṃ pu̍ru̱vīra̍ṃ bṛ̱hanta̱ṃ dasrā̱ mimā̍thāmu̱bhaye̍ṣva̱sme |

naro̱ yad vā̍maśvinā̱ stoma̱māva̍n tsa̱dhastu̍timājamī̱l̤ahāso̍ agman

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 7

i̱heha̱ yad vā̍ṃ sama̱nā pa̍pṛ̱kṣe seyama̱sme su̍ma̱tirvā̍jaratnā |

u̱ru̱ṣyata̍ṃ jari̱tāra̍ṃ yu̱vaṃ ha̍ śri̱taḥ kāmo̍ nāsatyā yuva̱drik
