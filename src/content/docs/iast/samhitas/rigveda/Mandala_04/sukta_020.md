# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 20

**11 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 1

ā na̱ indro̍ dū̱rādā na̍ ā̱sāda̍bhiṣṭi̱kṛdava̍se yāsadu̱graḥ |
oji̍ṣṭhebhirnṛ̱pati̱rvajra̍bāhuḥ sa̱ṃge sa̱matsu̍ tu̱rvaṇi̍: pṛta̱nyūn

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 2

ā na̱ indro̱ hari̍bhiryā̱tvacchā̍'rvācī̱no'va̍se̱ rādha̍se ca |

tiṣṭhā̍ti va̱jrī ma̱ghavā̍ vira̱pśīmaṃ ya̱jñamanu̍ no̱ vāja̍sātau

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 3

i̱maṃ ya̱jñaṃ tvama̱smāka̍mindra pu̱ro dadha̍t saniṣyasi̱ kratu̍ṃ naḥ |

śva̱ghnīva̍ vajrin tsa̱naye̱ dhanā̍nā̱ṃ tvayā̍ va̱yama̱rya ā̱jiṃ ja̍yema

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 4

u̱śannu̱ ṣu ṇa̍: su̱manā̍ upā̱ke soma̍sya̱ nu suṣu̍tasya svadhāvaḥ |

pā i̍ndra̱ prati̍bhṛtasya̱ madhva̱: samandha̍sā mamadaḥ pṛ̱ṣṭhye̍na

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 5

vi yo ra̍ra̱pśa ṛṣi̍bhi̱rnave̍bhirvṛ̱kṣo na pa̱kvaḥ sṛṇyo̱ na jetā̍ |

maryo̱ na yoṣā̍ma̱bhi manya̍mā̱no'cchā̍ vivakmi puruhū̱tamindra̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 6

gi̱rirna yaḥ svata̍vām̐ ṛ̱ṣva indra̍: sa̱nāde̱va saha̍se jā̱ta u̱graḥ |

āda̍rtā̱ vajra̱ṃ sthavi̍ra̱ṃ na bhī̱ma u̱dneva̱ kośa̱ṃ vasu̍nā̱ nyṛ̍ṣṭam

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 7

na yasya̍ va̱rtā ja̱nuṣā̱ nvasti̱ na rādha̍sa āmarī̱tā ma̱ghasya̍ |

u̱dvā̱vṛ̱ṣā̱ṇasta̍viṣīva ugrā̱smabhya̍ṃ daddhi puruhūta rā̱yaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 8

īkṣe̍ rā̱yaḥ kṣaya̍sya carṣaṇī̱nāmu̱ta vra̱jama̍pava̱rtāsi̱ gonā̍m |

śi̱kṣā̱na̱raḥ sa̍mi̱theṣu̍ pra̱hāvā̱n vasvo̍ rā̱śima̍bhine̱tāsi̱ bhūri̍m

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 9

kayā̱ tacchṛ̍ṇve̱ śacyā̱ śaci̍ṣṭho̱ yayā̍ kṛ̱ṇoti̱ muhu̱ kā ci̍dṛ̱ṣvaḥ |

pu̱ru dā̱śuṣe̱ vica̍yiṣṭho̱ aṃho'thā̍ dadhāti̱ dravi̍ṇaṃ jari̱tre

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 10

mā no̍ mardhī̱rā bha̍rā da̱ddhi tanna̱: pra dā̱śuṣe̱ dāta̍ve̱ bhūri̱ yatte̍ |

navye̍ de̱ṣṇe śa̱ste a̱smin ta̍ u̱kthe pra bra̍vāma va̱yami̍ndra stu̱vanta̍:

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 11

nū ṣṭu̱ta i̍ndra̱ nū gṛ̍ṇā̱na iṣa̍ṃ jari̱tre na̱dyo̱3 na pī̍peḥ |

akā̍ri te harivo̱ brahma̱ navya̍ṃ dhi̱yā syā̍ma ra̱thya̍: sadā̱sāḥ
