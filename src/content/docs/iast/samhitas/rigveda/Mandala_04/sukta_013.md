# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 13

**5 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| agniḥ(liṅgoktadevatā iti eke)|triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 1

pratya̱gniru̱ṣasā̱magra̍makhyad vibhātī̱nāṃ su̱manā̍ ratna̱dheya̍m |
yā̱tama̍śvinā su̱kṛto̍ duro̱ṇamutsūryo̱ jyoti̍ṣā de̱va e̍ti

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 2

ū̱rdhvaṃ bhā̱nuṃ sa̍vi̱tā de̱vo a̍śred dra̱psaṃ davi̍dhvad gavi̱ṣo na satvā̍ |

anu̍ vra̱taṃ varu̍ṇo yanti mi̱tro yatsūrya̍ṃ di̱vyā̍ro̱haya̍nti

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 3

yaṃ sī̱makṛ̍ṇva̱n tama̍se vi̱pṛce̍ dhru̱vakṣe̍mā̱ ana̍vasyanto̱ artha̍m |

taṃ sūrya̍ṃ ha̱rita̍: sa̱pta ya̱hvīḥ spaśa̱ṃ viśva̍sya̱ jaga̍to vahanti

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 4

vahi̍ṣṭhebhirvi̱hara̍nyāsi̱ tantu̍mava̱vyaya̱nnasi̍taṃ deva̱ vasma̍ |

davi̍dhvato ra̱śmaya̱: sūrya̍sya̱ carme̱vāvā̍dhu̱stamo̍ a̱psva1ntaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 5

anā̍yato̱ ani̍baddhaḥ ka̱thāyaṃ nya̍ṅṅuttā̱no'va̍ padyate̱ na |

kayā̍ yāti sva̱dhayā̱ ko da̍darśa di̱vaḥ ska̱mbhaḥ samṛ̍taḥ pāti̱ nāka̍m
