# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 34

**11 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| ṛbhavaḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 1

ṛ̱bhurvibhvā̱ vāja̱ indro̍ no̱ acche̱maṃ ya̱jñaṃ ra̍tna̱dheyopa̍ yāta |
i̱dā hi vo̍ dhi̱ṣaṇā̍ de̱vyahnā̱madhā̍t pī̱tiṃ saṃ madā̍ agmatā vaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 2

vi̱dā̱nāso̱ janma̍no vājaratnā u̱ta ṛ̱tubhi̍rṛbhavo mādayadhvam |

saṃ vo̱ madā̱ agma̍ta̱ saṃ pura̍ṃdhiḥ su̱vīrā̍ma̱sme ra̱yimera̍yadhvam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 3

a̱yaṃ vo̍ ya̱jña ṛ̍bhavo'kāri̱ yamā ma̍nu̱ṣvat pra̱divo̍ dadhi̱dhve |

pra vo'cchā̍ jujuṣā̱ṇāso̍ asthu̱rabhū̍ta̱ viśve̍ agri̱yota vā̍jāḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 4

abhū̍du vo vidha̱te ra̍tna̱dheya̍mi̱dā na̍ro dā̱śuṣe̱ martyā̍ya |

piba̍ta vājā ṛbhavo da̱de vo̱ mahi̍ tṛ̱tīya̱ṃ sava̍na̱ṃ madā̍ya

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 5

ā vā̍jā yā̱topa̍ na ṛbhukṣā ma̱ho na̍ro̱ dravi̍ṇaso gṛṇā̱nāḥ |

ā va̍: pī̱tayo̍'bhipi̱tve ahnā̍mi̱mā asta̍ṃ nava̱sva̍ iva gman

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 6

ā na̍pātaḥ śavaso yāta̱nope̱maṃ ya̱jñaṃ nama̍sā hū̱yamā̍nāḥ |

sa̱joṣa̍saḥ sūrayo̱ yasya̍ ca̱ stha madhva̍: pāta ratna̱dhā indra̍vantaḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 7

sa̱joṣā̍ indra̱ varu̍ṇena̱ soma̍ṃ sa̱joṣā̍: pāhi girvaṇo ma̱rudbhi̍: |

a̱gre̱pābhi̍rṛtu̱pābhi̍: sa̱joṣā̱ gnāspatnī̍bhī ratna̱dhābhi̍: sa̱joṣā̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 8

sa̱joṣa̍sa ādi̱tyairmā̍dayadhvaṃ sa̱joṣa̍sa ṛbhava̱: parva̍tebhiḥ |

sa̱joṣa̍so̱ daivye̍nā savi̱trā sa̱joṣa̍sa̱: sindhu̍bhī ratna̱dhebhi̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 9

ye a̱śvinā̱ ye pi̱tarā̱ ya ū̱tī dhe̱nuṃ ta̍ta̱kṣurṛ̱bhavo̱ ye aśvā̍ |

ye aṃsa̍trā̱ ya ṛdha̱groda̍sī̱ ye vibhvo̱ nara̍: svapa̱tyāni̍ ca̱kruḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 10

ye goma̍nta̱ṃ vāja̍vantaṃ su̱vīra̍ṃ ra̱yiṃ dha̱ttha vasu̍mantaṃ puru̱kṣum |

te a̍gre̱pā ṛ̍bhavo mandasā̱nā a̱sme dha̍tta̱ ye ca̍ rā̱tiṃ gṛ̱ṇanti̍

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 11

nāpā̍bhūta̱ na vo̍'tītṛṣā̱mā'ni̍:śastā ṛbhavo ya̱jñe a̱smin |

samindre̍ṇa̱ mada̍tha̱ saṃ ma̱rudbhi̱: saṃ rāja̍bhī ratna̱dheyā̍ya devāḥ
