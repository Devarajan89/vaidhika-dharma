# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 40

**5 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| dadhikrāḥ, 5 sūryaḥ|jagatī, 1 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 1

da̱dhi̱krāvṇa̱ idu̱ nu ca̍rkirāma̱ viśvā̱ inmāmu̱ṣasa̍: sūdayantu |
a̱pāma̱gneru̱ṣasa̱: sūrya̍sya̱ bṛha̱spate̍rāṅgira̱sasya̍ ji̱ṣṇoḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 2

satvā̍ bhari̱ṣo ga̍vi̱ṣo du̍vanya̱sacchra̍va̱syādi̱ṣa u̱ṣasa̍sturaṇya̱sat |

sa̱tyo dra̱vo dra̍va̱raḥ pa̍taṃga̱ro da̍dhi̱krāveṣa̱mūrja̱ṃ sva̍rjanat

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 3

u̱ta smā̍sya̱ drava̍tasturaṇya̱taḥ pa̱rṇaṃ na veranu̍ vāti praga̱rdhina̍: |

śye̱nasye̍va̱ dhraja̍to aṅka̱saṃ pari̍ dadhi̱krāvṇa̍: sa̱horjā tari̍trataḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 4

u̱ta sya vā̱jī kṣi̍pa̱ṇiṃ tu̍raṇyati grī̱vāyā̍ṃ ba̱ddho a̍pika̱kṣa ā̱sani̍ |

kratu̍ṃ dadhi̱krā anu̍ sa̱ṃtavī̍tvat pa̱thāmaṅkā̱ṃsyanvā̱panī̍phaṇat

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 5

ha̱ṃsaḥ śu̍ci̱ṣad vasu̍rantarikṣa̱saddhotā̍ vedi̱ṣadati̍thirduroṇa̱sat |

nṛ̱ṣadva̍ra̱sadṛ̍ta̱sad vyo̍ma̱sada̱bjā go̱jā ṛ̍ta̱jā a̍dri̱jā ṛ̱tam
