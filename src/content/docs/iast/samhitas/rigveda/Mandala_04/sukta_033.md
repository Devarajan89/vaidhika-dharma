# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 33

**11 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| ṛbhavaḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 1

pra ṛ̱bhubhyo̍ dū̱tami̍va̱ vāca̍miṣya upa̱stire̱ śvaita̍rīṃ dhe̱numī̍l̤e |
ye vāta̍jūtāsta̱raṇi̍bhi̱revai̱: pari̱ dyāṃ sa̱dyo a̱paso̍ babhū̱vuḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 2

ya̱dāra̱makra̍nnṛ̱bhava̍: pi̱tṛbhyā̱ṃ pari̍viṣṭī ve̱ṣaṇā̍ da̱ṃsanā̍bhiḥ |

ādid de̱vānā̱mupa̍ sa̱khyamā̍ya̱n dhīrā̍saḥ pu̱ṣṭima̍vahan ma̱nāyai̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 3

puna̱rye ca̱kruḥ pi̱tarā̱ yuvā̍nā̱ sanā̱ yūpe̍va jara̱ṇā śayā̍nā |

te vājo̱ vibhvā̍m̐ ṛ̱bhurindra̍vanto̱ madhu̍psaraso no'vantu ya̱jñam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 4

yat sa̱ṃvatsa̍mṛ̱bhavo̱ gāmara̍kṣa̱n yat sa̱ṃvatsa̍mṛ̱bhavo̱ mā api̍ṃśan |

yat sa̱ṃvatsa̱mabha̍ra̱n bhāso̍ asyā̱stābhi̱: śamī̍bhiramṛta̱tvamā̍śuḥ

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 5

jye̱ṣṭha ā̍ha cama̱sā dvā ka̱reti̱ kanī̍yā̱n trīn kṛ̍ṇavā̱metyā̍ha |

ka̱ni̱ṣṭha ā̍ha ca̱tura̍ska̱reti̱ tvaṣṭa̍ ṛbhava̱stat pa̍naya̱d vaco̍ vaḥ

## Verse 6

vi̱bhrāja̍mānām̐ścama̱sām̐ ahe̱vā've̍na̱t tvaṣṭā̍ ca̱turo̍ dadṛ̱śvān

## Verse 8

## Verse 9
