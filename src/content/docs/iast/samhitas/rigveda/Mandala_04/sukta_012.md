# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 12

**6 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| agniḥ|triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 1

yastvāma̍gna i̱nadha̍te ya̱tasru̱k triste̱ anna̍ṃ kṛ̱ṇava̱t sasmi̱nnaha̍n |
sa su dyu̱mnaira̱bhya̍stu pra̱sakṣa̱t tava̱ kratvā̍ jātavedaściki̱tvān

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 2

i̱dhmaṃ yaste̍ ja̱bhara̍cchaśramā̱ṇo ma̱ho a̍gne̱ anī̍ka̱mā sa̍pa̱ryan |

sa i̍dhā̱naḥ prati̍ do̱ṣāmu̱ṣāsa̱ṃ puṣya̍n ra̱yiṃ sa̍cate̱ ghnanna̱mitrā̍n

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 3

a̱gnirī̍śe bṛha̱taḥ kṣa̱triya̍syā̱'gnirvāja̍sya para̱masya̍ rā̱yaḥ |

dadhā̍ti̱ ratna̍ṃ vidha̱te yavi̍ṣṭho̱ vyā̍nu̱ṣaṅmartyā̍ya sva̱dhāvā̍n

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 4

yacci̱ddhi te̍ puruṣa̱trā ya̍vi̱ṣṭhāci̍ttibhiścakṛ̱mā kacci̱dāga̍: |

kṛ̱dhī ṣva1smām̐ adi̍te̱ranā̍gā̱n vyenā̍ṃsi śiśratho̱ viṣva̍gagne

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 5

ma̱haści̍dagna̱ ena̍so a̱bhīka̍ ū̱rvād devānā̍mu̱ta martyā̍nām |

mā te̱ sakhā̍ya̱: sada̱mid ri̍ṣāma̱ yacchā̍ to̱kāya̱ tana̍yāya̱ śaṃ yoḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 6

yathā̍ ha̱ tyad va̍savo gau̱rya̍ṃ cit pa̱di ṣi̱tāmamu̍ñcatā yajatrāḥ |

e̱vo ṣva1smanmu̍ñcatā̱ vyaṃha̱: pra tā̍ryagne prata̱raṃ na̱ āyu̍:
