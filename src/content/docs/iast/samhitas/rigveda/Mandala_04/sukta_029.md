# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 29

**5 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 1

ā na̍: stu̱ta upa̱ vāje̍bhirū̱tī indra̍ yā̱hi hari̍bhirmandasā̱naḥ |
ti̱raści̍da̱ryaḥ sava̍nā pu̱rūṇyā̍ṅgū̱ṣebhi̍rgṛṇā̱naḥ sa̱tyarā̍dhāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 2

ā hi ṣmā̱ yāti̱ narya̍ściki̱tvān hū̱yamā̍naḥ so̱tṛbhi̱rupa̍ ya̱jñam |

svaśvo̱ yo abhī̍ru̱rmanya̍mānaḥ suṣvā̱ṇebhi̱rmada̍ti̱ saṃ ha̍ vī̱raiḥ

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 3

u̱dvā̱vṛ̱ṣā̱ṇo rādha̍se̱ tuvi̍ṣmā̱n kara̍nna̱ indra̍: sutī̱rthābha̍yaṃ ca

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 4
