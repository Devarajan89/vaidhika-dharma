# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 2

**20 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 1

yo martye̍ṣva̱mṛta̍ ṛ̱tāvā̍ de̱vo de̱veṣva̍ra̱tirni̱dhāyi̍ |
hotā̱ yaji̍ṣṭho ma̱hnā śu̱cadhyai̍ ha̱vyaira̱gnirmanu̍ṣa īra̱yadhyai̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 2

i̱ha tvaṃ sū̍no sahaso no a̱dya jā̱to jā̱tām̐ u̱bhayā̍m̐ a̱ntara̍gne |

dū̱ta ī̍yase yuyujā̱na ṛ̍ṣva ṛjumu̱ṣkān vṛṣa̍ṇaḥ śu̱krām̐śca̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 3

atyā̍ vṛdha̱snū rohi̍tā ghṛ̱tasnū̍ ṛ̱tasya̍ manye̱ mana̍sā̱ javi̍ṣṭhā |

a̱ntarī̍yase aru̱ṣā yu̍jā̱no yu̱ṣmām̐śca̍ de̱vān viśa̱ ā ca̱ martā̍n

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 4

a̱rya̱maṇa̱ṃ varu̍ṇaṃ mi̱trame̍ṣā̱mindrā̱viṣṇū̍ ma̱ruto̍ a̱śvino̱ta |

svaśvo̍ agne su̱ratha̍: su̱rādhā̱ edu̍ vaha suha̱viṣe̱ janā̍ya

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 5

gomā̍m̐ a̱gne'vi̍mām̐ a̱śvī ya̱jño nṛ̱vatsa̍khā̱ sada̱mida̍pramṛ̱ṣyaḥ |

il̤ā̍vām̐ e̱ṣo a̍sura pra̱jāvā̍ndī̱rgho ra̱yiḥ pṛ̍thubu̱dhnaḥ sa̱bhāvā̍n

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 6

yasta̍ i̱dhmaṃ ja̱bhara̍t siṣvidā̱no mū̱rdhāna̍ṃ vā ta̱tapa̍te tvā̱yā |

bhuva̱stasya̱ svata̍vām̐: pā̱yura̍gne̱ viśva̍smātsī sīmaghāya̱ta u̍ruṣya

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 7

yaste̱ bharā̱danni̍yate ci̱danna̍ṃ ni̱śiṣa̍nma̱ndramati̍thimu̱dīra̍t |

ā de̍va̱yuri̱nadha̍te duro̱ṇe tasmi̍n ra̱yirdhru̱vo a̍stu̱ dāsvā̍n

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 8

yastvā̍ do̱ṣā ya u̱ṣasi̍ pra̱śaṃsā̍t pri̱yaṃ vā̍ tvā kṛ̱ṇava̍te ha̱viṣmā̍n |

aśvo̱ na sve dama̱ ā he̱myāvā̱n tamaṃha̍saḥ pīparo dā̱śvāṃsa̍m

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 9

yastubhya̍magne a̱mṛtā̍ya̱ dāśa̱d du̱va̱stve kṛ̱ṇava̍te ya̱tasru̍k |

na sa rā̱yā śa̍śamā̱no vi yo̍ṣa̱nnaina̱maṃha̱: pari̍ varadaghā̱yoḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 10

yasya̱ tvama̍gne adhva̱raṃ jujo̍ṣo de̱vo marta̍sya̱ sudhi̍ta̱ṃ rarā̍ṇaḥ |

prī̱teda̍sa̱ddhotrā̱ sā ya̍vi̱ṣṭhā'sā̍ma̱ yasya̍ vidha̱to vṛ̱dhāsa̍:

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 11

citti̱maci̍ttiṃ cinava̱d vi vi̱dvān pṛ̱ṣṭheva̍ vī̱tā vṛ̍ji̱nā ca̱ martā̍n |

rā̱ye ca̍ naḥ svapa̱tyāya̍ deva̱ diti̍ṃ ca̱ rāsvādi̍timuruṣya

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 12

ka̱viṃ śa̍śāsuḥ ka̱vayo'da̍bdhā nidhā̱raya̍nto̱ duryā̍svā̱yoḥ |

ata̱stvaṃ dṛśyā̍m̐ agna e̱tān pa̱ḍbhiḥ pa̍śye̱radbhu̍tām̐ a̱rya evai̍:

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 13

tvama̍gne vā̱ghate̍ su̱praṇī̍tiḥ su̱taso̍māya vidha̱te ya̍viṣṭha |

ratna̍ṃ bhara śaśamā̱nāya̍ ghṛṣve pṛ̱thu śca̱ndramava̍se carṣaṇi̱prāḥ

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 14

adhā̍ ha̱ yad va̱yama̍gne tvā̱yā pa̱ḍbhirhaste̍bhiścakṛ̱mā ta̱nūbhi̍: |

ratha̱ṃ na kranto̱ apa̍sā bhu̱rijo̍rṛtaṃ ye̍muḥ su̱dhya̍ āśuṣā̱ṇāḥ

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 15

adhā̍ mā̱turu̱ṣasa̍: sa̱pta viprā̱ jāye̍mahi pratha̱mā ve̱dhaso̱ nṝn |

di̱vaspu̱trā aṅgi̍raso bhave̱mādri̍ṃ rujema dha̱nina̍ṃ śu̱canta̍:

---

## Verse 16

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 16

adhā̱ yathā̍ naḥ pi̱tara̱: parā̍saḥ pra̱tnāso̍ agna ṛ̱tamā̍śuṣā̱ṇāḥ |

śucīda̍ya̱n dīdhi̍timuktha̱śāsa̱: kṣāmā̍ bhi̱ndanto̍ aru̱ṇīrapa̍ vran

---

## Verse 17

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 17

su̱karmā̍ṇaḥ su̱ruco̍ deva̱yanto'yo̱ na de̱vā jani̍mā̱ dhama̍ntaḥ |

śu̱canto̍ a̱gniṃ va̍vṛ̱dhanta̱ indra̍mū̱rvaṃ gavya̍ṃ pari̱ṣada̍nto agman

---

## Verse 18

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 18

ā yū̱theva̍ kṣu̱mati̍ pa̱śvo a̍khyad de̱vānā̱ṃ yajjani̱māntyu̍gra |

martā̍nāṃ cidu̱rvaśī̍rakṛpran vṛ̱dhe ci̍da̱rya upa̍rasyā̱yoḥ

---

## Verse 19

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 19

aka̍rma te̱ svapa̍so abhūma ṛ̱tama̍vasrannu̱ṣaso̍ vibhā̱tīḥ |

anū̍nama̱gniṃ pu̍ru̱dhā su̍śca̱ndraṃ de̱vasya̱ marmṛ̍jata̱ścāru̱ cakṣu̍:

---

## Verse 20

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 20

e̱tā te̍ agna u̱cathā̍ni ve̱dho'vo̍cāma ka̱vaye̱ tā ju̍ṣasva |

uccho̍casva kṛṇu̱hi vasya̍so no ma̱ho rā̱yaḥ pu̍ruvāra̱ pra ya̍ndhi
