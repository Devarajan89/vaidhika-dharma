# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 52

**7 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| uṣāḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 1

prati̱ ṣyā sū̱narī̱ janī̍ vyu̱cchantī̱ pari̱ svasu̍: | di̱vo a̍darśi duhi̱tā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 2

aśve̍va ci̱trāru̍ṣī mā̱tā gavā̍mṛ̱tāva̍rī | sakhā̍bhūda̱śvino̍ru̱ṣāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 3

u̱ta sakhā̍sya̱śvino̍ru̱ta mā̱tā gavā̍masi | u̱toṣo̱ vasva̍ īśiṣe

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 4

yā̱va̱yaddve̍ṣasaṃ tvā ciki̱tvit sū̍nṛtāvari | prati̱ stomai̍rabhutsmahi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 5

prati̍ bha̱drā a̍dṛkṣata̱ gavā̱ṃ sargā̱ na ra̱śmaya̍: | oṣā a̍prā u̱ru jraya̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 6

ā̱pa̱pruṣī̍ vibhāvari̱ vyā̍va̱rjyoti̍ṣā̱ tama̍: | uṣo̱ anu̍ sva̱dhāma̍va

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 7

ā dyāṃ ta̍noṣi ra̱śmibhi̱rāntari̍kṣamu̱ru pri̱yam | uṣa̍: śu̱kreṇa̍ śo̱ciṣā̍
