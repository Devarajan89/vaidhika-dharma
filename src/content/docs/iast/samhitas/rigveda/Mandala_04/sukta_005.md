# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 5

**15 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| vaiśvānaro'gniḥ|triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 1

vai̱śvā̱na̱rāya̍ mī̱l̤ahuṣe̍ sa̱joṣā̍: ka̱thā dā̍śemā̱gnaye̍ bṛ̱hadbhāḥ |
anū̍nena bṛha̱tā va̱kṣathe̱nopa̍ stabhāyadupa̱minna rodha̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 2

mā ni̍ndata̱ ya i̱māṃ mahya̍ṃ rā̱tiṃ de̱vo da̱dau martyā̍ya sva̱dhāvā̍n |

pākā̍ya̱ gṛtso̍ a̱mṛto̱ vice̍tā vaiśvāna̱ro nṛta̍mo ya̱hvo a̱gniḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 3

sāma̍ dvi̱barhā̱ mahi̍ ti̱gmabhṛ̍ṣṭiḥ sa̱hasra̍retā vṛṣa̱bhastuvi̍ṣmān |

pa̱daṃ na gorapa̍gūl̤ahaṃ vivi̱dvāna̱gnirmahya̱ṃ predu̍ vocanmanī̱ṣām

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 4

pra tām̐ a̱gnirba̍bhasat ti̱gmaja̍mbha̱stapi̍ṣṭhena śo̱ciṣā̱ yaḥ su̱rādhā̍: |

pra ye mi̱nanti̱ varu̍ṇasya̱ dhāma̍ pri̱yā mi̱trasya̱ ceta̍to dhru̱vāṇi̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 5

a̱bhrā̱taro̱ na yoṣa̍ṇo̱ vyanta̍: pati̱ripo̱ na jana̍yo du̱revā̍: |

pā̱pāsa̱: santo̍ anṛ̱tā a̍sa̱tyā i̱daṃ pa̱dama̍janatā gabhī̱ram

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 6

i̱daṃ me̍ agne̱ kiya̍te pāva̱kā'mi̍nate gu̱ruṃ bhā̱raṃ na manma̍ |

bṛ̱had da̍dhātha dhṛṣa̱tā ga̍bhī̱raṃ ya̱hvaṃ pṛ̱ṣṭhaṃ praya̍sā sa̱ptadhā̍tu

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 7

taminnve̱3va sa̍ma̱nā sa̍mā̱nama̱bhi kratvā̍ puna̱tī dhī̱tira̍śyāḥ |

sa̱sasya̱ carma̱nnadhi̱ cāru̱ pṛśne̱ragre̍ ru̱pa āru̍pita̱ṃ jabā̍ru

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 8

yadu̱sriyā̍ṇā̱mapa̱ vāri̍va̱ vran pāti̍ pri̱yaṃ ru̱po agra̍ṃ pa̱daṃ veḥ

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 9

ṛ̱tasya̍ pa̱de adhi̱ dīdyā̍na̱ṃ guhā̍ raghu̱ṣyad ra̍ghu̱yad vi̍veda

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 10
