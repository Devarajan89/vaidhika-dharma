# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 8

**8  vāmadevo gautamaḥ| agniḥ|gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 1

dū̱taṃ vo̍ vi̱śvave̍dasaṃ havya̱vāha̱mama̍rtyam | yaji̍ṣṭhamṛñjase gi̱rā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 2

sa hi vedā̱ vasu̍dhitiṃ ma̱hām̐ ā̱rodha̍naṃ di̱vaḥ | sa de̱vām̐ eha va̍kṣati

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 3

sa ve̍da de̱va ā̱nama̍ṃ de̱vām̐ ṛ̍tāya̱te dame̍ | dāti̍ pri̱yāṇi̍ ci̱d vasu̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 4

sa hotā̱ sedu̍ dū̱tya̍ṃ ciki̱tvām̐ a̱ntarī̍yate | vi̱dvām̐ ā̱rodha̍naṃ di̱vaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 5

te syā̍ma̱ ye a̱gnaye̍ dadā̱śurha̱vyadā̍tibhiḥ | ya ī̱ṃ puṣya̍nta indha̱te

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 6

te rā̱yā te su̱vīryai̍: sasa̱vāṃso̱ vi śṛ̍ṇvire | ye a̱gnā da̍dhi̱re duva̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 7

a̱sme rāyo̍ di̱vedi̍ve̱ saṃ ca̍rantu puru̱spṛha̍: | a̱sme vājā̍sa īratām

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 8

sa vipra̍ścarṣaṇī̱nāṃ śava̍sā̱ mānu̍ṣāṇām | ati̍ kṣi̱preva̍ vidhyati
