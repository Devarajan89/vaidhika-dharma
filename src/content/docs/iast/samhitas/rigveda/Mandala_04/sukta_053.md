# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 53

**7 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| savitā| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 1

tad de̱vasya̍ savi̱turvārya̍ṃ ma̱had vṛ̍ṇī̱mahe̱ asu̍rasya̱ prace̍tasaḥ |
cha̱rdiryena̍ dā̱śuṣe̱ yaccha̍ti̱ tmanā̱ tanno̍ ma̱hām̐ uda̍yān de̱vo a̱ktubhi̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 2

di̱vo dha̱rtā bhuva̍nasya pra̱jāpa̍tiḥ pi̱śaṅga̍ṃ drā̱piṃ prati̍ muñcate ka̱viḥ |

vi̱ca̱kṣa̱ṇaḥ pra̱thaya̍nnāpṛ̱ṇannu̱rvajī̍janat savi̱tā su̱mnamu̱kthya̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 3

āprā̱ rajā̍ṃsi di̱vyāni̱ pārthi̍vā̱ śloka̍ṃ de̱vaḥ kṛ̍ṇute̱ svāya̱ dharma̍ṇe |

pra bā̱hū a̍srāk savi̱tā savī̍mani nive̱śaya̍nprasu̱vanna̱ktubhi̱rjaga̍t

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 4

adā̍bhyo̱ bhuva̍nāni pra̱cāka̍śad vra̱tāni̍ de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tābhi ra̍kṣate |

prāsrā̍gbā̱hū bhuva̍nasya pra̱jābhyo̍ dhṛ̱tavra̍to ma̱ho ajma̍sya rājati

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 5

trira̱ntari̍kṣaṃ savi̱tā ma̍hitva̱nā trī rajā̍ṃsi pari̱bhustrīṇi̍ roca̱nā |

ti̱sro diva̍: pṛthi̱vīsti̱sra i̍nvati tri̱bhirvra̱taira̱bhi no̍ rakṣati̱ tmanā̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 6

bṛ̱hatsu̍mnaḥ prasavī̱tā ni̱veśa̍no̱ jaga̍taḥ sthā̱turu̱bhaya̍sya̱ yo va̱śī |

sa no̍ de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā śarma̍ yacchatva̱sme kṣayā̍ya tri̱varū̍tha̱maṃha̍saḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 7

āga̍n de̱va ṛ̱tubhi̱rvardha̍tu̱ kṣaya̱ṃ dadhā̍tu naḥ savi̱tā su̍pra̱jāmiṣa̍m |

sa na̍: kṣa̱pābhi̱raha̍bhiśca jinvatu pra̱jāva̍ntaṃ ra̱yima̱sme sami̍nvatu
