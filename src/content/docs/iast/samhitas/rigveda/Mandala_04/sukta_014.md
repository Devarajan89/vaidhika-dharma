# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 14

**5 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| agniḥ(liṅgoktadevatā iti eke)|triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 1

pratya̱gniru̱ṣaso̍ jā̱tave̍dā̱ akhya̍d de̱vo roca̍mānā̱ maho̍bhiḥ |
ā nā̍satyorugā̱yā rathe̍ne̱maṃ ya̱jñamupa̍ no yāta̱maccha̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 2

ū̱rdhvaṃ ke̱tuṃ sa̍vi̱tā de̱vo a̍śre̱jjyoti̱rviśva̍smai̱ bhuva̍nāya kṛ̱ṇvan |

āprā̱ dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī a̱ntari̍kṣa̱ṃ vi sūryo̍ ra̱śmibhi̱śceki̍tānaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 3

ā̱vaha̍ntyaru̱ṇīrjyoti̱ṣāgā̍nma̱hī ci̱trā ra̱śmibhi̱śceki̍tānā |

pra̱bo̱dhaya̍ntī suvi̱tāya̍ de̱vyu1ṣā ī̍yate su̱yujā̱ rathe̍na

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 4

ā vā̱ṃ vahi̍ṣṭhā i̱ha te va̍hantu̱ rathā̱ aśvā̍sa u̱ṣaso̱ vyu̍ṣṭau |

i̱me hi vā̍ṃ madhu̱peyā̍ya̱ somā̍ a̱smin ya̱jñe vṛ̍ṣaṇā mādayethām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 5

anā̍yato̱ ani̍baddhaḥ ka̱thāyaṃ nya̍ṅṅuttā̱no'va̍ padyate̱ na |

kayā̍ yāti sva̱dhayā̱ ko da̍darśa di̱vaḥ ska̱mbhaḥ samṛ̍taḥ pāti̱ nāka̍m
