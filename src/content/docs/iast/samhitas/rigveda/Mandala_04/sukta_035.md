# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 35

**9 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| ṛbhavaḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 1

i̱hopa̍ yāta śavaso napāta̱: saudha̍nvanā ṛbhavo̱ māpa̍ bhūta |
a̱smin hi va̱: sava̍ne ratna̱dheya̱ṃ gama̱ntvindra̱manu̍ vo̱ madā̍saḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 2

āga̍nnṛbhū̱ṇāmi̱ha ra̍tna̱dheya̱mabhū̱t soma̍sya̱ suṣu̍tasya pī̱tiḥ |

su̱kṛ̱tyayā̱ yat sva̍pa̱syayā̍ ca̱m̐ eka̍ṃ vica̱kra ca̍ma̱saṃ ca̍tu̱rdhā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 3

vya̍kṛṇota cama̱saṃ ca̍tu̱rdhā sakhe̱ vi śi̱kṣetya̍bravīta |

athai̍ta vājā a̱mṛta̍sya̱ panthā̍ṃ ga̱ṇaṃ de̱vānā̍mṛbhavaḥ suhastāḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 4

ki̱maya̍: sviccama̱sa e̱ṣa ā̍sa̱ yaṃ kāvye̍na ca̱turo̍ vica̱kra |

athā̍ sunudhva̱ṃ sava̍na̱ṃ madā̍ya pā̱ta ṛ̍bhavo̱ madhu̍naḥ so̱myasya̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 5

śacyā̍karta pi̱tarā̱ yuvā̍nā̱ śacyā̍karta cama̱saṃ de̍va̱pāna̍m |

śacyā̱ harī̱ dhanu̍tarāvataṣṭendra̱vāhā̍vṛbhavo vājaratnāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 6

yo va̍: su̱notya̍bhipi̱tve ahnā̍ṃ tī̱vraṃ vā̍jāsa̱: sava̍na̱ṃ madā̍ya |

tasmai̍ ra̱yimṛ̍bhava̱: sarva̍vīra̱mā ta̍kṣata vṛṣaṇo mandasā̱nāḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 7

prā̱taḥ su̱tama̍pibo haryaśva̱ mādhya̍ṃdina̱ṃ sava̍na̱ṃ keva̍laṃ te |

samṛ̱bhubhi̍: pibasva ratna̱dhebhi̱: sakhī̱m̐ryām̐ i̍ndra cakṛ̱ṣe su̍kṛ̱tyā

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 8

ye de̱vāso̱ abha̍vatā sukṛ̱tyā śye̱nā i̱vedadhi̍ di̱vi ni̍ṣe̱da |

te ratna̍ṃ dhāta śavaso napāta̱: saudha̍nvanā̱ abha̍vatā̱mṛtā̍saḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 9

yattṛ̱tīya̱ṃ sava̍naṃ ratna̱dheya̱makṛ̍ṇudhvaṃ svapa̱syā su̍hastāḥ |

tadṛ̍bhava̱: pari̍ṣiktaṃ va e̱tat saṃ made̍bhirindri̱yebhi̍: pibadhvam
