# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 23

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 1

ka̱thā ma̱hāma̍vṛdha̱t kasya̱ hotu̍rya̱jñaṃ ju̍ṣā̱ṇo a̱bhi soma̱mūdha̍: |
piba̍nnuśā̱no ju̱ṣamā̍ṇo̱ andho̍ vava̱kṣa ṛ̱ṣvaḥ śu̍ca̱te dhanā̍ya

## Verse 4
