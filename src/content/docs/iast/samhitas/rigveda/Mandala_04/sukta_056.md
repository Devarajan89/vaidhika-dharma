# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 56

**7 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| dyāvāpṛthivī|triṣṭup, 5-7 gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 1

ma̱hī dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī i̱ha jyeṣṭhe̍ ru̱cā bha̍vatāṃ śu̱caya̍dbhira̱rkaiḥ |
yat sī̍ṃ vari̍ṣṭhe bṛha̱tī vi̍mi̱nvan ru̱vaddho̱kṣā pa̍prathā̱nebhi̱revai̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 2

de̱vī de̱vebhi̍ryaja̱te yaja̍trai̱rami̍natī tasthaturu̱kṣamā̍ṇe |

ṛ̱tāva̍rī a̱druhā̍ de̱vapu̍tre ya̱jñasya̍ ne̱trī śu̱caya̍dbhira̱rkaiḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 3

sa it svapā̱ bhuva̍neṣvāsa̱ ya i̱me dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī ja̱jāna̍ |

u̱rvī ga̍bhī̱re raja̍sī su̱meke̍ ava̱ṃśe dhīra̱: śacyā̱ samai̍rat

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 4

nū ro̍dasī bṛ̱hadbhi̍rno̱ varū̍thai̱: patnī̍vadbhiri̱ṣaya̍ntī sa̱joṣā̍: |

u̱rū̱cī viśve̍ yaja̱te ni pā̍taṃ dhi̱yā syā̍ma ra̱thya̍: sadā̱sāḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 5

pra vā̱ṃ mahi̱ dyavī̍ a̱bhyupa̍stutiṃ bharāmahe | śucī̱ upa̱ praśa̍staye

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 6

pu̱nā̱ne ta̱nvā̍ mi̱thaḥ svena̱ dakṣe̍ṇa rājathaḥ | ū̱hyāthe̍ sa̱nādṛ̱tam

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 7

ma̱hī mi̱trasya̍ sādhatha̱stara̍ntī̱ pipra̍tī ṛ̱tam | pari̍ ya̱jñaṃ ni ṣe̍dathuḥ
