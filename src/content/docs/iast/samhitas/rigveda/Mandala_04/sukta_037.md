# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 37

**8 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| ṛbhavaḥ| triṣṭup, 5-8 anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 1

upa̍ no vājā adhva̱ramṛ̍bhukṣā̱ devā̍ yā̱ta pa̱thibhi̍rdeva̱yānai̍: |
yathā̍ ya̱jñaṃ manu̍ṣo vi̱kṣvā̱3su da̍dhi̱dhve ra̍ṇvāḥ su̱dine̱ṣvahnā̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 2

te vo̍ hṛ̱de mana̍se santu ya̱jñā juṣṭā̍so a̱dya ghṛ̱tani̍rṇijo guḥ |

pra va̍: su̱tāso̍ harayanta pū̱rṇāḥ kratve̱ dakṣā̍ya harṣayanta pī̱tāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 3

tryu̱dā̱yaṃ de̱vahi̍ta̱ṃ yathā̍ va̱: stomo̍ vājā ṛbhukṣaṇo da̱de va̍: |

ju̱hve ma̍nu̱ṣvadupa̍rāsu vi̱kṣu yu̱ṣme sacā̍ bṛ̱haddi̍veṣu̱ soma̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 4

pīvo̍aśvāḥ śu̱cadra̍thā̱ hi bhū̱tā'ya̍:śiprā vājinaḥ suni̱ṣkāḥ |

indra̍sya sūno śavaso napā̱to'nu̍ vaścetyagri̱yaṃ madā̍ya

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 5

ṛ̱bhumṛ̍bhukṣaṇo ra̱yiṃ vāje̍ vā̱jinta̍ma̱ṃ yuja̍m |

indra̍svantaṃ havāmahe sadā̱sāta̍mama̱śvina̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 6

sedṛ̍bhavo̱ yamava̍tha yū̱yamindra̍śca̱ martya̍m |

sa dhī̱bhira̍stu̱ sani̍tā me̱dhasā̍tā̱ so arva̍tā

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 7

a̱smabhya̍ṃ sūrayaḥ stu̱tā viśvā̱ āśā̍starī̱ṣaṇi̍

## Verse 8
