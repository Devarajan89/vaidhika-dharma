# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 16

**21 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 1

tasmā̱ idandha̍: suṣumā su̱dakṣa̍mi̱hābhi̍pi̱tvaṃ ka̍rate gṛṇā̱naḥ

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 2
