# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 3

**16 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| agniḥ, 1 rudraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 1

ā vo̱ rājā̍namadhva̱rasya̍ ru̱draṃ hotā̍raṃ satya̱yaja̱ṃ roda̍syoḥ |
a̱gniṃ pu̱rā ta̍nayi̱tnora̱cittā̱ddhira̍ṇyarūpa̱mava̍se kṛṇudhvam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 2

a̱yaṃ yoni̍ścakṛ̱mā yaṃ va̱yaṃ te̍ jā̱yeva̱ patya̍ uśa̱tī su̱vāsā̍: |

a̱rvā̱cī̱naḥ pari̍vīto̱ ni ṣī̍de̱mā u̍ te svapāka pratī̱cīḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 3

ā̱śṛ̱ṇva̱te adṛ̍pitāya̱ manma̍ nṛ̱cakṣa̍se sumṛl̤ī̱kāya̍ vedhaḥ |

de̱vāya̍ śa̱stima̱mṛtā̍ya śaṃsa̱ grāve̍va̱ sotā̍ madhu̱ṣud yamī̱l̤e

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 4

tvaṃ ci̍nna̱: śamyā̍ agne a̱syā ṛ̱tasya̍ bodhyṛtacit svā̱dhīḥ |

ka̱dā ta̍ u̱kthā sa̍dha̱mādyā̍ni ka̱dā bha̍vanti sa̱khyā gṛ̱he te̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 5

ka̱thā ha̱ tad varu̍ṇāya̱ tvama̍gne ka̱thā di̱ve ga̍rhase̱ kanna̱ āga̍: |

ka̱thā mi̱trāya̍ mī̱l̤ahuṣe̍ pṛthi̱vyai brava̱: kada̍rya̱mṇe kad bhagā̍ya

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 6

kaddhiṣṇyā̍su vṛdhasā̱no a̍gne̱ kad vātā̍ya̱ prata̍vase śubha̱ṃye |

pari̍jmane̱ nāsa̍tyāya̱ kṣe brava̱: kada̍gne ru̱drāya̍ nṛ̱ghne

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 7

ka̱thā ma̱he pu̍ṣṭimbha̱rāya̍ pū̱ṣṇe kadru̱drāya̱ suma̍khāya havi̱rde |

kad viṣṇa̍va urugā̱yāya̱ reto̱ brava̱: kada̍gne̱ śara̍ve bṛha̱tyai

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 8

ka̱thā śardhā̍ya ma̱rutā̍mṛ̱tāya̍ ka̱thā sū̱re bṛ̍ha̱te pṛ̱cchyamā̍naḥ |

prati̍ bra̱vo'di̍taye tu̱rāya̱ sādhā̍ di̱vo jā̍tavedaściki̱tvān

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 9

ṛ̱tena̍ ṛ̱taṃ niya̍tamīl̤a̱ ā gorā̱mā sacā̱ madhu̍mat pa̱kvama̍gne |

kṛ̱ṣṇā sa̱tī ruśa̍tā dhā̱sinai̱ṣā jāma̍ryeṇa̱ paya̍sā pīpāya

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 10

ṛ̱tena̱ hi ṣmā̍ vṛṣa̱bhaści̍da̱ktaḥ pumā̍m̐ a̱gniḥ paya̍sā pṛ̱ṣṭhye̍na |

aspa̍ndamāno acarad vayo̱dhā vṛṣā̍ śu̱kraṃ du̍duhe̱ pṛśni̱rūdha̍:

## Verse 12

## Verse 14

## Verse 15
