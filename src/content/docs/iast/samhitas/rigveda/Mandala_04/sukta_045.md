# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 45

**7 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| aśvinau| jagatī, 7 triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 1

e̱ṣa sya bhā̱nurudi̍yarti yu̱jyate̱ ratha̱: pari̍jmā di̱vo a̱sya sāna̍vi |
pṛ̱kṣāso̍ asmin mithu̱nā adhi̱ trayo̱ dṛti̍stu̱rīyo̱ madhu̍no̱ vi ra̍pśate

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 2

ud vā̍ṃ pṛ̱kṣāso̱ madhu̍manta īrate̱ rathā̱ aśvā̍sa u̱ṣaso̱ vyu̍ṣṭiṣu |

a̱po̱rṇu̱vanta̱stama̱ ā parī̍vṛta̱ṃ sva1rṇa śu̱kraṃ ta̱nvanta̱ ā raja̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 3

madhva̍: pibataṃ madhu̱pebhi̍rā̱sabhi̍ru̱ta pri̱yaṃ madhu̍ne yuñjāthā̱ṃ ratha̍m |

ā va̍rta̱niṃ madhu̍nā jinvathaspa̱tho dṛti̍ṃ vahethe̱ madhu̍mantamaśvinā

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 4

ha̱ṃsāso̱ ye vā̱ṃ madhu̍manto a̱sridho̱ hira̍ṇyaparṇā u̱huva̍ uṣa̱rbudha̍: |

u̱da̱pruto̍ ma̱ndino̍ mandini̱spṛśo̱ madhvo̱ na makṣa̱: sava̍nāni gacchathaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 5

sva̱dhva̱rāso̱ madhu̍manto a̱gnaya̍ u̱srā ja̍rante̱ prati̱ vasto̍ra̱śvinā̍ |

yanni̱ktaha̍stasta̱raṇi̍rvicakṣa̱ṇaḥ soma̍ṃ su̱ṣāva̱ madhu̍manta̱madri̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 6

ā̱ke̱ni̱pāso̱ aha̍bhi̱rdavi̍dhvata̱: sva1rṇa śu̱kraṃ ta̱nvanta̱ ā raja̍: |

sūra̍ści̱daśvā̍n yuyujā̱na ī̍yate̱ viśvā̱m̐ anu̍ sva̱dhayā̍ cetathaspa̱thaḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 7

pra vā̍mavocamaśvinā dhiya̱ṃdhā ratha̱: svaśvo̍ a̱jaro̱ yo asti̍ |

yena̍ sa̱dyaḥ pari̱ rajā̍ṃsi yā̱tho ha̱viṣma̍ntaṃ ta̱raṇi̍ṃ bho̱jamaccha̍
