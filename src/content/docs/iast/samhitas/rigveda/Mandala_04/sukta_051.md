# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 51

**11 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| uṣāḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 1

i̱damu̱ tyat pu̍ru̱tama̍ṃ pu̱rastā̱jjyoti̱stama̍so va̱yunā̍vadasthāt |
nū̱naṃ di̱vo du̍hi̱taro̍ vibhā̱tīrgā̱tuṃ kṛ̍ṇavannu̱ṣaso̱ janā̍ya

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 2

asthu̍ru ci̱trā u̱ṣasa̍: pu̱rastā̍nmi̱tā i̍va̱ svara̍vo'dhva̱reṣu̍ |

vyū̍ vra̱jasya̱ tama̍so̱ dvāro̱cchantī̍ravra̱ñchuca̍yaḥ pāva̱kāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 3

u̱cchantī̍ra̱dya ci̍tayanta bho̱jān rā̍dho̱deyā̍yo̱ṣaso̍ ma̱ghonī̍: |

a̱ci̱tre a̱ntaḥ pa̱ṇaya̍: sasa̱ntvabu̍dhyamānā̱stama̍so̱ vima̍dhye

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 4

ku̱vit sa de̍vīḥ sa̱nayo̱ navo̍ vā̱ yāmo̍ babhū̱yādu̍ṣaso vo a̱dya |

yenā̱ nava̍gve̱ aṅgi̍re̱ daśa̍gve sa̱ptāsye̍ revatī re̱vadū̱ṣa

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 5

yū̱yaṃ hi de̍vīrṛta̱yugbhi̱raśvai̍: pariprayā̱tha bhuva̍nāni sa̱dyaḥ |

pra̱bo̱dhaya̍ntīruṣasaḥ sa̱santa̍ṃ dvi̱pāccatu̍ṣpācca̱rathā̍ya jī̱vam

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 6

kva̍ svidāsāṃ kata̱mā pu̍rā̱ṇī yayā̍ vi̱dhānā̍ vida̱dhurṛ̍bhū̱ṇām |

śubha̱ṃ yacchu̱bhrā u̱ṣasa̱ścara̍nti̱ na vi jñā̍yante sa̱dṛśī̍raju̱ryāḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 7

tā ghā̱ tā bha̱drā u̱ṣasa̍: pu̱rāsu̍rabhi̱ṣṭidyu̍mnā ṛ̱tajā̍tasatyāḥ |

yāsvī̍jā̱naḥ śa̍śamā̱na u̱kthaiḥ stu̱vañchaṃsa̱n dravi̍ṇaṃ sa̱dya āpa̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 8

tā ā ca̍ranti sama̱nā pu̱rastā̍t samā̱nata̍: sama̱nā pa̍prathā̱nāḥ |

ṛ̱tasya̍ de̱vīḥ sada̍so budhā̱nā gavā̱ṃ na sargā̍ u̱ṣaso̍ jarante

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 9

tā innve̱3va sa̍ma̱nā sa̍mā̱nīramī̍tavarṇā u̱ṣasa̍ścaranti |

gūha̍ntī̱rabhva̱masi̍ta̱ṃ ruśa̍dbhiḥ śu̱krāsta̱nūbhi̱: śuca̍yo rucā̱nāḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 10

ra̱yiṃ di̍vo duhitaro vibhā̱tīḥ pra̱jāva̍ntaṃ yacchatā̱smāsu̍ devīḥ |

syo̱nādā va̍: prati̱budhya̍mānāḥ su̱vīrya̍sya̱ pata̍yaḥ syāma

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 11

tad vo̍ divo duhitaro vibhā̱tīrupa̍ bruva uṣaso ya̱jñake̍tuḥ |

va̱yaṃ syā̍ma ya̱śaso̱ jane̍ṣu̱ tad dyauśca̍ dha̱ttāṃ pṛ̍thi̱vī ca̍ de̱vī
