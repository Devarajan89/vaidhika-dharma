# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 31

**15 vāmadevo gautamaḥ| indraḥ| gāyatrī, 3 pāda nicṛt|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 1

kayā̍ naści̱tra ā bhu̍vadū̱tī sa̱dāvṛ̍dha̱: sakhā̍ | kayā̱ śaci̍ṣṭhayā vṛ̱tā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 2

kastvā̍ sa̱tyo madā̍nā̱ṃ maṃhi̍ṣṭho matsa̱dandha̍saḥ | dṛ̱l̤ahā ci̍dā̱ruje̱ vasu̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 3

a̱bhī ṣu ṇa̱: sakhī̍nāmavi̱tā ja̍ritṝ̱ṇām | śa̱taṃ bha̍vāsyū̱tibhi̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 4

a̱bhī na̱ ā va̍vṛtsva ca̱kraṃ na vṛ̱ttamarva̍taḥ | ni̱yudbhi̍ścarṣaṇī̱nām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 5

pra̱vatā̱ hi kratū̍nā̱mā hā̍ pa̱deva̱ gaccha̍si | abha̍kṣi̱ sūrye̱ sacā̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 6

saṃ yatta̍ indra ma̱nyava̱: saṃ ca̱krāṇi̍ dadhanvi̱re | adha̱ tve adha̱ sūrye̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 7

u̱ta smā̱ hi tvāmā̱hurinma̱ghavā̍naṃ śacīpate | dātā̍ra̱mavi̍dīdhayum

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 8

u̱ta smā̍ sa̱dya it pari̍ śaśamā̱nāya̍ sunva̱te | pu̱rū ci̍nmaṃhase̱ vasu̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 9

na̱hi ṣmā̍ te śa̱taṃ ca̱na rādho̱ vara̍nta ā̱mura̍: | na cyau̱tnāni̍ kariṣya̱taḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 10

a̱smām̐ a̍vantu te śa̱tama̱smān tsa̱hasra̍mū̱taya̍: | a̱smān viśvā̍ a̱bhiṣṭa̍yaḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 11

a̱smām̐ i̱hā vṛ̍ṇīṣva sa̱khyāya̍ sva̱staye̍ | ma̱ho rā̱ye di̱vitma̍te

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 12

a̱smām̐ a̍viḍḍhi vi̱śvahendra̍ rā̱yā parī̍ṇasā | a̱smān viśvā̍bhirū̱tibhi̍:

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 13

a̱smabhya̱ṃ tām̐ apā̍ vṛdhi vra̱jām̐ aste̍va̱ goma̍taḥ | navā̍bhirindro̱tibhi̍:

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 14

a̱smāka̍ṃ dhṛṣṇu̱yā ratho̍ dyu̱mām̐ i̱ndrāna̍pacyutaḥ | ga̱vyura̍śva̱yurī̍yate

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 15

a̱smāka̍mutta̱maṃ kṛ̍dhi̱ śravo̍ de̱veṣu̍ sūrya | varṣi̍ṣṭha̱ṃ dyāmi̍vo̱pari̍
