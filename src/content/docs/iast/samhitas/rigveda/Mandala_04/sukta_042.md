# Maṇḍala 4 — Sūktam 42

**10 trasadasyuḥ paurukutsyaḥ| trasadasyuḥ, 7-10 indrāvaruṇau| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 1

mama̍ dvi̱tā rā̱ṣṭraṃ kṣa̱triya̍sya vi̱śvāyo̱rviśve̍ a̱mṛtā̱ yathā̍ naḥ |
kratu̍ṃ sacante̱ varu̍ṇasya de̱vā rājā̍mi kṛ̱ṣṭeru̍pa̱masya̍ va̱vreḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 2

a̱haṃ rājā̱ varu̍ṇo̱ mahya̱ṃ tānya̍su̱ryā̍ṇi pratha̱mā dhā̍rayanta |

kratu̍ṃ sacante̱ varu̍ṇasya de̱vā rājā̍mi kṛ̱ṣṭeru̍pa̱masya̍ va̱vreḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 3

a̱hamindro̱ varu̍ṇa̱ste ma̍hi̱tvorvī ga̍bhī̱re raja̍sī su̱meke̍ |

tvaṣṭe̍va̱ viśvā̱ bhuva̍nāni vi̱dvān tsamai̍raya̱ṃ roda̍sī dhā̱raya̍ṃ ca

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 4

a̱hama̱po a̍pinvamu̱kṣamā̍ṇā dhā̱raya̱ṃ diva̱ṃ sada̍na ṛ̱tasya̍ |

ṛ̱tena̍ pu̱tro adi̍terṛ̱tāvo̱ta tri̱dhātu̍ prathaya̱dvi bhūma̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 5

māṃ nara̱: svaśvā̍ vā̱jaya̍nto̱ māṃ vṛ̱tāḥ sa̱mara̍ṇe havante |

kṛ̱ṇomyā̱jiṃ ma̱ghavā̱hamindra̱ iya̍rmi re̱ṇuma̱bhibhū̍tyojāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 6

a̱haṃ tā viśvā̍ cakara̱ṃ naki̍rmā̱ daivya̱ṃ saho̍ varate̱ apra̍tītam |

yanmā̱ somā̍so ma̱mada̱nyadu̱kthobhe bha̍yete̱ raja̍sī apā̱re

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 7

vi̱duṣṭe̱ viśvā̱ bhuva̍nāni̱ tasya̱ tā pra bra̍vīṣi̱ varu̍ṇāya vedhaḥ |

tvaṃ vṛ̱trāṇi̍ śṛṇviṣe jagha̱nvān tvaṃ vṛ̱tām̐ a̍riṇā indra̱ sindhū̍n

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 8

a̱smāka̱matra̍ pi̱tara̱sta ā̍san tsa̱pta ṛṣa̍yo daurga̱he ba̱dhyamā̍ne |

ta āya̍janta tra̱sada̍syumasyā̱ indra̱ṃ na vṛ̍tra̱tura̍mardhade̱vam

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 9

pu̱ru̱kutsā̍nī̱ hi vā̱madā̍śaddha̱vyebhi̍rindrāvaruṇā̱ namo̍bhiḥ |

athā̱ rājā̍naṃ tra̱sada̍syumasyā vṛtra̱haṇa̍ṃ dadathurardhade̱vam

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 4 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 10

rā̱yā va̱yaṃ sa̍sa̱vāṃso̍ madema ha̱vyena̍ de̱vā yava̍sena̱ gāva̍: |

tāṃ dhe̱numi̍ndrāvaruṇā yu̱vaṃ no̍ vi̱śvāhā̍ dhatta̱mana̍pasphurantīm
