# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 72

**10 parāśaraḥ śāktyaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 1

ni kāvyā̍ ve̱dhasa̱: śaśva̍taska̱rhaste̱ dadhā̍no̱ naryā̍ pu̱rūṇi̍ |
a̱gnirbhu̍vad rayi̱patī̍ rayī̱ṇāṃ sa̱trā ca̍krā̱ṇo a̱mṛtā̍ni̱ viśvā̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 2

a̱sme va̱tsaṃ pari̱ ṣanta̱ṃ na vi̍ndanni̱cchanto̱ viśve̍ a̱mṛtā̱ amū̍rāḥ |

śra̱ma̱yuva̍: pada̱vyo̍ dhiya̱ṃdhāsta̱sthuḥ pa̱de pa̍ra̱me cārva̱gneḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 3

ti̱sro yada̍gne śa̱rada̱stvāmicchuci̍ṃ ghṛ̱tena̱ śuca̍yaḥ sapa̱ryān |

nāmā̍ni cid dadhire ya̱jñiyā̱nyasū̍dayanta ta̱nva 1: sujā̍tāḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 4

ā roda̍sī bṛha̱tī vevi̍dānā̱: pra ru̱driyā̍ jabhrire ya̱jñiyā̍saḥ |

vi̱danmarto̍ ne̱madhi̍tā ciki̱tvāna̱gniṃ pa̱de pa̍ra̱me ta̍sthi̱vāṃsa̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 5

sa̱ṃjā̱nā̱nā upa̍ sīdannabhi̱jñu patnī̍vanto nama̱sya̍ṃ namasyan |

ri̱ri̱kvāṃsa̍sta̱nva̍: kṛṇvata̱ svāḥ sakhā̱ sakhyu̍rni̱miṣi̱ rakṣa̍māṇāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 6

triḥ sa̱pta yad guhyā̍ni̱ tve it pa̱dāvi̍da̱nnihi̍tā ya̱jñiyā̍saḥ |

tebhī̍ rakṣante a̱mṛta̍ṃ sa̱joṣā̍: pa̱śūñca̍ sthā̱tṝñca̱ratha̍ṃ ca pāhi

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 7

vi̱dvām̐ a̍gne va̱yunā̍ni kṣitī̱nāṃ vyā̍nu̱ṣak chu̱rudho̍ jī̱vase̍ dhāḥ |

a̱nta̱rvi̱dvām̐ adhva̍no deva̱yānā̱nata̍ndro dū̱to a̍bhavo havi̱rvāṭ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 8

svā̱dhyo̍ di̱va ā sa̱pta ya̱hvī rā̱yo duro̱ vyṛ̍ta̱jñā a̍jānan |

vi̱dad gavya̍ṃ sa̱ramā̍ dṛ̱hlamū̱rvaṃ yenā̱ nu ka̱ṃ mānu̍ṣī̱ bhoja̍te̱ viṭ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 9

ā ye viśvā̍ svapa̱tyāni̍ ta̱sthuḥ kṛ̍ṇvā̱nāso̍ amṛta̱tvāya̍ gā̱tum |

ma̱hnā ma̱hadbhi̍: pṛthi̱vī vi ta̍sthe mā̱tā pu̱trairadi̍ti̱rdhāya̍se̱ veḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 10

adhi̱ śriya̱ṃ ni da̍dhu̱ścāru̍masmin di̱vo yada̱kṣī a̱mṛtā̱ akṛ̍ṇvan |

adha̍ kṣaranti̱ sindha̍vo̱ na sṛ̱ṣṭāḥ pra nīcī̍ragne̱ aru̍ṣīrajānan
