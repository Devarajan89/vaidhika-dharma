# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 163

**13 dīrghatamā aucathyaḥ| aśvaḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 1

yadakra̍ndaḥ pratha̱maṃ jāya̍māna u̱dyan tsa̍mu̱drādu̱ta vā̱ purī̍ṣāt |
śye̱nasya̍ pa̱kṣā ha̍ri̱ṇasya̍ bā̱hū u̍pa̱stutya̱ṃ mahi̍ jā̱taṃ te̍ arvan

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 2

ya̱mena̍ da̱ttaṃ tri̱ta e̍namāyuna̱gindra̍ eṇaṃ pratha̱mo adhya̍tiṣṭhat |

ga̱ndha̱rvo a̍sya raśa̱nāma̍gṛbhṇā̱t sūrā̱daśva̍ṃ vasavo̱ nira̍taṣṭa

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 3

asi̍ ya̱mo asyā̍di̱tyo a̍rva̱nnasi̍ tri̱to guhye̍na vra̱tena̍ |

asi̱ some̍na sa̱mayā̱ vipṛ̍kta ā̱huste̱ trīṇi̍ di̱vi bandha̍nāni

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 4

trīṇi̍ ta āhurdi̱vi bandha̍nāni̱ trīṇya̱psu trīṇya̱ntaḥ sa̍mu̱dre |

u̱teva̍ me̱ varu̍ṇaśchantsyarva̱n yatrā̍ ta ā̱huḥ pa̍ra̱maṃ ja̱nitra̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 5

i̱mā te̍ vājinnava̱mārja̍nānī̱mā śa̱phānā̍ṃ sani̱turni̱dhānā̍ |

atrā̍ te bha̱drā ra̍śa̱nā a̍paśyamṛ̱tasya̱ yā a̍bhi̱rakṣa̍nti go̱pāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 6

ā̱tmāna̍ṃ te̱ mana̍sā̱rāda̍jānāma̱vo di̱vā pa̱taya̍ntaṃ pata̱ṃgam |

śiro̍ apaśyaṃ pa̱thibhi̍: su̱gebhi̍rare̱ṇubhi̱rjeha̍mānaṃ pata̱tri

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 7

atrā̍ te rū̱pamu̍tta̱mama̍paśya̱ṃ jigī̍ṣamāṇami̱ṣa ā pa̱de goḥ |

ya̱dā te̱ marto̱ anu̱ bhoga̱māna̱l̤ādid grasi̍ṣṭha̱ oṣa̍dhīrajīgaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 8

anu̍ tvā̱ ratho̱ anu̱ maryo̍ arva̱nnanu̱ gāvo'nu̱ bhaga̍: ka̱nīnā̍m |

anu̱ vrātā̍sa̱stava̍ sa̱khyamī̍yu̱ranu̍ de̱vā ma̍mire vī̱rya̍ṃ te

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 9

hira̍ṇyaśṛ̱ṅgo'yo̍ asya̱ pādā̱ mano̍javā̱ ava̍ra̱ indra̍ āsīt |

de̱vā ida̍sya havi̱radya̍māya̱n yo arva̍ntaṃ pratha̱mo a̱dhyati̍ṣṭhat

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 10

ī̱rmāntā̍sa̱: sili̍kamadhyamāsa̱: saṃ śūra̍ṇāso di̱vyāso̱ atyā̍: |

ha̱ṃsā i̍va śreṇi̱śo ya̍tante̱ yadākṣi̍ṣurdi̱vyamajma̱maśvā̍:

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 11

tava̱ śarī̍raṃ patayi̱ṣṇva̍rva̱n tava̍ ci̱ttaṃ vāta̍ iva̱ dhrajī̍mān |

tava̱ śṛṅgā̍ṇi̱ viṣṭhi̍tā puru̱trāra̍ṇyeṣa̱ja jarbhu̍rāṇā caranti

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 12

upa̱ prāgā̱cchasa̍naṃ vā̱jyarvā̍ deva̱drīcā̱ mana̍sā̱ dīdhyā̍naḥ |

a̱jaḥ pu̱ro nī̍yate̱ nābhi̍ra̱syānu̍ pa̱ścāt ka̱vayo̍ yanti re̱bhāḥ

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 163 | **Verse:** 13

upa̱ prāgā̍t para̱maṃ yat sa̱dhastha̱marvā̱m̐ acchā̍ pi̱tara̍ṃ mā̱tara̍ṃ ca |

a̱dyā de̱vāñjuṣṭa̍tamo̱ hi ga̱myā athā śā̍ste dā̱śuṣe̱ vāryā̍ṇi
