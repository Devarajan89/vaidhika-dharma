# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 41

**9 kaṇvo ghauraḥ| varuṇamitrāryamaṇaḥ, 4-6 ādityāḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 1

yaṃ rakṣa̍nti̱ prace̍taso̱ varu̍ṇo mi̱tro a̍rya̱mā | nū ci̱tsa da̍bhyate̱ jana̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 2

yaṃ bā̱hute̍va̱ pipra̍ti̱ pānti̱ martya̍ṃ ri̱ṣaḥ | ari̍ṣṭa̱: sarva̍ edhate

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 3

vi du̱rgā vi dviṣa̍: pu̱ro ghnanti̱ rājā̍na eṣām | naya̍nti duri̱tā ti̱raḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 4

su̱gaḥ panthā̍ anṛkṣa̱ra ādi̍tyāsa ṛ̱taṃ ya̱te | nātrā̍vakhā̱do a̍sti vaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 5

yaṃ ya̱jñaṃ naya̍thā nara̱ ādi̍tyā ṛ̱junā̍ pa̱thā | pra va̱: sa dhī̱taye̍ naśat

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 6

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 41 | **Verse:** 8

mā vo̱ ghnanta̱ṃ mā śapa̍nta̱ṃ prati̍ voce deva̱yanta̍m | su̱mnairid va̱ ā vi̍vāse
