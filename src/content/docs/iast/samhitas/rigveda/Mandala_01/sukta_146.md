# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 146

**5 dīrghatamā aucathyaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 146 | **Verse:** 1

tri̱mū̱rdhāna̍ṃ sa̱ptara̍śmiṃ gṛṇī̱ṣe 'nū̍nama̱gniṃ pi̱troru̱pasthe̍ |
ni̱ṣa̱ttama̍sya̱ cara̍to dhru̱vasya̱ viśvā̍ di̱vo ro̍ca̱nāpa̍pri̱vāṃsa̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 146 | **Verse:** 2

u̱kṣā ma̱hām̐ a̱bhi va̍vakṣa ene a̱jara̍stasthāvi̱taū̍tirṛ̱ṣvaḥ |

u̱rvyāḥ pa̱do ni da̍dhāti̱ sānau̍ ri̱hantyūdho̍ aru̱ṣāso̍ asya

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 146 | **Verse:** 3

sa̱mā̱naṃ va̱tsama̱bhi sa̱ṃcara̍ntī̱ viṣva̍gdhe̱nū vi ca̍rataḥ su̱meke̍ |

a̱na̱pa̱vṛ̱jyām̐ adhva̍no̱ mimā̍ne̱ viśvā̱n ketā̱m̐ adhi̍ ma̱ho dadhā̍ne

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 146 | **Verse:** 4

dhīrā̍saḥ pa̱daṃ ka̱vayo̍ nayanti̱ nānā̍ hṛ̱dā rakṣa̍māṇā aju̱ryam |

siṣā̍santa̱: parya̍paśyanta̱ sindhu̍mā̱vire̍bhyo abhava̱t sūryo̱ nṝn

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 146 | **Verse:** 5

di̱dṛ̱kṣeṇya̱: pari̱ kāṣṭhā̍su̱ jenya̍ ī̱l̤enyo̍ ma̱ho arbhā̍ya jī̱vase̍ |

pu̱ru̱trā yadabha̍va̱t sūrahai̍bhyo̱ garbhe̍bhyo ma̱ghavā̍ vi̱śvada̍rśataḥ
