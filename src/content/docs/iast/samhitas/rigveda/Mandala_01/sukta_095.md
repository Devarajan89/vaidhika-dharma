# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 95

**11 kutsa āṅgirasaḥ| agniḥ, auṣaso'gnirvā | triṣṭup**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 95 | **Verse:** 1

dve virū̍pe carata̱: svarthe̍ a̱nyānyā̍ va̱tsamupa̍ dhāpayete |
hari̍ra̱nyasyā̱ṃ bhava̍ti sva̱dhāvā̍ñ chu̱kro a̱nyasyā̍ṃ dadṛśe su̱varcā̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 95 | **Verse:** 2

daśe̱maṃ tvaṣṭu̍rjanayanta̱ garbha̱mata̍ndrāso yuva̱tayo̱ vibhṛ̍tram |

ti̱gmānī̍ka̱ṃ svaya̍śasa̱ṃ jane̍ṣu vi̱roca̍māna̱ṃ pari̍ ṣīṃ nayanti

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 95 | **Verse:** 3

trīṇi̱ jānā̱ pari̍ bhūṣantyasya samu̱dra eka̍ṃ di̱vyeka̍ma̱psu |

pūrvā̱manu̱ pra diśa̱ṃ pārthi̍vānāmṛ̱tūn pra̱śāsa̱d vi da̍dhāvanu̱ṣṭhu

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 95 | **Verse:** 4

ka i̱maṃ vo̍ ni̱ṇyamā ci̍keta va̱tso mā̱tṝrja̍nayata sva̱dhābhi̍: |

ba̱hvī̱nāṃ garbho̍ a̱pasā̍mu̱pasthā̍nma̱hān ka̱virniśca̍rati sva̱dhāvā̍n

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 95 | **Verse:** 5

ā̱viṣṭyo̍ vardhate̱ cāru̍rāsu ji̱hmānā̍mū̱rdhvaḥ svaya̍śā u̱pasthe̍ |

u̱bhe tvaṣṭu̍rbibhyatu̱rjāya̍mānāt pratī̱cī si̱ṃhaṃ prati̍ joṣayete

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 95 | **Verse:** 6

u̱bhe bha̱dre jo̍ṣayete̱ na mene̱ gāvo̱ na vā̱śrā upa̍ tasthu̱revai̍: |

sa dakṣā̍ṇā̱ṃ dakṣa̍patirbabhūvā̱ñjanti̱ yaṃ da̍kṣiṇa̱to ha̱virbhi̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 95 | **Verse:** 7

ud ya̍ṃyamīti savi̱teva̍ bā̱hū u̱bhe sicau̍ yatate bhī̱ma ṛ̱ñjan |

ucchu̱kramatka̍majate si̱masmā̱nnavā̍ mā̱tṛbhyo̱ vasa̍nā jahāti

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 95 | **Verse:** 8

tve̱ṣaṃ rū̱paṃ kṛ̍ṇuta̱ utta̍ra̱ṃ yat sa̍ṃpṛñcā̱naḥ sada̍ne̱ gobhi̍ra̱dbhiḥ |

ka̱virbu̱dhnaṃ pari̍ marmṛjyate̱ dhīḥ sā de̱vatā̍tā̱ sami̍tirbabhūva

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 95 | **Verse:** 9

u̱ru te̱ jraya̱: parye̍ti bu̱dhnaṃ vi̱roca̍mānaṃ mahi̱ṣasya̱ dhāma̍ |

viśve̍bhiragne̱ svaya̍śobhiri̱ddho 'da̍bdhebhiḥ pā̱yubhi̍: pāhya̱smān

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 95 | **Verse:** 10

dhanva̱ntsrota̍: kṛṇute gā̱tumū̱rmiṃ śu̱krairū̱rmibhi̍ra̱bhi na̍kṣati̱ kṣām |

viśvā̱ sanā̍ni ja̱ṭhare̍ṣu dhatte̱ 'ntarnavā̍su carati pra̱sūṣu̍

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 95 | **Verse:** 11

e̱vā no̍ agne sa̱midhā̍ vṛdhā̱no re̱vat pā̍vaka̱ śrava̍se̱ vi bhā̍hi |

tanno̍ mi̱tro varu̍ṇo māmahantā̱madi̍ti̱: sindhu̍: pṛthi̱vī u̱ta dyauḥ
