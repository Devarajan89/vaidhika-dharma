# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 173

**13 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ | indraḥ| triṣṭup, 4 virāṭ sthānā, viṣamapadā vā|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 1

gāya̱t sāma̍ nabha̱nyaṃ1 yathā̱ verarcā̍ma̱ tad vā̍vṛdhā̱naṃ sva̍rvat |
gāvo̍ dhe̱navo̍ ba̱rhiṣyada̍bdhā̱ ā yat sa̱dmāna̍ṃ di̱vyaṃ vivā̍sān

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 2

arca̱d vṛṣā̱ vṛṣa̍bhi̱: svedu̍havyairmṛ̱go nāśno̱ ati̱ yajju̍gu̱ryāt |

pra ma̍nda̱yurma̱nāṃ gū̍rta̱ hotā̱ bhara̍te̱ maryo̍ mithu̱nā yaja̍traḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 3

nakṣa̱ddhotā̱ pari̱ sadma̍ mi̱tā yan bhara̱d garbha̱mā śa̱rada̍: pṛthi̱vyāḥ |

kranda̱daśvo̱ naya̍māno ru̱vad gaura̱ntardū̱to na roda̍sī cara̱d vāk

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 4

tā ka̱rmāṣa̍tarāsmai̱ pra cyau̱tnāni̍ deva̱yanto̍ bharante |

jujo̍ṣa̱dindro̍ da̱smava̍rcā̱ nāsa̍tyeva̱ sugmyo̍ rathe̱ṣṭhāḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 5

tamu̍ ṣṭu̱hīndra̱ṃ yo ha̱ satvā̱ yaḥ śūro̍ ma̱ghavā̱ yo ra̍the̱ṣṭhāḥ |

pra̱tī̱caści̱d yodhī̍yā̱n vṛṣa̍ṇvān vava̱vruṣa̍ści̱ttama̍so viha̱ntā

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 6

pra yadi̱tthā ma̍hi̱nā nṛbhyo̱ astyara̱ṃ roda̍sī ka̱kṣye̱3 nāsmai̍ |

saṃ vi̍vya̱ indro̍ vṛ̱jana̱ṃ na bhūmā̱ bharti̍ sva̱dhāvā̍m̐ opa̱śami̍va̱ dyām

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 7

sa̱matsu̍ tvā śūra sa̱tāmu̍rā̱ṇaṃ pra̍pa̱thinta̍maṃ paritaṃsa̱yadhyai̍ |

sa̱joṣa̍sa̱ indra̱ṃ made̍ kṣo̱ṇīḥ sū̱riṃ ci̱dye a̍nu̱mada̍nti̱ vājai̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 8

e̱vā hi te̱ śaṃ sava̍nā samu̱dra āpo̱ yat ta̍ ā̱su mada̍nti de̱vīḥ |

viśvā̍ te̱ anu̱ joṣyā̍ bhū̱d gauḥ sū̱rīm̐ści̱d yadi̍ dhi̱ṣā veṣi̱ janā̍n

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 9

asā̍ma̱ yathā̍ suṣa̱khāya̍ ena svabhi̱ṣṭayo̍ na̱rāṃ na śaṃsai̍: |

asa̱d yathā̍ na̱ indro̍ vandane̱ṣṭhāstu̱ro na karma̱ naya̍māna u̱kthā

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 10

viṣpa̍rdhaso na̱rāṃ na śaṃsai̍ra̱smākā̍sa̱dindro̱ vajra̍hastaḥ |

mi̱trā̱yuvo̱ na pūrpa̍ti̱ṃ suśi̍ṣṭau madhyā̱yuva̱ upa̍ śikṣanti ya̱jñaiḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 11

ya̱jño hi ṣmendra̱ṃ kaści̍dṛ̱ndhañju̍hurā̱ṇaści̱nmana̍sā pari̱yan |

tī̱rthe nācchā̍ tātṛṣā̱ṇamoko̍ dī̱rgho na si̱dhramā kṛ̍ṇo̱tyadhvā̍

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 173 | **Verse:** 12

mo ṣū ṇa̍ i̱ndrātra̍ pṛ̱tsu de̱vairasti̱ hi ṣmā̍ te śuṣminnava̱yāḥ |

ma̱haści̱d yasya̍ mī̱l̤ahuṣo̍ ya̱vyā ha̱viṣma̍to ma̱ruto̱ vanda̍te̱ gīḥ

## Verse 13
