# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 13

**12 medhātithiḥ kāṇvaḥ|(āprīsūktaṃ, agnirūpā devatā devatāḥ) 1 idhmaḥ samiddho'gnirvā, 2 tanūnapāt, 3**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 1

narāśaṃsaḥ, 4 ilaḥ, 5 barhiḥ, 6 devīrdvāraḥ, 7 uṣāsānaktā, 8 daivyo hotārau pracetasau, 9 tisro devyaḥ
sarasvatīl̤ābhāratyaḥ, 10 tvaṣṭā, 11 vanaspatiḥ, 12 svāhā kṛtayaḥ | gāyatrī|
susa̍middho na̱ ā va̍ha de̱vām̐ a̍gne ha̱viṣma̍te | hota̍: pāvaka̱ yakṣi̍ ca

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 2

madhu̍mantaṃ tanūnapād ya̱jñaṃ de̱veṣu̍ naḥ kave | a̱dyā kṛ̍ṇuhi vī̱taye̍

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 3

narā̱śaṃsa̍mi̱ha pri̱yama̱smin ya̱jña upa̍ hvaye | madhu̍jihvaṃ havi̱ṣkṛta̍m

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 4
