# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 90

**9 gotamo rāhūgaṇaḥ| viśvedevāḥ,| gāyatrī, 9 anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 90 | **Verse:** 1

ṛ̱ju̱nī̱tī no̱ varu̍ṇo mi̱tro na̍yatu vi̱dvān | a̱rya̱mā de̱vaiḥ sa̱joṣā̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 90 | **Verse:** 2

te hi vasvo̱ vasa̍vānā̱ste apra̍mūrā̱ maho̍bhiḥ | vra̱tā ra̍kṣante vi̱śvāhā̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 90 | **Verse:** 3

te a̱smabhya̱ṃ śarma̍ yaṃsanna̱mṛtā̱ martye̍bhyaḥ | bādha̍mānā̱ apa̱ dviṣa̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 90 | **Verse:** 4

vi na̍: pa̱thaḥ su̍vi̱tāya̍ ci̱yantvindro̍ ma̱ruta̍: | pū̱ṣā bhago̱ vandyā̍saḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 90 | **Verse:** 5

u̱ta no̱ dhiyo̱ goa̍grā̱: pūṣa̱n viṣṇa̱veva̍yāvaḥ | kartā̍ naḥ svasti̱mata̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 90 | **Verse:** 6

madhu̱ vātā̍ ṛtāya̱te madhu̍ kṣaranti̱ sindha̍vaḥ | mādhvī̍rnaḥ sa̱ntvoṣa̍dhīḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 90 | **Verse:** 7

madhu̱ nakta̍mu̱toṣaso̱ madhu̍ma̱t pārthi̍va̱ṃ raja̍: | madhu̱ dyaura̍stu naḥ pi̱tā

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 90 | **Verse:** 8

madhu̍mānno̱ vana̱spati̱rmadhu̍mām̐ astu̱ sūrya̍: | mādhvī̱rgāvo̍ bhavantu naḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 90 | **Verse:** 9

śaṃ no̍ mi̱traḥ śaṃ varu̍ṇa̱: śaṃ no̍ bhavatvarya̱mā | śaṃ na̱ indro̱ bṛha̱spati̱: śaṃ no̱ viṣṇu̍rurukra̱maḥ
