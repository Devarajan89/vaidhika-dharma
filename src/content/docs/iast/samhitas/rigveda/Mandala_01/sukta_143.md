# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 143

**8 dīrghatamā aucathyaḥ| agniḥ| jagatī, 8 triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 143 | **Verse:** 1

pra tavya̍sī̱ṃ navya̍sīṃ dhī̱tima̱gnaye̍ vā̱co ma̱tiṃ saha̍saḥ sū̱nave̍ bhare |
a̱pāṃ napā̱d yo vasu̍bhiḥ sa̱ha pri̱yo hotā̍ pṛthi̱vyāṃ nyasī̍dadṛ̱tviya̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 143 | **Verse:** 2

sa jāya̍mānaḥ para̱me vyo̍manyā̱vira̱gnira̍bhavanmāta̱riśva̍ne |

a̱sya kratvā̍ samidhā̱nasya̍ ma̱jmanā̱ pra dyāvā̍ śo̱ciḥ pṛ̍thi̱vī a̍rocayat

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 143 | **Verse:** 3

a̱sya tve̱ṣā a̱jarā̍ a̱sya bhā̱nava̍: susa̱ṃdṛśa̍: su̱pratī̍kasya su̱dyuta̍: |

bhātva̍kṣaso̱ atya̱kturna sindha̍vo̱ 'gne re̍jante̱ asa̍santo a̱jarā̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 143 | **Verse:** 4

yame̍ri̱re bhṛga̍vo vi̱śvave̍dasa̱ṃ nābhā̍ pṛthi̱vyā bhuva̍nasya ma̱jmanā̍ |

a̱gniṃ taṃ gī̱rbhirhi̍nuhi̱ sva ā dame̱ ya eko̱ vasvo̱ varu̍ṇo̱ na rāja̍ti

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 143 | **Verse:** 5

na yo varā̍ya ma̱rutā̍miva sva̱naḥ sene̍va sṛ̱ṣṭā di̱vyā yathā̱śani̍: |

a̱gnirjambhai̍stigi̱taira̍tti̱ bharva̍ti yo̱dho na śatrū̱n tsa vanā̱ nyṛ̍ñjate

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 143 | **Verse:** 6

ku̱vinno̍ a̱gniru̱catha̍sya̱ vīrasa̱d vasu̍ṣku̱vid vasu̍bhi̱: kāma̍mā̱vara̍t |

co̱daḥ ku̱vit tu̍tu̱jyāt sā̱taye̱ dhiya̱: śuci̍pratīka̱ṃ tama̱yā dhi̱yā gṛ̍ṇe

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 143 | **Verse:** 7

ghṛ̱tapra̍tīkaṃ va ṛ̱tasya̍ dhū̱rṣada̍ma̱gniṃ mi̱traṃ na sa̍midhā̱na ṛ̍ñjate |

indhā̍no a̱kro vi̱dathe̍ṣu̱ dīdya̍cchu̱krava̍rṇā̱mudu̍ no yaṃsate̱ dhiya̍m

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 143 | **Verse:** 8

apra̍yuccha̱nnapra̍yucchadbhiragne śi̱vebhi̍rnaḥ pā̱yubhi̍: pāhi śa̱gmaiḥ |

ada̍bdhebhi̱radṛ̍pitebhiri̱ṣṭe 'ni̍miṣadbhi̱: pari̍ pāhi no̱ jāḥ
