# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 92

**18 gotamo rāhūgaṇaḥ| uṣāḥ, 16-18 aśvinau | 1-4 jagatī, 5-12 triṣṭup, 13-18 uṣṇik|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 1

e̱tā u̱ tyā u̱ṣasa̍: ke̱tuma̍krata̱ pūrve̱ ardhe̱ raja̍so bhā̱numa̍ñjate |
ni̱ṣkṛ̱ṇvā̱nā āyu̍dhānīva dhṛ̱ṣṇava̱: prati̱ gāvo'ru̍ṣīryanti mā̱tara̍:

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 2

akra̍nnu̱ṣāso̍ va̱yunā̍ni pū̱rvathā̱ ruśa̍ntaṃ bhā̱numaru̍ṣīraśiśrayuḥ

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 92 | **Verse:** 3

iṣa̱ṃ vaha̍ntīḥ su̱kṛte̍ su̱dāna̍ve̱ viśvedaha̱ yaja̍mānāya sunva̱te
