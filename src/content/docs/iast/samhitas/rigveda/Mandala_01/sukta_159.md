# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 159

**5 dīrghatamā aucathyaḥ| dyāvāpṛthivī| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 159 | **Verse:** 1

pra dyāvā̍ ya̱jñaiḥ pṛ̍thi̱vī ṛ̍tā̱vṛdhā̍ ma̱hī stu̍ṣe vi̱dathe̍ṣu̱ prace̍tasā |
de̱vebhi̱rye de̱vapu̍tre su̱daṃsa̍se̱tthā dhi̱yā vāryā̍ṇi pra̱bhūṣa̍taḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 159 | **Verse:** 2

u̱ta ma̍nye pi̱tura̱druho̱ mano̍ mā̱turmahi̱ svata̍va̱staddhavī̍mabhiḥ |

su̱reta̍sā pi̱tarā̱ bhūma̍ cakraturu̱ru pra̱jāyā̍ a̱mṛta̱ṃ varī̍mabhiḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 159 | **Verse:** 3

te sū̱nava̱: svapa̍saḥ su̱daṃsa̍so ma̱hī ja̍jñurmā̱tarā̍ pū̱rvaci̍ttaye |

sthā̱tuśca̍ sa̱tyaṃ jaga̍taśca̱ dharma̍ṇi pu̱trasya̍ pāthaḥ pa̱damadva̍yāvinaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 159 | **Verse:** 4

te mā̱yino̍ mamire su̱prace̍taso jā̱mī sayo̍nī mithu̱nā samo̍kasā |

navya̍ṃnavya̱ṃ tantu̱mā ta̍nvate di̱vi sa̍mu̱dre a̱ntaḥ ka̱vaya̍: sudī̱taya̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 159 | **Verse:** 5

tad rādho̍ a̱dya sa̍vi̱turvare̍ṇyaṃ va̱yaṃ de̱vasya̍ prasa̱ve ma̍nāmahe |

a̱smabhya̍ṃ dyāvāpṛthivī suce̱tunā̍ ra̱yiṃ dha̍tta̱ṃ vasu̍mantaṃ śata̱gvina̍m
