# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 20

**8 medhātithiḥ kāṇvaḥ| ṛbhavaḥ|gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 1

a̱yaṃ de̱vāya̱ janma̍ne̱ stomo̱ vipre̍bhirāsa̱yā | akā̍ri ratna̱dhāta̍maḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 2

ya indrā̍ya vaco̱yujā̍ tata̱kṣurmana̍sā̱ harī̍ | śamī̍bhirya̱jñamā̍śata

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 3

takṣa̱n nāsa̍tyābhyā̱ṃ pari̍jmānaṃ su̱khaṃ ratha̍m | takṣa̍n dhe̱nuṃ sa̍ba̱rdughā̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 4

yuvā̍nā pi̱tarā̱ puna̍: sa̱tyama̍ntrā ṛjū̱yava̍: | ṛ̱bhavo̍ vi̱ṣṭya̍krata

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 5

saṃ vo̱ madā̍so agma̱tendre̍ṇa ca ma̱rutva̍tā | ā̱di̱tyebhi̍śca̱ rāja̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 6

u̱ta tyaṃ ca̍ma̱saṃ nava̱ṃ tvaṣṭu̍rde̱vasya̱ niṣkṛ̍tam | aka̍rta ca̱tura̱: puna̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 7

te no̱ ratnā̍ni dhattana̱ trirā sāptā̍ni sunva̱te | eka̍mekaṃ suśa̱stibhi̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 8

adhā̍rayanta̱ vahna̱yo 'bha̍janta sukṛ̱tyayā̍ | bhā̱gaṃ de̱veṣu̍ ya̱jñiya̍m
