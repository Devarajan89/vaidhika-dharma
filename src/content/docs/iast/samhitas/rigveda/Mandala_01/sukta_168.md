# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 168

**10 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ | marutaḥ| jagatī, 8-10 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 168 | **Verse:** 1

ya̱jñāya̍jñā vaḥ sama̱nā tu̍tu̱rvaṇi̱rdhiya̍ṃdhiyaṃ vo deva̱yā u̍ dadhidhve |
ā vo̱'rvāca̍: suvi̱tāya̱ roda̍syorma̱he va̍vṛtyā̱mava̍se suvṛ̱ktibhi̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 168 | **Verse:** 2

va̱vrāso̱ na ye sva̱jāḥ svata̍vasa̱ iṣa̱ṃ sva̍rabhi̱jāya̍nta̱ dhūta̍yaḥ |

sa̱ha̱sriyā̍so a̱pāṃ normaya̍ ā̱sā gāvo̱ vandyā̍so̱ nokṣaṇa̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 168 | **Verse:** 3

somā̍so̱ na ye su̱tāstṛ̱ptāṃśa̍vo hṛ̱tsu pī̱tāso̍ du̱vaso̱ nāsa̍te |

aiṣā̱maṃse̍ṣu ra̱mbhiṇī̍va rārabhe̱ haste̍ṣu khā̱diśca̍ kṛ̱tiśca̱ saṃ da̍dhe

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 168 | **Verse:** 4

ava̱ svayu̍ktā di̱va ā vṛthā̍ yayu̱rama̍rtyā̱: kaśa̍yā codata̱ tmanā̍ |

a̱re̱ṇava̍stuvijā̱tā a̍cucyavurdṛ̱l̤ahāni̍ cinma̱ruto̱ bhrāja̍dṛṣṭayaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 168 | **Verse:** 5

ko vo̱'ntarma̍ruta ṛṣṭividyuto̱ reja̍ti̱ tmanā̱ hanve̍va ji̱hvayā̍ |

dha̱nva̱cyuta̍ i̱ṣāṃ na yāma̍ni puru̱praiṣā̍ aha̱nyo̱3 naita̍śaḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 168 | **Verse:** 6

kva̍ svida̱sya raja̍so ma̱haspara̱ṃ kvāva̍raṃ maruto̱ yasmi̍nnāya̱ya |

yaccyā̱vaya̍tha vithu̱reva̱ saṃhi̍ta̱ṃ vyadri̍ṇā patatha tve̱ṣama̍rṇa̱vam

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 168 | **Verse:** 7

sā̱tirna vo'ma̍vatī̱ sva̍rvatī tve̱ṣā vipā̍kā maruta̱: pipi̍ṣvatī |

bha̱drā vo̍ rā̱tiḥ pṛ̍ṇa̱to na dakṣi̍ṇā pṛthu̱jrayī̍ asu̱rye̍va̱ jañja̍tī

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 168 | **Verse:** 8

prati̍ ṣṭobhanti̱ sindha̍vaḥ pa̱vibhyo̱ yada̱bhriyā̱ṃ vāca̍mudī̱raya̍nti |

ava̍ smayanta vi̱dyuta̍: pṛthi̱vyāṃ yadī̍ ghṛ̱taṃ ma̱ruta̍: pruṣṇu̱vanti̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 168 | **Verse:** 9

asū̍ta̱ pṛśni̍rmaha̱te raṇā̍ya tve̱ṣama̱yāsā̍ṃ ma̱rutā̱manī̍kam |

te sa̍psa̱rāso̍'janaya̱ntābhva̱mādit sva̱dhāmi̍ṣi̱rāṃ parya̍paśyan

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 168 | **Verse:** 10

e̱ṣa va̱: stomo̍ maruta i̱yaṃ gīrmā̍ndā̱ryasya̍ mā̱nyasya̍ kā̱roḥ |

eṣā yā̍sīṣṭa ta̱nve̍ va̱yāṃ vi̱dyāme̱ṣaṃ vṛ̱jana̍ṃ jī̱radā̍num
