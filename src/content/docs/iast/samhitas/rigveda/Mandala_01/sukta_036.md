# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 36

**20 kaṇvo ghauraḥ| agniḥ, 13-14 yūpo vā| pragāthaḥ – viṣamā bṛhatyaḥ, samāḥ satobṛhaṃtyaḥ (13 upariṣṭād bṛhatī| ai0 brā0 2-2 caraṇacchedaḥ)**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 1

pra vo̍ ya̱hvaṃ pu̍rū̱ṇāṃ vi̱śāṃ de̍vaya̱tīnā̍m |
a̱gniṃ sū̱ktebhi̱rvaco̍bhirīmahe̱ yaṃ sī̱mida̱nya īl̤a̍te

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 2

janā̍so a̱gniṃ da̍dhire saho̱vṛdha̍ṃ ha̱viṣma̍nto vidhema te |

sa tvaṃ no̍ a̱dya su̱manā̍ i̱hāvi̱tā bhavā̱ vāje̍ṣu santya

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 3

pra tvā̍ dū̱taṃ vṛ̍ṇīmahe̱ hotā̍raṃ vi̱śvave̍dasam |

ma̱haste̍ sa̱to vi ca̍rantya̱rcayo̍ di̱vi spṛ̍śanti bhā̱nava̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 4

de̱vāsa̍stvā̱ varu̍ṇo mi̱tro a̍rya̱mā saṃ dū̱taṃ pra̱tnami̍ndhate |

viśva̱ṃ so a̍gne jayati̱ tvayā̱ dhana̱ṃ yaste̍ da̱dāśa̱ martya̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 5

ma̱ndro hotā̍ gṛ̱hapa̍ti̱ragne̍ dū̱to vi̱śāma̍si |

tve viśvā̱ saṃga̍tāni vra̱tā dhru̱vā yāni̍ de̱vā akṛ̍ṇvata

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 6

tve ida̍gne su̱bhage̍ yaviṣṭhya̱ viśva̱mā hū̍yate ha̱viḥ |

sa tvaṃ no̍ a̱dya su̱manā̍ u̱tāpa̱raṃ yakṣi̍ de̱vāntsu̱vīryā̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 7

taṃ ghe̍mi̱tthā na̍ma̱svina̱ upa̍ sva̱rāja̍māsate |

hotrā̍bhira̱gniṃ manu̍ṣa̱: sami̍ndhate titi̱rvāṃso̱ ati̱ sridha̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 8

ghnanto̍ vṛ̱trama̍tara̱n roda̍sī a̱pa u̱ru kṣayā̍ya cakrire |

bhuva̱t kaṇve̱ vṛṣā̍ dyu̱mnyāhu̍ta̱: kranda̱daśvo̱ gavi̍ṣṭiṣu

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 9

saṃ sī̍dasva ma̱hām̐ a̍si̱ śoca̍sva deva̱vīta̍maḥ |

vi dhū̱mama̍gne aru̱ṣaṃ mi̍yedhya sṛ̱ja pra̍śasta darśa̱tam

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 10

yaṃ tvā̍ de̱vāso̱ mana̍ve da̱dhuri̱ha yaji̍ṣṭhaṃ havyavāhana |

yaṃ kaṇvo̱ medhyā̍tithirdhana̱spṛta̱ṃ yaṃ vṛṣā̱ yamu̍pastu̱taḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 11

yama̱gniṃ medhyā̍tithi̱: kaṇva̍ ī̱dha ṛ̱tādadhi̍ |

tasya̱ preṣo̍ dīdiyu̱stami̱mā ṛca̱s tama̱gniṃ va̍rdhayāmasi

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 12

rā̱yaspū̍rdhi svadhā̱vo'sti̱ hi te 'gne̍ de̱veṣvāpya̍m |

tvaṃ vāja̍sya̱ śrutya̍sya rājasi̱ sa no̍ mṛl̤a ma̱hām̐ a̍si

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 13

ū̱rdhva ū̱ ṣu ṇa̍ ū̱taye̱ tiṣṭhā̍ de̱vo na sa̍vi̱tā |

ū̱rdhvo vāja̍sya̱ sani̍tā̱ yada̱ñjibhi̍rvā̱ghadbhi̍rvi̱hvayā̍mahe

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 14

ū̱rdhvo na̍: pā̱hyaṃha̍so̱ ni ke̱tunā̱ viśva̱ṃ sama̱triṇa̍ṃ daha |

kṛ̱dhī na̍ ū̱rdhvāñca̱rathā̍ya jī̱vase̍ vi̱dā de̱veṣu̍ no̱ duva̍:

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 15

pā̱hi no̍ agne ra̱kṣasa̍: pā̱hi dhū̱rterarā̍vṇaḥ |

pā̱hi rīṣa̍ta u̱ta vā̱ jighā̍ṃsato̱ bṛha̍dbhāno̱ yavi̍ṣṭhya

---

## Verse 16

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 16

gha̱neva̱ viṣva̱gvi ja̱hyarā̍vṇa̱s tapu̍rjambha̱ yo a̍sma̱dhruk |

yo martya̱: śiśī̍te̱ atya̱ktubhi̱rmā na̱: sa ri̱purī̍śata

---

## Verse 17

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 17

a̱gnirva̍vne su̱vīrya̍ma̱gniḥ kaṇvā̍ya̱ saubha̍gam |

a̱gniḥ prāva̍n mi̱trota medhyā̍tithima̱gniḥ sā̱tā u̍pastu̱tam

---

## Verse 18

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 18

a̱gninā̍ tu̱rvaśa̱ṃ yadu̍ṃ parā̱vata̍ u̱grāde̍vaṃ havāmahe |

a̱gnirna̍ya̱nnava̍vāstvaṃ bṛ̱hadra̍thaṃ tu̱rvīti̱ṃ dasya̍ve̱ saha̍:

---

## Verse 19

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 19

ni tvāma̍gne̱ manu̍rdadhe̱ jyoti̱rjanā̍ya̱ śaśva̍te |

dī̱detha̱ kaṇva̍ ṛ̱tajā̍ta ukṣi̱to yaṃ na̍ma̱syanti̍ kṛ̱ṣṭaya̍:

---

## Verse 20

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 20

tve̱ṣāso̍ a̱gnerama̍vanto a̱rcayo̍ bhī̱māso̱ na pratī̍taye |

ra̱kṣa̱svina̱: sada̱mid yā̍tu̱māva̍to̱ viśva̱ṃ sama̱triṇa̍ṃ daha
