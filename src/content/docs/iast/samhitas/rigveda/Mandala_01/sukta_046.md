# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 46

**15 praskaṇvaḥ kāṇvaḥ| aśvinau| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 1

e̱ṣo u̱ṣā apū̍rvyā̱ vyu̍cchati pri̱yā di̱vaḥ | stu̱ṣe vā̍maśvinā bṛ̱hat

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 2

yā da̱srā sindhu̍mātarā mano̱tarā̍ rayī̱ṇām | dhi̱yā de̱vā va̍su̱vidā̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 3

va̱cyante̍ vāṃ kaku̱hāso̍ jū̱rṇāyā̱madhi̍ vi̱ṣṭapi̍ | yad vā̱ṃ ratho̱ vibhi̱ṣpatā̍t

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 4

ha̱viṣā̍ jā̱ro a̱pāṃ pipa̍rti̱ papu̍rirnarā | pi̱tā kuṭa̍sya carṣa̱ṇiḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 5

ā̱dā̱ro vā̍ṃ matī̱nāṃ nāsa̍tyā matavacasā | pā̱taṃ soma̍sya dhṛṣṇu̱yā

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 6

yā na̱: pīpa̍radaśvinā̱ jyoti̍ṣmatī̱ tama̍sti̱raḥ | tāma̱sme rā̍sāthā̱miṣa̍m

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 7

ā no̍ nā̱vā ma̍tī̱nāṃ yā̱taṃ pā̱rāya̱ ganta̍ve | yu̱ñjāthā̍maśvinā̱ ratha̍m

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 8

a̱ritra̍ṃ vāṃ di̱vaspṛ̱thu tī̱rthe sindhū̍nā̱ṃ ratha̍: | dhi̱yā yu̍yujra̱ inda̍vaḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 9

di̱vaska̍ṇvāsa̱ inda̍vo̱ vasu̱ sindhū̍nāṃ pa̱de | svaṃ va̱vriṃ kuha̍ dhitsathaḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 10

abhū̍du̱ bhā u̍ a̱ṃśave̱ hira̍ṇya̱ṃ prati̱ sūrya̍: | vya̍khyajji̱hvayāsi̍taḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 11

abhū̍du pā̱rameta̍ve̱ panthā̍ ṛ̱tasya̍ sādhu̱yā | ada̍rśi̱ vi sru̱tirdi̱vaḥ

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 12

tatta̱dida̱śvino̱ravo̍ jari̱tā prati̍ bhūṣati | made̱ soma̍sya̱ pipra̍toḥ

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 13

vā̱va̱sā̱nā vi̱vasva̍ti̱ soma̍sya pī̱tyā gi̱rā | ma̱nu̱ṣvaccha̍mbhū̱ ā ga̍tam

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 14

yu̱voru̱ṣā anu̱ śriya̱ṃ pari̍jmanoru̱pāca̍rat | ṛ̱tā va̍natho a̱ktubhi̍:

## Verse 15
