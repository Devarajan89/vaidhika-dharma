# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 154

**6 dīrghatamā aucathyaḥ| viṣṇuḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 154 | **Verse:** 1

viṣṇo̱rnu ka̍ṃ vī̱ryā̍ṇi̱ pra vo̍ca̱ṃ yaḥ pārthi̍vāni vima̱me rajā̍ṃsi |
yo aska̍bhāya̱dutta̍raṃ sa̱dhastha̍ṃ vicakramā̱ṇastre̱dhoru̍gā̱yaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 154 | **Verse:** 2

pra tad viṣṇu̍: stavate vī̱rye̍ṇa mṛ̱go na bhī̱maḥ ku̍ca̱ro gi̍ri̱ṣṭhāḥ |

yasyo̱ruṣu̍ tri̱ṣu vi̱krama̍ṇeṣvadhikṣi̱yanti̱ bhuva̍nāni̱ viśvā̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 154 | **Verse:** 3

pra viṣṇa̍ve śū̱ṣame̍tu̱ manma̍ giri̱kṣita̍ urugā̱yāya̱ vṛṣṇe̍ |

ya i̱daṃ dī̱rghaṃ praya̍taṃ sa̱dhastha̱meko̍ vima̱me tri̱bhirit pa̱debhi̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 154 | **Verse:** 4

yasya̱ trī pū̱rṇā madhu̍nā pa̱dānyakṣī̍yamāṇā sva̱dhayā̱ mada̍nti |

ya u̍ tri̱dhātu̍ pṛthi̱vīmu̱ta dyāmeko̍ dā̱dhāra̱ bhuva̍nāni̱ viśvā̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 154 | **Verse:** 5

tada̍sya pri̱yama̱bhi pātho̍ aśyā̱ṃ naro̱ yatra̍ deva̱yavo̱ mada̍nti |

u̱ru̱kra̱masya̱ sa hi bandhu̍ri̱tthā viṣṇo̍: pa̱de pa̍ra̱me madhva̱ utsa̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 154 | **Verse:** 6

tā vā̱ṃ vāstū̍nyuśmasi̱ gama̍dhyai̱ yatra̱ gāvo̱ bhūri̍śṛṅgā a̱yāsa̍: |

atrāha̱ tadu̍rugā̱yasya̱ vṛṣṇa̍: para̱maṃ pa̱damava̍ bhāti̱ bhūri̍
