# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 180

**1-10 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ| aśvinau| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 180 | **Verse:** 1

yu̱vo rajā̍ṃsi su̱yamā̍so̱ aśvā̱ ratho̱ yadvā̱ṃ paryarṇā̍ṃsi̱ dīya̍t |
hi̱ra̱ṇyayā̍ vāṃ pa̱vaya̍: pruṣāya̱n madhva̱: piba̍ntā u̱ṣasa̍: sacethe

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 180 | **Verse:** 2

yu̱vamatya̱syāva̍ nakṣatho̱ yad vipa̍tmano̱ narya̍sya̱ praya̍jyoḥ |

svasā̱ yad vāṃ viśvagūrtī̱ bharā̍ti̱ vājā̱yeṭṭe̍ madhupāvi̱ṣe ca̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 180 | **Verse:** 3

yu̱vaṃ paya̍ u̱sriyā̍yāmadhattaṃ pa̱kvamā̱māyā̱mava̱ pūrvya̱ṃ goḥ |

a̱ntaryad va̱nino̍ vāmṛtapsū hvā̱ro na śuci̱ryaja̍te ha̱viṣmā̍n

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 180 | **Verse:** 4

yu̱vaṃ ha̍ gha̱rmaṃ madhu̍manta̱matra̍ye̱ 'po na kṣodo̍'vṛṇītame̱ṣe |

tad vā̍ṃ narāvaśvinā̱ paśva̍iṣṭī̱ rathye̍va ca̱krā prati̍ yanti̱ madhva̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 180 | **Verse:** 5

ā vā̍ṃ dā̱nāya̍ vavṛtīya dasrā̱ gorohe̍ṇa tau̱gryo na jivri̍: |

a̱paḥ kṣo̱ṇī sa̍cate̱ māhi̍nā vāṃ jū̱rṇo vā̱makṣu̱raṃha̍so yajatrā

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 180 | **Verse:** 6

ni yad yu̱vethe̍ ni̱yuta̍: sudānū̱ upa̍ sva̱dhābhi̍: sṛjatha̱: pura̍ṃdhim |

preṣa̱d veṣa̱d vāto̱ na sū̱rirā ma̱he da̍de suvra̱to na vāja̍m

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 180 | **Verse:** 7

va̱yaṃ ci̱ddhi vā̍ṃ jari̱tāra̍: sa̱tyā vi̍pa̱nyāma̍he̱ vi pa̱ṇirhi̱tāvā̍n |

adhā̍ ci̱ddhi ṣmā̍śvināvanindyā pā̱tho hi ṣmā̍ vṛṣaṇā̱vanti̍devam

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 180 | **Verse:** 8

yu̱vāṃ ci̱ddhi ṣmā̍śvinā̱vanu̱ dyūn viru̍drasya pra̱srava̍ṇasya sā̱tau |

a̱gastyo̍ na̱rāṃ nṛṣu̱ praśa̍sta̱: kārā̍dhunīva citayat sa̱hasrai̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 180 | **Verse:** 9

pra yad vahe̍the mahi̱nā ratha̍sya̱ pra sya̍ndrā yātho̱ manu̍ṣo̱ na hotā̍ |

dha̱ttaṃ sū̱ribhya̍ u̱ta vā̱ svaśvya̱ṃ nāsa̍tyā rayi̱ṣāca̍: syāma

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 180 | **Verse:** 10

taṃ vā̱ṃ ratha̍ṃ va̱yama̱dyā hu̍vema̱ stomai̍raśvinā suvi̱tāya̱ navya̍m |

ari̍ṣṭanemi̱ṃ pari̱ dyāmi̍yā̱naṃ vi̱dyāme̱ṣaṃ vṛ̱jana̍ṃ jī̱radā̍num
