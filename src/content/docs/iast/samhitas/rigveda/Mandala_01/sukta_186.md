# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 186

**11 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ | viśve devāḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 186 | **Verse:** 1

ā na̱ il̤ā̍bhirvi̱dathe̍ suśa̱sti vi̱śvāna̍raḥ savi̱tā de̱va e̍tu |
api̱ yathā̍ yuvāno̱ matsa̍thā no̱ viśva̱ṃ jaga̍dabhipi̱tve ma̍nī̱ṣā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 186 | **Verse:** 2

ā no̱ viśva̱ āskrā̍ gamantu de̱vā mi̱tro a̍rya̱mā varu̍ṇaḥ sa̱joṣā̍: |

bhuva̱n yathā̍ no̱ viśve̍ vṛ̱dhāsa̱: kara̍ntsu̱ṣāhā̍ vithu̱raṃ na śava̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 186 | **Verse:** 3

preṣṭha̍ṃ vo̱ ati̍thiṃ gṛṇīṣe̱ 'gniṃ śa̱stibhi̍stu̱rvaṇi̍: sa̱joṣā̍: |

asa̱d yathā̍ no̱ varu̍ṇaḥ sukī̱rtiriṣa̍śca parṣadarigū̱rtaḥ sū̱riḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 186 | **Verse:** 4

upa̍ va̱ eṣe̱ nama̍sā jigī̱ṣoṣāsā̱naktā̍ su̱dughe̍va dhe̱nuḥ |

sa̱mā̱ne aha̍n vi̱mimā̍no a̱rkaṃ viṣu̍rūpe̱ paya̍si̱ sasmi̱nnūdha̍n

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 186 | **Verse:** 5

u̱ta no 'hi̍rbu̱dhnyo̱3 maya̍ska̱: śiśu̱ṃ na pi̱pyuṣī̍va veti̱ sindhu̍: |

yena̱ napā̍tama̱pāṃ ju̱nāma̍ mano̱juvo̱ vṛṣa̍ṇo̱ yaṃ vaha̍nti

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 186 | **Verse:** 6

u̱ta na̍ ī̱ṃ tvaṣṭā ga̱ntvacchā̱ smat sū̱ribhi̍rabhipi̱tve sa̱joṣā̍: |

ā vṛ̍tra̱hendra̍ścarṣaṇi̱prāstu̱viṣṭa̍mo na̱rāṃ na̍ i̱ha ga̍myāḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 186 | **Verse:** 7

u̱ta na̍ īṃ ma̱tayo'śva̍yogā̱: śiśu̱ṃ na gāva̱staru̍ṇaṃ rihanti |

tamī̱ṃ giro̱ jana̍yo̱ na patnī̍: sura̱bhiṣṭa̍maṃ na̱rāṃ na̍santa

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 186 | **Verse:** 8

u̱ta na̍ īṃ ma̱ruto̍ vṛ̱ddhase̍nā̱: smad roda̍sī̱ sama̍nasaḥ sadantu |

pṛṣa̍daśvāso̱'vana̍yo̱ na rathā̍ ri̱śāda̍so mitra̱yujo̱ na de̱vāḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 186 | **Verse:** 9

pra nu yade̍ṣāṃ mahi̱nā ci̍ki̱tre pra yu̍ñjate pra̱yuja̱ste su̍vṛ̱kti |

adha̱ yade̍ṣāṃ su̱dine̱ na śaru̱rviśva̱meri̍ṇaṃ pruṣā̱yanta̱ senā̍:

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 186 | **Verse:** 10

pro a̱śvinā̱vava̍se kṛṇudhva̱ṃ pra pū̱ṣaṇa̱ṃ svata̍vaso̱ hi santi̍ |

a̱dve̱ṣo viṣṇu̱rvāta̍ ṛbhu̱kṣā acchā̍ su̱mnāya̍ vavṛtīya de̱vān

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 186 | **Verse:** 11

i̱yaṃ sā vo̍ a̱sme dīdhi̍tiryajatrā api̱prāṇī̍ ca̱ sada̍nī ca bhūyāḥ |

ni yā de̱veṣu̱ yata̍te vasū̱yurvi̱dyāme̱ṣaṃ vṛ̱jana̍ṃ jī̱radā̍num
