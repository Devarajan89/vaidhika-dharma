# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 26

**10 ājīgartiḥ śunaḥśepa sa kṛtrimo vaiśvāmitro devarātaḥ| agniḥ,| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 1

vasi̍ṣvā̱ hi mi̍yedhya̱ vastrā̍ṇyūrjāṃ pate | semaṃ no̍ adhva̱raṃ ya̍ja

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 2

ni no̱ hotā̱ vare̍ṇya̱: sadā̍ yaviṣṭha̱ manma̍bhiḥ | agne̍ di̱vitma̍tā̱ vaca̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 3

ā hi ṣmā̍ sū̱nave̍ pi̱tāpiryaja̍tyā̱paye̍ | sakhā̱ sakhye̱ vare̍ṇyaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 4

ā no̍ ba̱rhī ri̱śāda̍so̱ varu̍ṇo mi̱tro a̍rya̱mā | sīda̍ntu̱ manu̍ṣo yathā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 5

pūrvya̍ hotara̱sya no̱ manda̍sva sa̱khyasya̍ ca | i̱mā u̱ ṣu śru̍dhī̱ gira̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 6

yacci̱ddhi śaśva̍tā̱ tanā̍ de̱vaṃde̍va̱ṃ yajā̍mahe | tve iddhū̍yate ha̱viḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 7

pri̱yo no̍ astu vi̱śpati̱rhotā̍ ma̱ndro vare̍ṇyaḥ | pri̱yāḥ sva̱gnayo̍ va̱yam

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 8

sva̱gnayo̱ hi vārya̍ṃ de̱vāso̍ dadhi̱re ca̍ naḥ | sva̱gnayo̍ manāmahe

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 9

athā̍ na u̱bhaye̍ṣā̱mamṛ̍ta̱ martyā̍nām | mi̱thaḥ sa̍ntu̱ praśa̍stayaḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 10

viśve̍bhiragne a̱gnibhi̍ri̱maṃ ya̱jñami̱daṃ vaca̍: | cano̍ dhāḥ sahaso yaho
