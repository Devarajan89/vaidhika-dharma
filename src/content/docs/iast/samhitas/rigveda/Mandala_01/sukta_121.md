# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 121

**15 kakṣīvān dairghatamasa auśijaḥ| indro viśve devā vā| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 1

kadi̱tthā nṝm̐: pātra̍ṃ devaya̱tāṃ śrava̱d giro̱ aṅgi̍rasāṃ tura̱ṇyan |
pra yadāna̱ḍviśa̱ ā ha̱rmyasyo̱ru kra̍ṃsate adhva̱re yaja̍traḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 2

stambhī̍ddha̱ dyāṃ sa dha̱ruṇa̍ṃ pruṣāyadṛ̱bhurvājā̍ya̱ dravi̍ṇa̱ṃ naro̱ goḥ |

anu̍ sva̱jāṃ ma̍hi̱ṣaśca̍kṣata̱ vrāṃ menā̱maśva̍sya̱ pari̍ mā̱tara̱ṃ goḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 3

nakṣa̱ddhava̍maru̱ṇīḥ pū̱rvyaṃ rāṭ tu̱ro vi̱śāmaṅgi̍rasā̱manu̱ dyūn |

takṣa̱d vajra̱ṃ niyu̍taṃ ta̱stambha̱d dyāṃ catu̍ṣpade̱ naryā̍ya dvi̱pāde̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 4

a̱sya made̍ sva̱rya̍ṃ dā ṛ̱tāyāpī̍vṛtamu̱sriyā̍ṇā̱manī̍kam |

yaddha̍ pra̱sarge̍ trika̱kumni̱varta̱dapa̱ druho̱ mānu̍ṣasya̱ duro̍ vaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 5

tubhya̱ṃ payo̱ yat pi̱tarā̱vanī̍tā̱ṃ rādha̍: su̱reta̍stu̱raṇe̍ bhura̱ṇyū |

śuci̱ yat te rekṇa̱ āya̍janta saba̱rdughā̍yā̱: paya̍ u̱sriyā̍yāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 6

adha̱ pra ja̍jñe ta̱raṇi̍rmamattu̱ pra ro̍cya̱syā u̱ṣaso̱ na sūra̍: |

indu̱ryebhi̱rāṣṭa̱ svedu̍havyaiḥ sru̱veṇa̍ si̱ñcañja̱raṇā̱bhi dhāma̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 7

svi̱dhmā yad va̱nadhi̍tirapa̱syāt sūro̍ adhva̱re pari̱ rodha̍nā̱ goḥ |

yaddha̍ pra̱bhāsi̱ kṛtvyā̱m̐ anu̱ dyūnana̍rviśe pa̱śviṣe̍ tu̱rāya̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 8

a̱ṣṭā ma̱ho di̱va ādo̱ harī̍ i̱ha dyu̍mnā̱sāha̍ma̱bhi yo̍dhā̱na utsa̍m |

hari̱ṃ yatte̍ ma̱ndina̍ṃ du̱kṣan vṛ̱dhe gora̍bhasa̱madri̍bhirvā̱tāpya̍m

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 9

tvamā̍ya̱saṃ prati̍ vartayo̱ gordi̱vo aśmā̍na̱mupa̍nīta̱mṛbhvā̍ |

kutsā̍ya̱ yatra̍ puruhūta va̱nvañchuṣṇa̍mana̱ntaiḥ pa̍ri̱yāsi̍ va̱dhaiḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 10

pu̱rā yat sūra̱stama̍so̱ apī̍te̱stama̍drivaḥ phali̱gaṃ he̱tima̍sya |

śuṣṇa̍sya ci̱t pari̍hita̱ṃ yadojo̍ di̱vaspari̱ sugra̍thita̱ṃ tadāda̍:

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 11

anu̍ tvā ma̱hī pāja̍sī aca̱kre dyāvā̱kṣāmā̍ madatāmindra̱ karma̍n |

tvaṃ vṛ̱tramā̱śayā̍naṃ si̱rāsu̍ ma̱ho vajre̍ṇa siṣvapo va̱rāhu̍m

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 12

tvami̍ndra̱ naryo̱ yām̐ avo̱ nṝn tiṣṭhā̱ vāta̍sya su̱yujo̱ vahi̍ṣṭhān |

yaṃ te̍ kā̱vya u̱śanā̍ ma̱ndina̱ṃ dād vṛ̍tra̱haṇa̱ṃ pārya̍ṃ tatakṣa̱ vajra̍m

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 13

tvaṃ sūro̍ ha̱rito̍ rāmayo̱ nṝn bhara̍cca̱krameta̍śo̱ nāyami̍ndra |

prāsya̍ pā̱raṃ na̍va̱tiṃ nā̱vyā̍nā̱mapi̍ ka̱rtama̍varta̱yo'ya̍jyūn

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 14

tvaṃ no̍ a̱syā i̍ndra du̱rhaṇā̍yāḥ pā̱hi va̍jrivo duri̱tāda̱bhīke̍ |

pra no̱ vājā̍n ra̱thyo̱ 3 aśva̍budhyāni̱ṣe ya̍ndhi̱ śrava̍se sū̱nṛtā̍yai

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 121 | **Verse:** 15

mā sā te̍ a̱smat su̍ma̱tirvi da̍sa̱d vāja̍pramaha̱: samiṣo̍ varanta |

ā no̍ bhaja maghava̱n goṣva̱ryo maṃhi̍ṣṭhāste sadha̱māda̍: syāma
