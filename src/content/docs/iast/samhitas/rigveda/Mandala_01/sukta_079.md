# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 79

**12 gotamo rāhūgaṇaḥ| 1-3agniḥ madhyamo' gnirvā, 4-12 agniḥ| 1-3 triṣṭup, 4-6 uṣṇik, 7-12 gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 1

hira̍ṇyakeśo̱ raja̍so visā̱re 'hi̱rdhuni̱rvāta̍ iva̱ dhrajī̍mān |
śuci̍bhrājā u̱ṣaso̱ nave̍dā̱ yaśa̍svatīrapa̱syuvo̱ na sa̱tyāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 2

ā te̍ supa̱rṇā a̍minanta̱m̐ evai̍: kṛ̱ṣṇo no̍nāva vṛṣa̱bho yadī̱dam |

śi̱vābhi̱rna smaya̍mānābhi̱rāgā̱t pata̍nti̱ miha̍: sta̱naya̍ntya̱bhrā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 3

yadī̍mṛ̱tasya̱ paya̍sā̱ piyā̍no̱ naya̍nnṛ̱tasya̍ pa̱thibhī̱ raji̍ṣṭhaiḥ |

a̱rya̱mā mi̱tro varu̍ṇa̱: pari̍jmā̱ tvaca̍ṃ pṛñca̱ntyupa̍rasya̱ yonau̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 4

agne̱ vāja̍sya̱ goma̍ta̱ īśā̍naḥ sahaso yaho | a̱sme dhe̍hi jātavedo̱ mahi̱ śrava̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 5

sa i̍dhā̱no vasu̍ṣka̱vira̱gnirī̱l̤enyo̍ gi̱rā | re̱vada̱smabhya̍ṃ purvaṇīka dīdihi

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 6

kṣa̱po rā̍jannu̱ta tmanāgne̱ vasto̍ru̱toṣasa̍: | sa ti̍gmajambha ra̱kṣaso̍ daha̱ prati̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 7

avā̍ no agna ū̱tibhi̍rgāya̱trasya̱ prabha̍rmaṇi | viśvā̍su dhī̱ṣu va̍ndya

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 8

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 9

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 10

pra pū̱tāsti̱gmaśo̍ciṣe̱ vāco̍ gotamā̱gnaye̍ | bhara̍sva sumna̱yurgira̍:

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 11
