# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 153

**4 dīrghatamā aucathyaḥ| mitrāvaruṇau| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 153 | **Verse:** 1

yajā̍mahe vāṃ ma̱haḥ sa̱joṣā̍ ha̱vyebhi̍rmitrāvaruṇā̱ namo̍bhiḥ |
ghṛ̱tairghṛ̍tasnū̱ adha̱ yad vā̍ma̱sme a̍dhva̱ryavo̱ na dhī̱tibhi̱rbhara̍nti

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 153 | **Verse:** 2

prastu̍tirvā̱ṃ dhāma̱ na prayu̍kti̱rayā̍mi mitrāvaruṇā suvṛ̱ktiḥ |

a̱nakti̱ yad vā̍ṃ vi̱dathe̍ṣu̱ hotā̍ su̱mnaṃ vā̍ṃ sū̱rirvṛ̍ṣaṇā̱viya̍kṣan

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 153 | **Verse:** 3

pī̱pāya̍ dhe̱nuradi̍tirṛ̱tāya̱ janā̍ya mitrāvaruṇā havi̱rde |

hi̱noti̱ yad vā̍ṃ vi̱dathe̍ sapa̱ryan tsa rā̱taha̍vyo̱ mānu̍ṣo̱ na hotā̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 153 | **Verse:** 4

u̱ta vā̍ṃ vi̱kṣu madyā̱svandho̱ gāva̱ āpa̍śca pīpayanta de̱vīḥ |

u̱to no̍ a̱sya pū̱rvyaḥ pati̱rdan vī̱taṃ pā̱taṃ paya̍sa u̱sriyā̍yāḥ
