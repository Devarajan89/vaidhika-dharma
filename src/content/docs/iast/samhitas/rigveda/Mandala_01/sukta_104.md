# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 104

**9 kutsa āṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 1

yoni̍ṣṭa indra ni̱ṣade̍ akāri̱ tamā ni ṣī̍da svā̱no nārvā̍ |
vi̱mucyā̱ vayo̍'va̱sāyāśvā̍n do̱ṣā vasto̱rvahī̍yasaḥ prapi̱tve

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 2

o tye nara̱ indra̍mū̱taye̍ gu̱rnū ci̱t tān tsa̱dyo adhva̍no jagamyāt |

de̱vāso̍ ma̱nyuṃ dāsa̍sya ścamna̱n te na̱ ā va̍kṣan tsuvi̱tāya̱ varṇa̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 3

ava̱ tmanā̍ bharate̱ keta̍vedā̱ ava̱ tmanā̍ bharate̱ phena̍mu̱dan |

kṣī̱reṇa̍ snāta̱: kuya̍vasya̱ yoṣe̍ ha̱te te syā̍tāṃ prava̱ṇe śiphā̍yāḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 4

yu̱yopa̱ nābhi̱rupa̍rasyā̱yoḥ pra pūrvā̍bhistirate̱ rāṣṭi̱ śūra̍: |

a̱ñja̱sī ku̍li̱śī vī̱rapa̍tnī̱ payo̍ hinvā̱nā u̱dabhi̍rbharante

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 5

prati̱ yat syā nīthāda̍rśi̱ dasyo̱roko̱ nācchā̱ sada̍naṃ jāna̱tī gā̍t |

adha̍ smā no maghavañcarkṛ̱tādinmā no̍ ma̱gheva̍ niṣṣa̱pī parā̍ dāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 6

sa tvaṃ na̍ indra̱ sūrye̱ so a̱psva̍nāgā̱stva ā bha̍ja jīvaśa̱ṃse |

mānta̍rā̱ṃ bhuja̱mā rī̍riṣo na̱: śraddhi̍taṃ te maha̱ta i̍ndri̱yāya̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 7

adhā̍ manye̱ śrat te asmā adhāyi̱ vṛṣā̍ codasva maha̱te dhanā̍ya |

mā no̱ akṛ̍te puruhūta̱ yonā̱vindra̱ kṣudhya̍dbhyo̱ vaya̍ āsu̱tiṃ dā̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 8

mā no̍ vadhīrindra̱ mā parā̍ dā̱ mā na̍: pri̱yā bhoja̍nāni̱ pra mo̍ṣīḥ |

ā̱ṇḍā mā no̍ maghavañchakra̱ nirbhe̱nmā na̱: pātrā̍ bhet sa̱hajā̍nuṣāṇi

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 104 | **Verse:** 9

a̱rvāṅehi̱ soma̍kāmaṃ tvāhura̱yaṃ su̱tastasya̍ pibā̱ madā̍ya |

u̱ru̱vyacā̍ ja̱ṭhara̱ ā vṛ̍ṣasva pi̱teva̍ naḥ śṛṇuhi hū̱yamā̍naḥ
