# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 170

**(5) 1,3,4 indraḥ, (4agastyo vā,) 2,5 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ | indraḥ| 1bṛhatī, 2-4 anuṣṭup, 5 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 170 | **Verse:** 1

na nū̱namasti̱ no śvaḥ kastad ve̍da̱ yadadbhu̍tam |
a̱nyasya̍ ci̱ttama̱bhi sa̍ṃca̱reṇya̍mu̱tādhī̍ta̱ṃ vi na̍śyati

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 170 | **Verse:** 2

kiṃ na̍ indra jighāṃsasi̱ bhrāta̍ro ma̱ruta̱stava̍ |

tebhi̍: kalpasva sādhu̱yā mā na̍: sa̱mara̍ṇe vadhīḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 170 | **Verse:** 3

kiṃ no̍ bhrātaragastya̱ sakhā̱ sannati̍ manyase |

vi̱dmā hi te̱ yathā̱ mano̱ 'smabhya̱minna di̍tsasi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 170 | **Verse:** 4

ara̍ṃ kṛṇvantu̱ vedi̱ṃ sama̱gnimi̍ndhatāṃ pu̱raḥ |

tatrā̱mṛta̍sya̱ ceta̍naṃ ya̱jñaṃ te̍ tanavāvahai

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 170 | **Verse:** 5

tvamī̍śiṣe vasupate̱ vasū̍nā̱ṃ tvaṃ mi̱trāṇā̍ṃ mitrapate̱ dheṣṭha̍: |

indra̱ tvaṃ ma̱rudbhi̱: saṃ va̍da̱svādha̱ prāśā̍na ṛtu̱thā ha̱vīṃṣi̍
