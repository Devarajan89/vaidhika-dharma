# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 190

**8 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ | bṛhaspatiḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 190 | **Verse:** 1

a̱na̱rvāṇa̍ṃ vṛṣa̱bhaṃ ma̱ndraji̍hva̱ṃ bṛha̱spati̍ṃ vardhayā̱ navya̍ma̱rkaiḥ |
gā̱thā̱nya̍: su̱ruco̱ yasya̍ de̱vā ā̍śṛ̱ṇvanti̱ nava̍mānasya̱ martā̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 190 | **Verse:** 2

tamṛ̱tviyā̱ upa̱ vāca̍: sacante̱ sargo̱ na yo de̍vaya̱tāmasa̍rji |

bṛha̱spati̱: sa hyañjo̱ varā̍ṃsi̱ vibhvābha̍va̱t samṛ̱te mā̍ta̱riśvā̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 190 | **Verse:** 3

upa̍stuti̱ṃ nama̍sa̱ udya̍tiṃ ca̱ śloka̍ṃ yaṃsat savi̱teva̱ pra bā̱hū |

a̱sya kratvā̍ha̱nyo̱3 yo asti̍ mṛ̱go na bhī̱mo a̍ra̱kṣasa̱stuvi̍ṣmān

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 190 | **Verse:** 4

a̱sya śloko̍ di̱vīya̍te pṛthi̱vyāmatyo̱ na ya̍ṃsad yakṣa̱bhṛd vice̍tāḥ |

mṛ̱gāṇā̱ṃ na he̱tayo̱ yanti̍ ce̱mā bṛha̱spate̱rahi̍māyām̐ a̱bhi dyūn

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 190 | **Verse:** 5

ye tvā̍ devosri̱kaṃ manya̍mānāḥ pā̱pā bha̱dramu̍pa̱jīva̍nti pa̱jrāḥ |

na dū̱ḍhye̱3 anu̍ dadāsi vā̱maṃ bṛha̍spate̱ caya̍sa̱ it piyā̍rum

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 190 | **Verse:** 6

su̱praitu̍: sū̱yava̍so̱ na panthā̍ durni̱yantu̱: pari̍prīto̱ na mi̱traḥ |

a̱na̱rvāṇo̍ a̱bhi ye cakṣa̍te̱ no'pī̍vṛtā aporṇu̱vanto̍ asthuḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 190 | **Verse:** 7

saṃ yaṃ stubho̱'vana̍yo̱ na yanti̍ samu̱draṃ na sra̱vato̱ rodha̍cakrāḥ |

sa vi̱dvām̐ u̱bhaya̍ṃ caṣṭe a̱ntarbṛha̱spati̱stara̱ āpa̍śca̱ gṛdhra̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 190 | **Verse:** 8

e̱vā ma̱hastu̍vijā̱tastuvi̍ṣmā̱n bṛha̱spati̍rvṛṣa̱bho dhā̍yi de̱vaḥ |

sa na̍: stu̱to vī̱rava̍d dhātu̱ goma̍d vi̱dyāme̱ṣaṃ vṛ̱jana̍ṃ jī̱radā̍num
