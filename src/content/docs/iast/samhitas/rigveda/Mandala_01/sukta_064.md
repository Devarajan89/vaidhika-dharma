# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 64

**15 nedhā gautamaḥ| marutaḥ| jagatī, 15 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 1

vṛṣṇe̱ śardhā̍ya̱ suma̍khāya ve̱dhase̱ nodha̍: suvṛ̱ktiṃ pra bha̍rā ma̱rudbhya̍: |
a̱po na dhīro̱ mana̍sā su̱hastyo̱ gira̱: sama̍ñje vi̱dathe̍ṣvā̱bhuva̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 2

te ja̍jñire di̱va ṛ̱ṣvāsa̍ u̱kṣaṇo̍ ru̱drasya̱ maryā̱ asu̍rā are̱pasa̍: |

pā̱va̱kāsa̱: śuca̍ya̱: sūryā̍ iva̱ satvā̍no̱ na dra̱psino̍ gho̱rava̍rpasaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 3

yuvā̍no ru̱drā a̱jarā̍ abho̱gghano̍ vava̱kṣuradhri̍gāva̱: parva̍tā iva |

dṛ̱hlā ci̱d viśvā̱ bhuva̍nāni̱ pārthi̍vā̱ pra cyā̍vayanti di̱vyāni̍ ma̱jmanā̍

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 4

ci̱traira̱ñjibhi̱rvapu̍ṣe̱ vya̍ñjate̱ vakṣa̍ḥsu ru̱kmām̐ adhi̍ yetire śu̱bhe |

aṃse̍ṣveṣā̱ṃ ni mi̍mṛkṣṛrṛ̱ṣṭaya̍: sā̱kaṃ ja̍jñire sva̱dhayā̍ di̱vo nara̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 5

ī̱śā̱na̱kṛto̱ dhuna̍yo ri̱śāda̍so̱ vātā̍n vi̱dyuta̱stavi̍ṣībhirakrata |

du̱hantyūdha̍rdi̱vyāni̱ dhūta̍yo̱ bhūmi̍ṃ pinvanti̱ paya̍sā̱ pari̍jrayaḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 6

pinva̍ntya̱po ma̱ruta̍: su̱dāna̍va̱: payo̍ ghṛ̱tava̍d vidathe̍ṣvā̱bhuva̍: |

atya̱ṃ na mi̱he vi na̍yanti vā̱jina̱mutsa̍ṃ duhanti sta̱naya̍nta̱makṣi̍tam

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 7

ma̱hi̱ṣāso̍ mā̱yina̍ści̱trabhā̍navo gi̱rayo̱ na svata̍vaso raghu̱ṣyada̍: |

mṛ̱gā i̍va ha̱stina̍: khādathā̱ vanā̱ yadāru̍ṇīṣu̱ tavi̍ṣī̱rayu̍gdhvam

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 8

kṣapo̱ jinva̍nta̱: pṛṣa̍tībhirṛ̱ṣṭibhi̱: samitsa̱bādha̱: śava̱sāhi̍manyavaḥ

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 9

ā va̱ndhure̍ṣva̱mati̱rna da̍rśa̱tā vi̱dyunna ta̍sthau maruto̱ rathe̍ṣu vaḥ

## Verse 10

astā̍ra̱ iṣa̍ṃ dadhire̱ gabha̍styorana̱ntaśu̍ṣmā̱ vṛṣa̍khādayo̱ nara̍:

## Verse 11

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 13

arva̍dbhi̱rvāja̍ṃ bharate̱ dhanā̱ nṛbhi̍rā̱pṛcchya̱ṃ kratu̱mā kṣe̍ti̱ puṣya̍ti
