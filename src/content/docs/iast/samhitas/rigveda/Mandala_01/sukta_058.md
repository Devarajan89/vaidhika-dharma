# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 58

**9 nodhā gautamaḥ| agniḥ| jagatī, 6-9 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 1

nū ci̍t saho̱jā a̱mṛto̱ ni tu̍ndate̱ hotā̱ yad dū̱to abha̍vad vi̱vasva̍taḥ |
vi sādhi̍ṣṭhebhiḥ pa̱thibhī̱ rajo̍ mama̱ ā de̱vatā̍tā ha̱viṣā̍ vivāsati

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 2

ā svamadma̍ yu̱vamā̍no a̱jara̍stṛ̱ṣva̍vi̱ṣyanna̍ta̱seṣu̍ tiṣṭhati |

atyo̱ na pṛ̱ṣṭhaṃ pru̍ṣi̱tasya̍ rocate di̱vo na sānu̍ sta̱naya̍nnacikradat

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 3

krā̱ṇā ru̱drebhi̱rvasu̍bhiḥ pu̱rohi̍to̱ hotā̱ niṣa̍tto rayi̱ṣāl̤ama̍rtyaḥ |

ratho̱ na vi̱kṣvṛ̍ñjasā̱na ā̱yuṣu̱ vyā̍nu̱ṣagvāryā̍ de̱va ṛ̍ṇvati

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 4

vi vāta̍jūto ata̱seṣu̍ tiṣṭhate̱ vṛthā̍ ju̱hūbhi̱: sṛṇyā̍ tuvi̱ṣvaṇi̍: |

tṛ̱ṣu yada̍gne va̱nino̍ vṛṣā̱yase̍ kṛ̱ṣṇaṃ ta̱ ema̱ ruśa̍dūrme ajara

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 5

tapu̍rjambho̱ vana̱ ā vāta̍codito yū̱the na sā̱hvām̐ ava̍ vāti̱ vaṃsa̍gaḥ |

a̱bhi̱vraja̱nnakṣi̍ta̱ṃ pāja̍sā̱ raja̍: sthā̱tuśca̱ratha̍ṃ bhayate pata̱triṇa̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 6

da̱dhuṣṭvā̱ bhṛga̍vo̱ mānu̍ṣe̱ṣvā ra̱yiṃ na cāru̍ṃ su̱hava̱ṃ jane̍bhyaḥ |

hotā̍ramagne̱ ati̍thi̱ṃ vare̍ṇyaṃ mi̱traṃ na śeva̍ṃ di̱vyāya̱ janma̍ne

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 7

hotā̍raṃ sa̱pta ju̱hvo̱ 3 yaji̍ṣṭha̱ṃ yaṃ vā̱ghato̍ vṛ̱ṇate̍ adhva̱reṣu̍ |

a̱gniṃ viśve̍ṣāmara̱tiṃ vasū̍nāṃ sapa̱ryāmi̱ praya̍sā̱ yāmi̱ ratna̍m

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 8

acchi̍drā sūno sahaso no a̱dya sto̱tṛbhyo̍ mitramaha̱: śarma̍ yaccha |

agne̍ gṛ̱ṇanta̱maṃha̍sa uru̱ṣyorjo̍ napāt pū̱rbhirāya̍sībhiḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 9

bhavā̱ varū̍thaṃ gṛṇa̱te vi̍bhāvo̱ bhavā̍ maghavan ma̱ghava̍dbhya̱: śarma̍ |

u̱ru̱ṣyāgne̱ aṃha̍so gṛ̱ṇanta̍ṃ prā̱tarma̱kṣū dhi̱yāva̍surjagamyāt
