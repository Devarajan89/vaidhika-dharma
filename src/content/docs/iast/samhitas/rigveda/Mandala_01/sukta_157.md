# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 157

**6 dīrghatamā aucathyaḥ| aśvinau| jagatī, 5-6 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 157 | **Verse:** 1

abo̍dhya̱gnirjma ude̍ti̱ sūryo̱ vyu 1ṣāśca̱ndrā ma̱hyā̍vo a̱rciṣā̍ |
āyu̍kṣātāma̱śvinā̱ yāta̍ve̱ ratha̱ṃ prāsā̍vīd de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā jaga̱t pṛtha̍k

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 157 | **Verse:** 2

yad yu̱ñjāthe̱ vṛṣa̍ṇamaśvinā̱ ratha̍ṃ ghṛ̱tena̍ no̱ madhu̍nā kṣa̱tramu̍kṣatam |

a̱smāka̱ṃ brahma̱ pṛta̍nāsu jinvataṃ va̱yaṃ dhanā̱ śūra̍sātā bhajemahi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 157 | **Verse:** 3

a̱rvāṅ tri̍ca̱kro ma̍dhu̱vāha̍no̱ ratho̍ jī̱rāśvo̍ a̱śvino̍ryātu̱ suṣṭu̍taḥ |

tri̱va̱ndhu̱ro ma̱ghavā̍ vi̱śvasau̍bhaga̱: śaṃ na̱ ā va̍kṣad dvi̱pade̱ catu̍ṣpade

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 157 | **Verse:** 4

ā na̱ ūrja̍ṃ vahatamaśvinā yu̱vaṃ madhu̍matyā na̱: kaśa̍yā mimikṣatam |

prāyu̱stāri̍ṣṭa̱ṃ nī rapā̍ṃsi mṛkṣata̱ṃ sedha̍ta̱ṃ dveṣo̱ bhava̍taṃ sacā̱bhuvā̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 157 | **Verse:** 5

yu̱vaṃ ha̱ garbha̱ṃ jaga̍tīṣu dhattho yu̱vaṃ viśve̍ṣu̱ bhuva̍neṣva̱ntaḥ |

yu̱vama̱gniṃ ca̍ vṛṣaṇāva̱paśca̱ vana̱spatī̍m̐raśvinā̱vaira̍yethām

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 157 | **Verse:** 6

yu̱vaṃ ha̍ stho bhi̱ṣajā̍ bheṣa̱jebhi̱ratho̍ ha stho ra̱thyā̱ 3 rāthye̍bhiḥ |

atho̍ ha kṣa̱tramadhi̍ dhattha ugrā̱ yo vā̍ṃ ha̱viṣmā̱n mana̍sā da̱dāśa̍
