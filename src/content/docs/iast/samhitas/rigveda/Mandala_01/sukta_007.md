# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 7

**10 madhucchandā vaiśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 1

indra̱midgā̱thino̍ bṛ̱hadindra̍ma̱rkebhi̍ra̱rkiṇa̍:| indra̱ṃ vāṇī̍ranūṣata

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 2

indra̱ iddharyo̱: sacā̱ saṃmi̍śla̱ ā va̍co̱yujā̍| indro̍ va̱jrī hi̍ra̱ṇyaya̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 3

indro̍ dī̱rghāya̱ cakṣa̍sa̱ ā sūrya̍ṃ rohayad di̱vi| vi gobhi̱radri̍mairayat

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 4

indra̱ vāje̍ṣu no'va sa̱hasra̍pradhaneṣu ca| u̱gra u̱grābhi̍rū̱tibhi̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 5

indra̍ṃ va̱yaṃ ma̍hādha̱na indra̱marbhe̍ havāmahe| yuja̍ṃ vṛ̱treṣu̍ va̱jriṇa̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 6

sa no̍  vṛṣanna̱muṃ ca̱ruṃ satrā̍dāva̱nnapā̍ vṛdhi| a̱smabhya̱mapra̍tiṣkutaḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 7

tu̱ñjetu̍ñje̱ ya utta̍re̱ stomā̱ indra̍sya va̱jriṇa̍:| na vi̍ndhe asya suṣṭu̱tim

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 8

vṛṣā̍ yū̱theva̱ vaṃsa̍gaḥ kṛ̱ṣṭīri̍ya̱rtyoja̍sā| īśā̍no̱ apra̍tiṣkutaḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 9

ya eka̍ścarṣaṇī̱nāṃ vasū̍nāmira̱jyati̍| indra̱: pañca̍ kṣitī̱nām

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 10

indra̍ṃ vo vi̱śvata̱spari̱ havā̍mahe̱ jane̍bhyaḥ| a̱smāka̍mastu̱ keva̍laḥ
