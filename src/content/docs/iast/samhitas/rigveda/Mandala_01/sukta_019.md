# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 19

**9 medhātithiḥkāṇvaḥ| agnirmarutaśca|gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 1

prati̱ tyaṃ cāru̍madhva̱raṃ go̍pī̱thāya̱ pra hū̍yase | ma̱rudbhi̍ragna̱ ā ga̍hi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 2

na̱hi de̱vo na martyo̍ ma̱hastava̱ kratu̍ṃ pa̱raḥ | ma̱rudbhi̍ragna̱ ā ga̍hi

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 3

ye ma̱ho raja̍so vi̱durviśve̍ de̱vāso̍ a̱druha̍: | ma̱rudbhi̍ragna̱ ā ga̍hi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 4

ya u̱grā a̱rkamā̍nṛ̱curanā̍dhṛṣṭāsa̱ oja̍sā | ma̱rudbhi̍ragna̱ ā ga̍hi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 5

ye śu̱bhrā gho̱rava̍rpasaḥ sukṣa̱trāso̍ ri̱śāda̍saḥ | ma̱rudbhi̍ragna̱ ā ga̍hi

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 6

ye nāka̱syādhi̍ roca̱ne di̱vi de̱vāsa̱ āsa̍te | ma̱rudbhi̍ragna̱ ā ga̍hi

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 7

ya ī̱ṅkhaya̍nti̱ parva̍tān ti̱raḥ sa̍mu̱drama̍rṇa̱vam | ma̱rudbhi̍ragna̱ ā ga̍hi

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 8

ā ye ta̱nvanti̍ ra̱śmibhi̍s ti̱raḥ sa̍mu̱dramoja̍sā | ma̱rudbhi̍ragna̱ ā ga̍hi

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 9

a̱bhi tvā̍ pū̱rvapī̍taye sṛ̱jāmi̍ so̱myaṃ madhu̍ | ma̱rudbhi̍ragna̱ ā ga̍hi
