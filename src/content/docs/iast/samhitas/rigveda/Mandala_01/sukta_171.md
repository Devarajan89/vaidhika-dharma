# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 171

**6 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ | marutaḥ,3-6 marutvānindraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 171 | **Verse:** 1

prati̍ va e̱nā nama̍sā̱hame̍mi sū̱ktena̍ bhikṣe suma̱tiṃ tu̱rāṇā̍m |
ra̱rā̱ṇatā̍ maruto ve̱dyābhi̱rni hel̤o̍ dha̱tta vi mu̍cadhva̱maśvā̍n

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 171 | **Verse:** 2

e̱ṣa va̱: stomo̍ maruto̱ nama̍svān hṛ̱dā ta̱ṣṭo mana̍sā dhāyi devāḥ |

upe̱mā yā̍ta̱ mana̍sā juṣā̱ṇā yū̱yaṃ hi ṣṭhā nama̍sa̱ id vṛ̱dhāsa̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 171 | **Verse:** 3

stu̱tāso̍ no ma̱ruto̍ mṛl̤ayantū̱ta stu̱to ma̱ghavā̱ śambha̍viṣṭhaḥ |

ū̱rdhvā na̍: santu ko̱myā vanā̱nyahā̍ni̱ viśvā̍ maruto jigī̱ṣā

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 171 | **Verse:** 4

a̱smāda̱haṃ ta̍vi̱ṣādīṣa̍māṇa̱ indrā̍d bhi̱yā ma̍ruto̱ reja̍mānaḥ |

yu̱ṣmabhya̍ṃ ha̱vyā niśi̍tānyāsa̱n tānyā̱re ca̍kṛmā mṛ̱l̤atā̍ naḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 171 | **Verse:** 5

yena̱ mānā̍saści̱taya̍nta u̱srā vyu̍ṣṭiṣu̱ śava̍sā̱ śaśva̍tīnām |

sa no̍ ma̱rudbhi̍rvṛṣabha̱ śravo̍ dhā u̱gra u̱grebhi̱: sthavi̍raḥ saho̱dāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 171 | **Verse:** 6

tvaṃ pā̍hīndra̱ sahī̍yaso̱ nṝn bhavā̍ ma̱rudbhi̱rava̍yātahel̤āḥ |

su̱pra̱ke̱tebhi̍: sāsa̱hirdadhā̍no vi̱dyāme̱ṣaṃ vṛ̱jana̍ṃ jī̱radā̍num
