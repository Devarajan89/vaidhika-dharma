# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 177

**5 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ | indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 177 | **Verse:** 1

ā ca̍rṣaṇi̱prā vṛ̍ṣa̱bho janā̍nā̱ṃ rājā̍ kṛṣṭī̱nāṃ pu̍ruhū̱ta indra̍: |
stu̱taḥ śra̍va̱syannava̱sopa̍ ma̱drigyu̱ktvā harī̱ vṛṣa̱ṇā yā̍hya̱rvāṅ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 177 | **Verse:** 2

ye te̱ vṛṣa̍ṇo vṛṣa̱bhāsa̍ indra brahma̱yujo̱ vṛṣa̍rathāso̱ atyā̍: |

tām̐ ā ti̍ṣṭha̱ tebhi̱rā yā̍hya̱rvāṅ havā̍mahe tvā su̱ta i̍ndra̱ some̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 177 | **Verse:** 3

ā ti̍ṣṭha̱ ratha̱ṃ vṛṣa̍ṇa̱ṃ vṛṣā̍ te su̱taḥ soma̱: pari̍ṣiktā̱ madhū̍ni |

yu̱ktvā vṛṣa̍bhyāṃ vṛṣabha kṣitī̱nāṃ hari̍bhyāṃ yāhi pra̱vatopa̍ ma̱drik

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 177 | **Verse:** 4

a̱yaṃ ya̱jño de̍va̱yā a̱yaṃ mi̱yedha̍ i̱mā brahmā̍ṇya̱yami̍ndra̱ soma̍: |

stī̱rṇaṃ ba̱rhirā tu śa̍kra̱ pra yā̍hi̱ pibā̍ ni̱ṣadya̱ vi mu̍cā̱ harī̍ i̱ha

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 177 | **Verse:** 5

o suṣṭu̍ta indra yāhya̱rvāṅupa̱ brahmā̍ṇi mā̱nyasya̍ kā̱roḥ |

vi̱dyāma̱ vasto̱rava̍sā gṛ̱ṇanto̍ vi̱dyāme̱ṣaṃ vṛ̱jana̍ṃ jī̱radā̍num
