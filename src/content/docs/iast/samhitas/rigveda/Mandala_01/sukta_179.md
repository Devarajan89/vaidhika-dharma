# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 179

**(6) 1-2 lopāmudrā, 3-4 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ, 5-6 agastyaśiṣyo brahmacārī| ratiḥ| triṣṭup, 5 bṛhatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 179 | **Verse:** 1

pū̱rvīra̱haṃ śa̱rada̍: śaśramā̱ṇā do̱ṣā vasto̍ru̱ṣaso̍ ja̱raya̍ntīḥ |
mi̱nāti̱ śriya̍ṃ jari̱mā ta̱nūnā̱mapyū̱ nu patnī̱rvṛṣa̍ṇo jagamyuḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 179 | **Verse:** 2

ye ci̱ddhi pūrva̍ ṛta̱sāpa̱ āsa̍n tsā̱kaṃ de̱vebhi̱rava̍dannṛ̱tāni̍ |

te ci̱davā̍surna̱hyanta̍mā̱puḥ samū̱ nu patnī̱rvṛṣa̍bhirjagamyuḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 179 | **Verse:** 3

na mṛṣā̍ śrā̱ntaṃ yadava̍nti de̱vā viśvā̱ it spṛdho̍ a̱bhya̍śnavāva |

jayā̱vedatra̍ śa̱tanī̍thamā̱jiṃ yat sa̱myañcā̍ mithu̱nāva̱bhyajā̍va

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 179 | **Verse:** 4

na̱dasya̍ mā rudha̱taḥ kāma̱ āga̍nni̱ta ājā̍to a̱muta̱: kuta̍ścit |

lopā̍mudrā̱ vṛṣa̍ṇa̱ṃ nī ri̍ṇāti̱ dhīra̱madhī̍rā dhayati śva̱santa̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 179 | **Verse:** 5

i̱maṃ nu soma̱manti̍to hṛ̱tsu pī̱tamupa̍ bruve |

yat sī̱māga̍ścakṛ̱mā tat su mṛ̍l̤atu pulu̱kāmo̱ hi martya̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 179 | **Verse:** 6

a̱gastya̱: khana̍mānaḥ kha̱nitrai̍: pra̱jāmapa̍tya̱ṃ bala̍mi̱cchamā̍naḥ |

u̱bhau varṇā̱vṛṣi̍ru̱graḥ pu̍poṣa sa̱tyā de̱veṣvā̱śiṣo̍ jagāma
