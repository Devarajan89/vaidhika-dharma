# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 93

**12 gotamo rāhūgaṇaḥ| agniṣomau| 1-3 anuṣṭup, 4-7, 12 triṣṭup,  8 jagatī triṣṭubvā, 9-11 gāyatrī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 1

agnī̍ṣomāvi̱maṃ su me̍ śṛṇu̱taṃ vṛ̍ṣaṇā̱ hava̍m |
prati̍ sū̱ktāni̍ haryata̱ṃ bhava̍taṃ dā̱śuṣe̱ maya̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 2

agnī̍ṣomā̱ yo a̱dya vā̍mi̱daṃ vaca̍: sapa̱ryati̍ |

tasmai̍ dhattaṃ su̱vīrya̱ṃ gavā̱ṃ poṣa̱ṃ svaśvya̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 3

agnī̍ṣomā̱ ya āhu̍ti̱ṃ yo vā̱ṃ dāśā̍ddha̱viṣkṛ̍tim |

sa pra̱jayā̍ su̱vīrya̱ṃ viśva̱māyu̱rvya̍śnavat

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 4

agnī̍ṣomā̱ ceti̱ tad vī̱rya̍ṃ vā̱ṃ yadamu̍ṣṇītamava̱saṃ pa̱ṇiṃ gāḥ |

avā̍tirata̱ṃ bṛsa̍yasya̱ śeṣo 'vi̍ndata̱ṃ jyoti̱reka̍ṃ ba̱hubhya̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 5

yu̱vame̱tāni̍ di̱vi ro̍ca̱nānya̱gniśca̍ soma̱ sakra̍tū adhattam |

yu̱vaṃ sindhū̍m̐ra̱bhiśa̍sterava̱dyādagnī̍ṣomā̱vamu̍ñcataṃ gṛbhī̱tān

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 6

ānyaṃ di̱vo mā̍ta̱riśvā̍ jabhā̱rāma̍thnāda̱nyaṃ pari̍ śye̱no adre̍: |

agnī̍ṣomā̱ brahma̍ṇā vāvṛdhā̱noruṃ ya̱jñāya̍ cakrathuru lo̱kam

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 7

agnī̍ṣomā ha̱viṣa̱: prasthi̍tasya vī̱taṃ harya̍taṃ vṛṣaṇā ju̱ṣethā̍m |

su̱śarmā̍ṇā̱ svava̍sā̱ hi bhū̱tamathā̍ dhatta̱ṃ yaja̍mānāya̱ śaṃ yoḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 8

yo a̱gnīṣomā̍ ha̱viṣā̍ sapa̱ryād de̍va̱drīcā̱ mana̍sā̱ yo ghṛ̱tena̍ |

tasya̍ vra̱taṃ ra̍kṣataṃ pā̱tamaṃha̍so vi̱śe janā̍ya̱ mahi̱ śarma̍ yacchatam

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 9

agnī̍ṣomā̱ save̍dasā̱ sahū̍tī vanata̱ṃ gira̍: | saṃ de̍va̱trā ba̍bhūvathuḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 10

agnī̍ṣomāva̱nena̍ vā̱ṃ yo vā̍ṃ ghṛ̱tena̱ dāśa̍ti | tasmai̍ dīdayataṃ bṛ̱hat

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 11

agnī̍ṣomāvi̱māni̍ no yu̱vaṃ ha̱vyā ju̍joṣatam | ā yā̍ta̱mupa̍ na̱: sacā̍

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 93 | **Verse:** 12

agnī̍ṣomā pipṛ̱tamarva̍to na̱ ā pyā̍yantāmu̱sriyā̍ havya̱sūda̍: |

a̱sme balā̍ni ma̱ghava̍tsu dhattaṃ kṛṇu̱taṃ no̍ adhva̱raṃ śru̍ṣṭi̱manta̍m
