# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 166

**15 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ | marutaḥ| jagatī, 14-15 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 166 | **Verse:** 1

tannu vo̍cāma rabha̱sāya̱ janma̍ne̱ pūrva̍ṃ mahi̱tvaṃ vṛ̍ṣa̱bhasya̍ ke̱tave̍ |
ai̱dheva̱ yāma̍n marutastuviṣvaṇo yu̱dheva̍ śakrāstavi̱ṣāṇi̍ kartana

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 166 | **Verse:** 2

nitya̱ṃ na sū̱nuṃ madhu̱ bibhra̍ta̱ upa̱ krīl̤a̍nti krī̱l̤ā vi̱dathe̍ṣu̱ ghṛṣva̍yaḥ |

nakṣa̍nti ru̱drā ava̍sā nama̱svina̱ṃ na ma̍rdhanti̱ svata̍vaso havi̱ṣkṛta̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 166 | **Verse:** 3

yasmā̱ ūmā̍so a̱mṛtā̱ arā̍sata rā̱yaspoṣa̍ṃ ca ha̱viṣā̍ dadā̱śuṣe̍ |

u̱kṣantya̍smai ma̱ruto̍ hi̱tā i̍va pu̱rū rajā̍ṃsi̱ paya̍sā mayo̱bhuva̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 166 | **Verse:** 4

ā ye rajā̍ṃsi̱ tavi̍ṣībhi̱ravya̍ta̱ pra va̱ evā̍sa̱: svaya̍tāso adhrajan |

bhaya̍nte̱ viśvā̱ bhuva̍nāni ha̱rmyā ci̱tro vo̱ yāma̱: praya̍tāsvṛ̱ṣṭiṣu̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 166 | **Verse:** 5

yat tve̱ṣayā̍mā na̱daya̍nta̱ parva̍tān di̱vo vā̍ pṛ̱ṣṭhaṃ naryā̱ acu̍cyavuḥ |

viśvo̍ vo̱ ajma̍n bhayate̱ vana̱spatī̍ rathī̱yantī̍va̱ pra ji̍hīta̱ oṣa̍dhiḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 166 | **Verse:** 6

yū̱yaṃ na̍ ugrā marutaḥ suce̱tunā 'ri̍ṣṭagrāmāḥ suma̱tiṃ pi̍partana |

yatrā̍ vo di̱dyud rada̍ti̱ krivi̍rdatī ri̱ṇāti̍ pa̱śvaḥ sudhi̍teva ba̱rhaṇā̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 166 | **Verse:** 7

pra ska̱mbhade̍ṣṇā anava̱bhrarā̍dhaso 'lātṛ̱ṇāso̍ vi̱dathe̍ṣu̱ suṣṭu̍tāḥ |

arca̍ntya̱rkaṃ ma̍di̱rasya̍ pī̱taye̍ vi̱durvī̱rasya̍ pratha̱māni̱ pauṃsyā̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 166 | **Verse:** 8

śa̱tabhu̍jibhi̱stama̱bhihru̍tera̱ghāt pū̱rbhī ra̍kṣatā maruto̱ yamāva̍ta |

jana̱ṃ yamu̍grāstavaso virapśinaḥ pā̱thanā̱ śaṃsā̱t tana̍yasya pu̱ṣṭiṣu̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 166 | **Verse:** 9

viśvā̍ni bha̱drā ma̍ruto̱ rathe̍ṣu vo mitha̱spṛdhye̍va tavi̱ṣāṇyāhi̍tā |

aṃse̱ṣvā va̱: prapa̍theṣu khā̱dayo 'kṣo̍ vaśca̱krā sa̱mayā̱ vi vā̍vṛte

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 166 | **Verse:** 10

bhūrī̍ṇi bha̱drā narye̍ṣu bā̱huṣu̱ vakṣa̍ssu ru̱kmā ra̍bha̱sāso̍ a̱ñjaya̍: |

aṃse̱ṣvetā̍: pa̱viṣu̍ kṣu̱rā adhi̱ vayo̱ na pa̱kṣān vyanu̱ śriyo̍ dhire

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 166 | **Verse:** 11

ma̱hānto̍ ma̱hnā vi̱bhvo̱3 vibhū̍tayo dūre̱dṛśo̱ ye di̱vyā i̍va̱ stṛbhi̍: |

ma̱ndrāḥ su̍ji̱hvāḥ svari̍tāra ā̱sabhi̱: sammi̍ślā̱ indre̍ ma̱ruta̍: pari̱ṣṭubha̍:

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 166 | **Verse:** 12

tad va̍ḥ sujātā maruto mahitva̱naṃ dī̱rghaṃ vo̍ dā̱tramadi̍teriva vra̱tam |

indra̍śca̱na tyaja̍sā̱ vi hru̍ṇāti̱ tajjanā̍ya̱ yasmai̍ su̱kṛte̱ arā̍dhvam

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 166 | **Verse:** 13

tad vo̍ jāmi̱tvaṃ ma̍ruta̱: pare̍ yu̱ge pu̱rū yacchaṃsa̍mamṛtāsa̱ āva̍ta |

a̱yā dhi̱yā mana̍ve śru̱ṣṭimāvyā̍ sā̱kaṃ naro̍ da̱ṃsanai̱rā ci̍kitrire

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 166 | **Verse:** 14

yena̍ dī̱rghaṃ ma̍rutaḥ śū̱śavā̍ma yu̱ṣmāke̍na̱ parī̍ṇasā turāsaḥ |

ā yat ta̱tana̍n vṛ̱jane̱ janā̍sa e̱bhirya̱jñebhi̱stada̱bhīṣṭi̍maśyām

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 166 | **Verse:** 15

e̱ṣa va̱: stomo̍ maruta i̱yaṃ gīrmā̍ndā̱ryasya̍ mā̱nyasya̍ kā̱roḥ |

eṣā yā̍sīṣṭa ta̱nve̍ va̱yāṃ vi̱dyāme̱ṣaṃ vṛ̱jana̍ṃ jī̱radā̍num
