# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 167

**11 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ | 1 indraḥ, 2-11marutaḥ|  triṣṭup, (10purastājjyotiḥ|)**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 167 | **Verse:** 1

sa̱hasra̍ṃ ta indro̱tayo̍ naḥ sa̱hasra̱miṣo̍ harivo gū̱rtata̍māḥ |
sa̱hasra̱ṃ rāyo̍ māda̱yadhyai̍ saha̱sriṇa̱ upa̍ no yantu̱ vājā̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 167 | **Verse:** 2

ā no'vo̍bhirma̱ruto̍ yā̱ntvacchā̱ jyeṣṭhe̍bhirvā bṛ̱had di̍vaiḥ sumā̱yāḥ |

adha̱ yade̍ṣāṃ ni̱yuta̍: para̱māḥ sa̍mu̱drasya̍ cid dha̱naya̍nta pā̱re

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 167 | **Verse:** 3

mi̱myakṣa̱ yeṣu̱ sudhi̍tā ghṛ̱tācī̱ hira̍ṇyanirṇi̱gupa̍rā̱ na ṛ̱ṣṭiḥ |

guhā̱ cara̍ntī̱ manu̍ṣo̱ na yoṣā̍ sa̱bhāva̍tī vida̱thye̍va̱ saṃ vāk

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 167 | **Verse:** 4

parā̍ śu̱bhrā a̱yāso̍ ya̱vyā sā̍dhāra̱ṇyeva̍ ma̱ruto̍ mimikṣuḥ |

na ro̍da̱sī apa̍ nudanta gho̱rā ju̱ṣanta̱ vṛdha̍ṃ sa̱khyāya̍ de̱vāḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 167 | **Verse:** 5

joṣa̱d yadī̍masu̱ryā̍ sa̱cadhyai̱ viṣi̍tastukā roda̱sī nṛ̱maṇā̍: |

ā sū̱ryeva̍ vidha̱to ratha̍ṃ gāt tve̱ṣapra̍tīkā̱ nabha̍so̱ netyā

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 167 | **Verse:** 6

āsthā̍payanta yuva̱tiṃ yuvā̍naḥ śu̱bhe nimi̍ślāṃ vi̱dathe̍ṣu pa̱jrām |

a̱rko yad vo̍ maruto ha̱viṣmā̱n gāya̍d gā̱thaṃ su̱taso̍mo duva̱syan

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 167 | **Verse:** 7

pra taṃ vi̍vakmi̱ vakmyo̱ ya e̍ṣāṃ ma̱rutā̍ṃ mahi̱mā sa̱tyo asti̍ |

sacā̱ yadī̱ṃ vṛṣa̍maṇā aha̱ṃyuḥ sthi̱rā ci̱jjanī̱rvaha̍te subhā̱gāḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 167 | **Verse:** 8

pānti̍ mi̱trāvaru̍ṇāvava̱dyāccaya̍ta īmarya̱mo apra̍śastān |

u̱ta cya̍vante̱ acyu̍tā dhru̱vāṇi̍ vāvṛ̱dha ī̍ṃ maruto̱ dāti̍vāraḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 167 | **Verse:** 9

na̱hī nu vo̍ maruto̱ antya̱sme ā̱rāttā̍cci̱cchava̍so̱ anta̍mā̱puḥ |

te dhṛ̱ṣṇunā̱ śava̍sā śūśu̱vāṃso 'rṇo̱ na dveṣo̍ dhṛṣa̱tā pari̍ ṣṭhuḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 167 | **Verse:** 10

va̱yama̱dyendra̍sya̱ preṣṭhā̍ va̱yaṃ śvo vo̍cemahi sama̱rye |

va̱yaṃ pu̱rā mahi̍ ca no̱ anu̱ dyūn tanna̍ ṛbhu̱kṣā na̱rāmanu̍ ṣyāt

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 167 | **Verse:** 11

e̱ṣa va̱: stomo̍ maruta i̱yaṃ gīrmā̍ndā̱ryasya̍ mā̱nyasya̍ kā̱roḥ |

eṣā yā̍sīṣṭa ta̱nve̍ va̱yāṃ vi̱dyāme̱ṣaṃ vṛ̱jana̍ṃ jī̱radā̍num
