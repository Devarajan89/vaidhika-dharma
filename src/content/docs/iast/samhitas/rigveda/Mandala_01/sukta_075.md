# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 75

**5 gotamo rāhūgaṇaḥ| agniḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 1

ju̱ṣasva̍ sa̱pratha̍stama̱ṃ vaco̍ de̱vapsa̍rastamam |
ha̱vyā juhvā̍na ā̱sani̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 2

athā̍ te aṅgirasta̱māgne̍ vedhastama pri̱yam |

vo̱cema̱ brahma̍ sāna̱si

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 3

kaste̍ jā̱mirjanā̍nā̱magne̱ ko dā̱śva̍dhvaraḥ |

ko ha̱ kasmi̍nnasi śri̱taḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 4

tvaṃ jā̱mirjanā̍nā̱magne̍ mi̱tro a̍si pri̱yaḥ |

sakhā̱ sakhi̍bhya̱ īḍya̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 5

yajā̍ no mi̱trāvaru̍ṇā̱ yajā̍ de̱vām̐ ṛ̱taṃ bṛ̱hat |

agne̱ yakṣi̱ svaṃ dama̍m
