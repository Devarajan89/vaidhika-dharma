# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 43

**9 kaṇvo ghauraḥ| rudraḥ, mitrāvaruṇau ca, 7-9 somaḥ| gāyatrī, 9 anuṣṭap|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 1

kad ru̱drāya̱ prace̍tase mī̱hl̤uṣṭa̍māya̱ tavya̍se | vo̱cema̱ śaṃta̍maṃ hṛ̱de

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 2

yathā̍ no̱ adi̍ti̱: kara̱t paśve̱ nṛbhyo̱ yathā̱ gave̍ | yathā̍ to̱kāya̍ ru̱driya̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 3

yathā̍ no mi̱tro varu̍ṇo̱ yathā̍ ru̱draścike̍tati | yathā̱ viśve̍ sa̱joṣa̍saḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 4

gā̱thapa̍tiṃ me̱dhapa̍tiṃ ru̱draṃ jalā̍ṣabheṣajam | taccha̱ṃyoḥ su̱mnamī̍mahe

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 5

yaḥ śu̱kra i̍va̱ sūryo̱ hira̍ṇyamiva̱ roca̍te | śreṣṭho̍ de̱vānā̱ṃ vasu̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 6

śaṃ na̍: kara̱tyarva̍te su̱gaṃ me̱ṣāya̍ me̱ṣye̍ | nṛbhyo̱ nāri̍bhyo̱ gave̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 7

a̱sme so̍ma̱ śriya̱madhi̱ ni dhe̍hi śa̱tasya̍ nṛ̱ṇām | mahi̱ śrava̍stuvinṛ̱mṇam

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 8

mā na̍: somapari̱bādho̱ mārā̍tayo juhuranta | ā na̍ indo̱ vāje̍ bhaja

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 9

yāste̍ pra̱jā a̱mṛta̍sya̱ para̍smi̱ndhāma̍nnṛ̱tasya̍ |

mū̱rdhā nābhā̍ soma vena ā̱bhūṣa̍ntīḥ soma vedaḥ
