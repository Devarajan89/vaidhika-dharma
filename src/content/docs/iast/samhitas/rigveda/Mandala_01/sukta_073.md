# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 73

**10 parāśaraḥ śāktyaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 1

ra̱yirna yaḥ pi̍tṛvi̱tto va̍yo̱dhāḥ su̱praṇī̍tiściki̱tuṣo̱ na śāsu̍: |
syo̱na̱śīrati̍thi̱rna prī̍ṇā̱no hote̍va̱ sadma̍ vidha̱to vi tā̍rīt

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 2

de̱vo na yaḥ sa̍vi̱tā sa̱tyama̍nmā̱ kratvā̍ ni̱pāti̍ vṛ̱janā̍ni̱ viśvā̍ |

pu̱ru̱pra̱śa̱sto a̱mati̱rna sa̱tya ā̱tmeva̱ śevo̍ didhi̱ṣāyyo̍ bhūt

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 3

de̱vo na yaḥ pṛ̍thi̱vīṃ vi̱śvadhā̍yā upa̱kṣeti̍ hi̱tami̍tro̱ na rājā̍ |

pu̱ra̱:sada̍: śarma̱sado̱ na vī̱rā a̍nava̱dyā pati̍juṣṭeva̱ nārī̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 4

taṃ tvā̱ naro̱ dama̱ ā nitya̍mi̱ddhamagne̱ saca̍nta kṣi̱tiṣu̍ dhru̱vāsu̍ |

adhi̍ dyu̱mnaṃ ni da̍dhu̱rbhūrya̍smi̱n bhavā̍ vi̱śvāyu̍rdha̱ruṇo̍ rayī̱ṇām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 5

vi pṛkṣo̍ agne ma̱ghavā̍no aśyu̱rvi sū̱rayo̱ dada̍to̱ viśva̱māyu̍: |

sa̱nema̱ vāja̍ṃ sami̱theṣva̱ryo bhā̱gaṃ de̱veṣu̱ śrava̍se̱ dadhā̍nāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 6

ṛ̱tasya̱ hi dhe̱navo̍ vāvaśā̱nāḥ smadū̍dhnīḥ pī̱paya̍nta̱ dyubha̍ktāḥ |

pa̱rā̱vata̍: suma̱tiṃ bhikṣa̍māṇā̱ vi sindha̍vaḥ sa̱mayā̍ sasru̱radri̍m

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 7

tve a̍gne suma̱tiṃ bhikṣa̍māṇā di̱vi śravo̍ dadhire ya̱jñiyā̍saḥ |

naktā̍ ca ca̱kruru̱ṣasā̱ virū̍pe kṛ̱ṣṇaṃ ca̱ varṇa̍maru̱ṇaṃ ca̱ saṃ dhu̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 8

yān rā̱ye martā̱ntsuṣū̍do agne̱ te syā̍ma ma̱ghavā̍no va̱yaṃ ca̍ |

chā̱yeva̱ viśva̱ṃ bhuva̍naṃ sisakṣyāpapri̱vān roda̍sī a̱ntari̍kṣam

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 9

arva̍dbhiragne̱ arva̍to̱ nṛbhi̱rnṝn vī̱rairvī̱rān va̍nuyāmā̱ tvotā̍: |

ī̱śā̱nāsa̍: pitṛvi̱ttasya̍ rā̱yo vi sū̱raya̍: śa̱tahi̍mā no aśyuḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 10

e̱tā te̍ agna u̱cathā̍ni vedho̱ juṣṭā̍ni santu̱ mana̍se hṛ̱de ca̍ |

śa̱kema̍ rā̱yaḥ su̱dhuro̱ yama̱ṃ te 'dhi̱ śravo̍ de̱vabha̍kta̱ṃ dadhā̍nāḥ
