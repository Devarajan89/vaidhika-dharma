# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 81

**9 gotamo rāhūgaṇaḥ| indraḥ| paṃktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 1

indro̱ madā̍ya vāvṛdhe̱ śava̍se vṛtra̱hā nṛbhi̍: |
taminma̱hatsvā̱jiṣū̱temarbhe̍ havāmahe̱ sa vāje̍ṣu̱ pra no̍'viṣat

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 2

asi̱ hi vī̍ra̱ senyo 'si̱ bhūri̍ parāda̱diḥ |

asi̍ da̱bhrasya̍ cid vṛ̱dho yaja̍mānāya śikṣasi sunva̱te bhūri̍ te̱ vasu̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 3

yadu̱dīra̍ta ā̱jayo̍ dhṛ̱ṣṇave̍ dhīyate̱ dhanā̍ |

yu̱kṣvā ma̍da̱cyutā̱ harī̱ kaṃ hana̱: kaṃ vasau̍ dadho̱ 'smām̐ i̍ndra̱ vasau̍ dadhaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 4

kratvā̍ ma̱hām̐ a̍nuṣva̱dhaṃ bhī̱ma ā vā̍vṛdhe̱ śava̍: |

śri̱ya ṛ̱ṣva u̍pā̱kayo̱rni śi̱prī hari̍vān dadhe̱ hasta̍yo̱rvajra̍māya̱sam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 5

ā pa̍prau̱ pārthi̍va̱ṃ rajo̍ badba̱dhe ro̍ca̱nā di̱vi |

na tvāvā̍m̐ indra̱ kaśca̱na na jā̱to na ja̍niṣya̱te 'ti̱ viśva̍ṃ vavakṣitha

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 6

yo a̱ryo ma̍rta̱bhoja̍naṃ parā̱dadā̍ti dā̱śuṣe̍ |

indro̍ a̱smabhya̍ṃ śikṣatu̱ vi bha̍jā̱ bhūri̍ te̱ vasu̍ bhakṣī̱ya tava̱ rādha̍saḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 7

made̍made̱ hi no̍ da̱diryū̱thā gavā̍mṛju̱kratu̍: |

saṃ gṛ̍bhāya pu̱rū śa̱tobha̍yāha̱styā vasu̍ śiśī̱hi rā̱ya ā bha̍ra

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 8

mā̱daya̍sva su̱te sacā̱ śava̍se śūra̱ rādha̍se |

vi̱dmā hi tvā̍ purū̱vasu̱mupa̱ kāmā̍ntsasṛ̱jmahe 'thā̍ no'vi̱tā bha̍va

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 9

e̱te ta̍ indra ja̱ntavo̱ viśva̍ṃ puṣyanti̱ vārya̍m |

a̱ntarhi khyo janā̍nāma̱ryo vedo̱ adā̍śuṣā̱ṃ teṣā̍ṃ no̱ veda̱ ā bha̍ra
