# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 144

**7 dīrghatamā aucathyaḥ| agniḥ| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 144 | **Verse:** 1

eti̱ pra hotā̍ vra̱tama̍sya mā̱yayo̱rdhvāṃ dadhā̍na̱: śuci̍peśasa̱ṃ dhiya̍m |
a̱bhi sruca̍: kramate dakṣiṇā̱vṛto̱ yā a̍sya̱ dhāma̍ pratha̱maṃ ha̱ niṃsa̍te

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 144 | **Verse:** 2

a̱bhīmṛ̱tasya̍ do̱hanā̍ anūṣata̱ yonau̍ de̱vasya̱ sada̍ne̱ parī̍vṛtāḥ |

a̱pāmu̱pasthe̱ vibhṛ̍to̱ yadāva̍sa̱dadha̍ sva̱dhā a̍dhaya̱d yābhi̱rīya̍te

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 144 | **Verse:** 3

yuyū̍ṣata̱: sava̍yasā̱ tadid vapu̍: samā̱namartha̍ṃ vi̱tari̍tratā mi̱thaḥ |

ādī̱ṃ bhago̱ na havya̱: sama̱smadā vohlu̱rna ra̱śmīn tsama̍yaṃsta̱ sāra̍thiḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 144 | **Verse:** 4

yamī̱ṃ dvā sava̍yasā sapa̱ryata̍: samā̱ne yonā̍ mithu̱nā samo̍kasā |

divā̱ na nakta̍ṃ pali̱to yuvā̍jani pu̱rū cara̍nna̱jaro̱ mānu̍ṣā yu̱gā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 144 | **Verse:** 5

tamī̍ṃ hinvanti dhī̱tayo̱ daśa̱ vriśo̍ de̱vaṃ martā̍sa ū̱taye̍ havāmahe |

dhano̱radhi̍ pra̱vata̱ ā sa ṛ̍ṇvatyabhi̱vraja̍dbhirva̱yunā̱ navā̍dhita

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 144 | **Verse:** 6

tvaṃ hya̍gne di̱vyasya̱ rāja̍si̱ tvaṃ pārthi̍vasya paśu̱pā i̍va̱ tmanā̍ |

enī̍ ta e̱te bṛ̍ha̱tī a̍bhi̱śriyā̍ hira̱ṇyayī̱ vakva̍rī ba̱rhirā̍śāte

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 144 | **Verse:** 7

agne̍ ju̱ṣasva̱ prati̍ harya̱ tad vaco̱ mandra̱ svadhā̍va̱ ṛta̍jāta̱ sukra̍to |

yo vi̱śvata̍: pra̱tyaṅṅasi̍ darśa̱to ra̱ṇvaḥ saṃdṛ̍ṣṭau pitu̱mām̐ i̍va̱ kṣaya̍:
