# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 188

**11 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ | āprīsūktaṃ-(1 idhmaḥ samiddho'gnirvā, 2 tanūnapāt 3 il̤aḥ, 4 barhiḥ, 5 devīrdvāraḥ, 6 uṣā sānaktā, 7 daivyau hotārau pracetasau, 8 tisro devyaḥ sarasvatīl̤ābhāratyaḥ, 9 tvaṣṭā, 10 vanaspatiḥ, 11 svāhākṛtayaḥ)| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 188 | **Verse:** 1

sami̍ddho a̱dya rā̍jasi de̱vo de̱vaiḥ sa̍hasrajit | dū̱to ha̱vyā ka̱virva̍ha

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 188 | **Verse:** 2

tanū̍napādṛ̱taṃ ya̱te madhvā̍ ya̱jñaḥ sama̍jyate | dadha̍t saha̱sriṇī̱riṣa̍:

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 188 | **Verse:** 3

ā̱juhvā̍no na̱ īḍyo̍ de̱vām̐ ā va̍kṣi ya̱jñiyā̍n | agne̍ sahasra̱sā a̍si

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 188 | **Verse:** 4

prā̱cīna̍ṃ ba̱rhiroja̍sā sa̱hasra̍vīramastṛṇan | yatrā̍dityā vi̱rāja̍tha

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 188 | **Verse:** 5

vi̱rāṭ sa̱mrāḍvi̱bhvīḥ pra̱bhvīrba̱hvīśca̱ bhūya̍sīśca̱ yāḥ | duro̍ ghṛ̱tānya̍kṣaran

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 188 | **Verse:** 6

su̱ru̱kme hi su̱peśa̱sā 'dhi̍ śri̱yā vi̱rāja̍taḥ | u̱ṣāsā̱veha sī̍datām

## Verse 7

## Verse 8

bhāra̱tīl̤e̱ sara̍svati̱ yā va̱: sarvā̍ upabru̱ve | tā na̍ścodayata śri̱ye

## Verse 9

## Verse 10
