# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 133

**7 parucchepo daivodāsiḥ | indraḥ|1 triṣṭup, 2-4 anuṣṭup, 5 gāyatrī, 6 dhṛtiḥ, 7 aṣṭiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 133 | **Verse:** 1

u̱bhe pu̍nāmi̱ roda̍sī ṛ̱tena̱ druho̍ dahāmi̱ saṃ ma̱hīra̍ni̱ndrāḥ |
a̱bhi̱vlagya̱ yatra̍ ha̱tā a̱mitrā̍ vailasthā̱naṃ pari̍ tṛ̱hlā aśe̍ran

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 133 | **Verse:** 2

a̱bhi̱vlagyā̍ cidadrivaḥ śī̱rṣā yā̍tu̱matī̍nām |

chi̱ndhi va̍ṭū̱riṇā̍ pa̱dā ma̱hāva̍ṭūriṇā pa̱dā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 133 | **Verse:** 3

avā̍sāṃ maghavañjahi̱ śardho̍ yātu̱matī̍nām |

vai̱la̱sthā̱na̱ke a̍rma̱ke ma̱hāvai̍lasthe arma̱ke

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 133 | **Verse:** 4

yāsā̍ṃ ti̱sraḥ pa̍ñcā̱śato̍ 'bhivla̱ṅgaira̱pāva̍paḥ |

tat su te̍ manāyati ta̱kat su te̍ manāyati

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 133 | **Verse:** 5

pi̱śaṅga̍bhṛṣṭimambhṛ̱ṇaṃ pi̱śāci̍mindra̱ saṃ mṛ̍ṇa |

sarva̱ṃ rakṣo̱ ni ba̍rhaya

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 133 | **Verse:** 6

a̱varma̱ha i̍ndra dādṛ̱hi śru̱dhī na̍: śu̱śoca̱ hi dyauḥ kṣā na bhī̱ṣām̐ a̍drivo ghṛ̱ṇānna bhī̱ṣām̐ a̍drivaḥ |

śu̱ṣminta̍mo̱ hi śu̱ṣmibhi̍rva̱dhairu̱grebhi̱rīya̍se |

apū̍ruṣaghno apratīta śūra̱ satva̍bhistrisa̱ptaiḥ śū̍ra̱ satva̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 133 | **Verse:** 7

va̱noti̱ hi su̱nvan kṣaya̱ṃ parī̍ṇasaḥ sunvā̱no hi ṣmā̱ yaja̱tyava̱ dviṣo̍ de̱vānā̱mava̱ dviṣa̍: |

su̱nvā̱na it si̍ṣāsati sa̱hasrā̍ vā̱jyavṛ̍taḥ |

su̱nvā̱nāyendro̍ dadātyā̱bhuva̍ṃ ra̱yiṃ da̍dātyā̱bhuva̍m
