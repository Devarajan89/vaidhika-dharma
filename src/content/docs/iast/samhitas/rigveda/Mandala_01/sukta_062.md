# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 62

**13 nodhā gautamaḥ| indraḥ|triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 1

pra ma̍nmahe śavasā̱nāya̍ śū̱ṣamā̍ṅgū̱ṣaṃ girva̍ṇase aṅgira̱svat |
su̱vṛ̱ktibhi̍: stuva̱ta ṛ̍gmi̱yāyā'rcā̍mā̱rkaṃ nare̱ viśru̍tāya

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 2

pra vo̍ ma̱he mahi̱ namo̍ bharadhvamāṅgū̱ṣya̍ṃ śavasā̱nāya̱ sāma̍ |

yenā̍ na̱: pūrve̍ pi̱tara̍: pada̱jñā arca̍nto̱ aṅgi̍raso̱ gā avi̍ndan

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 3

indra̱syāṅgi̍rasāṃ ce̱ṣṭau vi̱dat sa̱ramā̱ tana̍yāya dhā̱sim |

bṛha̱spati̍rbhi̱nadadri̍ṃ vi̱dad gāḥ samu̱sriyā̍bhirvāvaśanta̱ nara̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 4

sa su̱ṣṭubhā̱ sa stu̱bhā sa̱pta viprai̍: sva̱reṇādri̍ṃ sva̱ryo̱ 3 nava̍gvaiḥ |

sa̱ra̱ṇyubhi̍: phali̱gami̍ndra śakra va̱laṃ rave̍ṇa darayo̱ daśa̍gvaiḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 5

gṛ̱ṇā̱no aṅgi̍robhirdasma̱ vi va̍ru̱ṣasā̱ sūrye̍ṇa̱ gobhi̱randha̍: |

vi bhūmyā̍ aprathaya indra̱ sānu̍ di̱vo raja̱ upa̍ramastabhāyaḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 6

tadu̱ praya̍kṣatamamasya̱ karma̍ da̱smasya̱ cāru̍tamamasti̱ daṃsa̍: |

u̱pa̱hva̱re yadupa̍rā̱ api̍nva̱n madhva̍rṇaso na̱dya 1 ścata̍sraḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 7

dvi̱tā vi va̍vre sa̱najā̱ sanī̍l̤e a̱yāsya̱: stava̍mānebhira̱rkaiḥ |

bhago̱ na mene̍ para̱me vyo̍ma̱nnadhā̍raya̱d roda̍sī su̱daṃsā̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 8

sa̱nād diva̱ṃ pari̱ bhūmā̱ virū̍pe puna̱rbhuvā̍ yuva̱tī svebhi̱revai̍: |

kṛ̱ṣṇebhi̍ra̱ktoṣā ruśa̍dbhi̱rvapu̍rbhi̱rā ca̍rato a̱nyānyā̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 9

sane̍mi sa̱khyaṃ sva̍pa̱syamā̍naḥ sū̱nurdā̍dhāra̱ śava̍sā su̱daṃsā̍: |

ā̱māsu̍ cid dadhiṣe pa̱kvama̱ntaḥ paya̍: kṛ̱ṣṇāsu̱ ruśa̱d rohi̍ṇīṣu

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 10

sa̱nāt sanī̍l̤ā a̱vanī̍ravā̱tā vra̱tā ra̍kṣante a̱mṛtā̱: saho̍bhiḥ |

pu̱rū sa̱hasrā̱ jana̍yo̱ na patnī̍rduva̱syanti̱ svasā̍ro̱ ahra̍yāṇam

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 11

sa̱nā̱yuvo̱ nama̍sā̱ navyo̍ a̱rkairva̍sū̱yavo̍ ma̱tayo̍ dasma dadruḥ |

pati̱ṃ na patnī̍ruśa̱tīru̱śanta̍ṃ spṛ̱śanti̍ tvā śavasāvan manī̱ṣāḥ

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 12

sa̱nāde̱va tava̱ rāyo̱ gabha̍stau̱ na kṣīya̍nte̱ nopa̍ dasyanti dasma |

dyu̱mām̐ a̍si̱ kratu̍mām̐ indra̱ dhīra̱: śikṣā̍ śacīva̱stava̍ na̱: śacī̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 13

su̱nī̱thāya̍ naḥ śavasāna no̱dhāḥ prā̱tarma̱kṣū dhi̱yāva̍surjagamyāt
