# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 102

**11 kutsa āṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ| jagatī, 11 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 1

i̱māṃ te̱ dhiya̱ṃ pra bha̍re ma̱ho ma̱hīma̱sya sto̱tre dhi̱ṣaṇā̱ yatta̍ āna̱je |
tamu̍tsa̱ve ca̍ prasa̱ve ca̍ sāsa̱himindra̍ṃ de̱vāsa̱: śava̍sāmada̱nnanu̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 2

a̱sya śravo̍ na̱dya̍: sa̱pta bi̍bhrati̱ dyāvā̱kṣāmā̍ pṛthi̱vī da̍rśa̱taṃ vapu̍: |

a̱sme sū̍ryācandra̱masā̍bhi̱cakṣe̍ śra̱ddhe kami̍ndra carato vitartu̱ram

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 3

taṃ smā̱ ratha̍ṃ maghava̱n prāva̍ sā̱taye̱ jaitra̱ṃ yaṃ te̍ anu̱madā̍ma saṃga̱me |

ā̱jā na̍ indra̱ mana̍sā puruṣṭuta tvā̱yadbhyo̍ maghava̱ñcharma̍ yaccha naḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 4

va̱yaṃ ja̍yema̱ tvayā̍ yu̱jā vṛta̍ma̱smāka̱maṃśa̱muda̍vā̱ bhare̍bhare |

a̱smabhya̍mindra̱ vari̍vaḥ su̱gaṃ kṛ̍dhi̱ pra śatrū̍ṇāṃ maghava̱n vṛṣṇyā̍ ruja

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 5

nānā̱ hi tvā̱ hava̍mānā̱ janā̍ i̱me dhanā̍nāṃ dharta̱rava̍sā vipa̱nyava̍: |

a̱smāka̍ṃ smā̱ ratha̱mā ti̍ṣṭha sā̱taye̱ jaitra̱ṃ hī̍ndra̱ nibhṛ̍ta̱ṃ mana̱stava̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 6

go̱jitā̍ bā̱hū ami̍takratuḥ si̱maḥ karma̍nkarmañcha̱tamū̍tiḥ khajaṃka̱raḥ |

a̱ka̱lpa indra̍: prati̱māna̱moja̱sāthā̱ janā̱ vi hva̍yante siṣā̱sava̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 7

utte̍ śa̱tānma̍ghava̱nnucca̱ bhūya̍sa̱ ut sa̱hasrā̍d ririce kṛ̱ṣṭiṣu̱ śrava̍: |

a̱mā̱traṃ tvā̍ dhi̱ṣaṇā̍ titviṣe ma̱hyadhā̍ vṛ̱trāṇi̍ jighnase puraṃdara

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 8

tri̱vi̱ṣṭi̱dhātu̍ prati̱māna̱moja̍sasti̱sro bhūmī̍rnṛpate̱ trīṇi̍ roca̱nā |

atī̱daṃ viśva̱ṃ bhuva̍naṃ vavakṣithāśa̱truri̍ndra ja̱nuṣā̍ sa̱nāda̍si

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 9

tvāṃ de̱veṣu̍ pratha̱maṃ ha̍vāmahe̱ tvaṃ ba̍bhūtha̱ pṛta̍nāsu sāsa̱hiḥ |

semaṃ na̍: kā̱rumu̍pama̱nyumu̱dbhida̱mindra̍: kṛṇotu prasa̱ve ratha̍ṃ pu̱raḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 10

tvaṃ ji̍getha̱ na dhanā̍ rurodhi̱thārbhe̍ṣvā̱jā ma̍ghavan ma̱hatsu̍ ca |

tvāmu̱gramava̍se̱ saṃ śi̍śīma̱syathā̍ na indra̱ hava̍neṣu codaya

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 102 | **Verse:** 11

tanno̍ mi̱tro varu̍ṇo māmahantā̱madi̍ti̱: sindhu̍: pṛthi̱vī u̱ta dyauḥ
