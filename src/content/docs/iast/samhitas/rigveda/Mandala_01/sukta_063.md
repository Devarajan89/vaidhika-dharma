# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 63

**9 nodhā gautamaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 63 | **Verse:** 1

tvaṃ ma̱hām̐ i̍ndra̱ yo ha̱ śuṣmai̱rdyāvā̍ jajñā̱naḥ pṛ̍thi̱vī ame̍ dhāḥ |
yaddha̍ te̱ viśvā̍ gi̱raya̍ści̱dabhvā̍ bhi̱yā dṛ̱hlāsa̍: ki̱raṇā̱ naija̍n

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 63 | **Verse:** 2

ā yaddharī̍ indra̱ vivra̍tā̱ verā te̱ vajra̍ṃ jari̱tā bā̱hvordhā̍t |

yenā̍viharyatakrato a̱mitrā̱n pura̍ i̱ṣṇāsi̍ puruhūta pū̱rvīḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 63 | **Verse:** 3

tvaṃ sa̱tya i̍ndra dhṛ̱ṣṇure̱tān tvamṛ̍bhu̱kṣā narya̱stvaṃ ṣāṭ |

tvaṃ śuṣṇa̍ṃ vṛ̱jane̍ pṛ̱kṣa ā̱ṇau yūne̱ kutsā̍ya dyu̱mate̱ sacā̍han

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 63 | **Verse:** 4

tvaṃ ha̱ tyadi̍ndra codī̱: sakhā̍ vṛ̱traṃ yad va̍jrin vṛṣakarmannu̱bhnāḥ |

yaddha̍ śūra vṛṣamaṇaḥ parā̱cairvi dasyū̱m̐ryonā̱vakṛ̍to vṛthā̱ṣāṭ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 63 | **Verse:** 5

tvaṃ ha̱ tyadi̱ndrāri̍ṣaṇyan dṛ̱hlasya̍ ci̱nmartā̍nā̱maju̍ṣṭau |

vya 1 smadā kāṣṭhā̱ arva̍te vargha̱neva̍ vajriñchnathihya̱mitrā̍n

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 63 | **Verse:** 6

tvāṃ ha̱ tyadi̱ndrārṇa̍sātau̱ sva̍rmīhle̱ nara̍ ā̱jā ha̍vante |

tava̍ svadhāva i̱yamā sa̍ma̱rya ū̱tirvāje̍ṣvata̱sāyyā̍ bhūt

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 63 | **Verse:** 7

tvaṃ ha̱ tyadi̍ndra sa̱pta yudhya̱npu puro̍ vajrin puru̱kutsā̍ya dardaḥ |

ba̱rhirna yat su̱dāse̱ vṛthā̱ varga̱ṃho rā̍ja̱n vari̍vaḥ pū̱rave̍ kaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 63 | **Verse:** 8

tvaṃ tyāṃ na̍ indra deva ci̱trāmiṣa̱māpo̱ na pī̍paya̱: pari̍jman |

yayā̍ śūra̱ pratya̱smabhya̱ṃ yaṃsi̱ tmana̱mūrja̱ṃ na vi̱śvadha̱ kṣara̍dhyai

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 63 | **Verse:** 9

akā̍ri ta indra̱ gota̍mebhi̱rbrahmā̱ṇyoktā̱ nama̍sā̱ hari̍bhyām |

su̱peśa̍sa̱ṃ vāja̱mā bha̍rā naḥ prā̱tarma̱kṣū dhi̱yāva̍surjagamyāt
