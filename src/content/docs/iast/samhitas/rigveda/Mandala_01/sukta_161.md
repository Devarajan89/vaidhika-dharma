# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 161

**14 dīrghatamā aucathyaḥ| ṛbhavaḥ|jagatī, 14 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 1

kimu̱ śreṣṭha̱: kiṃ yavi̍ṣṭho na̱ āja̍ga̱n kimī̍yate dū̱tyaṃ 1 kad yadū̍ci̱ma |
na ni̍ndima cama̱saṃ yo ma̍hāku̱lo 'gne̍ bhrāta̱rdruṇa̱ id bhū̱timū̍dima

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 2

eka̍ṃ cama̱saṃ ca̱tura̍: kṛṇotana̱ tadvo̍ de̱vā a̍bruva̱n tadva̱ āga̍mam |

saudha̍nvanā̱ yadye̱vā ka̍ri̱ṣyatha̍ sā̱kaṃ de̱vairya̱jñiyā̍so bhaviṣyatha

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 3

a̱gniṃ dū̱taṃ prati̱ yadabra̍vīta̱nāśva̱: kartvo̱ ratha̍ u̱teha kartva̍: |

dhe̱nuḥ kartvā̍ yuva̱śā kartvā̱ dvā tāni̍ bhrāta̱ranu̍ vaḥ kṛ̱tvyema̍si

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 4

ca̱kṛ̱vāṃsa̍ ṛbhava̱stada̍pṛcchata̱ kveda̍bhū̱d yaḥ sya dū̱to na̱ āja̍gan |

ya̱dāvākhya̍ccama̱sāñca̱tura̍: kṛ̱tānādit tvaṣṭā̱ gnāsva̱ntarnyā̍naje

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 5

hanā̍mainā̱m̐ iti̱ tvaṣṭā̱ yadabra̍vīccama̱saṃ ye de̍va̱pāna̱mani̍ndiṣuḥ |

a̱nyā nāmā̍ni kṛṇvate su̱te sacā̍m̐ a̱nyaire̍nānka̱nyā̱ 3 nāma̍bhiḥ sparat

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 6

indro̱ harī̍ yuyu̱je a̱śvinā̱ ratha̱ṃ bṛha̱spati̍rvi̱śvarū̍pā̱mupā̍jata |

ṛ̱bhurvibhvā̱ vājo̍ de̱vām̐ a̍gacchata̱ svapa̍so ya̱jñiya̍ṃ bhā̱gamai̍tana

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 7

niścarma̍ṇo̱ gāma̍riṇīta dhī̱tibhi̱ryā jara̍ntā yuva̱śā tākṛ̍ṇotana |

saudha̍nvanā̱ aśvā̱daśva̍matakṣata yu̱ktvā ratha̱mupa̍ de̱vām̐ a̍yātana

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 8

i̱damu̍da̱kaṃ pi̍ba̱tetya̍bravītane̱daṃ vā̍ ghā pibatā muñja̱neja̍nam |

saudha̍nvanā̱ yadi̱ tanneva̱ harya̍tha tṛ̱tīye̍ ghā̱ sava̍ne mādayādhvai

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 9

āpo̱ bhūyi̍ṣṭhā̱ ityeko̍ abravīda̱gnirbhūyi̍ṣṭha̱ itya̱nyo a̍bravīt |

va̱dha̱ryantī̍ṃ ba̱hubhya̱: praiko̍ abravīdṛ̱tā vada̍ntaścama̱sām̐ a̍piṃśata

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 10

śro̱ṇāmeka̍ uda̱kaṃ gāmavā̍jati mā̱ṃsameka̍: piṃśati sū̱nayābhṛ̍tam |

ā ni̱mruca̱: śakṛ̱deko̱ apā̍bhara̱t kiṃ svi̍t pu̱trebhya̍: pi̱tarā̱ upā̍vatuḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 11

u̱dvatsva̍smā akṛṇotanā̱ tṛṇa̍ṃ ni̱vatsva̱paḥ sva̍pa̱syayā̍ naraḥ |

ago̍hyasya̱ yadasa̍stanā gṛ̱he tada̱dyedamṛ̍bhavo̱ nānu̍ gacchatha

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 12

sa̱mmīlya̱ yad bhuva̍nā pa̱ryasa̍rpata̱ kva̍ svit tā̱tyā pi̱tarā̍ va āsatuḥ |

aśa̍pata̱ yaḥ ka̱rasna̍ṃ va āda̱de yaḥ prābra̍vī̱t pro tasmā̍ abravītana

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 13

su̱ṣu̱pvāṃsa̍ ṛbhava̱stada̍pṛccha̱tāgo̍hya̱ ka i̱daṃ no̍ abūbudhat |

śvāna̍ṃ ba̱sto bo̍dhayi̱tāra̍mabravīt saṃvatsa̱ra i̱dama̱dyā vya̍khyata

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 161 | **Verse:** 14

di̱vā yā̍nti ma̱ruto̱ bhūmyā̱gnira̱yaṃ vāto̍ a̱ntari̍kṣeṇa yāti |

a̱dbhiryā̍ti̱ varu̍ṇaḥ samu̱drairyu̱ṣmām̐ i̱cchanta̍: śavaso napātaḥ
