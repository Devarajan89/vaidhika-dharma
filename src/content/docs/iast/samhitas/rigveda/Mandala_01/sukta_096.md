# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 96

**9 kutsa āṅgirasaḥ| agniḥ, draviṇodā agnirvā| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 1

sa pra̱tnathā̱ saha̍sā̱ jāya̍mānaḥ sa̱dyaḥ kāvyā̍ni̱ bal̤a̍dhatta̱ viśvā̍ |
āpa̍śca mi̱traṃ dhi̱ṣaṇā̍ ca sādhan de̱vā a̱gniṃ dhā̍rayan draviṇo̱dām

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 2

sa pūrva̍yā ni̱vidā̍ ka̱vyatā̱yori̱māḥ pra̱jā a̍janaya̱nmanū̍nām |

vi̱vasva̍tā̱ cakṣa̍sā̱ dyāma̱paśca̍ de̱vā a̱gniṃ dhā̍rayan draviṇo̱dām

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 3

tamī̍l̤ata pratha̱maṃ ya̍jña̱sādha̱ṃ viśa̱ ārī̱rāhu̍tamṛñjasā̱nam |

ū̱rjaḥ pu̱traṃ bha̍ra̱taṃ sṛ̱pradā̍nuṃ de̱vā a̱gniṃ dhā̍rayan draviṇo̱dām

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 4

sa mā̍ta̱riśvā̍ puru̱vāra̍puṣṭirvi̱dad gā̱tuṃ tana̍yāya sva̱rvit |

vi̱śāṃ go̱pā ja̍ni̱tā roda̍syorde̱vā a̱gniṃ dhā̍rayan draviṇo̱dām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 5

nakto̱ṣāsā̱ varṇa̍mā̱memyā̍ne dhā̱paye̍te̱ śiśu̱meka̍ṃ samī̱cī |

dyāvā̱kṣāmā̍ ru̱kmo a̱ntarvi bhā̍ti de̱vā a̱gniṃ dhā̍rayan draviṇo̱dām

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 6

rā̱yo bu̱dhnaḥ sa̱ṃgama̍no̱ vasū̍nāṃ ya̱jñasya̍ ke̱turma̍nma̱sādha̍no̱ veḥ |

a̱mṛ̱ta̱tvaṃ rakṣa̍māṇāsa enaṃ de̱vā a̱gniṃ dhā̍rayan draviṇo̱dām

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 7

nū ca̍ pu̱rā ca̱ sada̍naṃ rayī̱ṇāṃ jā̱tasya̍ ca̱ jāya̍mānasya ca̱ kṣām |

sa̱taśca̍ go̱pāṃ bhava̍taśca̱ bhūre̍rde̱vā a̱gniṃ dhā̍rayan draviṇo̱dām

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 8

dra̱vi̱ṇo̱dā dravi̍ṇasastu̱rasya̍ draviṇo̱dāḥ sana̍rasya̱ pra ya̍ṃsat |

dra̱vi̱ṇo̱dā vī̱rava̍tī̱miṣa̍ṃ no draviṇo̱dā rā̍sate dī̱rghamāyu̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 96 | **Verse:** 9

e̱vā no̍ agne sa̱midhā̍ vṛdhā̱no re̱vat pā̍vaka̱ śrava̍sa̱ vi bhā̍hi |

tanno̍ mi̱tro varu̍ṇo māmahantā̱madi̍ti̱: sindhu̍: pṛthi̱vī u̱ta dyauḥ
