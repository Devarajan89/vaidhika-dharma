# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 51

**15 savya āṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ| jagatī, 14-15 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 51 | **Verse:** 1

a̱bhi tyaṃ me̱ṣaṃ pu̍ruhū̱tamṛ̱gmiya̱mindra̍ṃ gī̱rbhirma̍datā̱ vasvo̍ arṇa̱vam |
yasya̱ dyāvo̱ na vi̱cara̍nti̱ mānu̍ṣā bhu̱je maṃhi̍ṣṭhama̱bhi vipra̍marcata

## Verse 2

indra̱ṃ dakṣā̍sa ṛ̱bhavo̍ mada̱cyuta̍ṃ śa̱takra̍tu̱ṃ java̍nī sū̱nṛtāru̍hat
