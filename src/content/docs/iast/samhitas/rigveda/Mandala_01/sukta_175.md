# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 175

**6 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ | indraḥ| 1 skaṃdhogrīvī bṛhatī, 2-5 anuṣṭup, 6 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 175 | **Verse:** 1

matsyapā̍yi te̱ maha̱: pātra̍syeva harivo matsa̱ro mada̍: |
vṛṣā̍ te̱ vṛṣṇa̱ indu̍rvā̱jī sa̍hasra̱sāta̍maḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 175 | **Verse:** 2

ā na̍ste gantu matsa̱ro vṛṣā̱ mado̱ vare̍ṇyaḥ | sa̱hāvā̍m̐ indra sāna̱siḥ pṛ̍tanā̱ṣāl̤ama̍rtyaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 175 | **Verse:** 3

tvaṃ hi śūra̱: sani̍tā co̱dayo̱ manu̍ṣo̱ ratha̍m | sa̱hāvā̱n dasyu̍mavra̱tamoṣa̱: pātra̱ṃ na śo̱ciṣā̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 175 | **Verse:** 4

mu̱ṣā̱ya sūrya̍ṃ kave ca̱kramīśā̍na̱ oja̍sā | vaha̱ śuṣṇā̍ya va̱dhaṃ kutsa̱ṃ vāta̱syāśvai̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 175 | **Verse:** 5

śu̱ṣminta̍mo̱ hi te̱ mado̍ dyu̱mninta̍ma u̱ta kratu̍: | vṛ̱tra̱ghnā va̍rivo̱vidā̍ maṃsī̱ṣṭhā a̍śva̱sāta̍maḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 175 | **Verse:** 6

yathā̱ pūrve̍bhyo jari̱tṛbhya̍ indra̱ maya̍ i̱vāpo̱ na tṛṣya̍te ba̱bhūtha̍ |

tāmanu̍ tvā ni̱vida̍ṃ johavīmi vi̱dyāme̱ṣaṃ vṛ̱jana̍ṃ jī̱radā̍num
