# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 18

**9 medhātithiḥkāṇvaḥ| 1-3 brahmaṇaspatiḥ, 4 indro brahmaṇaspatiḥ somaśca, 5 brahmaṇaspatiḥ  soma iṃdro,  dakṣiṇā**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 1

ca, 6-8 sadasaspatiḥ, 9 sadasaspatirnarāśaṃso vā| gāyatrī|
so̱māna̱ṃ svara̍ṇaṃ kṛṇu̱hi bra̍hmaṇaspate | ka̱kṣīva̍nta̱ṃ ya au̍śi̱jaḥ
