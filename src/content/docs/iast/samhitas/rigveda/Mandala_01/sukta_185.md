# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 185

**11 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ | dyāvāpṛthivyau| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 185 | **Verse:** 1

ka̱ta̱rā pūrvā̍ kata̱rāpa̍rā̱yoḥ ka̱thā jā̱te ka̍vaya̱: ko vi ve̍da |
viśva̱ṃ tmanā̍ bibhṛto̱ yaddha̱ nāma̱ vi va̍rtete̱ aha̍nī ca̱kriye̍va

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 185 | **Verse:** 2

bhūri̱ṃ dve aca̍rantī̱ cara̍ntaṃ pa̱dvanta̱ṃ garbha̍ma̱padī̍ dadhāte |

nitya̱ṃ na sū̱nuṃ pi̱troru̱pasthe̱ dyāvā̱ rakṣa̍taṃ pṛthivī no̱ abhvā̍t

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 185 | **Verse:** 3

a̱ne̱ho dā̱tramadi̍terana̱rvaṃ hu̱ve sva̍rvadava̱dhaṃ nama̍svat |

tad ro̍dasī janayataṃ jari̱tre dyāvā̱ rakṣa̍taṃ pṛthivī no̱ abhvā̍t

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 185 | **Verse:** 4

ata̍pyamāne̱ ava̱sāva̍ntī̱ anu̍ ṣyāma̱ roda̍sī de̱vapu̍tre |

u̱bhe de̱vānā̍mu̱bhaye̍bhi̱rahnā̱ṃ dyāvā̱ rakṣa̍taṃ pṛthivī no̱ abhvā̍t

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 185 | **Verse:** 5

sa̱ṃgaccha̍māne yuva̱tī sama̍nte̱ svasā̍rā jā̱mī pi̱troru̱pasthe̍ |

a̱bhi̱jighra̍ntī̱ bhuva̍nasya̱ nābhi̱ṃ dyāvā̱ rakṣa̍taṃ pṛthivī no̱ abhvā̍t

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 185 | **Verse:** 6

u̱rvī sadma̍nī bṛha̱tī ṛ̱tena̍ hu̱ve de̱vānā̱mava̍sā̱ jani̍trī |

da̱dhāte̱ ye a̱mṛta̍ṃ su̱pratī̍ke̱ dyāvā̱ rakṣa̍taṃ pṛthivī no̱ abhvā̍t

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 185 | **Verse:** 7

u̱rvī pṛ̱thvī ba̍hu̱le dū̱rea̍nte̱ upa̍ bruve̱ nama̍sā ya̱jñe a̱smin |

da̱dhāte̱ ye su̱bhage̍ su̱pratū̍rtī̱ dyāvā̱ rakṣa̍taṃ pṛthivī no̱ abhvā̍t

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 185 | **Verse:** 8

de̱vān vā̱ yacca̍kṛ̱mā kacci̱dāga̱: sakhā̍yaṃ vā̱ sada̱mijjāspa̍tiṃ vā |

i̱yaṃ dhīrbhū̍yā ava̱yāna̍meṣā̱ṃ dyāvā̱ rakṣa̍taṃ pṛthivī no̱ abhvā̍t

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 185 | **Verse:** 9

u̱bhā śaṃsā̱ naryā̱ māma̍viṣṭāmu̱bhe māmū̱tī ava̍sā sacetām |

bhūri̍ cida̱ryaḥ su̱dāsta̍rāye̱ṣā mada̍nta iṣayema devāḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 185 | **Verse:** 10

ṛ̱taṃ di̱ve tada̍vocaṃ pṛthi̱vyā a̍bhiśrā̱vāya̍ pratha̱maṃ su̍me̱dhāḥ |

pā̱tāma̍va̱dyād du̍ri̱tāda̱bhīke̍ pi̱tā mā̱tā ca̍ rakṣatā̱mavo̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 185 | **Verse:** 11

i̱daṃ dyā̍vāpṛthivī sa̱tyama̍stu̱ pita̱rmāta̱ryadi̱hopa̍bru̱ve vā̍m |

bhū̱taṃ de̱vānā̍mava̱me avo̍bhirvi̱dyāme̱ṣaṃ vṛ̱jana̍ṃ jī̱radā̍num
