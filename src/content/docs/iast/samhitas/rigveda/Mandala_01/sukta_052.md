# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 52

**15 savya āṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ| jagatī, 13-15 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 1

tyaṃ su me̱ṣaṃ ma̍hayā sva̱rvida̍ṃ śa̱taṃ yasya̍ su̱bhva̍: sā̱kamīra̍te |
atya̱ṃ na vāja̍ṃ havana̱syada̱ṃ ratha̱mendra̍ṃ vavṛtyā̱mava̍se suvṛ̱ktibhi̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 2

sa parva̍to̱ na dha̱ruṇe̱ṣvacyu̍taḥ sa̱hasra̍mūti̱stavi̍ṣīṣu vāvṛdhe |

indro̱ yad vṛtramava̍dhīnnadī̱vṛta̍mu̱bjannarṇā̍ṃsi̱ jarhṛ̍ṣāṇo̱ andha̍sā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 3

sa hi dva̱ro dva̱riṣu̍ va̱vra ūdha̍ni ca̱ndrabu̍dhno̱ mada̍vṛddho manī̱ṣibhi̍: |

indra̱ṃ tama̍hve svapa̱syayā̍ dhi̱yā maṃhi̍ṣṭharāti̱ṃ sa hi papri̱randha̍saḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 4

ā yaṃ pṛ̱ṇanti̍ di̱vi sadma̍barhiṣaḥ samu̱draṃ na su̱bhva1: svā a̱bhiṣṭa̍yaḥ |

taṃ vṛ̍tra̱hatye̱ anu̍ tasthurū̱taya̱: śuṣmā̱ indra̍mavā̱tā ahru̍tapsavaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 5

a̱bhi svavṛ̍ṣṭi̱ṃ made̍ asya̱ yudhya̍to ra̱ghvīri̍va prava̱ṇe sa̍srurū̱taya̍: |

indro̱ yad va̱jrī dhṛ̱ṣamā̍ṇo̱ andha̍sā bhi̱nad va̱lasya̍ pari̱dhīm̐ri̍va tri̱taḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 6

parī̍ṃ ghṛ̱ṇā ca̍rati titvi̱ṣe śavo̱ 'po vṛ̱tvī raja̍so bu̱dhnamāśa̍yat |

vṛ̱trasya̱ yat pra̍va̱ṇe du̱rgṛbhi̍śvano nija̱ghantha̱ hanvo̍rindra tanya̱tum

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 7

hra̱daṃ na hi tvā̍ nyṛ̱ṣantyū̱rmayo̱ brahmā̍ṇīndra̱ tava̱ yāni̱ vardha̍nā |

tvaṣṭā̍ citte̱ yujya̍ṃ vāvṛdhe̱ śava̍sta̱takṣa̱ vajra̍ma̱bhibhū̍tyojasam

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 8

ja̱gha̱nvām̐ u̱ hari̍bhiḥ sambhṛtakrata̱vindra̍ vṛ̱traṃ manu̍ṣe gātu̱yanna̱paḥ |

aya̍cchathā bā̱hvorvajra̍māya̱samadhā̍rayo di̱vyā sūrya̍ṃ dṛ̱śe

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 9

bṛ̱hat svaśca̍ndra̱mama̍va̱d yadu̱kthya 1 makṛ̍ṇvata bhi̱yasā̱ roha̍ṇaṃ di̱vaḥ |

yanmānu̍ṣapradhanā̱ indra̍mū̱taya̱: sva̍rnṛ̱ṣāco̍ ma̱ruto'ma̍da̱nnanu̍

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 10

dyauści̍da̱syāma̍vā̱m̐ ahe̍: sva̱nādayo̍yavīd bhi̱yasā̱ vajra̍ indra te |

vṛ̱trasya̱ yad ba̍dbadhā̱nasya̍ rodasī̱ made̍ su̱tasya̱ śava̱sābhi̍na̱cchira̍:

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 11

yadinnvi̍ndra pṛthi̱vī daśa̍bhuji̱rahā̍ni̱ viśvā̍ ta̱tana̍nta kṛ̱ṣṭaya̍: |

atrāha̍ te maghava̱n viśru̍ta̱ṃ saho̱ dyāmanu̱ śava̍sā ba̱rhaṇā̍ bhuvat

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 12

tvama̱sya pā̱re raja̍so̱ vyo̍mana̱: svabhū̍tyojā̱ ava̍se dhṛṣanmanaḥ |

ca̱kṛ̱ṣe bhūmi̍ṃ prati̱māna̱moja̍so̱ 'paḥ sva̍: pari̱bhūre̱ṣyā diva̍m

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 13

tvaṃ bhu̍vaḥ prati̱māna̍ṃ pṛthi̱vyā ṛ̱ṣvavī̍rasya bṛha̱taḥ pati̍rbhūḥ |

viśva̱māprā̍ a̱ntari̍kṣaṃ mahi̱tvā sa̱tyama̱ddhā naki̍ra̱nyastvāvā̍n

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 14

na yasya̱ dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī anu̱ vyaco̱ na sindha̍vo̱ raja̍so̱ anta̍māna̱śuḥ |

nota svavṛ̍ṣṭi̱ṃ made̍ asya̱ yudhya̍ta̱ eko̍ a̱nyacca̍kṛṣe̱ viśva̍mānu̱ṣak

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 15

ārca̱nnatra̍ ma̱ruta̱: sasmi̍nnā̱jau viśve̍ de̱vāso̍ amada̱nnanu̍ tvā |

vṛ̱trasya̱ yad bhṛ̍ṣṭi̱matā̍ va̱dhena̱ ni tvami̍ndra̱ pratyā̱naṃ ja̱ghantha̍
