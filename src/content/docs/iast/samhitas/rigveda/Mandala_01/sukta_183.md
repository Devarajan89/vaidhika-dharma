# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 183

**6 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ | aśvinau| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 183 | **Verse:** 1

taṃ yu̍ñjāthā̱ṃ mana̍so̱ yo javī̍yān trivandhu̱ro vṛ̍ṣaṇā̱ yastri̍ca̱kraḥ |
yeno̍payā̱thaḥ su̱kṛto̍ duro̱ṇaṃ tri̱dhātu̍nā patatho̱ virna pa̱rṇaiḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 183 | **Verse:** 2

su̱vṛd ratho̍ vartate̱ yanna̱bhi kṣāṃ yat tiṣṭha̍tha̱: kratu̍ma̱ntānu̍ pṛ̱kṣe |

vapu̍rvapu̱ṣyā sa̍catāmi̱yaṃ gīrdi̱vo du̍hi̱troṣasā̍ sacethe

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 183 | **Verse:** 3

ā ti̍ṣṭhataṃ su̱vṛta̱ṃ yo ratho̍ vā̱manu̍ vra̱tāni̱ varta̍te ha̱viṣmā̍n |

yena̍ narā nāsatyeṣa̱yadhyai̍ va̱rtiryā̱thastana̍yāya̱ tmane̍ ca

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 183 | **Verse:** 4

mā vā̱ṃ vṛko̱ mā vṛ̱kīrā da̍dharṣī̱nmā pari̍ varktamu̱ta māti̍ dhaktam |

a̱yaṃ vā̍ṃ bhā̱go nihi̍ta i̱yaṃ gīrdasrā̍vi̱me vā̍ṃ ni̱dhayo̱ madhū̍nām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 183 | **Verse:** 5

yu̱vāṃ gota̍maḥ purumī̱l̤aho atri̱rdasrā̱ hava̱te'va̍se ha̱viṣmā̍n |

diśa̱ṃ na di̱ṣṭāmṛ̍jū̱yeva̱ yantā me̱ hava̍ṃ nāsa̱tyopa̍ yātam

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 183 | **Verse:** 6

atā̍riṣma̱ tama̍saspā̱rama̱sya prati̍ vā̱ṃ stomo̍ aśvināvadhāyi |

eha yā̍taṃ pa̱thibhi̍rdeva̱yānai̍rvi̱dyāme̱ṣaṃ vṛ̱jana̍ṃ jī̱radā̍num
