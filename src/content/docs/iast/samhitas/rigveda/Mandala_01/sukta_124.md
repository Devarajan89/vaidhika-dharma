# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 124

**13 kakṣīvān dairghatamasa auśijaḥ| uṣāḥ | triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 1

u̱ṣā u̱cchantī̍ samidhā̱ne a̱gnā u̱dyantsūrya̍ urvi̱yā jyoti̍raśret |
de̱vo no̱ atra̍ savi̱tā nvartha̱ṃ prāsā̍vīd dvi̱pat pra catu̍ṣpadi̱tyai

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 2

ami̍natī̱ daivyā̍ni vra̱tāni̍ pramina̱tī ma̍nu̱ṣyā̍ yu̱gāni̍ |

ī̱yuṣī̍ṇāmupa̱mā śaśva̍tīnāmāyatī̱nāṃ pra̍tha̱moṣā vya̍dyaut

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 3

e̱ṣā di̱vo du̍hi̱tā pratya̍darśi̱ jyoti̱rvasā̍nā sama̱nā pu̱rastā̍t |

ṛ̱tasya̱ panthā̱manve̍ti sā̱dhu pra̍jāna̱tīva̱ na diśo̍ mināti

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 4

upo̍ adarśi śu̱ndhyuvo̱ na vakṣo̍ no̱dhā i̍vā̱vira̍kṛta pri̱yāṇi̍ |

a̱dma̱sanna sa̍sa̱to bo̱dhaya̍ntī śaśvatta̱māgā̱t puna̍re̱yuṣī̍ṇām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 5

pūrve̱ ardhe̱ raja̍so a̱ptyasya̱ gavā̱ṃ jani̍tryakṛta̱ pra ke̱tum |

vyu̍ prathate vita̱raṃ varī̍ya̱ obhā pṛ̱ṇantī̍ pi̱troru̱pasthā̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 6

e̱vede̱ṣā pu̍ru̱tamā̍ dṛ̱śe kaṃ nājā̍mi̱ṃ na pari̍ vṛṇakti jā̱mim |

a̱re̱pasā̍ ta̱nvā̱ 3 śāśa̍dānā̱ nārbhā̱dīṣa̍te̱ na ma̱ho vi̍bhā̱tī

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 7

a̱bhrā̱teva̍ pu̱ṃsa e̍ti pratī̱cī ga̍rtā̱rugi̍va sa̱naye̱ dhanā̍nām |

jā̱yeva̱ patya̍ uśa̱tī su̱vāsā̍ u̱ṣā ha̱sreva̱ ni ri̍ṇīte̱ apsa̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 8

svasā̱ svasre̱ jyāya̍syai̱ yoni̍mārai̱gapai̍tyasyāḥ prati̱cakṣye̍va |

vyu̱cchantī̍ ra̱śmibhi̱: sūrya̍syā̱ñjya̍ṅkte samana̱gā i̍va̱ vrāḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 9

ā̱sāṃ pūrvā̍sā̱maha̍su̱ svasṝ̍ṇā̱mapa̍rā̱ pūrvā̍ma̱bhye̍ti pa̱ścāt |

tāḥ pra̍tna̱vannavya̍sīrnū̱nama̱sme re̱vadu̍cchantu su̱dinā̍ u̱ṣāsa̍:

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 10

pra bo̍dhayoṣaḥ pṛṇa̱to ma̍gho̱nyabu̍dhyamānāḥ pa̱ṇaya̍: sasantu |

re̱vadu̍ccha ma̱ghava̍dbhyo maghoni re̱vat sto̱tre sū̍nṛte jā̱raya̍ntī

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 11

ave̱yama̍śvaid yuva̱tiḥ pu̱rastā̍d yu̱ṅkte gavā̍maru̱ṇānā̱manī̍kam |

vi nū̱namu̍cchā̱dasa̍ti̱ pra ke̱turgṛ̱haṃgṛ̍ha̱mupa̍ tiṣṭhāte a̱gniḥ

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 12

ut te̱ vaya̍ścid vasa̱tera̍papta̱n nara̍śca̱ ye pi̍tu̱bhājo̱ vyu̍ṣṭau |

a̱mā sa̱te va̍hasi̱ bhūri̍ vā̱mamuṣo̍ devi dā̱śuṣe̱ martyā̍ya

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 124 | **Verse:** 13

asto̍ḍhvaṃ stomyā̱ brahma̍ṇā̱ me 'vī̍vṛdhadhvamuśa̱tīru̍ṣāsaḥ |

yu̱ṣmāka̍ṃ devī̱rava̍sā sanema saha̱sriṇa̍ṃ ca śa̱tina̍ṃ ca̱ vāja̍m
