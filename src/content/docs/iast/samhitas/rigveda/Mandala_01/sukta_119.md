# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 119

**10 kakṣīvān dairghatamasa auśijaḥ| aśvinau| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 119 | **Verse:** 1

ā vā̱ṃ ratha̍ṃ purumā̱yaṃ ma̍no̱juva̍ṃ jī̱rāśva̍ṃ ya̱jñiya̍ṃ jī̱vase̍ huve |
sa̱hasra̍ketuṃ va̱nina̍ṃ śa̱tadva̍suṃ śruṣṭī̱vāna̍ṃ varivo̱dhāma̱bhi praya̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 119 | **Verse:** 2

ū̱rdhvā dhī̱tiḥ pratya̍sya̱ prayā̍ma̱nyadhā̍yi̱ śasma̱ntsama̍yanta̱ ā diśa̍: |

svadā̍mi gha̱rmaṃ prati̍ yantyū̱taya̱ ā vā̍mū̱rjānī̱ ratha̍maśvināruhat

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 119 | **Verse:** 3

saṃ yanmi̱thaḥ pa̍spṛdhā̱nāso̱ agma̍ta śu̱bhe ma̱khā ami̍tā jā̱yavo̱ raṇe̍ |

yu̱voraha̍ prava̱ṇe ce̍kite̱ ratho̱ yada̍śvinā̱ vaha̍thaḥ sū̱rimā vara̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 119 | **Verse:** 4

yu̱vaṃ bhu̱jyuṃ bhu̱ramā̍ṇa̱ṃ vibhi̍rga̱taṃ svayu̍ktibhirni̱vaha̍ntā pi̱tṛbhya̱ ā |

yā̱si̱ṣṭaṃ va̱rtirvṛ̍ṣaṇā vije̱nyaṃ1 divo̍dāsāya̱ mahi̍ ceti vā̱mava̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 119 | **Verse:** 5

yu̱vora̍śvinā̱ vapu̍ṣe yuvā̱yuja̱ṃ ratha̱ṃ vāṇī̍ yematurasya̱ śardhya̍m |

ā vā̍ṃ pati̱tvaṃ sa̱khyāya̍ ja̱gmuṣī̱ yoṣā̍vṛṇīta̱ jenyā̍ yu̱vāṃ patī̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 119 | **Verse:** 6

yu̱vaṃ re̱bhaṃ pari̍ṣūteruruṣyatho hi̱mena̍ gha̱rmaṃ pari̍tapta̱matra̍ye |

yu̱vaṃ śa̱yora̍va̱saṃ pi̍pyathu̱rgavi̱ pra dī̱rgheṇa̱ vanda̍nastā̱ryāyu̍ṣā

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 119 | **Verse:** 7

yu̱vaṃ vanda̍na̱ṃ nirṛ̍taṃ jara̱ṇyayā̱ ratha̱ṃ na da̍srā kara̱ṇā sami̍nvathaḥ |

kṣetrā̱dā vipra̍ṃ janatho vipa̱nyayā̱ pra vā̱matra̍ vidha̱te da̱ṃsanā̍ bhuvat

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 119 | **Verse:** 8

aga̍cchata̱ṃ kṛpa̍māṇaṃ parā̱vati̍ pi̱tuḥ svasya̱ tyaja̍sā̱ nibā̍dhitam |

sva̍rvatīri̱ta ū̱tīryu̱voraha̍ ci̱trā a̱bhīke̍ abhavanna̱bhiṣṭa̍yaḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 119 | **Verse:** 9

u̱ta syā vā̱ṃ madhu̍ma̱nmakṣi̍kārapa̱nmade̱ soma̍syauśi̱jo hu̍vanyati |

yu̱vaṃ da̍dhī̱co mana̱ ā vi̍vāsa̱tho 'thā̱ śira̱: prati̍ vā̱maśvya̍ṃ vadat

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 119 | **Verse:** 10

yu̱vaṃ pe̱dave̍ puru̱vāra̍maśvinā spṛ̱dhāṃ śve̱taṃ ta̍ru̱tāra̍ṃ duvasyathaḥ |

śaryai̍ra̱bhidyu̱ṃ pṛta̍nāsu du̱ṣṭara̍ṃ ca̱rkṛtya̱mindra̍miva carṣaṇī̱saha̍m
