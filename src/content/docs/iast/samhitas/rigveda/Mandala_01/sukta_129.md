# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 129

**11 parucchepo daivodāsiḥ | indraḥ, induḥ|atyaṣṭiḥ, 8-9 atiśakvaryau, 11aṣṭiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 129 | **Verse:** 1

yaṃ tvaṃ ratha̍mindra me̱dhasā̍taye 'pā̱kā santa̍miṣira pra̱ṇaya̍si̱ prāna̍vadya̱ naya̍si |
sa̱dyaści̱ttama̱bhiṣṭa̍ye̱ karo̱ vaśa̍śca vā̱jina̍m |
sāsmāka̍manavadya tūtujāna ve̱dhasā̍mi̱māṃ vāca̱ṃ na ve̱dhasā̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 129 | **Verse:** 2

sa śru̍dhi̱ yaḥ smā̱ pṛta̍nāsu̱ kāsu̍ cid da̱kṣāyya̍ indra̱ bhara̍hūtaye̱ nṛbhi̱rasi̱ pratū̍rtaye̱ nṛbhi̍: |

yaḥ śūrai̱: sva 1: sani̍tā̱ yo viprai̱rvāja̱ṃ taru̍tā |

tamī̍śā̱nāsa̍ iradhanta vā̱jina̍ṃ pṛ̱kṣamatya̱ṃ na vā̱jina̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 129 | **Verse:** 3

da̱smo hi ṣmā̱ vṛṣa̍ṇa̱ṃ pinva̍si̱ tvaca̱ṃ kaṃ ci̍d yāvīra̱raru̍ṃ śūra̱ martya̍ṃ parivṛ̱ṇakṣi̱ martya̍m |

indro̱ta tubhya̱ṃ tad di̱ve tad ru̱drāya̱ svaya̍śase |

mi̱trāya̍ voca̱ṃ varu̍ṇāya sa̱pratha̍: sumṛl̤ī̱kāya̍ sa̱pratha̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 129 | **Verse:** 4

a̱smāka̍ṃ va̱ indra̍muśmasī̱ṣṭaye̱ sakhā̍yaṃ vi̱śvāyu̍ṃ prā̱saha̱ṃ yuja̱ṃ vāje̍ṣu prā̱saha̱ṃ yuja̍m |

a̱smāka̱ṃ brahmo̱taye 'vā̍ pṛ̱tsuṣu̱ kāsu̍ cit |

na̱hi tvā̱ śatru̱: stara̍teḥ stṛ̱ṇoṣi̱ yaṃ viśva̱ṃ śatru̍ṃ: stṛ̱ṇoṣi̱ yam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 129 | **Verse:** 5

ni ṣū na̱māti̍mati̱ṃ kaya̍sya ci̱t teji̍ṣṭhābhira̱raṇi̍bhi̱rnotibhi̍ru̱grābhi̍rugro̱tibhi̍: |

neṣi̍ ṇo̱ yathā̍ pu̱rā'ne̱nāḥ śū̍ra̱ manya̍se |

viśvā̍ni pū̱rorapa̍ parṣi̱ vahni̍rā̱sā vahni̍rno̱ accha̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 129 | **Verse:** 6

pra tad vo̍ceya̱ṃ bhavyā̱yenda̍ve̱ havyo̱ na ya i̱ṣavā̱n manma̱ reja̍ti rakṣo̱hā manma̱ reja̍ti |

sva̱yaṃ so a̱smadā ni̱do va̱dhaira̍jeta durma̱tim |

ava̍ sraveda̱ghaśa̍ṃso'vata̱ramava̍ kṣu̱drami̍va sravet

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 129 | **Verse:** 7

va̱nema̱ taddhotra̍yā ci̱tantyā̍ va̱nema̍ ra̱yiṃ ra̍yivaḥ su̱vīrya̍ṃ ra̱ṇvaṃ santa̍ṃ su̱vīrya̍m |

du̱rmanmā̍naṃ su̱mantu̍bhi̱remi̱ṣā pṛ̍cīmahi |

ā sa̱tyābhi̱rindra̍ṃ dyu̱mnahū̍tibhi̱ryaja̍traṃ dyu̱mnahū̍tibhiḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 129 | **Verse:** 8

praprā̍ vo a̱sme svaya̍śobhirū̱tī pa̍riva̱rga indro̍ durmatī̱nāṃ darī̍man durmatī̱nām |

sva̱yaṃ sā ri̍ṣa̱yadhyai̱ yā na̍ upe̱ṣe a̱traiḥ |

ha̱tema̍sa̱nna va̍kṣati kṣi̱ptā jū̱rṇirna va̍kṣati

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 129 | **Verse:** 9

tvaṃ na̍ indra rā̱yā parī̍ṇasā yā̱hi pa̱thām̐ a̍ne̱hasā̍ pu̱ro yā̍hyara̱kṣasā̍ |

saca̍sva naḥ parā̱ka ā saca̍svāstamī̱ka ā |

pā̱hi no̍ dū̱rādā̱rāda̱bhiṣṭi̍bhi̱: sadā̍ pāhya̱bhiṣṭi̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 129 | **Verse:** 10

tvaṃ na̍ indra rā̱yā tarū̍ṣaso̱graṃ ci̍t tvā mahi̱mā sa̍kṣa̱dava̍se ma̱he mi̱traṃ nāva̍se |

oji̍ṣṭha̱ trāta̱ravi̍tā̱ ratha̱ṃ kaṃ ci̍damartya |

a̱nyama̱smad ri̍riṣe̱: kaṃ ci̍dadrivo̱ riri̍kṣantaṃ cidadrivaḥ

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 129 | **Verse:** 11

pā̱hi na̍ indra suṣṭuta sri̱dho̍ 'vayā̱tā sada̱mid du̍rmatī̱nāṃ de̱vaḥ sandu̍rmatī̱nām |

ha̱ntā pā̱pasya̍ ra̱kṣasa̍strā̱tā vipra̍sya̱ māva̍taḥ |

adhā̱ hi tvā̍ jani̱tā jīja̍nad vaso rakṣo̱haṇa̍ṃ tvā̱ jīja̍nad vaso
