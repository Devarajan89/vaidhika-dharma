# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 85

**12 gotamo rāhūgaṇaḥ| marutaḥ| jagatī, 5, 12 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 1

pra ye śumbha̍nte̱ jana̍yo̱ na sapta̍yo̱ yāma̍n ru̱drasya̍ sū̱nava̍: su̱daṃsa̍saḥ |
roda̍sī̱ hi ma̱ruta̍ścakri̱re vṛ̱dhe mada̍nti vī̱rā vi̱dathe̍ṣu̱ ghṛṣva̍yaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 2

ta u̍kṣi̱tāso̍ mahi̱māna̍māśata di̱vi ru̱drāso̱ adhi̍ cakrire̱ sada̍: |

arca̍nto a̱rkaṃ ja̱naya̍nta indri̱yamadhi̱ śriyo̍ dadhire̱ pṛśni̍mātaraḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 3

gomā̍taro̱ yacchu̱bhaya̍nte a̱ñjibhi̍sta̱nūṣu̍ śu̱bhrā da̍dhire vi̱rukma̍taḥ |

bādha̍nte̱ viśva̍mabhimā̱tina̱mapa̱ vartmā̍nyeṣā̱manu̍ rīyate ghṛ̱tam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 4

vi ye bhrāja̍nte̱ suma̍khāsa ṛ̱ṣṭibhi̍: pracyā̱vaya̍nto̱ acyu̍tā ci̱doja̍sā |

ma̱no̱juvo̱ yanma̍ruto̱ rathe̱ṣvā vṛṣa̍vrātāsa̱: pṛṣa̍tī̱rayu̍gdhvam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 5

pra yad rathe̍ṣu̱ pṛṣa̍tī̱rayu̍gdhva̱ṃ vāje̱ adri̍ṃ maruto ra̱ṃhaya̍ntaḥ |

u̱tāru̱ṣasya̱ vi ṣya̍nti̱ dhārā̱ścarme̍vo̱dabhi̱rvyu̍ndanti̱ bhūma̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 6

ā vo̍ vahantu̱ sapta̍yo raghu̱ṣyado̍ raghu̱patvā̍na̱: pra ji̍gāta bā̱hubhi̍: |

sīda̱tā ba̱rhiru̱ru va̱: sada̍skṛ̱taṃ mā̱daya̍dhvaṃ maruto̱ madhvo̱ andha̍saḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 7

te̍'vardhanta̱ svata̍vaso mahitva̱nā nāka̍ṃ ta̱sthuru̱ru ca̍krire̱ sada̍: |

viṣṇu̱ryaddhāva̱dvṛṣa̍ṇaṃ mada̱cyuta̱ṃ vayo̱ na sī̍da̱nnadhi̍ ba̱rhiṣi̍ pri̱ye

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 8

śūrā̍ i̱vedyu yuyu̍dhayo̱ na jagma̍yaḥ śrava̱syavo̱ na pṛta̍nāsu yetire |

bhaya̍nte̱ viśvā̱ bhuva̍nā ma̱rudbhyo̱ rājā̍na iva tve̱ṣasa̍ṃdṛśo̱ nara̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 9

tvaṣṭā̱ yad vajra̱ṃ sukṛ̍taṃ hira̱ṇyaya̍ṃ sa̱hasra̍bhṛṣṭi̱ṃ svapā̱ ava̍rtayat |

dha̱tta indro̱ naryapā̍ṃsi̱ karta̱ve 'ha̍n vṛ̱traṃ nira̱pāmau̍bjadarṇa̱vam

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 10

ū̱rdhvaṃ nu̍nudre'va̱taṃ ta oja̍sā dādṛhā̱ṇaṃ ci̍d bibhidu̱rvi parva̍tam |

dhama̍nto vā̱ṇaṃ ma̱ruta̍: su̱dāna̍vo̱ made̱ soma̍sya̱ raṇyā̍ni cakrire

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 11

ji̱hmaṃ nu̍nudre'va̱taṃ tayā̍ di̱śāsi̍ñca̱nnutsa̱ṃ gota̍māya tṛ̱ṣṇaje̍ |

ā ga̍cchantī̱mava̍sā ci̱trabhā̍nava̱: kāma̱ṃ vipra̍sya tarpayanta̱ dhāma̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 12

yā va̱: śarma̍ śaśamā̱nāya̱ santi̍ tri̱dhātū̍ni dā̱śuṣe̍ yaccha̱tādhi̍ |

a̱smabhya̱ṃ tāni̍ maruto̱ vi ya̍nta ra̱yiṃ no̍ dhatta vṛṣaṇaḥ su̱vīra̍m
