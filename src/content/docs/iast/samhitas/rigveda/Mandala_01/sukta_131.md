# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 131

**7 parucchepo daivodāsiḥ | indraḥ| atyaṣṭiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 131 | **Verse:** 1

indrā̍ya̱ hi dyaurasu̍ro̱ ana̍mna̱tendrā̍ya ma̱hī pṛ̍thi̱vī varī̍mabhirdyu̱mnasā̍tā̱ varī̍mabhiḥ |
indra̱ṃ viśve̍ sa̱joṣa̍so de̱vāso̍ dadhire pu̱raḥ |
indrā̍ya̱ viśvā̱ sava̍nāni̱ mānu̍ṣā rā̱tāni̍ santu̱ mānu̍ṣā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 131 | **Verse:** 2

viśve̍ṣu̱ hi tvā̱ sava̍neṣu tu̱ñjate̍ samā̱nameka̱ṃ vṛṣa̍maṇyava̱: pṛtha̱k sva̍: sani̱ṣyava̱: pṛtha̍k |

taṃ tvā̱ nāva̱ṃ na pa̱rṣaṇi̍ṃ śū̱ṣasya̍ dhu̱ri dhī̍mahi |

indra̱ṃ na ya̱jñaiści̱taya̍nta ā̱yava̱: stome̍bhi̱rindra̍mā̱yava̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 131 | **Verse:** 3

vi tvā̍ tatasre mithu̱nā a̍va̱syavo̍ vra̱jasya̍ sā̱tā gavya̍sya ni̱:sṛja̱: sakṣa̍nta indra ni̱:sṛja̍: |

yad ga̱vyantā̱ dvā janā̱ sva 1ryantā̍ sa̱mūha̍si |

ā̱viṣkari̍kra̱d vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ sacā̱bhuva̱ṃ vajra̍mindra sacā̱bhuva̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 131 | **Verse:** 4

vi̱duṣṭe̍ a̱sya vī̱rya̍sya pū̱rava̱: puro̱ yadi̍ndra̱ śāra̍dīra̱vāti̍raḥ sāsahā̱no a̱vāti̍raḥ |

śāsa̱stami̍ndra̱ martya̱maya̍jyuṃ śavasaspate |

ma̱hīma̍muṣṇāḥ pṛthi̱vīmi̱mā a̱po ma̍ndasā̱na i̱mā a̱paḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 131 | **Verse:** 5

ādit te̱ a̱sya vī̱rya̍sya carkira̱n made̍ṣu vṛṣannu̱śijo̱ yadāvi̍tha sakhīya̱to yadāvi̍tha |

ca̱kartha̍ kā̱rame̍bhya̱: pṛta̍nāsu̱ prava̍ntave |

te a̱nyāma̍nyāṃ na̱dya̍ṃ saniṣṇata śrava̱syanta̍: saniṣṇata

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 131 | **Verse:** 6

u̱to no̍ a̱syā u̱ṣaso̍ ju̱ṣeta̱ hya 1rkasya̍ bodhi ha̱viṣo̱ havī̍mabhi̱: sva̍rṣātā̱ havī̍mabhiḥ |

yadi̍ndra̱ hanta̍ve̱ mṛdho̱ vṛṣā̍ vajri̱ñcike̍tasi |

ā me̍ a̱sya ve̱dhaso̱ navī̍yaso̱ manma̍ śrudhi̱ navī̍yasaḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 131 | **Verse:** 7

tvaṃ tami̍ndra vāvṛdhā̱no a̍sma̱yura̍mitra̱yanta̍ṃ tuvijāta̱ martya̱ṃ vajre̍ṇa śūra̱ martya̍m |

ja̱hi yo no̍ aghā̱yati̍ śṛṇu̱ṣva su̱śrava̍stamaḥ |

ri̱ṣṭaṃ na yāma̱nnapa̍ bhūtu durma̱tirviśvāpa̍ bhūtu durma̱tiḥ
