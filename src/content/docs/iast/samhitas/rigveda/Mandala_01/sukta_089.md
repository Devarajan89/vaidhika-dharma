# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 89

**10 gotamo rāhūgaṇaḥ| viśvedevāḥ, (1-2, 8-9 devāḥ, 10 aditiḥ)| jagatī, 6 virāṭ-sthānā, 8-10 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 1

ā no̍ bha̱drāḥ krata̍vo yantu vi̱śvato 'da̍bdhāso̱ apa̍rītāsa u̱dbhida̍: |
de̱vā no̱ yathā̱ sada̱mid vṛ̱dhe asa̱nnaprā̍yuvo rakṣi̱tāro̍ di̱vedi̍ve

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 2

de̱vānā̍ṃ bha̱drā su̍ma̱tirṛ̍jūya̱tāṃ de̱vānā̍ṃ rā̱tira̱bhi no̱ ni va̍rtatām |

de̱vānā̍ṃ sa̱khyamupa̍ sedimā va̱yaṃ de̱vā na̱ āyu̱: pra ti̍rantu jī̱vase̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 3

tān pūrva̍yā ni̱vidā̍ hūmahe va̱yaṃ bhaga̍ṃ mi̱tramadi̍ti̱ṃ dakṣa̍ma̱sridha̍m |

a̱rya̱maṇa̱ṃ varu̍ṇa̱ṃ soma̍ma̱śvinā̱ sara̍svatī naḥ su̱bhagā̱ maya̍skarat

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 4

tanno̱ vāto̍ mayo̱bhu vā̍tu bheṣa̱jaṃ tanmā̱tā pṛ̍thi̱vī tat pi̱tā dyauḥ |

tad grāvā̍ṇaḥ soma̱suto̍ mayo̱bhuva̱stada̍śvinā śṛṇutaṃ dhiṣṇyā yu̱vam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 5

tamīśā̍na̱ṃ jaga̍tasta̱sthuṣa̱spati̍ṃ dhiyaṃji̱nvamava̍se hūmahe va̱yam |

pū̱ṣā no̱ yathā̱ veda̍sā̱masa̍d vṛ̱dhe ra̍kṣi̱tā pā̱yurada̍bdhaḥ sva̱staye̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 6

sva̱sti na̱ indro̍ vṛ̱ddhaśra̍vāḥ sva̱sti na̍: pū̱ṣā vi̱śvave̍dāḥ |

sva̱sti na̱stārkṣyo̱ ari̍ṣṭanemiḥ sva̱sti no̱ bṛha̱spati̍rdadhātu

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 7

pṛṣa̍daśvā ma̱ruta̱: pṛśni̍mātaraḥ śubha̱ṃyāvā̍no vi̱dathe̍ṣu̱ jagma̍yaḥ |

a̱gni̱ji̱hvā mana̍va̱: sūra̍cakṣaso̱ viśve̍ no de̱vā ava̱sā ga̍manni̱ha

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 8

bha̱draṃ karṇe̍bhiḥ śṛṇuyāma devā bha̱draṃ pa̍śyemā̱kṣabhi̍ryajatrāḥ |

sthi̱rairaṅgai̍stuṣṭu̱vāṃsa̍sta̱nūbhi̱rvya̍śema de̱vahi̍ta̱ṃ yadāyu̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 9

śa̱taminnu śa̱rado̱ anti̍ devā̱ yatrā̍ naśca̱krā ja̱rasa̍ṃ ta̱nūnā̍m |

pu̱trāso̱ yatra̍ pi̱taro̱ bhava̍nti̱ mā no̍ ma̱dhyā rī̍riṣa̱tāyu̱rganto̍:

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 89 | **Verse:** 10

adi̍ti̱rdyauradi̍tira̱ntari̍kṣa̱madi̍tirmā̱tā sa pi̱tā sa pu̱traḥ |

viśve̍ de̱vā adi̍ti̱: pañca̱ janā̱ adi̍tirjā̱tamadi̍ti̱rjani̍tvam
