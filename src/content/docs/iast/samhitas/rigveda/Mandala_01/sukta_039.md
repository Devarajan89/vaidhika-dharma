# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 39

**10 kaṇvau ghauraḥ| marutaḥ| pragāthaḥ – viṣamā bṛhatyaḥ, samāḥ satobṛhatyaḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 1

pra yadi̱tthā pa̍rā̱vata̍: śo̱cirna māna̱masya̍tha |
kasya̱ kratvā̍ maruta̱: kasya̱ varpa̍sā̱ kaṃ yā̍tha̱ kaṃ ha̍ dhūtayaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 2

sthi̱rā va̍: sa̱ntvāyu̍dhā parā̱ṇude̍ vī̱l̤ū u̱ta pra̍ti̱ṣkabhe̍ |

yu̱ṣmāka̍mastu̱ tavi̍ṣī̱ panī̍yasī̱ mā martya̍sya mā̱yina̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 3

parā̍ ha̱ yat sthi̱raṃ ha̱tha naro̍ va̱rtaya̍thā gu̱ru |

vi yā̍thana va̱nina̍: pṛthi̱vyā vyāśā̱: parva̍tānām

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 4

na̱hi va̱: śatru̍rvivi̱de adhi̱ dyavi̱ na bhūmyā̍ṃ riśādasaḥ |

yu̱ṣmāka̍mastu̱ tavi̍ṣī̱ tanā̍ yu̱jā rudrā̍so̱ nū ci̍dā̱dhṛṣe̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 5

pra ve̍payanti̱ parva̍tā̱nvi vi̍ñcanti̱ vana̱spatī̍n |

pro ā̍rata maruto du̱rmadā̍ iva̱ devā̍sa̱: sarva̍yā vi̱śā

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 6

upo̱ rathe̍ṣu̱ pṛṣa̍tīrayugdhva̱ṃ praṣṭi̍rvahati̱ rohi̍taḥ |

ā vo̱ yāmā̍ya pṛthi̱vī ci̍daśro̱dabī̍bhayanta̱ mānu̍ṣāḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 7

ā vo̍ ma̱kṣū tanā̍ya̱ kaṃ rudrā̱ avo̍ vṛṇīmahe |

gantā̍ nū̱naṃ no'va̍sā̱ yathā̍ pu̱retthā kaṇvā̍ya bi̱bhyuṣe̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 8

yu̱ṣmeṣi̍to maruto̱ martye̍ṣita̱ ā yo no̱ abhva̱ īṣa̍te |

vi taṃ yu̍yota̱ śava̍sā̱ vyoja̍sā̱ vi yu̱ṣmākā̍bhirū̱tibhi̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 9

asā̍mi̱ hi pra̍yajyava̱: kaṇva̍ṃ da̱da pra̍cetasaḥ |

asā̍mibhirmaruta̱ ā na̍ ū̱tibhi̱rgantā̍ vṛ̱ṣṭiṃ na vi̱dyuta̍:

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 10

asā̱myojo̍ bibhṛthā sudāna̱vo 'sā̍mi dhūtaya̱: śava̍: |

ṛ̱ṣi̱dviṣe̍ marutaḥ parima̱nyava̱ iṣu̱ṃ na sṛ̍jata̱ dviṣa̍m
