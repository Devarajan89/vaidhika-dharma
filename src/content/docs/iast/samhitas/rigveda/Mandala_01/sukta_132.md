# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 132

**6 parucchepo daivodāsiḥ | indraḥ, 6 (1ardharcasya) indrāparvatau| atyaṣṭiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 132 | **Verse:** 1

tvayā̍ va̱yaṃ ma̍ghava̱n pūrvye̱ dhana̱ indra̍tvotāḥ sāsahyāma pṛtanya̱to va̍nu̱yāma̍ vanuṣya̱taḥ |
nedi̍ṣṭhe a̱sminnaha̱nyadhi̍ vocā̱ nu su̍nva̱te |
a̱smin ya̱jñe vi ca̍yemā̱ bhare̍ kṛ̱taṃ vā̍ja̱yanto̱ bhare̍ kṛ̱tam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 132 | **Verse:** 2

sva̱rje̱ṣe bhara̍ ā̱prasya̱ vakma̍nyuṣa̱rbudha̱: svasmi̱nnañja̍si krā̱ṇasya̱ svasmi̱nnañja̍si |

aha̱nnindro̱ yathā̍ vi̱de śī̱rṣṇāśī̍rṣṇopa̱vācya̍: |

a̱sma̱trā te̍ sa̱dhrya̍k santu rā̱tayo̍ bha̱drā bha̱drasya̍ rā̱taya̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 132 | **Verse:** 3

tat tu praya̍: pra̱tnathā̍ te śuśukva̱naṃ yasmi̍n ya̱jñe vāra̱makṛ̍ṇvata̱ kṣaya̍mṛ̱tasya̱ vāra̍si̱ kṣaya̍m |

vi tad vo̍ce̱radha̍ dvi̱tā'ntaḥ pa̍śyanti ra̱śmibhi̍: |

sa ghā̍ vide̱ anvindro̍ ga̱veṣa̍ṇo bandhu̱kṣidbhyo̍ ga̱veṣa̍ṇaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 132 | **Verse:** 4

nū i̱tthā te̍ pū̱rvathā̍ ca pra̱vācya̱ṃ yadaṅgi̍ro̱bhyo'vṛ̍ṇo̱rapa̍ vra̱jamindra̱ śikṣa̱nnapa̍ vra̱jam |

aibhya̍: samā̱nyā di̱śā 'smabhya̍ṃ jeṣi̱ yotsi̍ ca |

su̱nvadbhyo̍ randhayā̱ kaṃ ci̍davra̱taṃ hṛ̍ṇā̱yanta̍ṃ cidavra̱tam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 132 | **Verse:** 5

saṃ yajjanā̱n kratu̍bhi̱: śūra̍ ī̱kṣaya̱ddhane̍ hi̱te ta̍ruṣanta śrava̱syava̱: pra ya̍kṣanta śrava̱syava̍: |

tasmā̱ āyu̍: pra̱jāva̱did bādhe̍ arca̱ntyoja̍sā |

indra̍ o̱kya̍ṃ didhiṣanta dhī̱tayo̍ de̱vām̐ acchā̱ na dhī̱taya̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 132 | **Verse:** 6

yu̱vaṃ tami̍ndrāparvatā puro̱yudhā̱ yo na̍: pṛta̱nyādapa̱ taṃta̱middha̍ta̱ṃ vajre̍ṇa̱ taṃta̱middha̍tam |

dū̱re ca̱ttāya̍ cchantsa̱d gaha̍na̱ṃ yadina̍kṣat |

a̱smāka̱ṃ śatrū̱n pari̍ śūra vi̱śvato̍ da̱rmā da̍rṣīṣṭa vi̱śvata̍:
