# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 74

**9 gotamo rāhūgaṇaḥ| agniḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 1

u̱pa̱pra̱yanto̍ adhva̱raṃ mantra̍ṃ vocemā̱gnaye̍ | ā̱re a̱sme ca̍ śṛṇva̱te

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 2

yaḥ snīhi̍tīṣu pū̱rvyaḥ sa̍ṃjagmā̱nāsu̍ kṛ̱ṣṭiṣu̍ | ara̍kṣad dā̱śuṣe̱ gaya̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 3

u̱ta bru̍vantu ja̱ntava̱ uda̱gnirvṛ̍tra̱hāja̍ni | dha̱na̱ṃja̱yo raṇe̍raṇe

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 4

yasya̍ dū̱to asi̱ kṣaye̱ veṣi̍ ha̱vyāni̍ vī̱taye̍ | da̱smat kṛ̱ṇoṣya̍dhva̱ram

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 5

tamit su̍ha̱vyama̍ṅgiraḥ sude̱vaṃ sa̍haso yaho | janā̍ āhuḥ suba̱rhiṣa̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 6

ā ca̱ vahā̍si̱ tām̐ i̱ha de̱vām̐ upa̱ praśa̍staye | ha̱vyā su̍ścandra vī̱taye̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 7

na yoru̍pa̱bdiraśvya̍: śṛ̱ṇve ratha̍sya̱ kacca̱na | yada̍gne̱ yāsi̍ dū̱tya̍m

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 8

tvoto̍ vā̱jyahra̍yo̱ 'bhi pūrva̍smā̱dapa̍raḥ | pra dā̱śvām̐ a̍gne asthāt

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 74 | **Verse:** 9

u̱ta dyu̱mat su̱vīrya̍ṃ bṛ̱hada̍gne vivāsasi | de̱vebhyo̍ deva dā̱śuṣe̍
