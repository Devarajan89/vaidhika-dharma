# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 77

**5 gotamo rāhūgaṇaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 77 | **Verse:** 1

ka̱thā dā̍śemā̱gnaye̱ kāsmai̍ de̱vaju̍ṣṭocyate bhā̱mine̱ gīḥ |
yo martye̍ṣva̱mṛta̍ ṛ̱tāvā̱ hotā̱ yaji̍ṣṭha̱ it kṛ̱ṇoti̍ de̱vān

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 77 | **Verse:** 2

yo a̍dhva̱reṣu̱ śaṃta̍ma ṛ̱tāvā̱ hotā̱ tamū̱ namo̍bhi̱rā kṛ̍ṇudhvam |

a̱gniryad vermartā̍ya de̱vāntsa cā̱ bodhā̍ti̱ mana̍sā yajāti

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 77 | **Verse:** 3

sa hi kratu̱: sa marya̱: sa sā̱dhurmi̱tro na bhū̱dadbhu̍tasya ra̱thīḥ |

taṃ medhe̍ṣu pratha̱maṃ de̍va̱yantī̱rviśa̱ upa̍ bruvate da̱smamārī̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 77 | **Verse:** 4

sa no̍ nṛ̱ṇāṃ nṛta̍mo ri̱śādā̍ a̱gnirgiro'va̍sā vetu dhī̱tim |

tanā̍ ca̱ ye ma̱ghavā̍na̱: śavi̍ṣṭhā̱ vāja̍prasūtā i̱ṣaya̍nta̱ manma̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 77 | **Verse:** 5

e̱vāgnirgota̍mebhirṛ̱tāvā̱ vipre̍bhirastoṣṭa jā̱tave̍dāḥ |

sa e̍ṣu dyu̱mnaṃ pī̍paya̱t sa vāja̱ṃ sa pu̱ṣṭiṃ yā̍ti̱ joṣa̱mā ci̍ki̱tvān
