# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 45

**10 praskaṇvaḥ kāṇvaḥ| agniḥ, 10 (uttarārdhasya) devāḥ| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 1

tvama̍gne̱ vasū̍m̐ri̱ha ru̱drām̐ ā̍di̱tyām̐ u̱ta | yajā̍ svadhva̱raṃ jana̱ṃ manu̍jātaṃ ghṛta̱pruṣa̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 2

śru̱ṣṭī̱vāno̱ hi dā̱śuṣe̍ de̱vā a̍gne̱ vice̍tasaḥ | tān ro̍hidaśva girvaṇa̱s traya̍striṃśata̱mā va̍ha

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 3

pri̱ya̱me̱dha̱vada̍tri̱vaj jāta̍vedo virūpa̱vat | a̱ṅgi̱ra̱svanma̍hivrata̱ praska̍ṇvasya śrudhī̱ hava̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 4

mahi̍kerava ū̱taye̍ pri̱yame̍dhā ahūṣata | rāja̍ntamadhva̱rāṇā̍ma̱gniṃ śu̱kreṇa̍ śo̱ciṣā̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 5

ghṛtā̍havana santye̱mā u̱ ṣu śru̍dhī̱ gira̍: | yābhi̱: kaṇva̍sya sū̱navo̱ hava̱nte'va̍se tvā

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 6

tvāṃ ci̍traśravastama̱ hava̍nte vi̱kṣu ja̱ntava̍: | śo̱ciṣke̍śaṃ purupri̱yāgne̍ ha̱vyāya̱ vohl̤a̍ve

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 7

ni tvā̱ hotā̍ramṛ̱tvija̍ṃ dadhi̱re va̍su̱vitta̍mam | śrutka̍rṇaṃ sa̱pratha̍stama̱ṃ viprā̍ agne̱ divi̍ṣṭiṣu

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 8

ā tvā̱ viprā̍ acucyavuḥ su̱taso̍mā a̱bhi praya̍: | bṛ̱had bhā bibhra̍to ha̱viragne̱ martā̍ya dā̱śuṣe̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 9

prā̱ta̱ryāvṇa̍: sahaskṛta soma̱peyā̍ya santya | i̱hādya daivya̱ṃ jana̍ṃ ba̱rhirā sā̍dayā vaso

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 10

a̱rvāñca̱ṃ daivya̱ṃ jana̱magne̱ yakṣva̱ sahū̍tibhiḥ | a̱yaṃ soma̍: sudānava̱s taṃ pā̍ta ti̱roa̍hnyam
