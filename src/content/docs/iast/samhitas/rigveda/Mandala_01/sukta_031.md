# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 31

**18 hiraṇyastūpa āṅgirasaḥ| agniḥ| jagatī, 8, 16, 18 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 1

tvama̍gne pratha̱mo aṅgi̍rā̱ ṛṣi̍rde̱vo de̱vānā̍mabhavaḥ śi̱vaḥ sakhā̍
|tava̍ vra̱te ka̱vayo̍ vidma̱nāpa̱so'jā̍yanta ma̱ruto̱ bhrāja̍dṛṣṭayaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 2

tvama̍gne pratha̱mo aṅgi̍rastamaḥ ka̱virde̱vānā̱ṃ pari̍ bhūṣasi vra̱tam |

vi̱bhurviśva̍smai̱ bhuva̍nāya̱ medhi̍ro dvimā̱tā śa̱yuḥ ka̍ti̱dhā ci̍dā̱yave̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 3

tvama̍gne pratha̱mo mā̍ta̱riśva̍na ā̱virbha̍va sukratū̱yā vi̱vasva̍te |

are̍jetā̱ṃ roda̍sī hotṛ̱vūrye 'sa̍ghnorbhā̱ramaya̍jo ma̱ho va̍so

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 4

tvama̍gne̱ mana̍ve̱ dyāma̍vāśayaḥ purū̱rava̍se su̱kṛte̍ su̱kṛtta̍raḥ |

śvā̱treṇa̱ yat pi̱trormucya̍se̱ paryā tvā̱ pūrva̍manaya̱nnāpa̍ra̱ṃ puna̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 5

tvama̍gne vṛṣa̱bhaḥ pu̍ṣṭi̱vardha̍na̱ udya̍tasruce bhavasi śra̱vāyya̍: |

ya āhu̍ti̱ṃ pari̱ vedā̱ vaṣa̍ṭkṛti̱mekā̍yu̱ragre̱ viśa̍ ā̱vivā̍sasi

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 6

tvama̍gne vṛji̱nava̍rtani̱ṃ nara̱ṃ sakma̍n piparṣi vi̱dathe̍ vicarṣaṇe |

yaḥ śūra̍sātā̱ pari̍takmye̱ dhane̍ da̱bhrebhi̍ści̱t samṛ̍tā̱ haṃsi̱ bhūya̍saḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 7

tvaṃ tama̍gne amṛta̱tva u̍tta̱me marta̍ṃ dadhāsi̱ śrava̍se di̱vedi̍ve |

yastā̍tṛṣā̱ṇa u̱bhayā̍ya̱ janma̍ne̱ maya̍: kṛ̱ṇoṣi̱ praya̱ ā ca̍ sū̱raye̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 8

tvaṃ no̍ agne sa̱naye̱ dhanā̍nāṃ ya̱śasa̍ṃ kā̱ruṃ kṛ̍ṇuhi̱ stavā̍naḥ |

ṛ̱dhyāma̱ karmā̱pasā̱ nave̍na de̱vairdyā̍vāpṛthivī̱ prāva̍taṃ naḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 9

tvaṃ no̍ agne pi̱troru̱pastha̱ ā de̱vo de̱veṣva̍navadya̱ jāgṛ̍viḥ |

ta̱nū̱kṛd bo̍dhi̱ prama̍tiśca kā̱rave̱ tvaṃ ka̍lyāṇa̱ vasu̱ viśva̱mopi̍ṣe

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 10

tvama̍gne̱ prama̍ti̱stvaṃ pi̱tāsi̍ na̱stvaṃ va̍ya̱skṛt tava̍ jā̱mayo̍ va̱yam |

saṃ tvā̱ rāya̍: śa̱tina̱: saṃ sa̍ha̱sriṇa̍: su̱vīra̍ṃ yanti vrata̱pāma̍dābhya

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 11

tvāma̍gne pratha̱mamā̱yumā̱yave̍ de̱vā a̍kṛṇva̱n nahu̍ṣasya vi̱śpati̍m |

il̤ā̍makṛṇva̱n manu̍ṣasya̱ śāsa̍nīṃ pi̱turyat pu̱tro mama̍kasya̱ jāya̍te

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 12

tvaṃ no̍ agne̱ tava̍ deva pā̱yubhi̍rma̱ghono̍ rakṣa ta̱nva̍śca vandya |

trā̱tā to̱kasya̱ tana̍ye̱ gavā̍ma̱syani̍meṣa̱ṃ rakṣa̍māṇa̱stava̍ vra̱te

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 13

tvama̍gne̱ yajya̍ve pā̱yuranta̍ro 'niṣa̱ṅgāya̍ catura̱kṣa i̍dhyase |

yo rā̱taha̍vyo 'vṛ̱kāya̱ dhāya̍se kī̱reści̱n mantra̱ṃ mana̍sā va̱noṣi̱ tam

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 14

tvama̍gna uru̱śaṃsā̍ya vā̱ghate̍ spā̱rhaṃ yad rekṇa̍: para̱maṃ va̱noṣi̱ tat |

ā̱dhrasya̍ ci̱t prama̍tirucyase pi̱tā pra pāka̱ṃ śāssi̱ pra diśo̍ vi̱duṣṭa̍raḥ

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 15

tvama̍gne̱ praya̍tadakṣiṇa̱ṃ nara̱ṃ varme̍va syū̱taṃ pari̍ pāsi vi̱śvata̍: |

svā̱du̱kṣadmā̱ yo va̍sa̱tau syo̍na̱kṛj jī̍vayā̱jaṃ yaja̍te̱ sopa̱mā di̱vaḥ

---

## Verse 16

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 16

i̱māma̍gne śa̱raṇi̍ṃ mīmṛṣo na i̱mamadhvā̍na̱ṃ yamagā̍ma dū̱rāt |

ā̱piḥ pi̱tā prama̍tiḥ so̱myānā̱ṃ bhṛmi̍rasyṛṣi̱kṛn martyā̍nām

---

## Verse 17

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 17

ma̱nu̱ṣvada̍gne aṅgira̱svada̍ṅgiro yayāti̱vat sada̍ne pūrva̱vacchu̍ce |

accha̍ yā̱hyā va̍hā̱ daivya̱ṃ jana̱mā sā̍daya ba̱rhiṣi̱ yakṣi̍ ca pri̱yam

---

## Verse 18

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 18

e̱tenā̍gne̱ brahma̍ṇā vāvṛdhasva̱ śaktī̍ vā̱ yatte̍ cakṛ̱mā vi̱dā vā̍ |

u̱ta pra ṇe̍ṣya̱bhi vasyo̍ a̱smān tsaṃ na̍: sṛja suma̱tyā vāja̍vatyā
