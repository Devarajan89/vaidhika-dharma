# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 101

**11 kutsa āṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ (1 garbhasrāvi ṇyupaniṣad)| jagatī, 8-11 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 1

pra ma̱ndine̍ pitu̱mada̍rcatā̱ vaco̱ yaḥ kṛ̱ṣṇaga̍rbhā ni̱raha̍nnṛ̱jiśva̍nā |
a̱va̱syavo̱ vṛṣa̍ṇa̱ṃ vajra̍dakṣiṇaṃ ma̱rutva̍ntaṃ sa̱khyāya̍ havāmahe

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 2

yo vya̍ṃsaṃ jāhṛṣā̱ṇena̍ ma̱nyunā̱ yaḥ śamba̍ra̱ṃ yo aha̱n pipru̍mavra̱tam |

indro̱ yaḥ śuṣṇa̍ma̱śuṣa̱ṃ nyāvṛ̍ṇaṅ ma̱rutva̍ntaṃ sa̱khyāya̍ havāmahe

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 3

yasya̱ dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī pauṃsya̍ṃ ma̱had yasya̍ vra̱te varu̍ṇo̱ yasya̱ sūrya̍: |

yasyendra̍sya̱ sindha̍va̱: saśca̍ti vra̱taṃ ma̱rutva̍ntaṃ sa̱khyāya̍ havāmahe

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 4

yo aśvā̍nā̱ṃ yo gavā̱ṃ gopa̍tirva̱śī ya ā̍ri̱taḥ karma̍ṇikarmaṇi sthi̱raḥ |

vī̱l̤ości̱dindro̱ yo asu̍nvato va̱dho ma̱rutva̍ntaṃ sa̱khyāya̍ havāmahe

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 5

yo viśva̍sya̱ jaga̍taḥ prāṇa̱taspati̱ryo bra̱hmaṇe̍ pratha̱mo gā avi̍ndat |

indro̱ yo dasyū̱m̐radha̍rām̐ a̱vāti̍ran ma̱rutva̍ntaṃ sa̱khyāya̍ havāmahe

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 6

yaḥ śūre̍bhi̱rhavyo̱ yaśca̍ bhī̱rubhi̱ryo dhāva̍dbhirhū̱yate̱ yaśca̍ ji̱gyubhi̍: |

indra̱ṃ yaṃ viśvā̱ bhuva̍nā̱bhi sa̍ṃda̱dhurma̱rutva̍ntaṃ sa̱khyāya̍ havāmahe

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 7

ru̱drāṇā̍meti pra̱diśā̍ vicakṣa̱ṇo ru̱drebhi̱ryoṣā̍ tanute pṛ̱thu jraya̍: |

indra̍ṃ manī̱ṣā a̱bhya̍rcati śru̱taṃ ma̱rutva̍ntaṃ sa̱khyāya̍ havāmahe

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 8

yadvā̍ marutvaḥ para̱me sa̱dhasthe̱ yad vā̍va̱me vṛ̱jane̍ mā̱dayā̍se |

ata̱ ā yā̍hyadhva̱raṃ no̱ acchā̍ tvā̱yā ha̱viśca̍kṛmā satyarādhaḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 9

tvā̱yendra̱ soma̍ṃ suṣumā sudakṣa tvā̱yā ha̱viśca̍kṛmā brahmavāhaḥ |

adhā̍ niyutva̱: saga̍ṇo ma̱rudbhi̍ra̱smin ya̱jñe ba̱rhiṣi̍ mādayasva

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 10

mā̱daya̍sva̱ hari̍bhi̱rye ta̍ indra̱ vi ṣya̍sva̱ śipre̱ vi sṛ̍jasva̱ dhene̍ |

ā tvā̍ suśipra̱ hara̍yo vahantū̱śan ha̱vyāni̱ prati̍ no juṣasva

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 101 | **Verse:** 11

ma̱rutsto̍trasya vṛ̱jana̍sya go̱pā va̱yamindre̍ṇa sanuyāma̱ vāja̍m |

tanno̍ mi̱tro varu̍ṇo māmahantā̱madi̍ti̱: sindhu̍: pṛthi̱vī u̱ta dyauḥ
