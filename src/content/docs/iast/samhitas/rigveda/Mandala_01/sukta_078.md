# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 78

**5 gotamo rāhūgaṇaḥ| agniḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 78 | **Verse:** 1

a̱bhi tvā̱ gota̍mā gi̱rā jāta̍vedo̱ vica̍rṣaṇe |
dyu̱mnaira̱bhi pra ṇo̍numaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 78 | **Verse:** 2

tamu̍ tvā̱ gota̍mo gi̱rā rā̱yaskā̍mo duvasyati |

dyu̱mnaira̱bhi pra ṇo̍numaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 78 | **Verse:** 3

tamu̍ tvā vāja̱sāta̍mamaṅgira̱svaddha̍vāmahe |

dyu̱mnaira̱bhi pra ṇo̍numaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 78 | **Verse:** 4

tamu̍ tvā vṛtra̱hanta̍ma̱ṃ yo dasyū̍m̐ravadhūnu̱ṣe |

dyu̱mnaira̱bhi pra ṇo̍numaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 78 | **Verse:** 5

avo̍cāma̱ rahū̍gaṇā a̱gnaye̱ madhu̍ma̱d vaca̍: |

dyu̱mnaira̱bhi pra ṇo̍numaḥ
