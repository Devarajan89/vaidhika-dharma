# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 44

**14 praskaṇvaḥ kāṇvaḥ| agniḥ, 1-2 agniḥ, aśvinau, uṣāśca| pragāthaḥ – viṣamā bṛhatyaḥ, samāḥ satobṛhatyaḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 1

agne̱ viva̍svadu̱ṣasa̍ś ci̱traṃ rādho̍ amartya |
ā dā̱śuṣe̍ jātavedo vahā̱ tvama̱dyā de̱vām̐ u̍ṣa̱rbudha̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 2

sa̱jūra̱śvibhyā̍mu̱ṣasā̍ su̱vīrya̍ma̱sme dhe̍hi̱ śravo̍ bṛ̱hat

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 3

dhū̱make̍tu̱ṃ bhāṛ̍jīka̱ṃ vyu̍ṣṭiṣu ya̱jñānā̍madhvara̱śriya̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 4

śreṣṭha̱ṃ yavi̍ṣṭha̱mati̍thi̱ṃ svā̍huta̱ṃ juṣṭa̱ṃ janā̍ya dā̱śuṣe̍ |

de̱vām̐ acchā̱ yāta̍ve jā̱tave̍dasama̱gnimī̍l̤e̱ vyu̍ṣṭiṣu

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 5

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 6

praska̍ṇvasya prati̱rannāyu̍rjī̱vase̍ nama̱syā daivya̱ṃ jana̍m

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 7

sa ā va̍ha puruhūta̱ prace̍ta̱so 'gne̍ de̱vām̐ i̱ha dra̱vat

## Verse 8
