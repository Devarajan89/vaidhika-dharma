# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 111

**5 kutsa āṅgirasaḥ| ṛbhavaḥ|jagatī, 5 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 111 | **Verse:** 1

takṣa̱n ratha̍ṃ su̱vṛta̍ṃ vidma̱nāpa̍sa̱stakṣa̱n harī̍ indra̱vāhā̱ vṛṣa̍ṇvasū |
takṣa̍n pi̱tṛbhyā̍mṛ̱bhavo̱ yuva̱d vaya̱stakṣa̍n va̱tsāya̍ mā̱tara̍ṃ sacā̱bhuva̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 111 | **Verse:** 2

ā no̍ ya̱jñāya̍ takṣata ṛbhu̱madvaya̱: kratve̱ dakṣā̍ya supra̱jāva̍tī̱miṣa̍m |

yathā̱ kṣayā̍ma̱ sarva̍vīrayā vi̱śā tanna̱: śardhā̍ya dhāsathā̱ svi̍ndri̱yam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 111 | **Verse:** 3

ā ta̍kṣata sā̱tima̱smabhya̍mṛbhavaḥ sā̱tiṃ rathā̍ya sā̱timarva̍te naraḥ |

sā̱tiṃ no̱ jaitrī̱ṃ saṃ ma̍heta vi̱śvahā̍ jā̱mimajā̍mi̱ṃ pṛta̍nāsu sa̱kṣaṇi̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 111 | **Verse:** 4

ṛ̱bhu̱kṣaṇa̱mindra̱mā hu̍va ū̱taya̍ ṛ̱bhūn vājā̍n ma̱ruta̱: soma̍pītaye |

u̱bhā mi̱trāvaru̍ṇā nū̱nama̱śvinā̱ te no̍ hinvantu sā̱taye̍ dhi̱ye ji̱ṣe

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 111 | **Verse:** 5

ṛ̱bhurbharā̍ya̱ saṃ śi̍śātu sā̱tiṃ sa̍marya̱jidvājo̍ a̱smām̐ a̍viṣṭu |

tanno̍ mi̱tro varu̍ṇo māmahantā̱madi̍ti̱: sindhu̍: pṛthi̱vī u̱ta dyauḥ
