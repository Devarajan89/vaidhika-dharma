# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 86

**10 gotamo rāhūgaṇaḥ| marutaḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 1

maru̍to̱ yasya̱ hi kṣaye̍ pā̱thā di̱vo vi̍mahasaḥ | sa su̍go̱pāta̍mo̱ jana̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 2

ya̱jñairvā̍ yajñavāhaso̱ vipra̍sya vā matī̱nām | maru̍taḥ śṛṇu̱tā hava̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 3

u̱ta vā̱ yasya̍ vā̱jino 'nu̱ vipra̱mata̍kṣata | sa gantā̱ goma̍ti vra̱je

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 4

a̱sya vī̱rasya̍ ba̱rhiṣi̍ su̱taḥ somo̱ divi̍ṣṭiṣu | u̱kthaṃ mada̍śca śasyate

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 5

a̱sya śro̍ṣa̱ntvā bhuvo̱ viśvā̱ yaśca̍rṣa̱ṇīra̱bhi | sūra̍ṃ cit sa̱sruṣī̱riṣa̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 6

pū̱rvībhi̱rhi da̍dāśi̱ma śa̱radbhi̍rmaruto va̱yam | avo̍bhiścarṣaṇī̱nām

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 7

su̱bhaga̱: sa pra̍yajyavo̱ maru̍to astu̱ martya̍: | yasya̱ prayā̍ṃsi̱ parṣa̍tha

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 8

śa̱śa̱mā̱nasya̍ vā nara̱: sveda̍sya satyaśavasaḥ | vi̱dā kāma̍sya̱ vena̍taḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 9

yū̱yaṃ tat sa̍tyaśavasa ā̱viṣka̍rta mahitva̱nā | vidhya̍tā vi̱dyutā̱ rakṣa̍:

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 86 | **Verse:** 10

gūha̍tā̱ guhya̱ṃ tamo̱ vi yā̍ta̱ viśva̍ma̱triṇa̍m | jyoti̍ṣkartā̱ yadu̱śmasi̍
