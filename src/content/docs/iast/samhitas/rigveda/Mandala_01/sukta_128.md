# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 128

**8 parucchepo daivodāsiḥ | agniḥ| atyaṣṭiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 128 | **Verse:** 1

a̱yaṃ jā̍yata̱ manu̍ṣo̱ dharī̍maṇi̱ hotā̱ yaji̍ṣṭha u̱śijā̱manu̍ vra̱tama̱gniḥ svamanu̍ vra̱tam |
vi̱śvaśru̍ṣṭiḥ sakhīya̱te ra̱yiri̍va śravasya̱te |
ada̍bdho̱ hotā̱ ni ṣa̍dadi̱l̤aspa̱de pari̍vīta i̱l̤aspa̱de

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 128 | **Verse:** 2

taṃ ya̍jña̱sādha̱mapi̍ vātayāmasyṛ̱tasya̍ pa̱thā nama̍sā ha̱viṣma̍tā de̱vatā̍tā ha̱viṣma̍tā |

sa na̍ ū̱rjāmu̱pābhṛ̍tya̱yā kṛ̱pā na jū̍ryati |

yaṃ mā̍ta̱riśvā̱ mana̍ve parā̱vato̍ de̱vaṃ bhāḥ pa̍rā̱vata̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 128 | **Verse:** 3

eve̍na sa̱dyaḥ parye̍ti̱ pārthi̍vaṃ muhu̱rgī reto̍ vṛṣa̱bhaḥ kani̍krada̱d dadha̱d reta̱: kani̍kradat |

śa̱taṃ cakṣā̍ṇo a̱kṣabhi̍rde̱vo vane̍ṣu tu̱rvaṇi̍: |

sado̱ dadhā̍na̱ upa̍reṣu̱ sānu̍ṣva̱gniḥ pare̍ṣu̱ sānu̍ṣu

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 128 | **Verse:** 4

sa su̱kratu̍: pu̱rohi̍to̱ dame̍dame̱ 'gnirya̱jñasyā̍dhva̱rasya̍ cetati̱ kratvā̍ ya̱jñasya̍ cetati |

kratvā̍ ve̱dhā i̍ṣūya̱te viśvā̍ jā̱tāni̍ paspaśe |

yato̍ ghṛta̱śrīrati̍thi̱rajā̍yata̱ vahni̍rve̱dhā ajā̍yata

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 128 | **Verse:** 5

kratvā̱ yada̍sya̱ tavi̍ṣīṣu pṛ̱ñcate̱ 'gnerave̍ṇa ma̱rutā̱ṃ na bho̱jye̍ṣi̱rāya̱ na bho̱jyā̍ |

sa hi ṣmā̱ dāna̱minva̍ti̱ vasū̍nāṃ ca ma̱jmanā̍ |

sa na̍strāsate duri̱tāda̍bhi̱hruta̱: śaṃsā̍da̱ghāda̍bhi̱hruta̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 128 | **Verse:** 6

viśvo̱ vihā̍yā ara̱tirvasu̍rdadhe̱ haste̱ dakṣi̍ṇe ta̱raṇi̱rna śi̍śrathacchrava̱syayā̱ na śi̍śrathat |

viśva̍smā̱ idi̍ṣudhya̱te de̍va̱trā ha̱vyamohi̍ṣe |

viśva̍smā̱ it su̱kṛte̱ vāra̍mṛṇvatya̱gnirdvārā̱ vyṛ̍ṇvati

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 128 | **Verse:** 7

sa mānu̍ṣe vṛ̱jane̱ śaṃta̍mo hi̱to̱ 3 'gnirya̱jñeṣu̱ jenyo̱ na vi̱śpati̍: pri̱yo ya̱jñeṣu̍ vi̱śpati̍: |

sa ha̱vyā mānu̍ṣāṇāmi̱l̤ā kṛ̱tāni̍ patyate |

sa na̍strāsate̱ varu̍ṇasya dhū̱rterma̱ho de̱vasya̍ dhū̱rteḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 128 | **Verse:** 8

a̱gniṃ hotā̍ramīl̤ate̱ vasu̍dhitiṃ pri̱yaṃ ceti̍ṣṭhamara̱tiṃ nye̍rire havya̱vāha̱ṃ nye̍rire |

vi̱śvāyu̍ṃ vi̱śvave̍dasa̱ṃ hotā̍raṃ yaja̱taṃ ka̱vim |

de̱vāso̍ ra̱ṇvamava̍se vasū̱yavo̍ gī̱rbhī ra̱ṇvaṃ va̍sū̱yava̍:
