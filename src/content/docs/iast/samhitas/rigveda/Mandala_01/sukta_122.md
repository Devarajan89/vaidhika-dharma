# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 122

**15 kakṣīvān dairghatamasa auśijaḥ| viśve devāḥ | triṣṭup,- 5-6 virāḍ rūpā|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 1

pra va̱: pānta̍ṃ raghumanya̱vo'ndho̍ ya̱jñaṃ ru̱drāya̍ mī̱hluṣe̍ bharadhvam |
di̱vo a̍sto̱ṣyasu̍rasya vī̱rairi̍ṣu̱dhyeva̍ ma̱ruto̱ roda̍syoḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 2

patnī̍va pū̱rvahū̍tiṃ vāvṛ̱dhadhyā̍ u̱ṣāsā̱naktā̍ puru̱dhā vidā̍ne |

sta̱rīrnātka̱ṃ vyu̍ta̱ṃ vasā̍nā̱ sūrya̍sya śri̱yā su̱dṛśī̱ hira̍ṇyaiḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 3

ma̱mattu̍ na̱: pari̍jmā vasa̱rhā ma̱mattu̱ vāto̍ a̱pāṃ vṛṣa̍ṇvān |

śi̱śī̱tami̍ndrāparvatā yu̱vaṃ na̱stanno̱ viśve̍ varivasyantu de̱vāḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 4

u̱ta tyā me̍ ya̱śasā̍ śveta̱nāyai̱ vyantā̱ pāntau̍śi̱jo hu̱vadhyai̍ |

pra vo̱ napā̍tama̱pāṃ kṛ̍ṇudhva̱ṃ pra mā̱tarā̍ rāspi̱nasyā̱yoḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 5

ā vo̍ ruva̱ṇyumau̍śi̱jo hu̱vadhyai̱ ghoṣe̍va̱ śaṃsa̱marju̍nasya̱ naṃśe̍ |

pra va̍: pū̱ṣṇe dā̱vana̱ ām̐ acchā̍ voceya va̱sutā̍tima̱gneḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 6

śru̱taṃ me̍ mitrāvaruṇā̱ have̱mota śru̍ta̱ṃ sada̍ne vi̱śvata̍: sīm |

śrotu̍ na̱: śrotu̍rātiḥ su̱śrotu̍: su̱kṣetrā̱ sindhu̍ra̱dbhiḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 7

stu̱ṣe sā vā̍ṃ varuṇa mitra rā̱tirgavā̍ṃ śa̱tā pṛ̱kṣayā̍meṣu pa̱jre |

śru̱tara̍the pri̱yara̍the̱ dadhā̍nāḥ sa̱dyaḥ pu̱ṣṭiṃ ni̍rundhā̱nāso̍ agman

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 8

a̱sya stu̍ṣe̱ mahi̍maghasya̱ rādha̱: sacā̍ sanema̱ nahu̍ṣaḥ su̱vīrā̍: |

jano̱ yaḥ pa̱jrebhyo̍ vā̱jinī̍vā̱naśvā̍vato ra̱thino̱ mahya̍ṃ sū̱riḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 9

jano̱ yo mi̍trāvaruṇāvabhi̱dhruga̱po na vā̍ṃ su̱notya̍kṣṇayā̱dhruk |

sva̱yaṃ sa yakṣma̱ṃ hṛda̍ye̱ ni dha̍tta̱ āpa̱ yadī̱ṃ hotrā̍bhirṛ̱tāvā̍

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 10

sa vrādha̍to̱ nahu̍ṣo̱ daṃsu̍jūta̱: śardha̍staro na̱rāṃ gū̱rtaśra̍vāḥ |

visṛ̍ṣṭarātiryāti bāhla̱sṛtvā̱ viśvā̍su pṛ̱tsu sada̱micchūra̍:

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 11

adha̱ gmantā̱ nahu̍ṣo̱ hava̍ṃ sū̱reḥ śrotā̍ rājāno a̱mṛta̍sya mandrāḥ |

na̱bho̱juvo̱ yanni̍ra̱vasya̱ rādha̱: praśa̍staye mahi̱nā ratha̍vate

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 12

e̱taṃ śardha̍ṃ dhāma̱ yasya̍ sū̱reritya̍voca̱n daśa̍tayasya̱ naṃśe̍ |

dyu̱mnāni̱ yeṣu̍ va̱sutā̍tī rā̱ran viśve̍ sanvantu prabhṛ̱theṣu̱ vāja̍m

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 13

mandā̍mahe̱ daśa̍tayasya dhā̱serdviryat pañca̱ bibhra̍to̱ yantyannā̍ |

kimi̱ṣṭāśva̍ i̱ṣṭara̍śmire̱ta ī̍śā̱nāsa̱staru̍ṣa ṛñjate̱ nṝn

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 14

hira̍ṇyakarṇaṃ maṇigrīva̱marṇa̱stanno̱ viśve̍ varivasyantu de̱vāḥ |

a̱ryo gira̍: sa̱dya ā ja̱gmuṣī̱rosrāścā̍kantū̱bhaye̍ṣva̱sme

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 122 | **Verse:** 15

ca̱tvāro̍ mā maśa̱rśāra̍sya̱ śiśva̱strayo̱ rājña̱ āya̍vasasya ji̱ṣṇoḥ |

ratho̍ vāṃ mitrāvaruṇā dī̱rghāpsā̱: syūma̍gabhasti̱: sūro̱ nādyau̍t
