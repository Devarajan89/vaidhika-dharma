# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 49

**4 praskaṇvaḥ kāṇvaḥ| uṣāḥ| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 1

uṣo̍ bha̱drebhi̱rā ga̍hi di̱vaści̍d roca̱nādadhi̍ |
vaha̍ntvaru̱ṇapsa̍va̱ upa̍ tvā so̱mino̍ gṛ̱ham

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 2

su̱peśa̍saṃ su̱khaṃ ratha̱ṃ yama̱dhyasthā̍ uṣa̱stvam |

tenā̍ su̱śrava̍sa̱ṃ jana̱ṃ prāvā̱dya du̍hitardivaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 3

vaya̍ścit te pata̱triṇo̍ dvi̱paccatu̍ṣpadarjuni |

uṣa̱: prāra̍nnṛ̱tūm̐ranu̍ di̱vo ante̍bhya̱spari̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 49 | **Verse:** 4

vyu̱cchantī̱ hi ra̱śmibhi̱rviśva̍mā̱bhāsi̍ roca̱nam |

tāṃ tvāmu̍ṣarvasū̱yavo̍ gī̱rbhiḥ kaṇvā̍ ahūṣata
