# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 148

**5 dīrghatamā aucathyaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 148 | **Verse:** 1

mathī̱d yadī̍ṃ vi̱ṣṭo mā̍ta̱riśvā̱ hotā̍raṃ vi̱śvāpsu̍ṃ vi̱śvade̍vyam |
ni yaṃ da̱dhurma̍nu̱ṣyā̍su vi̱kṣu sva 1 rṇa ci̱traṃ vapu̍ṣe vi̱bhāva̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 148 | **Verse:** 2

da̱dā̱naminna da̍dabhanta̱ manmā̱gnirvarū̍tha̱ṃ mama̱ tasya̍ cākan |

ju̱ṣanta̱ viśvā̍nyasya̱ karmopa̍stuti̱ṃ bhara̍māṇasya kā̱roḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 148 | **Verse:** 3

nitye̍ ci̱nnu yaṃ sada̍ne jagṛ̱bhre praśa̍stibhirdadhi̱re ya̱jñiyā̍saḥ |

pra sū na̍yanta gṛ̱bhaya̍nta i̱ṣṭāvaśvā̍so̱ na ra̱thyo̍ rārahā̱ṇāḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 148 | **Verse:** 4

pu̱rūṇi̍ da̱smo ni ri̍ṇāti̱ jambhai̱rād ro̍cate̱ vana̱ ā vi̱bhāvā̍ |

āda̍sya̱ vāto̱ anu̍ vāti śo̱cirastu̱rna śaryā̍masa̱nāmanu̱ dyūn

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 148 | **Verse:** 5

na yaṃ ri̱pavo̱ na ri̍ṣa̱ṇyavo̱ garbhe̱ santa̍ṃ reṣa̱ṇā re̱ṣaya̍nti |

a̱ndhā a̍pa̱śyā na da̍bhannabhi̱khyā nityā̍sa īṃ pre̱tāro̍ arakṣan
