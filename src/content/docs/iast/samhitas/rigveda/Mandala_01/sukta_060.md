# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 60

**5 nodhā gautamaḥ| agniḥ|triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 1

vahni̍ṃ ya̱śasa̍ṃ vi̱datha̍sya ke̱tuṃ su̍prā̱vya̍ṃ dū̱taṃ sa̱dyoa̍rtham |
dvi̱janmā̍naṃ ra̱yimi̍va praśa̱staṃ rā̱tiṃ bha̍ra̱d bhṛga̍ve māta̱riśvā̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 2

a̱sya śāsu̍ru̱bhayā̍saḥ sacante ha̱viṣma̍nta u̱śijo̱ ye ca̱ martā̍: |

di̱vaści̱t pūrvo̱ nya̍sādi̱ hotā̱ ''pṛcchyo̍ vi̱śpati̍rvi̱kṣu ve̱dhāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 3

taṃ navya̍sī hṛ̱da ā jāya̍mānama̱smat su̍kī̱rtirmadhu̍jihvamaśyāḥ |

yamṛ̱tvijo̍ vṛ̱jane̱ mānu̍ṣāsa̱: praya̍svanta ā̱yavo̱ jīja̍nanta

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 4

u̱śik pā̍va̱ko vasu̱rmānu̍ṣeṣu̱ vare̍ṇyo̱ hotā̍dhāyi vi̱kṣu |

damū̍nā gṛ̱hapa̍ti̱rdama̱ ām̐ a̱gnirbhu̍vad rayi̱patī̍ rayī̱ṇām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 5

taṃ tvā̍ va̱yaṃ pati̍magne rayī̱ṇāṃ pra śa̍ṃsāmo ma̱tibhi̱rgota̍māsaḥ |

ā̱śuṃ na vā̍jambha̱raṃ ma̱rjaya̍ntaḥ prā̱tarma̱kṣū dhi̱yāva̍surjagamyāt
