# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 110

**9 kutsa āṅgirasaḥ| ṛbhavaḥ| jagatī, 5, 9 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 1

ta̱taṃ me̱ apa̱stadu̍ tāyate̱ puna̱: svādi̍ṣṭhā dhī̱tiru̱cathā̍ya śasyate |
a̱yaṃ sa̍mu̱dra i̱ha vi̱śvade̍vya̱: svāhā̍kṛtasya̱ samu̍ tṛpṇuta ṛbhavaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 2

ā̱bho̱gaya̱ṃ pra yadi̱cchanta̱ aita̱nāpā̍kā̱: prāñco̱ mama̱ ke ci̍dā̱paya̍: |

saudha̍nvanāsaścari̱tasya̍ bhū̱manāga̍cchata savi̱turdā̱śuṣo̍ gṛ̱ham

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 3

tat sa̍vi̱tā vo̍'mṛta̱tvamāsu̍va̱dago̍hya̱ṃ yacchra̱vaya̍nta̱ aita̍na |

tyaṃ ci̍ccama̱samasu̍rasya̱ bhakṣa̍ṇa̱meka̱ṃ santa̍makṛṇutā̱ catu̍rvayam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 4

vi̱ṣṭvī śamī̍ taraṇi̱tvena̍ vā̱ghato̱ martā̍sa̱: santo̍ amṛta̱tvamā̍naśuḥ |

sau̱dha̱nva̱nā ṛ̱bhava̱: sūra̍cakṣasaḥ saṃvatsa̱re sama̍pṛcyanta dhī̱tibhi̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 5

kṣetra̍miva̱ vi ma̍mu̱steja̍nena̱m̐ eka̱ṃ pātra̍mṛ̱bhavo̱ jeha̍mānam |

upa̍stutā upa̱maṃ nādha̍mānā̱ ama̍rtyeṣu̱ śrava̍ i̱cchamā̍nāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 6

ā ma̍nī̱ṣāma̱ntari̍kṣasya̱ nṛbhya̍: sru̱ceva̍ ghṛ̱taṃ ju̍havāma vi̱dmanā̍ |

ta̱ra̱ṇi̱tvā ye pi̱tura̍sya saści̱ra ṛ̱bhavo̱ vāja̍maruhan di̱vo raja̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 7

ṛ̱bhurna̱ indra̱: śava̍sā̱ navī̍yānṛ̱bhurvāje̍bhi̱rvasu̍bhi̱rvasu̍rda̱diḥ |

yu̱ṣmāka̍ṃ devā̱ ava̱sāha̍ni pri̱ye̱ 3 'bhi ti̍ṣṭhema pṛtsu̱tīrasu̍nvatām

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 8

niścarma̍ṇa ṛbhavo̱ gāma̍piṃśata̱ saṃ va̱tsenā̍sṛjatā mā̱tara̱ṃ puna̍: |

saudha̍nvanāsaḥ svapa̱syayā̍ naro̱ jivrī̱ yuvā̍nā pi̱tarā̍kṛṇotana

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 110 | **Verse:** 9

vāje̍bhirno̱ vāja̍sātāvaviḍḍhyṛbhu̱mām̐ i̍ndra ci̱tramā da̍rṣi̱ rādha̍: |

tanno̍ mi̱tro varu̍ṇo māmahantā̱madi̍ti̱: sindhu̍: pṛthi̱vī u̱ta dyauḥ
