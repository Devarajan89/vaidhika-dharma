# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 47

**10 praskaṇvaḥ kāṇvaḥ| aśvinau| pragāthaḥ – viṣamā bṛhatyaḥ, samā satobṛhatyaḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 1

a̱yaṃ vā̱ṃ madhu̍mattamaḥ su̱taḥ soma̍ ṛtāvṛdhā |
tama̍śvinā pibataṃ ti̱roa̍hnyaṃ dha̱ttaṃ ratnā̍ni dā̱śuṣe̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 2

tri̱va̱ndhu̱reṇa̍ tri̱vṛtā̍ su̱peśa̍sā̱ rathe̱nā yā̍tamaśvinā |

kaṇvā̍so vā̱ṃ brahma̍ kṛṇvantyadhva̱re teṣā̱ṃ su śṛ̍ṇuta̱ṃ hava̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 3

aśvi̍nā̱ madhu̍mattamaṃ pā̱taṃ soma̍mṛtāvṛdhā |

athā̱dya da̍srā̱ vasu̱ bibhra̍tā̱ rathe̍ dā̱śvāṃsa̱mupa̍ gacchatam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 4

tri̱ṣa̱dha̱sthe ba̱rhiṣi̍ viśvavedasā̱ madhvā̍ ya̱jñaṃ mi̍mikṣatam |

kaṇvā̍so vāṃ su̱taso̍mā a̱bhidya̍vo yu̱vāṃ ha̍vante aśvinā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 5

yābhi̱: kaṇva̍ma̱bhiṣṭi̍bhi̱: prāva̍taṃ yu̱vama̍śvinā |

tābhi̱: ṣva1smām̐ a̍vataṃ śubhaspatī pā̱taṃ soma̍mṛtāvṛdhā

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 6

su̱dāse̍ dasrā̱ vasu̱ bibhra̍tā̱ rathe̱ pṛkṣo̍ vahatamaśvinā |

ra̱yiṃ sa̍mu̱drādu̱ta vā̍ di̱vasparya̱sme dha̍ttaṃ puru̱spṛha̍m

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 7

yannā̍satyā parā̱vati̱ yadvā̱ stho adhi̍ tu̱rvaśe̍ |

ato̱ rathe̍na su̱vṛtā̍ na̱ ā ga̍taṃ sā̱kaṃ sūrya̍sya ra̱śmibhi̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 8

a̱rvāñcā̍ vā̱ṃ sapta̍yo'dhvara̱śriyo̱ vaha̍ntu̱ sava̱nedupa̍ |

iṣa̍ṃ pṛ̱ñcantā̍ su̱kṛte̍ su̱dāna̍va̱ ā ba̱rhiḥ sī̍dataṃ narā

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 9

tena̍ nāsa̱tyā ga̍ta̱ṃ rathe̍na̱ sūrya̍tvacā |

yena̱ śaśva̍dū̱hathu̍rdā̱śuṣe̱ vasu̱ madhva̱: soma̍sya pī̱taye̍

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 47 | **Verse:** 10

u̱kthebhi̍ra̱rvāgava̍se purū̱vasū̍ a̱rkaiśca̱ ni hva̍yāmahe |

śaśva̱t kaṇvā̍nā̱ṃ sada̍si pri̱ye hi ka̱ṃ soma̍ṃ pa̱pathu̍raśvinā
