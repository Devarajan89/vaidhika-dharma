# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 56

**6 savya āṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 1

e̱ṣa pra pū̱rvīrava̱ tasya̍ ca̱mriṣo 'tyo̱ na yoṣā̱muda̍yaṃsta bhu̱rvaṇi̍: |
dakṣa̍ṃ ma̱he pā̍yayate hira̱ṇyaya̱ṃ ratha̍mā̱vṛtyā̱ hari̍yoga̱mṛbhva̍sam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 2

taṃ gū̱rtayo̍ nema̱nniṣa̱: parī̍ṇasaḥ samu̱draṃ na sa̱ṃcara̍ṇe sani̱ṣyava̍: |

pati̱ṃ dakṣa̍sya vi̱datha̍sya̱ nū saho̍ gi̱riṃ na ve̱nā adhi̍ roha̱ teja̍sā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 3

sa tu̱rvaṇi̍rma̱hām̐ a̍re̱ṇu pauṃsye̍ gi̱rerbhṛ̱ṣṭirna bhrā̍jate tu̱jā śava̍: |

yena̱ śuṣṇa̍ṃ mā̱yina̍māya̱so made̍ du̱dhra ā̱bhūṣu̍ rā̱maya̱nni dāma̍ni

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 4

de̱vī yadi̱ tavi̍ṣī̱ tvāvṛ̍dho̱taya̱ indra̱ṃ siṣa̍ktyu̱ṣasa̱ṃ na sūrya̍: |

yo dhṛ̱ṣṇunā̱ śava̍sā̱ bādha̍te̱ tama̱ iya̍rti re̱ṇuṃ bṛ̱hada̍rhari̱ṣvaṇi̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 5

vi yat ti̱ro dha̱ruṇa̱macyu̍ta̱ṃ rajo 'ti̍ṣṭhipo di̱va ātā̍su ba̱rhaṇā̍ |

sva̍rmīhle̱ yanmada̍ indra̱ harṣyāha̍n vṛ̱traṃ nira̱pāmau̍bjo arṇa̱vam

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 56 | **Verse:** 6

tvaṃ di̱vo dha̱ruṇa̍ṃ dhiṣa̱ oja̍sā pṛthi̱vyā i̍ndra̱ sada̍neṣu̱ māhi̍naḥ |

tvaṃ su̱tasya̱ made̍ ariṇā a̱po vi vṛ̱trasya̍ sa̱mayā̍ pā̱ṣyā̍rujaḥ
