# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 103

**8 kutsa āṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 1

tat ta̍ indri̱yaṃ pa̍ra̱maṃ pa̍rā̱cairadhā̍rayanta ka̱vaya̍: pu̱redam |
kṣa̱medama̱nyad di̱vya 1 nyada̍sya̱ samī̍ pṛcyate sama̱neva̍ ke̱tuḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 2

sa dhā̍rayat pṛthi̱vīṃ pa̱pratha̍cca̱ vajre̍ṇa ha̱tvā nira̱paḥ sa̍sarja |

aha̱nnahi̱mabhi̍nadrauhi̱ṇaṃ vyaha̱n vya̍ṃsaṃ ma̱ghavā̱ śacī̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 3

sa jā̱tūbha̍rmā śra̱ddadhā̍na̱ oja̱: puro̍ vibhi̱ndanna̍cara̱d vi dāsī̍: |

vi̱dvān vajri̱n dasya̍ve he̱tima̱syā''rya̱ṃ saho̍ vardhayā dyu̱mnami̍ndra

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 4

tadū̱cuṣe̱ mānu̍ṣe̱mā yu̱gāni̍ kī̱rtenya̍ṃ ma̱ghavā̱ nāma̱ bibhra̍t |

u̱pa̱pra̱yan da̍syu̱hatyā̍ya va̱jrī yaddha̍ sū̱nuḥ śrava̍se̱ nāma̍ da̱dhe

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 5

tada̍sye̱daṃ pa̍śyatā̱ bhūri̍ pu̱ṣṭaṃ śradindra̍sya dhattana vī̱ryā̍ya |

sa gā a̍vinda̱t so a̍vinda̱daśvā̱n tsa oṣa̍dhī̱: so a̱paḥ sa vanā̍ni

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 6

bhūri̍karmaṇe vṛṣa̱bhāya̱ vṛṣṇe̍ sa̱tyaśu̍ṣmāya sunavāma̱ soma̍m |

ya ā̱dṛtyā̍ paripa̱nthīva̱ śūro 'ya̍jvano vi̱bhaja̱nneti̱ veda̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 7

tadi̍ndra̱ preva̍ vī̱rya̍ṃ cakartha̱ yat sa̱santa̱ṃ vajre̱ṇābo̍dha̱yo'hi̍m |

anu̍ tvā̱ patnī̍rhṛṣi̱taṃ vaya̍śca̱ viśve̍ de̱vāso̍ amada̱nnanu̍ tvā

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 103 | **Verse:** 8

śuṣṇa̱ṃ pipru̱ṃ kuya̍vaṃ vṛ̱trami̍ndra ya̱dāva̍dhī̱rvi pura̱: śamba̍rasya |

tanno̍ mi̱tro varu̍ṇo māmahantā̱madi̍ti̱: sindhu̍: pṛthi̱vī u̱ta dyauḥ
