# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 130

**10 parucchepo daivodāsiḥ | indraḥ| atyaṣṭiḥ, 10 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 130 | **Verse:** 1

endra̍ yā̱hyupa̍ naḥ parā̱vato̱ nāyamacchā̍ vi̱dathā̍nīva̱ satpa̍ti̱rasta̱ṃ rāje̍va̱ satpa̍tiḥ |
havā̍mahe tvā va̱yaṃ praya̍svantaḥ su̱te sacā̍ |
pu̱trāso̱ na pi̱tara̱ṃ vāja̍sātaye̱ maṃhi̍ṣṭha̱ṃ vāja̍sātaye

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 130 | **Verse:** 2

pibā̱ soma̍mindra suvā̱namadri̍bhi̱: kośe̍na si̱ktama̍va̱taṃ na vaṃsa̍gastātṛṣā̱ṇo na vaṃsa̍gaḥ |

madā̍ya harya̱tāya̍ te tu̱viṣṭa̍māya̱ dhāya̍se |

ā tvā̍ yacchantu ha̱rito̱ na sūrya̱mahā̱ viśve̍va̱ sūrya̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 130 | **Verse:** 3

avi̍ndad di̱vo nihi̍ta̱ṃ guhā̍ ni̱dhiṃ verna garbha̱ṃ pari̍vīta̱maśma̍nyana̱nte a̱ntaraśma̍ni |

vra̱jaṃ va̱jrī gavā̍miva̱ siṣā̍sa̱nnaṅgi̍rastamaḥ |

apā̍vṛṇo̱diṣa̱ indra̱: parī̍vṛtā̱ dvāra̱ iṣa̱: parī̍vṛtāḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 130 | **Verse:** 4

dā̱dṛ̱hā̱ṇo vajra̱mindro̱ gabha̍styo̱: kṣadme̍va ti̱gmamasa̍nāya̱ saṃ śya̍dahi̱hatyā̍ya̱ saṃ śya̍t |

sa̱ṃvi̱vyā̱na oja̍sā̱ śavo̍bhirindra ma̱jmanā̍ |

taṣṭe̍va vṛ̱kṣaṃ va̱nino̱ ni vṛ̍ścasi para̱śveva̱ ni vṛ̍ścasi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 130 | **Verse:** 5

tvaṃ vṛthā̍ na̱dya̍ indra̱ sarta̱ve 'cchā̍ samu̱drama̍sṛjo̱ rathā̍m̐ iva vājaya̱to rathā̍m̐ iva |

i̱ta ū̱tīra̍yuñjata samā̱namartha̱makṣi̍tam |

dhe̱nūri̍va̱ mana̍ve vi̱śvado̍haso̱ janā̍ya vi̱śvado̍hasaḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 130 | **Verse:** 6

i̱māṃ te̱ vāca̍ṃ vasū̱yanta̍ ā̱yavo̱ ratha̱ṃ na dhīra̱: svapā̍ atakṣiṣuḥ su̱mnāya̱ tvāma̍takṣiṣuḥ |

śu̱mbhanto̱ jenya̍ṃ yathā̱ vāje̍ṣu vipra vā̱jina̍m |

atya̍miva̱ śava̍se sā̱taye̱ dhanā̱ viśvā̱ dhanā̍ni sā̱taye̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 130 | **Verse:** 7

bhi̱nat puro̍ nava̱timi̍ndra pū̱rave̱ divo̍dāsāya̱ mahi̍ dā̱śuṣe̍ nṛto̱ vajre̍ṇa dā̱śuṣe̍ nṛto |

a̱ti̱thi̱gvāya̱ śamba̍raṃ gi̱reru̱gro avā̍bharat |

ma̱ho dhanā̍ni̱ daya̍māna̱ oja̍sā̱ viśvā̱ dhanā̱nyoja̍sā

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 130 | **Verse:** 8

indra̍: sa̱matsu̱ yaja̍māna̱mārya̱ṃ prāva̱d viśve̍ṣu śa̱tamū̍tirā̱jiṣu̱ sva̍rmīhleṣvā̱jiṣu̍ |

mana̍ve̱ śāsa̍davra̱tān tvaca̍ṃ kṛ̱ṣṇāma̍randhayat |

dakṣa̱nna viśva̍ṃ tatṛṣā̱ṇamo̍ṣati̱ nya̍rśasā̱namo̍ṣati

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 130 | **Verse:** 9

sūra̍śca̱kraṃ pra vṛ̍hajjā̱ta oja̍sā prapi̱tve vāca̍maru̱ṇo mu̍ṣāyatīśā̱na ā mu̍ṣāyati |

u̱śanā̱ yat pa̍rā̱vato 'ja̍gannū̱taye̍ kave |

su̱mnāni̱ viśvā̱ manu̍ṣeva tu̱rvaṇi̱rahā̱ viśve̍va tu̱rvaṇi̍:

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 130 | **Verse:** 10

sa no̱ navye̍bhirvṛṣakarmannu̱kthaiḥ purā̍ṃ dartaḥ pā̱yubhi̍: pāhi śa̱gmaiḥ |
