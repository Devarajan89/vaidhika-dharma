# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 126

**7 kakṣīvān dairghatamasa auśijaḥ| (6) svanayo bhāvayavyaḥ, (7) romaśā| 1-5 svanayo bhāvayavyaḥ, 6 romaśā, 7 svanayo bhāvayavyaḥ| triṣṭup, 6-7 anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 126 | **Verse:** 1

ama̍ndā̱n tstomā̱n pra bha̍re manī̱ṣā sindhā̱vadhi̍ kṣiya̱to bhā̱vyasya̍ |
yo me̍ sa̱hasra̱mami̍mīta sa̱vāna̱tūrto̱ rājā̱ śrava̍ i̱cchamā̍naḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 126 | **Verse:** 2

śa̱taṃ rājño̱ nādha̍mānasya ni̱ṣkāñ cha̱tamaśvā̱n praya̍tān tsa̱dya āda̍m |

śa̱taṃ ka̱kṣīvā̱m̐ asu̍rasya̱ gonā̍ṃ di̱vi śravo̱'jara̱mā ta̍tāna

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 126 | **Verse:** 3

upa̍ mā śyā̱vāḥ sva̱naye̍na da̱ttā va̱dhūma̍nto̱ daśa̱ rathā̍so asthuḥ |

ṣa̱ṣṭiḥ sa̱hasra̱manu̱ gavya̱māgā̱t sana̍t ka̱kṣīvā̍m̐ abhipi̱tve ahnā̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 126 | **Verse:** 4

ca̱tvā̱ri̱ṃśad daśa̍rathasya̱ śoṇā̍: sa̱hasra̱syāgre̱ śreṇi̍ṃ nayanti |

ma̱da̱cyuta̍: kṛśa̱nāva̍to̱ atyā̍n ka̱kṣīva̍nta̱ uda̍mṛkṣanta pa̱jrāḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 126 | **Verse:** 5

pūrvā̱manu̱ praya̍ti̱mā da̍de va̱strīn yu̱ktām̐ a̱ṣṭāva̱ridhā̍yaso̱ gāḥ |

su̱bandha̍vo̱ ye vi̱śyā̍ iva̱ vrā ana̍svanta̱: śrava̱ aiṣa̍nta pa̱jrāḥ

## Verse 6

## Verse 7
