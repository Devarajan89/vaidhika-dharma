# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 165

**(15) 1,2,4,6,8,10-12 indraḥ, 3,5,7,9 marutaḥ, 13-15 agastyo maitrāvaruṇiḥ| marutvānindraḥ|triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 1

kayā̍ śu̱bhā sava̍yasa̱: sanī̍l̤āḥ samā̱nyā ma̱ruta̱: saṃ mi̍mikṣuḥ |
kayā̍ ma̱tī kuta̱ etā̍sa e̱te 'rca̍nti̱ śuṣma̱ṃ vṛṣa̍ṇo vasū̱yā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 2

kasya̱ brahmā̍ṇi jujuṣu̱ryuvā̍na̱: ko a̍dhva̱re ma̱ruta̱ ā va̍varta |

śye̱nām̐ i̍va̱ dhraja̍to a̱ntari̍kṣe̱ kena̍ ma̱hā mana̍sā rīramāma

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 3

kuta̱stvami̍ndra̱ māhi̍na̱: sanneko̍ yāsi satpate̱ kiṃ ta̍ i̱tthā |

saṃ pṛ̍cchase samarā̱ṇaḥ śu̍bhā̱nairvo̱cestanno̍ harivo̱ yatte̍ a̱sme

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 4

brahmā̍ṇi me ma̱taya̱: śaṃ su̱tāsa̱: śuṣma̍ iyarti̱ prabhṛ̍to me̱ adri̍: |

ā śā̍sate̱ prati̍ haryantyu̱kthemā harī̍ vahata̱stā no̱ accha̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 5

ato̍ va̱yama̍nta̱mebhi̍ryujā̱nāḥ svakṣa̍trebhista̱nva1 śumbha̍mānāḥ |

maho̍bhi̱retā̱m̐ upa̍ yujmahe̱ nvindra̍ sva̱dhāmanu̱ hi no̍ ba̱bhūtha̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 6

kva1 syā vo̍ marutaḥ sva̱dhāsī̱d yanmāmeka̍ṃ sa̱madha̍ttāhi̱hatye̍ |

a̱haṃ hyu1grasta̍vi̱ṣastuvi̍ṣmā̱n viśva̍sya̱ śatro̱rana̍maṃ vadha̱snaiḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 7

bhūri̍ cakartha̱ yujye̍bhira̱sme sa̍mā̱nebhi̍rvṛṣabha̱ pauṃsye̍bhiḥ |

bhūrī̍ṇi̱ hi kṛ̱ṇavā̍mā śavi̱ṣṭhendra̱ kratvā̍ maruto̱ yad vaśā̍ma

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 8

vadhī̍ṃ vṛ̱traṃ ma̍ruta indri̱yeṇa̱ svena̱ bhāme̍na tavi̱ṣo ba̍bhū̱vān |

a̱hame̱tā mana̍ve vi̱śvaśca̍ndrāḥ su̱gā a̱paśca̍kara̱ vajra̍bāhuḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 9

anu̍tta̱mā te̍ maghava̱nnaki̱rnu na tvāvā̍m̐ asti de̱vatā̱ vidā̍naḥ |

na jāya̍māno̱ naśa̍te̱ na jā̱to yāni̍ kari̱ṣyā kṛ̍ṇu̱hi pra̍vṛddha

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 10

eka̍sya cinme vi̱bhva1stvojo̱ yā nu da̍dhṛ̱ṣvān kṛ̱ṇavai̍ manī̱ṣā |

a̱haṃ hyu1gro ma̍ruto̱ vidā̍no̱ yāni̱ cyava̱mindra̱ idī̍śa eṣām

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 11

ama̍ndanmā maruta̱: stomo̱ atra̱ yanme̍ nara̱: śrutya̱ṃ brahma̍ ca̱kra |

indrā̍ya̱ vṛṣṇe̱ suma̍khāya̱ mahya̱ṃ sakhye̱ sakhā̍yasta̱nve̍ ta̱nūbhi̍:

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 12

e̱vede̱te prati̍ mā̱ roca̍mānā̱ ane̍dya̱: śrava̱ eṣo̱ dadhā̍nāḥ |

sa̱ṃcakṣyā̍ marutaśca̱ndrava̍rṇā̱ acchā̍nta me cha̱dayā̍thā ca nū̱nam

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 13

ko nvatra̍ maruto māmahe va̱: pra yā̍tana̱ sakhī̱m̐racchā̍ sakhāyaḥ |

manmā̍ni citrā apivā̱taya̍nta e̱ṣāṃ bhū̍ta̱ nave̍dā ma ṛ̱tānā̍m

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 14

ā yad du̍va̱syād du̱vase̱ na kā̱rura̱smāñca̱kre mā̱nyasya̍ me̱dhā |

o ṣu va̍rtta maruto̱ vipra̱macche̱mā brahmā̍ṇi jari̱tā vo̍ arcat

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 165 | **Verse:** 15

e̱ṣa va̱: stomo̍ maruta i̱yaṃ gīrmā̍ndā̱ryasya̍ mā̱nyasya̍ kā̱roḥ |

eṣā yā̍sīṣṭa ta̱nve̍ va̱yāṃ vi̱dyāme̱ṣaṃ vṛ̱jana̍ṃ jī̱radā̍num
