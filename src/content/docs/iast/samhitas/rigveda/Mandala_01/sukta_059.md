# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 59

**7 nodhā gautamaḥ| agnirvaiśvānaraḥ|triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 1

va̱yā ida̍gne a̱gnaya̍ste a̱nye tve viśve̍ a̱mṛtā̍ mādayante |
vaiśvā̍nara̱ nābhi̍rasi kṣitī̱nāṃ sthūṇe̍va̱ janā̍m̐ upa̱mid ya̍yantha

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 2

mū̱rdhā di̱vo nābhi̍ra̱gniḥ pṛ̍thi̱vyā athā̍bhavadara̱tī roda̍syoḥ |

taṃ tvā̍ de̱vāso̍'janayanta de̱vaṃ vaiśvā̍nara̱ jyoti̱ridāryā̍ya

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 3

ā sūrye̱ na ra̱śmayo̍ dhru̱vāso̍ vaiśvāna̱re da̍dhire̱'gnā vasū̍ni |

yā parva̍te̱ṣvoṣa̍dhīṣva̱psu yā mānu̍ṣe̱ṣvasi̱ tasya̱ rājā̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 4

bṛ̱ha̱tī i̍va sū̱nave̱ roda̍sī̱ giro̱ hotā̍ manu̱ṣyo̱ 3 na dakṣa̍: |

sva̍rvate sa̱tyaśu̍ṣmāya pū̱rvīrvai̍śvāna̱rāya̱ nṛta̍māya ya̱hvīḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 5

di̱vaści̍t te bṛha̱to jā̍tavedo̱ vaiśvā̍nara̱ pra ri̍rice mahi̱tvam |

rājā̍ kṛṣṭī̱nāma̍si̱ mānu̍ṣīṇāṃ yu̱dhā de̱vebhyo̱ vari̍vaścakartha

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 6

pra nū ma̍hi̱tvaṃ vṛ̍ṣa̱bhasya̍ voca̱ṃ yaṃ pū̱ravo̍ vṛtra̱haṇa̱ṃ saca̍nte |

vai̱śvā̱na̱ro dasyu̍ma̱gnirja̍gha̱nvām̐ adhū̍no̱t kāṣṭhā̱ ava̱ śamba̍raṃ bhet
