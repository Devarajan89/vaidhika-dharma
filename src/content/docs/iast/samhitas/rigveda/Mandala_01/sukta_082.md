# Maṇḍala 1 — Sūktam 82

**6 gotamo rāhūgaṇaḥ| indraḥ| paṃktiḥ, 6 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 82 | **Verse:** 1

upo̱ ṣu śṛ̍ṇu̱hī giro̱ magha̍va̱n māta̍thā iva |
ya̱dā na̍: sū̱nṛtā̍vata̱: kara̱ āda̱rthayā̍sa̱ id yojā̱ nvi̍ndra te̱ harī̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 82 | **Verse:** 2

akṣa̱nnamī̍madanta̱ hyava̍ pri̱yā a̍dhūṣata |

asto̍ṣata̱ svabhā̍navo̱ viprā̱ navi̍ṣṭhayā ma̱tī yojā̱ nvi̍ndra te̱ harī̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 82 | **Verse:** 3

su̱sa̱ṃdṛśa̍ṃ tvā va̱yaṃ magha̍van vandiṣī̱mahi̍ |

pra nū̱naṃ pū̱rṇava̍ndhuraḥ stu̱to yā̍hi̱ vaśā̱m̐ anu̱ yojā̱ nvi̍ndra te̱ harī̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 82 | **Verse:** 4

sa ghā̱ taṃ vṛṣa̍ṇa̱ṃ ratha̱madhi̍ tiṣṭhāti go̱vida̍m |

yaḥ pātra̍ṃ hāriyoja̱naṃ pū̱rṇami̍ndra̱ cike̍tati̱ yojā̱ nvi̍ndra te̱ harī̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 82 | **Verse:** 5

yu̱ktaste̍ astu̱ dakṣi̍ṇa u̱ta sa̱vyaḥ śa̍takrato |

tena̍ jā̱yāmupa̍ pri̱yāṃ ma̍ndā̱no yā̱hyandha̍so̱ yojā̱ nvi̍ndra te̱ harī̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 1 | **Sukta:** 82 | **Verse:** 6

yu̱najmi̍ te̱ brahma̍ṇā ke̱śinā̱ harī̱ upa̱ pra yā̍hi dadhi̱ṣe gabha̍styoḥ |

ut tvā̍ su̱tāso̍ rabha̱sā a̍mandiṣuḥ pūṣa̱ṇvān va̍jri̱ntsamu̱ patnyā̍madaḥ
