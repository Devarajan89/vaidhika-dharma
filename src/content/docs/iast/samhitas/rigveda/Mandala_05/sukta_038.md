# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 38

**5 bhaumo'triḥ| indraḥ| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 1

u̱roṣṭa̍ indra̱ rādha̍so vi̱bhvī rā̱tiḥ śa̍takrato |
adhā̍ no viśvacarṣaṇe dyu̱mnā su̍kṣatra maṃhaya

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 2

yadī̍mindra śra̱vāyya̱miṣa̍ṃ śaviṣṭha dadhi̱ṣe |

pa̱pra̱the dī̍rgha̱śrutta̍ma̱ṃ hira̍ṇyavarṇa du̱ṣṭara̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 3

śuṣmā̍so̱ ye te̍ adrivo me̱hanā̍ keta̱sāpa̍: |

u̱bhā de̱vāva̱bhiṣṭa̍ye di̱vaśca̱ gmaśca̍ rājathaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 4

u̱to no̍ a̱sya kasya̍ ci̱d dakṣa̍sya̱ tava̍ vṛtrahan |

a̱smabhya̍ṃ nṛ̱mṇamā bha̍rā̱'smabhya̍ṃ nṛmaṇasyase

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 38 | **Verse:** 5

nū ta̍ ā̱bhira̱bhiṣṭi̍bhi̱stava̱ śarma̍ñchatakrato |

indra̱ syāma̍ sugo̱pāḥ śūra̱ syāma̍ sugo̱pāḥ
