# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 73

**10 paura ātreyaḥ| aśvinau| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 1

yada̱dya sthaḥ pa̍rā̱vati̱ yada̍rvā̱vatya̍śvinā | yad vā̍ pu̱rū pu̍rubhujā̱ yada̱ntari̍kṣa̱ ā ga̍tam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 2

i̱ha tyā pu̍ru̱bhūta̍mā pu̱rū daṃsā̍ṃsi̱ bibhra̍tā | va̱ra̱syā yā̱myadhri̍gū hu̱ve tu̱viṣṭa̍mā bhu̱je

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 3

ī̱rmānyad vapu̍ṣe̱ vapu̍śca̱kraṃ ratha̍sya yemathuḥ | parya̱nyā nāhu̍ṣā yu̱gā ma̱hnā rajā̍ṃsi dīyathaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 4

tadū̱ ṣu vā̍me̱nā kṛ̱taṃ viśvā̱ yad vā̱manu̱ ṣṭave̍ | nānā̍ jā̱tāva̍re̱pasā̱ sama̱sme bandhu̱meya̍thuḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 5

ā yad vā̍ṃ sū̱ryā ratha̱ṃ tiṣṭha̍d raghu̱ṣyada̱ṃ sadā̍ | pari̍ vāmaru̱ṣā vayo̍ ghṛ̱ṇā va̍ranta ā̱tapa̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 6

yu̱voratri̍ściketati̱ narā̍ su̱mnena̱ ceta̍sā | gha̱rmaṃ yad vā̍mare̱pasa̱ṃ nāsa̍tyā̱snā bhu̍ra̱ṇyati̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 7

u̱gro vā̍ṃ kaku̱ho ya̱yiḥ śṛ̱ṇve yāme̍ṣu saṃta̱niḥ | yad vā̱ṃ daṃso̍bhiraśvi̱nā'tri̍rnarāva̱varta̍ti

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 8

madhva̍ ū̱ ṣu ma̍dhūyuvā̱ rudrā̱ siṣa̍kti pi̱pyuṣī̍ | yat sa̍mu̱drāti̱ parṣa̍thaḥ pa̱kvāḥ pṛkṣo̍ bharanta vām

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 9

sa̱tyamid vā u̍ aśvinā yu̱vāmā̍hurmayo̱bhuvā̍ | tā yāma̍n yāma̱hūta̍mā̱ yāma̱nnā mṛ̍l̤a̱yatta̍mā

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 73 | **Verse:** 10
