# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 60

**8 śyāvāśva ātreyaḥ| maruto'gnāmarutau vā| triṣṭup, 7- 8 jagatī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 1

īl̤e̍ a̱gniṃ svava̍sa̱ṃ namo̍bhiri̱ha pra̍sa̱tto vi ca̍yat kṛ̱taṃ na̍: |
rathai̍riva̱ pra bha̍re vāja̱yadbhi̍: pradakṣi̱ṇinma̱rutā̱ṃ stoma̍mṛdhyām

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 2

ā ye ta̱sthuḥ pṛṣa̍tīṣu śru̱tāsu̍ su̱kheṣu̍ ru̱drā ma̱ruto̱ rathe̍ṣu |

vanā̍ cidugrā jihate̱ ni vo̍ bhi̱yā pṛ̍thi̱vī ci̍d rejate̱ parva̍taścit

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 3

parva̍taści̱nmahi̍ vṛ̱ddho bi̍bhāya di̱vaści̱t sānu̍ rejata sva̱ne va̍: |

yat krīl̤a̍tha maruta ṛṣṭi̱manta̱ āpa̍ iva sa̱dhrya̍ñco dhavadhve

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 4

va̱rā i̱ved rai̍va̱tāso̱ hira̍ṇyaira̱bhi sva̱dhābhi̍sta̱nva̍: pipiśre |

śri̱ye śreyā̍ṃsasta̱vaso̱ rathe̍ṣu sa̱trā mahā̍ṃsi cakrire ta̱nūṣu̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 5

a̱jye̱ṣṭhāso̱ aka̍niṣṭhāsa e̱te saṃ bhrāta̍ro vāvṛdhu̱: saubha̍gāya |

yuvā̍ pi̱tā svapā̍ ru̱dra e̍ṣāṃ su̱dughā̱ pṛśni̍: su̱dinā̍ ma̱rudbhya̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 6

yadu̍tta̱me ma̍ruto madhya̱me vā̱ yad vā̍va̱me su̍bhagāso di̱vi ṣṭha |

ato̍ no rudrā u̱ta vā̱ nva1syā'gne̍ vi̱ttāddha̱viṣo̱ yad yajā̍ma

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 7

a̱gniśca̱ yanma̍ruto viśvavedaso di̱vo vaha̍dhva̱ utta̍rā̱dadhi̱ ṣṇubhi̍: |

te ma̍ndasā̱nā dhuna̍yo riśādaso vā̱maṃ dha̍tta̱ yaja̍mānāya sunva̱te

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 60 | **Verse:** 8

agne̍ ma̱rudbhi̍: śu̱bhaya̍dbhi̱rṛkva̍bhi̱: soma̍ṃ piba mandasā̱no ga̍ṇa̱śribhi̍: |

pā̱va̱kebhi̍rviśvami̱nvebhi̍rā̱yubhi̱rvaiśvā̍nara pra̱divā̍ ke̱tunā̍ sa̱jūḥ
