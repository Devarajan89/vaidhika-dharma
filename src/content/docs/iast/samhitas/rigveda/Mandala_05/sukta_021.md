# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 21

**4 sasa ātreyaḥ| agniḥ| anuṣṭup, 4 paṃktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 1

ma̱nu̱ṣvat tvā̱ ni dhī̍mahi manu̱ṣvat sami̍dhīmahi | agne̍ manu̱ṣvada̍ṅgiro de̱vān de̍vaya̱te ya̍ja

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 2

tvaṃ hi mānu̍ṣe̱ jane'gne̱ suprī̍ta i̱dhyase̍ | sruca̍stvā yantyānu̱ṣak sujā̍ta̱ sarpi̍rāsute

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 3

tvāṃ viśve̍ sa̱joṣa̍so de̱vāso̍ dū̱tama̍krata | sa̱pa̱ryanta̍stvā kave ya̱jñeṣu̍ de̱vamī̍l̤ate

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 4

de̱vaṃ vo̍ devaya̱jyayā̱ 'gnimī̍l̤īta̱ martya̍: |

sami̍ddhaḥ śukra dīdihyṛ̱tasya̱ yoni̱māsa̍daḥ sa̱sasya̱ yoni̱māsa̍daḥ
