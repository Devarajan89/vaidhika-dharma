# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 34

**9 prājāpatyaḥ saṃvaraṇaḥ| indraḥ| jagatī, 9 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 1

ajā̍taśatruma̱jarā̱ sva̍rva̱tyanu̍ sva̱dhāmi̍tā da̱smamī̍yate |
su̱nota̍na̱ paca̍ta̱ brahma̍vāhase puruṣṭu̱tāya̍ prata̱raṃ da̍dhātana

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 2

ā yaḥ some̍na ja̱ṭhara̱mapi̍pra̱tā'ma̍ndata ma̱ghavā̱ madhvo̱ andha̍saḥ |

yadī̍ṃ mṛ̱gāya̱ hanta̍ve ma̱hāva̍dhaḥ sa̱hasra̍bhṛṣṭimu̱śanā̍ va̱dhaṃ yama̍t

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 3

yo a̍smai ghra̱ṃsa u̱ta vā̱ ya ūdha̍ni̱ soma̍ṃ su̱noti̱ bhava̍ti dyu̱mām̐ aha̍ |

apā̍pa śa̱krasta̍ta̱nuṣṭi̍mūhati ta̱nūśu̍bhraṃ ma̱ghavā̱ yaḥ ka̍vāsa̱khaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 4

yasyāva̍dhīt pi̱tara̱ṃ yasya̍ mā̱tara̱ṃ yasya̍ śa̱kro bhrāta̍ra̱ṃ nāta̍ īṣate |

vetīdva̍sya̱ praya̍tā yataṃka̱ro na kilbi̍ṣādīṣate̱ vasva̍ āka̱raḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 5

na pa̱ñcabhi̍rda̱śabhi̍rvaṣṭyā̱rabha̱ṃ nāsu̍nvatā sacate̱ puṣya̍tā ca̱na |

ji̱nāti̱ veda̍mu̱yā hanti̍ vā̱ dhuni̱rā de̍va̱yuṃ bha̍jati̱ goma̍ti vra̱je

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 6

vi̱tvakṣa̍ṇa̱: samṛ̍tau cakramāsa̱jo'su̍nvato̱ viṣu̍ṇaḥ sunva̱to vṛ̱dhaḥ |

indro̱ viśva̍sya dami̱tā vi̱bhīṣa̍ṇo yathāva̱śaṃ na̍yati̱ dāsa̱mārya̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 7

samī̍ṃ pa̱ṇera̍jati̱ bhoja̍naṃ mu̱ṣe vi dā̱śuṣe̍ bhajati sū̱nara̱ṃ vasu̍ |

du̱rge ca̱na dhri̍yate̱ viśva̱ ā pu̱ru jano̱ yo a̍sya̱ tavi̍ṣī̱macu̍krudhat

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 8

saṃ yajjanau̍ su̱dhanau̍ vi̱śvaśa̍rdhasā̱vave̱dindro̍ ma̱ghavā̱ goṣu̍ śu̱bhriṣu̍ |

yuja̱ṃ hya1nyamakṛ̍ta pravepa̱nyudī̱ṃ gavya̍ṃ sṛjate̱ satva̍bhi̱rdhuni̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 9

sa̱ha̱sra̱sāmāgni̍veśiṃ gṛṇīṣe̱ śatri̍magna upa̱māṃ ke̱tuma̱ryaḥ |

tasmā̱ āpa̍: sa̱ṃyata̍: pīpayanta̱ tasmi̍n kṣa̱tramama̍vat tve̱ṣama̍stu
