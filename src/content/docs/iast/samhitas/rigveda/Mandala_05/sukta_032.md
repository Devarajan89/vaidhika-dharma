# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 32

**12 gāturātreyaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 1

ada̍rda̱rutsa̱masṛ̍jo̱ vi khāni̱ tvama̍rṇa̱vān ba̍dbadhā̱nām̐ a̍ramṇāḥ |
ma̱hānta̍mindra̱ parva̍ta̱ṃ vi yad vaḥ sṛ̱jo vi dhārā̱ ava̍ dāna̱vaṃ ha̍n

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 2

tvamutsā̍m̐ ṛ̱tubhi̍rbadbadhā̱nām̐ ara̍ṃha̱ ūdha̱: parva̍tasya vajrin |

ahi̍ṃ cidugra̱ prayu̍ta̱ṃ śayā̍naṃ jagha̱nvām̐ i̍ndra̱ tavi̍ṣīmadhatthāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 3

tyasya̍ cinmaha̱to nirmṛ̱gasya̱ vadha̍rjaghāna̱ tavi̍ṣībhi̱rindra̍: |

ya eka̱ ida̍pra̱tirmanya̍māna̱ āda̍smāda̱nyo a̍janiṣṭa̱ tavyā̍n

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 4

tyaṃ ci̍deṣāṃ sva̱dhayā̱ mada̍ntaṃ mi̱ho napā̍taṃ su̱vṛdha̍ṃ tamo̱gām |

vṛṣa̍prabharmā dāna̱vasya̱ bhāma̱ṃ vajre̍ṇa va̱jrī ni ja̍ghāna̱ śuṣṇa̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 5

tyaṃ ci̍dasya̱ kratu̍bhi̱rniṣa̍ttamama̱rmaṇo̍ vi̱dadida̍sya̱ marma̍ |

yadī̍ṃ sukṣatra̱ prabhṛ̍tā̱ mada̍sya̱ yuyu̍tsanta̱ṃ tama̍si ha̱rmye dhāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 6

tyaṃ ci̍di̱tthā ka̍tpa̱yaṃ śayā̍namasū̱rye tama̍si vāvṛdhā̱nam |

taṃ ci̍nmandā̱no vṛ̍ṣa̱bhaḥ su̱tasyo̱ccairindro̍ apa̱gūryā̍ jaghāna

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 7

ud yadindro̍ maha̱te dā̍na̱vāya̱ vadha̱ryami̍ṣṭa̱ saho̱ apra̍tītam |

yadī̱ṃ vajra̍sya̱ prabhṛ̍tau da̱dābha̱ viśva̍sya ja̱ntora̍dha̱maṃ ca̍kāra

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 8

tyaṃ ci̱darṇa̍ṃ madhu̱paṃ śayā̍namasi̱nvaṃ va̱vraṃ mahyāda̍du̱graḥ |

a̱pāda̍ma̱traṃ ma̍ha̱tā va̱dhena̱ ni du̍ryo̱ṇa ā̍vṛṇaṅ mṛ̱dhravā̍cam

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 9

ko a̍sya̱ śuṣma̱ṃ tavi̍ṣīṃ varāta̱ eko̱ dhanā̍ bharate̱ apra̍tītaḥ |

i̱me ci̍dasya̱ jraya̍so̱ nu de̱vī indra̱syauja̍so bhi̱yasā̍ jihāte

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 10

nya̍smai de̱vī svadhi̍tirjihīta̱ indrā̍ya gā̱turu̍śa̱tīva̍ yeme |

saṃ yadojo̍ yu̱vate̱ viśva̍mābhi̱ranu̍ sva̱dhāvne̍ kṣi̱tayo̍ namanta

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 11

eka̱ṃ nu tvā̱ satpa̍ti̱ṃ pāñca̍janyaṃ jā̱taṃ śṛ̍ṇomi ya̱śasa̱ṃ jane̍ṣu |

taṃ me̍ jagṛbhra ā̱śaso̱ navi̍ṣṭhaṃ do̱ṣā vasto̱rhava̍mānāsa̱ indra̍m

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 32 | **Verse:** 12

e̱vā hi tvāmṛ̍tu̱thā yā̱taya̍ntaṃ ma̱ghā vipre̍bhyo̱ dada̍taṃ śṛ̱ṇomi̍ |

kiṃ te̍ bra̱hmāṇo̍ gṛhate̱ sakhā̍yo̱ ye tvā̱yā ni̍da̱dhuḥ kāma̍mindra
