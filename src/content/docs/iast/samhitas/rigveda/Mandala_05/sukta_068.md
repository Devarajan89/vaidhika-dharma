# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 68

**5 yajata ātreyaḥ| mitrāvaruṇau| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 1

pra vo̍ mi̱trāya̍ gāyata̱ varu̍ṇāya vi̱pā gi̱rā | mahi̍kṣatrāvṛ̱taṃ bṛ̱hat

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 2

sa̱mrājā̱ yā ghṛ̱tayo̍nī mi̱traśco̱bhā varu̍ṇaśca | de̱vā de̱veṣu̍ praśa̱stā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 3

tā na̍: śakta̱ṃ pārthi̍vasya ma̱ho rā̱yo di̱vyasya̍ | mahi̍ vāṃ kṣa̱traṃ de̱veṣu̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 4

ṛ̱tamṛ̱tena̱ sapa̍nteṣi̱raṃ dakṣa̍māśāte | a̱druhā̍ de̱vau va̍rdhete

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 5

vṛ̱ṣṭidyā̍vā rī̱tyā̍pe̱ṣaspatī̱ dānu̍matyāḥ | bṛ̱hanta̱ṃ garta̍māśāte
