# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 70

**4 urucakrirātreyaḥ| mitrāvaruṇau| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 70 | **Verse:** 1

pu̱rū̱ruṇā̍ ci̱ddhyastyavo̍ nū̱naṃ vā̍ṃ varuṇa | mitra̱ vaṃsi̍ vāṃ suma̱tim

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 70 | **Verse:** 2

tā vā̍ṃ sa̱myaga̍druhvā̱ṇeṣa̍maśyāma̱ dhāya̍se | va̱yaṃ te ru̍drā syāma

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 70 | **Verse:** 3

pā̱taṃ no̍ rudrā pā̱yubhi̍ru̱ta trā̍yethāṃ sutrā̱trā | tu̱ryāma̱ dasyū̍n ta̱nūbhi̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 70 | **Verse:** 4

mā kasyā̍dbhutakratū ya̱kṣaṃ bhu̍jemā ta̱nūbhi̍: | mā śeṣa̍sā̱ mā tana̍sā
