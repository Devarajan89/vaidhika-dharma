# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 30

**15 babhrurātreyaḥ| indraḥ, 12-15 ṛṇaṃcayendrau| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 1

kva1 sya vī̱raḥ ko a̍paśya̱dindra̍ṃ su̱khara̍tha̱mīya̍māna̱ṃ hari̍bhyām |
yo rā̱yā va̱jrī su̱taso̍mami̱cchan tadoko̱ gantā̍ puruhū̱ta ū̱tī

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 2

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 3

veda̱davi̍dvāñchṛ̱ṇava̍cca vi̱dvān vaha̍te̱'yaṃ ma̱ghavā̱ sarva̍senaḥ
