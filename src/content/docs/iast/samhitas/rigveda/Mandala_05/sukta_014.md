# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 14

**6 sutaṃbhara ātreyaḥ| agniḥ | gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 1

a̱gniṃ stome̍na bodhaya samidhā̱no ama̍rtyam | ha̱vyā de̱veṣu̍ no dadhat

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 2

tama̍dhva̱reṣvī̍l̤ate de̱vaṃ martā̱ ama̍rtyam | yaji̍ṣṭha̱ṃ mānu̍ṣe̱ jane̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 3

taṃ hi śaśva̍nta̱ īl̤a̍te sru̱cā de̱vaṃ ghṛ̍ta̱ścutā̍ | a̱gniṃ ha̱vyāya̱ vol̤aha̍ve

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 4

a̱gnirjā̱to a̍rocata̱ ghnan dasyū̱ñjyoti̍ṣā̱ tama̍: | avi̍nda̱d gā a̱paḥ sva̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 5

a̱gnimī̱l̤enya̍ṃ ka̱viṃ ghṛ̱tapṛ̍ṣṭhaṃ saparyata | vetu̍ me śṛ̱ṇava̱ddhava̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 6

a̱gniṃ ghṛ̱tena̍ vāvṛdhu̱: stome̍bhirvi̱śvaca̍rṣaṇim | svā̱dhībhi̍rvaca̱syubhi̍:
