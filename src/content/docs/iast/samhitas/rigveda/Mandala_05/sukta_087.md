# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 87

**9 evayāmarudātreyaḥ| marutaḥ| atijagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 1

pra vo̍ ma̱he ma̱tayo̍ yantu̱ viṣṇa̍ve ma̱rutva̍te giri̱jā e̍va̱yāma̍rut |
pra śardhā̍ya̱ praya̍jyave sukhā̱daye̍ ta̱vase̍ bha̱ndadi̍ṣṭaye̱ dhuni̍vratāya̱ śava̍se

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 2

pra ye jā̱tā ma̍hi̱nā ye ca̱ nu sva̱yaṃ pra vi̱dmanā̍ bru̱vata̍ eva̱yāma̍rut |

kratvā̱ tad vo̍ maruto̱ nādhṛṣe̱ śavo̍ dā̱nā ma̱hnā tade̍ṣā̱madhṛ̍ṣṭāso̱ nādra̍yaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 3

pra ye di̱vo bṛ̍ha̱taḥ śṛ̍ṇvi̱re gi̱rā su̱śukvā̍naḥ su̱bhva̍ eva̱yāma̍rut |

na yeṣā̱mirī̍ sa̱dhastha̱ īṣṭa̱ ām̐ a̱gnayo̱ na svavi̍dyuta̱: pra sya̱ndrāso̱ dhunī̍nām

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 4

sa ca̍krame maha̱to niru̍rukra̱maḥ sa̍mā̱nasmā̱t sada̍sa eva̱yāma̍rut |

ya̱dāyu̍kta̱ tmanā̱ svādadhi̱ ṣṇubhi̱rviṣpa̍rdhaso̱ vima̍haso̱ jigā̍ti̱ śevṛ̍dho̱ nṛbhi̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 5

sva̱no na vo'ma̍vān rejaya̱d vṛṣā̍ tve̱ṣo ya̱yista̍vi̱ṣa e̍va̱yāma̍rut |

yenā̱ saha̍nta ṛ̱ñjata̱ svaro̍ciṣa̱: sthāra̍śmāno hira̱ṇyayā̍: svāyu̱dhāsa̍ i̱ṣmiṇa̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 6

a̱pā̱ro vo̍ mahi̱mā vṛ̍ddhaśavasastve̱ṣaṃ śavo̍'vatveva̱yāma̍rut |

sthātā̍ro̱ hi prasi̍tau sa̱ṃdṛśi̱ sthana̱ te na̍ uruṣyatā ni̱daḥ śu̍śu̱kvāṃso̱ nāgnaya̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 7

te ru̱drāsa̱: suma̍khā a̱gnayo̍ yathā tuvidyu̱mnā a̍vantveva̱yāma̍rut |

dī̱rghaṃ pṛ̱thu pa̍prathe̱ sadma̱ pārthi̍va̱ṃ yeṣā̱majme̱ṣvā ma̱haḥ śardhā̱ṃsyadbhu̍tainasām

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 8

a̱dve̱ṣo no̍ maruto gā̱tumeta̍na̱ śrotā̱ hava̍ṃ jari̱ture̍va̱yāma̍rut |

viṣṇo̍rma̱haḥ sa̍manyavo yuyotana̱ smad ra̱thyo̱3 na da̱ṃsanā 'pa̱ dveṣā̍ṃsi sanu̱taḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 87 | **Verse:** 9

gantā̍ no ya̱jñaṃ ya̍jñiyāḥ su̱śami̱ śrotā̱ hava̍mara̱kṣa e̍va̱yāma̍rut |

jyeṣṭhā̍so̱ na parva̍tāso̱ vyo̍mani yū̱yaṃ tasya̍ pracetasa̱: syāta̍ du̱rdharta̍vo ni̱daḥ
