# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 64

**7 arcanānā ātreyaḥ| mitrāvaruṇau| anuṣṭup, 7 paṅktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 1

varu̍ṇaṃ vo ri̱śāda̍samṛ̱cā mi̱traṃ ha̍vāmahe | pari̍ vra̱jeva̍ bā̱hvorja̍ga̱nvāṃsā̱ sva̍rṇaram

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 2

tā bā̱havā̍ suce̱tunā̱ pra ya̍ntamasmā̱ arca̍te | śeva̱ṃ hi jā̱rya̍ṃ vā̱ṃ viśvā̍su̱ kṣāsu̱ jogu̍ve

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 3

yannū̱nama̱śyāṃ gati̍ṃ mi̱trasya̍ yāyāṃ pa̱thā | asya̍ pri̱yasya̱ śarma̱ṇyahi̍ṃsānasya saścire

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 4

yu̱vābhyā̍ṃ mitrāvaruṇopa̱maṃ dhe̍yāmṛ̱cā | yaddha̱ kṣaye̍ ma̱ghonā̍ṃ stotṝ̱ṇāṃ ca̍ spū̱rdhase̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 5

ā no̍ mitra sudī̱tibhi̱rvaru̍ṇaśca sa̱dhastha̱ ā | sve kṣaye̍ ma̱ghonā̱ṃ sakhī̍nāṃ ca vṛ̱dhase̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 6

yu̱vaṃ no̱ yeṣu̍ varuṇa kṣa̱traṃ bṛ̱hacca̍ bibhṛ̱thaḥ | u̱ru ṇo̱ vāja̍sātaye kṛ̱taṃ rā̱ye sva̱staye̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 7

u̱cchantyā̍ṃ me yaja̱tā de̱vakṣa̍tre̱ ruśa̍dgavi |

su̱taṃ soma̱ṃ na ha̱stibhi̱rā pa̱ḍbhirdhā̍vataṃ narā̱ bibhra̍tāvarca̱nāna̍sam
