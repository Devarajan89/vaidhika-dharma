# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 66

**6 rātahavya ātreyaḥ| mitrāvaruṇau| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 1

ā ci̍kitāna su̱kratū̍ de̱vau ma̍rta ri̱śāda̍sā | varu̍ṇāya ṛ̱tape̍śase dadhī̱ta praya̍se ma̱he

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 2

tā hi kṣa̱tramavi̍hrutaṃ sa̱myaga̍su̱rya1māśā̍te | adha̍ vra̱teva̱ mānu̍ṣa̱ṃ sva1rṇa dhā̍yi darśa̱tam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 3

tā vā̱meṣe̱ rathā̍nāmu̱rvīṃ gavyū̍timeṣām | rā̱taha̍vyasya suṣṭu̱tiṃ da̱dhṛk stomai̍rmanāmahe

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 4

adhā̱ hi kāvyā̍ yu̱vaṃ dakṣa̍sya pū̱rbhira̍dbhutā | ni ke̱tunā̱ janā̍nāṃ ci̱kethe̍ pūtadakṣasā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 5

tadṛ̱taṃ pṛ̍thivi bṛ̱hacchra̍vae̱ṣa ṛṣī̍ṇām | jra̱ya̱sā̱nāvara̍ṃ pṛ̱thvati̍ kṣaranti̱ yāma̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 6

ā yad vā̍mīyacakṣasā̱ mitra̍ va̱yaṃ ca̍ sū̱raya̍: | vyaci̍ṣṭhe bahu̱pāyye̱ yate̍mahi sva̱rājye̍
