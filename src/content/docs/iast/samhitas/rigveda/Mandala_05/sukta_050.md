# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 50

**5 svastyātreyaḥ| viśve devāḥ| anuṣṭup, 5 paṅktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 1

viśvo̍ de̱vasya̍ ne̱turmarto̍ vurīta sa̱khyam |
viśvo̍ rā̱ya i̍ṣudhyati dyu̱mnaṃ vṛ̍ṇīta pu̱ṣyase̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 2

te te̍ deva neta̱rye ce̱mām̐ a̍nu̱śase̍ |

te rā̱yā te hyā̱3pṛce̱ sace̍mahi saca̱thyai̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 3

ato̍ na̱ ā nṝnati̍thī̱nata̱: patnī̍rdaśasyata |

ā̱re viśva̍ṃ pathe̱ṣṭhāṃ dvi̱ṣo yu̍yotu̱ yūyu̍viḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 4

yatra̱ vahni̍ra̱bhihi̍to du̱drava̱d droṇya̍: pa̱śuḥ |

nṛ̱maṇā̍ vī̱rapa̱styo'rṇā̱ dhīre̍va̱ sani̍tā

## Verse 5
