# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 58

**8 śyāvāśva ātreyaḥ| marutaḥ| triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 1

tamu̍ nū̱naṃ tavi̍ṣīmantameṣāṃ stu̱ṣe ga̱ṇaṃ māru̍ta̱ṃ navya̍sīnām |
ya ā̱śva̍śvā̱ ama̍va̱d vaha̍nta u̱teśi̍re a̱mṛta̍sya sva̱rāja̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 2

tve̱ṣaṃ ga̱ṇaṃ ta̱vasa̱ṃ khādi̍hasta̱ṃ dhuni̍vrataṃ mā̱yina̱ṃ dāti̍vāram |

ma̱yo̱bhuvo̱ ye ami̍tā mahi̱tvā vanda̍sva vipra tuvi̱rādha̍so̱ nṝn

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 3

ā vo̍ yantūdavā̱hāso̍ a̱dya vṛ̱ṣṭiṃ ye viśve̍ ma̱ruto̍ ju̱nanti̍ |

a̱yaṃ yo a̱gnirma̍ruta̱: sami̍ddha e̱taṃ ju̍ṣadhvaṃ kavayo yuvānaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 4

yū̱yaṃ rājā̍na̱mirya̱ṃ janā̍ya vibhvata̱ṣṭaṃ ja̍nayathā yajatrāḥ |

yu̱ṣmade̍ti muṣṭi̱hā bā̱hujū̍to yu̱ṣmat sada̍śvo marutaḥ su̱vīra̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 5

a̱rā i̱vedaca̍ramā̱ ahe̍va̱ prapra̍ jāyante̱ aka̍vā̱ maho̍bhiḥ |

pṛśne̍: pu̱trā u̍pa̱māso̱ rabhi̍ṣṭhā̱: svayā̍ ma̱tyā ma̱ruta̱: saṃ mi̍mikṣuḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 6

yat prāyā̍siṣṭa̱ pṛṣa̍tībhi̱raśvai̍rvīl̤upa̱vibhi̍rmaruto̱ rathe̍bhiḥ |

kṣoda̍nta̱ āpo̍ riṇa̱te vanā̱nyavo̱sriyo̍ vṛṣa̱bhaḥ kra̍ndatu̱ dyauḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 7

prathi̍ṣṭa̱ yāma̍n pṛthi̱vī ci̍deṣā̱ṃ bharte̍va̱ garbha̱ṃ svamicchavo̍ dhuḥ |

vātā̱n hyaśvā̍n dhu̱ryā̍yuyu̱jre va̱rṣaṃ sveda̍ṃ cakrire ru̱driyā̍saḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 8

ha̱ye naro̱ maru̍to mṛ̱l̤atā̍ na̱stuvī̍maghāso̱ amṛ̍tā̱ ṛta̍jñāḥ |

satya̍śruta̱: kava̍yo̱ yuvā̍no̱ bṛha̍dgirayo bṛ̱hadu̱kṣamā̍ṇāḥ
