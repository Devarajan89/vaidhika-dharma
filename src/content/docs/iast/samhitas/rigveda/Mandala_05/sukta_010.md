# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 10

**7 gaya ātreyaḥ| agniḥ| anuṣṭup, 4-7 paṃktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 1

agna̱ oji̍ṣṭha̱mā bha̍ra dyu̱mnama̱smabhya̍madhrigo |
pra no̍ rā̱yā parī̍ṇasā̱ ratsi̱ vājā̍ya̱ panthā̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 2

tvaṃ no̍ agne adbhuta̱ kratvā̱ dakṣa̍sya ma̱ṃhanā̍ |

tve a̍su̱rya1māru̍hat krā̱ṇā mi̱tro na ya̱jñiya̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 3

tvaṃ no̍ agna eṣā̱ṃ gaya̍ṃ pu̱ṣṭiṃ ca̍ vardhaya |

ye stome̍bhi̱: pra sū̱rayo̱ naro̍ ma̱ghānyā̍na̱śuḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 4

ye a̍gne candra te̱ gira̍: śu̱mbhantyaśva̍rādhasaḥ |

śuṣme̍bhiḥ śu̱ṣmiṇo̱ naro̍ di̱vaści̱d yeṣā̍ṃ bṛ̱hat su̍kī̱rtirbodha̍ti̱ tmanā̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 5

tava̱ tye a̍gne a̱rcayo̱ bhrāja̍nto yanti dhṛṣṇu̱yā |

pari̍jmāno̱ na vi̱dyuta̍: svā̱no ratho̱ na vā̍ja̱yuḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 6

nū no̍ agna ū̱taye̍ sa̱bādha̍saśca rā̱taye̍ |

a̱smākā̍saśca sū̱rayo̱ viśvā̱ āśā̍starī̱ṣaṇi̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 7

tvaṃ no̍ agne aṅgiraḥ stu̱taḥ stavā̍na̱ ā bha̍ra |

hota̍rvibhvā̱saha̍ṃ ra̱yiṃ: sto̱tṛbhya̱: stava̍se ca na u̱taidhi̍ pṛ̱tsu no̍ vṛ̱dhe
