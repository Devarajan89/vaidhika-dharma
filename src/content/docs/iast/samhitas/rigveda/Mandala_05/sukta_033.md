# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 33

**10 prājāpatyaḥ saṃvaraṇaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 1

mahi̍ ma̱he ta̱vase̍ dīdhye̱ nṝnindrā̍ye̱tthā ta̱vase̱ ata̍vyān |
yo a̍smai suma̱tiṃ vāja̍sātau stu̱to jane̍ sama̱rya̍ści̱keta̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 2

sa tvaṃ na̍ indra dhiyasā̱no a̱rkairharī̍ṇāṃ vṛṣa̱n yoktra̍maśreḥ |

yā i̱tthā ma̍ghava̱nnanu̱ joṣa̱ṃ vakṣo̍ a̱bhi prāryaḥ sa̍kṣi̱ janā̍n

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 3

na te ta̍ indrā̱bhya1smadṛ̱ṣvā'yu̍ktāso abra̱hmatā̱ yadasa̍n |

tiṣṭhā̱ ratha̱madhi̱ taṃ va̍jraha̱stā'' ra̱śmiṃ de̍va yamase̱ svaśva̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 4

pu̱rū yat ta̍ indra̱ santyu̱kthā gave̍ ca̱kartho̱rvarā̍su̱ yudhya̍n |

ta̱ta̱kṣe sūryā̍ya ci̱doka̍si̱ sve vṛṣā̍ sa̱matsu̍ dā̱sasya̱ nāma̍ cit

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 5

va̱yaṃ te ta̍ indra̱ ye ca̱ nara̱: śardho̍ jajñā̱nā yā̱tāśca̱ rathā̍: |

āsmāñja̍gamyādahiśuṣma̱ satvā̱ bhago̱ na havya̍: prabhṛ̱theṣu̱ cāru̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 6

pa̱pṛ̱kṣeṇya̍mindra̱ tve hyojo̍ nṛ̱mṇāni̍ ca nṛ̱tamā̍no̱ ama̍rtaḥ |

sa na̱ enī̍ṃ vasavāno ra̱yiṃ dā̱: prāryaḥ stu̍ṣe tuvima̱ghasya̱ dāna̍m

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 7

e̱vā na̍ indro̱tibhi̍rava pā̱hi gṛ̍ṇa̱taḥ śū̍ra kā̱rūn |

u̱ta tvaca̱ṃ dada̍to̱ vāja̍sātau piprī̱hi madhva̱: suṣu̍tasya̱ cāro̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 8

u̱ta tye mā̍ pauruku̱tsyasya̍ sū̱restra̱sada̍syorhira̱ṇino̱ rarā̍ṇāḥ |

vaha̍ntu mā̱ daśa̱ śyetā̍so asya gairikṣi̱tasya̱ kratu̍bhi̱rnu sa̍śce

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 9

u̱ta tye mā̍ māru̱tāśva̍sya̱ śoṇā̱: kratvā̍maghāso vi̱datha̍sya rā̱tau |

sa̱hasrā̍ me̱ cyava̍tāno̱ dadā̍na ānū̱kama̱ryo vapu̍ṣe̱ nārca̍t

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 10

u̱ta tye mā̍ dhva̱nya̍sya̱ juṣṭā̍ lakṣma̱ṇya̍sya su̱ruco̱ yatā̍nāḥ |

ma̱hnā rā̱yaḥ sa̱ṃvara̍ṇasya̱ ṛṣe̍rvra̱jaṃ na gāva̱: praya̍tā̱ api̍ gman
