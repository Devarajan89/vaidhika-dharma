# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 29

**15 gaurivītiḥ śāktyaḥ| indraḥ, 9(prathamapādasya) uśanā vā| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 1

trya̍rya̱mā manu̍ṣo de̱vatā̍tā̱ trī ro̍ca̱nā di̱vyā dhā̍rayanta |
arca̍nti tvā ma̱ruta̍: pū̱tada̍kṣā̱stvame̍ṣā̱mṛṣi̍rindrāsi̱ dhīra̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 2

anu̱ yadī̍ṃ ma̱ruto̍ mandasā̱namārca̱nnindra̍ṃ papi̱vāṃsa̍ṃ su̱tasya̍ |

āda̍tta̱ vajra̍ma̱bhi yadahi̱ṃ hanna̱po ya̱hvīra̍sṛja̱t sarta̱vā u̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 3

u̱ta bra̍hmāṇo maruto me a̱syendra̱: soma̍sya̱ suṣu̍tasya peyāḥ |

taddhi ha̱vyaṃ manu̍ṣe̱ gā avi̍nda̱daha̱nnahi̍ṃ papi̱vām̐ indro̍ asya

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 4

ād roda̍sī vita̱raṃ vi ṣka̍bhāyat saṃvivyā̱naści̍d bhi̱yase̍ mṛ̱gaṃ ka̍: |

jiga̍rti̱mindro̍ apa̱jargu̍rāṇa̱: prati̍ śva̱santa̱mava̍ dāna̱vaṃ ha̍n

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 5

adha̱ kratvā̍ maghava̱n tubhya̍ṃ de̱vā anu̱ viśve̍ adaduḥ soma̱peya̍m |

yat sūrya̍sya ha̱rita̱: pata̍ntīḥ pu̱raḥ sa̱tīrupa̍rā̱ eta̍śe̱ kaḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 6

nava̱ yada̍sya nava̱tiṃ ca̍ bho̱gān tsā̱kaṃ vajre̍ṇa ma̱ghavā̍ vivṛ̱ścat |

arca̱ntīndra̍ṃ ma̱ruta̍: sa̱dhasthe̱ traiṣṭu̍bhena̱ vaca̍sā bādhata̱ dyām

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 7

sakhā̱ sakhye̍ apaca̱t tūya̍ma̱gnira̱sya kratvā̍ mahi̱ṣā trī śa̱tāni̍ |

trī sā̱kamindro̱ manu̍ṣa̱: sarā̍ṃsi su̱taṃ pi̍bad vṛtra̱hatyā̍ya̱ soma̍m

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 8

trī yaccha̱tā ma̍hi̱ṣāṇā̱magho̱ māstrī sarā̍ṃsi ma̱ghavā̍ so̱myāpā̍: |

kā̱raṃ na viśve̍ ahvanta de̱vā bhara̱mindrā̍ya̱ yadahi̍ṃ ja̱ghāna̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 9

u̱śanā̱ yat sa̍ha̱syai̱3rayā̍taṃ gṛ̱hami̍ndra jūjuvā̱nebhi̱raśvai̍: |

va̱nvā̱no atra̍ sa̱ratha̍ṃ yayātha̱ kutse̍na de̱vairava̍norha̱ śuṣṇa̍m

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 10

prānyacca̱krama̍vṛha̱: sūrya̍sya̱ kutsā̍yā̱nyad vari̍vo̱ yāta̍ve'kaḥ |

a̱nāso̱ dasyū̍m̐ramṛṇo va̱dhena̱ ni du̍ryo̱ṇa ā̍vṛṇaṅ mṛ̱dhravā̍caḥ

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 12

||

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 13

yā co̱ nu navyā̍ kṛ̱ṇava̍: śaviṣṭha̱ predu̱ tā te̍ vi̱dathe̍ṣu bravāma

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 14

yā ci̱nnu va̍jrin kṛ̱ṇavo̍ dadhṛ̱ṣvān na te̍ va̱rtā tavi̍ṣyā asti̱ tasyā̍:
