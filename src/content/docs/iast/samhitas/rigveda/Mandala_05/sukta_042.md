# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 42

**18 bhaumo'triḥ| viśve devāḥ, 11 rudraḥ| triṣṭup, 17 ekapadā virāṭ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 1

pra śaṃta̍mā̱ varu̍ṇa̱ṃ dīdhi̍tī̱ gīrmi̱traṃ bhaga̱madi̍tiṃ nū̱nama̍śyāḥ |
pṛṣa̍dyoni̱: pañca̍hotā śṛṇo̱tvatū̍rtapanthā̱ asu̍ro mayo̱bhuḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 2

prati̍ me̱ stoma̱madi̍tirjagṛbhyāt sū̱nuṃ na mā̱tā hṛdya̍ṃ su̱śeva̍m |

brahma̍ pri̱yaṃ de̱vahi̍ta̱ṃ yadastya̱haṃ mi̱tre varu̍ṇe̱ yanma̍yo̱bhu

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 3

udī̍raya ka̱vita̍maṃ kavī̱nāmu̱nattai̍nama̱bhi madhvā̍ ghṛ̱tena̍ |

sa no̱ vasū̍ni̱ praya̍tā hi̱tāni̍ ca̱ndrāṇi̍ de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā su̍vāti

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 4

sami̍ndra ṇo̱ mana̍sā neṣi̱ gobhi̱: saṃ sū̱ribhi̍rhariva̱: saṃ sva̱sti |

saṃ brahma̍ṇā de̱vahi̍ta̱ṃ yadasti̱ saṃ de̱vānā̍ṃ suma̱tyā ya̱jñiyā̍nām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 5

de̱vo bhaga̍: savi̱tā rā̱yo aṃśa̱ indro̍ vṛ̱trasya̍ sa̱ṃjito̱ dhanā̍nām |

ṛ̱bhu̱kṣā vāja̍ u̱ta vā̱ pura̍ṃdhi̱rava̍ntu no a̱mṛtā̍sastu̱rāsa̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 6

ma̱rutva̍to̱ apra̍tītasya ji̱ṣṇorajū̍ryata̱: pra bra̍vāmā kṛ̱tāni̍ |

na te̱ pūrve̍ maghava̱n nāpa̍rāso̱ na vī̱ryaṃ1 nūta̍na̱: kaśca̱nāpa̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 7

upa̍ stuhi pratha̱maṃ ra̍tna̱dheya̱ṃ bṛha̱spati̍ṃ sani̱tāra̱ṃ dhanā̍nām |

yaḥ śaṃsa̍te stuva̱te śambha̍viṣṭhaḥ purū̱vasu̍rā̱gama̱jjohu̍vānam

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 8

tavo̱tibhi̱: saca̍mānā̱ ari̍ṣṭā̱ bṛha̍spate ma̱ghavā̍naḥ su̱vīrā̍: |

ye a̍śva̱dā u̱ta vā̱ santi̍ go̱dā ye va̍stra̱dāḥ su̱bhagā̱steṣu̱ rāya̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 9

vi̱sa̱rmāṇa̍ṃ kṛṇuhi vi̱ttame̍ṣā̱ṃ ye bhu̱ñjate̱ apṛ̍ṇanto na u̱kthaiḥ |

apa̍vratān prasa̱ve vā̍vṛdhā̱nān bra̍hma̱dviṣa̱: sūryā̍d yāvayasva

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 10

ya oha̍te ra̱kṣaso̍ de̱vavī̍tāvaca̱krebhi̱staṃ ma̍ruto̱ ni yā̍ta |

yo va̱: śamī̍ṃ śaśamā̱nasya̱ nindā̍ttu̱cchyān kāmā̍n karate siṣvidā̱naḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 11

||

yakṣvā̍ ma̱he sau̍mana̱sāya̍ ru̱draṃ namo̍bhirde̱vamasu̍raṃ duvasya

## Verse 12

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 13

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 14

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 15
