# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 6

**10 vasuśruta ātreyaḥ| agniḥ| paṃktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 1

a̱gniṃ taṃ ma̍nye̱ yo vasu̱rasta̱ṃ yaṃ yanti̍ dhe̱nava̍: |
asta̱marva̍nta ā̱śavo'sta̱ṃ nityā̍so vā̱jina̱ iṣa̍ṃ sto̱tṛbhya̱ ā bha̍ra

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 2

so a̱gniryo vasu̍rgṛ̱ṇe saṃ yamā̱yanti̍ dhe̱nava̍: |

samarva̍nto raghu̱druva̱: saṃ su̍jā̱tāsa̍: sū̱raya̱ iṣa̍ṃ sto̱tṛbhya̱ ā bha̍ra

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 3

a̱gnirhi vā̱jina̍ṃ vi̱śe dadā̍ti vi̱śvaca̍rṣaṇiḥ |

a̱gnī rā̱ye svā̱bhuva̱ṃ sa prī̱to yā̍ti̱ vārya̱miṣa̍ṃ sto̱tṛbhya̱ ā bha̍ra

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 4

ā te̍ agna idhīmahi dyu̱manta̍ṃ devā̱jara̍m |

yaddha̱ syā te̱ panī̍yasī sa̱mid dī̱daya̍ti̱ dyavīṣa̍ṃ sto̱tṛbhya̱ ā bha̍ra

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 5

ā te̍ agna ṛ̱cā ha̱viḥ śukra̍sya śociṣaspate |

suśca̍ndra̱ dasma̱ viśpa̍te̱ havya̍vā̱ṭ tubhya̍ṃ hūyata̱ iṣa̍ṃ sto̱tṛbhya̱ ā bha̍ra

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 6

pro tye a̱gnayo̱'gniṣu̱ viśva̍ṃ puṣyanti̱ vārya̍m |

te hi̍nvire̱ ta i̍nvire̱ ta i̍ṣaṇyantyānu̱ṣagiṣa̍ṃ sto̱tṛbhya̱ ā bha̍ra

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 7

tava̱ tye a̍gne a̱rcayo̱ mahi̍ vrādhanta vā̱jina̍: |

ye patva̍bhiḥ śa̱phānā̍ṃ vra̱jā bhu̱ranta̱ gonā̱miṣa̍ṃ sto̱tṛbhya̱ ā bha̍ra

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 8

navā̍ no agna̱ ā bha̍ra sto̱tṛbhya̍: sukṣi̱tīriṣa̍: |

te syā̍ma̱ ya ā̍nṛ̱custvādū̍tāso̱ dame̍dama̱ iṣa̍ṃ sto̱tṛbhya̱ ā bha̍ra

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 9

u̱bhe su̍ścandra sa̱rpiṣo̱ darvī̍ śrīṇīṣa ā̱sani̍ |

u̱to na̱ ut pu̍pūryā u̱ktheṣu̍ śavasaspata̱ iṣa̍ṃ sto̱tṛbhya̱ ā bha̍ra

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 10

e̱vām̐ a̱gnima̍juryamurgī̱rbhirya̱jñebhi̍rānu̱ṣak |

dadha̍da̱sme su̱vīrya̍mu̱ta tyadā̱śvaśvya̱miṣa̍ṃ sto̱tṛbhya̱ ā bha̍ra
