# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 15

**5 dharuṇa aṅgirasaḥ| agniḥ | triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 1

pra ve̱dhase̍ ka̱vaye̱ vedyā̍ya̱ gira̍ṃ bhare ya̱śase̍ pū̱rvyāya̍ |
ghṛ̱tapra̍satto̱ asu̍raḥ su̱śevo̍ rā̱yo dha̱rtā dha̱ruṇo̱ vasvo̍ a̱gniḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 2

ṛ̱tena̍ ṛ̱taṃ dha̱ruṇa̍ṃ dhārayanta ya̱jñasya̍ śā̱ke pa̍ra̱me vyo̍man |

di̱vo dharma̍n dha̱ruṇe̍ se̱duṣo̱ nṝñjā̱tairajā̍tām̐ a̱bhi ye na̍na̱kṣuḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 3

a̱ṃho̱yuva̍sta̱nva̍stanvate̱ vi vayo̍ ma̱had du̱ṣṭara̍ṃ pū̱rvyāya̍ |

sa sa̱ṃvato̱ nava̍jātastuturyāt si̱ṃhaṃ na kru̱ddhama̱bhita̱: pari̍ ṣṭhuḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 4

mā̱teva̱ yad bhara̍se paprathā̱no jana̍ṃjana̱ṃ dhāya̍se̱ cakṣa̍se ca |

vayo̍vayo jarase̱ yaddadhā̍na̱: pari̱ tmanā̱ viṣu̍rūpo jigāsi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 5

vājo̱ nu te̱ śava̍saspā̱tvanta̍mu̱ruṃ dogha̍ṃ dha̱ruṇa̍ṃ deva rā̱yaḥ |

pa̱daṃ na tā̱yurguhā̱ dadhā̍no ma̱ho rā̱ye ci̱taya̱nnatri̍maspaḥ
