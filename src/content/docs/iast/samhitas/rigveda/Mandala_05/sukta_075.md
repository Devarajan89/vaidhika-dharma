# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 75

**9 avasyurātreyaḥ| aśvinau| paṅktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 1

prati̍ pri̱yata̍ma̱ṃ ratha̱ṃ vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ vasu̱vāha̍nam |
sto̱tā vā̍maśvinā̱vṛṣi̱: stome̍na̱ prati̍ bhūṣati̱ mādhvī̱ mama̍ śruta̱ṃ hava̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 2

a̱tyāyā̍tamaśvinā ti̱ro viśvā̍ a̱haṃ sanā̍ |

dasrā̱ hira̍ṇyavartanī̱ suṣu̍mnā̱ sindhu̍vāhasā̱ mādhvī̱ mama̍ śruta̱ṃ hava̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 3

ā no̱ ratnā̍ni̱ bibhra̍tā̱vaśvi̍nā̱ gaccha̍taṃ yu̱vam |

rudrā̱ hira̍ṇyavartanī juṣā̱ṇā vā̍jinīvasū̱ mādhvī̱ mama̍ śruta̱ṃ hava̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 4

su̱ṣṭubho̍ vāṃ vṛṣaṇvasū̱ rathe̱ vāṇī̱cyāhi̍tā |

u̱ta vā̍ṃ kaku̱ho mṛ̱gaḥ pṛkṣa̍: kṛṇoti vāpu̱ṣo mādhvī̱ mama̍ śruta̱ṃ hava̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 5

bo̱dhinma̍nasā ra̱thye̍ṣi̱rā ha̍vana̱śrutā̍ |

vibhi̱ścyavā̍namaśvinā̱ ni yā̍tho̱ adva̍yāvina̱ṃ mādhvī̱ mama̍ śruta̱ṃ hava̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 6

ā vā̍ṃ narā mano̱yujo'śvā̍saḥ pruṣi̱tapsa̍vaḥ |

vayo̍ vahantu pī̱taye̍ sa̱ha su̱mnebhi̍raśvinā̱ mādhvī̱ mama̍ śruta̱ṃ hava̍m

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 7

aśvi̍nā̱veha ga̍cchata̱ṃ nāsa̍tyā̱ mā vi ve̍natam |

ti̱raści̍darya̱yā pari̍ va̱rtiryā̍tamadābhyā̱ mādhvī̱ mama̍ śruta̱ṃ hava̍m

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 8

a̱smin ya̱jñe a̍dābhyā jari̱tāra̍ṃ śubhaspatī |

a̱va̱syuma̍śvinā yu̱vaṃ gṛ̱ṇanta̱mupa̍ bhūṣatho̱ mādhvī̱ mama̍ śruta̱ṃ hava̍m

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 75 | **Verse:** 9

abhū̍du̱ṣā ruśa̍t paśu̱rāgnira̍dhāyyṛ̱tviya̍: |

ayo̍ji vāṃ vṛṣaṇvasū̱ ratho̍ dasrā̱vama̍rtyo̱ mādhvī̱ mama̍ śruta̱ṃ hava̍m
