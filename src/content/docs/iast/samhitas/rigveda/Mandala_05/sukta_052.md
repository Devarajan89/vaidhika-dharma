# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 52

**17 śyāvāśva ātreyaḥ| marutaḥ| anuṣṭup, 6, 16-17 paṅktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 1

pra śyā̍vāśva dhṛṣṇu̱yārcā̍ ma̱rudbhi̱ṝkva̍bhiḥ |
ye a̍dro̱ghama̍nuṣva̱dhaṃ śravo̱ mada̍nti ya̱jñiyā̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 2

te hi sthi̱rasya̱ śava̍sa̱: sakhā̍ya̱: santi̍ dhṛṣṇu̱yā |

te yāma̱nnā dhṛ̍ṣa̱dvina̱stmanā̍ pānti̱ śaśva̍taḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 3

te sya̱ndrāso̱ nokṣaṇo'ti̍ ṣkandanti̱ śarva̍rīḥ |

ma̱rutā̱madhā̱ maho̍ di̱vi kṣa̱mā ca̍ manmahe

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 4

ma̱rutsu̍ vo dadhīmahi̱ stoma̍ṃ ya̱jñaṃ ca̍ dhṛṣṇu̱yā |

viśve̱ ye mānu̍ṣā yu̱gā pānti̱ martya̍ṃ ri̱ṣaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 5

arha̍nto̱ ye su̱dāna̍vo̱ naro̱ asā̍miśavasaḥ |

pra ya̱jñaṃ ya̱jñiye̍bhyo di̱vo a̍rcā ma̱rudbhya̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 6

ā ru̱kmairā yu̱dhā nara̍ ṛ̱ṣvā ṛ̱ṣṭīra̍sṛkṣata |

anve̍nā̱m̐ aha̍ vi̱dyuto̍ ma̱ruto̱ jajjha̍tīriva bhā̱nura̍rta̱ tmanā̍ di̱vaḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 7

ye vā̍vṛ̱dhanta̱ pārthi̍vā̱ ya u̱rāva̱ntari̍kṣa̱ ā |

vṛ̱jane̍ vā na̱dīnā̍ṃ sa̱dhasthe̍ vā ma̱ho di̱vaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 8

śardho̱ māru̍ta̱muccha̍ṃsa sa̱tyaśa̍vasa̱mṛbhva̍sam |

u̱ta sma̱ te śu̱bhe nara̱: pra sya̱ndrā yu̍jata̱ tmanā̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 9

u̱ta sma̱ te paru̍ṣṇyā̱mūrṇā̍ vasata śu̱ndhyava̍: |

u̱ta pa̱vyā rathā̍nā̱madri̍ṃ bhinda̱ntyoja̍sā

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 10

āpa̍thayo̱ vipa̍tha̱yo'nta̍spathā̱ anu̍pathāḥ |

e̱tebhi̱rmahya̱ṃ nāma̍bhirya̱jñaṃ vi̍ṣṭā̱ra o̍hate

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 11

adhā̱ naro̱ nyo̍ha̱te'dhā̍ ni̱yuta̍ ohate |

adhā̱ pārā̍vatā̱ iti̍ ci̱trā rū̱pāṇi̱ darśyā̍

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 12

cha̱nda̱:stubha̍: kubha̱nyava̱ utsa̱mā kī̱riṇo̍ nṛtuḥ |

te me̱ ke ci̱nna tā̱yava̱ ūmā̍ āsan dṛ̱śi tvi̱ṣe

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 13

ya ṛ̱ṣvā ṛ̱ṣṭivi̍dyutaḥ ka̱vaya̱: santi̍ ve̱dhasa̍: |

tamṛ̍ṣe̱ māru̍taṃ ga̱ṇaṃ na̍ma̱syā ra̱mayā̍ gi̱rā

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 14

accha̍ ṛṣe̱ māru̍taṃ ga̱ṇaṃ dā̱nā mi̱traṃ na yo̱ṣaṇā̍ |

di̱vo vā̍ dhṛṣṇava̱ oja̍sā stu̱tā dhī̱bhiri̍ṣaṇyata

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 15

nū ma̍nvā̱na e̍ṣāṃ de̱vām̐ acchā̱ na va̱kṣaṇā̍ |

dā̱nā sa̍ceta sū̱ribhi̱ryāma̍śrutebhira̱ñjibhi̍:

---

## Verse 16

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 16

pra ye me̍ bandhve̱ṣe gāṃ voca̍nta sū̱raya̱: pṛśni̍ṃ vocanta mā̱tara̍m |

adhā̍ pi̱tara̍mi̱ṣmiṇa̍ṃ ru̱draṃ vo̍canta̱ śikva̍saḥ

---

## Verse 17

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 17

ya̱munā̍yā̱madhi̍ śru̱tamud rādho̱ gavya̍ṃ mṛje̱ ni rādho̱ aśvya̍ṃ mṛje
