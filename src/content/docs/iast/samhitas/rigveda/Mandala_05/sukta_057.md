# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 57

**8 śyāvāśva ātreyaḥ| marutaḥ| jagatī, 7-8 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 1

ā ru̍drāsa̱ indra̍vantaḥ sa̱joṣa̍so̱ hira̍ṇyarathāḥ suvi̱tāya̍ gantana |
i̱yaṃ vo̍ a̱smat prati̍ haryate ma̱tistṛ̱ṣṇaje̱ na di̱va utsā̍ uda̱nyave̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 2

vāśī̍manta ṛṣṭi̱manto̍ manī̱ṣiṇa̍: su̱dhanvā̍na̱ iṣu̍manto niṣa̱ṅgiṇa̍: |

svaśvā̍: stha su̱rathā̍: pṛśnimātaraḥ svāyu̱dhā ma̍ruto yāthanā̱ śubha̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 3

dhū̱nu̱tha dyāṃ parva̍tān dā̱śuṣe̱ vasu̱ ni vo̱ vanā̍ jihate̱ yāma̍no bhi̱yā |

ko̱paya̍tha pṛthi̱vīṃ pṛ̍śnimātaraḥ śu̱bhe yadu̍grā̱: pṛṣa̍tī̱rayu̍gdhvam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 4

vāta̍tviṣo ma̱ruto̍ va̱rṣani̍rṇijo ya̱mā i̍va̱ susa̍dṛśaḥ su̱peśa̍saḥ |

pi̱śaṅgā̍śvā aru̱ṇāśvā̍ are̱pasa̱: pratva̍kṣaso mahi̱nā dyauri̍vo̱rava̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 5

pu̱ru̱dra̱psā a̍ñji̱manta̍: su̱dāna̍vastve̱ṣasa̍ṃdṛśo anava̱bhrarā̍dhasaḥ |

su̱jā̱tāso̍ ja̱nuṣā̍ ru̱kmava̍kṣaso di̱vo a̱rkā a̱mṛta̱ṃ nāma̍ bhejire

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 6

ṛ̱ṣṭayo̍ vo maruto̱ aṃsa̍yo̱radhi̱ saha̱ ojo̍ bā̱hvorvo̱ bala̍ṃ hi̱tam |

nṛ̱mṇā śī̱rṣasvāyu̍dhā̱ rathe̍ṣu vo̱ viśvā̍ va̱: śrīradhi̍ ta̱nūṣu̍ pipiśe

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 7

goma̱daśvā̍va̱d ratha̍vat su̱vīra̍ṃ ca̱ndrava̱d rādho̍ maruto dadā naḥ |

praśa̍stiṃ naḥ kṛṇuta rudriyāso bhakṣī̱ya vo'va̍so̱ daivya̍sya

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 57 | **Verse:** 8

ha̱ye naro̱ maru̍to mṛ̱l̤atā̍ na̱stuvī̍maghāso̱ amṛ̍tā̱ ṛta̍jñāḥ |

satya̍śruta̱: kava̍yo̱ yuvā̍no̱ bṛha̍dgirayo bṛ̱hadu̱kṣamā̍ṇāḥ
