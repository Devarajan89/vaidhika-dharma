# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 72

**3 bāhuvṛkta ātreyaḥ| mitrāvaruṇau| uṣṇik|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 1

ā mi̱tre varu̍ṇe va̱yaṃ gī̱rbhirju̍humo atri̱vat | ni ba̱rhiṣi̍ sadata̱ṃ soma̍pītaye

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 2

vra̱tena̍ stho dhru̱vakṣe̍mā̱ dharma̍ṇā yāta̱yajja̍nā | ni ba̱rhiṣi̍ sadata̱ṃ soma̍pītaye

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 72 | **Verse:** 3

mi̱traśca̍ no̱ varu̍ṇaśca ju̱ṣetā̍ṃ ya̱jñami̱ṣṭaye̍ | ni ba̱rhiṣi̍ sadatā̱ṃ soma̍pītaye
