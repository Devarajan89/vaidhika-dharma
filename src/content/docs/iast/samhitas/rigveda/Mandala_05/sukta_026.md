# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 26

**9 vasūyava ātreyāḥ| agniḥ, 9viśve devāḥ | gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 1

agne̍ pāvaka ro̱ciṣā̍ ma̱ndrayā̍ deva ji̱hvayā̍ | ā de̱vān va̍kṣi̱ yakṣi̍ ca

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 2

taṃ tvā̍ ghṛtasnavīmahe̱ citra̍bhāno sva̱rdṛśa̍m | de̱vām̐ ā vī̱taye̍ vaha

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 3

vī̱tiho̍traṃ tvā kave dyu̱manta̱ṃ sami̍dhīmahi | agne̍ bṛ̱hanta̍madhva̱re

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 4

agne̱ viśve̍bhi̱rā ga̍hi de̱vebhi̍rha̱vyadā̍taye | hotā̍raṃ tvā vṛṇīmahe

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 5

yaja̍mānāya sunva̱ta āgne̍ su̱vīrya̍ṃ vaha | de̱vairā sa̍tsi ba̱rhiṣi̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 6

sa̱mi̱dhā̱naḥ sa̍hasraji̱dagne̱ dharmā̍ṇi puṣyasi | de̱vānā̍ṃ dū̱ta u̱kthya̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 7

nya1gniṃ jā̱tave̍dasaṃ hotra̱vāha̱ṃ yavi̍ṣṭhyam | dadhā̍tā de̱vamṛ̱tvija̍m

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 8

pra ya̱jña e̍tvānu̱ṣaga̱dyā de̱vavya̍castamaḥ | stṛ̱ṇī̱ta ba̱rhirā̱sade̍

---

## Verse 9
