# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 23

**4 dyumno viśvacarṣaṇirātreyaḥ | agniḥ| anuṣṭup, 4 paṃktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 1

agne̱ saha̍nta̱mā bha̍ra dyu̱mnasya̍ prā̱sahā̍ ra̱yim | viśvā̱ yaśca̍rṣa̱ṇīra̱bhyā̱3sā vāje̍ṣu sā̱saha̍t

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 2

tama̍gne pṛtanā̱ṣaha̍ṃ ra̱yiṃ sa̍hasva̱ ā bha̍ra | tvaṃ hi sa̱tyo adbhu̍to dā̱tā vāja̍sya̱ goma̍taḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 3

viśve̱ hi tvā̍ sa̱joṣa̍so̱ janā̍so vṛ̱ktaba̍rhiṣaḥ | hotā̍ra̱ṃ sadma̍su pri̱yaṃ vyanti̱ vāryā̍ pu̱ru

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 4

sa hi ṣmā̍ vi̱śvaca̍rṣaṇira̱bhimā̍ti̱ saho̍ da̱dhe |

agna̍ e̱ṣu kṣaye̱ṣvā re̱vanna̍: śukra dīdihi dyu̱mat pā̍vaka dīdihi
