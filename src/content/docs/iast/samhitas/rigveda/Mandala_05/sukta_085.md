# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 85

**8 bhaumo'triḥ| varuṇa| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 1

pra sa̱mrāje̍ bṛ̱hada̍rcā gabhī̱raṃ brahma̍ pri̱yaṃ varu̍ṇāya śru̱tāya̍ |
vi yo ja̱ghāna̍ śami̱teva̱ carmo̍pa̱stire̍ pṛthi̱vīṃ sūryā̍ya

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 2

vane̍ṣu̱ vya1ntari̍kṣaṃ tatāna̱ vāja̱marva̍tsu̱ paya̍ u̱sriyā̍su |

hṛ̱tsu kratu̱ṃ varu̍ṇo a̱psva1gniṃ di̱vi sūrya̍madadhā̱t soma̱madrau̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 3

nī̱cīna̍bāra̱ṃ varu̍ṇa̱: kava̍ndha̱ṃ pra sa̍sarja̱ roda̍sī a̱ntari̍kṣam |

tena̱ viśva̍sya̱ bhuva̍nasya̱ rājā̱ yava̱ṃ na vṛ̱ṣṭirvyu̍natti̱ bhūma̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 4

u̱natti̱ bhūmi̍ṃ pṛthi̱vīmu̱ta dyāṃ ya̱dā du̱gdhaṃ varu̍ṇo̱ vaṣṭyādit |

sama̱bhreṇa̍ vasata̱ parva̍tāsastaviṣī̱yanta̍: śrathayanta vī̱rāḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 5

i̱māmū̱ ṣvā̍su̱rasya̍ śru̱tasya̍ ma̱hīṃ mā̱yāṃ varu̍ṇasya̱ pra vo̍cam |

māne̍neva tasthi̱vām̐ a̱ntari̍kṣe̱ vi yo ma̱me pṛ̍thi̱vīṃ sūrye̍ṇa

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 6

i̱māmū̱ nu ka̱vita̍masya mā̱yāṃ ma̱hīṃ de̱vasya̱ naki̱rā da̍dharṣa |

eka̱ṃ yadu̱dnā na pṛ̱ṇantyenī̍rāsi̱ñcantī̍ra̱vana̍yaḥ samu̱dram

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 7

a̱rya̱mya̍ṃ varuṇa mi̱trya̍ṃ vā̱ sakhā̍yaṃ vā̱ sada̱mid bhrāta̍raṃ vā |

ve̱śaṃ vā̱ nitya̍ṃ varu̱ṇāra̍ṇaṃ vā̱ yat sī̱māga̍ścakṛ̱mā śi̱śratha̱stat

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 85 | **Verse:** 8

ki̱ta̱vāso̱ yad ri̍ri̱purna dī̱vi yad vā̍ ghā sa̱tyamu̱ta yanna vi̱dma |

sarvā̱ tā vi ṣya̍ śithi̱reva̍ de̱vā'dhā̍ te syāma varuṇa pri̱yāsa̍:
