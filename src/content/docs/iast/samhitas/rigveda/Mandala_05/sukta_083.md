# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 83

**10 bhaumo'triḥ| parjanyaḥ| triṣṭup, 2-4 jagatī, 9 anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 1

acchā̍ vada ta̱vasa̍ṃ gī̱rbhirā̱bhiḥ stu̱hi pa̱rjanya̱ṃ nama̱sā vi̍vāsa |
kani̍kradad vṛṣa̱bho jī̱radā̍nū̱ reto̍ dadhā̱tyoṣa̍dhīṣu̱ garbha̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 2

vi vṛ̱kṣān ha̍ntyu̱ta ha̍nti ra̱kṣaso̱ viśva̍ṃ bibhāya̱ bhuva̍naṃ ma̱hāva̍dhāt |

u̱tānā̍gā īṣate̱ vṛṣṇyā̍vato̱ yat pa̱rjanya̍: sta̱naya̱nhanti̍ du̱ṣkṛta̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 3

ra̱thīva̱ kaśa̱yāśvā̍m̐ abhikṣi̱pannā̱virdū̱tān kṛ̍ṇute va̱rṣyā̱m̐3 aha̍ |

dū̱rāt si̱ṃhasya̍ sta̱nathā̱ udī̍rate̱ yat pa̱rjanya̍: kṛṇu̱te va̱rṣyaṃ1 nabha̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 4

pra vātā̱ vānti̍ pa̱taya̍nti vi̱dyuta̱ udoṣa̍dhī̱rjiha̍te̱ pinva̍te̱ sva̍: |

irā̱ viśva̍smai̱ bhuva̍nāya jāyate̱ yat pa̱rjanya̍: pṛthi̱vīṃ reta̱sāva̍ti

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 5

yasya̍ vra̱te pṛ̍thi̱vī nanna̍mīti̱ yasya̍ vra̱te śa̱phava̱jjarbhu̍rīti |

yasya̍ vra̱ta oṣa̍dhīrvi̱śvarū̍pā̱: sa na̍: parjanya̱ mahi̱ śarma̍ yaccha

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 6

di̱vo no̍ vṛ̱ṣṭiṃ ma̍ruto rarīdhva̱ṃ pra pi̍nvata̱ vṛṣṇo̱ aśva̍sya̱ dhārā̍: |

a̱rvāṅe̱tena̍ stanayi̱tnunehya̱po ni̍ṣi̱ñcannasu̍raḥ pi̱tā na̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 7

a̱bhi kra̍nda sta̱naya̱ garbha̱mā dhā̍ uda̱nvatā̱ pari̍ dīyā̱ rathe̍na |

dṛti̱ṃ su ka̍rṣa̱ viṣi̍ta̱ṃ nya̍ñcaṃ sa̱mā bha̍vantū̱dvato̍ nipā̱dāḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 8

ma̱hānta̱ṃ kośa̱muda̍cā̱ ni ṣi̍ñca̱ syanda̍ntāṃ ku̱lyā viṣi̍tāḥ pu̱rastā̍t |

ghṛ̱tena̱ dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī vyu̍ndhi suprapā̱ṇaṃ bha̍vatva̱ghnyābhya̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 9

yat pa̍rjanya̱ kani̍kradat sta̱naya̱nhaṃsi̍ du̱ṣkṛta̍: |

pratī̱daṃ viśva̍ṃ modate̱ yat kiṃ ca̍ pṛthi̱vyāmadhi̍

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 83 | **Verse:** 10

ava̍rṣīrva̱rṣamudu̱ ṣū gṛ̍bhā̱yā'ka̱rdhanvā̱nyatye̍ta̱vā u̍ |

ajī̍jana̱ oṣa̍dhī̱rbhoja̍nāya̱ kamu̱ta pra̱jābhyo̍'vido manī̱ṣām
