## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 2

saṃ bhā̱nunā̍ yatate̱ sūrya̍syā̱''juhvā̍no ghṛ̱tapṛ̍ṣṭha̱: svañcā̍: |
tasmā̱ amṛ̍dhrā u̱ṣaso̱ vyu̍cchā̱n ya indrā̍ya su̱navā̱metyāha̍ || 1||
