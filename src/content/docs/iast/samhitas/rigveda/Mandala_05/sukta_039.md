# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 39

**5 bhaumo'triḥ| indraḥ| anuṣṭup, 5paṅktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 1

yadi̍ndra citra me̱hanāsti̱ tvādā̍tamadrivaḥ |
rādha̱stanno̍ vidadvasa ubhayāha̱styā bha̍ra

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 2

yanmanya̍se̱ vare̍ṇya̱mindra̍ dyu̱kṣaṃ tadā bha̍ra |

vi̱dyāma̱ tasya̍ te va̱yamakū̍pārasya dā̱vane̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 3

yat te̍ di̱tsu pra̱rādhya̱ṃ mano̱ asti̍ śru̱taṃ bṛ̱hat |

tena̍ dṛ̱l̤ahā ci̍dadriva̱ ā vāja̍ṃ darṣi sā̱taye̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 4

maṃhi̍ṣṭhaṃ vo ma̱ghonā̱ṃ rājā̍naṃ carṣaṇī̱nām |

indra̱mupa̱ praśa̍staye pū̱rvībhi̍rjujuṣe̱ gira̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 5

asmā̱ it kāvya̱ṃ vaca̍ u̱kthamindrā̍ya̱ śaṃsya̍m |

tasmā̍ u̱ brahma̍vāhase̱ giro̍ vardha̱ntyatra̍yo̱ gira̍: śumbha̱ntyatra̍yaḥ
