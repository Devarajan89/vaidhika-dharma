# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 28

**6 viśvavārātreyī| agniḥ| 1, 3 triṣṭup, 2 jagatī, 4 anuṣṭup, 5-6 gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 1

sami̍ddho a̱gnirdi̱vi śo̱cira̍śret pra̱tyaṅṅu̱ṣasa̍murvi̱yā vi bhā̍ti |
eti̱ prācī̍ vi̱śvavā̍rā̱ namo̍bhirde̱vām̐ īl̤ā̍nā ha̱viṣā̍ ghṛ̱tācī̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 2

sa̱mi̱dhyamā̍no a̱mṛta̍sya rājasi ha̱viṣkṛ̱ṇvanta̍ṃ sacase sva̱staye̍ |

viśva̱ṃ sa dha̍tte̱ dravi̍ṇa̱ṃ yaminva̍syāti̱thyama̍gne̱ ni ca̍ dhatta̱ it pu̱raḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 3

agne̱ śardha̍ maha̱te saubha̍gāya̱ tava̍ dyu̱mnānyu̍tta̱māni̍ santu |

saṃ jā̍spa̱tyaṃ su̱yama̱mā kṛ̍ṇuṣva śatrūya̱tāma̱bhi ti̍ṣṭhā̱ mahā̍ṃsi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 4

sami̍ddhasya̱ prama̍ha̱so'gne̱ vande̱ tava̱ śriya̍m |

vṛ̱ṣa̱bho dyu̱mnavā̍m̐ asi̱ sama̍dhva̱reṣvi̍dhyase

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 5

sami̍ddho agna āhuta de̱vān ya̍kṣi svadhvara | tvaṃ hi ha̍vya̱vāl̤asi̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 6

ā ju̍hotā duva̱syatā̱'gniṃ pra̍ya̱tya̍dhva̱re | vṛ̱ṇī̱dhvaṃ ha̍vya̱vāha̍nam
