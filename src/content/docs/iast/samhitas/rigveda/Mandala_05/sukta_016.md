# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 16

**5 pūrurātreyaḥ| agniḥ| anuṣṭup, 5 paṃktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 1

bṛ̱had vayo̱ hi bhā̱nave'rcā̍ de̱vāyā̱gnaye̍ |
yaṃ mi̱traṃ na praśa̍stibhi̱rmartā̍so dadhi̱re pu̱raḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 2

sa hi dyubhi̱rjanā̍nā̱ṃ hotā̱ dakṣa̍sya bā̱hvoḥ |

vi ha̱vyama̱gnirā̍nu̱ṣagbhago̱ na vāra̍mṛṇvati

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 3

a̱sya stome̍ ma̱ghona̍: sa̱khye vṛ̱ddhaśo̍ciṣaḥ |

viśvā̱ yasmi̍n tuvi̱ṣvaṇi̱ sama̱rye śuṣma̍māda̱dhuḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 4

adhā̱ hya̍gna eṣāṃ su̱vīrya̍sya ma̱ṃhanā̍ |

tamid ya̱hvaṃ na roda̍sī̱ pari̱ śravo̍ babhūvatuḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 5

nū na̱ ehi̱ vārya̱magne̍ gṛṇā̱na ā bha̍ra |

ye va̱yaṃ ye ca̍ sū̱raya̍: sva̱sti dhāma̍he̱ saco̱taidhi̍ pṛ̱tsu no̍ vṛ̱dhe
