# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 35

**8 prabhūvasurāṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ | anuṣṭup, 8 paṅktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 1

yaste̱ sādhi̱ṣṭho'va̍sa̱ indra̱ kratu̱ṣṭamā bha̍ra |
a̱smabhya̍ṃ carṣaṇī̱saha̱ṃ sasni̱ṃ vāje̍ṣu du̱ṣṭara̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 2

yadi̍ndra te̱ cata̍sro̱ yacchū̍ra̱ santi̍ ti̱sraḥ |

yad vā̱ pañca̍ kṣitī̱nāmava̱stat su na̱ ā bha̍ra

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 3

ā te'vo̱ vare̍ṇya̱ṃ vṛṣa̍ntamasya hūmahe |

vṛṣa̍jūti̱rhi ja̍jñi̱ṣa ā̱bhūbhi̍rindra tu̱rvaṇi̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 4

vṛṣā̱ hyasi̱ rādha̍se jajñi̱ṣe vṛṣṇi̍ te̱ śava̍: |

svakṣa̍traṃ te dhṛ̱ṣanmana̍: satrā̱hami̍ndra̱ pauṃsya̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 5

tvaṃ tami̍ndra̱ martya̍mamitra̱yanta̍madrivaḥ |

sa̱rva̱ra̱thā śa̍takrato̱ ni yā̍hi śavasaspate

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 6

tvāmid vṛ̍trahantama̱ janā̍so vṛ̱ktaba̍rhiṣaḥ |

u̱graṃ pū̱rvīṣu̍ pū̱rvyaṃ hava̍nte̱ vāja̍sātaye

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 7

a̱smāka̍mindra du̱ṣṭara̍ṃ puro̱yāvā̍namā̱jiṣu̍ |

sa̱yāvā̍na̱ṃ dhane̍dhane vāja̱yanta̍mavā̱ ratha̍m

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 8

a̱smāka̍mi̱ndrehi̍ no̱ ratha̍mavā̱ pura̍ṃdhyā |

va̱yaṃ śa̍viṣṭha̱ vārya̍ṃ di̱vi śravo̍ dadhīmahi di̱vi stoma̍ṃ manāmahe
