# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 25

**9 vasūyava ātreyāḥ| agniḥ | anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 1

acchā̍ vo a̱gnimava̍se de̱vaṃ gā̍si̱ sa no̱ vasu̍: |
rāsa̍t pu̱tra ṛ̍ṣū̱ṇāmṛ̱tāvā̍ parṣati dvi̱ṣaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 2

sa hi sa̱tyo yaṃ pūrve̍ cid de̱vāsa̍ści̱d yamī̍dhi̱re |

hotā̍raṃ ma̱ndraji̍hva̱mit su̍dī̱tibhi̍rvi̱bhāva̍sum

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 3

sa no̍ dhī̱tī vari̍ṣṭhayā̱ śreṣṭha̍yā ca suma̱tyā |

agne̍ rā̱yo di̍dīhi naḥ suvṛ̱ktibhi̍rvareṇya

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 4

a̱gnirde̱veṣu̍ rājatya̱gnirmarte̍ṣvāvi̱śan |

a̱gnirno̍ havya̱vāha̍no̱'gniṃ dhī̱bhiḥ sa̍paryata

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 5

a̱gnistu̱viśra̍vastamaṃ tu̱vibra̍hmāṇamutta̱mam |

a̱tūrta̍ṃ śrāva̱yatpa̍tiṃ pu̱traṃ da̍dāti dā̱śuṣe̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 6

a̱gnirda̍dāti̱ satpa̍tiṃ sā̱sāha̱ yo yu̱dhā nṛbhi̍: |

a̱gniratya̍ṃ raghu̱ṣyada̱ṃ jetā̍ra̱mapa̍rājitam

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 7

yad vāhi̍ṣṭha̱ṃ tada̱gnaye̍ bṛ̱hada̍rca vibhāvaso |

mahi̍ṣīva̱ tvad ra̱yistvad vājā̱ udī̍rate

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 8

tava̍ dyu̱manto̍ a̱rcayo̱ grāve̍vocyate bṛ̱hat |

u̱to te̍ tanya̱turya̍thā svā̱no a̍rta̱ tmanā̍ di̱vaḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 9

e̱vām̐ a̱gniṃ va̍sū̱yava̍: sahasā̱naṃ va̍vandima |

sa no̱ viśvā̱ ati̱ dviṣa̱: parṣa̍nnā̱veva̍ su̱kratu̍:
