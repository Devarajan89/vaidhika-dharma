# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 62

**9 śrutavidātreyaḥ| mitrāvaruṇau| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 1

ṛ̱tena̍ ṛ̱tamapi̍hitaṃ dhru̱vaṃ vā̱ṃ sūrya̍sya̱ yatra̍ vimu̱cantyaśvā̍n |
daśa̍ śa̱tā sa̱ha ta̍sthu̱stadeka̍ṃ de̱vānā̱ṃ śreṣṭha̱ṃ vapu̍ṣāmapaśyam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 2

tat su vā̍ṃ mitrāvaruṇā mahi̱tvamī̱rmā ta̱sthuṣī̱raha̍bhirduduhre |

viśvā̍: pinvatha̱: svasa̍rasya̱ dhenā̱ anu̍ vā̱meka̍: pa̱virā va̍varta

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 3

adhā̍rayataṃ pṛthi̱vīmu̱ta dyāṃ mitra̍rājānā varuṇā̱ maho̍bhiḥ |

va̱rdhaya̍ta̱moṣa̍dhī̱: pinva̍ta̱ṃ gā ava̍ vṛ̱ṣṭiṃ sṛ̍jataṃ jīradānū

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 4

ā vā̱maśvā̍saḥ su̱yujo̍ vahantu ya̱tara̍śmaya̱ upa̍ yantva̱rvāk |

ghṛ̱tasya̍ ni̱rṇiganu̍ vartate vā̱mupa̱ sindha̍vaḥ pra̱divi̍ kṣaranti

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 5

anu̍ śru̱tāma̱mati̱ṃ vardha̍du̱rvīṃ ba̱rhiri̍va̱ yaju̍ṣā̱ rakṣa̍māṇā |

nama̍svantā dhṛtada̱kṣādhi̱ garte̱ mitrāsā̍the varu̱ṇel̤ā̍sva̱ntaḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 6

akra̍vihastā su̱kṛte̍ para̱spā yaṃ trāsā̍the varu̱ṇel̤ā̍sva̱ntaḥ |

rājā̍nā kṣa̱tramahṛ̍ṇīyamānā sa̱hasra̍sthūṇaṃ bibhṛthaḥ sa̱ha dvau

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 7

hira̍ṇyanirṇi̱gayo̍ asya̱ sthūṇā̱ vi bhrā̍jate di̱vya1śvāja̍nīva |

bha̱dre kṣetre̱ nimi̍tā̱ tilvi̍le vā sa̱nema̱ madhvo̱ adhi̍gartyasya

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 8

hira̍ṇyarūpamu̱ṣaso̱ vyu̍ṣṭā̱vaya̍:sthūṇa̱mudi̍tā̱ sūrya̍sya |

ā ro̍hatho varuṇa mitra̱ garta̱mata̍ścakṣāthe̱ adi̍ti̱ṃ diti̍ṃ ca

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 9

yad baṃhi̍ṣṭha̱ṃ nāti̱vidhe̍ sudānū̱ acchi̍dra̱ṃ śarma̍ bhuvanasya gopā |

tena̍ no mitrāvaruṇāvaviṣṭa̱ṃ siṣā̍santo jigī̱vāṃsa̍: syāma
