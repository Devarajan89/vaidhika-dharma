# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 48

**5 pratibhānurātreyaḥ| viśve devāḥ| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 1

kadu̍ pri̱yāya̱ dhāmne̍ manāmahe̱ svakṣa̍trāya̱ svaya̍śase ma̱he va̱yam |
ā̱me̱nyasya̱ raja̍so̱ yada̱bhra ām̐ a̱po vṛ̍ṇā̱nā vi̍ta̱noti̍ mā̱yinī̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 2

tā a̍tnata va̱yuna̍ṃ vī̱rava̍kṣaṇaṃ samā̱nyā vṛ̱tayā̱ viśva̱mā raja̍: |

apo̱ apā̍cī̱rapa̍rā̱ ape̍jate̱ pra pūrvā̍bhistirate deva̱yurjana̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 3

ā grāva̍bhiraha̱nye̍bhira̱ktubhi̱rvari̍ṣṭha̱ṃ vajra̱mā ji̍gharti mā̱yini̍ |

śa̱taṃ vā̱ yasya̍ pra̱cara̱n tsve dame̍ saṃva̱rtaya̍nto̱ vi ca̍ vartaya̱nnahā̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 48 | **Verse:** 4

tāma̍sya rī̱tiṃ pa̍ra̱śori̍va̱ pratyanī̍kamakhyaṃ bhu̱je a̍sya̱ varpa̍saḥ |

sacā̱ yadi̍ pitu̱manta̍miva̱ kṣaya̱ṃ ratna̱ṃ dadhā̍ti̱ bhara̍hūtaye vi̱śe

## Verse 5
