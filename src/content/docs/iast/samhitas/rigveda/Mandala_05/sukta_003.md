# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 3

**12 vasuśruta ātreyaḥ| agniḥ, 3 marudrudraviṣṇavaḥ| triṣṭup, 1 virāṭ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 1

tvama̍gne̱ varu̍ṇo̱ jāya̍se̱ yattvaṃ mi̱tro bha̍vasi̱ yatsami̍ddhaḥ |
tve viśve̍ sahasasputra de̱vāstvamindro̍ dā̱śuṣe̱ martyā̍ya

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 2

tvama̍rya̱mā bha̍vasi̱ yat ka̱nīnā̱ṃ nāma̍ svadhāva̱n guhya̍ṃ bibharṣi |

a̱ñjanti̍ mi̱traṃ sudhi̍ta̱ṃ na gobhi̱ryad dampa̍tī̱ sama̍nasā kṛ̱ṇoṣi̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 3

tava̍ śri̱ye ma̱ruto̍ marjayanta̱ rudra̱ yat te̱ jani̍ma̱ cāru̍ ci̱tram |

pa̱daṃ yad viṣṇo̍rupa̱maṃ ni̱dhāyi̱ tena̍ pāsi̱ guhya̱ṃ nāma̱ gonā̍m

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 4

tava̍ śri̱yā su̱dṛśo̍ deva de̱vāḥ pu̱rū dadhā̍nā a̱mṛta̍ṃ sapanta |

hotā̍rama̱gniṃ manu̍ṣo̱ ni ṣe̍durdaśa̱syanta̍ u̱śija̱: śaṃsa̍mā̱yoḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 5

na tvaddhotā̱ pūrvo̍ agne̱ yajī̍yā̱n na kāvyai̍: pa̱ro a̍sti svadhāvaḥ |

vi̱śaśca̱ yasyā̱ ati̍thi̱rbhavā̍si̱ sa ya̱jñena̍ vanavad deva̱ martā̍n

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 6

va̱yama̍gne vanuyāma̱ tvotā̍ vasū̱yavo̍ ha̱viṣā̱ budhya̍mānāḥ |

va̱yaṃ sa̍ma̱rye vi̱dathe̱ṣvahnā̍ṃ va̱yaṃ rā̱yā sa̍hasasputra̱ martā̍n

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 7

yo na̱ āgo̍ a̱bhyeno̱ bharā̱tyadhīda̱ghama̱ghaśa̍ṃse dadhāta |

ja̱hī ci̍kitvo a̱bhiśa̍stime̱tāmagne̱ yo no̍ ma̱rcaya̍ti dva̱yena̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 8

tvāma̱syā vyuṣi̍ deva̱ pūrve̍ dū̱taṃ kṛ̍ṇvā̱nā a̍yajanta ha̱vyaiḥ |

sa̱ṃsthe yada̍gna̱ īya̍se rayī̱ṇāṃ de̱vo martai̱rvasu̍bhiri̱dhyamā̍naḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 9

ava̍ spṛdhi pi̱tara̱ṃ yodhi̍ vi̱dvān pu̱tro yaste̍ sahasaḥ sūna ū̱he |

ka̱dā ci̍kitvo a̱bhi ca̍kṣase̱ no'gne̍ ka̱dām̐ ṛ̍ta̱cid yā̍tayāse

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 10

bhūri̱ nāma̱ vanda̍māno dadhāti pi̱tā va̍so̱ yadi̱ tajjo̱ṣayā̍se |

ku̱vid de̱vasya̱ saha̍sā cakā̱naḥ su̱mnama̱gnirva̍nate vāvṛdhā̱naḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 11

tvama̱ṅga ja̍ri̱tāra̍ṃ yaviṣṭha̱ viśvā̍nyagne duri̱tāti̍ parṣi |

ste̱nā a̍dṛśran ri̱pavo̱ janā̱so'jñā̍taketā vṛji̱nā a̍bhūvan

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 12

i̱me yāmā̍sastva̱driga̍bhūva̱n vasa̍ve vā̱ tadidāgo̍ avāci |

nāhā̱yama̱gnira̱bhiśa̍staye no̱ na rīṣa̍te vāvṛdhā̱naḥ parā̍ dāt
