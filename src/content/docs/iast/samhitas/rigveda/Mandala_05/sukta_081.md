# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 81

**5 śyāvāśva ātreyaḥ| savitā| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 1

yu̱ñjate̱ mana̍ u̱ta yu̍ñjate̱ dhiyo̱ viprā̱ vipra̍sya bṛha̱to vi̍pa̱ścita̍: |
vi hotrā̍ dadhe vayunā̱videka̱ inma̱hī de̱vasya̍ savi̱tuḥ pari̍ṣṭutiḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 2

viśvā̍ rū̱pāṇi̱ prati̍ muñcate ka̱viḥ prāsā̍vīd bha̱draṃ dvi̱pade̱ catu̍ṣpade |

vi nāka̍makhyat savi̱tā vare̱ṇyo'nu̍ pra̱yāṇa̍mu̱ṣaso̱ vi rā̍jati

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 3

yasya̍ pra̱yāṇa̱manva̱nya id ya̱yurde̱vā de̱vasya̍ mahi̱māna̱moja̍sā |

yaḥ pārthi̍vāni vima̱me sa eta̍śo̱ rajā̍ṃsi de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā ma̍hitva̱nā

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 4

u̱ta yā̍si savita̱strīṇi̍ roca̱nota sūrya̍sya ra̱śmibhi̱: samu̍cyasi |

u̱ta rātrī̍mubha̱yata̱: parī̍yasa u̱ta mi̱tro bha̍vasi deva̱ dharma̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 81 | **Verse:** 5

u̱teśi̍ṣe prasa̱vasya̱ tvameka̱ idu̱ta pū̱ṣā bha̍vasi deva̱ yāma̍bhiḥ |

u̱tedaṃ viśva̱ṃ bhuva̍na̱ṃ vi rā̍jasi śyā̱vāśva̍ste savita̱ stoma̍mānaśe
