# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 12

**6 sutaṃbhara ātreyaḥ| agniḥ | triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 1

prāgnaye̍ bṛha̱te ya̱jñiyā̍ya ṛ̱tasya̱ vṛṣṇe̱ asu̍rāya̱ manma̍ |
ghṛ̱taṃ na ya̱jña ā̱sye̱3 supū̍ta̱ṃ gira̍ṃ bhare vṛṣa̱bhāya̍ pratī̱cīm

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 2

ṛ̱taṃ ci̍kitva ṛ̱tamicci̍kiddhyṛ̱tasya̱ dhārā̱ anu̍ tṛndhi pū̱rvīḥ |

nāhaṃ yā̱tuṃ saha̍sā̱ na dva̱yena̍ ṛ̱taṃ sa̍pāmyaru̱ṣasya̱ vṛṣṇa̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 3

kayā̍ no agna ṛ̱taya̍nnṛ̱tena̱ bhuvo̱ nave̍dā u̱catha̍sya̱ navya̍: |

vedā̍ me de̱va ṛ̍tu̱pā ṛ̍tū̱nāṃ nāhaṃ pati̍ṃ sani̱tura̱sya rā̱yaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 4

ke te̍ agne ri̱pave̱ bandha̍nāsa̱: ke pā̱yava̍: saniṣanta dyu̱manta̍: |

ke dhā̱sima̍gne̱ anṛ̍tasya pānti̱ ka āsa̍to̱ vaca̍saḥ santi go̱pāḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 5

sakhā̍yaste̱ viṣu̍ṇā agna e̱te śi̱vāsa̱: santo̱ aśi̍vā abhūvan |

adhū̍rṣata sva̱yame̱te vaco̍bhirṛjūya̱te vṛ̍ji̱nāni̍ bru̱vanta̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 6

yaste̍ agne̱ nama̍sā ya̱jñamīṭṭa̍ ṛ̱taṃ sa pā̍tyaru̱ṣasya̱ vṛṣṇa̍: |

tasya̱ kṣaya̍: pṛ̱thurā sā̱dhure̍tu pra̱sarsrā̍ṇasya̱ nahu̍ṣasya̱ śeṣa̍:
