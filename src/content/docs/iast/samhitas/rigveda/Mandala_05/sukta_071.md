# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 71

**3 bāhuvṛkta ātreyaḥ| mitrāvaruṇau| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 1

ā no̍ gantaṃ riśādasā̱ varu̍ṇa̱ mitra̍ ba̱rhaṇā̍ | upe̱maṃ cāru̍madhva̱ram

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 2

viśva̍sya̱ hi pra̍cetasā̱ varu̍ṇa̱ mitra̱ rāja̍thaḥ | ī̱śā̱nā pi̍pyata̱ṃ dhiya̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 71 | **Verse:** 3

upa̍ naḥ su̱tamā ga̍ta̱ṃ varu̍ṇa̱ mitra̍ dā̱śuṣa̍: | a̱sya soma̍sya pī̱taye̍
