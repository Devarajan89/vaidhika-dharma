# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 18

**5 dvito mṛktavāhā ātreyaḥ| agniḥ| anuṣṭup, 5 paṃktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 1

prā̱tara̱gniḥ pu̍rupri̱yo vi̱śaḥ sta̍ve̱tāti̍thiḥ |
viśvā̍ni̱ yo ama̍rtyo ha̱vyā marte̍ṣu̱ raṇya̍ti

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 2

dvi̱tāya̍ mṛ̱ktavā̍hase̱ svasya̱ dakṣa̍sya ma̱ṃhanā̍ |

indu̱ṃ  sa dha̍tta ānu̱ṣak sto̱tā ci̍tte amartya

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 3

taṃ vo̍ dī̱rghāyu̍śociṣaṃ gi̱rā hu̍ve ma̱ghonā̍m |

ari̍ṣṭo̱ yeṣā̱ṃ ratho̱ vya̍śvadāva̱nnīya̍te

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 4

ci̱trā vā̱ yeṣu̱ dīdhi̍tirā̱sannu̱kthā pānti̱ ye |

stī̱rṇaṃ ba̱rhiḥ sva̍rṇare̱ śravā̍ṃsi dadhire̱ pari̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 5

ye me̍ pañcā̱śata̍ṃ da̱duraśvā̍nāṃ sa̱dhastu̍ti |

dyu̱mada̍gne̱ mahi̱ śravo̍ bṛ̱hat kṛ̍dhi ma̱ghonā̍ṃ nṛ̱vada̍mṛta nṛ̱ṇām
