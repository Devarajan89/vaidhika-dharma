# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 1

**12 budhagaviṣṭhirāvātreyau| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 1

abo̍dhya̱gniḥ sa̱midhā̱ janā̍nā̱ṃ prati̍ dhe̱numi̍vāya̱tīmu̱ṣāsa̍m| ya̱hvā i̍va̱ pra va̱yāmu̱jjihā̍nā̱: pra bhā̱nava̍: sisrate̱ nāka̱maccha̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 2

abo̍dhi̱ hotā̍ ya̱jathā̍ya de̱vānū̱rdhvo a̱gniḥ su̱manā̍: prā̱tara̍sthāt | sami̍ddhasya̱ ruśa̍dadarśi̱ pājo̍ ma̱hān de̱vastama̍so̱ nira̍moci

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 3

yadī̍ṃ ga̱ṇasya̍ raśa̱nāmajī̍ga̱: śuci̍raṅkte̱ śuci̍bhi̱rgobhi̍ra̱gniḥ | ād dakṣi̍ṇā yujyate vāja̱yantyu̍ttā̱nāmū̱rdhvo a̍dhayajju̱hūbhi̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 4

a̱gnimacchā̍ devaya̱tāṃ manā̍ṃsi̱ cakṣū̍ṃṣīva̱ sūrye̱ saṃ ca̍ranti | yadī̱ṃ suvā̍te u̱ṣasā̱ virū̍pe śve̱to vā̱jī jā̍yate̱ agre̱ ahnā̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 5

jani̍ṣṭa̱ hi jenyo̱ agre̱ ahnā̍ṃ hi̱to hi̱teṣva̍ru̱ṣo vane̍ṣu | dame̍dame sa̱pta ratnā̱ dadhā̍no̱'gnirhotā̱ ni ṣa̍sādā̱ yajī̍yān

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 6

a̱gnirhotā̱ nya̍sīda̱d yajī̍yānu̱pasthe̍ mā̱tuḥ su̍ra̱bhā u̍ lo̱ke | yuvā̍ ka̱viḥ pu̍runi̱ṣṭha ṛ̱tāvā̍ dha̱rtā kṛ̍ṣṭī̱nāmu̱ta madhya̍ i̱ddhaḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 7

pra ṇu tyaṃ vipra̍madhva̱reṣu̍ sā̱dhuma̱gniṃ hotā̍ramīl̤ate̱ namo̍bhiḥ | ā yasta̱tāna̱ roda̍sī ṛ̱tena̱ nitya̍ṃ mṛjanti vā̱jina̍ṃ ghṛ̱tena̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 8

mā̱rjā̱lyo̍ mṛjyate̱ sve damū̍nāḥ kavipraśa̱sto ati̍thiḥ śi̱vo na̍: | sa̱hasra̍śṛṅgo vṛṣa̱bhastado̍jā̱ viśvā̍m̐ agne̱ saha̍sā̱ prāsya̱nyān

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 9

pra sa̱dyo a̍gne̱ atye̍ṣya̱nyānā̱viryasmai̱ cāru̍tamo ba̱bhūtha̍ | ī̱l̤enyo̍ vapu̱ṣyo̍ vi̱bhāvā̍ pri̱yo vi̱śāmati̍thi̱rmānu̍ṣīṇām

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 10

tubhya̍ṃ bharanti kṣi̱tayo̍ yaviṣṭha ba̱lima̍gne̱ anti̍ta̱ ota dū̱rāt | ā bhandi̍ṣṭhasya suma̱tiṃ ci̍kiddhi bṛ̱hat te̍ agne̱ mahi̱ śarma̍ bha̱dram

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 11

ādya ratha̍ṃ bhānumo bhānu̱manta̱magne̱ tiṣṭha̍ yaja̱tebhi̱: sama̍ntam | vi̱dvān pa̍thī̱nāmu̱rva1ntari̍kṣa̱meha de̱vān ha̍vi̱radyā̍ya vakṣi

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 12

avo̍cāma ka̱vaye̱ medhyā̍ya̱ vaco̍ va̱ndāru̍ vṛṣa̱bhāya̱ vṛṣṇe̍ | gavi̍ṣṭhiro̱ nama̍sā̱ stoma̍ma̱gnau di̱vī̍va ru̱kmamu̍ru̱vyañca̍maśret
