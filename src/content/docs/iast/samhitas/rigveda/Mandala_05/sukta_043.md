# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 43

**17 bhaumo'triḥ| viśve devāḥ | triṣṭup, 16 ekapadā virāṭ |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 1

ā dhe̱nava̱: paya̍sā̱ tūrṇya̍rthā̱ ama̍rdhantī̱rupa̍ no yantu̱ madhvā̍ |
ma̱ho rā̱ye bṛ̍ha̱tīḥ sa̱pta vipro̍ mayo̱bhuvo̍ jari̱tā jo̍havīti

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 2

ā su̍ṣṭu̱tī nama̍sā varta̱yadhyai̱ dyāvā̱ vājā̍ya pṛthi̱vī amṛ̍dhre |

pi̱tā mā̱tā madhu̍vacāḥ su̱hastā̱ bhare̍bhare no ya̱śasā̍vaviṣṭām

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 3

adhva̍ryavaścakṛ̱vāṃso̱ madhū̍ni̱ pra vā̱yave̍ bharata̱ cāru̍ śu̱kram |

hote̍va naḥ pratha̱maḥ pā̍hya̱sya deva̱ madhvo̍ rari̱mā te̱ madā̍ya

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 4

daśa̱ kṣipo̍ yuñjate bā̱hū adri̱ṃ soma̍sya̱ yā śa̍mi̱tārā̍ su̱hastā̍ |

madhvo̱ rasa̍ṃ su̱gabha̍stirgiri̱ṣṭhāṃ cani̍ścadad duduhe śu̱krama̱ṃśuḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 5

asā̍vi te jujuṣā̱ṇāya̱ soma̱: kratve̱ dakṣā̍ya bṛha̱te madā̍ya |

harī̱ rathe̍ su̱dhurā̱ yoge̍ a̱rvāgindra̍ pri̱yā kṛ̍ṇuhi hū̱yamā̍naḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 6

ā no̍ ma̱hīma̱rama̍tiṃ sa̱joṣā̱ gnāṃ de̱vīṃ nama̍sā rā̱taha̍vyām |

madho̱rmadā̍ya bṛha̱tīmṛ̍ta̱jñāmāgne̍ vaha pa̱thibhi̍rdeva̱yānai̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 7

a̱ñjanti̱ yaṃ pra̱thaya̍nto̱ na viprā̍ va̱pāva̍nta̱ṃ nāgninā̱ tapa̍ntaḥ |

pi̱turna pu̱tra u̱pasi̱ preṣṭha̱ ā gha̱rmo a̱gnimṛ̱taya̍nnasādi

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 8

acchā̍ ma̱hī bṛ̍ha̱tī śaṃta̍mā̱ gīrdū̱to na ga̍ntva̱śvinā̍ hu̱vadhyai̍ |

ma̱yo̱bhuvā̍ sa̱rathā yā̍tama̱rvāgga̱ntaṃ ni̱dhiṃ dhura̍mā̱ṇirna nābhi̍m

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 9

pra tavya̍so̱ nama̍uktiṃ tu̱rasyā̱'haṃ pū̱ṣṇa u̱ta vā̱yora̍dikṣi |

yā rādha̍sā codi̱tārā̍ matī̱nāṃ yā vāja̍sya draviṇo̱dā u̱ta tman

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 10

ā nāma̍bhirma̱ruto̍ vakṣi̱ viśvā̱nā rū̱pebhi̍rjātavedo huvā̱naḥ |

ya̱jñaṃ giro̍ jari̱tuḥ su̍ṣṭu̱tiṃ ca̱ viśve̍ ganta maruto̱ viśva̍ ū̱tī
