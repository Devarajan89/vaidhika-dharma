# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 17

**5 pūrurātreyaḥ| agniḥ| anuṣṭup, 5 paṃktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 1

ā ya̱jñairde̍va̱ martya̍ i̱tthā tavyā̍ṃsamū̱taye̍ |
a̱gniṃ kṛ̱te sva̍dhva̱re pū̱rurī̍l̤ī̱tāva̍se

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 2

asya̱ hi svaya̍śastara ā̱sā vi̍dharma̱n manya̍se |

taṃ nāka̍ṃ ci̱traśo̍ciṣaṃ ma̱ndraṃ pa̱ro ma̍nī̱ṣayā̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 3

a̱sya vāsā u̍ a̱rciṣā̱ ya āyu̍kta tu̱jā gi̱rā |

di̱vo na yasya̱ reta̍sā bṛ̱hacchoca̍ntya̱rcaya̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 4

a̱sya kratvā̱ vice̍taso da̱smasya̱ vasu̱ ratha̱ ā |

adhā̱ viśvā̍su̱ havyo̱'gnirvi̱kṣu pra śa̍syate

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 5

nū na̱ iddhi vārya̍mā̱sā sa̍canta sū̱raya̍: |

ūrjo̍ napāda̱bhiṣṭa̍ye pā̱hi śa̱gdhi sva̱staya̍ u̱taidhi̍ pṛ̱tsu no̍ vṛ̱dhe
