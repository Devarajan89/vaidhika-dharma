# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 67

**5 yajata ātreyaḥ| mitrāvaruṇau| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 1

bal̤i̱tthā de̍va niṣkṛ̱tamādi̍tyā yaja̱taṃ bṛ̱hat | varu̍ṇa̱ mitrārya̍ma̱n varṣi̍ṣṭhaṃ kṣa̱tramā̍śāthe

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 2

ā yad yoni̍ṃ hira̱ṇyaya̱ṃ varu̍ṇa̱ mitra̱ sada̍thaḥ | dha̱rtārā̍ carṣaṇī̱nāṃ ya̱ntaṃ su̱mnaṃ ri̍śādasā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 3

viśve̱ hi vi̱śvave̍daso̱ varu̍ṇo mi̱tro a̍rya̱mā | vra̱tā pa̱deva̍ saścire̱ pānti̱ martya̍ṃ ri̱ṣaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 4

te hi sa̱tyā ṛ̍ta̱spṛśa̍ ṛ̱tāvā̍no̱ jane̍jane | su̱nī̱thāsa̍: su̱dāna̍vo̱ṃ'hości̍duru̱cakra̍yaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 5

ko nu vā̍ṃ mi̱trāstu̍to̱ varu̍ṇo vā ta̱nūnā̍m | tat su vā̱meṣa̍te ma̱tiratri̍bhya̱ eṣa̍te ma̱tiḥ
