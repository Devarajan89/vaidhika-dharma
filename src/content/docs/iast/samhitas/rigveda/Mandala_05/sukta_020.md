# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 20

**4 prayasvanta ātreyāḥ| agniḥ| anuṣṭup, 4 paṃktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 1

yama̍gne vājasātama̱ tvaṃ ci̱n manya̍se ra̱yim |
taṃ no̍ gī̱rbhiḥ śra̱vāyya̍ṃ deva̱trā pa̍nayā̱ yuja̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 2

ye a̍gne̱ neraya̍nti te vṛ̱ddhā u̱grasya̱ śava̍saḥ |

apa̱ dveṣo̱ apa̱ hvaro̱'nyavra̍tasya saścire

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 3

hotā̍raṃ tvā vṛṇīma̱he'gne̱ dakṣa̍sya̱ sādha̍nam |

ya̱jñeṣu̍ pū̱rvyaṃ gi̱rā praya̍svanto havāmahe

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 4

i̱tthā yathā̍ ta ū̱taye̱ saha̍sāvandi̱vedi̍ve |

rā̱ya ṛ̱tāya̍ sukrato̱ gobhi̍: ṣyāma sadha̱mādo̍ vī̱raiḥ syā̍ma sadha̱māda̍:
