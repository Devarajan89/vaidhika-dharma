# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 9

**7 gaya ātreyaḥ| agniḥ| anuṣṭup, 5-7 paṃktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 1

tvāma̍gne ha̱viṣma̍nto de̱vaṃ martā̍sa īl̤ate |
manye̍ tvā jā̱tave̍dasa̱ṃ sa ha̱vyā va̍kṣyānu̱ṣak

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 2

a̱gnirhotā̱ dāsva̍ta̱: kṣaya̍sya vṛ̱ktaba̍rhiṣaḥ |

saṃ ya̱jñāsa̱ścara̍nti̱ yaṃ saṃ vājā̍saḥ śrava̱syava̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 3

u̱ta sma̱ yaṃ śiśu̍ṃ yathā̱ nava̱ṃ jani̍ṣṭā̱raṇī̍ |

dha̱rtāra̱ṃ mānu̍ṣīṇāṃ vi̱śāma̱gniṃ sva̍dhva̱ram

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 4

u̱ta sma̍ durgṛbhīyase pu̱tro na hvā̱ryāṇā̍m |

pu̱rū yo dagdhāsi̱ vanā 'gne̍ pa̱śurna yava̍se

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 5

adha̍ sma̱ yasyā̱rcaya̍: sa̱myak saṃyanti̍ dhū̱mina̍: |

yadī̱maha̍ tri̱to di̱vyupa̱ dhmāte̍va̱ dhama̍ti̱ śiśī̍te dhmā̱tarī̍ yathā

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 6

tavā̱hama̍gna ū̱tibhi̍rmi̱trasya̍ ca̱ praśa̍stibhiḥ |

dve̱ṣo̱yuto̱ na du̍ri̱tā tu̱ryāma̱ martyā̍nām

---

## Verse 7

sa kṣe̍paya̱t sa po̍ṣaya̱d bhuva̱d vāja̍sya sā̱taya̍ u̱taidhi̍ pṛ̱tsu no̍ vṛ̱dhe
