# Maṇḍala 5 — Sūktam 79

**10 satyaśravā ātreyaḥ| uṣā| paṅktiḥ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 1

ma̱he no̍ a̱dya bo̍dha̱yoṣo̍ rā̱ye di̱vitma̍tī |
yathā̍ cinno̱ abo̍dhayaḥ sa̱tyaśra̍vasi vā̱yye sujā̍te̱ aśva̍sūnṛte

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 2

yā su̍nī̱the śau̍cadra̱the vyauccho̍ duhitardivaḥ |

sā vyu̍ccha̱ sahī̍yasi sa̱tyaśra̍vasi vā̱yye sujā̍te̱ aśva̍sūnṛte

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 3

sā no̍ a̱dyābha̱radva̍su̱rvyu̍cchā duhitardivaḥ |

yo vyauccha̱: sahī̍yasi sa̱tyaśra̍vasi vā̱yye sujā̍te̱ aśva̍sūnṛte

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 4

a̱bhi ye tvā̍ vibhāvari̱ stomai̍rgṛ̱ṇanti̱ vahna̍yaḥ |

ma̱ghairma̍ghoni su̱śriyo̱ dāma̍nvantaḥ surā̱taya̱: sujā̍ta̍ aśva̍sūnṛte

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 5

yacci̱ddhi te̍ ga̱ṇā i̱me cha̱daya̍nti ma̱ghatta̍ye |

pari̍ ci̱d vaṣṭa̍yo dadhu̱rdada̍to̱ rādho̱ ahra̍ya̱ṃ sujā̍te̱ aśva̍sūnṛte

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 6

aiṣu̍ dhā vī̱rava̱d yaśa̱ uṣo̍ maghoni sū̱riṣu̍ |

ye no̱ rādhā̱ṃsyahra̍yā ma̱ghavā̍no̱ arā̍sata̱ sujā̍te̱ aśva̍sūnṛte

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 7

tebhyo̍ dyu̱mnaṃ bṛ̱had yaśa̱ uṣo̍ magho̱nyā va̍ha |

ye no̱ rādhā̱ṃsyaśvyā̍ ga̱vyā bhaja̍nta sū̱raya̱: sujā̍te̱ aśva̍sūnṛte

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 8

u̱ta no̱ goma̍tī̱riṣa̱ ā va̍hā duhitardivaḥ |

sā̱kaṃ sūrya̍sya ra̱śmibhi̍: śu̱kraiḥ śoca̍dbhira̱rcibhi̱: sujā̍te̱ aśva̍sūnṛte

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 9

vyu̍cchā duhitardivo̱ mā ci̱raṃ ta̍nuthā̱ apa̍: |

net tvā̍ ste̱naṃ yathā̍ ri̱puṃ tapā̍ti̱ sūro̍ a̱rciṣā̱ sujā̍te̱ aśva̍sūnṛte

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 5 | **Sukta:** 79 | **Verse:** 10

e̱tāva̱d vedu̍ṣa̱stvaṃ bhūyo̍ vā̱ dātu̍marhasi |

yā sto̱tṛbhyo̍ vibhāvaryu̱cchantī̱ na pra̱mīya̍se̱ sujā̍te̱ aśva̍sūnṛte
