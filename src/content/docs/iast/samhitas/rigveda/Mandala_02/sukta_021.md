# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 21

**6 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| indraḥ| jagatī, 6 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 1

vi̱śva̱jite̍ dhana̱jite̍ sva̱rjite̍ satrā̱jite̍ nṛ̱jita̍ urvarā̱jite̍ |
a̱śva̱jite̍ go̱jite̍ a̱bjite̍ bha̱rendrā̍ya̱ soma̍ṃ yaja̱tāya̍ harya̱tam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 2

a̱bhi̱bhuve̍'bhibha̱ṅgāya̍ vanva̱te 'ṣā̍l̤ahāya̱ saha̍mānāya ve̱dhase̍ |

tu̱vi̱graye̱ vahna̍ye du̱ṣṭarī̍tave satrā̱sāhe̱ nama̱ indrā̍ya vocata

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 3

sa̱trā̱sā̱ho ja̍nabha̱kṣo ja̍naṃsa̱haścyava̍no yu̱dhmo anu̱ joṣa̍mukṣi̱taḥ |

vṛ̱ta̱ṃca̱yaḥ sahu̍rirvi̱kṣvā̍ri̱ta indra̍sya voca̱ṃ pra kṛ̱tāni̍ vī̱ryā̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 4

a̱nā̱nu̱do vṛ̍ṣa̱bho dodha̍to va̱dho ga̍mbhī̱ra ṛ̱ṣvo asa̍maṣṭakāvyaḥ |

ra̱dhra̱co̱daḥ śnatha̍no vīl̤i̱taspṛ̱thurindra̍: suya̱jña u̱ṣasa̱: sva̍rjanat

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 5

ya̱jñena̍ gā̱tuma̱pturo̍ vividrire̱ dhiyo̍ hinvā̱nā u̱śijo̍ manī̱ṣiṇa̍: |

a̱bhi̱svarā̍ ni̱ṣadā̱ gā a̍va̱syava̱ indre̍ hinvā̱nā dravi̍ṇānyāśata

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 6

poṣa̍ṃ rayī̱ṇāmari̍ṣṭiṃ ta̱nūnā̍ṃ svā̱dmāna̍ṃ vā̱caḥ su̍dina̱tvamahnā̍m
