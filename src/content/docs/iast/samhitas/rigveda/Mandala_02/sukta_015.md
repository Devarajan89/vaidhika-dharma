# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 15

**10 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 1

pra ghā̱ nva̍sya maha̱to ma̱hāni̍ sa̱tyā sa̱tyasya̱ kara̍ṇāni vocam |
trika̍drukeṣvapibat su̱tasyā̱sya made̱ ahi̱mindro̍ jaghāna

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 2

a̱va̱ṃśe dyāma̍stabhāyad bṛ̱hanta̱mā roda̍sī apṛṇada̱ntari̍kṣam |

sa dhā̍rayat pṛthi̱vīṃ pa̱pratha̍cca̱ soma̍sya̱ tā mada̱ indra̍ścakāra

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 3

sadme̍va̱ prāco̱ vi mi̍māya̱ mānai̱rvajre̍ṇa̱ khānya̍tṛṇanna̱dīnā̍m |

vṛthā̍sṛjat pa̱thibhi̍rdīrghayā̱thaiḥ soma̍sya̱ tā mada̱ indra̍ścakāra

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 4

sa pra̍vo̱l̤ahṛn pa̍ri̱gatyā̍ da̱bhīte̱rviśva̍madhā̱gāyu̍dhami̱ddhe a̱gnau |

saṃ gobhi̱raśvai̍rasṛja̱d rathe̍bhi̱: soma̍sya̱ tā mada̱ indra̍ścakāra

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 5

sa ī̍ṃ ma̱hīṃ dhuni̱meto̍raramṇā̱t so a̍snā̱tṝna̍pārayat sva̱sti |

ta u̱tsnāya̍ ra̱yima̱bhi pra ta̍sthu̱: soma̍sya̱ tā mada̱ indra̍ścakāra

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 6

soda̍ñca̱ṃ sindhu̍mariṇānmahi̱tvā vajre̱ṇāna̍ u̱ṣasa̱: saṃ pi̍peṣa |

a̱ja̱vaso̍ ja̱vinī̍bhirvivṛ̱ścan tsoma̍sya̱ tā mada̱ indra̍ścakāra

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 7

sa vi̱dvām̐ a̍pago̱haṃ ka̱nīnā̍mā̱virbhava̱nnuda̍tiṣṭhat parā̱vṛk |

prati̍ śro̱ṇaḥ sthā̱dvya1naga̍caṣṭa̱ soma̍sya̱ tā mada̱ indra̍ścakāra

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 8

bhi̱nad va̱lamaṅgi̍robhirgṛṇā̱no vi parva̍tasya dṛṃhi̱tānyai̍rat |

ri̱ṇagrodhā̍ṃsi kṛ̱trimā̍ṇyeṣā̱ṃ soma̍sya̱ tā mada̱ indra̍ścakāra

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 9

svapne̍nā̱bhyupyā̱ cumu̍ri̱ṃ dhuni̍ṃ ca ja̱ghantha̱ dasyu̱ṃ pra da̱bhīti̍māvaḥ |

ra̱mbhī ci̱datra̍ vivide̱ hira̍ṇya̱ṃ soma̍sya̱ tā mada̱ indra̍ścakāra

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 10
