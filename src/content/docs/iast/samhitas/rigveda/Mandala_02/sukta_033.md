# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 33

**15 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| rudraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 1

ā te̍ pitarmarutāṃ su̱mname̍tu̱ mā na̱: sūrya̍sya sa̱ṃdṛśo̍ yuyothāḥ |
a̱bhi no̍ vī̱ro arva̍ti kṣameta̱ pra jā̍yemahi rudra pra̱jābhi̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 2

tvāda̍ttebhī rudra̱ śaṃta̍mebhiḥ śa̱taṃ himā̍ aśīya bheṣa̱jebhi̍: |

vya1smaddveṣo̍ vita̱raṃ vyaṃho̱ vyamī̍vāścātayasvā̱ viṣū̍cīḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 3

śreṣṭho̍ jā̱tasya̍ rudra śri̱yāsi̍ ta̱vasta̍masta̱vasā̍ṃ vajrabāho |

parṣi̍ ṇaḥ pā̱ramaṃha̍saḥ sva̱sti viśvā̍ a̱bhī̍tī̱ rapa̍so yuyodhi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 4

mā tvā̍ rudra cukrudhāmā̱ namo̍bhi̱rmā duṣṭu̍tī vṛṣabha̱ mā sahū̍tī |

unno̍ vī̱rām̐ a̍rpaya bheṣa̱jebhi̍rbhi̱ṣakta̍maṃ tvā bhi̱ṣajā̍ṃ śṛṇomi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 5

havī̍mabhi̱rhava̍te̱ yo ha̱virbhi̱rava̱ stome̍bhī ru̱draṃ di̍ṣīya |

ṛ̱dū̱dara̍: su̱havo̱ mā no̍ a̱syai ba̱bhruḥ su̱śipro̍ rīradhanma̱nāyai̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 6

unmā̍ mamanda vṛṣa̱bho ma̱rutvā̱n tvakṣī̍yasā̱ vaya̍sā̱ nādha̍mānam |

ghṛṇī̍va cchā̱yāma̍ra̱pā a̍śī̱yā'' vi̍vāseyaṃ ru̱drasya̍ su̱mnam

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 7

kva1 sya te̍ rudra mṛl̤a̱yāku̱rhasto̱ yo asti̍ bheṣa̱jo jalā̍ṣaḥ |

a̱pa̱bha̱rtā rapa̍so̱ daivya̍syā̱bhī nu mā̍ vṛṣabha cakṣamīthāḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 8

pra ba̱bhrave̍ vṛṣa̱bhāya̍ śvitī̱ce ma̱ho ma̱hīṃ su̍ṣṭu̱timī̍rayāmi |

na̱ma̱syā ka̍lmalī̱kina̱ṃ namo̍bhirgṛṇī̱masi̍ tve̱ṣaṃ ru̱drasya̱ nāma̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 9

sthi̱rebhi̱raṅgai̍: puru̱rūpa̍ u̱gro ba̱bhruḥ śu̱krebhi̍: pipiśe̱ hira̍ṇyaiḥ |

īśā̍nāda̱sya bhuva̍nasya̱ bhūre̱rna vā u̍ yoṣad ru̱drāda̍su̱rya̍m

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 10

arha̍n bibharṣi̱ sāya̍kāni̱ dhanvārha̍n ni̱ṣkaṃ ya̍ja̱taṃ vi̱śvarū̍pam |

arha̍nni̱daṃ da̍yase̱ viśva̱mabhva̱ṃ na vā ojī̍yo rudra̱ tvada̍sti

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 11

stu̱hi śru̱taṃ ga̍rta̱sada̱ṃ yuvā̍naṃ mṛ̱gaṃ na bhī̱mamu̍paha̱tnumu̱gram |

mṛ̱l̤ā ja̍ri̱tre ru̍dra̱ stavā̍no̱ 'nyaṃ te̍ a̱smanni va̍pantu̱ senā̍:

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 12

ku̱mā̱raści̍t pi̱tara̱ṃ vanda̍māna̱ṃ prati̍ nānāma rudropa̱yanta̍m |

bhūre̍rdā̱tāra̱ṃ satpa̍tiṃ gṛṇīṣe stu̱tastvaṃ bhe̍ṣa̱jā rā̍sya̱sme

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 13

yā vo̍ bheṣa̱jā ma̍ruta̱: śucī̍ni̱ yā śaṃta̍mā vṛṣaṇo̱ yā ma̍yo̱bhu |

yāni̱ manu̱ravṛ̍ṇītā pi̱tā na̱stā śaṃ ca̱ yośca̍ ru̱drasya̍ vaśmi

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 14

pari̍ ṇo he̱tī ru̱drasya̍ vṛjyā̱: pari̍ tve̱ṣasya̍ durma̱tirma̱hī gā̍t |

ava̍ sthi̱rā ma̱ghava̍dbhyastanuṣva̱ mīḍhva̍sto̱kāya̱ tana̍yāya mṛl̤a

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 33 | **Verse:** 15

ha̱va̱na̱śrunno̍ rudre̱ha bo̍dhi bṛ̱had va̍dema vi̱dathe̍ su̱vīrā̍:
