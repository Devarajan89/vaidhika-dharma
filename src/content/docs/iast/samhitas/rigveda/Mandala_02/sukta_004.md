# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 4

**9 somāhutirbhārgavaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 1

hu̱ve va̍: su̱dyotmā̍naṃ suvṛ̱ktiṃ vi̱śāma̱gnimati̍thiṃ supra̱yasa̍m |
mi̱tra i̍va̱ yo di̍dhi̱ṣāyyo̱ bhūd de̱va āde̍ve̱ jane̍ jā̱tave̍dāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 2

i̱maṃ vi̱dhanto̍ a̱pāṃ sa̱dhasthe̍ dvi̱tāda̍dhu̱rbhṛga̍vo vi̱kṣvā̱3yoḥ |

e̱ṣa viśvā̍nya̱bhya̍stu̱ bhūmā̍ de̱vānā̍ma̱gnira̍ra̱tirjī̱rāśva̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 3

a̱gniṃ de̱vāso̱ mānu̍ṣīṣu vi̱kṣu pri̱yaṃ dhu̍: kṣe̱ṣyanto̱ na mi̱tram |

sa dī̍dayaduśa̱tīrūrmyā̱ ā da̱kṣāyyo̱ yo dāsva̍te̱ dama̱ ā

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 4

a̱sya ra̱ṇvā svasye̍va pu̱ṣṭiḥ saṃdṛ̍ṣṭirasya hiyā̱nasya̱ dakṣo̍: |

vi yo bhari̍bhra̱doṣa̍dhīṣu ji̱hvāmatyo̱ na rathyo̍ dodhavīti̱ vārā̍n

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 5

ā yanme̱ abhva̍ṃ va̱nada̱: pana̍nto̱śigbhyo̱ nāmi̍mīta̱ varṇa̍m |

sa ci̱treṇa̍ cikite̱ raṃsu̍ bhā̱sā ju̍ju̱rvām̐ yo muhu̱rā yuvā̱ bhūt

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 6

ā yo vanā̍ tātṛṣā̱ṇo na bhāti̱ vārṇa pa̱thā rathye̍va svānīt |

kṛ̱ṣṇādhvā̱ tapū̍ ra̱ṇvaści̍keta̱ dyauri̍va̱ smaya̍māno̱ nabho̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 7

sa yo vyasthā̍da̱bhi dakṣa̍du̱rvīṃ pa̱śurnaiti̍ sva̱yurago̍pāḥ |

a̱gniḥ śo̱ciṣmā̍m̐ ata̱sānyu̱ṣṇan kṛ̱ṣṇavya̍thirasvadaya̱nna bhūma̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 8

nū te̱ pūrva̱syāva̍so̱ adhī̍tau tṛ̱tīye̍ vi̱dathe̱ manma̍ śaṃsi |

a̱sme a̍gne sa̱ṃyadvī̍raṃ bṛ̱hanta̍ṃ kṣu̱manta̱ṃ vāja̍ṃ svapa̱tyaṃ ra̱yiṃ dā̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 9

tvayā̱ yathā̍ gṛtsama̱dāso̍ agne̱ guhā̍ va̱nvanta̱ upa̍rām̐ a̱bhi ṣyuḥ |

su̱vīrā̍so abhimāti̱ṣāha̱: smat sū̱ribhyo̍ gṛṇa̱te tad vayo̍ dhāḥ
