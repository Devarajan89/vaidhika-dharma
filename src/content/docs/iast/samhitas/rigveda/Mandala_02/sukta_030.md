# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 30

**11 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ|indraḥ, 6 indrasomau, 8(pūrvā'rdharca sya)| sarasvatī, 9 bṛhaspatiḥ, 11 marutaḥ| triṣṭup, 11 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 1

ṛ̱taṃ de̱vāya̍ kṛṇva̱te sa̍vi̱tra indrā̍yāhi̱ghne na ra̍manta̱ āpa̍: |
aha̍raharyātya̱ktura̱pāṃ kiyā̱tyā pra̍tha̱maḥ sarga̍ āsām

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 2

yo vṛ̱trāya̱ sina̱matrābha̍riṣya̱t pra taṃ jani̍trī vi̱duṣa̍ uvāca |

pa̱tho rada̍ntī̱ranu̱ joṣa̍masmai di̱vedi̍ve̱ dhuna̍yo ya̱ntyartha̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 3

ū̱rdhvo hyasthā̱dadhya̱ntari̱kṣe 'dhā̍ vṛ̱trāya̱ pra va̱dhaṃ ja̍bhāra |

miha̱ṃ vasā̍na̱ upa̱ hīmadu̍drot ti̱gmāyu̍dho ajaya̱cchatru̱mindra̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 4

bṛha̍spate̱ tapu̱ṣāśne̍va vidhya̱ vṛka̍dvaraso̱ asu̍rasya vī̱rān |

yathā̍ ja̱ghantha̍ dhṛṣa̱tā pu̱rā ci̍de̱vā ja̍hi̱ śatru̍ma̱smāka̍mindra

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 5

ava̍ kṣipa di̱vo aśmā̍namu̱ccā yena̱ śatru̍ṃ mandasā̱no ni̱jūrvā̍: |

to̱kasya̍ sā̱tau tana̍yasya̱ bhūre̍ra̱smām̐ a̱rdhaṃ kṛ̍ṇutādindra̱ gonā̍m

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 6

indrā̍somā yu̱vama̱smām̐ a̍viṣṭama̱smin bha̱yasthe̍ kṛṇutamu lo̱kam

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 7

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 8

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 9

bṛha̍spata̱ āyu̍dhairjeṣi̱ śatrū̍n dru̱he rīṣa̍nta̱ṃ pari̍ dhehi rājan

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 10

jyoga̍bhūva̱nnanu̍dhūpitāso ha̱tvī teṣā̱mā bha̍rā no̱ vasū̍ni
