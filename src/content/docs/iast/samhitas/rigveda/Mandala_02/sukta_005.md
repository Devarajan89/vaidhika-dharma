# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 5

**8 somāhutirbhārgavaḥ| agniḥ| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 1

hotā̍janiṣṭa̱ ceta̍naḥ pi̱tā pi̱tṛbhya̍ ū̱taye̍ |
pra̱yakṣa̱ñjenya̱ṃ vasu̍ śa̱kema̍ vā̱jino̱ yama̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 2

ā yasmi̍n tsa̱pta ra̱śmaya̍sta̱tā ya̱jñasya̍ ne̱tari̍ |

ma̱nu̱ṣvad daivya̍maṣṭa̱maṃ potā̱ viśva̱ṃ tadi̍nvati

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 3

da̱dha̱nve vā̱ yadī̱manu̱ voca̱d brahmā̍ṇi̱ veru̱ tat |

pari̱ viśvā̍ni̱ kāvyā̍ ne̱miśca̱krami̍vābhavat

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 4

sā̱kaṃ hi śuci̍nā̱ śuci̍: praśā̱stā kratu̱nāja̍ni |

vi̱dvām̐ a̍sya vra̱tā dhru̱vā va̱yā i̱vānu̍ rohate

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 5

tā a̍sya̱ varṇa̍mā̱yuvo̱ neṣṭu̍: sacanta dhe̱nava̍: |

ku̱vit ti̱sṛbhya̱ ā vara̱ṃ svasā̍ro̱ yā i̱daṃ ya̱yuḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 6

yadī̍ mā̱turupa̱ svasā̍ ghṛ̱taṃ bhara̱ntyasthi̍ta |

tāsā̍madhva̱ryurāga̍tau̱ yavo̍ vṛ̱ṣṭīva̍ modate

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 7

svaḥ svāya̱ dhāya̍se kṛṇu̱tāmṛ̱tvigṛ̱tvija̍m |

stoma̍ṃ ya̱jñaṃ cādara̍ṃ va̱nemā̍ rari̱mā va̱yam

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 5 | **Verse:** 8

yathā̍ vi̱dvām̐ ara̱ṃ kara̱d viśve̍bhyo yaja̱tebhya̍: |

a̱yama̍gne̱ tve api̱ yaṃ ya̱jñaṃ ca̍kṛ̱mā va̱yam
