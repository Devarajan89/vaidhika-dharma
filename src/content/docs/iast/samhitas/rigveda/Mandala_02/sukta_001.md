# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 1

**16 gṛtsamada(āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| agniḥ|jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 1

tvama̍gne̱ dyubhi̱stvamā̍śuśu̱kṣaṇi̱stvama̱dbhyastvamaśma̍na̱spari̍ |
tvaṃ vane̍bhya̱stvamoṣa̍dhībhya̱stvaṃ nṛ̱ṇāṃ nṛ̍pate jāyase̱ śuci̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 2

tavā̍gne ho̱traṃ tava̍ po̱tramṛ̱tviya̱ṃ tava̍ ne̱ṣṭraṃ tvama̱gnidṛ̍tāya̱taḥ |

tava̍ praśā̱straṃ tvama̍dhvarīyasi bra̱hmā cāsi̍ gṛ̱hapa̍tiśca no̱ dame̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 3

tvama̍gna̱ indro̍ vṛṣa̱bhaḥ sa̱tāma̍si̱ tvaṃ viṣṇu̍rurugā̱yo na̍ma̱sya̍: |

tvaṃ bra̱hmā ra̍yi̱vid bra̍hmaṇaspate̱ tvaṃ vi̍dhartaḥ  sacase̱ pura̍ṃdhyā

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 4

tvama̍gne̱ rājā̱ varu̍ṇo dhṛ̱tavra̍ta̱stvaṃ mi̱tro bha̍vasi da̱sma īḍya̍: |

tvama̍rya̱mā satpa̍ti̱ryasya̍ sa̱ṃbhuja̱ṃ tvamaṃśo̍ vi̱dathe̍ deva bhāja̱yuḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 5

tvama̍gne̱ tvaṣṭā̍ vidha̱te su̱vīrya̱ṃ tava̱ gnāvo̍ mitramahaḥ sajā̱tya̍m |

tvamā̍śu̱hemā̍ rariṣe̱ svaśvya̱ṃ tvaṃ na̱rāṃ śardho̍ asi purū̱vasu̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 6

tvama̍gne ru̱dro asu̍ro ma̱ho di̱vastvaṃ śardho̱ māru̍taṃ pṛ̱kṣa ī̍śiṣe |

tvaṃ vātai̍raru̱ṇairyā̍si śaṃga̱yastvaṃ pū̱ṣā vi̍dha̱taḥ pā̍si̱ nu tmanā̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 7

tvama̍gne draviṇo̱dā a̍ra̱ṃkṛte̱ tvaṃ de̱vaḥ sa̍vi̱tā ra̍tna̱dhā a̍si |

tvaṃ bhago̍ nṛpate̱ vasva̍ īśiṣe̱ tvaṃ pā̱yurdame̱ yaste'vi̍dhat

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 8

tvāma̍gne̱ dama̱ ā vi̱śpati̱ṃ viśa̱stvāṃ rājā̍naṃ suvi̱datra̍mṛñjate |

tvaṃ viśvā̍ni svanīka patyase̱ tvaṃ sa̱hasrā̍ṇi śa̱tā daśa̱ prati̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 9

tvāma̍gne pi̱tara̍mi̱ṣṭibhi̱rnara̱stvāṃ bhrā̱trāya̱ śamyā̍ tanū̱ruca̍m |

tvaṃ pu̱tro bha̍vasi̱ yaste'vi̍dha̱ttvaṃ sakhā̍ su̱śeva̍: pāsyā̱dhṛṣa̍:

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 10

tvama̍gna ṛ̱bhurā̱ke na̍ma̱sya1stvaṃ vāja̍sya kṣu̱mato̍ rā̱ya ī̍śiṣe |

tvaṃ vi bhā̱syanu̍ dakṣi dā̱vane̱ tvaṃ vi̱śikṣu̍rasi ya̱jñamā̱tani̍:

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 11

tvama̍gne̱ adi̍tirdeva dā̱śuṣe̱ tvaṃ hotrā̱ bhāra̍tī vardhase gi̱rā |

tvamil̤ā̍ śa̱tahi̍māsi̱ dakṣa̍se̱ tvaṃ vṛ̍tra̱hā va̍supate̱ sara̍svatī

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 12

tvama̍gne̱ subhṛ̍ta utta̱maṃ vaya̱stava̍ spā̱rhe varṇa̱ ā sa̱ṃdṛśi̱ śriya̍: |

tvaṃ vāja̍: pra̱tara̍ṇo bṛ̱hanna̍si̱ tvaṃ ra̱yirba̍hu̱lo vi̱śvata̍spṛ̱thuḥ

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 13

tvāma̍gna ādi̱tyāsa̍ ā̱syaṃ1 tvāṃ ji̱hvāṃ śuca̍yaścakrire kave |

tvāṃ rā̍ti̱ṣāco̍ adhva̱reṣu̍ saścire̱ tve de̱vā ha̱vira̍da̱ntyāhu̍tam

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 14

tve a̍gne̱ viśve̍ a̱mṛtā̍so a̱druha̍ ā̱sā de̱vā ha̱vira̍da̱ntyāhu̍tam |

tvayā̱ martā̍saḥ svadanta āsu̱tiṃ tvaṃ garbho̍ vī̱rudhā̍ṃ jajñiṣe̱ śuci̍:

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 15

tvaṃ tān tsaṃ ca̱ prati̍ cāsi ma̱jmanā 'gne̍ sujāta̱ pra ca̍ deva ricyase |

pṛ̱kṣo yadatra̍ mahi̱nā vi te̱ bhuva̱danu̱ dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī roda̍sī u̱bhe

---

## Verse 16

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 16

ye sto̱tṛbhyo̱ goa̍grā̱maśva̍peśasa̱magne̍ rā̱timu̍pasṛ̱janti̍ sū̱raya̍: |

a̱smāñca̱ tāṃśca̱ pra hi neṣi̱ vasya̱ ā bṛ̱had va̍dema vi̱dathe̍ su̱vīrā̍:
