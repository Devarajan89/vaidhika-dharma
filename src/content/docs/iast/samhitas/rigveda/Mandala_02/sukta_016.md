# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 16

**9 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| indraḥ| jagatī,  9 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 1

pra va̍: sa̱tāṃ jyeṣṭha̍tamāya suṣṭu̱tima̱gnāvi̍va samidhā̱ne ha̱virbha̍re |
indra̍maju̱ryaṃ ja̱raya̍ntamukṣi̱taṃ sa̱nād yuvā̍na̱mava̍se havāmahe

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 2

yasmā̱dindrā̍d bṛha̱taḥ kiṃ ca̱nemṛ̱te viśvā̍nyasmi̱n tsaṃbhṛ̱tādhi̍ vī̱ryā̍ |

ja̱ṭhare̱ soma̍ṃ ta̱nvī̱3 saho̱ maho̱ haste̱ vajra̱ṃ bhara̍ti śī̱rṣaṇi̱ kratu̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 3

na kṣo̱ṇībhyā̍ṃ pari̱bhve̍ ta indri̱yaṃ na sa̍mu̱draiḥ parva̍tairindra te̱ ratha̍: |

na te̱ vajra̱manva̍śnoti̱ kaśca̱na yadā̱śubhi̱: pata̍si̱ yoja̍nā pu̱ru

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 4

viśve̱ hya̍smai yaja̱tāya̍ dhṛ̱ṣṇave̱ kratu̱ṃ bhara̍nti vṛṣa̱bhāya̱ saśca̍te |

vṛṣā̍ yajasva ha̱viṣā̍ vi̱duṣṭa̍ra̱: pibe̍ndra̱ soma̍ṃ vṛṣa̱bheṇa̍ bhā̱nunā̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 5

vṛṣṇa̱: kośa̍: pavate̱ madhva̍ ū̱rmirvṛ̍ṣa̱bhānnā̍ya vṛṣa̱bhāya̱ pāta̍ve |

vṛṣa̍ṇādhva̱ryū vṛ̍ṣa̱bhāso̱ adra̍yo̱ vṛṣa̍ṇa̱ṃ soma̍ṃ vṛṣa̱bhāya̍ suṣvati

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 6

vṛṣā̍ te̱ vajra̍ u̱ta te̱ vṛṣā̱ ratho̱ vṛṣa̍ṇā̱ harī̍ vṛṣa̱bhāṇyāyu̍dhā |

vṛṣṇo̱ mada̍sya vṛṣabha̱ tvamī̍śiṣa̱ indra̱ soma̍sya vṛṣa̱bhasya̍ tṛpṇuhi

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 7

pra te̱ nāva̱ṃ na sama̍ne vaca̱syuva̱ṃ brahma̍ṇā yāmi̱ sava̍neṣu̱ dādhṛ̍ṣiḥ |

ku̱vinno̍ a̱sya vaca̍so ni̱bodhi̍ṣa̱dindra̱mutsa̱ṃ na vasu̍naḥ sicāmahe

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 8

pu̱rā sa̍mbā̱dhāda̱bhyā va̍vṛtsva no dhe̱nurna va̱tsaṃ yava̍sasya pi̱pyuṣī̍ |

sa̱kṛtsu te̍ suma̱tibhi̍: śatakrato̱ saṃ patnī̍bhi̱rna vṛṣa̍ṇo nasīmahi

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 9

nū̱naṃ sā te̱ prati̱ vara̍ṃ jari̱tre du̍hī̱yadi̍ndra̱ dakṣi̍ṇā ma̱ghonī̍ |

śikṣā̍ sto̱tṛbhyo̱ māti̍ dha̱gbhago̍ no bṛ̱had va̍dema vi̱dathe̍ su̱vīrā̍:
