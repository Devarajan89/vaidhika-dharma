# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 28

**11 kūrmo gārtsamado, gṛtsamado vā| varuṇaḥ (10 duḥsvapnanāśinīḥ)| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 1

i̱daṃ ka̱verā̍di̱tyasya̍ sva̱rājo̱ viśvā̍ni̱ sāntya̱bhya̍stu ma̱hnā |
ati̱ yo ma̱ndro ya̱jathā̍ya de̱vaḥ su̍kī̱rtiṃ bhi̍kṣe̱ varu̍ṇasya̱ bhūre̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 2

tava̍ vra̱te su̱bhagā̍saḥ syāma svā̱dhyo̍ varuṇa tuṣṭu̱vāṃsa̍: |

u̱pāya̍na u̱ṣasā̱ṃ goma̍tīnāma̱gnayo̱ na jara̍māṇā̱ anu̱ dyūn

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 3

tava̍ syāma puru̱vīra̍sya̱ śarma̍nnuru̱śaṃsa̍sya varuṇa praṇetaḥ |

yū̱yaṃ na̍: putrā aditeradabdhā a̱bhi kṣa̍madhva̱ṃ yujyā̍ya devāḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 4

pra sī̍mādi̱tyo a̍sṛjadvidha̱rtām̐ ṛ̱taṃ sindha̍vo̱ varu̍ṇasya yanti |

na śrā̍myanti̱ na vi mu̍cantye̱te vayo̱ na pa̍ptū raghu̱yā pari̍jman

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 5

vi macchra̍thāya raśa̱nāmi̱vāga̍ ṛ̱dhyāma̍ te varuṇa̱ khāmṛ̱tasya̍ |

mā tantu̍śchedi̱ vaya̍to̱ dhiya̍ṃ me̱ mā mātrā̍ śārya̱pasa̍: pu̱ra ṛ̱toḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 6

apo̱ su mya̍kṣa varuṇa bhi̱yasa̱ṃ matsamrā̱l̤ṛtā̱vo'nu̍ mā gṛbhāya |

dāme̍va va̱tsād vi mu̍mu̱gdhyaṃho̍ na̱hi tvadā̱re ni̱miṣa̍śca̱neśe̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 7

mā no̍ va̱dhairva̍ruṇa̱ ye ta̍ i̱ṣṭāvena̍: kṛ̱ṇvanta̍masura bhrī̱ṇanti̍ |

mā jyoti̍ṣaḥ pravasa̱thāni̍ ganma̱ vi ṣū mṛdha̍: śiśratho jī̱vase̍ naḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 8

nama̍: pu̱rā te̍ varuṇo̱ta nū̱namu̱tāpa̱raṃ tu̍vijāta bravāma |

tve hi ka̱ṃ parva̍te̱ na śri̱tānyapra̍cyutāni dūl̤abha vra̱tāni̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 9

para̍ ṛ̱ṇā sā̍vī̱radha̱ matkṛ̍tāni̱ māhaṃ rā̍janna̱nyakṛ̍tena bhojam |

avyu̍ṣṭā̱ innu bhūya̍sīru̱ṣāsa̱ ā no̍ jī̱vān va̍ruṇa̱ tāsu̍ śādhi

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 10

yo me̍ rāja̱n yujyo̍ vā̱ sakhā̍ vā̱ svapne̍ bha̱yaṃ bhī̱rave̱ mahya̱māha̍ |

ste̱no vā̱ yo dipsa̍ti no̱ vṛko̍ vā̱ tvaṃ tasmā̍d varuṇa pāhya̱smān

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 11

māhaṃ ma̱ghono̍ varuṇa pri̱yasya̍ bhūri̱dāvna̱ ā vi̍da̱ṃ śūna̍mā̱peḥ |

mā rā̱yo rā̍jan tsu̱yamā̱dava̍ sthāṃ bṛ̱had va̍dema vi̱dathe̍ su̱vīrā̍:
