# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 26

**4 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| brahmaṇaspatiḥ| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 1

ṛ̱juricchaṃso̍ vanavad vanuṣya̱to de̍va̱yannidade̍vayantama̱bhya̍sat |

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 2
