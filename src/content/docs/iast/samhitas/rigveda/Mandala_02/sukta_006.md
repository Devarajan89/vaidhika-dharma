# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 6

**8 somāhutirbhārgavaḥ| agniḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 1

i̱māṃ me̍ agne sa̱midha̍mi̱māmu̍pa̱sada̍ṃ vaneḥ | i̱mā u̱ ṣu śru̍dhī̱ gira̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 2

a̱yā te̍ agne vidhe̱morjo̍ napā̱daśva̍miṣṭe | e̱nā sū̱ktena̍ sujāta

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 3

taṃ tvā̍ gī̱rbhirgirva̍ṇasaṃ draviṇa̱syuṃ dra̍viṇodaḥ | sa̱pa̱ryema̍ sapa̱ryava̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 4

sa bo̍dhi sū̱rirma̱ghavā̱ vasu̍pate̱ vasu̍dāvan | yu̱yo̱dhya1smad dveṣā̍ṃsi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 5

sa no̍ vṛ̱ṣṭiṃ di̱vaspari̱ sa no̱ vāja̍mana̱rvāṇa̍m | sa na̍: saha̱sriṇī̱riṣa̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 6

īl̤ā̍nāyāva̱syave̱ yavi̍ṣṭha dūta no gi̱rā | yaji̍ṣṭha hota̱rā ga̍hi

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 7

a̱ntarhya̍gna̱ īya̍se vi̱dvān janmo̱bhayā̍ kave | dū̱to janye̍va̱ mitrya̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 8

sa vi̱dvām̐ ā ca̍ piprayo̱ yakṣi̍ cikitva ānu̱ṣak | ā cā̱smin tsa̍tsi ba̱rhiṣi̍
