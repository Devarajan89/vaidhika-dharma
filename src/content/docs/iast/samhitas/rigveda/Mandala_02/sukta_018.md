# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 18

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 1

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 2

## Verse 3
