# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 19

**9 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 1

apā̍yya̱syāndha̍so̱ madā̍ya̱ manī̍ṣiṇaḥ suvā̱nasya̱ praya̍saḥ |
yasmi̱nnindra̍: pra̱divi̍ vāvṛdhā̱na oko̍ da̱dhe bra̍hma̱ṇyanta̍śca̱ nara̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 2

a̱sya ma̍ndā̱no madhvo̱ vajra̍ha̱sto 'hi̱mindro̍ arṇo̱vṛta̱ṃ vi vṛ̍ścat |

pra yad vayo̱ na svasa̍rā̱ṇyacchā̱ prayā̍ṃsi ca na̱dīnā̱ṃ cakra̍manta

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 3

sa māhi̍na̱ indro̱ arṇo̍ a̱pāṃ praira̍yadahi̱hācchā̍ samu̱dram |

aja̍naya̱t sūrya̍ṃ vi̱dadgā a̱ktunāhnā̍ṃ va̱yunā̍ni sādhat

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 4

so a̍pra̱tīni̱ mana̍ve pu̱rūṇīndro̍ dāśad dā̱śuṣe̱ hanti̍ vṛ̱tram |

sa̱dyo yo nṛbhyo̍ ata̱sāyyo̱ bhūtpa̍spṛdhā̱nebhya̱: sūrya̍sya sā̱tau

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 5

sa su̍nva̱ta indra̱: sūrya̱mā '' de̱vo ri̍ṇa̱ṅmartyā̍ya sta̱vān |

ā yad ra̱yiṃ gu̱hada̍vadyamasmai̱ bhara̱daṃśa̱ṃ naita̍śo daśa̱syan

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 6

sa ra̍ndhayatsa̱diva̱: sāra̍thaye̱ śuṣṇa̍ma̱śuṣa̱ṃ kuya̍va̱ṃ kutsā̍ya |

divo̍dāsāya nava̱tiṃ ca̱ navendra̱: puro̱ vyai̍ra̱cchamba̍rasya

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 7

e̱vā ta̍ indro̱catha̍mahema śrava̱syā na tmanā̍ vā̱jaya̍ntaḥ |

a̱śyāma̱ tat sāpta̍māśuṣā̱ṇā na̱namo̱ vadha̱rade̍vasya pī̱yoḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 8

e̱vā te̍ gṛtsama̱dāḥ śū̍ra̱ manmā̍va̱syavo̱ na va̱yunā̍ni takṣuḥ |

bra̱hma̱ṇyanta̍ indra te̱ navī̍ya̱ iṣa̱mūrja̍ṃ sukṣi̱tiṃ su̱mnama̍śyuḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 9

nū̱naṃ sā te̱ prati̱ vara̍ṃ jari̱tre du̍hī̱yadi̍ndra̱ dakṣi̍ṇā ma̱ghonī̍ |

śikṣā̍ sto̱tṛbhyo̱ māti̍ dha̱gbhago̍ no bṛ̱had va̍dema vi̱dathe̍ su̱vīrā̍:
