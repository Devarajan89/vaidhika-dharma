# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 37

**6 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| ṛtudevatāḥ- 1-4 draviṇodā ṛtavaśca, 5 aśvinau ṛtavaśca, 6 agniḥ ṛtuśca| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 1

manda̍sva ho̱trādanu̱ joṣa̱mandha̱so 'dhva̍ryava̱: sa pū̱rṇāṃ va̍ṣṭyā̱sica̍m |
tasmā̍ e̱taṃ bha̍rata tadva̱śo da̱dirho̱trāt soma̍ṃ draviṇoda̱: piba̍ ṛ̱tubhi̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 2

yamu̱ pūrva̱mahu̍ve̱ tami̱daṃ hu̍ve̱ sedu̱ havyo̍ da̱diryo nāma̱ patya̍te |

a̱dhva̱ryubhi̱: prasthi̍taṃ so̱myaṃ madhu̍ po̱trāt soma̍ṃ draviṇoda̱: piba̍ ṛ̱tubhi̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 3

medya̍ntu te̱ vahna̍yo̱ yebhi̱rīya̱se 'ri̍ṣaṇyan vīl̤ayasvā vanaspate |

ā̱yūyā̍ dhṛṣṇo abhi̱gūryā̱ tvaṃ ne̱ṣṭrāt soma̍ṃ draviṇoda̱: piba̍ ṛ̱tubhi̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 4

apā̍ddho̱trādu̱ta po̱trāda̍matto̱ta ne̱ṣṭrāda̍juṣata̱ prayo̍ hi̱tam |

tu̱rīya̱ṃ pātra̱mamṛ̍kta̱mama̍rtyaṃ draviṇo̱dāḥ pi̍batu drāviṇoda̱saḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 5

a̱rvāñca̍ma̱dya ya̱yya̍ṃ nṛ̱vāha̍ṇa̱ṃ ratha̍ṃ yuñjāthāmi̱ha vā̍ṃ vi̱moca̍nam |

pṛ̱ṅktaṃ ha̱vīṃṣi̱ madhu̱nā hi ka̍ṃ ga̱tamathā̱ soma̍ṃ pibataṃ vājinīvasū

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 6

joṣya̍gne sa̱midha̱ṃ joṣyāhu̍ti̱ṃ joṣi̱ brahma̱ janya̱ṃ joṣi̍ suṣṭu̱tim |

viśve̍bhi̱rviśvā̍m̐ ṛ̱tunā̍ vaso ma̱ha u̱śan de̱vām̐ u̍śa̱taḥ pā̍yayā ha̱viḥ
