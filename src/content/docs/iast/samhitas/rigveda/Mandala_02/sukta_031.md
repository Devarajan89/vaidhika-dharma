# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 31

**7 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| viśve devāḥ| jagatī, 7 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 1

a̱smāka̍ṃ mitrāvaruṇāvata̱ṃ ratha̍mādi̱tyai ru̱drairvasu̍bhiḥ sacā̱bhuvā̍ |
pra yad vayo̱ na papta̱nvasma̍na̱spari̍ śrava̱syavo̱ hṛṣī̍vanto vana̱rṣada̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 2

adha̍ smā na̱ uda̍vatā sajoṣaso̱ ratha̍ṃ devāso a̱bhi vi̱kṣu vā̍ja̱yum |

yadā̱śava̱: padyā̍bhi̱stitra̍to̱ raja̍: pṛthi̱vyāḥ sānau̱ jaṅgha̍nanta pā̱ṇibhi̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 3

u̱ta sya na̱ indro̍ vi̱śvaca̍rṣaṇirdi̱vaḥ śardhe̍na̱ māru̍tena su̱kratu̍: |

anu̱ nu sthā̍tyavṛ̱kābhi̍rū̱tibhī̱ ratha̍ṃ ma̱he sa̱naye̱ vāja̍sātaye

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 4

u̱ta sya de̱vo bhuva̍nasya sa̱kṣaṇi̱stvaṣṭā̱ gnābhi̍: sa̱joṣā̍ jūjuva̱d ratha̍m |

il̤ā̱ bhago̍ bṛhaddi̱vota roda̍sī pū̱ṣā pura̍ṃdhira̱śvinā̱vadhā̱ patī̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 5

u̱ta tye de̱vī su̱bhage̍ mithū̱dṛśo̱ṣāsā̱naktā̱ jaga̍tāmapī̱juvā̍ |

stu̱ṣe yad vāṃ pṛthivi̱ navya̍sā̱ vaca̍: sthā̱tuśca̱ vaya̱striva̍yā upa̱stire̍

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 6

tri̱ta ṛ̍bhu̱kṣāḥ sa̍vi̱tā cano̍ dadhe̱'pāṃ napā̍dāśu̱hemā̍ dhi̱yā śami̍

## Verse 7
