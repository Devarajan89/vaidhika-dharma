# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 9

**6 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 1

ni hotā̍ hotṛ̱ṣada̍ne̱ vidā̍nastve̱ṣo dī̍di̱vām̐ a̍sadat su̱dakṣa̍: |
ada̍bdhavratapramati̱rvasi̍ṣṭhaḥ sahasrambha̱raḥ śuci̍jihvo a̱gniḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 2

tvaṃ dū̱tastvamu̍ naḥ para̱spāstvaṃ vasya̱ ā vṛ̍ṣabha praṇe̱tā |

agne̍ to̱kasya̍ na̱stane̍ ta̱nūnā̱mapra̍yuccha̱n dīdya̍d bodhi go̱pāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 3

vi̱dhema̍ te para̱me janma̍nnagne vi̱dhema̱ stomai̱rava̍re sa̱dhasthe̍ |

yasmā̱d yone̍ru̱dāri̍thā̱ yaje̱ taṃ pra tve ha̱vīṃṣi̍ juhure̱ sami̍ddhe

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 4

agne̱ yaja̍sva ha̱viṣā̱ yajī̍yāñchru̱ṣṭī de̱ṣṇama̱bhi gṛ̍ṇīhi̱ rādha̍: |

tvaṃ hyasi̍ rayi̱patī̍ rayī̱ṇāṃ tvaṃ śu̱krasya̱ vaca̍so ma̱notā̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 5

u̱bhaya̍ṃ te̱ na kṣī̍yate vasa̱vya̍ṃ di̱vedi̍ve̱ jāya̍mānasya dasma |

kṛ̱dhi kṣu̱manta̍ṃ jari̱tāra̍magne kṛ̱dhi pati̍ṃ svapa̱tyasya̍ rā̱yaḥ

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 6
