# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 36

**6 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| ṛtudevatāḥ- 1 indro madhuśca, 2maruto mādhavaśca, 3 tvaṣṭā śukraśca, 4 agniḥ śaciśca, 5 indro nabhaśca, 6 mitrāvaruṇau nabhasyaśca| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 1

tubhya̍ṃ hinvā̱no va̍siṣṭa̱ gā a̱po 'dhu̍kṣan tsī̱mavi̍bhi̱radri̍bhi̱rnara̍: |
pibe̍ndra̱ svāhā̱ prahu̍ta̱ṃ vaṣa̍ṭkṛtaṃ ho̱trādā soma̍ṃ pratha̱mo ya īśi̍ṣe

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 2

ya̱jñaiḥ sammi̍ślā̱: pṛṣa̍tībhiṝ̱ṣṭibhi̱ryāma̍ñchu̱bhrāso̍ a̱ñjiṣu̍ pri̱yā u̱ta |

ā̱sadyā̍ ba̱rhirbha̍ratasya sūnavaḥ po̱trādā soma̍ṃ pibatā divo naraḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 3

a̱meva̍ naḥ suhavā̱ ā hi ganta̍na̱ ni ba̱rhiṣi̍ sadatanā̱ raṇi̍ṣṭana |

athā̍ mandasva jujuṣā̱ṇo andha̍sa̱stvaṣṭa̍rde̱vebhi̱rjani̍bhiḥ su̱madga̍ṇaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 4

ā va̍kṣi de̱vām̐ i̱ha vi̍pra̱ yakṣi̍ co̱śan ho̍ta̱rni ṣa̍dā̱ yoni̍ṣu tri̱ṣu |

prati̍ vīhi̱ prasthi̍taṃ so̱myaṃ madhu̱ pibāgnī̍dhrā̱t tava̍ bhā̱gasya̍ tṛpṇuhi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 5

e̱ṣa sya te̍ ta̱nvo̍ nṛmṇa̱vardha̍na̱: saha̱ oja̍: pra̱divi̍ bā̱hvorhi̱taḥ |

tubhya̍ṃ su̱to ma̍ghava̱n tubhya̱mābhṛ̍ta̱stvama̍sya̱ brāhma̍ṇā̱dā tṛ̱pat pi̍ba

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 6

ju̱ṣethā̍ṃ ya̱jñaṃ bodha̍ta̱ṃ hava̍sya me sa̱tto hotā̍ ni̱vida̍: pū̱rvyā anu̍ |

acchā̱ rājā̍nā̱ nama̍ etyā̱vṛta̍ṃ praśā̱strādā pi̍bataṃ so̱myaṃ madhu̍
