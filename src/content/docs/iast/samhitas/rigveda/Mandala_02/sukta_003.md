# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 3

**11 gṛtsamada(āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| āprīsūktaṃ- (1 idhmaḥ samiddho'gnirvā, 2 narāśaṃsaḥ, 3 il̤aḥ, 4 barhiḥ,  5 devīrdvāraḥ, 6 uṣāsānaktā, 7 daivyo hotārau pracetasau, 8 tisro devyaḥsarasvatīl̤ābhāratyaḥ, 9 tvaṣṭā, 10 vanaspatiḥ, 11 svāhākṛtaya,) | triṣṭup, jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 1

sami̍ddho a̱gnirnihi̍taḥ pṛthi̱vyāṃ pra̱tyaṅ viśvā̍ni̱ bhuva̍nānyasthāt |
hotā̍ pāva̱kaḥ pra̱diva̍: sume̱dhā de̱vo de̱vān ya̍jatva̱gnirarha̍n

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 2

narā̱śaṃsa̱: prati̱ dhāmā̍nya̱ñjan ti̱sro diva̱: prati̍ ma̱hnā sva̱rciḥ |

ghṛ̱ta̱pruṣā̱ mana̍sā ha̱vyamu̱ndan mū̱rdhanya̱jñasya̱ sama̍naktu de̱vān

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 3

ī̱l̤i̱to a̍gne̱ mana̍sā no̱ arha̍n de̱vān ya̍kṣi̱ mānu̍ṣā̱tpūrvo̍ a̱dya |

sa ā va̍ha ma̱rutā̱ṃ śardho̱ acyu̍ta̱mindra̍ṃ naro barhi̱ṣada̍ṃ yajadhvam

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 4

deva̍ barhi̱rvardha̍mānaṃ su̱vīra̍ṃ stī̱rṇaṃ rā̱ye su̱bhara̱ṃ vedya̱syām |

ghṛ̱tenā̱ktaṃ va̍savaḥ sīdate̱daṃ viśve̍ devā ādityā ya̱jñiyā̍saḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 5

vi śra̍yantāmurvi̱yā hū̱yamā̍nā̱ dvāro̍ de̱vīḥ su̍prāya̱ṇā namo̍bhiḥ |

vyaca̍svatī̱rvi pra̍thantāmaju̱ryā varṇa̍ṃ punā̱nā ya̱śasa̍ṃ su̱vīra̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 6

sā̱dhvapā̍ṃsi sa̱natā̍ na ukṣi̱te u̱ṣāsā̱naktā̍ va̱yye̍va raṇvi̱te |

tantu̍ṃ ta̱taṃ sa̱ṃvaya̍ntī samī̱cī ya̱jñasya̱ peśa̍: su̱dughe̱ paya̍svatī

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 7

daivyā̱ hotā̍rā pratha̱mā vi̱duṣṭa̍ra ṛ̱ju ya̍kṣata̱: samṛ̱cā va̱puṣṭa̍rā |

de̱vān yaja̍ntāvṛtu̱thā sama̍ñjato̱ nābhā̍ pṛthi̱vyā adhi̱ sānu̍ṣu tri̱ṣu

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 8

sara̍svatī sā̱dhaya̍ntī̱ dhiya̍ṃ na̱ il̤ā̍ de̱vī bhāra̍tī vi̱śvatū̍rtiḥ |

ti̱sro de̱vīḥ sva̱dhayā̍ ba̱rhiredamacchi̍draṃ pāntu śara̱ṇaṃ ni̱ṣadya̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 9

pi̱śaṅga̍rūpaḥ su̱bharo̍ vayo̱dhāḥ śru̱ṣṭī vī̱ro jā̍yate de̱vakā̍maḥ |

pra̱jāṃ tvaṣṭā̱ vi ṣya̍tu̱ nābhi̍ma̱sme athā̍ de̱vānā̱mapye̍tu̱ pātha̍:

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 10

vana̱spati̍ravasṛ̱jannupa̍ sthāda̱gnirha̱viḥ sū̍dayāti̱ pra dhī̱bhiḥ |

tridhā̱ sama̍ktaṃ nayatu prajā̱nan de̱vebhyo̱ daivya̍: śami̱topa̍ ha̱vyam

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 11

ghṛ̱taṃ mi̍mikṣe ghṛ̱tama̍sya̱ yoni̍rghṛ̱te śri̱to ghṛ̱tamva̍sya̱ dhāma̍ |

a̱nu̱ṣva̱dhamā va̍ha mā̱daya̍sva̱ svāhā̍kṛtaṃ vṛṣabha vakṣi ha̱vyam
