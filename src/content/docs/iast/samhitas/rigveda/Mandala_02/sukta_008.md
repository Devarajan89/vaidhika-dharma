# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 8

**6 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| agniḥ|gāyatrī, 6 anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 1

vā̱ja̱yanni̍va̱ nū rathā̱n yogā̍m̐ a̱gnerupa̍ stuhi | ya̱śasta̍masya mī̱l̤ahuṣa̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 2

yaḥ su̍nī̱tho da̍dā̱śuṣe̍ 'ju̱ryo ja̱raya̍nna̱rim | cāru̍pratīka̱ āhu̍taḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 3

ya u̍ śri̱yā dame̱ṣvā do̱ṣoṣasi̍ praśa̱syate̍ | yasya̍ vra̱taṃ na mīya̍te

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 4

ā yaḥ sva1rṇa bhā̱nunā̍ ci̱tro vi̱bhātya̱rciṣā̍ | a̱ñjā̱no a̱jarai̍ra̱bhi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 5

atri̱manu̍ sva̱rājya̍ma̱gnimu̱kthāni̍ vāvṛdhuḥ | viśvā̱ adhi̱ śriyo̍ dadhe

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 8 | **Verse:** 6

a̱gnerindra̍sya̱ soma̍sya de̱vānā̍mū̱tibhi̍rva̱yam | ari̍ṣyantaḥ sacemahya̱bhi ṣyā̍ma pṛtanya̱taḥ
