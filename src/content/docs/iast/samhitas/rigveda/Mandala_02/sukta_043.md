# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 43

**3 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| śakuntaḥ(-kapiñjalarūpīndraḥ)| jagatī, 2 atiśakvarī aṣṭirvā|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 1

pra̱da̱kṣi̱ṇida̱bhi gṛ̍ṇanti kā̱ravo̱ vayo̱ vada̍nta ṛtu̱thā śa̱kunta̍yaḥ |
u̱bhe vācau̍ vadati sāma̱gā i̍va gāya̱traṃ ca̱ traiṣṭu̍bha̱ṃ cānu̍ rājati

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 2

u̱dgā̱teva̍ śakune̱ sāma̍ gāyasi brahmapu̱tra i̍va̱ sava̍neṣu śaṃsasi |

vṛṣe̍va vā̱jī śiśu̍matīra̱pītyā̍ sa̱rvato̍ naḥ śakune bha̱dramā va̍da

vi̱śvato̍ naḥ śakune̱ puṇya̱mā va̍da

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 3

ā̱vada̱ṃstvaṃ śa̍kune bha̱dramā va̍da tū̱ṣṇīmāsī̍naḥ suma̱tiṃ ci̍kiddhi naḥ |

yadu̱tpata̱n vada̍si karka̱rirya̍thā bṛ̱had va̍dema vi̱dathe̍ su̱vīrā̍:
