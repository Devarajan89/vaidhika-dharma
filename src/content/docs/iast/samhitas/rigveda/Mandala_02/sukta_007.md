# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 7

**6 somāhutirbhārgavaḥ| agniḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 1

śreṣṭha̍ṃ yaviṣṭha bhāra̱tā'gne̍ dyu̱manta̱mā bha̍ra | vaso̍ puru̱spṛha̍ṃ ra̱yim

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 2

mā no̱ arā̍tirīśata de̱vasya̱ martya̍sya ca | parṣi̱ tasyā̍ u̱ta dvi̱ṣaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 3

viśvā̍ u̱ta tvayā̍ va̱yaṃ dhārā̍ uda̱nyā̍ iva | ati̍ gāhemahi̱ dviṣa̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 4

śuci̍: pāvaka̱ vandyo 'gne̍ bṛ̱had vi ro̍case | tvaṃ ghṛ̱tebhi̱rāhu̍taḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 5

tvaṃ no̍ asi bhāra̱tā'gne̍ va̱śābhi̍ru̱kṣabhi̍: | a̱ṣṭāpa̍dībhi̱rāhu̍taḥ

## Verse 6
