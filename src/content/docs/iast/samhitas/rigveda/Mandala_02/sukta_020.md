# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 20

**9 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup, 3 virāḍ rūpā|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 1

va̱yaṃ te̱ vaya̍ indra vi̱ddhi ṣu ṇa̱: pra bha̍rāmahe vāja̱yurna ratha̍m |
vi̱pa̱nyavo̱ dīdhya̍to manī̱ṣā su̱mnamiya̍kṣanta̱stvāva̍to̱ nṝn

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 2

tvaṃ na̍ indra̱ tvābhi̍rū̱tī tvā̍ya̱to a̍bhiṣṭi̱pāsi̱ janā̍n |

tvami̱no dā̱śuṣo̍ varū̱tetthādhī̍ra̱bhi yo nakṣa̍ti tvā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 3

sa no̱ yuvendro̍ jo̱hūtra̱: sakhā̍ śi̱vo na̱rāma̍stu pā̱tā |

yaḥ śaṃsa̍nta̱ṃ yaḥ śa̍śamā̱namū̱tī paca̍ntaṃ ca stu̱vanta̍ṃ ca pra̱ṇeṣa̍t

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 4

tamu̍ stuṣa̱ indra̱ṃ taṃ gṛ̍ṇīṣe̱ yasmi̍npu̱rā vā̍vṛ̱dhuḥ śā̍śa̱duśca̍ |

sa vasva̱: kāma̍ṃ pīparadiyā̱no bra̍hmaṇya̱to nūta̍nasyā̱yoḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 5

so aṅgi̍rasāmu̱cathā̍ juju̱ṣvān brahmā̍ tūto̱dindro̍ gā̱tumi̱ṣṇan |

mu̱ṣṇannu̱ṣasa̱: sūrye̍ṇa sta̱vānaśna̍sya cicchiśnathat pū̱rvyāṇi̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 6

sa ha̍ śru̱ta indro̱ nāma̍ de̱va ū̱rdhvo bhu̍va̱nmanu̍ṣe da̱smata̍maḥ |

ava̍ pri̱yama̍rśasā̱nasya̍ sā̱hvāñchiro̍ bharad dā̱sasya̍ sva̱dhāvā̍n

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 7

sa vṛ̍tra̱hendra̍: kṛ̱ṣṇayo̍nīḥ puraṃda̱ro dāsī̍rairaya̱dvi |

aja̍naya̱nmana̍ve̱ kṣāma̱paśca̍ sa̱trā śaṃsa̱ṃ yaja̍mānasya tūtot

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 8

tasmai̍ tava̱sya1manu̍ dāyi sa̱trendrā̍ya de̱vebhi̱rarṇa̍sātau |

prati̱ yada̍sya̱ vajra̍ṃ bā̱hvordhurha̱tvī dasyū̱n pura̱ āya̍sī̱rni tā̍rīt

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 9

nū̱naṃ sā te̱ prati̱ vara̍ṃ jari̱tre du̍hī̱yadi̍ndra̱ dakṣi̍ṇā ma̱ghonī̍ |

śikṣā̍ sto̱tṛbhyo̱ māti̍ dha̱gbhago̍ no bṛ̱had va̍dema vi̱dathe̍ su̱vīrā̍:
