# Maṇḍala 2 — Sūktam 14

**12 gṛtsamada (āṅgirasaḥ śaunahotraḥ paścād) bhārgavaḥ śaunakaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 1

adhva̍ryavo̱ bhara̱tendrā̍ya̱ soma̱māma̍trebhiḥ siñcatā̱ madya̱mandha̍: |
kā̱mī hi vī̱raḥ sada̍masya pī̱tiṃ ju̱hota̱ vṛṣṇe̱ tadide̱ṣa va̍ṣṭi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 2

adhva̍ryavo̱ yo a̱po va̍vri̱vāṃsa̍ṃ vṛ̱traṃ ja̱ghānā̱śanye̍va vṛ̱kṣam |

tasmā̍ e̱taṃ bha̍rata tadva̱śāya̍m̐ e̱ṣa indro̍ arhati pī̱tima̍sya

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 3

adhva̍ryavo̱ yo dṛbhī̍kaṃ ja̱ghāna̱ yo gā u̱dāja̱dapa̱ hi va̱laṃ vaḥ |

tasmā̍ e̱tama̱ntari̍kṣe̱ na vāta̱mindra̱ṃ somai̱rorṇu̍ta̱ jūrna vastrai̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 4

adhva̍ryavo̱ ya ura̍ṇaṃ ja̱ghāna̱ nava̍ ca̱khvāṃsa̍ṃ nava̱tiṃ ca̍ bā̱hūn |

yo arbu̍da̱mava̍ nī̱cā ba̍bā̱dhe tamindra̱ṃ soma̍sya bhṛ̱the hi̍nota

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 5

adhva̍ryavo̱ yaḥ svaśna̍ṃ ja̱ghāna̱ yaḥ śuṣṇa̍ma̱śuṣa̱ṃ yo vya̍ṃsam |

yaḥ pipru̱ṃ namu̍ci̱ṃ yo ru̍dhi̱krāṃ tasmā̱ indrā̱yāndha̍so juhota

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 6

adhva̍ryavo̱ yaḥ śa̱taṃ śamba̍rasya̱ puro̍ bi̱bhedāśma̍neva pū̱rvīḥ |

yo va̱rcina̍: śa̱tamindra̍: sa̱hasra̍ma̱pāva̍pa̱d bhara̍tā̱ soma̍masmai

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 7

adhva̍ryavo̱ yaḥ śa̱tamā sa̱hasra̱ṃ bhūmyā̍ u̱pasthe'va̍pajjagha̱nvān |

kutsa̍syā̱yora̍tithi̱gvasya̍ vī̱rān nyāvṛ̍ṇa̱g bhara̍tā̱ soma̍masmai

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 8

adhva̍ryavo̱ yanna̍raḥ kā̱mayā̍dhve śru̱ṣṭī vaha̍nto naśathā̱ tadindre̍ |

gabha̍stipūtaṃ bharata śru̱tāyendrā̍ya̱ soma̍ṃ yajyavo juhota

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 9

adhva̍ryava̱: karta̍nā śru̱ṣṭima̍smai̱ vane̱ nipū̍ta̱ṃ vana̱ unna̍yadhvam |

ju̱ṣā̱ṇo hastya̍ma̱bhi vā̍vaśe va̱ indrā̍ya̱ soma̍ṃ madi̱raṃ ju̍hota

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 10

adhva̍ryava̱: paya̱sodha̱ryathā̱ goḥ some̍bhirīṃ pṛṇatā bho̱jamindra̍m |

vedā̱hama̍sya̱ nibhṛ̍taṃ ma e̱tad ditsa̍nta̱ṃ bhūyo̍ yaja̱taści̍keta

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 11

adhva̍ryavo̱ yo di̱vyasya̱ vasvo̱ yaḥ pārthi̍vasya̱ kṣamya̍sya̱ rājā̍ |

tamūrda̍ra̱ṃ na pṛ̍ṇatā̱ yave̱nendra̱ṃ some̍bhi̱stadapo̍ vo astu

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 2 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 12

a̱smabhya̱ṃ tad va̍so dā̱nāya̱ rādha̱: sama̍rthayasva ba̱hu te̍ vasa̱vya̍m |

indra̱ yacci̱traṃ śra̍va̱syā anu̱ dyūnbṛ̱hadva̍dema vi̱dathe̍ su̱vīrā̍:
