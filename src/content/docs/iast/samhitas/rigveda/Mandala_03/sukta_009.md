# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 9

**9 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| agniḥ| bṛhatī, 9 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 1

sakhā̍yastvā vavṛmahe de̱vaṃ martā̍sa ū̱taye̍ |
a̱pāṃ napā̍taṃ su̱bhaga̍ṃ su̱dīdi̍tiṃ su̱pratū̍rtimane̱hasa̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 2

kāya̍māno va̱nā tvaṃ yanmā̱tṝraja̍ganna̱paḥ |

na tat te̍ agne pra̱mṛṣe̍ ni̱varta̍na̱ṃ yad dū̱re sanni̱hābha̍vaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 3

ati̍ tṛ̱ṣṭaṃ va̍vakṣi̱thāthai̱va su̱manā̍ asi |

praprā̱nye yanti̱ parya̱nya ā̍sate̱ yeṣā̍ṃ sa̱khye asi̍ śri̱taḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 4

ī̱yi̱vāṃsa̱mati̱ sridha̱: śaśva̍tī̱rati̍ sa̱ścata̍: |

anvī̍mavindan nici̱rāso̍ a̱druho̱'psu si̱ṃhami̍va śri̱tam

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 5

sa̱sṛ̱vāṃsa̍miva̱ tmanā̱ 'gnimi̱tthā ti̱rohi̍tam |

aina̍ṃ nayanmāta̱riśvā̍ parā̱vato̍ de̱vebhyo̍ mathi̱taṃ pari̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 6

taṃ tvā̱ martā̍ agṛbhṇata de̱vebhyo̍ havyavāhana |

viśvā̱n yad ya̱jñām̐ a̍bhi̱pāsi̍ mānuṣa̱ tava̱ kratvā̍ yaviṣṭhya

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 7

tad bha̱draṃ tava̍ da̱ṃsanā̱ pākā̍ya cicchadayati |

tvāṃ yada̍gne pa̱śava̍: sa̱māsa̍te̱ sami̍ddhamapiśarva̱re

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 8

ā ju̍hotā svadhva̱raṃ śī̱raṃ pā̍va̱kaśo̍ciṣam |

ā̱śuṃ dū̱tama̍ji̱raṃ pra̱tnamīḍya̍ṃ śru̱ṣṭī de̱vaṃ sa̍paryata

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 9 | **Verse:** 9

trīṇi̍ śa̱tā trī sa̱hasrā̍ṇya̱gniṃ tri̱ṃśacca̍ de̱vā nava̍ cāsaparyan |

aukṣa̍n ghṛ̱tairastṛ̍ṇan ba̱rhira̍smā̱ ādiddhotā̍ra̱ṃ nya̍sādayanta
