# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 40

**9 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 1

indra̍ tvā vṛṣa̱bhaṃ va̱yaṃ su̱te some̍ havāmahe | sa pā̍hi̱ madhvo̱ andha̍saḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 2

indra̍ kratu̱vida̍ṃ su̱taṃ soma̍ṃ harya puruṣṭuta | pibā vṛ̍ṣasva̱ tātṛ̍pim

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 3

indra̱ pra ṇo̍ dhi̱tāvā̍naṃ ya̱jñaṃ viśve̍bhirde̱vebhi̍: | ti̱ra sta̍vāna viśpate

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 4

indra̱ somā̍: su̱tā i̱me tava̱ pra ya̍nti satpate | kṣaya̍ṃ ca̱ndrāsa̱ inda̍vaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 5

da̱dhi̱ṣvā ja̱ṭhare̍ su̱taṃ soma̍mindra̱ vare̍ṇyam | tava̍ dyu̱kṣāsa̱ inda̍vaḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 6

girva̍ṇaḥ pā̱hi na̍: su̱taṃ madho̱rdhārā̍bhirajyase | indra̱ tvādā̍ta̱midyaśa̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 7

a̱bhi dyu̱mnāni̍ va̱nina̱ indra̍ṃ sacante̱ akṣi̍tā | pī̱tvī soma̍sya vāvṛdhe

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 8

a̱rvā̱vato̍ na̱ ā ga̍hi parā̱vata̍śca vṛtrahan | i̱mā ju̍ṣasva no̱ gira̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 9

yada̍nta̱rā pa̍rā̱vata̍marvā̱vata̍ṃ ca hū̱yase̍ | indre̱ha tata̱ ā ga̍hi
