# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 7

**11 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 1

pra ya ā̱ruḥ śi̍tipṛ̱ṣṭhasya̍ dhā̱serā mā̱tarā̍ viviśuḥ sa̱pta vāṇī̍: |
pa̱ri̱kṣitā̍ pi̱tarā̱ saṃ ca̍rete̱ pra sa̍rsrāte dī̱rghamāyu̍: pra̱yakṣe̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 2

di̱vakṣa̍so dhe̱navo̱ vṛṣṇo̱ aśvā̍ de̱vīrā ta̍sthau̱ madhu̍ma̱d vaha̍ntīḥ |

ṛ̱tasya̍ tvā̱ sada̍si kṣema̱yanta̱ṃ paryekā̍ carati varta̱niṃ gauḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 3

ā sī̍marohat su̱yamā̱ bhava̍ntī̱: pati̍ściki̱tvān ra̍yi̱vid ra̍yī̱ṇām |

pra nīla̍pṛṣṭho ata̱sasya̍ dhā̱sestā a̍vāsayat puru̱dhapra̍tīkaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 4

mahi̍ tvā̱ṣṭramū̱rjaya̍ntīraju̱ryaṃ sta̍bhū̱yamā̍naṃ va̱hato̍ vahanti |

vyaṅge̍bhirdidyutā̱naḥ sa̱dhastha̱ ekā̍miva̱ roda̍sī̱ ā vi̍veśa

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 5

jā̱nanti̱ vṛṣṇo̍ aru̱ṣasya̱ śeva̍mu̱ta bra̱dhnasya̱ śāsa̍ne raṇanti |

di̱vo̱ruca̍: su̱ruco̱ roca̍mānā̱ il̤ā̱ yeṣā̱ṃ gaṇyā̱ māhi̍nā̱ gīḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 6

u̱to pi̱tṛbhyā̍ṃ pra̱vidānu̱ ghoṣa̍ṃ ma̱ho ma̱hadbhyā̍manayanta śū̱ṣam |

u̱kṣā ha̱ yatra̱ pari̱ dhāna̍ma̱ktoranu̱ svaṃ dhāma̍ jari̱turva̱vakṣa̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 7

a̱dhva̱ryubhi̍: pa̱ñcabhi̍: sa̱pta viprā̍: pri̱yaṃ ra̍kṣante̱ nihi̍taṃ pa̱daṃ veḥ |

prāñco̍ madantyu̱kṣaṇo̍ aju̱ryā de̱vā de̱vānā̱manu̱ hi vra̱tā guḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 8

daivyā̱ hotā̍rā pratha̱mā nyṛ̍ñje sa̱pta pṛ̱kṣāsa̍: sva̱dhayā̍ madanti |

ṛ̱taṃ śaṃsa̍nta ṛ̱tamit ta ā̍hu̱ranu̍ vra̱taṃ vra̍ta̱pā dīdhyā̍nāḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 9

vṛ̱ṣā̱yante̍ ma̱he atyā̍ya pū̱rvīrvṛṣṇe̍ ci̱trāya̍ ra̱śmaya̍: suyā̱māḥ |

deva̍ hotarma̱ndrata̍raściki̱tvān ma̱ho de̱vān roda̍sī̱ eha va̍kṣi

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 7 | **Verse:** 10

pṛ̱kṣapra̍yajo draviṇaḥ su̱vāca̍: suke̱tava̍ u̱ṣaso̍ re̱vadū̍ṣuḥ |

u̱to ci̍dagne mahi̱nā pṛ̍thi̱vyāḥ kṛ̱taṃ ci̱dena̱: saṃ ma̱he da̍śasya
