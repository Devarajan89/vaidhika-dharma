# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 50

**5 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 1

indra̱: svāhā̍ pibatu̱ yasya̱ soma̍ ā̱gatyā̱ tumro̍ vṛṣa̱bho ma̱rutvā̍n |
oru̱vyacā̍: pṛṇatāme̱bhirannai̱rāsya̍ ha̱vista̱nva1: kāma̍mṛdhyāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 2

ā te̍ sapa̱ryū ja̱vase̍ yunajmi̱ yayo̱ranu̍ pra̱diva̍: śru̱ṣṭimāva̍: |

i̱ha tvā̍ dheyu̱rhara̍yaḥ suśipra̱ pibā̱ tva1sya suṣu̍tasya̱ cāro̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 3

gobhi̍rmimi̱kṣuṃ da̍dhire supā̱ramindra̱ṃ jyaiṣṭhyā̍ya̱ dhāya̍se gṛṇā̱nāḥ |

ma̱ndā̱naḥ soma̍ṃ papi̱vām̐ ṛ̍jīṣi̱n tsama̱smabhya̍ṃ puru̱dhā gā i̍ṣaṇya

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 4

i̱maṃ kāma̍ṃ mandayā̱ gobhi̱raśvai̍śca̱ndrava̍tā̱ rādha̍sā pa̱pratha̍śca |

sva̱ryavo̍ ma̱tibhi̱stubhya̱ṃ viprā̱ indrā̍ya̱ vāha̍: kuśi̱kāso̍ akran

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 50 | **Verse:** 5

śu̱naṃ hu̍vema ma̱ghavā̍na̱mindra̍ma̱smin bhare̱ nṛta̍ma̱ṃ vāja̍sātau |

śṛ̱ṇvanta̍mu̱gramū̱taye̍ sa̱matsu̱ ghnanta̍ṃ vṛ̱trāṇi̍ sa̱ṃjita̱ṃ dhanā̍nām
