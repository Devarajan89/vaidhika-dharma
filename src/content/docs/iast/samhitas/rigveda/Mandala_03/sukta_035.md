# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 35

**11 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 1

tiṣṭhā̱ harī̱ ratha̱ ā yu̱jyamā̍nā yā̱hi vā̱yurna ni̱yuto̍ no̱ accha̍ |
pibā̱syandho̍ a̱bhisṛ̍ṣṭo a̱sme indra̱ svāhā̍ rari̱mā te̱ madā̍ya

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 2

upā̍ji̱rā pu̍ruhū̱tāya̱ saptī̱ harī̱ ratha̍sya dhū̱rṣvā yu̍najmi |

dra̱vad yathā̱ sambhṛ̍taṃ vi̱śvata̍ści̱dupe̱maṃ ya̱jñamā va̍hāta̱ indra̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 3

upo̍ nayasva̱ vṛṣa̍ṇā tapu̱ṣpotema̍va̱ tvaṃ vṛ̍ṣabha svadhāvaḥ |

grase̍tā̱maśvā̱ vi mu̍ce̱ha śoṇā̍ di̱vedi̍ve sa̱dṛśī̍raddhi dhā̱nāḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 4

brahma̍ṇā te brahma̱yujā̍ yunajmi̱ harī̱ sakhā̍yā sadha̱māda̍ ā̱śū |

sthi̱raṃ ratha̍ṃ su̱khami̍ndrādhi̱tiṣṭha̍n prajā̱nan vi̱dvām̐ upa̍ yāhi̱ soma̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 5

mā te̱ harī̱ vṛṣa̍ṇā vī̱tapṛ̍ṣṭhā̱ ni rī̍rama̱nyaja̍mānāso a̱nye |

a̱tyāyā̍hi̱ śaśva̍to va̱yaṃ te'ra̍ṃ su̱tebhi̍: kṛṇavāma̱ somai̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 6

tavā̱yaṃ soma̱stvamehya̱rvāṅ śa̍śvatta̱maṃ su̱manā̍ a̱sya pā̍hi |

a̱smin ya̱jñe ba̱rhiṣyā ni̱ṣadyā̍ dadhi̱ṣvemaṃ ja̱ṭhara̱ indu̍mindra

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 7

stī̱rṇaṃ te̍ ba̱rhiḥ su̱ta i̍ndra̱ soma̍: kṛ̱tā dhā̱nā atta̍ve te̱ hari̍bhyām |

tado̍kase puru̱śākā̍ya̱ vṛṣṇe̍ ma̱rutva̍te̱ tubhya̍ṃ rā̱tā ha̱vīṃṣi̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 8

i̱maṃ nara̱: parva̍tā̱stubhya̱māpa̱: sami̍ndra̱ gobhi̱rmadhu̍mantamakran |

tasyā̱gatyā̍ su̱manā̍ ṛṣva pāhi prajā̱nan vi̱dvānpa̱thyā̱3 anu̱ svāḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 9

yām̐ ābha̍jo ma̱ruta̍ indra̱ some̱ ye tvāmava̍rdha̱nnabha̍van ga̱ṇaste̍ |

tebhi̍re̱taṃ sa̱joṣā̍ vāvaśā̱no̱3'gneḥ pi̍ba ji̱hvayā̱ soma̍mindra

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 10

indra̱ piba̍ sva̱dhayā̍ cit su̱tasyā̱'gnervā̍ pāhi ji̱hvayā̍ yajatra |

a̱dhva̱ryorvā̱ praya̍taṃ śakra̱ hastā̱ddhotu̍rvā ya̱jñaṃ ha̱viṣo̍ juṣasva
