# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 59

**9 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| mitraḥ| triṣṭup, 6-9 gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 1

mi̱tro janā̍n yātayati bruvā̱ṇo mi̱tro dā̍dhāra pṛthi̱vīmu̱ta dyām |
mi̱traḥ kṛ̱ṣṭīrani̍miṣā̱bhi ca̍ṣṭe mi̱trāya̍ ha̱vyaṃ ghṛ̱tava̍jjuhota

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 2

pra sa mi̍tra̱ marto̍ astu̱ praya̍svā̱n yasta̍ āditya̱ śikṣa̍ti vra̱tena̍ |

na ha̍nyate̱ na jī̍yate̱ tvoto̱ naina̱maṃho̍ aśno̱tyanti̍to̱ na dū̱rāt

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 3

a̱na̱mī̱vāsa̱ il̤a̍yā̱ mada̍nto mi̱tajña̍vo̱ vari̍ma̱nnā pṛ̍thi̱vyāḥ |

ā̱di̱tyasya̍ vra̱tamu̍pakṣi̱yanto̍ va̱yaṃ mi̱trasya̍ suma̱tau syā̍ma

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 4

a̱yaṃ mi̱tro na̍ma̱sya̍: su̱śevo̱ rājā̍ sukṣa̱tro a̍janiṣṭa ve̱dhāḥ |

tasya̍ va̱yaṃ su̍ma̱tau ya̱jñiya̱syā'pi̍ bha̱dre sau̍mana̱se syā̍ma

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 5

ma̱hām̐ ā̍di̱tyo nama̍sopa̱sadyo̍ yāta̱yajja̍no gṛṇa̱te su̱śeva̍: |

tasmā̍ e̱tatpanya̍tamāya̱ juṣṭa̍ma̱gnau mi̱trāya̍ ha̱virā ju̍hota

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 6

mi̱trasya̍ carṣaṇī̱dhṛto'vo̍ de̱vasya̍ sāna̱si | dyu̱mnaṃ ci̱traśra̍vastamam

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 7

a̱bhi yo ma̍hi̱nā diva̍ṃ mi̱tro ba̱bhūva̍ sa̱prathā̍: | a̱bhi śravo̍bhiḥ pṛthi̱vīm

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 8

mi̱trāya̱ pañca̍ yemire̱ janā̍ a̱bhiṣṭi̍śavase | sa de̱vān viśvā̍n bibharti

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 9

mi̱tro de̱veṣvā̱yuṣu̱ janā̍ya vṛ̱ktaba̍rhiṣe | iṣa̍ i̱ṣṭavra̍tā akaḥ
