# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 22

**5 gāthī kauśikaḥ| agniḥ, 4 purīṣyā agnayaḥ | triṣṭup, 4 anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 1

a̱yaṃ so a̱gniryasmi̱n tsoma̱mindra̍: su̱taṃ da̱dhe ja̱ṭhare̍ vāvaśā̱naḥ |
sa̱ha̱sriṇa̱ṃ vāja̱matya̱ṃ na sapti̍ṃ sasa̱vān tsan tstū̍yase jātavedaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 2

agne̱ yat te̍ di̱vi varca̍: pṛthi̱vyāṃ yadoṣa̍dhīṣva̱psvā ya̍jatra |

yenā̱ntari̍kṣamu̱rvā̍ta̱tantha̍ tve̱ṣaḥ sa bhā̱nura̍rṇa̱vo nṛ̱cakṣā̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 3

agne̍ di̱vo arṇa̱macchā̍ jigā̱syacchā̍ de̱vām̐ ū̍ciṣe̱ dhiṣṇyā̱ ye |

yā ro̍ca̱ne pa̱rastā̱t sūrya̍sya̱ yāścā̱vastā̍dupa̱tiṣṭha̍nta̱ āpa̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 4

pu̱rī̱ṣyā̍so a̱gnaya̍: prāva̱ṇebhi̍: sa̱joṣa̍saḥ |

ju̱ṣantā̍ṃ ya̱jñama̱druho̍'namī̱vā iṣo̍ ma̱hīḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 22 | **Verse:** 5

il̤ā̍magne puru̱daṃsa̍ṃ sa̱niṃ goḥ śa̍śvatta̱maṃ hava̍mānāya sādha |

syānna̍: sū̱nustana̍yo vi̱jāvā 'gne̱ sā te̍ suma̱tirbhū̍tva̱sme
