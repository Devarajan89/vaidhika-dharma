# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 2

**15 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| vaiśvānaro'gniḥ| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 1

vai̱śvā̱na̱rāya̍ dhi̱ṣaṇā̍mṛtā̱vṛdhe̍ ghṛ̱taṃ na pū̱tama̱gnaye̍ janāmasi |
dvi̱tā hotā̍ra̱ṃ manu̍ṣaśca vā̱ghato̍ dhi̱yā ratha̱ṃ na kuli̍śa̱: samṛ̍ṇvati

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 2

sa ro̍cayajja̱nuṣā̱ roda̍sī u̱bhe sa mā̱trora̍bhavatpu̱tra īḍya̍: |

ha̱vya̱vāl̤a̱gnira̱jara̱ścano̍hito dū̱l̤abho̍ vi̱śāmati̍thirvi̱bhāva̍suḥ

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 3

kratvā̱ dakṣa̍sya̱ taru̍ṣo̱ vidha̍rmaṇi de̱vāso̍ a̱gniṃ ja̍nayanta̱ citti̍bhiḥ |

ru̱ru̱cā̱naṃ bhā̱nunā̱ jyoti̍ṣā ma̱hāmatya̱ṃ na vāja̍ṃ sani̱ṣyannupa̍ bruve

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 4

rā̱tiṃ bhṛgū̍ṇāmu̱śija̍ṃ ka̱vikra̍tuma̱gniṃ rāja̍ntaṃ di̱vyena̍ śo̱ciṣā̍

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 5

ya̱tasru̍caḥ su̱ruca̍ṃ vi̱śvade̍vyaṃ ru̱draṃ ya̱jñānā̱ṃ sādha̍diṣṭima̱pasā̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 6

pāva̍kaśoce̱ tava̱ hi kṣaya̱ṃ pari̱ hota̍rya̱jñeṣu̍ vṛ̱ktaba̍rhiṣo̱ nara̍: |

agne̱ duva̍ i̱cchamā̍nāsa̱ āpya̱mupā̍sate̱ dravi̍ṇaṃ dhehi̱ tebhya̍:

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 7

so a̍dhva̱rāya̱ pari̍ ṇīyate ka̱viratyo̱ na vāja̍sātaye̱ cano̍hitaḥ
