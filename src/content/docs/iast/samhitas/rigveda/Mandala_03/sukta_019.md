# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 19

**5 gāthī kauśikaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 1

a̱gniṃ hotā̍ra̱ṃ pra vṛ̍ṇe mi̱yedhe̱ gṛtsa̍ṃ ka̱viṃ vi̍śva̱vida̱mamū̍ram |
sa no̍ yakṣad de̱vatā̍tā̱ yajī̍yān rā̱ye vājā̍ya vanate ma̱ghāni̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 2

pra te̍ agne ha̱viṣma̍tīmiya̱rmyacchā̍ sudyu̱mnāṃ rā̱tinī̍ṃ ghṛ̱tācī̍m |

pra̱da̱kṣi̱ṇid de̱vatā̍timurā̱ṇaḥ saṃ rā̱tibhi̱rvasu̍bhirya̱jñama̍śret

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 3

sa tejī̍yasā̱ mana̍sā̱ tvota̍ u̱ta śi̍kṣa svapa̱tyasya̍ śi̱kṣoḥ |

agne̍ rā̱yo nṛta̍masya̱ prabhū̍tau bhū̱yāma̍ te suṣṭu̱taya̍śca̱ vasva̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 4

bhūrī̍ṇi̱ hi tve da̍dhi̱re anī̱kā 'gne̍ de̱vasya̱ yajya̍vo̱ janā̍saḥ |

sa ā va̍ha de̱vatā̍tiṃ yaviṣṭha̱ śardho̱ yada̱dya di̱vyaṃ yajā̍si

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 19 | **Verse:** 5

yat tvā̱ hotā̍rama̱naja̍n mi̱yedhe̍ niṣā̱daya̍nto ya̱jathā̍ya de̱vāḥ |

sa tvaṃ no̍ agne'vi̱teha bo̱dhyadhi̱ śravā̍ṃsi dhehi nasta̱nūṣu̍
