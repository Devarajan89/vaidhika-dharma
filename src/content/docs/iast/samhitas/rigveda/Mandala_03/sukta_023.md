# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 23

**5 devaśravā devavātaśca bhāratau| agniḥ|triṣṭup, 3 satobṛhatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 1

nirma̍thita̱: sudhi̍ta̱ ā sa̱dhasthe̱ yuvā̍ ka̱vira̍dhva̱rasya̍ praṇe̱tā |
jūrya̍tsva̱gnira̱jaro̱ vane̱ṣvatrā̍ dadhe a̱mṛta̍ṃ jā̱tave̍dāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 2

ama̍nthiṣṭā̱ṃ bhāra̍tā re̱vada̱gniṃ de̱vaśra̍vā de̱vavā̍taḥ su̱dakṣa̍m |

agne̱ vi pa̍śya bṛha̱tābhi rā̱yeṣāṃ no̍ ne̱tā bha̍vatā̱danu̱ dyūn

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 3

daśa̱ kṣipa̍: pū̱rvyaṃ sī̍majījana̱n tsujā̍taṃ mā̱tṛṣu̍ pri̱yam |

a̱gniṃ stu̍hi daivavā̱taṃ de̍vaśravo̱ yo janā̍nā̱masa̍d va̱śī

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 4

ni tvā̍ dadhe̱ vara̱ ā pṛ̍thi̱vyā il̤ā̍yāspa̱de su̍dina̱tve ahnā̍m |

dṛ̱ṣadva̍tyā̱ṃ mānu̍ṣa āpa̱yāyā̱ṃ sara̍svatyāṃ re̱vada̍gne didīhi

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 5

il̤ā̍magne puru̱daṃsa̍ṃ sa̱niṃ goḥ śa̍śvatta̱maṃ hava̍mānāya sādha |

syānna̍: sū̱nustana̍yo vi̱jāvāgne̱ sā te̍ suma̱tirbhū̍tva̱sme
