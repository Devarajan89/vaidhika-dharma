# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 44

**5 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| bṛhatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 1

a̱yaṃ te̍ astu harya̱taḥ soma̱ ā hari̍bhiḥ su̱taḥ |
ju̱ṣā̱ṇa i̍ndra̱ hari̍bhirna̱ ā ga̱hyā ti̍ṣṭha̱ hari̍ta̱ṃ ratha̍m

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 44 | **Verse:** 2

vi̱dvām̐ści̍ki̱tvān ha̍ryaśva vardhasa̱ indra̱ viśvā̍ a̱bhi śriya̍:
