# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 52

**8 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ|indraḥ| triṣṭup, 1-4gāyatrī,  6 jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 1

dhā̱nāva̍ntaṃ kara̱mbhiṇa̍mapū̱pava̍ntamu̱kthina̍m | indra̍ prā̱tarju̍ṣasva naḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 2

pu̱ro̱l̤āśa̍ṃ paca̱tya̍ṃ ju̱ṣasve̱ndrā gu̍rasva ca | tubhya̍ṃ ha̱vyāni̍ sisrate

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 3

pu̱ro̱l̤āśa̍ṃ ca no̱ ghaso̍ jo̱ṣayā̍se̱ gira̍śca naḥ | va̱dhū̱yuri̍va̱ yoṣa̍ṇām

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 4

pu̱ro̱l̤āśa̍ṃ sanaśruta prātaḥsā̱ve ju̍ṣasva naḥ | indra̱ kratu̱rhi te̍ bṛ̱han

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 5

mādhya̍ṃdinasya̱ sava̍nasya dhā̱nāḥ pu̍ro̱l̤āśa̍mindra kṛṣve̱ha cāru̍m |

pra yat sto̱tā ja̍ri̱tā tūrṇya̍rtho vṛṣā̱yamā̍ṇa̱ upa̍ gī̱rbhirīṭṭe̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 6

tṛ̱tīye̍ dhā̱nāḥ sava̍ne puruṣṭuta puro̱l̤āśa̱māhu̍taṃ māmahasva naḥ |

ṛ̱bhu̱manta̱ṃ vāja̍vantaṃ tvā kave̱ praya̍svanta̱ upa̍ śikṣema dhī̱tibhi̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 7

pū̱ṣa̱ṇvate̍ te cakṛmā kara̱mbhaṃ hari̍vate̱ harya̍śvāya dhā̱nāḥ |

a̱pū̱pama̍ddhi̱ saga̍ṇo ma̱rudbhi̱: soma̍ṃ piba vṛtra̱hā śū̍ra vi̱dvān

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 52 | **Verse:** 8

prati̍ dhā̱nā bha̍rata̱ tūya̍masmai puro̱l̤āśa̍ṃ vī̱rata̍māya nṛ̱ṇām |

di̱vedi̍ve sa̱dṛśī̍rindra̱ tubhya̱ṃ vardha̍ntu tvā soma̱peyā̍ya dhṛṣṇo
