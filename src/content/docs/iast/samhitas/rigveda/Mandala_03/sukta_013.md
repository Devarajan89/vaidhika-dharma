# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 13

**7 ṛṣabho vaiśvāmitraḥ| agniḥ| anuṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 1

pra vo̍ de̱vāyā̱gnaye̱ barhi̍ṣṭhamarcāsmai |
gama̍d de̱vebhi̱rā sa no̱ yaji̍ṣṭho ba̱rhirā sa̍dat

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 2

ṛ̱tāvā̱ yasya̱ roda̍sī̱ dakṣa̱ṃ saca̍nta ū̱taya̍: |

ha̱viṣma̍nta̱stamī̍l̤ate̱ taṃ sa̍ni̱ṣyanto'va̍se

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 3

sa ya̱ntā vipra̍ eṣā̱ṃ sa ya̱jñānā̱mathā̱ hi ṣaḥ |

a̱gniṃ taṃ vo̍ duvasyata̱ dātā̱ yo vani̍tā ma̱gham

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 4

sa na̱: śarmā̍ṇi vī̱taye̱'gnirya̍cchatu̱ śaṃta̍mā |

yato̍ naḥ pru̱ṣṇava̱d vasu̍ di̱vi kṣi̱tibhyo̍ a̱psvā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 5

dī̱di̱vāṃsa̱mapū̍rvya̱ṃ vasvī̍bhirasya dhī̱tibhi̍: |

ṛkvā̍ṇo a̱gnimi̍ndhate̱ hotā̍raṃ vi̱śpati̍ṃ vi̱śām

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 6

u̱ta no̱ brahma̍nnaviṣa u̱ktheṣu̍ deva̱hūta̍maḥ |

śaṃ na̍: śocā ma̱rudvṛ̱dho'gne̍ sahasra̱sāta̍maḥ
