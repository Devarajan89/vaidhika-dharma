# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 15

**7 kātya utkīlaḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 1

vi pāja̍sā pṛ̱thunā̱ śośu̍cāno̱ bādha̍sva dvi̱ṣo ra̱kṣaso̱ amī̍vāḥ |
su̱śarma̍ṇo bṛha̱taḥ śarma̍ṇi syāma̱gnera̱haṃ su̱hava̍sya̱ praṇī̍tau

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 2

tvaṃ no̍ a̱syā u̱ṣaso̱ vyu̍ṣṭau̱ tvaṃ sūra̱ udi̍te bodhi go̱pāḥ |

janme̍va̱ nitya̱ṃ tana̍yaṃ juṣasva̱ stoma̍ṃ me agne ta̱nvā̍ sujāta

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 3

tvaṃ nṛ̱cakṣā̍ vṛṣa̱bhānu̍ pū̱rvīḥ kṛ̱ṣṇāsva̍gne aru̱ṣo vi bhā̍hi |

vaso̱ neṣi̍ ca̱ parṣi̱ cātyaṃha̍: kṛ̱dhī no̍ rā̱ya u̱śijo̍ yaviṣṭha

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 4

aṣā̍l̤aho agne vṛṣa̱bho di̍dīhi̱ puro̱ viśvā̱: saubha̍gā saṃjigī̱vān |

ya̱jñasya̍ ne̱tā pra̍tha̱masya̍ pā̱yorjāta̍vedo bṛha̱taḥ su̍praṇīte

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 5

acchi̍drā̱ śarma̍ jaritaḥ pu̱rūṇi̍ de̱vām̐ acchā̱ dīdyā̍naḥ sume̱dhāḥ |

ratho̱ na sasni̍ra̱bhi va̍kṣi̱ vāja̱magne̱ tvaṃ roda̍sī naḥ su̱meke̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 6

pra pī̍paya vṛṣabha̱ jinva̱ vājā̱nagne̱ tvaṃ roda̍sī naḥ su̱doghe̍ |

de̱vebhi̍rdeva su̱rucā̍ rucā̱no mā no̱ marta̍sya durma̱tiḥ pari̍ ṣṭhāt

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 7
