# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 3

**11 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| vaiśvānaro'gniḥ| jagatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 1

vai̱śvā̱na̱rāya̍ pṛthu̱pāja̍se̱ vipo̱ ratnā̍ vidhanta dha̱ruṇe̍ṣa̱ca gāta̍ve |
a̱gnirhi de̱vām̐ a̱mṛto̍ duva̱syatyathā̱ dharmā̍ṇi sa̱natā̱ na dū̍duṣat

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 2

a̱ntardū̱to roda̍sī da̱sma ī̍yate̱ hotā̱ niṣa̍tto̱ manu̍ṣaḥ pu̱rohi̍taḥ |

kṣaya̍ṃ bṛ̱hanta̱ṃ pari̍ bhūṣati̱ dyubhi̍rde̱vebhi̍ra̱gniri̍ṣi̱to dhi̱yāva̍suḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 3

ke̱tuṃ ya̱jñānā̍ṃ vi̱datha̍sya̱ sādha̍na̱ṃ viprā̍so a̱gniṃ ma̍hayanta̱ citti̍bhiḥ |

apā̍ṃsi̱ yasmi̱nnadhi̍ saṃda̱dhurgira̱stasmi̍ntsu̱mnāni̱ yaja̍māna̱ ā ca̍ke

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 4

pi̱tā ya̱jñānā̱masu̍ro vipa̱ścitā̍ṃ vi̱māna̍ma̱gnirva̱yuna̍ṃ ca vā̱ghatā̍m |

ā vi̍veśa̱ roda̍sī̱ bhūri̍varpasā purupri̱yo bha̍ndate̱ dhāma̍bhiḥ ka̱viḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 5

ca̱ndrama̱gniṃ ca̱ndrara̍tha̱ṃ hari̍vrataṃ vaiśvāna̱rama̍psu̱ṣada̍ṃ sva̱rvida̍m |

vi̱gā̱haṃ tūrṇi̱ṃ tavi̍ṣībhi̱rāvṛ̍ta̱ṃ bhūrṇi̍ṃ de̱vāsa̍ i̱ha su̱śriya̍ṃ dadhuḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 6

a̱gnirde̱vebhi̱rmanu̍ṣaśca ja̱ntubhi̍stanvā̱no ya̱jñaṃ pu̍ru̱peśa̍saṃ dhi̱yā |

ra̱thīra̱ntarī̍yate̱ sādha̍diṣṭibhirjī̱ro damū̍nā abhiśasti̱cāta̍naḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 7

agne̱ jara̍sva svapa̱tya āyu̍nyū̱rjā pi̍nvasva̱ samiṣo̍ didīhi naḥ |

vayā̍ṃsi jinva bṛha̱taśca̍ jāgṛva u̱śigde̱vānā̱masi̍ su̱kratu̍rvi̱pām

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 8

vi̱śpati̍ṃ ya̱hvamati̍thi̱ṃ nara̱: sadā̍ ya̱ntāra̍ṃ dhī̱nāmu̱śija̍ṃ ca vā̱ghatā̍m |

a̱dhva̱rāṇā̱ṃ ceta̍naṃ jā̱tave̍dasa̱ṃ pra śa̍ṃsanti̱ nama̍sā jū̱tibhi̍rvṛ̱dhe

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 9

vi̱bhāvā̍ de̱vaḥ su̱raṇa̱: pari̍ kṣi̱tīra̱gnirba̍bhūva̱ śava̍sā su̱madra̍thaḥ |

tasya̍ vra̱tāni̍ bhūripo̱ṣiṇo̍ va̱yamupa̍ bhūṣema̱ dama̱ ā su̍vṛ̱ktibhi̍:

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 10

vaiśvā̍nara̱ tava̱ dhāmā̱nyā ca̍ke̱ yebhi̍: sva̱rvidabha̍vo vicakṣaṇa |

jā̱ta āpṛ̍ṇo̱ bhuva̍nāni̱ roda̍sī̱ agne̱ tā viśvā̍ pari̱bhūra̍si̱ tmanā̍

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 11

||

vai̱śvā̱na̱rasya̍ da̱ṃsanā̍bhyo bṛ̱hadari̍ṇā̱deka̍: svapa̱syayā̍ ka̱viḥ |

u̱bhā pi̱tarā̍ ma̱haya̍nnajāyatā̱gnirdyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī bhūri̍retasā
