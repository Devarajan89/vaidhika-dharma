# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 42

**9 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 2

upa̍ naḥ su̱tamā ga̍hi̱ soma̍mindra̱ gavā̍śiram | hari̍bhyā̱ṃ yaste̍ asma̱yuḥ || 1||
tami̍ndra̱ mada̱mā ga̍hi barhi̱:ṣṭhāṃ grāva̍bhiḥ su̱tam | ku̱vinnva̍sya tṛ̱pṇava̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 3

indra̍mi̱tthā giro̱ mamācchā̍guriṣi̱tā i̱taḥ | ā̱vṛte̱ soma̍pītaye

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 4

indra̱ṃ soma̍sya pī̱taye̱ stomai̍ri̱ha ha̍vāmahe | u̱kthebhi̍: ku̱vidā̱gama̍t

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 5

indra̱ somā̍: su̱tā i̱me tān da̍dhiṣva śatakrato | ja̱ṭhare̍ vājinīvaso

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 6

vi̱dmā hi tvā̍ dhanaṃja̱yaṃ vāje̍ṣu dadhṛ̱ṣaṃ ka̍ve | adhā̍ te su̱mnamī̍mahe

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 7

i̱mami̍ndra̱ gavā̍śira̱ṃ yavā̍śiraṃ ca naḥ piba | ā̱gatyā̱ vṛṣa̍bhiḥ su̱tam

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 8

tubhyedi̍ndra̱ sva o̱kye̱3 soma̍ṃ codāmi pī̱taye̍ | e̱ṣa rā̍rantu te hṛ̱di

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 9

tvāṃ su̱tasya̍ pī̱taye̍ pra̱tnami̍ndra havāmahe | ku̱śi̱kāso̍ ava̱syava̍:
