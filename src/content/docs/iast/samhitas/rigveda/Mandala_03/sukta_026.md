# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 26

**9 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ, 7 ātmā| 1-3 vaiśvānaro'gniḥ, 4-6 marutaḥ, 7-8 ātmā (agnirvā), 9 viśvāmitropadhyāyaḥ|1-6 jagatī, 7-9 triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 1

vai̱śvā̱na̱raṃ mana̍sā̱gniṃ ni̱cāyyā̍ ha̱viṣma̍nto anuṣa̱tyaṃ sva̱rvida̍m |
su̱dānu̍ṃ de̱vaṃ ra̍thi̱raṃ va̍sū̱yavo̍ gī̱rbhī ra̱ṇvaṃ ku̍śi̱kāso̍ havāmahe

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 2

taṃ śu̱bhrama̱gnimava̍se havāmahe vaiśvāna̱raṃ mā̍ta̱riśvā̍namu̱kthya̍m |

bṛha̱spati̱ṃ manu̍ṣo de̱vatā̍taye̱ vipra̱ṃ śrotā̍ra̱mati̍thiṃ raghu̱ṣyada̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 3

aśvo̱ na kranda̱ñjani̍bhi̱: sami̍dhyate vaiśvāna̱raḥ ku̍śi̱kebhi̍ryu̱geyu̍ge |

sa no̍ a̱gniḥ su̱vīrya̱ṃ svaśvya̱ṃ dadhā̍tu̱ ratna̍ma̱mṛte̍ṣu̱ jāgṛ̍viḥ

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 4

bṛ̱ha̱dukṣo̍ ma̱ruto̍ vi̱śvave̍dasa̱: pra ve̍payanti̱ parva̍tā̱m̐ adā̍bhyāḥ

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 5

te svā̱nino̍ ru̱driyā̍ va̱rṣani̍rṇijaḥ si̱ṃhā na he̱ṣakra̍tavaḥ su̱dāna̍vaḥ

## Verse 6

pṛṣa̍daśvāso anava̱bhrarā̍dhaso̱ gantā̍ro ya̱jñaṃ vi̱dathe̍ṣu̱ dhīrā̍:

## Verse 7
