# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 24

**5 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| agniḥ| gāyatrī, 1 anuṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 24 | **Verse:** 1

agne̱ saha̍sva̱ pṛta̍nā a̱bhimā̍tī̱rapā̍sya | du̱ṣṭara̱stara̱nnarā̍tī̱rvarco̍ dhā ya̱jñavā̍hase

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 24 | **Verse:** 2

agna̍ i̱l̤ā sami̍dhyase vī̱tiho̍tro̱ ama̍rtyaḥ | ju̱ṣasva̱ sū no̍ adhva̱ram

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 24 | **Verse:** 3

agne̍ dyu̱mnena̍ jāgṛve̱ saha̍saḥ sūnavāhuta | edaṃ ba̱rhiḥ sa̍do̱ mama̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 24 | **Verse:** 4

agne̱ viśve̍bhira̱gnibhi̍rde̱vebhi̍rmahayā̱ gira̍: | ya̱jñeṣu̱ ya u̍ cā̱yava̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 24 | **Verse:** 5

agne̱ dā dā̱śuṣe̍ ra̱yiṃ vī̱rava̍nta̱ṃ parī̍ṇasam | śi̱śī̱hi na̍: sūnu̱mata̍:
