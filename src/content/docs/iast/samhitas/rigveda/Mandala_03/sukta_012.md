# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 12

**9 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| indrāgnī| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 1

indrā̍gnī̱ ā ga̍taṃ su̱taṃ gī̱rbhirnabho̱ vare̍ṇyam | a̱sya pā̍taṃ dhi̱yeṣi̱tā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 2

indrā̍gnī jari̱tuḥ sacā̍ ya̱jño ji̍gāti̱ ceta̍naḥ | a̱yā pā̍tami̱maṃ su̱tam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 3

indra̍ma̱gniṃ ka̍vi̱cchadā̍ ya̱jñasya̍ jū̱tyā vṛ̍ṇe | tā soma̍sye̱ha tṛ̍mpatām

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 4

to̱śā vṛ̍tra̱haṇā̍ huve sa̱jitvā̱nāpa̍rājitā | i̱ndrā̱gnī vā̍ja̱sāta̍mā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 5

pra vā̍marcantyu̱kthino̍ nīthā̱vido̍ jari̱tāra̍: | indrā̍gnī̱ iṣa̱ ā vṛ̍ṇe

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 6

indrā̍gnī nava̱tiṃ puro̍ dā̱sapa̍tnīradhūnutam | sā̱kameke̍na̱ karma̍ṇā

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 7

indrā̍gnī̱ apa̍sa̱sparyupa̱ pra ya̍nti dhī̱taya̍: | ṛ̱tasya̍ pa̱thyā̱3 anu̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 8

indrā̍gnī tavi̱ṣāṇi̍ vāṃ sa̱dhasthā̍ni̱ prayā̍ṃsi ca | yu̱vora̱ptūrya̍ṃ hi̱tam

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 12 | **Verse:** 9

indrā̍gnī roca̱nā di̱vaḥ pari̱ vāje̍ṣu bhūṣathaḥ | tadvā̍ṃ ceti̱ pra vī̱rya̍m
