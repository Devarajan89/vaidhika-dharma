# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 18

**5 kato vaiśvāmitraḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 1

bhavā̍ no agne su̱manā̱ upe̍tau̱ sakhe̍va̱ sakhye̍ pi̱tare̍va sā̱dhuḥ |
pu̱ru̱druho̱ hi kṣi̱tayo̱ janā̍nā̱ṃ prati̍ pratī̱cīrda̍hatā̱darā̍tīḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 2

tapo̱ ṣva̍gne̱ anta̍rām̐ a̱mitrā̱n tapā̱ śaṃsa̱mara̍ruṣa̱: para̍sya |

tapo̍ vaso cikitā̱no a̱cittā̱n vi te̍ tiṣṭhantāma̱jarā̍ a̱yāsa̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 3

i̱dhmenā̍gna i̱cchamā̍no ghṛ̱tena̍ ju̱homi̍ ha̱vyaṃ tara̍se̱ balā̍ya |

yāva̱dīśe̱ brahma̍ṇā̱ vanda̍māna i̱māṃ dhiya̍ṃ śata̱seyā̍ya de̱vīm

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 4

uccho̱ciṣā̍ sahasasputra stu̱to bṛ̱had vaya̍: śaśamā̱neṣu̍ dhehi |

re̱vada̍gne vi̱śvāmi̍treṣu̱ śaṃ yorma̍rmṛ̱jmā te̍ ta̱nvaṃ1 bhūri̱ kṛtva̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 18 | **Verse:** 5

kṛ̱dhi ratna̍ṃ susanita̱rdhanā̍nā̱ṃ sa gheda̍gne bhavasi̱ yat sami̍ddhaḥ |

sto̱turdu̍ro̱ṇe su̱bhaga̍sya re̱vat sṛ̱prā ka̱rasnā̍ dadhiṣe̱ vapū̍ṃṣi
