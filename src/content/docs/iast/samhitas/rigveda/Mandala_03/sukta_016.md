# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 16

**6 kātya utkīlaḥ| agniḥ| pragāthaḥ(1, 3, 5 bṛhatī, 2, 4, 6 sato bṛhatī)|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 1

a̱yama̱gniḥ su̱vīrya̱syeśe̍ ma̱haḥ saubha̍gasya |
rā̱ya ī̍śe svapa̱tyasya̱ goma̍ta̱ īśe̍ vṛtra̱hathā̍nām

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 2

i̱maṃ na̍ro marutaḥ saścatā̱ vṛdha̱ṃ yasmi̱n rāya̱: śevṛ̍dhāsaḥ |

a̱bhi ye santi̱ pṛta̍nāsu dū̱ḍhyo̍ vi̱śvāhā̱ śatru̍māda̱bhuḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 3

sa tvaṃ no̍ rā̱yaḥ śi̍śīhi̱ mīḍhvo̍ agne su̱vīrya̍sya |

tuvi̍dyumna̱ varṣi̍ṣṭhasya pra̱jāva̍to'namī̱vasya̍ śu̱ṣmiṇa̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 4

cakri̱ryo viśvā̱ bhuva̍nā̱bhi sā̍sa̱hiścakri̍rde̱veṣvā duva̍: |

ā de̱veṣu̱ yata̍ta̱ ā su̱vīrya̱ ā śaṃsa̍ u̱ta nṛ̱ṇām

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 5

mā no̍ a̱gne'ma̍taye̱ māvīra̍tāyai rīradhaḥ |

māgotā̍yai sahasasputra̱ mā ni̱de'pa̱ dveṣā̱ṃsyā kṛ̍dhi

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 16 | **Verse:** 6

śa̱gdhi vāja̍sya subhaga pra̱jāva̱to'gne̍ bṛha̱to a̍dhva̱re |

saṃ rā̱yā bhūya̍sā sṛja mayo̱bhunā̱ tuvi̍dyumna̱ yaśa̍svatā
