# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 25

**5 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| agniḥ, 4 agnīndrau| virāṭ|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 1

agne̍ di̱vaḥ sū̱nura̍si̱ prace̍tā̱stanā̍ pṛthi̱vyā u̱ta vi̱śvave̍dāḥ |
ṛdha̍gde̱vām̐ i̱ha ya̍jā cikitvaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 2

a̱gniḥ sa̍noti vī̱ryā̍ṇi vi̱dvān tsa̱noti̱ vāja̍ma̱mṛtā̍ya̱ bhūṣa̍n |

sa no̍ de̱vām̐ eha va̍hā purukṣo

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 3

a̱gnirdyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī vi̱śvaja̍nye̱ ā bhā̍ti de̱vī a̱mṛte̱ amū̍raḥ |

kṣaya̱n vājai̍: puruśca̱ndro namo̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 4

agna̱ indra̍śca dā̱śuṣo̍ duro̱ṇe su̱tāva̍to ya̱jñami̱hopa̍ yātam |

ama̍rdhantā soma̱peyā̍ya devā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 25 | **Verse:** 5

agne̍ a̱pāṃ sami̍dhyase duro̱ṇe nitya̍: sūno sahaso jātavedaḥ |

sa̱dhasthā̍ni ma̱haya̍māna ū̱tī
