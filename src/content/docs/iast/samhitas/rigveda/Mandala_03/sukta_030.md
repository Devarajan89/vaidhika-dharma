# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 30

**22 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 1

i̱cchanti̍ tvā so̱myāsa̱: sakhā̍yaḥ su̱nvanti̱ soma̱ṃ dadha̍ti̱ prayā̍ṃsi |
titi̍kṣante a̱bhiśa̍sti̱ṃ janā̍nā̱mindra̱ tvadā kaśca̱na hi pra̍ke̱taḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 2

na te̍ dū̱re pa̍ra̱mā ci̱d rajā̱ṃsyā tu pra yā̍hi harivo̱ hari̍bhyām |

sthi̱rāya̱ vṛṣṇe̱ sava̍nā kṛ̱temā yu̱ktā grāvā̍ṇaḥ samidhā̱ne a̱gnau

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 3

indra̍: su̱śipro̍ ma̱ghavā̱ taru̍tro ma̱hāvrā̍tastuvikū̱rmiṝghā̍vān |

yadu̱gro dhā bā̍dhi̱to martye̍ṣu̱ kva1 tyā te̍ vṛṣabha vī̱ryā̍ṇi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 4

tvaṃ hi ṣmā̍ cyā̱vaya̱nnacyu̍tā̱nyeko̍ vṛ̱trā cara̍si̱ jighna̍mānaḥ |

tava̱ dyāvā̍pṛthi̱vī parva̍tā̱so'nu̍ vra̱tāya̱ nimi̍teva tasthuḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 5

u̱tābha̍ye puruhūta̱ śravo̍bhi̱reko̍ dṛ̱l̤ahama̍vado vṛtra̱hā san |

i̱me ci̍dindra̱ roda̍sī apā̱re yat sa̍ṃgṛ̱bhṇā ma̍ghavan kā̱śirit te̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 6

pra sū ta̍ indra pra̱vatā̱ hari̍bhyā̱ṃ pra te̱ vajra̍: pramṛ̱ṇanne̍tu̱ śatrū̍n |

ja̱hi pra̍tī̱co a̍nū̱caḥ parā̍co̱ viśva̍ṃ sa̱tyaṃ kṛ̍ṇuhi vi̱ṣṭama̍stu

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 7

yasmai̱ dhāyu̱rada̍dhā̱ martyā̱yābha̍ktaṃ cid bhajate ge̱hyaṃ1 saḥ |

bha̱drā ta̍ indra suma̱tirghṛ̱tācī̍ sa̱hasra̍dānā puruhūta rā̱tiḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 8

sa̱hadā̍nuṃ puruhūta kṣi̱yanta̍maha̱stami̍ndra̱ saṃ pi̍ṇa̱kkuṇā̍rum |

a̱bhi vṛ̱traṃ vardha̍māna̱ṃ piyā̍ruma̱pāda̍mindra ta̱vasā̍ jaghantha

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 9

ni sā̍ma̱nāmi̍ṣi̱rāmi̍ndra̱ bhūmi̍ṃ ma̱hīma̍pā̱rāṃ sada̍ne sasattha |

asta̍bhnā̱d dyāṃ vṛ̍ṣa̱bho a̱ntari̍kṣa̱marṣa̱ntvāpa̱stvaye̱ha prasū̍tāḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 10

a̱lā̱tṛ̱ṇo va̱la i̍ndra vra̱jo goḥ pu̱rā hanto̱rbhaya̍māno̱ vyā̍ra |

su̱gān pa̱tho a̍kṛṇonni̱raje̱ gāḥ prāva̱n vāṇī̍: puruhū̱taṃ dhama̍ntīḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 11

||

eko̱ dve vasu̍matī samī̱cī indra̱ ā pa̍prau pṛthi̱vīmu̱ta dyām |

u̱tāntari̍kṣāda̱bhi na̍: samī̱ka i̱ṣo ra̱thīḥ sa̱yuja̍: śūra̱ vājā̍n

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 12

||

diśa̱: sūryo̱ na mi̍nāti̱ pradi̍ṣṭā di̱vedi̍ve̱ harya̍śvaprasūtāḥ |

saṃ yadāna̱l̤adhva̍na̱ ādidaśvai̍rvi̱moca̍naṃ kṛṇute̱ tattva̍sya

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 13

didṛ̍kṣanta u̱ṣaso̱ yāma̍nna̱ktorvi̱vasva̍tyā̱ mahi̍ ci̱tramanī̍kam |

viśve̍ jānanti mahi̱nā yadāgā̱dindra̍sya̱ karma̱ sukṛ̍tā pu̱rūṇi̍

---

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 14

mahi̱ jyoti̱rnihi̍taṃ va̱kṣaṇā̍svā̱mā pa̱kvaṃ ca̍rati̱ bibhra̍tī̱ gauḥ |

viśva̱ṃ svādma̱ sambhṛ̍tamu̱sriyā̍yā̱ṃ yat sī̱mindro̱ ada̍dhā̱d bhoja̍nāya

---

## Verse 15

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 15

indra̱ dṛhya̍ yāmako̱śā a̍bhūvan ya̱jñāya̍ śikṣa gṛṇa̱te sakhi̍bhyaḥ |

du̱rmā̱yavo̍ du̱revā̱ martyā̍so niṣa̱ṅgiṇo̍ ri̱pavo̱ hantvā̍saḥ

---

## Verse 16

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 16

saṃ ghoṣa̍: śṛṇve'va̱maira̱mitrai̍rja̱hī nye̍ṣva̱śani̱ṃ tapi̍ṣṭhām |

vṛ̱ścema̱dhastā̱d vi ru̍jā̱ saha̍sva ja̱hi rakṣo̍ maghavan ra̱ndhaya̍sva

---

## Verse 17

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 17

ud vṛ̍ha̱ rakṣa̍: sa̱hamū̍lamindra vṛ̱ścā madhya̱ṃ pratyagra̍ṃ śṛṇīhi |

ā kīva̍taḥ sala̱lūka̍ṃ cakartha brahma̱dviṣe̱ tapu̍ṣiṃ he̱tima̍sya

---

## Verse 18

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 18

sva̱staye̍ vā̱jibhi̍śca praṇeta̱: saṃ yanma̱hīriṣa̍ ā̱satsi̍ pū̱rvīḥ |

rā̱yo va̱ntāro̍ bṛha̱taḥ syā̍mā̱sme a̍stu̱ bhaga̍ indra pra̱jāvā̍n

---

## Verse 19

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 19

ā no̍ bhara̱ bhaga̍mindra dyu̱manta̱ṃ ni te̍ de̱ṣṇasya̍ dhīmahi prare̱ke |

ū̱rva i̍va paprathe̱ kāmo̍ a̱sme tamā pṛ̍ṇa vasupate̱ vasū̍nām

---

## Verse 20

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 20

i̱maṃ kāma̍ṃ mandayā̱ gobhi̱raśvai̍śca̱ndrava̍tā̱ rādha̍sā pa̱pratha̍śca |

sva̱ryavo̍ ma̱tibhi̱stubhya̱ṃ viprā̱ indrā̍ya̱ vāha̍: kuśi̱kāso̍ akran

---

## Verse 21

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 21

||

ā no̍ go̱trā da̍rdṛhi gopate̱ gāḥ sama̱smabhya̍ṃ sa̱nayo̍ yantu̱ vājā̍: |

di̱vakṣā̍ asi vṛṣabha sa̱tyaśu̍ṣmo̱'smabhya̱ṃ su ma̍ghavan bodhi go̱dāḥ
