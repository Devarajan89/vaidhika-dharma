# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 14

**7 ṛṣabho vaiśvāmitraḥ| agniḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 1

ā hotā̍ ma̱ndro vi̱dathā̍nyasthāt sa̱tyo yajvā̍ ka̱vita̍ma̱: sa ve̱dhāḥ |
vi̱dyudra̍tha̱: saha̍saspu̱tro a̱gniḥ śo̱ciṣke̍śaḥ pṛthi̱vyāṃ pājo̍ aśret

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 2

ayā̍mi te̱ nama̍uktiṃ juṣasva̱ ṛtā̍va̱stubhya̱ṃ ceta̍te sahasvaḥ |

vi̱dvām̐ ā va̍kṣi vi̱duṣo̱ ni ṣa̍tsi̱ madhya̱ ā ba̱rhirū̱taye̍ yajatra

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 14 | **Verse:** 3

||
