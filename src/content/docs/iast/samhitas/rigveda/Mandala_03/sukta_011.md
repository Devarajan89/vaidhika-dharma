# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 11

**9 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| agniḥ| gāyatrī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 1

a̱gnirhotā̍ pu̱rohi̍to'dhva̱rasya̱ vica̍rṣaṇiḥ | sa ve̍da ya̱jñamā̍nu̱ṣak

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 2

sa ha̍vya̱vāl̤ama̍rtya u̱śigdū̱taścano̍hitaḥ | a̱gnirdhi̱yā samṛ̍ṇvati

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 3

a̱gnirdhi̱yā sa ce̍tati ke̱turya̱jñasya̍ pū̱rvyaḥ | artha̱ṃ hya̍sya ta̱raṇi̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 4

a̱gniṃ sū̱nuṃ sana̍śruta̱ṃ saha̍so jā̱tave̍dasam | vahni̍ṃ de̱vā a̍kṛṇvata

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 5

adā̍bhyaḥ purae̱tā vi̱śāma̱gnirmānu̍ṣīṇām | tūrṇī̱ ratha̱: sadā̱ nava̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 6

sā̱hvān viśvā̍ abhi̱yuja̱: kratu̍rde̱vānā̱mamṛ̍ktaḥ | a̱gnistu̱viśra̍vastamaḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 7

a̱bhi prayā̍ṃsi̱ vāha̍sā dā̱śvām̐ a̍śnoti̱ martya̍: | kṣaya̍ṃ pāva̱kaśo̍ciṣaḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 8

pari̱ viśvā̍ni̱ sudhi̍tā̱ 'gnera̍śyāma̱ manma̍bhiḥ | viprā̍so jā̱tave̍dasaḥ

---

## Verse 9

agne̱ viśvā̍ni̱ vāryā̱ vāje̍ṣu saniṣāmahe | tve de̱vāsa̱ eri̍re
