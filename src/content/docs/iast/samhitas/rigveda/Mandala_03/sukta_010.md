# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 10

**9 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| agniḥ| uṣṇik|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 1

tvāma̍gne manī̱ṣiṇa̍: sa̱mrāja̍ṃ carṣaṇī̱nām | de̱vaṃ martā̍sa indhate̱ sama̍dhva̱re

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 2

tvāṃ ya̱jñeṣvṛ̱tvija̱magne̱ hotā̍ramīl̤ate | go̱pā ṛ̱tasya̍ dīdihi̱ sve dame̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 3

sa ghā̱ yaste̱ dadā̍śati sa̱midhā̍ jā̱tave̍dase | so a̍gne dhatte su̱vīrya̱ṃ sa pu̍ṣyati

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 4

sa ke̱tura̍dhva̱rāṇā̍ma̱gnirde̱vebhi̱rā ga̍mat | a̱ñjā̱naḥ sa̱pta hotṛ̍bhirha̱viṣma̍te

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 5

pra hotre̍ pū̱rvyaṃ vaco̱'gnaye̍ bharatā bṛ̱hat | vi̱pāṃ jyotī̍ṃṣi̱ bibhra̍te̱ na ve̱dhase̍

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 6

a̱gniṃ va̍rdhantu no̱ giro̱ yato̱ jāya̍ta u̱kthya̍: | ma̱he vājā̍ya̱ dravi̍ṇāya darśa̱taḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 7

agne̱ yaji̍ṣṭho adhva̱re de̱vān de̍vaya̱te ya̍ja | hotā̍ ma̱ndro vi rā̍ja̱syati̱ sridha̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 8

sa na̍: pāvaka dīdihi dyu̱mada̱sme su̱vīrya̍m | bhavā̍ sto̱tṛbhyo̱ anta̍maḥ sva̱staye̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 10 | **Verse:** 9

taṃ tvā̱ viprā̍ vipa̱nyavo̍ jāgṛ̱vāṃsa̱: sami̍ndhate | ha̱vya̱vāha̱mama̍rtyaṃ saho̱vṛdha̍m
