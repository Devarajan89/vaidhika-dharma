# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 36

**11 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ, 10 ghora āṅgirasaḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 1

i̱māmū̱ ṣu prabhṛ̍tiṃ sā̱taye̍ dhā̱: śaśva̍cchaśvadū̱tibhi̱ryāda̍mānaḥ |
su̱tesu̍te vāvṛdhe̱ vardha̍nebhi̱ryaḥ karma̍bhirma̱hadbhi̱: suśru̍to̱ bhūt

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 2

indrā̍ya̱ somā̍: pra̱divo̱ vidā̍nā ṛ̱bhuryebhi̱rvṛṣa̍parvā̱ vihā̍yāḥ |

pra̱ya̱myamā̍nā̱n prati̱ ṣū gṛ̍bhā̱yendra̱ piba̱ vṛṣa̍dhūtasya̱ vṛṣṇa̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 3

pibā̱ vardha̍sva̱ tava̍ ghā su̱tāsa̱ indra̱ somā̍saḥ pratha̱mā u̱teme |

yathāpi̍baḥ pū̱rvyām̐ i̍ndra̱ somā̍m̐ e̱vā pā̍hi̱ panyo̍ a̱dyā navī̍yān

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 4

ma̱hām̐ ama̍tro vṛ̱jane̍ vira̱pśyu1 graṃ śava̍: patyate dhṛ̱ṣṇvoja̍: |

nāha̍ vivyāca pṛthi̱vī ca̱naina̱ṃ yat somā̍so̱ harya̍śva̱mama̍ndan

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 5

ma̱hām̐ u̱gro vā̍vṛdhe vī̱ryā̍ya sa̱māca̍kre vṛṣa̱bhaḥ kāvye̍na |

indro̱ bhago̍ vāja̱dā a̍sya̱ gāva̱: pra jā̍yante̱ dakṣi̍ṇā asya pū̱rvīḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 6

pra yat sindha̍vaḥ prasa̱vaṃ yathāya̱nnāpa̍: samu̱draṃ ra̱thye̍va jagmuḥ |

ata̍ści̱dindra̱: sada̍so̱ varī̍yā̱n yadī̱ṃ soma̍: pṛ̱ṇati̍ du̱gdho a̱ṃśuḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 7

sa̱mu̱dreṇa̱ sindha̍vo̱ yāda̍mānā̱ indrā̍ya̱ soma̱ṃ suṣu̍ta̱ṃ bhara̍ntaḥ |

a̱ṃśuṃ du̍hanti ha̱stino̍ bha̱ritrai̱rmadhva̍: punanti̱ dhāra̍yā pa̱vitrai̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 8

hra̱dā i̍va ku̱kṣaya̍: soma̱dhānā̱: samī̍ vivyāca̱ sava̍nā pu̱rūṇi̍ |

annā̱ yadindra̍: pratha̱mā vyāśa̍ vṛ̱traṃ ja̍gha̱nvām̐ a̍vṛṇīta̱ soma̍m

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 9

ā tū bha̍ra̱ māki̍re̱tat pari̍ ṣṭhād vi̱dmā hi tvā̱ vasu̍pati̱ṃ vasū̍nām |

indra̱ yat te̱ māhi̍na̱ṃ datra̱mastya̱smabhya̱ṃ taddha̍ryaśva̱ pra ya̍ndhi

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 10

a̱sme pra ya̍ndhi maghavannṛjīṣi̱nnindra̍ rā̱yo vi̱śvavā̍rasya̱ bhūre̍: |

a̱sme śa̱taṃ śa̱rado̍ jī̱vase̍ dhā a̱sme vī̱rāñchaśva̍ta indra śiprin

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 36 | **Verse:** 11

śu̱naṃ hu̍vema ma̱ghavā̍na̱mindra̍ma̱smin bhare̱ nṛta̍ma̱ṃ vāja̍sātau |

śṛ̱ṇvanta̍mu̱gramū̱taye̍ sa̱matsu̱ ghnanta̍ṃ vṛ̱trāṇi̍ sa̱ṃjita̱ṃ dhanā̍nām
