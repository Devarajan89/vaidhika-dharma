# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 46

**5 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 1

yu̱dhmasya̍ te vṛṣa̱bhasya̍ sva̱rāja̍ u̱grasya̱ yūna̱: sthavi̍rasya̱ ghṛṣve̍: |
ajū̍ryato va̱jriṇo̍ vī̱ryā̱3ṇīndra̍ śru̱tasya̍ maha̱to ma̱hāni̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 2

ma̱hām̐ a̍si mahiṣa̱ vṛṣṇye̍bhirdhana̱spṛdu̍gra̱ saha̍māno a̱nyān |

eko̱ viśva̍sya̱ bhuva̍nasya̱ rājā̱ sa yo̱dhayā̍ ca kṣa̱yayā̍ ca̱ janā̍n

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 3

pra mātrā̍bhī ririce̱ roca̍māna̱: pra de̱vebhi̍rvi̱śvato̱ apra̍tītaḥ |

pra ma̱jmanā̍ di̱va indra̍: pṛthi̱vyāḥ prororma̱ho a̱ntari̍kṣādṛjī̱ṣī

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 4

u̱ruṃ ga̍bhī̱raṃ ja̱nuṣā̱bhyu1graṃ vi̱śvavya̍casamava̱taṃ ma̍tī̱nām |

indra̱ṃ somā̍saḥ pra̱divi̍ su̱tāsa̍: samu̱draṃ na sra̱vata̱ ā vi̍śanti

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 5

yaṃ soma̍mindra pṛthi̱vīdyāvā̱ garbha̱ṃ na mā̱tā bi̍bhṛ̱tastvā̱yā |

taṃ te̍ hinvanti̱ tamu̍ te mṛjantyadhva̱ryavo̍ vṛṣabha̱ pāta̱vā u̍
