# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 21

**5 gāthī kauśikaḥ| agniḥ,| 1triṣṭup, 2-3 anuṣṭup, 4 virāḍ rūpā, 5 satobṛhatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 1

i̱maṃ no̍ ya̱jñama̱mṛte̍ṣu dhehī̱mā ha̱vyā jā̍tavedo juṣasva |
sto̱kānā̍magne̱ meda̍so ghṛ̱tasya̱ hota̱: prāśā̍na pratha̱mo ni̱ṣadya̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 2

ghṛ̱tava̍ntaḥ pāvaka te sto̱kāḥ śco̍tanti̱ meda̍saḥ |

svadha̍rman de̱vavī̍taye̱ śreṣṭha̍ṃ no dhehi̱ vārya̍m

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 3

tubhya̍ṃ sto̱kā ghṛ̍ta̱ścuto'gne̱ viprā̍ya santya |

ṛṣi̱: śreṣṭha̱: sami̍dhyase ya̱jñasya̍ prāvi̱tā bha̍va

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 4

tubhya̍ṃ ścotantyadhrigo śacīvaḥ sto̱kāso̍ agne̱ meda̍so ghṛ̱tasya̍ |

ka̱vi̱śa̱sto bṛ̍ha̱tā bhā̱nunāgā̍ ha̱vyā ju̍ṣasva medhira

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 5

oji̍ṣṭhaṃ te madhya̱to meda̱ udbhṛ̍ta̱ṃ pra te̍ va̱yaṃ da̍dāmahe |

ścota̍nti te vaso sto̱kā adhi̍ tva̱ci prati̱ tān de̍va̱śo vi̍hi
