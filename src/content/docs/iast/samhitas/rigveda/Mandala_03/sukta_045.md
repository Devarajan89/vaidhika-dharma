# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 45

**5 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| bṛhatī|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 1

ā ma̱ndrairi̍ndra̱ hari̍bhiryā̱hi ma̱yūra̍romabhiḥ |
mā tvā̱ ke ci̱nni ya̍ma̱nviṃ na pā̱śino'ti̱ dhanve̍va̱ tām̐ i̍hi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 2

vṛ̱tra̱khā̱do va̍laṃru̱jaḥ pu̱rāṃ da̱rmo a̱pāma̱jaḥ |

sthātā̱ ratha̍sya̱ haryo̍rabhisva̱ra indro̍ dṛ̱l̤ahā ci̍dāru̱jaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 3

ga̱mbhī̱rām̐ u̍da̱dhīm̐ri̍va̱ kratu̍ṃ puṣyasi̱ gā i̍va |

pra su̍go̱pā yava̍saṃ dhe̱navo̍ yathā hra̱daṃ ku̱lyā i̍vāśata

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 4

ā na̱stuja̍ṃ ra̱yiṃ bha̱rāṃśa̱ṃ na pra̍tijāna̱te |

vṛ̱kṣaṃ pa̱kvaṃ phala̍ma̱ṅkīva̍ dhūnu̱hīndra̍ sa̱mpāra̍ṇa̱ṃ vasu̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 45 | **Verse:** 5

sva̱yuri̍ndra sva̱rāl̤a̍si̱ smaddi̍ṣṭi̱: svaya̍śastaraḥ |

sa vā̍vṛdhā̱na oja̍sā puruṣṭuta̱ bhavā̍ naḥ su̱śrava̍stamaḥ
