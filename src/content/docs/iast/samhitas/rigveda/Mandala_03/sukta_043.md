# Maṇḍala 3 — Sūktam 43

**8 gāthino viśvāmitraḥ| indraḥ| triṣṭup|**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 1

ā yā̍hya̱rvāṅupa̍ vandhure̱ṣṭhāstavedanu̍ pra̱diva̍: soma̱peya̍m |
pri̱yā sakhā̍yā̱ vi mu̱copa̍ ba̱rhistvāmi̱me ha̍vya̱vāho̍ havante

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 2

ā yā̍hi pū̱rvīrati̍ carṣa̱ṇīrām̐ a̱rya ā̱śiṣa̱ upa̍ no̱ hari̍bhyām |

i̱mā hi tvā̍ ma̱taya̱: stoma̍taṣṭā̱ indra̱ hava̍nte sa̱khyaṃ ju̍ṣā̱ṇāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 3

ā no̍ ya̱jñaṃ na̍mo̱vṛdha̍ṃ sa̱joṣā̱ indra̍ deva̱ hari̍bhiryāhi̱ tūya̍m |

a̱haṃ hi tvā̍ ma̱tibhi̱rjoha̍vīmi ghṛ̱tapra̍yāḥ sadha̱māde̱ madhū̍nām

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 4

ā ca̱ tvāme̱tā vṛṣa̍ṇā̱ vahā̍to̱ harī̱ sakhā̍yā su̱dhurā̱ svaṅgā̍ |

dhā̱nāva̱dindra̱: sava̍naṃ juṣā̱ṇaḥ sakhā̱ sakhyu̍: śṛṇava̱d vanda̍nāni

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 5

ku̱vinmā̍ go̱pāṃ kara̍se̱ jana̍sya ku̱vid rājā̍naṃ maghavannṛjīṣin |

ku̱vinma̱ ṛṣi̍ṃ papi̱vāṃsa̍ṃ su̱tasya̍ ku̱vinme̱ vasvo̍ a̱mṛta̍sya̱ śikṣā̍:

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 6

ā tvā̍ bṛ̱hanto̱ hara̍yo yujā̱nā a̱rvāgi̍ndra sadha̱mādo̍ vahantu |

pra ye dvi̱tā di̱va ṛ̱ñjantyātā̱: susa̍mmṛṣṭāso vṛṣa̱bhasya̍ mū̱rāḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 7

indra̱ piba̱ vṛṣa̍dhūtasya̱ vṛṣṇa̱ ā yaṃ te̍ śye̱na u̍śa̱te ja̱bhāra̍ |

yasya̱ made̍ cyā̱vaya̍si̱ pra kṛ̱ṣṭīryasya̱ made̱ apa̍ go̱trā va̱vartha̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 3 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 8

śu̱naṃ hu̍vema ma̱ghavā̍na̱mindra̍ma̱smin bhare̱ nṛta̍ma̱ṃ vāja̍sātau |

śṛ̱ṇvanta̍mu̱gramū̱taye̍ sa̱matsu̱ ghnanta̍ṃ vṛ̱trāṇi̍ sa̱ṃjita̱ṃ dhanā̍nām
