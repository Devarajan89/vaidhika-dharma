# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 58

**4 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | pūṣā |triṣṭup , 2 jagatī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 1

śu̱kraṃ te̍ a̱nyadya̍ja̱taṃ te̍ a̱nyadviṣu̍rūpe̱ aha̍nī̱ dyauri̍vāsi |
viśvā̱ hi mā̱yā ava̍si svadhāvo bha̱drā te̍ pūṣanni̱ha rā̱tira̍stu

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 2

a̱jāśva̍: paśu̱pā vāja̍pastyo dhiyaṃji̱nvo bhuva̍ne̱ viśve̱ arpi̍taḥ |

aṣṭrā̍ṃ pū̱ṣā śi̍thi̱rāmu̱dvarī̍vṛjatsa̱ṃcakṣā̍ṇo̱ bhuva̍nā de̱va ī̍yate

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 3

yāste̍ pūṣa̱nnāvo̍ a̱ntaḥ sa̍mu̱dre hi̍ra̱ṇyayī̍ra̱ntari̍kṣe̱ cara̍nti |

tābhi̍ryāsi dū̱tyāṃ sūrya̍sya̱ kāme̍na kṛta̱ śrava̍ i̱cchamā̍naḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 58 | **Verse:** 4

pū̱ṣā su̱bandhu̍rdi̱va ā pṛ̍thi̱vyā i̱l̤aspati̍rma̱ghavā̍ da̱smava̍rcāḥ |

yaṃ de̱vāso̱ ada̍duḥ sū̱ryāyai̱ kāme̍na kṛ̱taṃ ta̱vasa̱ṃ svañca̍m
