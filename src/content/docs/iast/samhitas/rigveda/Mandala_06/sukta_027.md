# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 27

**8 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | indra:, 8 abhyāvartī cāyamāna: [ dāna: ] | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 1

kima̍sya̱ made̱ kimva̍sya pī̱tāvindra̱: kima̍sya sa̱khye ca̍kāra |
raṇā̍ vā̱ ye ni̱ṣadi̱ kiṃ te a̍sya pu̱rā vi̍vidre̱ kimu̱ nūta̍nāsaḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 2

sada̍sya̱ made̱ sadva̍sya pī̱tāvindra̱: sada̍sya sa̱khye ca̍kāra |

raṇā̍ vā̱ ye ni̱ṣadi̱ satte a̍sya pu̱rā vi̍vidre̱ sadu̱ nūta̍nāsaḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 27 | **Verse:** 3

na rādha̍sorādhaso̱ nūta̍na̱syendra̱ naki̍rdadṛśa indri̱yaṃ te̍

## Verse 4

## Verse 5
