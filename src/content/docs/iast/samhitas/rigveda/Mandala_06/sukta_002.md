# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 2

**11 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | agni : |anuṣṭup | 11 śakvarī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 1

tvaṃ hi kṣaita̍va̱d yaśo 'gne̍ mi̱tro na patya̍se | tvaṃ vi̍carṣaṇe̱ śravo̱ vaso̍ pu̱ṣṭiṃ na pu̍ṣyasi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 2

tvāṃ hi ṣmā̍ carṣa̱ṇayo̍ ya̱jñebhi̍rgī̱rbhirīl̤a̍te | tvāṃ vā̱jī yā̍tyavṛ̱ko ra̍ja̱stūrvi̱śvaca̍rṣaṇiḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 3

sa̱joṣa̍stvā di̱vo naro̍ ya̱jñasya̍ ke̱tumi̍ndhate | yaddha̱ sya mānu̍ṣo̱ jana̍: sumnā̱yurju̱hve a̍dhva̱re

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 4

ṛdha̱d yaste̍ su̱dāna̍ve dhi̱yā marta̍: śa̱śama̍te | ū̱tī ṣa bṛ̍ha̱to di̱vo dvi̱ṣo aṃho̱ na ta̍rati

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 5

sa̱midhā̱ yasta̱ āhu̍ti̱ṃ niśi̍ti̱ṃ martyo̱ naśa̍t | va̱yāva̍nta̱ṃ sa pu̍ṣyati̱ kṣaya̍magne śa̱tāyu̍ṣam

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 6

tve̱ṣaste̍ dhū̱ma ṛ̍ṇvati di̱vi ṣañchu̱kra āta̍taḥ | sūro̱ na hi dyu̱tā tvaṃ kṛ̱pā pā̍vaka̱ roca̍se

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 7

adhā̱ hi vi̱kṣvīḍyo 'si̍ pri̱yo no̱ ati̍thiḥ | ra̱ṇvaḥ pu̱rī̍va̱ jūrya̍: sū̱nurna tra̍ya̱yāyya̍:

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 8

kratvā̱ hi droṇe̍ a̱jyase 'gne̍ vā̱jī na kṛtvya̍: | pari̍jmeva sva̱dhā gayo 'tyo̱ na hvā̱ryaḥ śiśu̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 9

tvaṃ tyā ci̱dacyu̱tā 'gne̍ pa̱śurna yava̍se | dhāmā̍ ha̱ yat te̍ ajara̱ vanā̍ vṛ̱ścanti̱ śikva̍saḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 10

veṣi̱ hya̍dhvarīya̱tāmagne̱ hotā̱ dame̍ vi̱śāṃ | sa̱mṛdho̍ viśpate kṛṇu ju̱ṣasva̍ ha̱vyama̍ṅgiraḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 2 | **Verse:** 11

acchā̍ no mitramaho deva de̱vānagne̱ voca̍: suma̱tiṃ roda̍syoḥ | vī̱hi sva̱stiṃ su̍kṣi̱tiṃ di̱vo nṝn dvi̱ṣo aṃhā̍ṃsi duri̱tā ta̍rema̱ tā ta̍rema̱ tavāva̍sā tarema
