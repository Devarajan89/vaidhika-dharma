# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 29

**6 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | indra: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 1

indra̍ṃ vo̱ nara̍: sa̱khyāya̍ sepurma̱ho yanta̍: suma̱taye̍ cakā̱nāḥ |
ma̱ho hi dā̱tā vajra̍hasto̱ asti̍ ma̱hāmu̍ ra̱ṇvamava̍se yajadhvam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 2

ā yasmi̱nhaste̱ naryā̍ mimi̱kṣurā rathe̍ hira̱ṇyaye̍ rathe̱ṣṭhāḥ |

ā ra̱śmayo̱ gabha̍styoḥ sthū̱rayo̱rādhva̱nnaśvā̍so̱ vṛṣa̍ṇo yujā̱nāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 3

śri̱ye te̱ pādā̱ duva̱ ā mi̍mikṣurdhṛ̱ṣṇurva̱jrī śava̍sā̱ dakṣi̍ṇāvān |

vasā̍no̱ atka̍ṃ sura̱bhiṃ dṛ̱śe kaṃ sva1rṇa nṛ̍taviṣi̱ro ba̍bhūtha

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 4

sa soma̱ āmi̍ślatamaḥ su̱to bhū̱dyasmi̍npa̱ktiḥ pa̱cyate̱ santi̍ dhā̱nāḥ |

indra̱ṃ nara̍: stu̱vanto̍ brahmakā̱rā u̱kthā śaṃsa̍nto de̱vavā̍tatamāḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 5

na te̱ anta̱: śava̍so dhāyya̱sya vi tu bā̍badhe̱ roda̍sī mahi̱tvā |

ā tā sū̱riḥ pṛ̍ṇati̱ tūtu̍jāno yū̱thevā̱psu sa̱mīja̍māna ū̱tī

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 29 | **Verse:** 6

e̱vedindra̍: su̱hava̍ ṛ̱ṣvo a̍stū̱tī anū̍tī hiriśi̱praḥ satvā̍ |

e̱vā hi jā̱to asa̍mātyojāḥ pu̱rū ca̍ vṛ̱trā ha̍nati̱ ni dasyū̍n
