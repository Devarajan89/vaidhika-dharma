# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 67

**11 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | mitrāvaruṇau | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 1

viśve̍ṣāṃ vaḥ sa̱tāṃ jyeṣṭha̍tamā gī̱rbhirmi̱trāvaru̍ṇā vāvṛ̱dhadhyai̍ |
saṃ yā ra̱śmeva̍ ya̱matu̱ryami̍ṣṭhā̱ dvā janā̱m̐ asa̍mā bā̱hubhi̱: svaiḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 2

i̱yaṃ madvā̱ṃ pra stṛ̍ṇīte manī̱ṣopa̍ pri̱yā nama̍sā ba̱rhiraccha̍ |

ya̱ntaṃ no̍ mitrāvaruṇā̱vadhṛ̍ṣṭaṃ cha̱rdiryadvā̍ṃ varū̱thya̍ṃ sudānū

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 3

ā yā̍taṃ mitrāvaruṇā suśa̱styupa̍ pri̱yā nama̍sā hū̱yamā̍nā |

saṃ yāva̍pna̱:stho a̱pase̍va̱ janā̍ñchrudhīya̱taści̍dyatatho mahi̱tvā

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 4

aśvā̱ na yā vā̱jinā̍ pū̱taba̍ndhū ṛ̱tā yadgarbha̱madi̍ti̱rbhara̍dhyai |

pra yā mahi̍ ma̱hāntā̱ jāya̍mānā gho̱rā martā̍ya ri̱pave̱ ni dī̍dhaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 5

viśve̱ yadvā̍ṃ ma̱ṃhanā̱ manda̍mānāḥ kṣa̱traṃ de̱vāso̱ ada̍dhuḥ sa̱joṣā̍: |

pari̱ yadbhū̱tho roda̍sī cidu̱rvī santi̱ spaśo̱ ada̍bdhāso̱ amū̍rāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 6

tā hi kṣa̱traṃ dhā̱raye̍the̱ anu̱ dyūndṛ̱ṃhethe̱ sānu̍mupa̱mādi̍va̱ dyoḥ |

dṛ̱l̤ho nakṣa̍tra u̱ta vi̱śvade̍vo̱ bhūmi̱mātā̱ndyāṃ dhā̱sinā̱yoḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 7

tā vi̱graṃ dhai̍the ja̱ṭhara̍ṃ pṛ̱ṇadhyā̱ ā yatsadma̱ sabhṛ̍tayaḥ pṛ̱ṇanti̍ |

na mṛ̍ṣyante yuva̱tayo'vā̍tā̱ vi yatpayo̍ viśvajinvā̱ bhara̍nte

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 8

tā ji̱hvayā̱ sada̱medaṃ su̍me̱dhā ā yadvā̍ṃ sa̱tyo a̍ra̱tiṝ̱te bhūt |

tadvā̍ṃ mahi̱tvaṃ ghṛ̍tānnāvastu yu̱vaṃ dā̱śuṣe̱ vi ca̍yiṣṭa̱maṃha̍:

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 9

pra yadvā̍ṃ mitrāvaruṇā spū̱rdhanpri̱yā dhāma̍ yu̱vadhi̍tā mi̱nanti̍ |

na ye de̱vāsa̱ oha̍sā̱ na martā̱ aya̍jñasāco̱ apyo̱ na pu̱trāḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 10

vi yadvāca̍ṃ kī̱stāso̱ bhara̍nte̱ śaṃsa̍nti̱ ke ci̍nni̱vido̍ manā̱nāḥ |

ādvā̍ṃ bravāma sa̱tyānyu̱kthā naki̍rde̱vebhi̍ryatatho mahi̱tvā

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 67 | **Verse:** 11

anu̱ yadgāva̍: sphu̱rānṛ̍ji̱pyaṃ dhṛ̱ṣṇuṃ yadraṇe̱ vṛṣa̍ṇaṃ yu̱naja̍n
