# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 26

**8 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | indra: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 1

śru̱dhī na̍ indra̱ hvayā̍masi tvā ma̱ho vāja̍sya sā̱tau vā̍vṛṣā̱ṇāḥ |
saṃ yad viśo'ya̍nta̱ śūra̍sātā u̱graṃ no'va̱: pārye̱ aha̍n dā:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 2

tvāṃ vā̱jī ha̍vate vājine̱yo ma̱ho vāja̍sya̱ gadhya̍sya sā̱tau |

tvāṃ vṛ̱treṣvi̍ndra̱ satpa̍ti̱ṃ taru̍tra̱ṃ tvāṃ ca̍ṣṭe muṣṭi̱hā goṣu̱ yudhya̍n

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 3

tvaṃ ka̱viṃ co̍dayo̱'rkasā̍tau̱ tvaṃ kutsā̍ya̱ śuṣṇa̍ṃ dā̱śuṣe̍ vark |

tvaṃ śiro̍ ama̱rmaṇa̱: parā̍hannatithi̱gvāya̱ śaṃsya̍ṃ kari̱ṣyan

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 4

tvaṃ ratha̱ṃ pra bha̍ro yo̱dhamṛ̱ṣvamāvo̱ yudhya̍ntaṃ vṛṣa̱bhaṃ daśa̍dyum |

tvaṃ tugra̍ṃ veta̱save̱ sacā̍ha̱n tvaṃ tuji̍ṃ gṛ̱ṇanta̍mindra tūtoḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 5

tvaṃ tadu̱kthami̍ndra ba̱rhaṇā̍ ka̱: pra yaccha̱tā sa̱hasrā̍ śūra̱ darṣi̍ |

ava̍ gi̱rerdāsa̱ṃ śamba̍raṃ ha̱nprāvo̱ divo̍dāsaṃ ci̱trābhi̍rū̱tī

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 6

tvaṃ ra̱jiṃ piṭhī̍nase daśa̱syanṣa̱ṣṭiṃ sa̱hasrā̱ śacyā̱ sacā̍han

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 26 | **Verse:** 7

## Verse 8

prāta̍rdaniḥ kṣatra̱śrīra̍stu̱ śreṣṭho̍ gha̱ne vṛ̱trāṇā̍ṃ sa̱naye̱ dhanā̍nām
