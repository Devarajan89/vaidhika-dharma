# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 42

**4 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | indra: | anuṣṭup, 4 bṛhatī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 1

pratya̍smai̱ pipī̍ṣate̱ viśvā̍ni vi̱duṣe̍ bhara | a̱ra̱ṃga̱māya̱ jagma̱ye'pa̍ścāddaghvane̱ nare̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 2

eme̍naṃ pra̱tyeta̍na̱ some̍bhiḥ soma̱pāta̍mam | ama̍trebhirṝjī̱ṣiṇa̱mindra̍ṃ su̱tebhi̱rindu̍bhiḥ

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 42 | **Verse:** 3

yadī̍ su̱tebhi̱rindu̍bhi̱: some̍bhiḥ prati̱bhūṣa̍tha | vedā̱ viśva̍sya̱ medhi̍ro dhṛ̱ṣattaṃta̱mideṣa̍te
