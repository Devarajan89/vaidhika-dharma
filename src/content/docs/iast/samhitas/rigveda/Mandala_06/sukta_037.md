# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 37

**5 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | indra: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 1

a̱rvāgratha̍ṃ vi̱śvavā̍raṃ ta u̱grendra̍ yu̱ktāso̱ hara̍yo vahantu |
kī̱riści̱ddhi tvā̱ hava̍te̱ sva̍rvānṛdhī̱mahi̍ sadha̱māda̍ste a̱dya

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 2

pro droṇe̱ hara̍ya̱: karmā̍gmanpunā̱nāsa̱ ṛjya̍nto abhūvan |

indro̍ no a̱sya pū̱rvyaḥ pa̍pīyāddyu̱kṣo mada̍sya so̱myasya̱ rājā̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 3

ā̱sa̱srā̱ṇāsa̍: śavasā̱namacchendra̍ṃ suca̱kre ra̱thyā̍so̱ aśvā̍: |

a̱bhi śrava̱ ṛjya̍nto vaheyu̱rnū ci̱nnu vā̱yora̱mṛta̱ṃ vi da̍syet

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 4

vari̍ṣṭho asya̱ dakṣi̍ṇāmiya̱rtīndro̍ ma̱ghonā̍ṃ tuvikū̱rmita̍maḥ |

yayā̍ vajrivaḥ pari̱yāsyaṃho̍ ma̱ghā ca̍ dhṛṣṇo̱ daya̍se̱ vi sū̱rīn

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 37 | **Verse:** 5

indro̱ vāja̍sya̱ sthavi̍rasya dā̱tendro̍ gī̱rbhirva̍rdhatāṃ vṛ̱ddhama̍hāḥ |

indro̍ vṛ̱traṃ hani̍ṣṭho astu̱ satvā ''tā sū̱riḥ pṛ̍ṇati̱ tūtu̍jānaḥ
