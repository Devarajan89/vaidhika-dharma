# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 35

**5 naro bhāradvāja:| indra: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 1

ka̱dā bhu̍va̱nratha̍kṣayāṇi̱ brahma̍ ka̱dā sto̱tre sa̍hasrapo̱ṣya̍ṃ dāḥ |
ka̱dā stoma̍ṃ vāsayo'sya rā̱yā ka̱dā dhiya̍: karasi̱ vāja̍ratnāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 2

karhi̍ svi̱ttadi̍ndra̱ yannṛbhi̱rnṝnvī̱rairvī̱rānnī̱l̤ayā̍se̱ jayā̱jīn |

tri̱dhātu̱ gā adhi̍ jayāsi̱ goṣvindra̍ dyu̱mnaṃ sva̍rvaddhehya̱sme

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 3

karhi̍ svi̱ttadi̍ndra̱ yajja̍ri̱tre vi̱śvapsu̱ brahma̍ kṛ̱ṇava̍: śaviṣṭha |

ka̱dā dhiyo̱ na ni̱yuto̍ yuvāse ka̱dā goma̍ghā̱ hava̍nāni gacchāḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 4

sa goma̍ghā jari̱tre aśva̍ścandrā̱ vāja̍śravaso̱ adhi̍ dhehi̱ pṛkṣa̍: |

pī̱pi̱hīṣa̍: su̱dughā̍mindra dhe̱nuṃ bha̱radvā̍jeṣu su̱ruco̍ rurucyāḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 35 | **Verse:** 5

tamā nū̱naṃ vṛ̱jana̍ma̱nyathā̍ ci̱cchūro̱ yaccha̍kra̱ vi duro̍ gṛṇī̱ṣe |

mā nira̍raṃ śukra̱dugha̍sya dhe̱norā̍ṅgira̱sānbrahma̍ṇā vipra jinva
