# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 66

**11 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | maruta:| triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 1

vapu̱rnu tacci̍ki̱tuṣe̍ cidastu samā̱naṃ nāma̍ dhe̱nu patya̍mānam |
marte̍ṣva̱nyaddo̱hase̍ pī̱pāya̍ sa̱kṛcchu̱kraṃ du̍duhe̱ pṛśni̱rūdha̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 2

ye a̱gnayo̱ na śośu̍cannidhā̱nā dviryattrirma̱ruto̍ vāvṛ̱dhanta̍ |

a̱re̱ṇavo̍ hira̱ṇyayā̍sa eṣāṃ sā̱kaṃ nṛ̱mṇaiḥ pauṃsye̍bhiśca bhūvan

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 3

ru̱drasya̱ ye mī̱l̤huṣa̱: santi̍ pu̱trā yām̐śco̱ nu dādhṛ̍vi̱rbhara̍dhyai |

vi̱de hi mā̱tā ma̱ho ma̱hī ṣā setpṛśni̍: su̱bhve̱3 garbha̱mādhā̍t

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 4

na ya īṣa̍nte ja̱nuṣo'yā̱ nva1ntaḥ santo̍'va̱dyāni̍ punā̱nāḥ |

niryaddu̱hre śuca̱yo'nu̱ joṣa̱manu̍ śri̱yā ta̱nva̍mu̱kṣamā̍ṇāḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 5

ma̱kṣū na yeṣu̍ do̱hase̍ cida̱yā ā nāma̍ dhṛ̱ṣṇu māru̍ta̱ṃ dadhā̍nāḥ |

na ye stau̱nā a̱yāso̍ ma̱hnā nū ci̍tsu̱dānu̱rava̍ yāsadu̱grān

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 6

ta idu̱grāḥ śava̍sā dhṛ̱ṣṇuṣe̍ṇā u̱bhe yu̍janta̱ roda̍sī su̱meke̍ |

adha̍ smaiṣu roda̱sī svaśo̍ci̱rāma̍vatsu tasthau̱ na roka̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 7

a̱ne̱no vo̍ maruto̱ yāmo̍ astvana̱śvaści̱dyamaja̱tyara̍thīḥ |

a̱na̱va̱so a̍nabhī̱śū ra̍ja̱stūrvi roda̍sī pa̱thyā̍ yāti̱ sādha̍n

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 8

nāsya̍ va̱rtā na ta̍ru̱tā nva̍sti̱ maru̍to̱ yamava̍tha̱ vāja̍sātau |

to̱ke vā̱ goṣu̱ tana̍ye̱ yama̱psu sa vra̱jaṃ dartā̱ pārye̱ adha̱ dyoḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 9

pra ci̱trama̱rkaṃ gṛ̍ṇa̱te tu̱rāya̱ māru̍tāya̱ svata̍vase bharadhvam |

ye sahā̍ṃsi̱ saha̍sā̱ saha̍nte̱ reja̍te agne pṛthi̱vī ma̱khebhya̍:

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 10

tviṣī̍manto adhva̱rasye̍va di̱dyuttṛ̍ṣu̱cyava̍so ju̱hvo̱3 nāgneḥ |

a̱rcatra̍yo̱ dhuna̍yo̱ na vī̱rā bhrāja̍jjanmāno ma̱ruto̱ adhṛ̍ṣṭāḥ

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 66 | **Verse:** 11

taṃ vṛ̱dhanta̱ṃ māru̍ta̱ṃ bhrāja̍dṛṣṭiṃ ru̱drasya̍ sū̱nuṃ ha̱vasā vi̍vāse |

di̱vaḥ śardhā̍ya̱ śuca̍yo manī̱ṣā gi̱rayo̱ nāpa̍ u̱grā a̍spṛdhran
