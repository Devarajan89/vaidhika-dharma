# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 11

**6 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | agni : | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 1

yaja̍sva hotariṣi̱to yajī̍yā̱nagne̱ bādho̍ ma̱rutā̱ṃ na prayu̍kti |
ā no̍ mi̱trāvaru̍ṇā̱ nāsa̍tyā̱ dyāvā̍ ho̱trāya̍ pṛthi̱vī va̍vṛtyāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 2

tvaṃ hotā̍ ma̱ndrata̍mo no a̱dhruga̱ntarde̱vo vi̱dathā̱ martye̍ṣu |

pā̱va̱kayā̍ ju̱hvā̱3 vahni̍rā̱sā 'gne̱ yaja̍sva ta̱nvaṃ1 tava̱ svām

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 3

dhanyā̍ ci̱ddhi tve dhi̱ṣaṇā̱ vaṣṭi̱ pra de̱vāñjanma̍ gṛṇa̱te yaja̍dhyai |

vepi̍ṣṭho̱ aṅgi̍rasā̱ṃ yaddha̱ vipro̱ madhu̍ ccha̱ndo bhana̍ti re̱bha i̱ṣṭau

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 4

adi̍dyuta̱t svapā̍ko vi̱bhāvā 'gne̱ yaja̍sva̱ roda̍sī urū̱cī |

ā̱yuṃ na yaṃ nama̍sā rā̱taha̍vyā a̱ñjanti̍ supra̱yasa̱ṃ pañca̱ janā̍:

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 11 | **Verse:** 5

vṛ̱ñje ha̱ yannama̍sā ba̱rhira̱gnāvayā̍mi̱ srugghṛ̱tava̍tī suvṛ̱ktiḥ |

amya̍kṣi̱ sadma̱ sada̍ne pṛthi̱vyā aśrā̍yi ya̱jñaḥ sūrye̱ na cakṣu̍:
