# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 6

**7 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | agni : |triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 1

pra navya̍sā̱ saha̍saḥ sū̱numacchā̍ ya̱jñena̍ gā̱tumava̍ i̱cchamā̍naḥ |
vṛ̱ścadva̍naṃ kṛ̱ṣṇayā̍ma̱ṃ ruśa̍ntaṃ vī̱tī hotā̍raṃ di̱vyaṃ ji̍gāti

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 2

sa śvi̍tā̱nasta̍nya̱tū ro̍cana̱sthā a̱jare̍bhi̱rnāna̍dadbhi̱ryavi̍ṣṭhaḥ |

yaḥ pā̍va̱kaḥ pu̍ru̱tama̍: pu̱rūṇi̍ pṛ̱thūnya̱gnira̍nu̱yāti̱ bharva̍n

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 3

vi te̱ viṣva̱gvāta̍jūtāso agne̱ bhāmā̍saḥ śuce̱ śuca̍yaścaranti |

tu̱vi̱mra̱kṣāso̍ di̱vyā nava̍gvā̱ vanā̍ vananti dhṛṣa̱tā ru̱janta̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 4

ye te̍ śu̱krāsa̱: śuca̍yaḥ śuciṣma̱: kṣāṃ vapa̍nti̱ viṣi̍tāso̱ aśvā̍: |

adha̍ bhra̱masta̍ urvi̱yā vi bhā̍ti yā̱taya̍māno̱ adhi̱ sānu̱ pṛśne̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 5

adha̍ ji̱hvā pā̍patīti̱ pra vṛṣṇo̍ goṣu̱yudho̱ nāśani̍: sṛjā̱nā |

śūra̍syeva̱ prasi̍tiḥ kṣā̱tira̱gnerdu̱rvartu̍rbhī̱mo da̍yate̱ vanā̍ni

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 6

ā bhā̱nunā̱ pārthi̍vāni̱ jrayā̍ṃsi ma̱hasto̱dasya̍ dhṛṣa̱tā ta̍tantha |

sa bā̍dha̱svāpa̍ bha̱yā saho̍bhi̱: spṛdho̍ vanu̱ṣyan va̱nuṣo̱ ni jū̍rva

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 6 | **Verse:** 7

sa ci̍tra ci̱traṃ ci̱taya̍ntama̱sme citra̍kṣatra ci̱trata̍maṃ vayo̱dhām |

ca̱ndraṃ ra̱yiṃ pu̍ru̱vīra̍ṃ bṛ̱hanta̱ṃ candra̍ ca̱ndrābhi̍rgṛṇa̱te yu̍vasva
