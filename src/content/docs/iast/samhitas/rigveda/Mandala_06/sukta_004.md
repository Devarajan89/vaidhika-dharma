# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 4

**8 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | agni : |triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 1

yathā̍ hota̱rmanu̍ṣo de̱vatā̍tā ya̱jñebhi̍: sūno sahaso̱ yajā̍si |
e̱vā no̍ a̱dya sa̍ma̱nā sa̍mā̱nānu̱śanna̍gna uśa̱to ya̍kṣi de̱vān

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 2

sa no̍ vi̱bhāvā̍ ca̱kṣaṇi̱rna vasto̍ra̱gnirva̱ndāru̱ vedya̱ścano̍ dhāt |

vi̱śvāyu̱ryo a̱mṛto̱ martye̍ṣūṣa̱rbhud bhūdati̍thirjā̱tave̍dāḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 3

dyāvo̱ na yasya̍ pa̱naya̱ntyabhva̱ṃ bhāsā̍ṃsi vaste̱ sūryo̱ na śu̱kraḥ |

vi ya i̱notya̱jara̍: pāva̱ko 'śna̍sya cicchiśnathat pū̱rvyāṇi̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 4

va̱dmā hi sū̍no̱ asya̍dma̱sadvā̍ ca̱kre a̱gnirja̱nuṣājmānna̍m |

sa tvaṃ na̍ ūrjasana̱ ūrja̍ṃ dhā̱ rāje̍va jeravṛ̱ke kṣe̍ṣya̱ntaḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 5

niti̍kti̱ yo vā̍ra̱ṇamanna̱matti̍ vā̱yurna rāṣṭryatye̍tya̱ktūn |

tu̱ryāma̱ yasta̍ ā̱diśā̱marā̍tī̱ratyo̱ na hruta̱: pata̍taḥ pari̱hrut

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 6

ā sūryo̱ na bhā̍nu̱madbhi̍ra̱rkairagne̍ ta̱tantha̱ roda̍sī̱ vi bhā̱sā |

ci̱tro na̍ya̱t pari̱ tamā̍ṃsya̱ktaḥ śo̱ciṣā̱ patma̍nnauśi̱jo na dīya̍n

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 7

tvāṃ hi ma̱ndrata̍mamarkaśo̱kairva̍vṛ̱mahe̱ mahi̍ na̱: śroṣya̍gne |

indra̱ṃ na tvā̱ śava̍sā de̱vatā̍ vā̱yuṃ pṛ̍ṇanti̱ rādha̍sā̱ nṛta̍māḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 4 | **Verse:** 8

nū no̍ agne'vṛ̱kebhi̍: sva̱sti veṣi̍ rā̱yaḥ pa̱thibhi̱: parṣyaṃha̍: |

tā sū̱ribhyo̍ gṛṇa̱te rā̍si su̱mnaṃ made̍ma śa̱tahi̍māḥ su̱vīrā̍:
