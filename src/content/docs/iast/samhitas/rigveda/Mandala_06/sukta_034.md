# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 34

**5 śunahotro bhāradvāja: | indra: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 1

saṃ ca̱ tve ja̱gmurgira̍ indra pū̱rvīrvi ca̱ tvadya̍nti vi̱bhvo̍ manī̱ṣāḥ |
pu̱rā nū̱naṃ ca̍ stu̱taya̱ ṛṣī̍ṇāṃ paspṛ̱dhra indre̱ adhyu̍kthā̱rkā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 2

pu̱ru̱hū̱to yaḥ pu̍rugū̱rta ṛbhvā̱m̐ eka̍: purupraśa̱sto asti̍ ya̱jñaiḥ |

ratho̱ na ma̱he śava̍se yujā̱no̱3'smābhi̱rindro̍ anu̱mādyo̍ bhūt

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 3

na yaṃ hiṃsa̍nti dhī̱tayo̱ na vāṇī̱rindra̱ṃ nakṣa̱ntīda̱bhi va̱rdhaya̍ntīḥ |

yadi̍ sto̱tāra̍: śa̱taṃ yatsa̱hasra̍ṃ gṛ̱ṇanti̱ girva̍ṇasa̱ṃ śaṃ tada̍smai

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 34 | **Verse:** 4

asmā̍ e̱taddi̱vya1rceva̍ mā̱sā mi̍mi̱kṣa indre̱ nya̍yāmi̱ soma̍: |

jana̱ṃ na dhanva̍nna̱bhi saṃ yadāpa̍: sa̱trā vā̍vṛdhu̱rhava̍nāni ya̱jñaiḥ

## Verse 5
