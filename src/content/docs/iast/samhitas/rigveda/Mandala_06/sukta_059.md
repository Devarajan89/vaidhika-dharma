# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 59

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 59 | **Verse:** 1

ha̱tāso̍ vāṃ pi̱taro̍ de̱vaśa̍trava̱ indrā̍gnī̱ jīva̍tho yu̱vam
