# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 69

**8 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | indrāviṣṇū| triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 1

saṃ vā̱ṃ karma̍ṇā̱ sami̱ṣā hi̍no̱mīndrā̍viṣṇū̱ apa̍saspā̱re a̱sya |
ju̱ṣethā̍ṃ ya̱jñaṃ dravi̍ṇaṃ ca dhatta̱mari̍ṣṭairnaḥ pa̱thibhi̍: pā̱raya̍ntā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 2

yā viśvā̍sāṃ jani̱tārā̍ matī̱nāmindrā̱viṣṇū̍ ka̱laśā̍ soma̱dhānā̍ |

pra vā̱ṃ gira̍: śa̱syamā̍nā avantu̱ pra stomā̍so gī̱yamā̍nāso a̱rkaiḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 3

indrā̍viṣṇū madapatī madānā̱mā soma̍ṃ yāta̱ṃ dravi̍ṇo̱ dadhā̍nā |

saṃ vā̍mañjantva̱ktubhi̍rmatī̱nāṃ saṃ stomā̍saḥ śa̱syamā̍nāsa u̱kthaiḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 4

ā vā̱maśvā̍so abhimāti̱ṣāha̱ indrā̍viṣṇū sadha̱mādo̍ vahantu |

ju̱ṣethā̱ṃ viśvā̱ hava̍nā matī̱nāmupa̱ brahmā̍ṇi śṛṇuta̱ṃ giro̍ me

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 5

indrā̍viṣṇū̱ tatpa̍na̱yāyya̍ṃ vā̱ṃ soma̍sya̱ mada̍ u̱ru ca̍kramāthe |

akṛ̍ṇutama̱ntari̍kṣa̱ṃ varī̱yo'pra̍thataṃ jī̱vase̍ no̱ rajā̍ṃsi

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 6

indrā̍viṣṇū ha̱viṣā̍ vāvṛdhā̱nā'grā̍dvānā̱ nama̍sā rātahavyā |

ghṛtā̍sutī̱ dravi̍ṇaṃ dhattama̱sme sa̍mu̱draḥ stha̍: ka̱laśa̍: soma̱dhāna̍:

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 7

indrā̍viṣṇū̱ piba̍ta̱ṃ madhvo̍ a̱sya soma̍sya dasrā ja̱ṭhara̍ṃ pṛṇethām |

ā vā̱mandhā̍ṃsi madi̱rāṇya̍gma̱nnupa̱ brahmā̍ṇi śṛṇuta̱ṃ hava̍ṃ me

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 69 | **Verse:** 8

u̱bhā ji̍gyathu̱rna parā̍ jayethe̱ na parā̍ jigye kata̱raśca̱naino̍: |

indra̍śca viṣṇo̱ yadapa̍spṛdhethāṃ tre̱dhā sa̱hasra̱ṃ vi tadai̍rayethām
