# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 40

**5 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | indra: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 1

indra̱ piba̱ tubhya̍ṃ su̱to madā̱yā'va̍ sya̱ harī̱ vi mu̍cā̱ sakhā̍yā |
u̱ta pra gā̍ya ga̱ṇa ā ni̱ṣadyā'thā̍ ya̱jñāya̍ gṛṇa̱te vayo̍ dhāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 2

asya̍ piba̱ yasya̍ jajñā̱na i̍ndra̱ madā̍ya̱ kratve̱ api̍bo virapśin |

tamu̍ te̱ gāvo̱ nara̱ āpo̱ adri̱rindu̱ṃ sama̍hyanpī̱taye̱ sama̍smai

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 3

sami̍ddhe a̱gnau su̱ta i̍ndra̱ soma̱ ā tvā̍ vahantu̱ hara̍yo̱ vahi̍ṣṭhāḥ |

tvā̱ya̱tā mana̍sā johavī̱mīndrā yā̍hi suvi̱tāya̍ ma̱he na̍:

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 40 | **Verse:** 4
