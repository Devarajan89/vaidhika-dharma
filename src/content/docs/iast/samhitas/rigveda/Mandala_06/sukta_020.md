# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 20

**13 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | indra: | triṣṭup , 7 virāṭ |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 1

dyaurna ya i̍ndrā̱bhi bhūmā̱ryasta̱sthau ra̱yiḥ śava̍sā pṛ̱tsu janā̍n |
taṃ na̍: sa̱hasra̍bharamurvarā̱sāṃ da̱ddhi sū̍no sahaso vṛtra̱tura̍m

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 2

di̱vo na tubhya̱manvi̍ndra sa̱trā 'su̱rya̍ṃ de̱vebhi̍rdhāyi̱ viśva̍m |

ahi̱ṃ yad vṛtrama̱po va̍vri̱vāṃsa̱ṃ hannṛ̍jīṣi̱n viṣṇu̍nā sacā̱naḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 3

tūrva̱nnojī̍yān ta̱vasa̱stavī̍yān kṛ̱tabra̱hmendro̍ vṛ̱ddhama̍hāḥ |

rājā̍bhava̱nmadhu̍naḥ so̱myasya̱ viśvā̍sā̱ṃ yat pu̱rāṃ da̱rtnumāva̍t

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 4

śa̱taira̍padran pa̱ṇaya̍ i̱ndrātra̱ daśo̍ṇaye ka̱vaye̱'rkasā̍tau |

va̱dhaiḥ śuṣṇa̍syā̱śuṣa̍sya mā̱yāḥ pi̱tvo nāri̍recī̱t kiṃ ca̱na pra

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 5

ma̱ho dru̱ho apa̍ vi̱śvāyu̍ dhāyi̱ vajra̍sya̱ yat pata̍ne̱ pādi̱ śuṣṇa̍: |

u̱ru ṣa sa̱ratha̱ṃ sāra̍thaye ka̱rindra̱: kutsā̍ya̱ sūrya̍sya sā̱tau

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 6

pra śye̱no na ma̍di̱rama̱ṃśuma̍smai̱ śiro̍ dā̱sasya̱ namu̍cermathā̱yan |

prāva̱nnamī̍ṃ sā̱pyaṃ sa̱santa̍ṃ pṛ̱ṇagrā̱yā sami̱ṣā saṃ sva̱sti

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 7

vi pipro̱rahi̍māyasya dṛ̱l̤hāḥ puro̍ vajri̱ñchava̍sā̱ na da̍rdaḥ |

sudā̍ma̱n tad rekṇo̍ apramṛ̱ṣyamṛ̱jiśva̍ne dā̱traṃ dā̱śuṣe̍ dāḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 8

sa ve̍ta̱suṃ daśa̍māya̱ṃ daśo̍ṇi̱ṃ tūtu̍ji̱mindra̍: svabhi̱ṣṭisu̍mnaḥ |

ā tugra̱ṃ śaśva̱dibha̱ṃ dyota̍nāya mā̱turna sī̱mupa̍ sṛjā i̱yadhyai̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 9

sa ī̱ṃ spṛdho̍ vanate̱ apra̍tīto̱ bibhra̱d vajra̍ṃ vṛtra̱haṇa̱ṃ gabha̍stau |

tiṣṭha̱ddharī̱ adhyaste̍va̱ garte̍ vaco̱yujā̍ vahata̱ indra̍mṛ̱ṣvam

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 10

sa̱nema̱ te'va̍sā̱ navya̍ indra̱ pra pū̱rava̍: stavanta e̱nā ya̱jñaiḥ |

sa̱pta yat pura̱: śarma̱ śāra̍dī̱rdarddhan dāsī̍: puru̱kutsā̍ya̱ śikṣa̍n

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 11

tvaṃ vṛ̱dha i̍ndra pū̱rvyo bhū̍rvariva̱syannu̱śane̍ kā̱vyāya̍ |

parā̱ nava̍vāstvamanu̱deya̍ṃ ma̱he pi̱tre da̍dātha̱ svaṃ napā̍tam

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 12

tvaṃ dhuni̍rindra̱ dhuni̍matīṝ̱ṇora̱paḥ sī̱rā na srava̍ntīḥ |

pra yatsa̍mu̱dramati̍ śūra̱ parṣi̍ pā̱rayā̍ tu̱rvaśa̱ṃ yadu̍ṃ sva̱sti

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 20 | **Verse:** 13

tava̍ ha̱ tyadi̍ndra̱ viśva̍mā̱jau sa̱sto dhunī̱cumu̍rī̱ yā ha̱ siṣva̍p |

dī̱daya̱dit tubhya̱ṃ some̍bhiḥ su̱nvan dabhīti̍ri̱dhmabhṛ̍tiḥ pa̱kthya1rkaiḥ
