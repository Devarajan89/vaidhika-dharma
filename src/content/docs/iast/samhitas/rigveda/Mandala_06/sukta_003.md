# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 3

**8 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | agni : |triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 1

agne̱ sa kṣe̍ṣadṛta̱pā ṛ̍te̱jā u̱ru jyoti̍rnaśate deva̱yuṣṭe̍ |
yaṃ tvaṃ mi̱treṇa̱ varu̍ṇaḥ sa̱joṣā̱ deva̱ pāsi̱ tyaja̍sā̱ marta̱maṃha̍:

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 2

ī̱je ya̱jñebhi̍: śaśa̱me śamī̍bhiṝ̱dhadvā̍rāyā̱gnaye̍ dadāśa |

e̱vā ca̱na taṃ ya̱śasā̱maju̍ṣṭi̱rnāṃho̱ marta̍ṃ naśate̱ na pradṛ̍ptiḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 3

sūro̱ na yasya̍ dṛśa̱tira̍re̱pā bhī̱mā yadeti̍ śuca̱tasta̱ ā dhīḥ |

heṣa̍svataḥ śu̱rudho̱ nāyama̱ktoḥ kutrā̍ cid ra̱ṇvo va̍sa̱tirva̍ne̱jāḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 4

ti̱gmaṃ ci̱dema̱ mahi̱ varpo̍ asya̱ bhasa̱daśvo̱ na ya̍masā̱na ā̱sā |

vi̱jeha̍mānaḥ para̱śurna ji̱hvāṃ dra̱virna drā̍vayati̱ dāru̱ dhakṣa̍t

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 5

sa idaste̍va̱ prati̍ dhādasi̱ṣyañchiśī̍ta̱ tejo'ya̍so̱ na dhārā̍m |

ci̱tradhra̍jatirara̱tiryo a̱ktorverna dru̱ṣadvā̍ raghu̱patma̍jaṃhāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 6

sa ī̍ṃ re̱bho na prati̍ vasta u̱srāḥ śo̱ciṣā̍ rārapīti mi̱trama̍hāḥ |

nakta̱ṃ ya ī̍maru̱ṣo yo divā̱ nṝnama̍rtyo aru̱ṣo yo divā̱ nṝn

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 7

di̱vo na yasya̍ vidha̱to navī̍no̱d vṛṣā̍ ru̱kṣa oṣa̍dhīṣu nūnot |

ghṛṇā̱ na yo dhraja̍sā̱ patma̍nā̱ yannā roda̍sī̱ vasu̍nā̱ daṃ su̱patnī̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 3 | **Verse:** 8

dhāyo̍bhirvā̱ yo yujye̍bhira̱rkairvi̱dyunna da̍vidyo̱t svebhi̱: śuṣmai̍: |

śardho̍ vā̱ yo ma̱rutā̍ṃ ta̱takṣa̍ ṛ̱bhurna tve̱ṣo ra̍bhasā̱no a̍dyaut
