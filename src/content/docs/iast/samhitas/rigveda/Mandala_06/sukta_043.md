# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 43

**4 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | indra: |uṣṇik |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 1

yasya̱ tyacchamba̍ra̱ṃ made̱ divo̍dāsāya ra̱ndhaya̍: | a̱yaṃ sa soma̍ indra te su̱taḥ piba̍

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 43 | **Verse:** 2
