# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 28

**8 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | gāva:; 2, 8  indro gāvo vā | triṣṭup , 2 – 4 jagatī, 8 anuṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 1

ā gāvo̍ agmannu̱ta bha̱drama̍kra̱ntsīda̍ntu go̱ṣṭhe ra̱ṇaya̍ntva̱sme |
pra̱jāva̍tīḥ puru̱rūpā̍ i̱ha syu̱rindrā̍ya pū̱rvīru̱ṣaso̱ duhā̍nāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 2

indro̱ yajva̍ne pṛṇa̱te ca̍ śikṣa̱tyupedda̍dāti̱ na svaṃ mu̍ṣāyati |

bhūyo̍bhūyo ra̱yimida̍sya va̱rdhaya̱nnabhi̍nne khi̱lye ni da̍dhāti deva̱yum

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 3

na tā na̍śanti̱ na da̍bhāti̱ taska̍ro̱ nāsā̍māmi̱tro vyathi̱rā da̍dharṣati |

de̱vām̐śca̱ yābhi̱ryaja̍te̱ dadā̍ti ca̱ jyogittābhi̍: sacate̱ gopa̍tiḥ sa̱ha

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 4

na tā arvā̍ re̱ṇuka̍kāṭo aśnute̱ na sa̍ṃskṛta̱tramupa̍ yanti̱ tā a̱bhi |

u̱ru̱gā̱yamabha̍ya̱ṃ tasya̱ tā anu̱ gāvo̱ marta̍sya̱ vi ca̍ranti̱ yajva̍naḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 5

gāvo̱ bhago̱ gāva̱ indro̍ me acchā̱ngāva̱: soma̍sya pratha̱masya̍ bha̱kṣaḥ |

i̱mā yā gāva̱: sa ja̍nāsa̱ indra̍ i̱cchāmīddhṛ̱dā mana̍sā ci̱dindra̍m

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 6

yū̱yaṃ gā̍vo medayathā kṛ̱śaṃ ci̍daśrī̱raṃ ci̍tkṛṇuthā su̱pratī̍kam |

bha̱draṃ gṛ̱haṃ kṛ̍ṇutha bhadravāco bṛ̱hadvo̱ vaya̍ ucyate sa̱bhāsu̍

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 7

pra̱jāva̍tīḥ sū̱yava̍saṃ ri̱śantī̍: śu̱ddhā a̱paḥ su̍prapā̱ṇe piba̍ntīḥ |

mā va̍: ste̱na ī̍śata̱ māghaśa̍ṃsa̱: pari̍ vo he̱tī ru̱drasya̍ vṛjyāḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 28 | **Verse:** 8

upe̱damu̍pa̱parca̍namā̱su goṣūpa̍ pṛcyatām | upa̍ ṛṣa̱bhasya̱ reta̱syupe̍ndra̱ tava̍ vī̱rye̍
