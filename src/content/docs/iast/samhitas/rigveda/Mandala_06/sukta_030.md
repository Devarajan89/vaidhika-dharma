# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 30

**5 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | indra: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 1

bhūya̱ idvā̍vṛdhe vī̱ryā̍ya̱m̐ eko̍ aju̱ryo da̍yate̱ vasū̍ni |
pra ri̍rice di̱va indra̍: pṛthi̱vyā a̱rdhamida̍sya̱ prati̱ roda̍sī u̱bhe

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 2

adhā̍ manye bṛ̱hada̍su̱rya̍masya̱ yāni̍ dā̱dhāra̱ naki̱rā mi̍nāti |

di̱vedi̍ve̱ sūryo̍ darśa̱to bhū̱dvi sadmā̍nyurvi̱yā su̱kratu̍rdhāt

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 3

a̱dyā ci̱nnū ci̱ttadapo̍ na̱dīnā̱ṃ yadā̍bhyo̱ ara̍do gā̱tumi̍ndra |

ni parva̍tā adma̱sado̱ na se̍du̱stvayā̍ dṛ̱l̤hāni̍ sukrato̱ rajā̍ṃsi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 4

sa̱tyamittanna tvāvā̍m̐ a̱nyo a̱stīndra̍ de̱vo na martyo̱ jyāyā̍n |

aha̱nnahi̍ṃ pari̱śayā̍na̱marṇo'vā̍sṛjo a̱po acchā̍ samu̱dram

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 30 | **Verse:** 5

rājā̍bhavo̱ jaga̍taścarṣaṇī̱nāṃ sā̱kaṃ sūrya̍ṃ ja̱naya̱ndyāmu̱ṣāsa̍m
