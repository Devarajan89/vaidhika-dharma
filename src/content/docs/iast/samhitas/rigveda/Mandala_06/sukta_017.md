# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 17

**15 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | indra: |triṣṭup , 15 dvipadā virāṭ |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 1

pibā̱ soma̍ma̱bhi yamu̍gra̱ tarda̍ ū̱rvaṃ gavya̱ṃ mahi̍ gṛṇā̱na i̍ndra |
vi yo dhṛ̍ṣṇo̱ vadhi̍ṣo vajrahasta̱ viśvā̍ vṛ̱trama̍mi̱triyā̱ śavo̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 2

sa ī̍ṃ pāhi̱ ya ṛ̍jī̱ṣī taru̍tro̱ yaḥ śipra̍vān vṛṣa̱bho yo ma̍tī̱nām |

yo go̍tra̱bhid va̍jra̱bhṛd yo ha̍ri̱ṣṭhāḥ sa i̍ndra ci̱trām̐ a̱bhi tṛ̍ndhi̱ vājā̍n

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 3

ā̱viḥ sūrya̍ṃ kṛṇu̱hi pī̍pi̱hīṣo̍ ja̱hi śatrū̍m̐ra̱bhi gā i̍ndra tṛndhi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 4

ma̱hāmanū̍naṃ ta̱vasa̱ṃ vibhū̍tiṃ matsa̱rāso̍ jarhṛṣanta pra̱sāha̍m

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 5

ma̱hāmadri̱ṃ pari̱ gā i̍ndra̱ santa̍ṃ nu̱tthā acyu̍ta̱ṃ sada̍sa̱spari̱ svāt

## Verse 6

aurṇo̱rdura̍ u̱sriyā̍bhyo̱ vi dṛ̱l̤hodū̱rvād gā a̍sṛjo̱ aṅgi̍rasvān

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 7

adhā̍rayo̱ roda̍sī de̱vapu̍tre pra̱tne mā̱tarā̍ ya̱hvī ṛ̱tasya̍

## Verse 8

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 17 | **Verse:** 9

ahi̱ṃ yadindro̍ a̱bhyoha̍sāna̱ṃ ni ci̍d vi̱śvāyu̍: śa̱yathe̍ ja̱ghāna̍

## Verse 10

nikā̍mama̱rama̍ṇasa̱ṃ yena̱ nava̍nta̱mahi̱ṃ saṃ pi̍ṇagṛjīṣin

## Verse 11
