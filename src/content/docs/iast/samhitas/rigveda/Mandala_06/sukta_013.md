# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 13

**6 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | agni : |triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 1

tvad viśvā̍ subhaga̱ saubha̍gā̱nyagne̱ vi ya̍nti va̱nino̱ na va̱yāḥ |
śru̱ṣṭī ra̱yirvājo̍ vṛtra̱tūrye̍ di̱vo vṛ̱ṣṭirīḍyo̍ rī̱tira̱pām

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 2

tvaṃ bhago̍ na̱ ā hi ratna̍mi̱ṣe pari̍jmeva kṣayasi da̱smava̍rcāḥ |

agne̍ mi̱tro na bṛ̍ha̱ta ṛ̱tasyā'si̍ kṣa̱ttā vā̱masya̍ deva̱ bhūre̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 3

sa satpa̍ti̱: śava̍sā hanti vṛ̱tramagne̱ vipro̱ vi pa̱ṇerbha̍rti̱ vāja̍m |

yaṃ tvaṃ pra̍ceta ṛtajāta rā̱yā sa̱joṣā̱ naptrā̱pāṃ hi̱noṣi̍

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 4

yaste̍ sūno sahaso gī̱rbhiru̱kthairya̱jñairmarto̱ niśi̍tiṃ ve̱dyāna̍ṭ |

viśva̱ṃ sa de̍va̱ prati̱ vāra̍magne dha̱tte dhā̱nyaṃ1 patya̍te vasa̱vyai̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 5

tā nṛbhya̱ ā sau̍śrava̱sā su̱vīrā 'gne̍ sūno sahasaḥ pu̱ṣyase̍ dhāḥ |

kṛ̱ṇoṣi̱ yacchava̍sā̱ bhūri̍ pa̱śvo vayo̱ vṛkā̍yā̱raye̱ jasu̍raye

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 13 | **Verse:** 6

va̱dmā sū̍no sahaso no̱ vihā̍yā̱ agne̍ to̱kaṃ tana̍yaṃ vā̱ji no̍ dāḥ |

viśvā̍bhirgī̱rbhira̱bhi pū̱rtima̍śyā̱ṃ made̍ma śa̱tahi̍māḥ su̱vīrā̍:
