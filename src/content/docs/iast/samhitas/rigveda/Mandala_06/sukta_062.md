# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 62

**11 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: |aśvinau | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 1

stu̱ṣe narā̍ di̱vo a̱sya pra̱santā̱'śvinā̍ huve̱ jara̍māṇo a̱rkaiḥ |
yā sa̱dya u̱srā vyuṣi̱ jmo antā̱nyuyū̍ṣata̱: paryu̱rū varā̍ṃsi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 2

tā ya̱jñamā śuci̍bhiścakramā̱ṇā ratha̍sya bhā̱nuṃ ru̍rucū̱ rajo̍bhiḥ |

pu̱rū varā̱ṃsyami̍tā̱ mimā̍nā̱'po dhanvā̱nyati̍ yātho̱ ajrā̍n

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 3

tā ha̱ tyadva̱rtiryadara̍dhramugre̱tthā dhiya̍ ūhathu̱: śaśva̱daśvai̍: |

mano̍javebhiriṣi̱raiḥ śa̱yadhyai̱ pari̱ vyathi̍rdā̱śuṣo̱ martya̍sya

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 4

tā navya̍so̱ jara̍māṇasya̱ manmopa̍ bhūṣato yuyujā̱nasa̍ptī |

śubha̱ṃ pṛkṣa̱miṣa̱mūrja̱ṃ vaha̍ntā̱ hotā̍ yakṣatpra̱tno a̱dhrugyuvā̍nā

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 5

tā va̱lgū da̱srā pu̍ru̱śāka̍tamā pra̱tnā navya̍sā̱ vaca̱sā vi̍vāse |

yā śaṃsa̍te stuva̱te śambha̍viṣṭhā babhū̱vatu̍rgṛṇa̱te ci̱trarā̍tī

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 6

tā bhu̱jyuṃ vibhi̍ra̱dbhyaḥ sa̍mu̱drāttugra̍sya sū̱numū̍hathū̱ rajo̍bhiḥ |

a̱re̱ṇubhi̱ryoja̍nebhirbhu̱jantā̍ pata̱tribhi̱rarṇa̍so̱ niru̱pasthā̍t

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 7

vi ja̱yuṣā̍ rathyā yāta̱madri̍ṃ śru̱taṃ hava̍ṃ vṛṣaṇā vadhrima̱tyāḥ |

da̱śa̱syantā̍ śa̱yave̍ pipyathu̱rgāmiti̍ cyavānā suma̱tiṃ bhu̍raṇyū

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 8

yadro̍dasī pra̱divo̱ asti̱ bhūmā̱ hel̤o̍ de̱vānā̍mu̱ta ma̍rtya̱trā |

tadā̍dityā vasavo rudriyāso rakṣo̱yuje̱ tapu̍ra̱ghaṃ da̍dhāta

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 9

ya ī̱ṃ rājā̍nāvṛtu̱thā vi̱dadha̱draja̍so mi̱tro varu̍ṇa̱ścike̍tat |

ga̱mbhī̱rāya̱ rakṣa̍se he̱tima̍sya̱ droghā̍ya ci̱dvaca̍sa̱ āna̍vāya

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 10

anta̍raiśca̱kraistana̍yāya va̱rtirdyu̱matā yā̍taṃ nṛ̱vatā̱ rathe̍na |

sanu̍tyena̱ tyaja̍sā̱ martya̍sya vanuṣya̱tāmapi̍ śī̱rṣā va̍vṛktam

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 62 | **Verse:** 11

ā pa̍ra̱mābhi̍ru̱ta ma̍dhya̱mābhi̍rni̱yudbhi̍ryātamava̱mābhi̍ra̱rvāk |

dṛ̱l̤hasya̍ ci̱dgoma̍to̱ vi vra̱jasya̱ duro̍ vartaṃ gṛṇa̱te ci̍trarātī
