# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 21

**12 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | indra:, 9 , 11 viśve devā: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 1

i̱mā u̍ tvā puru̱tama̍sya kā̱rorhavya̍ṃ vīra̱ havyā̍ havante |
dhiyo̍ rathe̱ṣṭhāma̱jara̱ṃ navī̍yo ra̱yirvibhū̍tirīyate vaca̱syā

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 2

tamu̍ stuṣa̱ indra̱ṃ yo vidā̍no̱ girvā̍hasaṃ gī̱rbhirya̱jñavṛ̍ddham |

yasya̱ diva̱mati̍ ma̱hnā pṛ̍thi̱vyāḥ pu̍rumā̱yasya̍ riri̱ce ma̍hi̱tvam

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 3

sa it tamo̍'vayu̱naṃ ta̍ta̱nvat sūrye̍ṇa va̱yuna̍vaccakāra |

ka̱dā te̱ martā̍ a̱mṛta̍sya̱ dhāmeya̍kṣanto̱ na mi̍nanti svadhāvaḥ

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 4

yastā ca̱kāra̱ sa kuha̍ svi̱dindra̱: kamā jana̍ṃ carati̱ kāsu̍ vi̱kṣu |

kaste̍ ya̱jño mana̍se̱ śaṃ varā̍ya̱ ko a̱rka i̍ndra kata̱maḥ sa hotā̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 5

i̱dā hi te̱ vevi̍ṣataḥ purā̱jāḥ pra̱tnāsa̍ ā̱suḥ pu̍rukṛ̱t sakhā̍yaḥ |

ye ma̍dhya̱māsa̍ u̱ta nūta̍nāsa u̱tāva̱masya̍ puruhūta bodhi

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 6

taṃ pṛ̱cchanto'va̍rāsa̱: parā̍ṇi pra̱tnā ta̍ indra̱ śrutyānu̍ yemuḥ |

arcā̍masi vīra brahmavāho̱ yāde̱va vi̱dma tāt tvā̍ ma̱hānta̍m

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 7

a̱bhi tvā̱ pājo̍ ra̱kṣaso̱ vi ta̍sthe̱ mahi̍ jajñā̱nama̱bhi tat su ti̍ṣṭha |

tava̍ pra̱tnena̱ yujye̍na̱ sakhyā̱ vajre̍ṇa dhṛṣṇo̱ apa̱ tā nu̍dasva

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 8

sa tu śru̍dhīndra̱ nūta̍nasya brahmaṇya̱to vī̍ra kārudhāyaḥ |

tvaṃ hyā̱3piḥ pra̱divi̍ pitṝ̱ṇāṃ śaśva̍d ba̱bhūtha̍ su̱hava̱ eṣṭau̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 9

protaye̱ varu̍ṇaṃ mi̱tramindra̍ṃ ma̱ruta̍: kṛ̱ṣvāva̍se no a̱dya |

pra pū̱ṣaṇa̱ṃ viṣṇu̍ma̱gniṃ pura̍ṃdhiṃ savi̱tāra̱moṣa̍dhī̱: parva̍tām̐śca

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 10

i̱ma u̍ tvā puruśāka prayajyo jari̱tāro̍ a̱bhya̍rcantya̱rkaiḥ |

śru̱dhī hava̱mā hu̍va̱to hu̍vā̱no na tvāvā̍m̐ a̱nyo a̍mṛta̱ tvada̍sti

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 11

nū ma̱ ā vāca̱mupa̍ yāhi vi̱dvān viśve̍bhiḥ sūno sahaso̱ yaja̍traiḥ |

ye a̍gniji̱hvā ṛ̍ta̱sāpa̍ ā̱surye manu̍ṃ ca̱krurupa̍ra̱ṃ dasā̍ya

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 21 | **Verse:** 12

sa no̍ bodhi purae̱tā su̱geṣū̱ta du̱rgeṣu̍ pathi̱kṛd vidā̍naḥ |

ye aśra̍māsa u̱ravo̱ vahi̍ṣṭhā̱stebhi̍rna indrā̱bhi va̍kṣi̱ vāja̍m
