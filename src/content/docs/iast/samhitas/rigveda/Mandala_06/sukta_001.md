# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 1

**13 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | agni : | triṣṭup  |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 1

tvaṃ hya̍gne pratha̱mo ma̱notā̱ 'syā dhi̱yo abha̍vo dasma̱ hotā̍ |
tvaṃ sī̍ṃ vṛṣannakṛṇordu̱ṣṭarī̍tu̱ saho̱ viśva̍smai̱ saha̍se̱ saha̍dhyai

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 2

adhā̱ hotā̱ nya̍sīdo̱ yajī̍yāni̱l̤aspa̱da i̱ṣaya̱nnīḍya̱: san |

taṃ tvā̱ nara̍: pratha̱maṃ de̍va̱yanto̍ ma̱ho rā̱ye ci̱taya̍nto̱ anu̍ gman

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 3

vṛ̱teva̱ yanta̍ṃ ba̱hubhi̍rvasa̱vyai̱3stve ra̱yiṃ jā̍gṛ̱vāṃso̱ anu̍ gman |

ruśa̍ntama̱gniṃ da̍rśa̱ta bṛ̱hanta̍ṃ va̱pāva̍ntaṃ vi̱śvahā̍ dīdi̱vāṃsa̍m

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 4

pa̱daṃ de̱vasya̱ nama̍sā̱ vyanta̍: śrava̱syava̱: śrava̍ āpa̱nnamṛ̍ktam |

nāmā̍ni cid dadhire ya̱jñiyā̍ni bha̱drāyā̍ṃ te raṇayanta̱ saṃdṛ̍ṣṭau

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 5

tvāṃ va̍rdhanti kṣi̱taya̍: pṛthi̱vyāṃ tvāṃ rāya̍ u̱bhayā̍so̱ janā̍nām |

tvaṃ trā̱tā ta̍raṇe̱ cetyo̍ bhūḥ pi̱tā mā̱tā sada̱minmānu̍ṣāṇām

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 6

taṃ tvā̍ va̱yaṃ dama̱ ā dī̍di̱vāṃsa̱mupa̍ jñu̱bādho̱ nama̍sā sadema

## Verse 7

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 1 | **Verse:** 8

## Verse 9
