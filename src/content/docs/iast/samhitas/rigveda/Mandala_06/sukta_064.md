# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 64

**6 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: |uṣā: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 1

udu̍ śri̱ya u̱ṣaso̱ roca̍mānā̱ asthu̍ra̱pāṃ normayo̱ ruśa̍ntaḥ |
kṛ̱ṇoti̱ viśvā̍ su̱pathā̍ su̱gānyabhū̍du̱ vasvī̱ dakṣi̍ṇā ma̱ghonī̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 2

bha̱drā da̍dṛkṣa urvi̱yā vi bhā̱syutte̍ śo̱cirbhā̱navo̱ dyāma̍paptan |

ā̱virvakṣa̍: kṛṇuṣe śu̱mbhamā̱noṣo̍ devi̱ roca̍mānā̱ maho̍bhiḥ

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 3

vaha̍nti sīmaru̱ṇāso̱ ruśa̍nto̱ gāva̍: su̱bhagā̍murvi̱yā pra̍thā̱nām |

ape̍jate̱ śūro̱ aste̍va̱ śatrū̱nbādha̍te̱ tamo̍ aji̱ro na vol̤hā̍

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 4

su̱gota te̍ su̱pathā̱ parva̍teṣvavā̱te a̱pasta̍rasi svabhāno |

sā na̱ ā va̍ha pṛthuyāmannṛṣve ra̱yiṃ di̍vo duhitariṣa̱yadhyai̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 64 | **Verse:** 5

sā va̍ha̱ yokṣabhi̱ravā̱toṣo̱ vara̱ṃ vaha̍si̱ joṣa̱manu̍ |

tvaṃ di̍vo duhita̱ryā ha̍ de̱vī pū̱rvahū̍tau ma̱ṃhanā̍ darśa̱tā bhū̍:

## Verse 6
