# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 31

**5 suhotro bhāradvāja: | indra: | triṣṭup ,4 śakvarī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 1

abhū̱reko̍ rayipate rayī̱ṇāmā hasta̍yoradhithā indra kṛ̱ṣṭīḥ |
vi to̱ke a̱psu tana̍ye ca̱ sūre'vo̍canta carṣa̱ṇayo̱ vivā̍caḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 2

tvadbhi̱yendra̱ pārthi̍vāni̱ viśvācyu̍tā ciccyāvayante̱ rajā̍ṃsi |

dyāvā̱kṣāmā̱ parva̍tāso̱ vanā̍ni̱ viśva̍ṃ dṛ̱l̤haṃ bha̍yate̱ ajma̱nnā te̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 3

tvaṃ kutse̍nā̱bhi śuṣṇa̍mindrā̱śuṣa̍ṃ yudhya̱ kuya̍va̱ṃ gavi̍ṣṭau |

daśa̍ prapi̱tve adha̱ sūrya̍sya muṣā̱yaśca̱kramavi̍ve̱ rapā̍ṃsi

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 4

tvaṃ śa̱tānyava̱ śamba̍rasya̱ puro̍ jaghanthāpra̱tīni̱ dasyo̍: |

aśi̍kṣo̱ yatra̱ śacyā̍ śacīvo̱ divo̍dāsāya sunva̱te su̍takre bha̱radvā̍jāya gṛṇa̱te vasū̍ni

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 31 | **Verse:** 5

sa sa̍tyasatvanmaha̱te raṇā̍ya̱ ratha̱mā ti̍ṣṭha tuvinṛmṇa bhī̱mam |

yā̱hi pra̍pathi̱nnava̱sopa̍ ma̱drikpra ca̍ śruta śrāvaya carṣa̱ṇibhya̍:
