# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 68

**11 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | indrāvaruṇau | triṣṭup , 9-10 jagatī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 1

śru̱ṣṭī vā̍ṃ ya̱jña udya̍taḥ sa̱joṣā̍ manu̱ṣvadvṛ̱ktaba̍rhiṣo̱ yaja̍dhyai |
ā ya indrā̱varu̍ṇāvi̱ṣe a̱dya ma̱he su̱mnāya̍ ma̱ha ā̍va̱varta̍t

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 2

tā hi śreṣṭhā̍ de̱vatā̍tā tu̱jā śūrā̍ṇā̱ṃ śavi̍ṣṭhā̱ tā hi bhū̱tam |

ma̱ghonā̱ṃ maṃhi̍ṣṭhā tuvi̱śuṣma̍ ṛ̱tena̍ vṛtra̱turā̱ sarva̍senā

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 3

tā gṛ̍ṇīhi nama̱sye̍bhiḥ śū̱ṣaiḥ su̱mnebhi̱rindrā̱varu̍ṇā cakā̱nā |

vajre̍ṇā̱nyaḥ śava̍sā̱ hanti̍ vṛ̱traṃ siṣa̍ktya̱nyo vṛ̱jane̍ṣu̱ vipra̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 4

gnāśca̱ yannara̍śca vāvṛ̱dhanta̱ viśve̍ de̱vāso̍ na̱rāṃ svagū̍rtāḥ |

praibhya̍ indrāvaruṇā mahi̱tvā dyauśca̍ pṛthivi bhūtamu̱rvī

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 5

sa itsu̱dānu̱: svavā̍m̐ ṛ̱tāvendrā̱ yo vā̍ṃ varuṇa̱ dāśa̍ti̱ tman |

i̱ṣā sa dvi̱ṣasta̍re̱ddāsvā̱nvaṃsa̍dra̱yiṃ ra̍yi̱vata̍śca̱ janā̍n

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 6

yaṃ yu̱vaṃ dā̱śva̍dhvarāya devā ra̱yiṃ dha̱ttho vasu̍mantaṃ puru̱kṣum |

a̱sme sa i̍ndrāvaruṇā̱vapi̍ ṣyā̱tpra yo bha̱nakti̍ va̱nuṣā̱maśa̍stīḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 7

u̱ta na̍: sutrā̱tro de̱vago̍pāḥ sū̱ribhya̍ indrāvaruṇā ra̱yiḥ ṣyā̍t |

yeṣā̱ṃ śuṣma̱: pṛta̍nāsu sā̱hvānpra sa̱dyo dyu̱mnā ti̱rate̱ tatu̍riḥ

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 8

nū na̍ indrāvaruṇā gṛṇā̱nā pṛ̱ṅktaṃ ra̱yiṃ sau̍śrava̱sāya̍ devā |

i̱tthā gṛ̱ṇanto̍ ma̱hina̍sya̱ śardho̱'po na nā̱vā du̍ri̱tā ta̍rema

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 9

pra sa̱mrāje̍ bṛha̱te manma̱ nu pri̱yamarca̍ de̱vāya̱ varu̍ṇāya sa̱pratha̍: |

a̱yaṃ ya u̱rvī ma̍hi̱nā mahi̍vrata̱: kratvā̍ vi̱bhātya̱jaro̱ na śo̱ciṣā̍

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 10

indrā̍varuṇā sutapāvi̱maṃ su̱taṃ soma̍ṃ pibata̱ṃ madya̍ṃ dhṛtavratā |

yu̱vo ratho̍ adhva̱raṃ de̱vavī̍taye̱ prati̱ svasa̍ra̱mupa̍ yāti pī̱taye̍

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 68 | **Verse:** 11

indrā̍varuṇā̱ madhu̍mattamasya̱ vṛṣṇa̱: soma̍sya vṛṣa̱ṇā vṛ̍ṣethām |

i̱daṃ vā̱mandha̱: pari̍ṣiktama̱sme ā̱sadyā̱sminba̱rhiṣi̍ mādayethām
