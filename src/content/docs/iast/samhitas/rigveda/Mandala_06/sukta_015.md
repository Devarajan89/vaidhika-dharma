# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 15

**19 bārhaspatyo bharadvājo, vītahavya āṅgiraso vā | agni : |jagatī; 3; 15 śakvarī; 6 atiśakvarī; 10 – 14, 16,19 triṣṭup , 17 anuṣṭup ; 18 bṛhatī |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 1

i̱mamū̱ ṣu vo̱ ati̍thimuṣa̱rbudha̱ṃ viśvā̍sāṃ vi̱śāṃ pati̍mṛñjase gi̱rā |
vetīd di̱vo ja̱nuṣā̱ kacci̱dā śuci̱rjyok ci̍datti̱ garbho̱ yadacyu̍tam

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 2

sa tvaṃ suprī̍to vī̱taha̍vye adbhuta̱ praśa̍stibhirmahayase di̱vedi̍ve

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 3

## Verse 5

tūrva̱n na yāma̱nneta̍śasya̱ nū raṇa̱ ā yo ghṛ̱ṇe na ta̍tṛṣā̱ṇo a̱jara̍:

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 15 | **Verse:** 6

upa̍ vo gī̱rbhira̱mṛta̍ṃ vivāsata de̱vo de̱veṣu̱ vana̍te̱ hi vārya̍ṃ de̱vo de̱veṣu̱ vana̍te̱ hi no̱ duva̍:

## Verse 7
