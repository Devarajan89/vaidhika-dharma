# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 61

**14 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | sarasvatī| gāyatrī; 1-3, 13 jagatī; 14 triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 1

i̱yama̍dadādrabha̱samṛ̍ṇa̱cyuta̱ṃ divo̍dāsaṃ vadhrya̱śvāya̍ dā̱śuṣe̍ |
yā śaśva̍ntamāca̱khādā̍va̱saṃ pa̱ṇiṃ tā te̍ dā̱trāṇi̍ tavi̱ṣā sa̍rasvati

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 2

i̱yaṃ śuṣme̍bhirbisa̱khā i̍vāruja̱tsānu̍ girī̱ṇāṃ ta̍vi̱ṣebhi̍rū̱rmibhi̍: |

pā̱rā̱va̱ta̱ghnīmava̍se suvṛ̱ktibhi̱: sara̍svatī̱mā vi̍vāsema dhī̱tibhi̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 3

sara̍svati deva̱nido̱ ni ba̍rhaya pra̱jāṃ viśva̍sya̱ bṛsa̍yasya mā̱yina̍: |

u̱ta kṣi̱tibhyo̱'vanī̍ravindo vi̱ṣame̍bhyo asravo vājinīvati

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 4

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 61 | **Verse:** 5
