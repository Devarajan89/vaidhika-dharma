# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 23

**10 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | indra: |triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 1

su̱ta it tvaṃ nimi̍śla indra̱ some̱ stome̱ brahma̍ṇi śa̱syamā̍na u̱kthe |
yad vā̍ yu̱ktābhyā̍ṃ maghava̱n hari̍bhyā̱ṃ bibhra̱d vajra̍ṃ bā̱hvori̍ndra̱ yāsi̍

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 2

yad vā̍ di̱vi pārye̱ suṣvi̍mindra vṛtra̱hatye'va̍si̱ śūra̍sātau |

yad vā̍ dakṣa̍sya bi̱bhyuṣo̱ abi̍bhya̱dara̍ndhaya̱: śardha̍ta indra̱ dasyū̍n

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 3

pātā̍ su̱tamindro̍ astu̱ soma̍ṃ praṇe̱nīru̱gro ja̍ri̱tāra̍mū̱tī |

kartā̍ vī̱rāya̱ suṣva̍ya u lo̱kaṃ dātā̱ vasu̍ stuva̱te kī̱raye̍ cit

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 4

ganteyā̍nti̱ sava̍nā̱ hari̍bhyāṃ ba̱bhrirvajra̍ṃ pa̱piḥ soma̍ṃ da̱dirgāḥ |

kartā̍ vī̱raṃ narya̱ṃ sarva̍vīra̱ṃ śrotā̱ hava̍ṃ gṛṇa̱taḥ stoma̍vāhāḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 5

asmai̍ va̱yaṃ yad vā̱vāna̱ tad vi̍viṣma̱ indrā̍ya̱ yo na̍: pra̱divo̱ apa̱skaḥ |

su̱te some̍ stu̱masi̱ śaṃsa̍du̱kthendrā̍ya̱ brahma̱ vardha̍na̱ṃ yathāsa̍t

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 6

brahmā̍ṇi̱ hi ca̍kṛ̱ṣe vardha̍nāni̱ tāva̍t ta indra ma̱tibhi̍rviviṣmaḥ |

su̱te some̍ sutapā̱: śaṃta̍māni̱ rāndyā̍ kriyāsma̱ vakṣa̍ṇāni ya̱jñaiḥ

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 7

sa no̍ bodhi puro̱l̤āśa̱ṃ rarā̍ṇa̱: pibā̱ tu soma̱ṃ goṛ̍jīkamindra |

edaṃ ba̱rhiryaja̍mānasya sīdo̱ruṃ kṛ̍dhi tvāya̱ta u̍ lo̱kam

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 8

sa ma̍ndasvā̱ hyanu̱ joṣa̍mugra̱ pra tvā̍ ya̱jñāsa̍ i̱me a̍śnuvantu |

preme havā̍saḥ puruhū̱tama̱sme ā tve̱yaṃ dhīrava̍sa indra yamyāḥ

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 9

taṃ va̍: sakhāya̱: saṃ yathā̍ su̱teṣu̱ some̍bhirīṃ pṛṇatā bho̱jamindra̍m |

ku̱vit tasmā̱ asa̍ti no̱ bharā̍ya̱ na suṣvi̱mindro'va̍se mṛdhāti

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 23 | **Verse:** 10
