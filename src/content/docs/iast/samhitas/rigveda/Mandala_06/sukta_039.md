# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 39

**5 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: | indra: | triṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 1

ma̱ndrasya̍ ka̱verdi̱vyasya̱ vahne̱rvipra̍manmano vaca̱nasya̱ madhva̍: |
apā̍ na̱stasya̍ saca̱nasya̍ de̱veṣo̍ yuvasva gṛṇa̱te goa̍grāḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 2

a̱yamu̍śā̱naḥ paryadri̍mu̱srā ṛ̱tadhī̍tibhiṝta̱yugyu̍jā̱naḥ |

ru̱jadaru̍gṇa̱ṃ vi va̱lasya̱ sānu̍ṃ pa̱ṇīm̐rvaco̍bhira̱bhi yo̍dha̱dindra̍:

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 3

a̱yaṃ dyo̍tayada̱dyuto̱ vya1ktūndo̱ṣā vasto̍: śa̱rada̱ indu̍rindra |

i̱maṃ ke̱tuma̍dadhu̱rnū ci̱dahnā̱ṃ śuci̍janmana u̱ṣasa̍ścakāra

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 4

a̱yaṃ ro̍cayada̱ruco̍ rucā̱no̱3'yaṃ vā̍saya̱dvyṛ1tena̍ pū̱rvīḥ |

a̱yamī̍yata ṛta̱yugbhi̱raśvai̍: sva̱rvidā̱ nābhi̍nā carṣaṇi̱prāḥ

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 39 | **Verse:** 5

nū gṛ̍ṇā̱no gṛ̍ṇa̱te pra̍tna rāja̱nniṣa̍: pinva vasu̱deyā̍ya pū̱rvīḥ |

a̱pa oṣa̍dhīravi̱ṣā vanā̍ni̱ gā arva̍to̱ nṝnṛ̱case̍ rirīhi
