# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 53

**10 bārhaspatyo bharadvāja: |pūṣā | gāyatrī, 8 anuṣṭup |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 1

va̱yamu̍ tvā pathaspate̱ ratha̱ṃ na vāja̍sātaye | dhi̱ye pū̍ṣannayujmahi

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 2

a̱bhi no̱ narya̱ṃ vasu̍ vī̱raṃ praya̍tadakṣiṇam | vā̱maṃ gṛ̱hapa̍tiṃ naya

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 3

adi̍tsantaṃ cidāghṛṇe̱ pūṣa̱ndānā̍ya codaya | pa̱ṇeści̱dvi mra̍dā̱ mana̍:

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 4

vi pa̱tho vāja̍sātaye cinu̱hi vi mṛdho̍ jahi | sādha̍ntāmugra no̱ dhiya̍:

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 5

pari̍ tṛndhi paṇī̱nāmāra̍yā̱ hṛda̍yā kave | athe̍ma̱smabhya̍ṃ randhaya

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 53 | **Verse:** 6
