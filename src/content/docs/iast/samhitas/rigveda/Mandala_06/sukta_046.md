# Maṇḍala 6 — Sūktam 46

**14 śaṃyurbārhaspatya:| indra: pragātha: [= viṣamā bṛhatī, samā satobṛhatī ] |**

---

## Verse 1

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 1

tvāmiddhi havā̍mahe sā̱tā vāja̍sya kā̱rava̍: |
tvāṃ vṛ̱treṣvi̍ndra̱ satpa̍ti̱ṃ nara̱stvāṃ kāṣṭhā̱svarva̍taḥ

---

## Verse 2

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 2

sa tvaṃ na̍ścitra vajrahasta dhṛṣṇu̱yā ma̱haḥ sta̍vā̱no a̍drivaḥ |

gāmaśva̍ṃ ra̱thya̍mindra̱ saṃ ki̍ra sa̱trā vāja̱ṃ na ji̱gyuṣe̍

---

## Verse 3

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 3

yaḥ sa̍trā̱hā vica̍rṣaṇi̱rindra̱ṃ taṃ hū̍mahe va̱yam |

saha̍sramuṣka̱ tuvi̍nṛmṇa̱ satpa̍te̱ bhavā̍ sa̱matsu̍ no vṛ̱dhe

---

## Verse 4

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 4

bādha̍se̱ janā̍nvṛṣa̱bheva̍ ma̱nyunā̱ ghṛṣau̍ mī̱l̤ha ṛ̍cīṣama |

a̱smāka̍ṃ bodhyavi̱tā ma̍hādha̱ne ta̱nūṣva̱psu sūrye̍

---

## Verse 5

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 5

indra̱ jyeṣṭha̍ṃ na̱ ā bha̍ra̱m̐ oji̍ṣṭha̱ṃ papu̍ri̱ śrava̍: |

yene̱me ci̍tra vajrahasta̱ roda̍sī̱ obhe su̍śipra̱ prāḥ

---

## Verse 6

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 6

tvāmu̱gramava̍se carṣaṇī̱saha̱ṃ rāja̍nde̱veṣu̍ hūmahe |

viśvā̱ su no̍ vithu̱rā pi̍bda̱nā va̍so̱'mitrā̍ntsu̱ṣahā̍nkṛdhi

---

## Verse 7

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 7

yadi̍ndra̱ nāhu̍ṣī̱ṣvām̐ ojo̍ nṛ̱mṇaṃ ca̍ kṛ̱ṣṭiṣu̍ |

yadvā̱ pañca̍ kṣitī̱nāṃ dyu̱mnamā bha̍ra sa̱trā viśvā̍ni̱ pauṃsyā̍

---

## Verse 8

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 8

yadvā̍ tṛ̱kṣau ma̍ghavandru̱hyāvā jane̱ yatpū̱rau kacca̱ vṛṣṇya̍m |

a̱smabhya̱ṃ tadri̍rīhi̱ saṃ nṛ̱ṣāhye̱'mitrā̍npṛ̱tsu tu̱rvaṇe̍

---

## Verse 9

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 9

indra̍ tri̱dhātu̍ śara̱ṇaṃ tri̱varū̍thaṃ svasti̱mat |

cha̱rdirya̍ccha ma̱ghava̍dbhyaśca̱ mahya̍ṃ ca yā̱vayā̍ di̱dyume̍bhyaḥ

---

## Verse 10

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 10

ye ga̍vya̱tā mana̍sā̱ śatru̍māda̱bhura̍bhipra̱ghnanti̍ dhṛṣṇu̱yā |

adha̍ smā no maghavannindra girvaṇastanū̱pā anta̍mo bhava

---

## Verse 11

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 11

adha̍ smā no vṛ̱dhe bha̱vendra̍ nā̱yama̍vā yu̱dhi |

yada̱ntari̍kṣe pa̱taya̍nti pa̱rṇino̍ di̱dyava̍sti̱gmamū̍rdhānaḥ

---

## Verse 12

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 12

yatra̱ śūrā̍sasta̱nvo̍ vitanva̱te pri̱yā śarma̍ pitṝ̱ṇām |

adha̍ smā yaccha ta̱nve̱3 tane̍ ca cha̱rdira̱citta̍ṃ yā̱vaya̱ dveṣa̍:

---

## Verse 13

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 13

yadi̍ndra̱ sarge̱ arva̍taśco̱dayā̍se mahādha̱ne |

a̱sa̱ma̱ne adhva̍ni vṛji̱ne pa̱thi śye̱nām̐ i̍va śravasya̱taḥ

## Verse 14

**Mandala:** 6 | **Sukta:** 46 | **Verse:** 14
